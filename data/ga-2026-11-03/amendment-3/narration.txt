Amendment 3 is about paying for emergency 9-1-1 systems in Georgia.
Today, the constitution has no Georgia Next Generation 9-1-1 Fund.
The amendment would let the General Assembly create the fund by law.
Lawmakers could dedicate certain existing revenues to the fund, including revenues reallocated by law.
The money could be used only for expanding, maintaining and operating 9-1-1 systems, including the move to Next Generation 9-1-1.
Money in the fund would not lapse.
The state's amendment booklet does not include a cost estimate for the amendment.
The state's booklet includes no official argument for the amendment.
The state's booklet includes no official argument against the amendment.
A yes vote adds the Next Generation 9-1-1 Fund to the constitution.
A no vote leaves the Next Generation 9-1-1 Fund out of the constitution.
