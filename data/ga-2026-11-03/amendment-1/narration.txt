Amendment 1 is about farm and timber land that Georgia taxes as conservation use property.
Such land is taxed on a formula that includes its current use and yearly productivity.
Owners promise to keep the land in that use; breaking the promise within ten years means repaying the tax savings.
Today, the acreage ceiling for one owner is 2,000 acres.
The amendment would raise the ceiling to 4,000 acres.
The state's amendment booklet does not include a cost estimate for the amendment.
The Department of Community Affairs said current data did not allow a local fiscal note.
The state's booklet includes no official argument against the amendment.
The state's booklet includes no official argument for the amendment.
A yes vote raises the acreage ceiling to 4,000 acres.
A no vote keeps the acreage ceiling at 2,000 acres.
