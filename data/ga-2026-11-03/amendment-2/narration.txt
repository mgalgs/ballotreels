Amendment 2 is about how Georgia elects probate court judges.
Each Georgia county has a probate court, and the office of its judge keeps these amendments on file for the public.
The state constitution has superior court, state court, Supreme Court and Court of Appeals judges elected on a nonpartisan basis.
The amendment would require nonpartisan elections for all probate judges too.
Other judges would still be chosen as they were in 1983, unless local law changes that.
The state's amendment booklet does not include a cost estimate for the amendment.
The state's booklet includes no official argument for the amendment.
The state's booklet includes no official argument against the amendment.
A yes vote adds nonpartisan elections for all probate judges to the constitution.
A no vote does not add nonpartisan elections for all probate judges to the constitution.
