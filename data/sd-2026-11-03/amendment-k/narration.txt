Amendment K is about unclaimed property, like bank deposits and refunds.
Today, property left unclaimed for over three years goes to the State Treasurer, who holds it for the owner.
Amendment K would put that property in a trust fund in the state constitution.
Each year, part of the interest and income from the trust would go into the general fund, in an amount set by law.
South Dakota's ballot question pamphlet does not include a cost estimate for the amendment.
Supporters say Amendment K keeps unclaimed money safe for owners, and its interest pays for services without raising taxes.
The state's ballot question pamphlet says no statement against Amendment K was provided, so only the supporters' statement is printed.
A yes vote adopts the amendment.
A no vote leaves the Constitution as it is.
