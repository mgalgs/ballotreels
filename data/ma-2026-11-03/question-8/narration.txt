Question 8 is about recreational marijuana stores.
Today, state laws legalize, regulate and tax those stores.
The question would repeal those laws, starting January 1, 2028.
Adults 21 and older could still have up to one ounce, and medical sales would continue.
The Executive Office of Administration and Finance says repeal would reduce revenue from taxes that raised about $280 million for the state and $50 million locally in fiscal 2025.
Opponents say the question would return marijuana to an illicit market with no safety testing, and shut down hundreds of small businesses.
Supporters say marijuana is now the most common drug in fatal car accidents here, and modern concentrates and edibles are linked to psychosis.
A yes vote would prohibit retail sales of recreational marijuana.
A no vote would make no change to the law on marijuana.
