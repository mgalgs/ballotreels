Question 5 is about a limit on state revenue.
Today, state law already limits revenue and can require refunds to taxpayers.
The new limit would be revenue from the year before, plus average wage and salary growth.
Revenue over the limit would be refunded to taxpayers the following year.
The Executive Office of Administration and Finance says the state budget, including local aid, and the rainy day fund would get less money.
Supporters say taxpayers deserve refunds when the state collects more than it needs, and important services would not be cut.
Opponents say the strict limit would force repeated budget cuts to schools and health care, and give millionaires frequent rebates.
A yes vote would change the revenue limit and provide rebates above it.
A no vote would make no change in the revenue law.
