Question 9 asks whether to keep a firearms law already in effect.
The Legislature passed it in July 2024, 124 to 33 in the House and 35 to 5 in the Senate.
Today, the law regulates ghost guns, machine-gun devices like bump stocks, and assault-style weapons.
Repeal would undo its changes.
The Executive Office of Administration and Finance says the state has made significant investments in the law, and repeal would require resources to suspend or redirect them.
Opponents say the state already had some of the strictest gun laws, and this law pulls officers from violent crime into paperwork.
Supporters say the law bans ghost guns and devices that turn guns into machine guns, and is already helping keep communities safe.
A yes vote would keep the law in place.
A no vote would repeal the law.
