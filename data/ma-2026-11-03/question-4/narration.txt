Question 4 is about registering to vote on Election Day.
Today, voters cannot register at their polling place on Election Day.
The question would let eligible people register, or update their address, at the polls.
Registrants would show proof of residency, and sign an oath that false information is a felony.
The Executive Office of Administration and Finance finds no discernible material fiscal consequences.
Opponents say officials would lose the time to check registrants in advance, and errors might surface only after ballots are cast.
Supporters say the rest of New England already offers it, and proof of residence and a felony-backed oath keep it secure.
A yes vote would permit eligible citizens to register at their polling place on Election Day.
A no vote would make no change to the laws governing voter registration.
