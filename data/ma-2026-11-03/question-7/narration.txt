Question 7 is about where single-family homes may be built.
Today, local zoning rules decide where single-family homes may go.
The question would allow single-family homes on residential lots of at least 5,000 square feet, with public water and sewer.
Towns could still reasonably regulate height, spacing, parking and short-term rentals.
The Executive Office of Administration and Finance finds no discernible material fiscal consequences.
Opponents say a statewide mandate would override the local planning by officials who answer to taxpayers and know their communities best.
Supporters say requiring huge lot sizes makes houses expensive, and smaller lots mean more affordable homes for young families and seniors.
A yes vote would allow single-family homes on lots of 5,000 square feet or more.
A no vote would make no change to the law on building single-family homes.
