Question 6 is about a new natural resource conservation fund.
Today, sales taxes on sporting goods, recreational vehicles and golf courses support the state operating budget.
A new Nature for All Fund would get half of those taxes, then all after July 2028, subject to appropriation by the Legislature.
The Executive Office of Administration and Finance says it would divert tens of millions of dollars a year from the budget, with substantial implementation costs.
The argument against says the earmark would take money from other state programs, and the Legislature may not fund it.
Supporters say the question protects water, land and wildlife without raising taxes, using the sales tax already being paid.
A yes vote would create the fund, paid from some state sales taxes.
A no vote would not create the fund or change how sales taxes are spent.
