Question 1 is about who must follow the Public Records Law.
Today, the Public Records Law does not cover the Legislature or the Office of the Governor.
The question would make most of their records public.
Policy development documents, and constituent requests for help with government services, would be exempt.
The Executive Office of Administration and Finance finds no discernible material fiscal consequences.
Supporters say the Legislature and the Office of the Governor should follow the same records law every city, town and state agency follows.
The argument against says the limited exemptions would chill talk by lawmakers, and expose residents' letters, so fewer would write to officials.
A yes vote would make most records of the Legislature and the Office of the Governor public.
A no vote would make no change to the Public Records Law.
