Constitutional Amendment 1 is about when a Governor may grant pardons or commute sentences.
Today, the Governor may grant pardons and commute sentences, except in cases of impeachment.
The amendment would add an exception starting sixty days before each election for Governor.
That exception would end the fifth Tuesday succeeding the election.
The ballot question says the exception ends when the Governor is sworn in.
The ballot does not include a cost estimate for the amendment.
The Legislative Research Commission called the local cost of adding the amendment to ballots minimal.
That statement covered an earlier version of the bill.
Kentucky publishes no official argument against the amendment.
Kentucky publishes no official argument for the amendment.
A yes vote adds that exception for the election period.
A no vote leaves the pardon power unchanged.
