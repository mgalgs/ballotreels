Proposition 144 is about voter ID, ballot counting and other election rules.
Today, state law accepts more than government-issued ID as proof of identity.
The measure would require government-issued ID to vote in each election, including by mail.
Voters could choose to have their ballots counted where they vote on election day.
A new court standard could make election laws harder to challenge, the ballot says.
The Legislative Council says the Legislature must fund whatever the measure needs.
Opponents say the measure makes voting by mail harder, especially for seniors, rural and disabled voters.
Supporters say the measure delivers trusted results on election night, and locks protections in the constitution.
A yes vote means adding these election rules to the constitution.
A no vote means the constitution's current election provisions stay the same.
