Proposition 320 is about how school districts split their spending.
Today, the state sends districts money through its Classroom Site Fund.
The measure covers districts of 7,500 or more students, or in counties of 500,000 or more.
They would have to put at least 60 percent of operating spending into direct instruction.
Those below must raise their share at least half a percent yearly, or face growing Classroom Site Fund cuts, barring waivers.
The Legislative Council says withheld money would go to complying districts.
Opponents say the measure risks cuts to nurses and counselors, and overrides local control.
Supporters say the measure sends money to classrooms, not bureaucracy, and supports teacher pay.
A yes vote sets a 60 percent direct instruction minimum for those districts.
A no vote keeps current school budget laws.
