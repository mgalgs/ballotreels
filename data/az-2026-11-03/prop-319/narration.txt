Proposition 319 is about cameras that catch drivers speeding or ignoring traffic signals and signs.
Today, cities, towns and state agencies may use photo enforcement under state law.
Under the measure, cameras would be allowed only under a contract in place by December 31, 2026.
Cities and towns with cameras would need local voters' approval at the next general election to keep them.
Voters would decide again every 10 years.
Arizona's guide does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Supporters say the measure stops for-profit cameras that invade privacy, and gives voters control.
Opponents say cameras save lives, and the measure takes that tool from local communities.
A yes vote means no new camera contracts after 2026, and local votes on existing cameras.
A no vote means current photo enforcement laws stay the same.
