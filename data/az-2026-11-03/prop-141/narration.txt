Proposition 141 is about taxes or fees based on how many miles people drive.
Today, Arizona's Constitution has no section on taxing or tracking miles driven.
The measure would bar the state and local governments from taxing people by the miles they drive.
Governments also could not monitor or limit miles driven, unless a driver agrees.
Government vehicles, and in certain cases interstate commercial vehicles, would be exempt.
Arizona's guide prints no cost estimate for the measure.
Supporters say mileage tracking is government surveillance, and the measure bans it permanently in the constitution.
Opponents say fuel tax revenue is eroding, and the measure bans a road funding option prematurely.
A yes vote means banning mileage taxes, and mileage tracking without consent.
A no vote means the constitution's current taxation language stays the same.
