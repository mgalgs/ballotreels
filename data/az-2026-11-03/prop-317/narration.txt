Proposition 317 is about drug cartels.
The Legislature says the measure carries out Proposition 314, a border law voters passed in 2024.
Arizona already has a Department of Homeland Security.
The measure would declare drug cartels to be terrorist organizations.
Cartels would be defined as groups engaged in human smuggling, drug trafficking for profit, or terrorism.
The department would have to use all lawful means within its authority against cartels.
Arizona's guide prints no cost estimate for the measure.
Supporters say cartels act as terrorists, and the measure requires Arizona to fight back.
Opponents say the measure is symbolic, does nothing, and wastes voters' and lawmakers' time.
A yes vote means declaring drug cartels terrorist organizations.
A no vote means keeping the current laws on terrorism and the department.
