Proposition 142 is about preferential treatment by race in Arizona's public institutions.
Today, Arizona's Constitution already bars the state from preferential treatment or discrimination based on race or ethnicity.
The measure would apply that ban even within federal programs.
Governments could not require or ask for statements supporting certain race-based diversity, equity and inclusion.
Public schools and colleges could not fund offices that promote preferential treatment by race.
Arizona's guide does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Supporters say the measure ensures equal treatment based on merit, and ends forced support for DEI.
Opponents say the measure rolls back equal opportunity efforts, and invites confusion, fear and costly lawsuits.
A yes vote means adding these specific bans to the constitution.
A no vote means keeping the constitution's current ban as it is.
