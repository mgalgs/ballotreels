Amendment 3 is about bail for people convicted of certain aggravated offenses against a minor.
Today, the state constitution provides a right to bail, with limited exceptions.
After a conviction, bail may still be allowed until the case is final, depending on the sentence.
The amendment would allow no bail at all after a conviction for an aggravated offense against a minor.
A state law already lists which offenses count as aggravated.
The Legislative Fiscal Office says those convicted would stay in parish jails until any transfer.
The office says local government spending may rise, by an amount it cannot determine.
Louisiana publishes no official argument for the amendment.
