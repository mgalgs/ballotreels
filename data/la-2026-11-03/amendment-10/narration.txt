Amendment 10 is about a tax exemption for blighted or derelict property that has been rehabilitated.
Today, the state constitution lists which property is exempt from property tax, and allows some optional exemptions.
The amendment would add an optional exemption for blighted or derelict property once it is rehabilitated.
The Legislature would define blighted and derelict property, and set how long the exemption lasts and how it is run.
The Legislative Fiscal Office says the effect on local property tax revenue cannot be determined.
The office says that depends on how many parishes adopt the exemption and how much rehabilitation follows.
Louisiana publishes no official argument against the amendment.
Louisiana publishes no official argument for the amendment.
A yes vote allows this exemption, with details set later by the Legislature.
A no vote means no such exemption is added.
