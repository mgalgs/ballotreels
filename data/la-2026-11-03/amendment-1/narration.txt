Amendment 1 is about a tax exemption for surviving spouses of disabled veterans.
Today, surviving spouses of disabled veterans keep an extra property tax exemption while living in and owning the property.
The amendment would let a surviving spouse transfer the exemption once, to a new home.
The new exemption could be no larger than the one last claimed on the old home.
The Legislative Fiscal Office says local tax revenue may fall, but expects no significant effect overall.
In 2025, 229 surviving spouses had the exemption; the office expects few to move in any year.
Louisiana publishes no official argument for the amendment.
Louisiana publishes no official argument against the amendment.
A yes vote allows a one-time transfer of the exemption to a new home.
A no vote keeps today's rule, with no transfer.
