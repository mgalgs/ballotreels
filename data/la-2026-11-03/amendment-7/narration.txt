Amendment 7 is about public money for drinking water service lines on private property.
Today, the state constitution bars lending or donating public funds to any person or organization, with listed exceptions.
The amendment would let local governments use public funds to identify, remove or replace certain water lines on property owned by utility customers.
The lines covered are those made of or affected by materials named in a federal Environmental Protection Agency rule on lead and copper.
The Legislative Fiscal Office says local government spending may rise, to the extent local governments use public funds on these lines.
Louisiana publishes no official argument against the amendment.

A no vote keeps the ban, with no exception for these lines.
