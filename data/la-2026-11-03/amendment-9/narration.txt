Amendment 9 is about the income limit for the special assessment level on homes.
For homeowners 65 and older, some disabled owners and others, the special assessment level keeps the assessment of the home from rising.
Today, an owner also needs adjusted gross income of $100,000 or less, adjusted for inflation from 2026.
The amendment would raise the limit to $150,000 from 2027, adjusted for inflation from 2028.
Taxing authorities would absorb the lost revenue, not other taxpayers.
The Legislative Fiscal Office says the change can only decrease local property tax revenue, by an amount that cannot be determined.
Louisiana publishes no official argument against the amendment.
Louisiana publishes no official argument for the amendment.
A yes vote raises the income limit to $150,000.
A no vote keeps the $100,000 limit, adjusted for inflation.
