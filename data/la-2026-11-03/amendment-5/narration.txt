Amendment 5 is about nonrecurring state money paid to state retirement systems.
At least 25 percent of nonrecurring money in the official state forecast must go to state retirement systems.
Today, each system must apply the money to its oldest unfunded accrued liability first.
The amendment would let a system apply the money to any of its unfunded liability instead.
The Legislative Auditor's actuarial note expects no measurable effect on retirement costs or revenues over five years.
Without instructions from the Legislature, the note says, each system would need its own policy for the money.
Louisiana publishes no official argument against the amendment.
