Amendment 4 is about how many terms a person may serve as governor.
Today, a governor who has served more than one and one-half terms in a row cannot be elected to the next term.
The amendment would bar anyone who has served more than one and one-half terms from being elected governor again, for any future term.
The limit would no longer depend on serving the terms in a row.
Louisiana's official statement does not include a cost estimate for the amendment.
Louisiana publishes no official argument for the amendment.
Louisiana publishes no official argument against the amendment.
A yes vote places a lifetime limit on anyone who has served more than one and one-half terms as governor.
A no vote keeps today's limit, which bars only the next term.
