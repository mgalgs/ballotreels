Amendment 8 is about expropriation, the taking of property, by a foreign adversary.
Today, a private entity that the law allows to expropriate may take property only for a public and necessary purpose, with just compensation to the owner.
A court decides whether the purpose is public and necessary.
The amendment would bar any private entity that is a foreign adversary, or an agent of one, from taking or damaging property.
Who counts as a foreign adversary is defined by law.
Louisiana's official statement does not include a cost estimate for the amendment.
Louisiana publishes no official argument against the amendment.
Louisiana publishes no official argument for the amendment.
A yes vote bars expropriation by a foreign adversary or its agent.
A no vote keeps today's expropriation rules, with no such ban.
