Amendment 6 is about an extra tax exemption for homeowners 65 and older, if local voters approve.
Today, the homestead exemption covers the first $75,000 of market value.
A parish or municipality could offer the new exemption only after its own voters approve it.
Where approved, owners at the special assessment level would get an exemption that grows with age, from $60,000 to $300,000 of market value.
Taxing authorities would absorb the lost revenue, not other taxpayers.
The Legislative Fiscal Office expects a significant drop in local property tax revenue where voters approve, by an amount it cannot determine.
Louisiana publishes no official argument against the amendment.
