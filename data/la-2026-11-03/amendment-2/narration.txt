Amendment 2 is about local property tax rates after a reassessment.
A millage rate is a local property tax rate.
After each reassessment, every four years, rates are adjusted so the tax collected stays the same.
Today, a taxing authority may vote to raise its rate back to the prior maximum, but only until the next reassessment.
The amendment would let a taxing authority keep a lower rate and still return to the maximum later.
The Legislative Fiscal Office says property tax revenue may go uncollected if rates stay lower for longer, by an amount it cannot determine.
Louisiana publishes no official argument for the amendment.
Louisiana publishes no official argument against the amendment.
A yes vote lets taxing authorities keep lower rates without losing the maximum.
A no vote keeps today's rule, ending that ability at the next reassessment.
