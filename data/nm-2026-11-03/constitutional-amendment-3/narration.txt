Constitutional Amendment 3 is about how regents are chosen.
Today, the governor appoints the regents who run state colleges, universities and specialized schools; the Senate approves them.
Amendment 3 would have the Legislature create a bipartisan or nonpartisan nominating committee for each institution.
The governor could appoint only the nominees a committee selects.
Regents, except students, could not have changed parties in the prior 12 months.
The 2026 General Election Voter Guide does not include a cost estimate for the amendment.
Opponents say the amendment would alter checks and balances, and nominating committees are not accountable to voters.
Supporters say committees would offer nominees who best serve each institution, and could limit politics in appointments.
A vote for means the governor's appointments would be limited to committee nominees.
A vote against means the governor's appointments would not be limited to committee nominees.
