Question 2 is about marriage in Virginia's Constitution.
Today, the Constitution prohibits same-sex marriages, civil unions and similar legal relationships.
The explanation says a 2015 United States Supreme Court decision made it unenforceable.
If that decision were overturned, the explanation says, it would be enforceable again.
The amendment would replace the prohibition with a fundamental right to marry.
The state would have to treat any lawful marriage between two adults equally, regardless of sex, gender or race.
The Department of Planning and Budget estimates $205,000 for public notice of statewide referendums, not the amendment itself.
Virginia's explanation includes no official argument against the amendment.
Virginia's explanation includes no official argument for the amendment.
A yes vote removes the same-sex marriage ban.
A no vote keeps the same-sex marriage ban.
