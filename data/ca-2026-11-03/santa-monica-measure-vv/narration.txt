Measure VV asks the voters of this city whether to extend eviction protections to tenants who rent single-unit homes and condominiums.
Today, the charter allows evictions only for listed reasons, but those protections generally leave out single-unit homes and condominiums.
An owner who evicts a tenant to move in must intend to do so within 30 days, and to stay at least one year.
The City Council says stricter rules set in 2022 for rent-controlled units should apply to other rentals too.
Tenants of single-unit homes and condominiums would get the same eviction protections as other renters.
An owner moving in would have to intend to do so within 60 days, and to stay at least two years.
An owner who fails to, without extenuating circumstances, would have to offer the unit back and pay the moving costs of the tenant.
The official list of measures from the county does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
The official county list of measures for this election says that no argument against Measure VV was submitted, by any group or person.
Two council members, the rent board vice chair and one other person filed an argument for Measure VV; its text was not gathered.
A yes vote extends the protections and sets the stricter move-in rule.
A no vote leaves the eviction rules in the charter as they are.
