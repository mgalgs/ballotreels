ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE VV

Protect Santa Monica Homeowners and Rental Housing Stock: Vote NO on Measure VV

By expanding rent control to single-family homes and condominiums, this proposed Charter Amendment represents an extreme and counterproductive policy shift. It unfairly penalizes individual families and small property owners while actively worsening Santa Monica's housing shortage.

Unfair Financial Penalties on Mom-and-Pop Homeowners. Single-family homes and condominiums are predominantly owned by individual families, seniors, and small property owners—not large corporate landlords. If a homeowner temporarily rents out their home to cover medical costs, care for an aging parent, or accommodate a job relocation, this measure would force them to pay $20,000 to over $40,000 in mandatory relocation fees just to move back into their own property. A family should never be forced to pay tens of thousands of dollars simply to reoccupy a residence they legally own.

Shrinks Santa Monica's Rental Supply. Imposing excessive financial penalties and rigid administrative barriers on property owners will inevitably drive single-family homes and condos off the rental market entirely. Rather than protecting tenants, this measure will shrink the overall pool of available rental units, increase competition, and ultimately push housing costs higher across the city.

Harms Multi-Generational Families and Aging Seniors. Many residents rely on renting out their home temporarily with plans to return for retirement, downsize, or provide housing support for children or elderly relatives. Penalizing owner move-ins with exorbitant fees and lengthy delays severely disrupts these multi-generational family plans and hurts residents trying to care for their loved ones.

Protect local homeowners, preserve housing availability, and support Santa Monica families. Vote NO on Measure VV.

JAY JOHNSON
Former Chair, Santa Monica Rent Control Bd.
