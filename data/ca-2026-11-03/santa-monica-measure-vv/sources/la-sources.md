**Measure VV has two resolutions in the historical record.** The Council first adopted Resolution No. 11785 on July 14, 2026 with broader language (it also covered new-occupant and holdover-tenant eviction rules). At a special meeting on July 28, 2026, the Council **rescinded Resolution 11785** and adopted the narrower, Article XXIII-only resolution captured here as `resolution.pdf`. Only the final (July 28) text is staged; its ballot question matches the county's final-measures list word for word (confirmed below). The superseded July 14 draft was not saved by the earlier research pass and is not in this directory.

## Ballot question and supporters/opponents (verbatim, from the county's final measures list)

> MEASURE VV: Shall Article XXIII of the City Charter related to housing policies be amended to extend existing eviction protections to tenants renting single-unit dwellings and condominiums and require that for owner occupancy evictions from non-rent controlled units, the owner must intend in good faith to occupy the unit within 60 days after the tenant vacates and for at least 2 years thereafter?
>
> Supporters: Ellis Raskin, Councilmember; Barry Snell, Councilmember; Kay Ambriz, Rent Bd. Vice Chair; Soledad Marcial
>
> Opponents: None submitted.

Vote threshold: Majority, 50%+1.

## What's present and what's missing

- **Full charter-amendment text**: present (Exhibit A, within `resolution.pdf`, final July 28 version).
- **Resolution placing the measure on the ballot**: present, final/operative version (`resolution.pdf`); plus the staff report explaining why the original July 14 version was rescinded and replaced (`staff-report.pdf`).
- **Impartial analysis**: missing — not found. `santamonica.gov`, where the City posts these, returned an Imperva/Incapsula bot-challenge page on every path tried.
- **Argument for**: four supporters are named in the county's final list, after the filing deadline, so an argument for was almost certainly filed — the text itself is missing, not found, same block.
- **Argument against**: **confirmed not filed** — the county's official list states "Opponents: None submitted."
- **Rebuttals**: not applicable to either side. California law lets each side rebut only the opposing side's filed argument; since no argument against was filed, there is no argument against for proponents to rebut, and no official opponent authors exist to file a rebuttal to the argument for.
- **Tax rate statement**: not applicable — VV is a charter amendment, not a property-tax-secured bond measure.
