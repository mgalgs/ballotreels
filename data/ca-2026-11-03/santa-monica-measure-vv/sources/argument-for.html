ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE VV

No family should be evicted from their home for arbitrary reasons or without good cause.

The Santa Monica City Charter already specifies that tenants in multi-family buildings can only be evicted for specific reasons listed in the charter. The same protections apply to tenants in rent-controlled single-family homes and condominiums.

This Charter amendment would extend those same protections to tenants in non-rent-controlled single-family homes and condominiums. In other words, it ensures that non-rent-controlled tenants have the same rights as rent-controlled tenants when their landlord serves them with an eviction notice. This measure also makes minor technical revisions to the procedures for owner move-in evictions in non-rent-controlled units to align the procedures with those for rent-controlled units.

The amendments in this measure are crucial to preserving the stability and diversity of our community. It is important for all tenants to enjoy the same rights, and this measure helps accomplish that goal.
