CITY OF SANTA MONICA – (5)
VOTE PASSAGE – Majority Vote 50%+1
VV    MEASURE VV: Shall Article XXIII of the City Charter related to housing policies be
      amended to extend existing eviction protections to tenants renting single-unit dwellings
      and condominiums and require that for owner occupancy evictions from non-rent
      controlled units, the owner must intend in good faith to occupy the unit within 60 days
      after the tenant vacates and for at least 2 years thereafter? Supporters: Ellis Raskin,
      Councilmember; Barry Snell, Councilmember; Kay Ambriz, Rent Bd. Vice Chair; Soledad
      Marcial Opponents: None submitted.
