REBUTTAL TO ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE VV

Measure VV is simple and straightforward. It will (1) ensure that renters in non-rent-controlled condominiums and single-family homes have the same eviction protections as renters in controlled condominiums and single-family homes; and (2) align owner-occupancy eviction requirements for rent-controlled and non-controlled units.

Let's fact-check some of the claims in the arguments opposing this measure:

i. The measure will not establish new price controls for condominiums or single-family homes. It just establishes that tenants can only be evicted for specified reasons.

ii. The measure does not create new "unfair financial penalties." Existing Santa Monica law already requires relocation payments to qualifying tenants in a "single-family home" or "a room in a single-family home" (see SMMC Chpt. 4.36). This measure would not change those existing requirements or add any new relocation payment requirements.

iii. The measure does not shrink Santa Monica's housing supply. On the contrary, by providing new eviction protections, it ensures that longtime Santa Monica families can stay in their homes without fear of eviction for arbitrary reasons.

iv. The measure does not harm multigenerational families and seniors. Property owners still have the right to take any rentals units off the market, but this measure provides extra safeguards that ensure the owner will reside at the unit on a long-term basis.

Please vote YES on Measure VV to ensure that all tenants have the same protections.
