REBUTTAL TO ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE VV

The proponents' own argument inadvertently proves our point: no family should be arbitrarily barred from their own home or forced to pay tens of thousands of dollars just to move into it. Yet, this is precisely what Measure VV demands.

Extreme financial penalties

While masked as "eviction protection," this proposal effectively forced local homeowners out of their properties. By claiming to expand rights for one group, it strips fundamental rights from another. Do not be misled by a "minor technical revision" label. This measure imposes extreme financial penalties on homeowners and violates the foundational promise made to all Santa Monica residents that homes and condominiums remain protected for their owners to inhabit.

Not "Minor Technical Revisions"

Labeling extensive changes to the Santa Monica City Charter as "minor technical revisions" misleads voters. These amendments create rigid regulatory hurdles and complex legal procedures, turning straightforward residential arrangements into bureaucratic minefields.

A Flawed "One-Size-Fits-All" Approach

Proponents argue that all housing types should be treated identically. But an individual condo or single-family residence is fundamentally different from a commercial multi-family apartment complex. Forcing individual residences into regulatory structure designed for large commercial landlords ignores reality and invites costly administrative disputes and legal conflict between tenants and homeowners.

Reject promises of slippery politicians seeking a few quick votes. Vote NO on Measure VV.
