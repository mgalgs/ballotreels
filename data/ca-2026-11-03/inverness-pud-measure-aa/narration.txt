Measure AA asks the voters of this utility district whether to adjust its spending limit for four fiscal years.
Since 1979, the state constitution has required the board to set the most tax money the district may spend each fiscal year.
The district's staff report says its calculated limit is substantially smaller than the tax money it is entitled to, and that keeping the difference takes a vote every four years.
The board placed Measure AA on the ballot on May 19, 2026, and it needs a majority of the vote to pass.
For fiscal years 2026-2027 through 2029-2030, the limit would include the combined total of all applicable revenue sources.
Neither the resolution nor the staff report on it includes a cost estimate for the measure.
The elections office of the county says on its page for this measure that no argument against Measure AA was filed.
Supporters say Measure AA keeps the district's full share of property taxes for fire protection, and will not raise taxes.
A yes vote adjusts the limit to include the combined total of all applicable revenue sources, for those four years.
A no vote does not adjust the limit.
