## Facts and the passages that establish them

### Ballot question

S1 PDF p. 77:

> MEASURE N
> Without increasing current tax rates, to repair and upgrade aging classrooms, science labs, school facilities, and instructional technology to support college/career readiness in math, science, technology, engineering, arts, and skilled trades and fix deteriorating roofs, plumbing, sewer, and electrical systems shall Vista Unified School District's measure be adopted authorizing $543,000,000 in bonds at legal rates, levying $58 per $100,000 assessed value ($31,000,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding, with citizen oversight, independent audits, and all money locally-controlled?
> Bonds – Yes / Bonds – No

### Vote required

S1 PDF p. 77: "This measure requires 55% of those voting on the measure."

### Who votes on it

S3, Resolution 26-25, Section 1 (PDF p. 4):

> an election shall be held within the District on November 3, 2026, for the purpose of submitting to the registered voters of the District the full ballot proposition contained in Exhibit B

S1 PDF p. 80 (full text): "By approval of this measure by at least fifty-five percent (55%) of the registered voters voting thereon, the Vista Unified School District shall be authorized to issue and sell bonds of up to $543,000,000".

In short: the registered voters of the Vista Unified School District.

### Who placed it on the ballot

S1 PDF p. 77: "The Board of Education for the Vista Unified School District ("District") placed this measure on the ballot."

S3 PDF p. 7: "PASSED AND ADOPTED this day, May 14, 2026, by the following vote: AYES: 5" (NAYS, ABSTAIN, ABSENT blank). The cover letter (PDF p. 1) lists board members Rena Marrocco, Sue Martin, Mike Markov, Martha Alvarado and Cipriano Vargas.

Recorded as found: the cover letter asks the ROV "to consolidate the bond election with the Statewide presidential general election" and a recital (PDF p. 3) says "on November 3, 2026, a statewide primary election is scheduled". Both are as printed; the election is the gubernatorial general election. The cover letter also asks the ROV "not assign the letters "D," "F," "L" or "LL" to the measure".

### Impartial analysis

S1 PDF p. 77, "COUNTY COUNSEL IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS". Key statements:

> If approved by 55% of the votes cast, this measure will authorize the District to issue and sell general obligation bonds in an amount not to exceed $543,000,000.

> The District estimates the average tax rate would be $58 for every $100,000 of assessed valuation, and the highest tax rate would be approximately $58 for every $100,000 of assessed valuation. The tax is anticipated to be collected through fiscal year 2062-2063.

> The estimated total debt service, including principal and interest, required to be repaid if the bonds are issued and sold is approximately $1,154,254,750.

### Tax rate statement

S1 PDF p. 77, "TAX RATE STATEMENT", "By: /s/ Matt Doyle, Ed.D., Superintendent":

> If the bonds are approved, the District expects to sell the bonds in multiple series as a "bond extension" to the prior bond authorization, with bond maturities structured so that tax rate in each year is not projected to increase as compared to the current tax rate.

> 1. ... is estimated to be $58 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. The final fiscal year in which the tax is anticipated to be collected is 2062-63.
> 2. ... is estimated to be $58 per $100,000 of assessed valuation first occurring in fiscal year 2028-29.
> 3. ... the total debt service ... is $1,154,254,750.

### Arguments and rebuttals

All four are printed.

- **Argument in favor**, S1 PDF p. 78. Headline: "Vote YES on Measure N for Safer, Better Local Schools, Without Raising Taxes!" Signers: Michael Fowlkes (President, Vista Unified Council of PTAs (VUCPTA)); Mark Kersey (President & CEO, San Diego Taxpayers Association); Sean Keoni Ellis (Master Instructor and Fmr. In 2018 voters approved Measure LL a 30 year VUSD bond totaling $247 million." Signers: Sandra Levee, Colette Kelley, Adrianne Silveria, James R. Silveria, Rafael Martinez.
- **Rebuttal to the argument against**, S1 PDF p. 79. First line: "Voters approved Measure LL a decade ago, and projects were done on time, on budget and as promised." Signers: Lisa De Jesus (Realtor & School Oversight Volunteer); Michael Saldaña (President, Vista Fire Fighters Association); Roberto Rodriguez (Trustee, Palomar College Governing Board); Kristin Childs (Special Educator); Vince Hinojosa (Financial Planner, Education Volunteer & Retired Marine).

Recorded as found: the argument in favor gives Mark Kersey's organization as "San Diego Taxpayers Association"; other measures' arguments print "San Diego County Taxpayers Association".
