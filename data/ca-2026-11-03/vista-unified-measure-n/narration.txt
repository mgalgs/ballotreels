Vista Unified Measure N asks the district's voters whether to approve $543 million in school bonds.
The district would borrow the money and repay it from a tax on property in the district.
Measure N needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would repair and upgrade aging classrooms, science labs and technology, and fix roofs, plumbing, sewer and electrical systems.
The district's tax statement estimates a tax of $58 per $100,000 of assessed value, collected through 2062-63.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $1.15 billion.
Opponents say Measure N repeats a 2018 bond's purpose, costing the average home $505 a year.
Supporters say Measure N fixes failing pipes, roof leaks and aging portable classrooms, without raising taxes.
A yes vote is in favor of letting the district sell the bonds.
A no vote is against letting the district sell the bonds.
