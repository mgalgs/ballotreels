Measure I asks the voters of this city whether to adopt a 1 percent sales tax for streets, sidewalks, drainage and sewers.
The City Council voted in July 2026 to place Measure I on the ballot.
The tax would be 1 percent of retail sales in the city, and would last until ended by voters.
It is a special tax: the money would go into a separate fund for streets, sidewalks, curbs, drainage and sewer mains, and for running the tax.
The city could also borrow against the tax to pay for that work.
The tax would be audited each year, and an advisory committee of three residents would review the spending.
Measure I needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The ballot question estimates that Measure I would raise about $1,864,000 a year.

A yes vote on Measure I adopts the 1 percent sales tax for streets and sidewalks.
A no vote on Measure I does not adopt the tax.
