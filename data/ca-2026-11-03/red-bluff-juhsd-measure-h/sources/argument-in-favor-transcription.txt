Argument in Favor of Red Bluff Joint Union High School District Measure H

For Healthy Students and Healthy Community, vote YES on H.

By investing in career training facilities at Red Bluff High, Yes on H prepares our students for good-paying local jobs as First Responders, keeping all our families SAFE.

Not every student wants to go to college! Over the years, Red Bluff High and CAL Fire have worked together to train dozens of firefighters who've wanted to stay LOCAL to serve OUR community.

Yes on H is a win-win: Help our students get hands-on training for good jobs as EMTs, firefighters, and nurses that keep our local seniors and families safe.

We all know we don't get our fair share of attention or money from Sacramento. But this year, we won't have to do it alone. Yes on H unlocks our fair share of MILLIONS of dollars from Sacramento—but only if voters say Yes on H. Let's finally bring home our share of the pot!

Yes on H also ensures student health and safety by:
• Replacing old air conditioning in classrooms that are over 100 degrees in summer
• Continuing to provide clean drinking water
• Improving campus safety and security

Every penny of Measure H must be spent to benefit our students at Red Bluff High and Salisbury High. NO money can be taken by out-of-town Sacramento politicians or used for administrator salaries.

Yes on H includes strict accountability provisions: annual independent audits, a review of all expenditures by a citizens' oversight committee, and public disclosure of all spending.

By voting Yes on H you can ensure money will be spent responsibly and as promised.

Join firefighters, teachers, parents, small business owners, neighbors, and a unanimous Board of Education. Maintain a Healthy, Safe Community. Help our Students and Firefighters. Yes on H!

For official Measure H information, visit: www.rbhsd.org
