## Facts with quotes

- **Ballot question (Exhibit B, p. 11):** "Red Bluff Joint Union High School District Career Preparation/ Repairs Measure. To provide career/ technical education facilities training students for local jobs as nurses, EMTs, firefighters, others; repair/ upgrade deteriorating classrooms and buildings; maintain clean drinking water; improve campus safety, shall Red Bluff Joint Union High School District's measure authorizing $19,000,000 of bonds at legal rates, raising $1,600,000 annually via levying $19 per $100,000 of assessed value while bonds are outstanding, requiring local control, citizen oversight, audits, public spending disclosure, no money for administrator salaries, be adopted?" Yes / No. "(75 words)"
- **Two counties:** resolution p. 6: "the Board of Education of the Red Bluff Joint Union High School District, of Tehama and Shasta Counties, State of California, this 16th day of July 2026"; AYES 4, NOES 0, ABSENT 1; signed Ashley Fisher, President. Tax rate statement: estimates are based on "the official tax rolls of Tehama County and Shasta County".
- **Tax rate statement (p. 12):** average "$18.73 per $100,000 (1.873¢ per $100) of assessed valuation. The final fiscal year in which the tax is anticipated to be collected is 2050-51"; highest "$18.77 per $100,000 (1.877¢ per $100) of assessed valuation which would first occur in fiscal year 2036-37"; total debt service "$37,695,817".
- **Analysis:** "the District will seek to levy, upon a 55% vote, an ad valorem tax ... approximately nineteen ($19.00) dollars per hundred thousand ($100,000.00) dollars of assessed valuation ... generating approximately one million six hundred thousand ($1,600,000.00) dollars annually while bonds are outstanding." / "No bond proceeds may be used for administrator salaries, pensions, or other school operating expenses." / "The Board shall appoint a citizens' oversight committee within sixty (60) days of the date the Board enters the election results in its minutes".
- **Full text (Exhibit A p. 1):** "the Red Bluff Joint Union High School District (the "District") shall be authorized to issue bonds in the aggregate amount of $19,000,000"; project list headings "PREPARING STUDENTS FOR LOCAL JOBS AND CAREERS" and "ENSURING SAFE AND HEALTHY CLASSROOMS"; "Red Bluff High School has partnered with CAL FIRE to help train dozens of future local firefighters" (district's text).
