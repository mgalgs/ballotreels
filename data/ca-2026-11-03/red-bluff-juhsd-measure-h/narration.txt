Measure H asks the voters of this high school district whether to approve $19 million in school bonds.
The district would borrow the money and repay it from a tax on property in the district.
Measure H needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would pay for career training facilities for local jobs such as nurses and firefighters, and for classroom, drinking water and safety repairs.
The district's tax rate statement estimates an average tax of $18.73 per $100,000 of assessed value, and at most $18.77, through 2050-2051.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $37.7 million.
No argument against Measure H appears among the filings the county elections office has posted for this election.
Supporters say Measure H would train students for local first responder jobs and bring millions in state money.
A yes vote approves selling the bonds, to be repaid by a tax on property.
A no vote does not approve selling the bonds.
