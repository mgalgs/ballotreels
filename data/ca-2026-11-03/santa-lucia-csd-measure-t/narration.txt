Measure T asks the voters of this community services district whether to approve a yearly special tax for its roads, for 15 years.
The district runs and maintains the roads within its boundaries.
The impartial analysis says a study found the road work needed, and the district lacked the funding to pay for it.
Measure T needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The board formed a financing district with the same boundaries to charge the tax.
Estate home lots, built on or not, would pay up to $2,968 in the first year, other parcels their own set rates, and all rates would rise 2 percent a year.
The tax would run for 15 years, from as early as 2027 through 2042, into a special account used only for the road work.
The impartial analysis says the tax would first raise about $999,736 a year.
The measure would also set the financing district's spending limit at $1,350,000.
