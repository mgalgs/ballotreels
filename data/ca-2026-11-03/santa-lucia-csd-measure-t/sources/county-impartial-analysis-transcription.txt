Impartial Analysis of Measure T

The Santa Lucia Community Services District (CSD) placed this measure on the ballot. The CSD is responsible for operating and maintaining a system of roads within the CSD's boundaries. The CSD engaged an outside contractor to study the condition of the CSD's roads and to identify the work that needed to be done to bring the roads to satisfactory condition and to maintain them at that condition. The CSD lacked the funding stream to pay for that road work. Pursuant to Section 61121(b) of the Government Code, the CSD is authorized to levy special taxes in the manner set forth in the Mello-Roos Community Facilities Act (Government Code section 53311 et. seq.).

In order to levy a tax, the Board of Directors on the Santa Lucia Community Services District was required to establish a Community Facilities District (a Financing District). This occurred after a public hearing on July 22, 2026. The Board of Directors of the Santa Lucia Community Services District passed Resolution No. 26-08, to establish Community Facilities District No. 2 (Road Quality).

Community Facilities District No. 2 (Road Quality) will fund the maintenance and restoration of the CSD's roads through the levy of a special tax.

The special tax will be used to maintain and operate the Santa Lucia roads (including necessary resurfacing, rehabilitation, preventative treatments, and similar work) to be levied annually for fifteen (15) consecutive years starting in as early as 2027 and ending in 2042, at an initial rate of $2,968 per year for developed and undeveloped estate residential parcels, with rates for other parcels shown in Resolution No. 26-08, with all rates subject to a 2% annual inflation adjustment, initially generating approximately $999,736. Pursuant to Article XIIIB, Section 8(h) of the California Constitution, and as set forth in Resolution 26-08, Community Facilities District No.2 (Road Quality) would have an Appropriations Limit of $1,350,000. The special tax will be collected in the same manner as ordinary ad valorem taxes. An Ad Valorem tax is paid by an annual property tax levy.

Proceeds of the special tax will be deposited in a special account and used only to fund the services identified in Section 5 of Resolution 26-08. An annual report will be filed at least once a year containing a description of the amount of funds in the Special Account and the status of the services identified in Section 5 of Resolution 26-08.

A "Yes" vote on this measure is a vote in favor of a special tax to maintain and operate the Santa Lucia's roads.

A "No" vote on this measure is a vote against a special tax to maintain and operate the Santa Lucia's roads.
