## Facts and quoted passages

- **Amount:** "The proposed measure is a $23.0 million general obligation (G.O.) bond program. This measure is intended to address the needs of the student population through modernization projects at the District's seven schools."
- **Tax rate (district estimate):** "The tax rate per property owner is estimated to be $38.70 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. The proposed measure will not increase the current annual tax rate. Instead, it will extend the tax rate that property owners are currently paying."
- **Projects listed:** "Repairing or replacing leaky roofs; Replacing outdated heating, ventilation and air-conditioning systems; Modernizing/renovating outdated classrooms, restrooms and school facilities, including science labs; Making health, safety and security improvements; Improving the pool at Exeter High School, including installing a cover structure".
- **Oversight:** "an independent citizens' oversight committee will be established to ensure that bond funds are properly spent. Also, by law, there must be annual audits and no bond money can be used for teacher or administrative salaries."
- **Measure letter T, the 55% threshold, the placement date:** from news (Sun-Gazette 2026-10-01 "Measure T goes to ballot for Exeter voters") and CalTax only. NOT confirmed by an official record.
