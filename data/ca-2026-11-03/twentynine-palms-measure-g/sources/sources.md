## Quoted facts

- **Who votes:** Twentynine Palms voters. Impartial analysis: "If approved by a majority of Twentynine Palms voters, this Measure would implement a one percent "sales tax" on applicable transactions within the City."
- **Ballot question** (RES_TP.pdf, Section 1): "Shall the measure funding services for City of Twentynine Palms residents (including those required for maintaining 911 and emergency response times, police officers and patrols; repairing streets/sidewalks; responding to floods/natural disasters; maintaining clean and safe public parks; and upgrading the animal shelter) by levying a transaction (sales) and use tax of 1%, providing approximately $2,000,000 in funds annually until ended by voters, requiring audits, public spending disclosures, and that all funds are used locally, be adopted?"
- **Impartial analysis** (VIG p. 199, City Attorney): "On July 14, 2026, the City Council voted to place Measure "G" on the November 3, 2026 general municipal election ballot to seek voter approval of an ordinance enacting a one percent transactions and use tax locally in the City of Twentynine Palms" … "For example, for every $100 spent on taxable goods, the proposed tax would be one dollar ($1.00)."
