Measure G asks this city's voters whether to add a 1 percent sales tax for city services.
Measure G needs a majority of the vote to pass.
The tax would be one dollar for every $100 spent on taxable goods in the city.
The tax would start April 1, 2027, and stay until voters repeal it.
The money could go only to local services and local priorities, checked by a yearly audit.
The city attorney's impartial analysis estimates the tax would bring the city $2,000,000 a year.
No argument against Measure G was filed, so the county voter guide prints only the argument of supporters.
Supporters say Measure G funds roads, public safety and the animal shelter, and has visitors pay their share.
A yes vote will authorize the 1 percent sales tax.
A no vote will not authorize the 1 percent sales tax.
