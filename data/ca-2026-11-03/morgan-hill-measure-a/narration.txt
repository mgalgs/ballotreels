Measure A asks the voters of this city whether the City Treasurer should be appointed instead of elected.
Voters elect the City Treasurer every four years, and the current term ends in 2028.
An elected treasurer must be at least 18, live in the city and be registered to vote, with no required education or professional background.
The City Treasurer oversees collecting and safeguarding city funds.
If Measure A passes, the city manager would appoint the treasurer based on experience and ability, starting in 2028 or at an earlier vacancy.
Measure A needs a majority to pass.
The city estimates savings of about $40,000 a year in personnel costs, and $15,000 in election costs every four years.
No argument against Measure A was filed with the county Registrar of Voters.
Supporters say treasurers need public finance expertise, and most California cities appoint theirs.
A yes vote makes the City Treasurer an appointed position.
A no vote means voters keep electing the City Treasurer.
