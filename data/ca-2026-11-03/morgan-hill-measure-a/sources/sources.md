## Ballot question (city Measures page)

> Shall the office of the City Treasurer be appointive?

From the city page, not from the printed ballot (ROV not fetchable).

## Full text (`full-text-ordinance.pdf`, p. 1)

> SECTION 1: At the expiration of the existing term of the City Treasurer of the City of Morgan Hill, the City Manager shall appoint the City Treasurer on the basis of their experience and ability. The City Treasurer shall hold office at the pleasure of the City Manager.

> SECTION 3. Effective Date; Posting. This ordinance shall take effect on the 31st day after the certification of the election results.

## Impartial analysis

"CITY ATTORNEY'S IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE A", signed "/s/ [City Attorney] Elisa Tolentino". Key statements:

> State law requires general law cities, including the City of Morgan Hill, to have a City Treasurer. The position may be elected or appointed. In Morgan Hill, voters currently elect the City Treasurer every four years. The current City Treasurer's term ends in 2028.

> If this measure is approved, the City Treasurer would become an appointed position upon expiration of the current Treasurer's term in 2028, or earlier if a vacancy occurs. If the measure is approved, the City Manager would appoint the City Treasurer, based on their experience and ability.

> There are no minimum educational or professional qualifications for the elected position.

> If the measure is approved, the city estimates savings of approximately $40,000 annually in personnel costs and approximately $15,000 in election costs every four years.

> A "yes" vote on Measure A will make the City Treasurer position appointive. A "no" vote on Measure A means the City Treasurer will continue to be elected by the voters.
