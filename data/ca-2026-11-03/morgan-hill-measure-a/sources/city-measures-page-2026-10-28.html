Measures

The City of Morgan Hill Has One Measure on the November 3, 2026, Ballot

 

Measure A - Appointed City Treasurer

Shall the office of the City Treasurer be appointive?

About Measure A

Morgan Hill voters will decide whether the City Treasurer should remain an elected position or become an appointed position.

What does the City Treasurer do?

The City Treasurer oversees the City’s treasury functions. Most day-to-day responsibilities are currently handled by the Finance Director and Finance Department. These responsibilities include:

  • Collecting and safeguarding City funds
  • Managing banking, investments, accounting, debt, and financial reporting
  • Maintaining financial accountability through professional staff, independent audits, and internal controls

Why is this measure on the ballot?

Under the current system, a candidate for City Treasurer must be at least 18 years old, a Morgan Hill resident, and a registered voter in the City. No degree, financial education, professional training, or experience in finance or accounting is required.

Municipal finance has become increasingly specialized and technical. Most California cities, including all other cities in Santa Clara County, use an appointed treasurer.

What happens if Measure A passes?

  • The City Treasurer would become an appointed position.
  • The Finance Director would serve as City Treasurer.
  • The change would take effect after the current Treasurer’s term ends in 2028, or sooner if the position becomes vacant.
  • The City estimates savings of approximately $15,000 in election costs every four years and approximately $40,000 annually in personnel costs.

What happens if Measure A does not pass?

  • The City Treasurer would remain an elected position.
  • Morgan Hill voters would continue electing the Treasurer every four years.
  • The current qualifications and structure would remain unchanged.
