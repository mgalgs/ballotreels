Argument in Favor of Measure A

A YES vote on Measure A will modernize the process by which Morgan Hill selects its City Treasurer by ensuring the position is filled based on professional qualifications, experience, and expertise.

When Morgan Hill was incorporated in 1906, electing a City Treasurer was common practice. Today, municipal finance has become highly specialized and technical. The City Treasurer oversees important treasury functions that help safeguard public funds and support the City's long-term financial health. These responsibilities require expertise in public finance, investments, banking, accounting, debt management, and compliance with complex state laws.

Measure A authorizes the City Manager to appoint a qualified professional for this important office.

Most California cities, including every city in Santa Clara County, already appoint their City Treasurer. Measure A aligns Morgan Hill with this proven approach.

Professional financial oversight will continue through experienced finance staff, independent audits, strong internal controls, regular financial reporting, and City Council oversight.

The measure will also reduce ongoing personnel and election costs, allowing taxpayer dollars to be used more efficiently while maintaining the high standards residents expect.

A YES vote is a vote for qualified leadership, responsible stewardship of public funds, and efficient local government.

Vote YES on Measure A.
