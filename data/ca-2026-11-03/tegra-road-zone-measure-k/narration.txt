Measure K asks the voters of this road zone whether to replace its $260 yearly road assessment with a $520 tax per parcel.
The county formed the zone in 1985 to pay for road improvements and maintenance.
The county counts 62 registered voters living in the zone.
The Board of Supervisors placed Measure K on the ballot at the request of the zone's advisory committee.
Measure K needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The new tax would apply to every parcel, built on or not, starting in the 2027-2028 fiscal year.
The tax could rise each year with a construction cost index, by no more than 3 percent a year.
The money could pay only for the roads in the zone, and the tax would last until voters change or repeal it.
If Measure K fails, the $260 assessment stays.
The county attorney's impartial analysis expects the tax to raise about $11,960 a year.
No argument for Measure K was submitted.
No argument against Measure K was submitted.
A yes vote authorizes the tax of $520 per parcel a year.
A no vote is against the tax, and the $260 assessment stays.
