# Shasta County Elections documents (retrieved 2026-10-29)

Publisher: Shasta County Elections. The county's own site blocks plain GETs; this
page is from the official Voter Information Guide fetched via a browser session
(`https://elections.shastacounty.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Shasta-VIG-BT17.pdf`).
Shasta publishes 66 near-identical per-ballot-type guides; Measure G (Shasta Fire
Protection District, countywide but district-specific) appears in only a few of
them (the ones covering the district's precincts) — BT17, BT20 and BT23 are
confirmed; this dir is cut from BT17 only.
