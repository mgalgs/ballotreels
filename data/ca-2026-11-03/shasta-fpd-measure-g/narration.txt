Measure G asks the voters of this fire protection district whether to enlarge its board of trustees from three members to five.
Today, the district has a three-person board of trustees.
The district's board of trustees placed the measure on the ballot.
Under state law, a fire district can change the size of its board if a majority of voters approve, so Measure G needs a majority to pass.
Measure G would add two seats to the board, filled by election, each for a four-year term.
The ballot question says the change is to provide better representation for the district.
The county voter guide does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
No argument for Measure G appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
No argument against Measure G appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
A yes vote means the board of trustees would be increased to five members.
A no vote means there would be no change to the number of members on the board.
