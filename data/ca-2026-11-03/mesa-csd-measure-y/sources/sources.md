## Quoted facts

- Ballot question: "MESA COMMUNITY SERVICES DISTRICT SPECIAL PARCEL TAX FOR FIRE PROTECTION SERVICES. Shall the Mesa Community Services District adopt an ordinance establishing annual special parcel tax of up to $500 per developed parcel and up to $250 per undeveloped parcel, with up to a 3.5% annual escalator, raising approximately $64,500 in year one, to fund an Out of Area Service Agreement with the Bishop Fire Protection District providing fire protection and related services, with all funds used locally for these purposes only, continuing until ended by voters, and subject to annual review?"
- Analysis, purpose: "This measure would adopt an ordinance establishing a special parcel tax within the Mesa Community Services District (the “District”) to fund fire protection and related safety services." "revenues may only be used to fund a service agreement with Bishop Fire Protection District or other fire protection service provider for fire suppression and related services."
- Analysis, term and rate: "The tax would begin July 1, 2027 and remain in effect until June 30, 2037 or until such a time that the out-of-area service agreement is no longer in effect, whichever occurs first. The rate would be up to $500 per developed parcel and up to $250 per undeveloped parcel. This measure also allows the per-parcel rate to include up to a 3.5% annual tax increase if such an escalator is included in the service agreement."
- Vote needed: "This measure would take effect only if approved by at least two-thirds (2/3) of the voters voting on Measure Y."
- Full text, rate tie: "The actual per parcel tax rate imposed shall be tied to the negotiated service agreement with the Bishop Fire Protection District, which shall be executed prior to any Tax being imposed."
- **Inconsistency in the official record:** the ballot question says "continuing until ended by voters", while the full text (§4) says the tax "shall remain in effect for a period of ten years, or until such a time that the out-of-area service agreement is no longer in effect" and the analysis says it ends "June 30, 2037" at the latest. Report both; do not resolve it.
