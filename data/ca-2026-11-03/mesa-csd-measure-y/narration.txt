Measure Y asks the voters of this community services district whether to approve a yearly parcel tax for fire protection.
The money could pay only for a fire protection service agreement with another fire protection provider, and the costs of running the tax.
Measure Y needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
Each developed parcel would pay up to $500 a year, and each undeveloped parcel up to $250.
The rate could rise up to 3.5 percent a year, if the service agreement includes that increase.
The ordinance and the impartial analysis say the tax would begin July 1, 2027, and end by June 30, 2037, or sooner if the service agreement ends.
The ballot question itself describes the tax as continuing until ended by voters.
The ballot question estimates the tax would raise about $64,500 in its first year.
No argument for Measure Y was submitted, the county voter guide says.
No argument against Measure Y was submitted, the county voter guide says.
A yes vote is a vote to approve a special tax on property in the district for fire protection.
A no vote is a vote against a special tax on property in the district for fire protection.
