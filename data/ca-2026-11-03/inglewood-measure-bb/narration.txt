Measure BB asks the voters of this city whether to end the authority of the city to allow advertising on the public right-of-way.
Measure BB is a citizens' initiative, on the ballot after its petition was certified.
Measure BB would amend the General Plan and the municipal code, ending the power to allow advertising on the public right-of-way and on certain available properties.
Measure BB needs a majority yes vote to pass.
The ballot question says the measure would result in a loss of general fund revenues, but gives no amount.
Supporters say residents, not one private company, should control advertising on public streets, and Measure BB limits billboard clutter, except for major events.
The official county list of measures, which names who filed each argument, says no argument in opposition to Measure BB was submitted.
A yes vote approves the ordinance to eliminate advertising on the public right-of-way.
A no vote rejects the ordinance to eliminate advertising on the public right-of-way.
