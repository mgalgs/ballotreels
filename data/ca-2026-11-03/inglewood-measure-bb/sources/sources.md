Sources gathered 2026-10-30.

county-measure-list-excerpt.txt: the City of Inglewood lines of the Los Angeles County
Registrar-Recorder/County Clerk's "Measures Appearing on the Ballot", November 3, 2026,
https://content.lavote.gov/docs/rrcc/documents/final-measures-appearing-on-the-ballot-9-22-2026-v-2.pdf (text extracted from the PDF). It prints the vote needed (majority), the ballot
question, and who filed arguments ("Supporters", "Opponents", or "None submitted").

argument-for.html: the argument the county's Interactive Sample Ballot prints for this measure,
https://isb.lavote.gov/parsed/4348/contests/bmc10914.json (English HTML saved; header names
the source). It ends with the county's statement that no argument on the other side was
submitted.

city-draft-ordinance.pdf: City of Inglewood, City Clerk, "Measure - BB",
https://www.cityofinglewood.org/DocumentCenter/View/22201. An unsigned, undated draft
ordinance by which the City Council could adopt the citizens' initiative petition without
alteration (Elections Code section 9215(a)); it names the initiative and the petition's
certification. Its Exhibit A (the initiative's text) is not attached.

Not found: the initiative's full text, an impartial analysis, the resolution placing the
