ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE BB

VOTE YES ON MEASURE BB — PROTECT INGLEWOOD'S PUBLIC SPACES

Inglewood's streets, sidewalks and medians belong to the people of Inglewood — not an out-of-town digital billboard company.

Yet the Mayor approved a 40-year backroom agreement with the billboard company without competitive bidding or a public hearing, giving one private company MONOPOLY control over advertising on our public streets, sidewalks and medians.

Inglewood is one of the most valuable entertainment markets in the country. Despite sacrificing our public spaces and removing millions of dollars of trees and landscaping for unsightly steel poles, a government budget expert concluded billboard revenues would add barely 1% to City revenues. Cities like Santa Monica and West Hollywood negotiated better deals — fewer billboards, stronger protections and more money for their city services.

This is a bad deal for Inglewood.

Even more troubling, just days before the agreement was approved, the billboard company's affiliate contributed $25,000 to the Mayor's political campaign.

Inglewood is not for sale.

YES on Measure BB puts residents back in control.

Measure BB prohibits even more commercial billboard advertising in the public right-of-way. Existing billboards can carry public service advertising and messages. It protects our neighborhoods from being flooded with massive Vegas-style digital advertising and reduces billboard clutter, light pollution and visual distractions.

Measure BB recognizes the importance of major events like the Super Bowl and Olympics. When necessary to compete for a specific national or international event, the City may temporarily allow commercial advertising in the public right-of-way as part of the bid — protecting our neighborhoods without costing Inglewood world-class events.

Please join the nearly 13,000 Inglewood residents who signed this measure to stop billboard blight, Neighbors for a Beautiful Inglewood, Pastor K.W. Tulloss, President of the Baptist Ministers Conference, Senator Steve Bradford, small business owners and local employers including the Kia Forum, SoFi Stadium and the Intuit Dome and vote YES on Measure BB.
