Santa Ana Measure N asks city voters whether to keep the 1.5 cent sales tax with no end date.
In 2018, Santa Ana voters approved a 1.5 cent sales tax on most store and restaurant purchases.
Under that law, the tax drops to 1 cent in 2029 and ends in 2039.
Measure N needs a majority of the vote to pass.
Measure N would keep the tax at 1.5 cents until voters end it.
The city attorney's impartial analysis says the tax brings in about $84 million a year for city services.
No argument against Measure N was submitted to be printed in the official Orange County Voter Information Guide.
Supporters say Measure N keeps the rate voters approved in 2018, and enough first responders for 911 calls.
A yes vote continues the 1.5 cent tax without a rate reduction or an end date.
A no vote keeps the scheduled 2029 rate reduction and the 2039 end of the tax.
