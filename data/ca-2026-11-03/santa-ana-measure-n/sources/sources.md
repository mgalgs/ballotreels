## Quoted passages

- Question (p.1): "To maintain city services, such as fixing potholes; keeping streets, sidewalks, parks, and playgrounds safe and clean; removing graffiti and litter; providing fire protection, paramedic, crime prevention, and 911 emergency response, shall City of Santa Ana’s ordinance be amended to maintain the 1.5 cent sales tax without sunset providing approximately $84,000,000 annually for services until ended by voters, with independent audits, resident oversight, and all money locally controlled?"
- Who votes / threshold (p.3): "Measure N requires a majority of the Santa Ana voters that vote in the election of November 3, 2026 to vote in favor of the measure to become law."
- Who placed it (p.3): "Measure N titled the “Santa Ana Streets, Public Safety, Essential City Services Measure” was placed on the ballot by a vote of the Santa Ana City Council."
- Analysis (p.3): "In 2018, Santa Ana’s voters enacted an ordinance creating a 20-year local transactions and use tax in at a rate of one and one half (1.5) cents, until March 31, 2029 with a reduction to one (1) cent until March 31, 2039, to fund general city services."
- Analysis (p.3): "If the measure is approved, the local transactions and use tax would remain at 1.5 cents without an end date."
- Analysis (p.3): "Measure N does not amend the existing requirement for a citizen oversight committee, an annual audit by an independent certified public accountant, or an annual disclosure by the City of expenditures made from the transactions and use tax revenue."
- Argument against (p.1): "No argument against this measure was submitted."

Note: the impartial analysis is signed "Santa Ana City Attorney" with no name in the extracted text.
