## Quoted passages

- Question (p.22): "Shall the measure with no tax on residents, funding public services including maintaining 911 emergency response, public safety; repairing streets/potholes, improving traffic/safety; retaining small businesses, by establishing a business license tax of up to 10¢ per square foot of industrial/ warehouse/ manufacturing businesses with large truck activity, 3% annual increase limit, providing approximately $2,500,000 annually until ended by voters, requiring spending disclosure, all funds used locally, be adopted?"
- Who placed it (p.47): "The City Council placed Measure C on the ballot. Subject to voter approval, Measure C adds Chapter 3.42 to the Perris Municipal Code establishing a business license tax on “distribution facilities,” “industrial businesses,” and “truck yards,” as defined."
- Analysis (p.47): "The initial voter-authorized maximum tax rate is 10 cents per square foot of “taxable area” per fiscal year. The City Council may set the “operative rate” (i.e., the rate that will actually be charged) anywhere up to that maximum. If no initial operative rate is set, it defaults to 10 cents per square foot."
- Analysis (p.47): "“E-commerce facilities,” meaning facilities or areas principally used to fulfill online orders for direct consumer delivery, are exempt."
