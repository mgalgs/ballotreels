              IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE “C”
The City Council placed Measure C on the ballot. Subject to voter
approval, Measure C adds Chapter 3.42 to the Perris Municipal Code
establishing a business license tax on “distribution facilities,” “industrial
businesses,” and “truck yards,” as defined.
The initial voter-authorized maximum tax rate is 10 cents per square
foot of “taxable area” per fiscal year. The City Council may set the
“operative rate” (i.e., the rate that will actually be charged) anywhere up
to that maximum. If no initial operative rate is set, it defaults to 10 cents
per square foot.
The ordinance generally defines:
●        A “distribution facility” as premises principally used to receive,
         store, consolidate, handle, stage, transfer, or distribute goods
         for eventual delivery elsewhere.
●        An “industrial business” as primarily indoor or outdoor
         manufacturing with regular “large-truck” activity; a “large truck”
         exceeds five tons gross weight.
●        A “truck yard” as premises principally used to park, store, stage,
         or dispatch commercial trucks, trailers, chassis, or intermodal
         containers. Incidental commercial-vehicle parking subordinate
         to another business is excluded.
The tax equals the operative rate multiplied by “taxable area,” meaning
the combined gross floor and outdoor business area principally used
in operating the business. Outdoor business area includes storage,
staging, loading, handling, and truck parking areas. Circulation and fire
lanes, stormwater facilities, and queuing, passenger vehicle parking,
landscaping, and vacant or unused areas are excluded.
“E-commerce facilities,” meaning facilities or areas principally used to
fulfill online orders for direct consumer delivery, are exempt. At mixed-
use facilities, only separately identifiable e-commerce areas are exempt.
Businesses with 50,000 square feet or less of combined gross floor and
outdoor business area are also exempt.
Beginning July 1, 2027, the voter-authorized maximum rate and operative
rate automatically increase annually by the lesser of 3% or the annual
increase in the Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario Consumer Price
Index (CPI). If the CPI does not increase, neither rate increases. Except
for these automatic annual increases, any increase or reduction of the
operative rate requires a four-fifths vote of the entire City Council.
Measure C is a general tax. Proceeds are available to be spent for any
lawful municipal purpose. The City estimates $2,523,877 in annual
revenue. Annual reporting and audit review are required.
The City Council may amend the ordinance but may not set the operative
rate higher than the voter-authorized maximum rate or increase the
voter-authorized maximum rate. Only voters may repeal the tax, which
has no expiration date.
A “YES” vote on Measure C is a vote in favor of the general business
license tax on “distribution facilities,” “industrial businesses,” and “truck
yards.”
A “NO” vote on Measure C is a vote against the general business license
tax on “distribution facilities,” “industrial businesses,” and “truck yards.”
The above statement is an impartial analysis of Measure C. If you desire
a copy of Measure C, please call the elections official’s office at (951)
943-6100 and a copy will be mailed at no cost to you.
