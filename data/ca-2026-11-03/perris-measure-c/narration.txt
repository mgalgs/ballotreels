Measure C asks this city's voters whether to tax large warehouse, industrial and truckyard businesses by their size in square feet.
The City Council placed Measure C on the ballot.
Each year, a business would pay up to 10 cents per square foot of the area it uses, at a rate the City Council sets.
The rate would rise each year with inflation, by no more than 3 percent.
Online-order warehouses, and businesses of 50,000 square feet or less, would not pay.
The money could go to any lawful city purpose, and only voters could repeal the tax.
The City Attorney's impartial analysis says the city estimates about $2.5 million a year in revenue.
Supporters say heavy trucks damage local streets, and Measure C would help pay for repairs without raising taxes on residents.
Opponents say the money is not restricted to road repairs, and the cost would mean higher prices and fewer jobs.
A yes vote is a vote in favor of the new business license tax.
A no vote is a vote against the new business license tax.
