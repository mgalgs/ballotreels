Argument in Favor of Measure _

This ballot measure updates the Half Moon Bay downtown map associated with Measure D, which prioritizes housing development near the Main Street core. The current map, created decades ago, is outdated: it includes undeveloped farmland to the east and north that falls outside any reasonable assessment of the downtown area, yet excludes adjacent lands nearly surrounded by the current downtown area. The proposed map corrects these discrepancies, and adds and subtracts other areas to align this map more closely with the Local Coastal Land Use Plan, ensuring that Measure D ordinances shaped by this map reflect the City's long-term planning goals.
