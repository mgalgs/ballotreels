## Key facts, quoted

- **Ballot question:** "To address the decline in Federal funding for Special Education and to expand early intervention programs to every school, shall Sacramento City Unified School District adopt a $95 per year Special Education tax on each parcel of property until repealed or replaced by voters, raising $10 million annually, with exemptions for seniors, including Social Security recipients, and recipients of Supplemental Security and Social Security Disability and annual inflation adjustments, providing funds exclusively for Special Education and intervention programs, be adopted?"
- **Threshold:** "2/3 Vote Required"; analysis: "Passage of Measure M requires approval by two-thirds of the voters voting thereon."
- **Analysis** ("Prepared by County Counsel"): "Measure M, if approved by the voters, would impose a special tax in the amount of $95 per year on each parcel of land within the boundaries of the Sacramento City Unified School District. The tax would be in effect beginning July 1, 2027, and will continue until amended or repealed by the voters. Beginning the second fiscal year after approval, the tax rate will be increased annually in proportion to the increase in the Consumer Price Index (CPI), however, the increase shall not exceed three percent in any fiscal year."
- **Exemptions (analysis):** "an exemption from the proposed tax will be granted on any parcel owned and occupied by persons who are 65 years of age or older, or persons receiving Supplemental Security Income for a disability regardless of age, or persons receiving Social Security Disability Insurance benefits, regardless of age, whose yearly income does not exceed 250 percent of the 2012 federal poverty guidelines". Full text (§ C): the exemption is for "any parcel used solely for owner-occupied, single-family purposes".
- **Accountability (analysis):** "(1) depositing the proceeds into a fund that is separate and apart from other District funds …; and (2) providing an annual written report to the Board stating the amount of funds collected and expended".
- **Collection:** "The special tax will be collected by the Sacramento County Tax Collector at the same time as, and along with, the ad valorem property tax".
- **Yes/No:** "A "YES" vote on Measure M means you wish to enact the special tax." / "A "NO" vote on Measure M means you do not wish to enact the special tax."
- **Full text** names it "a "qualified special tax" under Government Code section 50079"; CPI is "San Francisco Bay Area, All Items".
- **Argument in favor and rebuttal to argument against signers:** Cancy McArn, Superintendent, Sacramento City Unified School District; Nicky Davis Milevsky, President, Sacramento City Teachers Association; Barry Broome, Business Leader; Jim Keddy, Child Advocate; Carl Pinkston, President, Black Parallel School Board.
- **Argument against signers:** Judith Farina, Retired Educator; Loretta Hom, Retired Teacher; Eva S. Garcia, Past School Board Member.
- **Rebuttal to argument in favor signers:** Alma De Santiago, Kellie Swayne, Alana Miyake, Cam Villa (each "Parent").
- Claims in the arguments (e.g. FCMAT "HIGH risk of insolvency", "$221.8 million budget gap", federal share "about 13%") are the authors' statements; not verified against the cited official records.
