## Quoted passages

- Question (p.23): "Without increasing current tax rates, to repair/ acquire/ upgrade/ construct classrooms, career technical education / school sites and facilities to support college/career readiness in math, science, technology, engineering, and skilled trades; remove hazardous materials where needed; and improve school safety/security systems, shall Perris Union High School District's measure be adopted authorizing $187,000,000 in bonds at legal rates, levying $20 per $100,000 assessed value (averaging $12 million annually) while bonds are outstanding, with citizen oversight, audits and independent audits?"
- Tax rate (p.45, Tax Rate Statement): average rate "is $18.50 per $100,000 of assessed valuation of all property to be taxed. It is estimated that the final fiscal year in which the tax is anticipated to be collected is fiscal year 2056-2057."; highest rate "is $20.00 per $100,000 of assessed valuation of all property to be taxed for fiscal years 2030-2031 through and including 2056-2057."; total debt service "is approximately $356,671,141."
- Who placed it (p.46): "The Board of Trustees of the District, by adopting Resolution No. 04:26-27 (“Resolution”), elected to call an election to place this Measure on the ballot."
- Analysis (p.46): "The purpose of Measure AA is to enable voters to authorize or reject the Perris Union High School District (hereinafter, “the District”) issuing and selling General Obligations Bonds to provide financing for school facilities projects."
