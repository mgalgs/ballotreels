Measure AA asks this high school district's voters whether to approve $187 million in school bonds.
The district would borrow the money and repay it from a tax on property in the district.
Measure AA needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would upgrade classrooms and career training facilities, remove hazardous materials, and improve school safety and security systems.
The district's tax statement estimates an average tax of $18.50 per $100,000 of assessed value, and at most $20, through 2056-2057.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $357 million.
Supporters say Measure AA repairs aging classrooms and leaky roofs, without raising tax rates.
No argument against Measure AA was filed for this year's County Voter Information Guide.
A yes vote authorizes the district to issue and sell the bonds.
A no vote rejects the district issuing and selling the bonds.
