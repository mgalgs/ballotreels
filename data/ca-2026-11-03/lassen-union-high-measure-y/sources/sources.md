## Facts with quotes

- **Ballot question (guide p. 34, "Abbreviated Form"):** "To improve local schools; modernize, and renovate old classrooms, restrooms and school facilities; repair HVAC systems; make safety and security improvements; and build career technical, agriculture/ FFA, and science classrooms and labs; shall Lassen Union High School District's measure be adopted authorizing $17,500,000 of bonds at legal rates, generating approximately $1,083,000 annually while bonds are outstanding at average rates of $29.55 per $100,000 assessed value, with annual audits, citizens' oversight, no money for salaries and all money staying local?"
- **Analysis (p. 28):** "Proposal by the Lassen Union High School District (the "District") to issue up to $17,500,000 in general obligation bonds provided the measure passes with at least 55% voter approval. The District estimates an average levy of $29.55 per $100,000 assessed value."
- **Totals:** "The District estimates that the total amount repayable during the life of the bond, including principal and interest, will be approximately $36,816,735. The highest estimated tax rate is $30 per $100,000 of assessed value estimated to be levied fiscal years 2027-28 through 2058-59. No funds would be used for teacher salaries, administrator salaries, or operating expenses."
- **Board action:** "on June 9, 2026, by Resolution No. 2526.0609.2, the District's Board of Trustees adopted a resolution authorizing and requesting consolidation".
- **Caveat:** "Approval of this measure does not guarantee that all proposed projects to be improved with bond proceeds will be funded beyond the local revenues generated by the measure. The District's proposal for the projects assumes the receipt of matching state funds".
