Barstow Community College District Measure C asks the district's voters whether to approve $37 million in college bonds.
The district would borrow the money and repay it from a tax on property in the district.
Measure C needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would go to listed projects, such as skilled trades classrooms, facilities for veterans starting civilian careers, and roof and air conditioning repairs.
The district's tax statement estimates a tax of $25 per $100,000 of assessed value, on average and at most, through 2056-2057.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $73.7 million.
Supporters say Measure C repairs aging buildings and keeps college affordable close to home.
No argument against Measure C was filed for the San Bernardino County voter guide.
A yes vote is in favor of letting the district issue the bonds.
A no vote is against letting the district issue the bonds.
