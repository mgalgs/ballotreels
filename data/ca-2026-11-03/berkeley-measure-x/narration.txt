City of Berkeley Measure X asks voters in the city whether to change the rent control and eviction ordinance.
The City Council placed Measure X on the ballot, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
Some exemptions from rent control, such as for certain accessory units and owner-occupied duplexes, would apply only if the unit was exempt when the current tenancy began.
Rent increases would be capped at 10 percent a year.
The Rent Board could waive or reduce fees for subsidized units run by nonprofit groups.
The Alameda County guide does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Opponents say Measure X adds costs, complexity and legal risk, and would make rental housing too risky for small property owners.
Supporters say Measure X caps yearly rent increases at 10 percent, like state law, and keeps tenants from losing protections arbitrarily.
A yes vote amends the rent ordinance with these changes.
A no vote leaves the rent ordinance as it is.
