CITY OF LARKSPUR
RESOLUTION 46/26

SECTION 2. ELECTION CALLED AND BALLOT QUESTION. There is hereby called and ordered to be held in the City of Larkspur of California on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, a General Municipal Election at which election the issue presented to the voters of the City of Larkspur in the form of the following question shall be:

To maintain rapid 9-1-1 emergency response times and preserve the number of on-duty paramedics ready to respond to accidents/medical emergencies, shall the Ross Valley Paramedic Authority measure be adopted continuing for four years the paramedic services special tax of up to $107.50 / residential living unit or 1,000 square feet of developed nonresidential property, adjusted up to $4.00/ year, generating approximately $834,000 annually, with annual audits, public spending disclosure and all funds for local paramedic services?
