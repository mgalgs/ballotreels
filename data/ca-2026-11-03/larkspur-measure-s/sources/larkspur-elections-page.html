How to Run for Office:

The nomination period to run for City Council opens on July 13. To be eligible, a candidate must be a registered voter living within Larkspur city limits. To run for office, candidates should:

  1. Request Nomination Papers. Nomination papers are available by appointment from the Larkspur City Clerk. Please contact cityclerk@cityoflarkspur.org or call (415) 927-5002 to request an appointment and complete a candidate application.
  2. Fill Out Nomination Papers. In order to qualify, candidates must gather signatures of not less than twenty nor more than thirty registered voters in the City of Larkspur on a nomination petition received from the City Clerk. Other required and optional paperwork will be provided and reviewed at your initial appointment with the City Clerk.
  3. File your Nomination Papers. Nomination papers must be filed by 5:00 p.m. on Friday, August 7. The nomination period will be extended to August 12 if at least one incumbent has not filed.  Please contact cityclerk@cityoflarkspur.org or call (415) 927-5002 to request a filing appointment.

Any questions about running for City Council may be directed to the City Clerk at cityclerk@cityoflarkspur.org or (415) 927-5002.

Campaign Disclosure Requirements and Filings

California campaign finance disclosure law requires local elected officeholders, candidates for local elected offices, and committees in support or opposed to local candidates to file their campaign disclosure reports with the City Clerk at specified intervals.

Beginning January 1, 2021, AB2151 requires campaign statements and reports to be posted online within 72 hours of each applicable filing deadline. If the final day of the 72-hour period is a Saturday, Sunday, or holiday, the period is extended to the next day that is not a Saturday, Sunday, or holiday.

Statements of Economic Interest

The elected officials identified in Government Code Section 87200 who file statements of economic interest with the City of Larkspur City Clerk are the five members of the City Council. Copies of these statements may be obtained by visiting the office of the Fair Political Practices Commission, 1102 Q Street, Suite 3000, Sacramento, CA 95811, or the office of the City Clerk at 400 Magnolia Avenue, Larkspur, CA 94939. 

Statements of economic interest for some state and local government agency officers may be available in an electronic format at the Commission's Internet Web site.

Policy for Tickets and Passes

The Form 802 is used by state and local government agencies to disclose detailed information about the distribution of tickets and passes, including the identity of persons who receive the tickets and passes and the public purpose of each ticket distribution. The Form  802 is also used to disclose information about ceremonial role events.
