## Facts and quotes

**Ballot question** (§2): "To maintain rapid 9-1-1 emergency response times and preserve the number of on-duty paramedics ready to respond to accidents/medical emergencies, shall the Ross Valley Paramedic Authority measure be adopted continuing for four years the paramedic services special tax of up to $107.50 / residential living unit or 1,000 square feet of developed nonresidential property, adjusted up to $4.00/ year, generating approximately $834,000 annually, with annual audits, public spending disclosure and all funds for local paramedic services?"

**Who votes** (§2): "the issue presented to the voters of the City of Larkspur".

**Vote needed** (recitals): a special tax "approved by a two-thirds vote of the voters voting in an election on the issue".

**Background** (recitals): "the Ross Valley Paramedic Authority ("RVPA") is an eight member California Joint Powers Authority formed by agreement in 1982"; "the voters of the City of Larkspur have consistently voted in favor of the RVPA special tax for paramedic services and last approved such a tax in 2022".

**Arguments** (§5): "an argument in favor of the ballot measure shall be submitted on behalf of the City of Larkspur. The deadline to file arguments for or against the measure is August 17, 2026." Rebuttals due August 24, 2026 (§6).
