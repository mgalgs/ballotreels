Measure S asks the voters of this city whether to continue a parcel tax for paramedic services for four more years.
The tax pays for paramedic care from the regional paramedic authority, which eight local governments formed in 1982.
This year the tax is $103.50 per housing unit, and the current tax ends in June 2027.
Measure S would allow up to $107.50 per housing unit or 1,000 square feet of other buildings, rising up to $4 a year.
Each year the city confirms the rate, and it may keep the tax below the most allowed.
The ballot question says all of the money would go to local paramedic services, with yearly audits.
Measure S needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The ballot question estimates the tax would raise about $834,000 a year, about 22 percent of the roughly $3.8 million yearly budget of the paramedic authority.

No argument for Measure S appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
A yes vote continues the paramedic tax for four more years.
A no vote does not continue the paramedic tax.
