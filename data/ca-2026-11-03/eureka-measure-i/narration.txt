Measure I asks the voters of this city whether to raise the hotel tax from 10 percent to 12 percent.
Today, guests staying 30 days or less at hotels, motels, campgrounds and short-term rentals pay a 10 percent tax on top of the rent.
Measure I would add 2 percent more, starting January 1, 2027, until voters change or repeal it.
As a general tax, all of the money would go into the city general fund.
Measure I needs a simple majority to pass.
The city attorney's impartial analysis estimates that Measure I would bring in about $700,000 a year.
The county voter guide prints only the argument in favor of Measure I, and none against it.
Supporters say visitors, not residents, pay the tax, and help pay for the city services they use.
A yes vote raises the hotel tax by 2 percent, starting January 1, 2027.
A no vote is a vote against the 2 percent increase.
