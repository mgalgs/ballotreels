## Facts with quotes

- **Ballot question (Resolution 2026-42 §1):** "To maintain police, fire, 911 emergency medical response, street repair and road safety, parks and recreation, community health and mental health services, local business and economic development, and other essential services impacted by tourism, shall an ordinance increasing the City of Eureka's existing hotel tax by 2%, paid only by guests of hotels and short-term vacation rentals, be adopted until ended by voters, providing approximately $700,000 annually, with independent financial audits and oversight by the City's Finance Advisory Committee?"
- **Rate (guide p. 22):** "this ballot measure would increase Eureka's transient occupancy tax (TOT) by two percent (2%) of rent charged for stays of less than 30 days, up from ten percent (10%) currently, to a total of twelve percent (12%)."
- **Start:** "The additional 2% tax would take effect on January 1, 2027."
- **General tax / vote:** "The TOT, including the proposed 2% increase, is a general-purpose tax, meaning that all revenue from this tax will be deposited into the City's General Fund." / "Because this is a "general purpose tax," it requires a simple-majority vote to pass."
- **Duration:** "would remain in effect until amended or terminated by a majority of Eureka voters."
- **Revenue:** "The tax imposed by Measure I is estimated to generate approximately $700,000 per year, with independent auditing required by State Law and oversight by the City's Financial Advisory Committee."
- **Yes/No:** "A "yes" vote is a vote to enact a 2% increase in the City's Transient Occupancy Tax to take effect January 1, 2027. A "no" vote is a vote against the enactment of a 2% increase in the City's Transient Occupancy Tax."
- **Argument in favor (guide p. 23)** opens: "A YES vote on Measure I is a vote to support the quality of life in Eureka" and states "Measure I increases the City's Transient Occupancy Tax (hotel tax) from 10% to 12%, bringing Eureka in line with" (proponents' text — see PDF for full argument and signers).
