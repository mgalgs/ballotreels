City of Santee Measure Y asks voters in the city whether to add a one-cent sales tax for 10 years.
The City Council placed Measure Y on the ballot, and the guide says it needs a simple majority to pass.
The 1 percent tax would start in April 2027 and end in March 2037.
The money would go into the general fund of the city, for any lawful city program or service.
A five-member citizens' committee appointed by the City Council would review the spending each year.
The City Attorney's impartial analysis estimates the tax would raise about $14 million a year.
Supporters say Santee runs a lean budget, and Measure Y would repair what is failing now, before it becomes an emergency.
Opponents say families would pay more every time they shop or buy gas, and the money could be spent on anything.
A yes vote will authorize the 1 percent sales tax for 10 years.
A no vote will not authorize the 1 percent sales tax.
