City of Pleasanton Measure HH asks voters in the city whether to raise the hotel tax from 8 percent to 12 percent.
Today, guests staying 30 days or less at Pleasanton hotels, motels and other lodging pay an 8 percent tax.
The City Council placed Measure HH on the ballot, and it needs a majority to pass.
The rate would rise to 10 percent in July 2027, and to 12 percent in July 2028.
As a general tax with no end date, its money could pay for general city services until voters repeal it.
The City Attorney's impartial analysis gives the city estimate: about $1.4 million in new revenue the first year, then $2.8 million a year.
Opponents say the tax harms hotel businesses, has no end date, and guarantees no money for public safety.
Supporters say visitors, not residents, mainly pay the tax, which protects city services as the city faces deficits.
A yes vote supports raising the hotel tax to 10 percent, then to 12 percent.
A no vote opposes the increase and keeps the hotel tax at 8 percent.
