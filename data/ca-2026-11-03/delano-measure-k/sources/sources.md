# Measures K, L, M — City of Delano (elective mayor) — sources

Measures K, L and M are placed by one resolution, so their documents live here;
`../measure-l/sources.md` and `../measure-m/sources.md` point back to this file.
Retrieval date for all files: 2026-10-28.

## Files

### `agenda-2026-05-04-pp1-3-item7-pp41-44.pdf`
- Publisher: City of Delano (City Clerk)
- Title: Agenda, Delano City Council Regular Meeting, May 4, 2026 — pages 1–3
  (agenda) and 41–44 (Agenda Item 7: staff report and draft resolution), cut with
  `qpdf --pages` from the full packet
- URL: https://www.cityofdelano.org/ArchiveCenter/ViewFile/Item/3116
- Facts:
  - Origin: "on the June 2, 2025 Special Meeting, the City Council also approved
    Resolution 2025-43 to place the Election to Establish the Office of an Elected
    Mayor on the 2026 General Election."
  - Order: "The City Council hereby orders pursuant to Resolution No. 2025-43 that
    the following questions be submitted to the voters in the City of Delano,
    California, on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, and that the three (3) measures are to
    appear on the ballot as follows:"
  - Question 1 (K): "ELECTIVE OFFICE OF MAYOR — Shall the electors elect a mayor
    and four city council members? A "Yes" vote means the citizens directly elect
    the mayor; a "No" vote continues the practice that the council chooses one of
    its number to preside as Mayor on an annual basis."
  - Question 2 (L): "TWO YEAR TERM OF ELECTIVE OFFICE OF MAYOR — Shall the term of
    office of mayor be two years?"
  - Question 3 (M): "FOUR YEAR TERM OF ELECTIVE OFFICE OF MAYOR — Shall the term of
    office of mayor be four years?"
  - Who votes: "submitted to the voters in the City of Delano, California".
  - Note: the attached resolution is the unnumbered draft ("RESOLUTION NO. 2026 -").
