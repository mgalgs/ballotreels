Measure K asks the voters of this city whether to elect the mayor directly, along with four council members.
The City Council put three mayor questions on this ballot: K on electing the mayor, and L and M on a two-year or a four-year term.
Four of the five council members voted to place them, in June 2025.
Today, the council chooses one of its own members to preside as mayor each year.
Under Measure K, voters would elect the mayor and four council members.
The first mayoral election would be in November 2028.
Each of the three measures needs a majority vote to pass.
The county elections page for this election does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
No argument for Measure K appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
No argument against Measure K appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
A yes vote means the voters directly elect the mayor.
A no vote keeps the council choosing one of its members to preside as mayor each year.
