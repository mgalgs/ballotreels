ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE ___

Vote YES on Measure ___ to ensure that voters choose their representatives—not the other way around.

This measure establishes an Independent Redistricting Commission to draw County supervisorial district boundaries after each federal census, as provided by California law.

Currently, the San Mateo County Board of Supervisors has final authority over district boundaries. But this is a self-interested group that has, most recently, overridden the recommendations of citizen members of a redistricting commission. What’s the point of an independent commission if it isn’t independent? Allowing elected officials to make final decisions creates an inherent conflict of interest.

A truly independent redistricting commission:

• Reduces conflicts of interest by removing direct control from elected officials
• Increases transparency and public trust
• Creates broader and more inclusive community participation.

Under existing law, the commission must follow a ranked set of criteria for redistricting. Of these the highest priority is equal population. The commission must avoid splitting or diluting communities of interest whenever possible, preserve the geographic integrity of cities and census tracts where practicable, draw districts that are contiguous with clearly identifiable boundaries, and create districts that are reasonably compact. But nowhere does the law prohibit Supervisors from drawing districts favoring their own re-election. A truly independent redistricting commission is our best tool to keep districts competitive, so voters have meaningful choices.

It’s time to put the public first and to create meaningful competition.

Vote YES on Measure ___.
