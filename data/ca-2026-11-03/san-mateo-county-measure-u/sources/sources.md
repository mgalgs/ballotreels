# Measure U — County of San Mateo — Charter amendment (Sec. 202: independent redistricting commission)

- **Jurisdiction:** County of San Mateo (countywide).
- **Kind:** County Charter amendment, placed by the Board of Supervisors (Resolution No. 081882 and Ordinance No. 4920). It is the "Fourth Measure" of four in that resolution (L, AA, J, U).
- **Who votes:** all county voters. Resolution Sec. 9(b), PDF p. 8: "All qualified voters in San Mateo County shall be permitted to vote in the special election on the Charter amendments set forth in the Measures."
- **Threshold:** simple majority. Resolution Sec. 6, PDF p. 8: each Measure "will become effective only if submitted to the voters at the Countywide special election on November 3, 2026, and only after approval by a majority (i.e., 50% + 1) of the qualified voters voting in the special election."

## Ballot question (verbatim)

Resolution Sec. 5(a), PDF p. 6 (repeated in Ordinance Sec. 9(a), PDF p. 19), under the heading "COUNTY CHARTER AMENDMENT":

> Shall the San Mateo County Charter be amended to require that supervisorial districts be reapportioned following each federal decennial census by an independent redistricting commission pursuant to applicable provisions of the California Elections Code?

## Full text

- **Resolution No. 081882**, Board of Supervisors, County of San Mateo. PDF p. 11: "Regularly passed and adopted this 9th day of June, 2026". AYES: "JACKIE SPEIER", "NOELIA CORZO", "RAY MUELLER", "LISA GAUTHIER", "DAVID J. CANEPA". NOES: "NONE".
- **Ordinance No. 4920**, calling the election. PDF p. 21: "Regularly passed and adopted this 9th day of June, 2026"; same five AYES, NOES "NONE". Introduced "at its regular meeting on May 19, 2026" (resolution recital, PDF p. 2).
- **What it changes** (Resolution Sec. 5(b), PDF pp. 6-7): the Measure "would amend and replace in its entirety Section 202 of Article II of the Charter". The new Sec. 202 keeps: "Supervisors are elected by district." and "Supervisors shall not be eligible for election to nor serve more than three consecutive terms in office." It adds (PDF p. 7): "202.1. Independent Redistricting Commission. The five supervisorial districts of the County shall be reapportioned in the manner specified in general law by an independent redistricting commission, as defined in and governed by applicable provisions of the California Elections Code."
- Printing quirk (PDF p. 7, and Ordinance PDF p. 19): the lead-in says the Measure "would add Section 201.1 to Article II", but the section printed beneath is numbered "202.1", and the recital (PDF p. 2) and the impartial analysis both say "a new Section 202.1". Recorded as printed.
- Recital, PDF p. 1: "the County may establish an independent redistricting commission by resolution, ordinance, or charter amendment (Cal. Const. Art. 11, § 4; Elec. Code § 23001)".
- Sec. 9(g), PDF p. 9: "The County Attorney's Office shall prepare the impartial analysis of the Measures."

## Impartial analysis

`impartial-analysis.pdf`, p. 1. Headed "IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE [TBD]". **No author or signature is printed** on it; the resolution assigns it to the County Attorney's Office. It ends "(500 word limit. 363 words)".

- "Article II, Section 202, of the Charter currently provides that the five supervisorial districts shall be apportioned by ordinance pursuant to general law. Under current law, the Board itself adopts the ordinance that sets district boundaries."
- "Approval of Measure [TBD] ("Measure") would amend Section 202 of the Charter and add a new Section 202.1 to require that the County's five supervisorial districts be reapportioned after each federal decennial census by an independent redistricting commission, rather than by the Board by ordinance."
- "Elections Code Section 23000 defines an "independent redistricting commission" as a body, other than the legislative body itself, that is empowered to adopt those district boundaries, and state law separately imposes requirements intended to ensure the commission's independence, including restrictions on which individuals may serve as members."
- "The Measure would not change or otherwise affect the remaining provisions of Section 202, including the requirement that Supervisors be elected by district, that a Supervisor reside in the district represented, and that Supervisors serve four-year terms with a limit of three consecutive terms."
- "This Measure requires approval by a majority of the qualified voters voting in the election."
