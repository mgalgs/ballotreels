City of Coronado Measure D asks voters in the city whether to let multifamily buildings in one residential zone rise to four stories.
Today, multifamily buildings in that zone may be at most three stories and 35 feet tall.
The state housing department says that limit holds back the 40 homes per acre the zone allows.
Coronado's housing plan requires the city to put Measure D before voters, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
Measure D would raise the limit to four stories, and no more than 42 feet.
The City Attorney says the new limit would meet the state housing department's requirements.
The San Diego County guide does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Opponents say Measure D gives away a three-story limit that has protected the village character of Coronado since 1972.
No argument in favor of Measure D appears in the San Diego County Voter Information Guide for this election.
A yes vote is a vote in favor of revising the height limits.
A no vote is a vote against revising the height limits.
