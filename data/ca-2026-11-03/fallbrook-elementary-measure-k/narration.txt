Fallbrook Union Elementary School District Measure K asks the district's voters whether to approve $76 million in school bonds.
The district would borrow the money and repay it from a tax on property in the district.
Measure K needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would replace failing roofs, plumbing and electrical systems, improve school safety, and build or repair classrooms and labs.
The district's tax statement estimates a tax of $30 per $100,000 of assessed value, collected through 2062-2063.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $167 million.
Supporters say Measure K repairs aging neighborhood schools without raising the current tax rate.
No argument against Measure K was filed for the San Diego County voter guide.
A yes vote is in favor of letting the district sell the bonds.
A no vote is against letting the district sell the bonds.
