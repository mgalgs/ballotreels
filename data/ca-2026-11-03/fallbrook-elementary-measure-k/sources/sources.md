## Facts and the passages that establish them

### Ballot question

S1 PDF p. 78 (same wording in S3 Exhibit A, PDF p. 6):

> MEASURE K
> To update local elementary and middle schools without increasing tax rates; replace failing roofs, plumbing and electrical systems; improve school safety; construct/repair/acquire classrooms, labs, facilities, equipment, and sites; and qualify for State matching funds; shall Fallbrook Union Elementary School District's measure be adopted, authorizing $76,000,000 in bonds at legal rates, levying a projected $30 per $100,000 assessed value while bonds are outstanding, generating $5,000,000 annually, with public oversight, audits, no funds for administrators, all funds staying local?
> Bonds – Yes / Bonds – No

### Vote required

S1 PDF p. 78: "This measure requires 55% of those voting on the measure."

### Who votes on it

S3, Resolution, Section 1 (PDF p. 14):

> hereby requests the San Diego County Registrar of Voters to conduct an election under the provisions of Proposition 39 and the Act and submit to the electors of the District the question of whether bonds of the District in the aggregate principal amount of $76,000,000 (the "Bonds") shall be issued and sold

Section 3: "the purpose of the election shall be for the voters in the District to vote on a proposition". S1 PDF p. 78 (Tax Rate Statement): "for the purpose of submitting to the electors of the District the question of incurring bonded indebtedness of the District in the principal amount of $76,000,000."

In short: the electors (voters) of the Fallbrook Union Elementary School District.

### Who placed it on the ballot

S1 PDF p. 78: "The Governing Board for the Fallbrook Union Elementary School District ("District") placed this measure on the ballot."

S3 PDF p. 1 (cover letter, signed by the Superintendent): Resolution No. 01-26/27 "adopted on August 6, 2026 ... This Resolution orders a Proposition 39 election which requires 55% voter approval to be successful." **The ROV's copy stops at the resolution's page 5 (S3 PDF p. 16, mid-Section 11); it contains no adoption page or vote tally.**

### Impartial analysis

S1 PDF p. 78, "COUNTY COUNSEL IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS". Key statements:

> If approved by 55% of the votes cast, this measure will authorize the District to issue and sell general obligation bonds in an amount not to exceed $76,000,000.

> The District estimates the average tax rate would be $30 for every $100,000 of assessed valuation, and the highest tax rate would be approximately $30 for every $100,000 of assessed valuation. The tax is anticipated to be collected through fiscal year 2062-2063.

> The estimated total debt service, including principal and interest, required to be repaid if the bonds are issued and sold is approximately $167,000,000.

### Tax rate statement

S1 PDF p. 78, "TAX RATE STATEMENT / FALLBROOK UNION ELEMENTARY SCHOOL DISTRICT GENERAL OBLIGATION BONDS / MEASURE K", "Dated: August 6, 2026", "/s/ Monika Hazel, Superintendent":

> 1. ... is $30.00 per $100,000 of assessed valuation of all property to be taxed. It is estimated that the final fiscal year in which the tax is anticipated to be collected is fiscal year 2062-63.
> 2. ... is $30.00 per $100,000 of assessed valuation, which is projected to be the same in every fiscal year the bonds remain outstanding.
> 3. ... the total debt service ... is approximately $167,000,000.

### Arguments and rebuttals

- **Argument in favor**, S1 PDF p. 79. First line: "WITHOUT raising your tax rate, VOTE YES on Measure K to repair aging TK-8 neighborhood schools". It says "it's been nearly 25 years since we passed a FUESD bond" and Measure K "extends WITHOUT increasing the soon-to-expire bond authorization originally approved by FUESD voters in 2002." Signers: Mark Kersey (San Diego County Taxpayers Association President and CEO); Dr. Ana Arias (Elementary School Principal, 30-yr. Educator); Dr. Malcolm "Steve" Jones (Veterinarian, Local Business Owner); Dale Mitchell (Retired Superintendent, FUHSD); Dr. Connie Fish (Retired, 33-yr. Educator, Fallbrook Center for the Arts, Inc. Liaison).
- **Argument against**: none. S1 PDF p. 79: "No Argument Against Measure K was filed with the Office of the Registrar of Voters."
- **Rebuttals**: none printed.
