Proposition 42 is about taxes on personal property: what people own besides real estate.
Today, some personal property, like cars, is taxed, but simply owning financial assets is not.
New taxes on owning financial assets or other personal property would be banned.
Retroactive taxes, on past events, would be limited.
Conflicting measures on this ballot would be void if Proposition 42 got more yes votes.
The Legislative Analyst's Office says raising state taxes could become somewhat harder.
The office says future revenues could be lower, by an unclear amount.
Opponents say Proposition 42 is designed specifically to undo the Billionaire Tax, Proposition 40.
Supporters say Proposition 42 protects retirement and savings from being taxed a second time.
A yes vote means the state could not establish new personal property taxes.
A no vote means the state keeps the option to establish new personal property taxes.
