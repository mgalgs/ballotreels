Cabrillo Unified School District Measure Z asks the district's voters whether to replace its current parcel tax with a tax of $239 per parcel.
Today, the district levies a parcel tax that voters passed in November 2024.
Measure Z needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
A $239 tax per parcel would replace the 2024 tax from July 1, 2027, for up to five years.
The money could pay for teachers, classes and career training, but not administrators' pay.
The impartial analysis says the tax is estimated to raise about $2.6 million a year.
No argument against Measure Z appears in the published measure documents of San Mateo County for this election.
Supporters say Measure Z keeps qualified educators and grows career training, with local money the state cannot take.
A yes vote would impose the $239 parcel tax for up to five years.
A no vote would not impose the new tax, leaving the 2024 parcel tax in place.
