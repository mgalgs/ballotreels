IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE [TBD]

The governing board of the Cabrillo Unified School District (District) proposes to replace the District's existing special parcel tax with a new special parcel tax to support various educational purposes, as outlined below. The District's existing parcel tax (Measure N) will cease to be collected as of July 1, 2027. [45]

This Measure, if approved by voters, would authorize a new parcel tax in the annual amount of $239 per parcel, for a period of up to five years, commencing July 1, 2027, which tax is estimated to raise approximately $2.6 million annually. [40]

The proposed parcel tax applies to any unit of real property in the District that receives a separate tax bill for ad valorem property taxes from San Mateo County tax collection officials. Any person who is 65 years of age or older as of July 1 of the tax year, and who owns and occupies a parcel within the District, may apply for an exemption from the tax. An exemption is also available to any person who receives Supplemental Security Income for a disability, regardless of age, and to any person who receives Social Security Disability Insurance benefits, regardless of age, whose annual income does not exceed 250 percent of the 2012 federal poverty guidelines. [108]

Property owners who are currently exempt from the District's existing parcel tax (Measure N) under a comparable owner-occupied, single-family residence exemption will be automatically exempted from this Measure without filing a new application. [33]

The stated purposes of the proposed parcel tax are to: [10]

- Maintain science, technology, engineering, math, reading, and writing instruction;
- Attract and retain highly qualified teachers and staff;
- Support music and art programs; and
- Provide high-quality education to students. [28]

The Measure does not authorize use of parcel tax proceeds for administrators' salaries, benefits, or pensions. [16]

The proceeds of the parcel tax will be placed in a special account. A report accounting for the amount collected and expended, and the status of any projects or programs funded by the tax, will be filed at least once a year. An independent citizens' oversight committee will be established to review the expenditures of the tax and ensure the proceeds are applied only to the purposes authorized. [68]

The Board shall provide in each year for an increase in the District's appropriations limit as shall be necessary to ensure that the proceeds of the parcel tax may be spent for the authorized purposes. [35]

A "yes" vote on this Measure would impose on property within the District an annual parcel tax of $239 for up to five years beginning July 1, 2027, for the purposes listed above. [31]
