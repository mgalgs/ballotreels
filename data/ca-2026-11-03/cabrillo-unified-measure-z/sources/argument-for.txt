Vote Yes on Measure _ To Keep Coastside Schools Strong and Support Student Success!

Measure _ will help Cabrillo Unified attract and retain qualified, dedicated educators, strengthen career training and college readiness programs, and provide specialized reading and math instruction for younger students, so every child builds a strong foundation early.

- Retain Top-Tier Educators: Maintain competitive wages and benefits to attract and retain highly qualified teachers, counselors, specialized educators, and staff.

- No Administrator Salaries: Funds are prohibited from being used for administrator salaries, pensions, or benefits.

Measure _ protects taxpayers and seniors, retains highly qualified teachers, and strengthens the career training and college readiness programs our students rely on every day.
