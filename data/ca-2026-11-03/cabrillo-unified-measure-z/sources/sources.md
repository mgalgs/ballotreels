# Measure Z — Cabrillo Unified School District (parcel tax, $239 per parcel, 5 years)

- **Jurisdiction:** Cabrillo Unified School District (Half Moon Bay and the Coastside).
- **Kind:** Qualified special tax (education parcel tax), $239 per parcel for five years from July 1, 2027; replaces "Measure N" (November 2024).
- **Who votes:** the voters of the District. Recital (resolution p. 2): the Board asks the County Superintendent of Schools to "call an election and submit to the voters of the District the question of whether the District shall levy a qualified special tax". Section 3 (p. 2): "an election shall be held within the boundaries of the District on Tuesday, November 3, 2026".
- **Vote threshold:** two-thirds. Recital (p. 2): Article XIIIA, section 4 and Government Code sections 50075 et seq. authorize the District, "upon approval of two-thirds of the electorate, to levy qualified special taxes".
- **Placed by:** the Board of Trustees, Resolution No. 09-2026; the election is called by the San Mateo County Superintendent of Schools at the Board's order (Section 2, p. 2).
- **Election:** November 3, 2026.

## Ballot question

> With funds that cannot be taken by the State or Federal Government and spent elsewhere, shall Cabrillo Unified School District's measure to support local students by hiring and retaining highly qualified teachers and specialized educators; and expanding career training and college readiness programs, including agriculture, construction, and welding be adopted, replacing the current parcel tax at $239 per parcel for five years, (raising $2,600,000 annually) with senior exemptions, no administrator salaries or pensions, and public disclosure of spending?

(resolution-full-text.pdf p. 7, Exhibit A "Summary of Measure", between "**** BEGIN BALLOT LABEL ****" and "**** END BALLOT LABEL ****"; repeated under "TERMS" in Exhibit B, p. 8. Transcribed from the scan.)

## Tax rate statement / fiscal

Parcel tax, so no tax rate statement. From Exhibit B, section A "Amount, Basis, & Collection of Tax" (p. 9):
- "Beginning July 1, 2027 (fiscal year 2027-28), the District shall be authorized to levy an annual qualified special tax at the rate of $239 per parcel on all assessor's parcels, replacing the current parcel tax approved by voters in November 2024. The authorization to levy this qualified special tax shall expire on June 30, 2032."
- "This qualified special tax is estimated to raise $2.6 million in annual local funding for District schools."
- Exemptions (Section 4, p. 2; Exhibit B section B, p. 9), on application: persons "who will attain 65 years of age prior to May 1 of the tax year and occupying said parcel as his or her principal residence ("Senior Citizen Exemption")"; persons "receiving Supplemental Security Income for a disability, regardless of age" ("SSI Exemption"); and persons "receiving Social Security Disability Insurance benefits, regardless of age, whose yearly income does not exceed 250 percent of the 2012 federal poverty guidelines" ("SSDI Exemption"), each occupying the parcel as a principal residence.
- Section 4 (p. 3): "Exemptions granted under prior special taxes levied by the District will not require re-approval".
- No cost-of-living escalator appears in the pages read.
