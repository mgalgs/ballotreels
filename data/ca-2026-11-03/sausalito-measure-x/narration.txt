City of Sausalito Measure X asks voters in the city whether to raise building limits in two zoning districts and repeal the Marinship Specific Plan.
Today, a city ordinance bars zoning changes in commercial and industrial districts without voter approval.
Measure X reached the ballot by an initiative petition.
The City Council could then change zoning without a further vote, except downtown, where voter approval would still be needed.
In the I and W zones, buildings could cover up to 70 percent of a lot, up from 50 percent and 30 percent.
Height limits could rise from 32 to 47 feet, in the W zone only for some marine uses with a permit.
The W zone would allow new uses, such as arts and some manufacturing.
The City Attorney's impartial analysis does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Supporters say Measure X lets Marinship businesses innovate and grow, helping to pay for city repairs without raising taxes.
Opponents say Measure X ends the public vote on zoning and allows 900 percent more building on waterfront lots.
A yes vote adopts the zoning changes in Measure X.
A no vote leaves the zoning rules as they are.
