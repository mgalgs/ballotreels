Measure X - Arts, Industrial & Maritime Preservation and Improvement Initiative (AIM Initiative)

  • On July 07, 2026, the City Clerk certified that the initiative petition for the Arts, Industrial & Maritime Preservation and Improvement Initiative ("AIM Initiative") qualified for the ballot.
  • Marin County Registrar of Voters certified on May 29, 2026 that signature verification on the petition was complete pursuant to Elections Code §9114: of 860 raw signatures submitted (against a requirement of 558), a random sample of 500 was checked, with 493 found valid. See the Certificate of Signature Verification.
  • The AIM Initiative will appear on the November 3, 2026 General Municipal Election ballot, as required by Elections Code Section 9215.

AIM Ballot Question:

  • Shall the measure named the "Arts, Industrial & Maritime Preservation and Improvement Initiative," which revises zoning standards in the I and W zoning districts to increase permitted height, building coverage limits and floor area ratio and allow certain new land uses; repeals the Marinship Specific Plan; revises Ordinance No. 1022 to allow future city council modifications to zoning outside the Downtown Historic District; and modifies various portions of the Sausalito General Plan, be adopted? 

Correction Notice*

  • The June 16, 2026 agenda packet mistakenly included an earlier, withdrawn draft of the measure rather than the final version that was circulated for signature-gathering and used to prepare the City Attorney's title and summary. This was a clerical error in assembling the packet and did not affect the County's signature verification, which was based on the correct, final version of the measure throughout.
  • To correct the record, the City Council rescinded Resolution 30-2026 on July 7, 2026, and adopted Resolution 33-2026, which submits the correct, final version of the AIM Initiative to voters at the November 3, 2026 election.

Register to Vote

  • Residents who have moved or changed their name must re-register to be eligible to vote in future elections. Postage-paid, pre-addressed registration forms may be obtained from any post office, city/town hall, public library or fire station in Marin County. Online voter registration: registertovote.ca.gov
  • For more voting information, please visit the Marin County Registrar of Voters website.

Please contact the City Clerk at (415) 289-4165 to obtain any election forms you may require.
