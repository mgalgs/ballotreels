## Facts and quotes

**Ballot question** (city page, "AIM Ballot Question"): "Shall the measure named the "Arts, Industrial & Maritime Preservation and Improvement Initiative," which revises zoning standards in the I and W zoning districts to increase permitted height, building coverage limits and floor area ratio and allow certain new land uses; repeals the Marinship Specific Plan; revises Ordinance No. 1022 to allow future city council modifications to zoning outside the Downtown Historic District; and modifies various portions of the Sausalito General Plan, be adopted?"

**How it got on the ballot** (city page): "On July 07, 2026, the City Clerk certified that the initiative petition for the Arts, Industrial & Maritime Preservation and Improvement Initiative ("AIM Initiative") qualified for the ballot." "...the City Council rescinded Resolution 30-2026 on July 7, 2026, and adopted Resolution 33-2026, which submits the correct, final version of the AIM Initiative to voters at the November 3, 2026 election."

**Who votes:** Sausalito voters ("The AIM Initiative will appear on the November 3, 2026 General Municipal Election ballot"). Vote threshold: not stated in the documents I read.

**Impartial analysis** (signed Sergio Rudin, City Attorney), key statements:
- "This initiative measure would revise Ordinance No. 1022, known as the Fair Traffic Limits Initiative ... The measure changes Ordinance No. 1022 to allow the City Council to make changes to land use standards and zoning ... without further voter approval, with exception of the Downtown Historic District."
- "The measure would increase the building coverage limits in these zones to 70% (up from 50% in the "I" district and 30% in the "W" district), increase the maximum floor area ratio to 3.0 (up from 0.4 in the "I" district and 0.3 in the "W" district)".
- "For the "I" zone, the measure would ... increase the height limit from 32 to 47 feet. For the "W" district, the 32 foot height limit could be up to 47 feet for marine commercial service and marine industrial uses approved with a use permit."
- "The measure would repeal the Marinship Specific Plan".
- "City regulations on number of liveaboard slips in marinas would be repealed, and instead governed by BCDC permit requirements and state law."

**Arguments and rebuttals:** both primary arguments and both rebuttals are posted by the city (files above). Contents not summarised here. These are the city's copies; not confirmed identical to the printed county guide.
