## Ballot question (guide p. 947)

> Shall the measure amending Oakland's Real Property Transfer Tax Ordinance to eliminate the foreclosure exception for all property transfers except transfers converting commercial property to certain housing or healthcare uses, transfers to community banking organizations, and certain transfers of residential properties of four units or less, and allowing the City Council to establish, amend, or remove foreclosure-related exceptions, raising approximately $4-$13 million annually pursuant to tax rates in Oakland Municipal Code section 4.20.020, beginning January 1, 2027 until repealed, be adopted?

Transcribed from the guide; line-break hyphens rejoined and the YES/NO box labels omitted.

## Who votes on it

Voters in the City of Oakland. The guide section is headed "CITY OF OAKLAND MEASURE FF", and the Registrar's measure list (<https://alamedacountyca.gov/rov_app/measures/election/260>) files it under that jurisdiction. Vote threshold: see the analysis quotes below.

## Fiscal analysis (City Auditor)

The guide prints a **City Auditor's Impartial Analysis of Measure FF** (financial analysis, signed by the City Auditor, Michael C. Houston) on p. 947, beside the ballot question:

- p. 947: "If passed, the Office of the City Auditor estimates this ballot measure would generate between $10.4 and $13.8 million of revenue in the first year."
- p. 947: "We estimate the total costs to the City of implementing this ballot measure would range from $592,900 to $651,100 in the first year."

Note: the ballot question says "raising approximately $4-$13 million annually"; the Auditor's first-year estimate is $10.4–$13.8 million. Both are quoted as printed.

City Attorney's analysis, additional quote:

- p. 947: "Currently, the Real Property Transfer Tax generally exempts transfers made pursuant to foreclosure proceedings and transfers in lieu of foreclosure, meaning such transfers are not subject to the tax."
