YosemiteUSD
Argument in Favor of Measure Y

Support Yosemite High School repairs with local control and accountability.

Vote YES on Measure Y to repair and improve Yosemite High School, keep tax dollars local, and maintain one of our community's most important assets.

Yosemite High School is aging. Major repairs and updates are now needed to keep the school safe and functional. Delaying will make it more expensive tomorrow.

Measure Y Taxpayer Protections:

Local Control: Funds cannot be taken by the State and spent elsewhere. Every dollar must stay local and be spent on voter-approved Yosemite High School projects.

Strict Accountability: Taxpayer oversight and full public disclosure of all spending are required.

Funds Benefit Schools, Not Administrators: No funds can be used for administrator salaries, pensions or benefits.

Measure Y Projects Include:

• Repairing and replacing leaky, deteriorating roofs;
• Replacing inefficient heating, ventilation, filtration and air-conditioning systems
• Updating fire alarms and emergency communication systems;
• Renovating career-training classrooms giving students hands-on skills for college, skilled trades and local careers in fields such as agriculture, auto mechanics, culinary arts and welding.

Yosemite High School is a source of local pride and a cornerstone of our mountain community. It provides our students with a close-knit school environment, strong academics and career-training programs.

Measure Y means safer classrooms and improved career-training facilities, while protecting property values and supporting local businesses.

Vote YES on Measure Y.
