**Swapped tax rate statements.** As posted by the registrar, the Measure Y resolution file (pp. 19-20) carries the tax rate statement for the **$18 million SFID No. 1** bonds, and the Measure W file (pp. 19-20) carries the statement for the **$61.2 million Yosemite USD** bonds. I cut each statement into the measure it belongs to. The original files are kept unchanged.

**No argument against and no rebuttals** are posted on the registrar's page. The printed guide was not found online, so "the official guide prints none" is not confirmed.

## Facts

**Ballot question.** Registrar's legal notice, July 24, 2026:
> "With funds that cannot be taken by the State, shall Yosemite Unified School District's measure to renovate career training classrooms; repair/replace aging roofs, heating, ventilation and air-conditioning; and update fire alarms and emergency communication systems at Yosemite High School be adopted, authorizing $61.2 million in bonds with legal rates, average levies below $60 per $100,000 of assessed valuation ($4 million annually) while outstanding, taxpayer oversight, no funds for administrator salaries, pensions, or benefits and public disclosure of all spending?"

**Who votes.** Same notice: "NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN to the qualified electors of the Yosemite Unified School District, located in Madera County and Mariposa County, California". The cover letter (resolution.pdf p. 1) is addressed to both the Madera and the Mariposa County Registrars of Voters.

**Board resolution date.** The notice says "This election has been called pursuant to a Resolution of the Board of Trustees of the Yosemite Unified School District, adopted on July 18, 2026." The cover letter is dated July 13, 2026, and the filing is stamped "2026 JUL 20". A July 18 adoption after a July 13 cover letter looks inconsistent. The date on the resolution's signature page (p. 9) has not been checked.

**Vote needed; what it does.** Impartial Analysis of Measure "Y" (Regina A. Garza, Madera County Counsel):
> "If approved, the Measure would authorize the District to issue $61,200,000 in general obligation bonds to fund educational facility projects at the District's high schools. ... To pass, the Measure must be approved by fifty-five (55%) of voters who vote in the election."
> "The District's best estimate of the highest tax rate to be collected to repay the bonds is $59.98 per $100,000 of assessed value of property within the District. The District's best estimate of the total possible amount, including interest, that will be required to repay the bonds is $84.9 million. The District estimates the final year that taxes will be collected to repay the bonds is fiscal year 2047-2048."

**Tax rate statement** ("Tax Rate Statement Regarding Proposed $61.2 Million Yosemite Unified School District General Obligation Bonds"; found in the W filing, p. 19):
> "1. The best estimate of the average annual tax rate ... is $59.98 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. The final fiscal year in which the tax is anticipated to be collected is 2047-48."
> "2. The best estimate of the highest tax rate ... is $59.98 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. It is estimated that such rate would be levied in fiscal year 2027-28 through 2047-48."
> "3. The best estimate of the total debt service ... is approximately $84.9 million."
