## Quoted facts

- **Who votes:** Grover Beach voters. Impartial analysis: "The purpose of this measure is to ask registered voters in the City of Grover Beach if they wish to adopt an ordinance to limit building height within the commercial and industrial zones, and to establish a minimum percentage for designated commercial use within mixed-use developments."
- **Ballot question** (Resolution No. 26-20): "CITY OF GROVER BEACH INITIATIVE MEASURE F-26 Grover Beach Citizen Initiative Ballot Measure. Shall the City of Grover Beach implement a building height limit and establish a minimum thirty-three percent (33%) commercial component in mixed-use development?"
- **Impartial analysis** (p. 33): "Currently, the Development Code, Article IX of the City's Municipal Code, allows for building heights up to fifty-five feet (55') in certain commercial zones, and building heights up to forty feet (40') in industrial zones. The measure would limit building heights in Commercial Zoning Districts to three (3) stories and no more than forty feet (40') in height, and limit buildings within the Industrial Zoning Districts to thirty-three feet (33') in height. The measure would additionally change how building height is measured."
- **Arguments:** for, against, and both rebuttals printed (pp. 34–37).
