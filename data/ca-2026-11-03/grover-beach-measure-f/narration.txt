Measure F asks the voters of this city whether to lower building height limits and require a minimum of commercial space in mixed-use buildings.
Measure F is a citizen initiative that reached the ballot by petition.
Today, city rules allow buildings up to 55 feet tall in some commercial zones, and up to 40 feet in industrial zones.
Measure F would limit commercial buildings to three stories and 40 feet, and industrial buildings to 33 feet.
Height would be measured to the very top, counting rooftop features like equipment and elevator towers, which today's rules leave out.
At least 33 percent of a mixed-use building would have to be commercial, with commercial uses on the ground floor facing the main street.
Measure F needs a simple majority to pass.
The county voter guide does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Opponents say Measure F risks state fines and lawsuits under housing law, and could push taller buildings into residential neighborhoods.
Supporters say restoring earlier height limits preserves a small coastal-town character, and commercial space brings in more city revenue.
A yes vote is in favor of the lower height limits and the minimum commercial share.
A no vote is against the lower height limits and the minimum commercial share.
