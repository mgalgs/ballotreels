City of Needles Measure D asks Needles voters whether to raise City Council pay from $1 a month to $150 a month.
Needles voters adopted the city charter in 1958, and only voters can change it, including council pay.
Today, the charter pays each council member $1 a month.
Measure D would raise that to $150 a month for each member.
Measure D needs a majority of the vote to pass.
The city attorney's impartial analysis says the city has found the raise would cost it $20,916 more each year.
Supporters say Measure D recognizes the time council members give, and removes a financial barrier to public service.
No argument against Measure D was filed, so the county voter guide prints only the argument of supporters.
A yes vote is in favor of raising council pay from $1 to $150 a month.
A no vote leaves the charter unchanged, with council pay staying at $1 a month.
