## Quoted facts

- **Who votes:** City of Needles voters (General Municipal Election, November 3, 2026).
- **Ballot question** (RES_CityofNeedles.pdf, Section 3): "MEASURE_: CITY COUNCIL COMPENSATION-CHARTER AMENDMENT Shall the proposed amendment to section 503 of the City Charter of the City of Needles to increase the compensation for each member of the City Council from $1 per month to $150 per month be approved?"
- **Impartial analysis** (VIG p. 189, City Attorney): "Measure D would amend Section 503 of the City Charter to increase the compensation paid to each member of the City Council from one dollar ($1.00) per month to one hundred fifty dollars ($150.00) per month." … "The City of Needles is a charter city operating under a City Charter adopted by the voters in 1958. Section 503 of the Charter currently provides that each member of the City Council shall receive compensation in the amount of one dollar ($1.00) per month."
- **Vote needed:** 50% + 1 (Registrar's measures page).
