## Facts and the passages that establish them

### Ballot question (ballot label)

S1 PDF p. 67 and S2 PDF p. 20:

> MEASURE B
> MEASURE TO FUND SAN DIEGO COUNTY HEALTH & SAFETY SERVICES
> To clean-up sewage pollution in Tijuana River Valley, improve wildfire prevention, emergency response, access to healthcare, food assistance, childhood development services, and authorized administrative expenses; shall the measure authorizing a half-cent sales tax raising an estimated $400,000,000 to $450,000,000 annually, requiring citizen oversight, public spending disclosure, and independent annual audits, and lasting until ended by voters, be adopted?

### Vote required

S1 PDF p. 67:

> This measure requires Simple Majority (50%+1) of those voting on the measure.

S3, section 22.503(a):

> which authorize the County to adopt this tax Ordinance which shall be operative if a majority of the electors voting on the measure vote to approve the imposition of the tax at an election called for that purpose.

The measure also calls itself a special tax. S1 PDF p. 74, Section 6: "This measure is an exercise of the initiative power of the people of the County of San Diego to implement a special tax to fund the purposes set forth in the Ordinance". I quote both as printed. The official record I archived does not discuss which vote threshold applies beyond what is quoted here.

### Who placed it on the ballot

S1 PDF p. 67 (impartial analysis):

> This measure was placed on the ballot by a petition signed by the requisite number of voters.

S4 lists "MEASURE TO FUND SAN DIEGO COUNTY HEALTH & SAFETY SERVICES" as type "Initiative": signatures required 102,923; notice of intent 12/12/2025; circulation deadline 6/24/2026; petition filed 5/4/2026.

I did not find the Board of Supervisors' action certifying the petition or calling the election. It is not on the ROV filing page. See "Missing" below.

### Who votes on it, and where the tax applies (county-wide)

S4 is titled "COUNTY OF SAN DIEGO / COUNTYWIDE PETITION STATUS" and lists this initiative. S2 prints the measure under the heading "COUNTY".

S1 PDF p. 67 (impartial analysis):

> This measure, if approved by a majority vote of qualified electors, would impose a transaction and use tax of a half cent per dollar on the sale of all tangible personal property in the County of San Diego, in both incorporated and unincorporated areas.

S3, section 22.505:

> a tax is hereby imposed upon all retailers in the incorporated and unincorporated territory of the County at the rate of one half of one percent (0.5%) of the gross receipts of any retailer from the sale of all tangible personal property sold at retail in said territory

### Full text

S1 PDF pp. 69-74, headed "FULL TEXT OF MEASURE B". It begins "The people of the County of San Diego ordain as follows:" and "Section 1. Title / This measure shall be known and may be cited as the "Protect San Diego County's Health and Safety Act."". S3 has the same opening text. I did not compare S1 and S3 word for word.

### Impartial analysis

S1 PDF p. 67, headed "COUNTY COUNSEL IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS / MEASURE TO FUND SAN DIEGO COUNTY HEALTH & SAFETY SERVICES". On revenue and allocation:

> It is estimated that the sales tax would generate approximately $400,000,000 to $450,000,000 per year.

> • Stopping sewage pollution in the Tijuana River Valley: 22.5%
> • Public safety services and wildfire prevention: 17.5%
> • Healthcare, childcare, and nutrition services: 60%

On oversight:

> The measure would establish a twelve-member oversight committee ... The Board of Supervisors for the County of San Diego would appoint eleven of the twelve members of this committee ... The twelfth member of this committee would be the County of San Diego Auditor.

### Arguments and rebuttals

- **Argument in favor**, S1 PDF p. 67. Signers: Cheyenne Borello (Fire Battalion Chief CAL-Fire San Diego Station 50, Julian); Pat Zaharopoulos (Board Of Directors SD Middle Class Taxpayers Assoc.); Laura Wilkinson Sinton (Founder Stopthesewage.org); Kathy Griffee, RN (Public Health Nurse San Diego County Children's Services); Bruce Frieberg, MD (Emergency Medicine Physician Palomar Medical Center Escondido). First line: "Join SAN DIEGO COUNTY FIREFIGHTERS, EMERGENCY ROOM DOCTORS & NURSES…"
- **Rebuttal to the argument in favor**: none. S1 PDF p. 67 says: "NO REBUTTAL TO THE ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE B FILED AT THE REGISTRAR OF VOTERS".
- **Argument against**, S1 PDF p. 68. Signer: Mark Kersey (President & CEO San Diego County Taxpayers Association). First line: "We've carefully analyzed Measure B and strongly urge you to VOTE NO!"
- **Rebuttal to the argument against**, S1 PDF p. 68. Signers: the same five as the argument in favor. First line: "THE OPPONENTS HAVE THEIR FACTS WRONG…"

### Tax rate statement or fiscal analysis

None is printed for Measure B in S1. A tax rate statement is a bond-measure document (Elections Code 9401), and Measure B is a sales tax, not a bond. The only revenue estimate in the official record I archived is the one in the ballot label and the impartial analysis ($400,000,000 to $450,000,000 per year).
