## Ballot question and supporters/opponents (verbatim, from the county's final measures list)

> MEASURE TT: Shall the measure, which maintains the existing transient occupancy tax rates; authorizes the City Council to establish Major Event Periods, as defined, when the tax applies to lodging paid for or obtained by corporate entities for stays over 30 days, and to adjust the hotel and motel tax rate for such stays; clarifies that refunds require a natural person to complete at least thirty-one consecutive days of occupancy, and authorizes administrative regulations, be adopted?
>
> Supporters: Santa Monica Firefighters Local 1109; Manju Raman; Richard Bloom, Fmr Mayor, Assemblymember; Jesse Zwick, Natalya Zernitskaya
>
> Opponents: None submitted.

Vote threshold: Majority, 50%+1.

## What's present and what's missing

- **Full ordinance text**: present (within `resolution.pdf`, Municipal Code Chapter 6.68 amendment).
- **Resolution placing the measure on the ballot**: present (`resolution.pdf`).
- **Impartial analysis**: missing — not found. `santamonica.gov`, where the City posts these, returned an Imperva/Incapsula bot-challenge page on every path tried (the specific measure page, `.../measures/measure-tot-major-event-period`, included; a Wayback Machine snapshot of that same page turned out to be a capture of the same block page).
- **Argument for**: five supporters are named in the county's final list, after the filing deadline, so an argument for was almost certainly filed — the text itself is missing, not found, same block.
- **Argument against**: **confirmed not filed** — the county's official list states "Opponents: None submitted."
- **Rebuttals**: not applicable to either side. California law lets each side rebut only the opposing side's filed argument; since no argument against was filed, there is no argument against for proponents to rebut, and no official opponent authors exist to file a rebuttal to the argument for.
- **Tax rate statement**: not applicable — TT amends an existing transient occupancy tax, not a property-tax-secured bond measure.
