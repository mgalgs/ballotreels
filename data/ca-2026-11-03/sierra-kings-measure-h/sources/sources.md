## Facts

**Ballot question.** Resolution No. 328, para. 2, PDF page 2:
> "Shall the measure to assure continued operation of Sierra Kings Health Care District's hospital facility at 372 West Cypress Avenue, Reedley, California, as an acute care hospital, in accordance with District Board Resolution No. 328 adopted July 27, 2026, by having the District enter with Reedley Community Hospital, the existing operator, a twenty year lease with options to extend the lease and purchase the property, with no new taxes, bonds, or assessments on District residents, be adopted?"

**Who votes; why.** Resolution No. 328, PDF pages 1-2:
> "WHEREAS, Section 32121(p)(1) of the Code provides in part that the transfer of more than fifty percent (50%) of a district's assets requires that a measure be placed on a ballot and is subject to voter approval"
> "... hereby calls for a regular election of the voters of the District to be held on November 3, 2026, for the purpose of submitting a ballot measure to the District voters the question whether the New Lease should be approved."

**Impartial analysis.** Impartial Analysis by County Counsel (Douglas T. Sloan, Fresno County Counsel; filed Aug 24 2026):
> "This measure seeks voter approval allowing the board of directors ("Board") of the Sierra Kings Health Care District ("District") to enter into a new lease and option to purchase agreement with Reedley Community Hospital ("RCH"), an affiliate of Adventist Health System, for the lease and possible sale of the hospital facility and parcel located at 372 West Cypress Avenue, Reedley, California and an adjacent parcel ("New Lease")."
> "In 2011, the District's voters approved the original lease and option to purchase agreement with RCH ("Original Lease"). ... The New Lease will include a 20-year initial term and two five-year renewal terms for the continued operation and maintenance of the hospital."
> "The measure was placed on the ballot by the District's Board. ... A majority vote (more than 50% of votes cast) is required to pass the measure. The measure makes no change to existing law."

Note: the resolution's recital says the 2011 original lease was approved "in the November 3, 2011 election". The analysis says only "In 2011".
