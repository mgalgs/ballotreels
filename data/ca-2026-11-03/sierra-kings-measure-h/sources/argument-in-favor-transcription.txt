A Yes vote on Measure H is a vote to continue operation of the Reedley Hospital.

The quality of life in the community in which you live is one of the most important factors for why residents root themselves in a community. Reedley, Parlier, Miramonte, and Yuokuts Valley residents have a high on quality of life, and one of those reasons is the easy access of our full-service Reedley Hospital with Emergency Room, birthing center, and health specialties brought to the community by Adventist Hospital.

In 2012, Adventist Health assumed operation of the Reedley Hospital under a lease agreement with the Sierra Kings Healthcare District and has transformed the Reedley Hospital into the gem it is today. It is now time to renew the lease agreement. The State's laws require that the renewing of the lease between the District and Adventist go to the voters for approval.

This Measure is not a tax, bond, or assessment. It is solely a vote to renew the lease with Adventist Health for ongoing hospital operation.

Without Adventist running the hospital, the hospital could close.

Ensure this critical service remains available to you and your family - Vote Yes on Measure H.
