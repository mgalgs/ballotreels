Measure H asks the voters of this health care district whether to lease the district's hospital to its current operator for another 20 years.
The district owns the hospital, and in 2011 its voters approved a 15-year lease, with an option to buy, to the operator that runs it today.
State law requires voter approval when a health care district transfers more than half of its assets, and the new lease involves more than half.
The new lease would run 20 years, with two five-year renewals and an option for the operator to buy the property.
The board's approval depends on an independent appraisal of the new lease's market value.
Measure H needs a simple majority to pass.
The ballot question says the lease comes with no new taxes, bonds or assessments on district residents.
No argument against Measure H was filed with the county registrar of voters, so the published measure documents include only the supporters' argument.
Supporters say the hospital gives easy access to an emergency room, a birthing center and specialties, and could close without its operator.
A yes vote approves the 20-year lease and its options.
A no vote does not approve the 20-year lease.
