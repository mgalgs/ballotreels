Town of Windsor Measure I asks voters in Windsor whether to add a 1 percent sales tax.
The sales tax in Windsor is 9.25 percent today, and the town receives 1 percent of it.
Measure I would raise the total to 10.25 percent, and the town's share to 2 percent, until voters repeal it.
As a general tax, the town could use the money for any government purpose, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
The Town Attorney's impartial analysis says the tax would provide about $5.7 million a year for the general fund of the town.
Supporters say rising costs strain town services and deep cuts are coming without Measure I, and its money stays in Windsor.
Opponents say Windsor has a spending problem, not a revenue problem, and sales taxes hit working families and seniors hardest.
A yes vote is a vote to approve a 1 percent sales tax in Windsor.
A no vote is a vote against the tax.
