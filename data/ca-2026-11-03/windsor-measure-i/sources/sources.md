## Facts and quotes

**Ballot question (p1):** "To provide funding for the Town of Windsor to maintain essential local services, such as repairing potholes and maintaining streets; neighborhood police patrols / emergency response / traffic enforcement; preserving parks / community events; disaster preparedness and fire prevention — removing brush / flammable fuels, and for general government use; shall the measure establishing a 1% sales tax, providing approximately $5,700,000 annually until ended by voters, requiring independent audits, citizen oversight, and all funds remaining in Windsor under local control, be adopted?"

**Vote needed:** "Simple Majority Needed to Pass" (p1); "Measure I will pass if it receives a simple majority of "Yes" votes." (p2)

**Who votes:** "...to ask the Town's voters to approve a one percent (1%) local transactions and use (sales) tax." (p2)

**Impartial analysis (p2), key statements:**
- "Currently, the tax on retail sales in Windsor is 9.25% of the purchase price. Of the 9.25%, the Town directly receives 1% ... The Town is the only municipality in Sonoma County that has not previously implemented a local transactions and use tax."
- "Measure I would authorize a 1% transactions and use tax, which would increase the total sales tax rate in Windsor to 10.25% (subject to the adoption of other countywide taxes)."
- "...the tax would provide approximately $5.7 million annually for the Town's general fund ... Because Measure I does not limit the use of tax revenue, it is a "general tax,""
- "The Measure requires the establishment of an independent Citizens' Oversight Commission"
- "If Measure I is approved, the tax would continue until ended by Town of Windsor voters."
- Dated August 14, 2026; signed Jose M. Sanchez, Town Attorney.

**Argument in favor (p3):** signed Windsor Town Council — Rosa Reynoza (Mayor), Sam Salmon (Councilmember); Michael Raasch (Retired Windsor Police Chief); Guadalupe Navarro; Jim Curry (Windsor Chamber Past Chair).

**Rebuttal to argument in favor (p3):** signed Concerned Windsor Citizens — John Leyba (Chairperson), John Broughton (Treasurer); Mary Ann Bainbridge-Krause; Betsy Mallace; Bob Kruse.

**Argument against (p4):** signed the same five (Leyba, Broughton, Bainbridge-Krause, Mallace, Kruse).
