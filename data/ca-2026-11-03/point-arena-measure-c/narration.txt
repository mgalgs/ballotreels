Measure C asks the voters of this city whether to raise the hotel tax from 12 percent to 14 percent.
Today, guests who stay 30 days or less at a hotel, inn, motel or other lodging pay a 12 percent tax on the room rent.
Measure C would add 2 percent more, starting April 1, 2027, until it is repealed.
The added money would go into the city general fund.
Measure C needs a simple majority to pass.
The city attorney's impartial analysis says the added 2 percent would bring in about $35,833 a year.
Supporters say visitors, not residents, pay the tax, and every dollar stays local for services visitors use.
No argument against Measure C was submitted for the printed voter guide pages on this city measure.
A yes vote approves the proposed increase to the hotel tax.
A no vote rejects the proposed increase to the hotel tax.
