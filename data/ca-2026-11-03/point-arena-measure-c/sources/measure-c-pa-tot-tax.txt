               ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE C


Vote YES on Measure C
A YES Vote on Measure C will help strengthen Point Arena’s local
economy and provide funding for essential public services that benefit
our entire community.
Measure C would increase the City’s Transient Occupancy Tax (TOT)
from 12% to 14% on the cost of overnight lodging. The TOT is paid by
visitors staying in local hotels, motels, inns, and vacation rentals—not
by Point Arena residents unless they choose to stay in one of these
accommodations.
Visitors enjoy and rely on our roads, sidewalks, parks, parking areas,
public restrooms, and the City pier. Maintaining and improving these
public facilities requires ongoing investment. Revenue generated by
the Transient Occupancy Tax supplements the City’s General Fund,
helping support essential public services, maintain community
facilities, and fund projects that benefit both residents and visitors.
TOT revenue also helps promote Point Arena as a destination,
supporting local businesses and encouraging economic activity
throughout the community. Every dollar generated by Measure C will
remain in Point Arena and be used to support local services and
community needs. These locally generated funds cannot be taken by
the State.
During Fiscal Year 2024-2025, the City’s Transient Occupancy Tax
generated approximately $200,000 in revenue. Increasing the tax by 2
% is expected to generate approximately $36,000 in additional annual
revenue.
