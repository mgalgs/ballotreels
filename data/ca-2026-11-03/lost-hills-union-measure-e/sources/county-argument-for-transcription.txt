Argument in Favor of Measure " " - Lost Hills Union School District

Lost Hills Union School District is committed to student achievement and is proud to be a California Distinguished School. Now, to help our students be safer and better prepared for futures in high school, college, or skilled trades jobs, we need your help - vote YES on Measure "_" !

It has been a decade since local voters and taxpayers last agreed to invest in Lost Hills schools and make very important improvements. But in the years since, the State of California has been unable to provide the District with enough money to adequately repair schools to provide an optimal learning environment for all of our students.

Our schools need to be upgraded to improve student safety and school security. Schools play an important role in keeping our students away from crime. Supporting our youngest learners with excellent schools keeps them safe and focused on learning. Our schools also need to be better equipped to promote an education with a strong foundation in science, technology, arts and math. So, this summer, the Board decided to place Measure " " on the ballot.

Measure" "will:
• Improve student safety and security including fencing, cameras, and access entries.
• Improve the health and comfort of students including replacing aged heating and air conditioning, flooring, lighting, and windows, updating classrooms and restrooms, and repairing plumbing, roofing, parking lots, and playgrounds.
• Improve accessibility for students with disabilities,
• Help retain and attract quality teachers
• Help maintain a job-ready local workforce beginning with our youngest learners
• Help protect the value of our homes and local economy

Taxpayer safeguards are in place. Measure"_" requires:
• Public disclosure of all spending
• Oversight by local citizens
• Annual independent audits of bond expenditures
• All funds staying local in Lost Hills

Let's keep on improving Lost Hills schools -vote YES on Measure " ".
