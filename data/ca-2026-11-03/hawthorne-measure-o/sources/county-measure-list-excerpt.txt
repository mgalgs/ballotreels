CITY OF HAWTHORNE – (1)
VOTE PASSAGE – Majority Vote 50%+1
O      Shall the measure increasing the City of Hawthorne's transient occupancy (hotel) tax
       from 12% to 17%, to maintain City services such as police and fire protection, street
       repairs, park/recreation programs, and other general City services, generating
       approximately $3.1 million annually, paid only by hotel/lodging guests until ended by
       voters, with all funds staying local, be adopted? Supporters: Angie Reyes English,
       Hawthorne City Council Member; Alex Monteiro, Hawthorne City Council Member
       Opponents: None submitted.
