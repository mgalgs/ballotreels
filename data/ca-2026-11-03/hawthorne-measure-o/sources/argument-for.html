ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE O

Vote YES on Measure O to protect Hawthorne's future.

Measure O is a locally controlled funding measure that will help maintain the essential services our community depends on every day. As the costs of providing public safety, maintaining streets and infrastructure, supporting parks, and delivering City services continue to rise, Hawthorne needs a reliable and sustainable source of local funding.

Measure O increases the City's Transient Occupancy Tax (hotel tax), which is paid primarily by visitors staying in local hotels not Hawthorne residents. If approved by voters, the measure is projected to generate $1.2 million and $3.1 million annually to help preserve essential City services without increasing property or sales taxes on Hawthorne residents.

Hawthorne's current hotel tax rate is 12%, which is lower than that of several neighboring cities. Measure O would adjust the rate to 17%, helping align Hawthorne with regional market conditions while capturing additional revenue from visitors, especially as Southern California prepares to host major international events expected to increase tourism.

Revenue generated by Measure O will remain in Hawthorne and may be used to:

- Maintain police, fire, and emergency response services.

- Repair and maintain streets, sidewalks, and public facilities.

- Keep parks, recreational programs, and community spaces safe and well maintained.

- Support neighborhood cleanliness and other essential City services.

- Strengthen the City's long-term financial stability.

The City has demonstrated a strong commitment to fiscal responsibility by implementing cost-saving measures and maintaining sound financial management. However, independent financial projections indicate that, without additional ongoing revenue, Hawthorne is expected to face a structural budget deficit in future years as operating costs continue to outpace revenues.

Every dollar generated by Measure O will stay in Hawthorne to support local services and cannot be taken by the State.

Vote YES on Measure O to help protect Hawthorne's public safety, infrastructure, parks, and quality of life for years to come.
