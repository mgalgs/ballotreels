## Ballot question and supporters/opponents (verbatim, from the county's final measures list)

> Shall the measure increasing the City of Hawthorne's transient occupancy (hotel) tax from 12% to 17%, to maintain City services such as police and fire protection, street repairs, park/recreation programs, and other general City services, generating approximately $3.1 million annually, paid only by hotel/lodging guests until ended by voters, with all funds staying local, be adopted?
>
> Supporters: Angie Reyes English, Hawthorne City Council Member; Alex Monteiro, Hawthorne City Council Member
>
> Opponents: None submitted.

Vote threshold: Majority Vote 50%+1.

## What's present and what's missing

- **Full ordinance text**: present (`staff-report.pdf`, Ordinance No. 2264, Exhibit A, Sections 1-3 amending Muni. Code Ch. 3.40).
- **Resolution placing the measure on the ballot**: present (Resolution No. 8613, with the exact ballot question).
- **Impartial analysis**: missing — Resolution 8613 §5 directs the City Attorney to prepare one; not found online (`cityofhawthorne.org` returns HTTP 403 to every direct GET, Akamai edge block).
- **Arguments for/against and rebuttals**: not located as standalone documents; not found on the blocked main city site.
- **Staff report fiscal detail** (in the staged file, not a separate category): TOT increase projected to raise ~$3.1M/yr, bringing total TOT collections to ~$10.57M/yr; staff cite a projected structural General Fund deficit beginning FY2035-36 absent new revenue; neighboring cities charge 13-15.5% TOT.
