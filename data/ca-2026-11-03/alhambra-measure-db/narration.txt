Measure DB asks the voters of this city whether to adopt the council's ban on data centers as a voter-approved law.
The City Council has already banned data centers throughout the city.
Data centers are facilities used mainly to process, store and transmit electronic data.
As things stand, the council could change or repeal that ban on its own.
The City Council placed Measure DB on the ballot.
Measure DB would adopt the ban as a law of the voters, stating that data centers are prohibited in all zones.
Only the voters could then change or repeal the ban.
Measure DB needs a majority yes vote to pass.
The city attorney's impartial analysis does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
The official county list of measures, which names who filed each argument, says no argument against Measure DB was submitted.
Supporters say data centers can use millions of gallons of water, and so much electricity that they strain the power grid.
A yes vote adopts the ban as a voter-approved law that only voters could change.
A no vote leaves the council's ban in place, which the council could later change.
