## Ballot question and supporters/opponents (verbatim, from the county's final measures list)

> Data Centers Prohibited. Shall an ordinance be adopted affirming the Council adopted ban on "Data Centers" by amending the Alhambra Municipal Code to again define "Data Centers" and prohibit them in all zones of the City?
>
> Supporters: Jeffrey K. Maloney; Adele Andrade-Stadler; Ross Maza; Noya Wang; Katherine Lee
>
> Opponents: None submitted.

Vote threshold: Majority Vote 50%+1. Council placed it on the ballot July 27, 2026.
