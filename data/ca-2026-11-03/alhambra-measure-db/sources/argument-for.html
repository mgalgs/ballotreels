ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE DB

VOTE YES ON MEASURE DB

The City Council banned data centers in Alhambra by adopting an ordinance that does not list them as allowed uses in any zone in the City. This measure would further strengthen that ban by preventing a change to that ban without the consent of Alhambra voters.

Protect Our Water
Data centers can use millions of gallons of water to keep their computers cool. Our precious water should be used for homes, schools, and parks instead.

Keep Our Air Clean and Healthy
Data centers use massive amounts of electricity, adding stress to the power grid and may increase costs for residents. They require massive backup generators which can pollute our air. We need to do everything we can to keep our air clean for our children, our seniors, and our general public health.

Protect Our Neighborhoods
Data centers create noise from cooling systems which run day and night. They bring little positive benefit to our communities and they can stress our infrastructure and public services even further.

Preserve Our Quality of Life
Data centers take up valuable land but give little back to our community. They pay minimal taxes and create few local jobs as they require few onsite workers at the facilities.

Protect Future Generations
Once we allow data centers, it is very hard to remove them. A YES vote protects our city for the long term.

The Choice Is Clear
If you want to protect our water, air and neighborhoods, vote YES.

If you want to keep our community safe and healthy, vote YES.

It's simple: Vote YES on Measure DB to stop data centers in Alhambra. Your YES vote protects our community now and for future generations.
