## Facts, with quotes

- **Question:** "Shall the City Charter be amended to: establish a capital infrastructure plan and two-year budget cycle for the City; increase the authority of the Director of Public Works and allow for the elimination of or changes to the Board of Public Works; remove restrictions prohibiting the City from engaging in business enterprises and mortgaging City-owned property; and change certain contracting rules and procedures?"
- **Who votes / threshold:** City of Los Angeles voters; RR/CC list p. 11 "Majority Vote 50%+1". Impartial Summary: "This measure will become effective if approved by a majority of voters."
- **Impartial Summary:** "This measure would require the Mayor to submit a proposed two-year budget to the City Council (Council) by April 1." "The measure would require the City to establish a CIP by ordinance." "The measure adds an exception to these rules for software services or maintenance contracts obtained from the software manufacturer." "The measure also authorizes the Council, by ordinance, to make changes to or eliminate the Board." "Currently, the Charter prohibits the City from mortgaging City-owned property and prevents the City from operating purely commercial or industrial businesses. The measure would remove these restrictions"
- **Financial Impact:** "Transitioning to the two-year budget cycle and establishing a capital infrastructure plan will require an estimated $4 million to $6 million in one-time costs" "The financial and revenue impacts of the remaining charter amendments—including contracting changes, property mortgages, and business enterprises—cannot be quantified at this time."
- **Argument against** signer: "MONICA RODRIGUEZ Los Angeles City Councilwoman, District 7". **Rebuttal to argument against** signers: Kenneth Mejia (Controller), Katy Yaroslavsky, Bob Blumenfield, Jessica Meaney (Investing in Place), Ron Galperin, David Levitus (LA Forward).
