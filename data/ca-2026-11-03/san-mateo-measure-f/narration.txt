City of San Mateo Measure F asks voters in the city whether to update the City Charter for district elections.
Today, the City Charter says voters citywide elect the City Council.
Since 2022, under a 2021 city ordinance, voters in each district have elected their own council member.
The City Council placed Measure F on the ballot, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
The Charter would say council members are elected by district.
The city would have 60 days to fill a council vacancy, instead of 30 days.
The Council would set its meeting times by resolution, and outdated rules would be removed.
The published measure documents of San Mateo County do not include a cost estimate for the measure.
No argument against Measure F appears in the published measure documents of San Mateo County.
Supporters say decades-old parts of the Charter are outdated, and the changes raise no taxes.
A yes vote approves Measure F and its Charter changes.
A no vote rejects Measure F and its Charter changes.
