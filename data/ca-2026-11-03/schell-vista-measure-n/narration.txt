Measure N asks the voters of this fire protection district whether to keep its higher spending limit for four more years.
The state constitution sets a yearly limit on a government's spending of tax money, which voters may raise for up to four years at a time.
In 2018, district voters approved a special tax for fire and ambulance services, and raised the limit so the district could spend it.
Voters continued the higher limit in 2022.
Measure N needs a simple majority to pass.
Measure N would let the district keep spending all of the special tax through the 2029-2030 fiscal year.
The impartial analysis says that if Measure N fails, the limit would be reduced, reducing the funds for the district's current level of service.
The county voter guide does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Supporters say Measure N is not a new tax, and simply lets the fire department keep using voter-approved funding.
No argument against Measure N was submitted for the county Registrar of Voters' printed voter guide pages on this measure.
A yes vote continues the higher spending limit for four more years.
A no vote is a vote against continuing the higher limit.
