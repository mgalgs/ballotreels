## Facts and quotes

**Ballot question (p1):** "Shall the Schell Vista Fire Protection District adopt Ordinance No. 2026-01 retaining the District's increased limits on appropriations to assure continued fire, rescue and emergency medical services?"

**Vote needed:** "Simple Majority Needed to Pass" (p1); "If approved by a majority of those voting, Measure N would permit the District to maintain its current spending limitation for another four years." (p2)

**Who votes:** voters of the Schell-Vista Fire Protection District.

**Impartial analysis (p2), key statements:**
- "The Schell Vista Fire Protection District ("District") has called a special election to ask voters to increase the District's appropriations (spending) limit for four years, the maximum period allowed by law."
- "In 2018, District voters approved a special tax ("Special Tax") to support obtaining, furnishing, operation, and maintaining ambulance and fire suppression equipment and apparatus ... The voters approved the continuation of the spending limit increase for additional four-year periods in 2022."
- "If approved, this measure will continue the District's authority to spend the Special Tax through fiscal year 2029-30 ... If this measure fails, the District's current spending limits will be reduced".
- "Measure N does not authorize the District to impose a new assessment or increase the property tax rate."
- Signed Joshua A. Myers, County Counsel, by Ethan Pawson, Deputy County Counsel.
- Note: one sentence of the printed analysis says "The District Board of Directors has placed Measure A on the ballot" — as printed; the measure is lettered N everywhere else.

**Argument in favor (p2):** names Kenneth C. Finn, Jr (Board Member - SVFPD), Juan Velasquez (Engineer at SVFPD), Ray Mulas (Chief of Department), Jack Todeschini (Capt SVFPD), Hal Stober (Board Member - SVFPD). Opens: "Measure N is not a new tax."

**Argument against:** "No Argument Was Submitted Against Measure N" (p2).
