Soledad Mission Recreation District Argument in Favor of Measure U

For more than 60 years, the Soledad-Mission Recreation District has served local families using essentially the same primary local funding approved in 1962. While the costs of lifeguards, insurance, utilities, maintenance, recreation programs, and other services and supplies have continued to rise, local funding has not kept pace.

Generations of Soledad families have felt the difference. Kids learn to swim at the same pool their parents did. Seniors still gather for water aerobics at the same facilities their neighbors have used for decades. Continuing these programs and services ongoing investment in the aging pool, park, and equipment.

Without additional local funding, it will become increasingly difficult to maintain affordable swim lessons, youth sports and art programs, senior programs, and other recreation services our community relies on.

This measure asks property owners for about 3.5 cents per square foot of building space. Revenue may only be used for the purposes identified in the measure, including keeping recreation programs affordable, maintaining swim instruction and youth and toddler sports, including T- ball, softball, soccer, and tumbling, improving accessibility for residents with disabilities, attracting and retaining qualified recreation staff, and improvements to Rotary Park and Soledad Pool facilities.

All funds raised remain local and may only be used for the purposes described in this measure. Independent citizen oversight and annual public reporting will help ensure funds are spent as promised.

Please vote YES to help keep Soledad's pool open, maintain our park, and preserve affordable recreation programs for local families, seniors, and future generations.
