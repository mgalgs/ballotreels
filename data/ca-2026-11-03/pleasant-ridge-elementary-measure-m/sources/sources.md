## Key facts, quoted

- **Ballot question** (p. 56, "55% Majority Needed to Pass"): "To update local elementary and middle schools, replace leaky roofs and failing heating/ cooling systems, improve student and school safety, weatherproof schools to avoid mold and dry rot, and qualify for matching funds from the State, shall Pleasant Ridge Union Elementary School District's measure authorizing $19,800,000 in bonds, levying approximately $22.50 per $100,000 assessed value, generating on average $1,400,000, annually, while bonds are outstanding, with citizen oversight and audits, all funds staying local and none for administrator salaries, be adopted?"
- **Analysis** (p. 57; unsigned in the guide): "Measure M was placed on the ballot by the Board of Trustees of the Pleasant Ridge Union Elementary School District ("District"). This Measure, if approved by 55% of voters voting on the Measure, would authorize the District to issue and sell up to $19,800,000 in general obligation bonds." "Approval of the bond measure does not guarantee any particular project will be completed or funded beyond the local revenues generated by the Measure."
- **Yes/No:** "A 'yes' vote is a vote in favor of authorizing the Pleasant Ridge Union Elementary School District to issue and sell up to $19,800,000 in general obligation bonds." / "A 'no' vote is a vote against authorizing …"
- **Tax rate statement** (p. 62, signed "/s/ Gregg S. Motarjeme, Superintendent, Pleasant Ridge Union Elementary School District"): average "$22.50 per $100,000 of assessed valuation … collected until fiscal year 2052-53"; highest "$22.50 per $100,000 … projected to apply during each fiscal year that bonds are outstanding"; total debt service "approximately $37,050,000".
- **For signers** (p. 56): John "Mac" Young, Chairman, Nevada County Republican Party; John Baggett, Nevada County Superintendent of Schools Elect; Edie Kodet, Former PRSD Parent, Former PTC President; Emil Baldoni, Owner, Weiss Brothers Nursery; 50-Year Alta Sierra Resident; Chris Roberts, Principal, Bear River High School; Former PRSD Parent.
- **Against:** "No argument was submitted against Measure M".
