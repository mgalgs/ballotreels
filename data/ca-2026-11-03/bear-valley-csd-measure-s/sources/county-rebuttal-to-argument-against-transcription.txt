Rebuttal to Argument Against Measure S

If the existing police tax isn't outdated, why is the BVCSD Board of Directors intending to call a special election in March for a new police tax measure? The answer is, they want two taxes. There shouldn't be two taxes for the same thing. There should be one tax, and voters should get to decide it.

Measure S prevents Bear Valley from having multiple confusing tax measures for police services. Measure S affords voters the opportunity to accept or reject a single, consolidated police tax when the Board of Directors proposes a modern replacement tax measure in March.

BVCSD Board members have insisted the upcoming modernized tax measure must include citizen oversight. Measure S thus ensures all police tax money will be protected by citizen oversight.

Voting YES on Measure S repeals the existing legacy police tax, ensuring voters will get to decide on a single, modernized police tax protected by citizen oversight as the Board of Directors moves forward.

Yes on Measure S.
