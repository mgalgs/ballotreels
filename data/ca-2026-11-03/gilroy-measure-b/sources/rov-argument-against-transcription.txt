Argument against Measure B – Gilroy Hotel Tax Increase

The Gilroy City Council wants voters to approve a huge, 31 PERCENT tax hike by saying that we, the residents, won't have to pay it.

But the City wants to dramatically increase the cost of visitors’ coming here for business or pleasure. Forever.

Councilmembers are basically saying they prefer to discourage people from coming to Gilroy to spend their business dollars here, and/ or to visit us, their family and friends. Even when they’ve traveled here to help while we’re in the hospital!

Does that make any sense?

Vote NO.

The ballot question says the tax hike would fund “critical” “street/pothole repair … emergency preparedness…, and police and fire response." Which begs the question:

Why aren't critical police, pothole, and fire emergency services covered by the current budget?

Don’t trust politicians!

The budget demonstrates our representatives’ priorities.

Why in the world aren’t those core services (maintaining streets, police, fire response) the highest priority?

Measure B’s tax increase wouldn’t be needed, if only councilmembers would safeguard our streets, police, and fire services in the regular, annual budget.

But those improvements are not in the city budget, so clearly, they do NOT see them as essential and not needed according to the City Council.

Don’t we want more business from out-of-town visitors?

Vote NO!

Send a message to councilmembers:
Those core services are vital to us residents. Don’t treat them as a mere afterthought, and penalize our visitors!

Vote NO on Measure B!
