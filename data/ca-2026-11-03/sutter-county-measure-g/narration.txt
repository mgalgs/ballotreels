Measure G asks the voters of this county whether to add a 1 percent sales tax.
The tax would apply across the county, inside its cities and outside them.
The state would collect it, like today's sales taxes, and pay it to the county and two of its cities under an agreement.
As a general tax, the money would go to the county general fund and could pay for any county purpose.
If a majority votes yes, the board of supervisors may then adopt the tax, to start no earlier than April 1, 2027.
The impartial analysis estimates $22.5 million a year, with the tax in place until voters repeal it.
Opponents say the listed uses are not binding, future boards could spend it on anything, and it hits seniors and low-income families hardest.
Supporters say it protects fire and emergency response, patrols, water and roads, and that all of it stays local, with audits and oversight.
A yes vote is in favor of approving the ordinance that establishes the tax.
A no vote is against approving the ordinance that establishes the tax.
