City of Berkeley Measure W asks voters in the city whether to change election rules in the City Charter.
The City Council placed Measure W on the ballot, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
The candidate filing fee would drop from $150 to the state amount, now $25.
Candidate statements would no longer have to list a place of birth or residence.
Up to $400,000 of public financing money, instead of $250,000, could pay to run the program each four-year cycle.
Recall, initiative and referendum rules, and the timelines for filling vacancies, would also change.
The Alameda County guide does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Supporters say Measure W corrects outdated deadlines and reduces barriers to running for office.
No argument against Measure W was submitted to appear in the Alameda County guide.
A yes vote amends the City Charter with these changes.
A no vote leaves the City Charter as it is.
