Beaumont Unified School District Measure B asks the district's voters whether to approve $86 million in school bonds.
The district would borrow the money and repay it from taxes on property in the district.
Measure B needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would go to listed projects, such as repairing roofs, plumbing and air conditioning, and upgrading classrooms and science labs.
The district's tax statement estimates a tax of $20 per $100,000 of assessed value, on average and at most, through 2056-2057.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $178 million.
Supporters say Measure B would repair aging schools and leaky roofs without increasing taxes.
No argument against Measure B was filed for the San Bernardino County voter guide.
A yes vote is a vote to let the district issue the bonds.
A no vote is a vote to stop the district issuing the bonds.
