Measure K asks the voters of this city whether to tax cannabis businesses on what they take in.
Measure K would not, by itself, permit any cannabis business to operate in the city.
The fiscal analysis says the rate would start at 5 percent for one year; the city council could then raise it, up to 10 percent.
As a general tax, the money could pay for any city purpose, until the voters or the council repeal it.
Measure K needs a simple majority to pass.
The ballot question estimates about $280,000 a year, and the fiscal analysis estimates about $260,000 a year.
The fiscal analysis adds that the first year may bring in less, and the cost of running the tax is not yet known.

No argument against Measure K appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
A yes vote approves the cannabis business tax, up to 10 percent.
A no vote does not approve the cannabis business tax.
