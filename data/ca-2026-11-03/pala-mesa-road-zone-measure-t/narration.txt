Pala Mesa Road Zone Measure T asks the zone's voters whether to replace its road assessment with a special tax on each parcel.
Today, the zone's 305 developed and 40 undeveloped parcels pay an assessment set by a county ordinance.
Measure T needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
Measure T would replace the assessment with a yearly tax of up to $1,786 per developed parcel and $893 per undeveloped parcel.
The tax would pay for ongoing road repair and maintenance, including administrative costs, starting in the 2027-2028 fiscal year.
The tax could rise by up to 5 percent a year, following a construction cost index.
The impartial analysis estimates the tax would raise $580,450 in its first year.
No argument for Measure T was filed with the Registrar of Voters.
No argument against Measure T was filed with the Registrar of Voters.
A yes vote is in favor of replacing the existing assessment with the special tax for road repair and maintenance.
A no vote is against replacing the existing assessment with that special tax.
