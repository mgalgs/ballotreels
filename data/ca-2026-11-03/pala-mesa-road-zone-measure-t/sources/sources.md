## Facts and the passages that establish them

### Ballot question

S1 PDF p. 82 (same wording in S3 Section 5):

> MEASURE T
> Shall the County of San Diego, beginning Fiscal Year 2027-28 and annually thereafter, levy a special tax of up to $1,786.00 per developed / $893.00 per undeveloped parcel within Permanent Road Division No. 1000, Zone No. 13A – Pala Mesa Zone A, replacing the existing assessment? The tax may be adjusted by up to 5% annually based on the Los Angeles Construction Cost Index or a comparable Southern California index and remain until modified or dissolved by law.
> Tax – YES / Tax – NO

### Vote required

S1 PDF p. 82: "This measure requires 2/3rds Majority of those voting on the measure."

### Who votes on it

S3 Section 5 (p. 3):

> the Board of Supervisors hereby calls and orders an election to be conducted in the Permanent Road Division No. 1000, Zone No. 13A – Pala Mesa Zone A and be consolidated with the November 3, 2026 Gubernatorial General Election, for the purpose of submitting the proposed Ordinance, attached as Exhibit A and incorporated herein by reference, to the registered voters of said PRD Zone.

S3 recital (p. 2): "the Board of Supervisors intends to levy the special taxes authorized by this ballot measure only in the event that two-thirds of the votes cast by the registered voters within the PRD Zone, voting on said special tax ballot measure for road maintenance, approve said measure." Section 6(b): "there shall be a single precinct consisting of the area within the PRD Zone."

In short: the registered voters of Permanent Road Division No. 1000, Zone No. 13A – Pala Mesa Zone A.

### Who placed it on the ballot

S1 PDF p. 82: "The Board of Supervisors for the County of San Diego placed this Measure on the ballot."

S3 p. 5: "ON MOTION of Supervisor Desmond, seconded by Supervisor Anderson, the above Resolution was passed and adopted by the Board of Supervisors, County of San Diego, State of California, on this 24th day of June 2026, by the following vote: AYES: Aguirre, Anderson, Montgomery Steppe, Desmond; NOT PRESENT: Lawson-Remer".

S3 recitals (p. 1) give the history: CSA 13 – Pala Mesa formed October 28, 1968; CSA 13A – Pala Mesa Zone A formed October 6, 1970; on June 21, 2000 the Board transferred CSA zones to Permanent Road Division No. 1000 and adopted Ordinance No. 9171; "the Department of Public Works conducted community outreach on June 30, 2025, and subsequently distributed an unofficial survey to residents in February 2026".

### Impartial analysis

S1 PDF p. 82, "COUNTY COUNSEL IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS / PERMANENT ROAD DIVISION ZONE 13A PALA MESA SPECIAL TAX MEASURE". Key statements:

> If this Measure is approved by two-thirds of the votes cast, parcels within Permanent Road Division Zone 13A Pala Mesa shall pay a maximum parcel tax of $1,786 per parcel for each developed parcel and $893 per parcel for each undeveloped parcel commencing in Fiscal Year 2027-2028. The stated purpose of the special tax is to fund ongoing road repair and maintenance, which includes administrative expenses.

> The special tax will replace the existing assessment imposed in the Permanent Road Division by repealing Ordinance No. 9171. Total estimated revenue derived from the special tax from the 305 developed and 40 undeveloped parcels currently in the Permanent Road Division for the first fiscal year is $580,450.

> A "YES" vote is in favor of authorizing the County of San Diego to replace the existing assessment with a special tax of up to $1,786 per developed parcel and up to $893 per undeveloped parcel to fund ongoing road repair and maintenance within the Permanent Road Division.

### Arguments and rebuttals

S1 PDF p. 82: "NO ARGUMENTS WERE FILED WITH THE REGISTRAR OF VOTERS." No argument for or against and no rebuttals are printed.

### Full text — NOT ARCHIVED

The guide does not print the ordinance; it says "If you desire a copy of the ordinance or measure, please call the district elections official's office at 858-505-7260". S3 refers to "the proposed Ordinance, attached as Exhibit A", but **the ROV's copy of S3 has only the 5 resolution pages, without Exhibit A**. S3 Section 4 also says "A special tax report is on file with the Department of Public Works." Neither document is archived. They would likely be in the Board of Supervisors' June 24, 2026 agenda materials (item 07); unconfirmed.

### Tax rate statement or fiscal analysis

No separate statement. The fiscal facts are County Counsel's (above): $1,786 / $893 per parcel, 305 developed and 40 undeveloped parcels, $580,450 in the first year, up to 5% a year adjustment.
