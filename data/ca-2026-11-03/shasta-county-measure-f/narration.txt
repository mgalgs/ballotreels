Measure F asks the voters of this county whether to add a rule for filling vacancies in elected department head offices to the county charter.
The elected department heads are the assessor-recorder, county clerk and registrar of voters, sheriff-coroner, auditor-controller, district attorney, and treasurer-tax collector.
Today, under state law, the board of supervisors fills a vacancy in an elected county office by appointment.
Measure F would have the board fill a vacancy by election, at the next regularly scheduled election, if one falls within 12 months.
If no election falls within 12 months, the board would appoint someone through open recruitment, by a four-fifths vote.
Measure F needs a majority of the vote to pass.
The county voter guide does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Opponents say the change does not guarantee voters a say in a replacement, and frequent elections for vacancies are expensive.
Supporters say the amendment gives the public the chance to elect people to critically important county offices at every opportunity.
A yes vote would let voters choose sooner who serves in an elected department head office after an unexpected vacancy.
A no vote means the board keeps its current ability to fill all vacancies of elected county officers by appointment.
