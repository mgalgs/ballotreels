- Facts:
  - Background (staff report): "In 2026, an initiative petition concerning
    concentrated animal feeding operations ("CAFOs") qualified for City Council
    consideration ... The City Council subsequently adopted the ordinance without
    submitting the measure to the voters." and "Section 9217 provides that an
    initiative ordinance adopted by the legislative body may not be repealed or
    amended except by a vote of the people unless the ordinance itself provides
    otherwise."
  - Ballot question (Res. 2026-95 §4): "Shall the measure authorizing the McFarland
    City Council, after a duly noticed public hearing, to amend, revise, extend,
    supplement, or repeal provisions of Chapter 17.200 of the McFarland Municipal
    Code, relating to concentrated animal feeding operations, be adopted?"
  - Passage (§5): "The measure shall be approved if it receives a majority of the
    votes cast on the measure."
  - Full text (Exhibit A, new §17.200.120): "the City Council of the City of
    McFarland is hereby authorized to amend, revise, extend, supplement, or repeal
    any provision of Chapter 17.200 of the McFarland Municipal Code by ordinance,
    provided that any such ordinance is adopted following a duly noticed public
    hearing."
  - Short title: "This measure shall be known as the CAFO Ordinance Council
    Amendment Authorization Act."
