Measure P asks the voters of this city whether the City Council may amend the city law on concentrated animal feeding operations.
The council adopted that law after a citizen initiative petition qualified.
Under state election law, the council cannot amend it without another vote of the people.
All five council members voted to place Measure P on the ballot.
Today, that law prohibits new operations, such as those keeping dairy cattle, and phases out existing ones.
Measure P would let the council amend, revise, extend, supplement or repeal that law by ordinance.
Any change would first need a public hearing, with proper notice.
Measure P itself would not change the current rules.
Measure P needs a majority vote to pass.
The county elections page for this election does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
No argument for Measure P appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
No argument against Measure P appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
A yes vote lets the council change the animal feeding operations law itself, after proper notice.
A no vote keeps the rule that any change to that law needs the approval of voters.
