Measure P asks the voters of this city whether to raise the hotel tax from 8 percent to 12 percent.
Today, visitors pay an 8 percent tax on stays of 30 days or less at hotels, motels, inns and other short-term rentals.
Measure P would raise the rate to 12 percent, until voters end the tax.
As a general tax, the money would go into the city general fund, for any general fund purpose.
Measure P needs a majority of the vote to pass.
The ballot label estimates that the measure would provide about $512,000 a year for general city services.
No argument against Measure P was filed, the impartial analysis says.
No argument for Measure P was filed, the impartial analysis says.
A yes vote raises the hotel tax from 8 percent to 12 percent.
A no vote keeps the hotel tax at 8 percent.
