Measure Q asks the voters of this city whether to replace its short-term rental registration rules with a new permit system.
Today, the owner of any single-family home in the city can register it for rentals of under thirty days.
Voters adopted those rules by petition, so only voters can change them.
Measure Q would require a permit to rent out a whole home, or rooms where a host lives.
Whole-home permits would be capped at 2 percent of the homes in the city, and 1 percent for accessory units.
No new permit could go within 300 feet of another permitted rental.
The city council could amend the new rules.
The voter guide for this county does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Opponents say Measure Q puts broad new restrictions on responsible homeowners, and could send visitors and their spending to nearby towns.
Supporters say clear rules let rentals keep operating responsibly, help keep more homes available for local residents, and strengthen public safety.
A yes vote replaces the registration rules with a new permit system and more detailed rules.
A no vote keeps the existing short-term rental registration rules.
