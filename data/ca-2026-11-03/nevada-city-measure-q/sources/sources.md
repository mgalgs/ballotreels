## Key facts, quoted

- **Ballot question** (p. 70): "Nevada City Residential Short-Term Rental Measure Shall the measure that replaces the existing residential short-term rental registration rules with a new permitting system and more detailed permit eligibility and operating rules be adopted?"
- **Threshold:** "BALLOT QUESTION (50% plus 1 Vote Majority Needed to Pass)".
- **Yes/No** (p. 70): "A 'yes' vote is a vote to replace Nevada City's existing residential short-term rental registration rules with a new permitting system and more detailed rules." / "A 'no' vote is a vote to retain the existing short-term rental registration rules."
- **Current rules** (analysis): "A section of the Nevada City Municipal Code currently allows the owner of any single-family residential property in the City to register that property as a "hosted short-term rental." … There is no requirement that a "host" live on the property, but there is a requirement that the owner or their agent live in Nevada County. … Because these rules were proposed by initiative petition, only the voters can amend or repeal these rules."
- **Change:** "Measure Q repeals the Municipal Code section and adopts new rules that can be amended by the City Council." "Under Measure Q, it would be illegal to advertise, maintain, or operate a short-term rental unless the owner has obtained a short-term rental permit."
- **Caps:** "No new permit … would be issued for any property within 300 feet of another property with a permit. A new whole-home permit would not be issued if it would cause the total number of whole-home permits for main residences to exceed 2% of the City's housing stock or the total number of whole-home permits for accessory dwelling units to exceed 1% of the City's housing stock."
- **Placed by:** "Measure Q was placed on the ballot by the City Council of the City of Nevada City." Analysis signed "Dean Pucci, City Attorney".
- **For signers:** Daniela Fernández, Mayor of Nevada City; Adam Kline, Councilmember.
- **Against signers:** John "Mac" Young, Chairman, Nevada County Republican Party; David Vertin, Pauline Halstead, Curtis Brown (Nevada City Residents); Michael Taylor, Nevada County Resident.
- All four argument/rebuttal texts are printed (pp. 72–74).
