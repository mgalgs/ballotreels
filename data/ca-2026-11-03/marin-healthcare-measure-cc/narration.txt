Measure CC asks the voters of this healthcare district whether to adopt a 30-year parcel tax on buildings for emergency, intensive care and hospital facilities.
Measure CC is a citizens' initiative, placed on the ballot by a voter petition.
The tax would be charged each year on each square foot of building area on a parcel, for 30 years.
The money could expand and modernize intensive care, trauma, triage and hospital facilities, and add emergency room and patient room capacity.
It could also pay to hire and keep doctors and nurses, but not administrator salaries.
A committee of district residents would report each year on whether the money is spent as the measure requires, with a yearly independent audit.
As an initiative, Measure CC needs a simple majority to pass.
The district estimates the tax would raise about $12.4 million a year on average.
Opponents say charging by the square foot makes a modest house pay like a mansion, with no senior or low-income exemption, atop current bills.
Supporters say the hospital is the only trauma center in this county, and without investment it risks falling behind safety standards and losing services.
A yes vote adopts the parcel tax for 30 years.
A no vote does not adopt the tax.
