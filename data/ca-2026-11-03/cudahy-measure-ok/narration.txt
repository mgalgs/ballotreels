Measure OK asks the voters of this city whether to keep an existing local sales tax of three-quarters of a percent, with no end date.
Voters approved the tax in 2018, and it is set to expire on March 31, 2029.
The City Council placed Measure OK on the ballot, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
Today, the tax is paid on top of the countywide sales tax, on most goods and restaurant meals sold in the city.
The measure would remove the 2029 end date, so the tax would continue until voters repeal it.
Prescription drugs and most groceries would stay untaxed.
The money would keep going only to the city, for any public purpose.
The ballot question says the tax would provide about $1.3 million a year for local use.
Supporters say the measure is no new tax or higher rate, and without it the city would lose local funding for everyday services.
The official county list of measures for this election says that no argument against Measure OK was submitted, by any group or person.
A yes vote continues the tax until voters repeal it.
A no vote leaves the tax to expire in March 2029.
