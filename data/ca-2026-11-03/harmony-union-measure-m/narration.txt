Measure M asks the voters of this school district whether to approve an education parcel tax of $75 a year for four years.
A parcel tax charges the same amount on each parcel of property in the district that gets its own property tax bill.
Measure M needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The money would fund a farm-to-table garden program at district schools, student mental health services, and extra help in reading and math.
The tax would start in the 2027-2028 fiscal year and last four years.
Homeowners 65 or older, and some homeowners with disabilities, could apply to be exempt.
The impartial analysis estimates that Measure M would raise about $177,600 a year.
Supporters say state funding alone is not enough, and stable local funding keeps the programs that help every student succeed.
No argument against Measure M was submitted for the county Registrar of Voters' printed voter guide pages on this measure.
A yes vote approves the parcel tax of $75 a year per parcel, at a fixed rate for four years.
A no vote is a vote against the parcel tax.
