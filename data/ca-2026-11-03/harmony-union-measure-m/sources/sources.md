## Facts and quotes

**Ballot question (p1):** "To maintain and enhance quality education programs by funding Harmony Union School District's Garden Enrichment Program at Harmony and Salmon Creek School, a comprehensive farm-to-table interactive education program, as well as student mental health and academic intervention services, shall Harmony Union School District's education parcel tax measure be adopted, at the rate of $75 per year (raising approximately $177,600 annually), for four years, with an exemption for those 65 years and older and all funds spent on local students?"

**Vote needed:** "2/3 (Two-Thirds) Needed to Pass" (p1); "If approved by two-thirds (2/3) of those voting, Measure M would permit the District to raise revenue" (p2).

**Who votes:** voters of the Harmony Union School District ("The special tax would be applied to each unit of real property in the District", p2).

**Impartial analysis (p2), key statements:**
- "Measure M would impose a special tax of $75 per year per parcel, raising approximately $177,600 annually to fund educational programs. This special tax would commence in fiscal year 2027-28 for a period of 4 years."
- "None of the funds could be used for administrator's salaries or pensions."
- Exemptions "may be granted for parcels owned and occupied as a principal residence by persons 65 years of age or older as of July 1 of the tax year, parcels owned and occupied by persons receiving Supplemental Security Income for a disability regardless of age, and persons receiving Social Security Disability Insurance benefits, regardless of age, whose yearly income does not exceed 250% of the 2021 federal poverty guidelines".
- "Measure M was placed on the ballot by the Governing Board of the District."
- Signed Joshua A. Myers, County Counsel, by Ethan Pawson, Deputy County Counsel.

**Argument in favor (p2):** signed Susan F. Kirk (School Neighbor), Yuri Koslen (Board Chair/President Harmony USD), Susan McGovern (Retired HUSD teacher), Colleen Lunardi (Former Employee & Local Historian), Jacqueline Gleason (Harmony Ark Education Foundation).

**Argument against:** "No Argument Was Submitted Against Measure M" (p2).
