Burlingame Elementary School District Measure R asks the district's voters whether to approve $100 million in school bonds.
The district would borrow the money and repay it from a tax on property in the district.
Measure R needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would go to listed projects, such as classrooms and science labs, roofs, plumbing, electrical and safety systems.
The district's tax statement estimates a tax of $16 per $100,000 of assessed value, on average and at most, through 2057-2058.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $195.5 million.
Supporters say Measure R repairs schools over 100 years old, with dry rot and leaky roofs, keeping funds local.
Opponents say voters approved bonds to update classrooms in 2012 and 2016, and bond debt routinely doubles the cost.
A yes vote would let the district issue the bonds.
A no vote would prevent the district from issuing the bonds.
