Measure R asks the voters of this city whether the city treasurer should be appointed instead of elected.
Today, the voters of the city elect the treasurer to four-year terms.
The treasurer prepares reports on the bank deposits and payments of the city, and has other financial duties.
An elected treasurer must live and be registered to vote in the city, and needs no minimum training or qualifications.
The city council, or an officer it names, such as the city manager, would hire, fire and supervise the treasurer.
The treasurer would become a city employee from December 2026, and anyone elected treasurer on this ballot would not be seated.
Measure R needs a simple majority to pass.
The county election documents for Measure R do not include a cost estimate for the measure.
No argument against Measure R was submitted: the county ballot question page lists its opponents as none submitted.
Supporters say staff already do nearly all treasury work, so an elected treasurer adds duplication and election costs.
A yes vote makes the city treasurer an appointed position.
A no vote keeps the city treasurer an elected position.
