## Ballot question (guide p. 795)

> Proposed City Charter Amendment Ballot Measure. Shall the measure amending Section 3-10 of the City Charter to change sales or leases of City real property exceeding one year to be approved by resolution rather than by ordinance and to reduce the City Council approval threshold from supermajority four affirmative votes to simple majority three affirmative votes for leases of City real property not exceeding ten years be adopted?

Transcribed from the guide; line-break hyphens rejoined and the YES/NO box labels omitted.

## Who votes on it

Voters in the City of Alameda. The guide section is headed "CITY OF ALAMEDA MEASURE N", and the Registrar's measure list (<https://alamedacountyca.gov/rov_app/measures/election/260>) files it under that jurisdiction. Vote threshold: see the analysis quotes below.

## Quoted passages

Quotes are from the guide's text layer (decoded; some glyphs in the source fonts lack a Unicode map). Where a quote and the PDF page differ, the PDF page governs.

- p. 795: "CITY ATTORNEY’S IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE N This Measure was placed on the ballot by the City Council of the City of Alameda acting upon the recommendations of an ad-hoc Charter Review Subcommittee."
- p. 795: "If approved by voters, this Measure would amend the Alameda City Charter to 1) allow the City Council to approve acquisitions, transfers (including sales), or leases of City-owned real property by resolution rather than by ordinance, and 2) allow the City Council to approve leases of City-owned real property for terms of less than 10 years by majority rather than supermajority vote."
- p. 795: "Additionally, under the current version of the City Charter, all acts of the Council providing for the acquisition, transfer/ sale, or lease of City-owned real property for a term longer than one year must be approved by a Council supermajority (i.e., at least 4 out 5 Councilmembers voting “yes”)."
- p. 795: "If approved by voters, this Charter amendment would reduce this requirement to a simple majority (i.e., at least 3 out of 5 Councilmembers voting “yes”) for leases with terms 10 years or less."
- p. 795: "However, leases with terms greater than 10 years will still need approval by a Council supermajority."
- p. 795: "A “Yes” vote is a vote to allow Council approval of acquisitions, transfers, or leases of City real property by resolution; and to allow Council approval of leases of less than 10 years by simple majority."
- p. 795: "A “No” vote is a vote to retain the requirement that Council approval of acquisitions, AMN-1 transfers, or leases of City real property must occur by ordinance, and that leases of more than one year but less than 10 years be approved by a Council supermajority."
- p. 795: "This Measure would take effect if approved by a majority of the voters who vote on the Measure."
