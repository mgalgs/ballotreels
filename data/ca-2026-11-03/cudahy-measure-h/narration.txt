Measure H asks the voters of this city whether to require their approval to raise the 3 percent cap on yearly rent increases.
The City Council adopted the rent stabilization law in 2023, and placed Measure H on this ballot.
Today, a landlord may raise the rent on a covered unit once a year, by the change in consumer prices or 3 percent, whichever is lower.
The City Council can amend or repeal the law by a simple majority vote.
Raising the 3 percent cap, or removing it, would need the approval of voters.
Any other change, including a cap below 3 percent, would need two-thirds of the full City Council.
The rent limits, exemptions and procedures in the law would stay as they are.
The county Registrar's list of measures and the impartial analysis do not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Supporters say the measure protects renters from sudden rent hikes, and keeps a future council majority from weakening or repealing the rules.
The official county list of measures for this election says that no argument against Measure H was submitted, by any group or person.
A yes vote requires voter approval to raise the 3 percent cap, and two-thirds of the council for other changes.
A no vote leaves the rent stabilization law as it is.
