ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE H

In Cudahy, rent control isn't just about rent, it's about whether families can afford to stay in the community they call home.

The rent is too high, and with the cost of living on the rise, it is necessary to protect Cudahy's Rent Stabilization Ordinance. Passed in 2023, the City Council introduced local rent control in Cudahy with a maximum 3% annual rent increase.

Supporting local rent control in Cudahy is critical because over 85% of Cudahy's residents are renters, while the average rent in Cudahy is now about $2,400 per month. These high rents are leaving working families, workers, and seniors behind, forcing them to make tough decisions about what they can afford for their households.

Supporting this measure has direct benefits to the community, including protecting renters from sudden, extreme rent hikes and keeping low- and middle- income families in their homes. Additionally, supporting this measure would mean supporting Cudahy's neighborhood stability and preventing economic displacement.

Supporting this measure would lock in protection beyond council control and protect stricter rules from being repealed or weakened by a future City Council majority. If voters were to approve this measure, this would permanently protect rent control in the city of Cudahy.

Vote YES on the Cudahy Rent Stabilization Protection Measure.
