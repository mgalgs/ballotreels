City of Hemet Measure Q asks voters in the city whether to add a one-cent sales tax.
The City Council placed Measure Q on the ballot, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
The 1 percent tax would add one cent for each dollar spent on taxable goods, until voters repeal it.
The money would go into the general fund of the city, for any lawful city program or service.
The sales tax the city already collects under an earlier measure would stay in effect.
The City Attorney's impartial analysis estimates the tax would provide $16 million a year.
Opponents say Hemet has a spending problem, not a revenue problem, and its sales tax would top every city in six counties.
Supporters say Measure Q keeps police and firefighters available, and the city has no more room to cut without hurting public safety.
A yes vote will authorize the 1 percent sales tax.
A no vote will not authorize the 1 percent sales tax.
