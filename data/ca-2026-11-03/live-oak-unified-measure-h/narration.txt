Measure H asks the voters of this school district whether to approve $33.2 million in school bonds.
The district would sell the bonds and repay them from a yearly tax on property in the district.
Measure H needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The bond money could pay only for the projects on the list in the measure.
The ballot question names a new elementary school and upgraded athletic fields for school and community use.
The district's tax rate statement estimates an average tax of $55.62 per $100,000 of assessed value, and at most $59, through 2060-2061.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $67.1 million.
Supporters say elementary classrooms are overcrowded, and that annual audits, citizen oversight and no money for salaries protect taxpayers.
No argument against Measure H appears among the official documents gathered for this video; the pamphlet prints one in favor.
A yes vote is a vote to authorize the $33.2 million in bonds.
A no vote is a vote against issuing the bonds.
