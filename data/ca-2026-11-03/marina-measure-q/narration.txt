Measure Q asks the voters of this city, by simple majority, whether to create a 7 percent utility tax.
The city has no utility users' tax today.
It would tax telecommunications, video, electricity, gas and water charges.
Low-income seniors, and residents in the CARE energy discount program, would pay half.
The ordinance binds this general tax to no specific purpose, and requires yearly audits.
The ballot question estimates about $3.83 million a year.
Supporters say unsafe, outgrown police, fire and city buildings and slow emergency responses need fixing, with every dollar disclosed and audited.
Opponents say this massive utility tax has no project or financing plan, and nothing legally requires spending it on the facilities named.
A yes vote creates the 7 percent utility users' tax.
A no vote means the city does not create the tax.
