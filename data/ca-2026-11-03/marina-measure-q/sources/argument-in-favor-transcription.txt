Vote YES on Measure Q: The City of Marina 911 Emergency, Public Safety, and Community Service Measure.

We are asking Marina voters to approve a Utility Users Tax (UUT) that almost every community around us uses to support critical City services and infrastructure. Three current city buildings are accidents waiting to happen and undermine the safety of Marina residents. We can no longer kick these safety issues down the road.

Marina must fix:

• EARTHQUAKE RISKS: The Public Safety Building housing our Police and Fire Departments fails modern California’s earthquake safety standards. When a major quake hits, our local emergency operations could be completely paralyzed when we need them most.

• LONG 911 RESPONSES: Our city has outgrown the Police/Fire Department building. Our 911 response times to several neighborhoods average nearly 10 minutes—almost double the national five-minute safety standard. Minutes matter when life is on the line!

• DEPLORABLE FACILITIES: Our City Hall and our Council Chambers are in 50-year-old temporary, rotting, portable trailers, stitched together in 1975. They hinder employee recruitment and hurt staff productivity, and they deny equal public access to our neighbors with disabilities. We must do better.

The 7% UUT was recommended by a Citizens Advisory Committee. This local UUT applies to both households and businesses. Businesses will support their fair share of the tax. Relief for low-income residents and qualified seniors is included with a 50% rate reduction.

Every dollar is subject to mandatory public spending disclosures and annual independent audits. All funds must be invested in Marina. Voters retain the power to end the UUT if desired.

Vote YES on Measure Q for a safer, inclusive, and resilient Marina.
