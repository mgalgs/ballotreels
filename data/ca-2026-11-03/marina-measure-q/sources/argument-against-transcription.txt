ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE Q

Stop the Tax Hike: Vote NO on Measure Q

California’s cost of living is crushing working families. Now, this city council wants a massive 7% utility users’ tax on your water, gas, electricity, and phones. This will be the largest UUT among the cities along the coastline of Monterey County and it will bleed the average household of $336 annually. This is a burden that will only explode as utility rates skyrocket.

We all support modern police, fire, and emergency facilities, but we must demand transparency and accountability.

Measure Q forces taxpayers to hand over $3.83 million every single year without a financing plan, a project plan, or a clear evaluation of possibly cheaper alternatives.

In 2025, the City had $29.8 million unassigned fund balance after committed reserves. Why are they taxing you more instead of using these available funds? They haven’t explained or justified this unnecessary raid on your wallet.

Don’t be fooled: this measure absolutely lacks any legal requirement for future councils to spend this money on the emergency facilities mentioned in the ballot text. It’s a blank check that can be spent on anything by any future council with no accountability.

This isn’t about public safety - it’s about preventing a permanent, unchecked financial burden on you.

Demand that the council propose a tax with an end date and that comes with a legally enforceable commitment to spend on city safety needs. We deserve facts and a plan, not empty promises promoted by fear.

Until that happens, the responsible vote is NO on Measure Q.
