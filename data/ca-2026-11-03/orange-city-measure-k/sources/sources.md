## Quoted passages

- Question (p.1): "Shall the proposed Charter for the City of Orange, establishing the City as a charter city under the California Constitution and providing the City’s governing document for municipal affairs, be adopted?"
- Who placed it / threshold (p.7): "The City Council of the City of Orange is submitting Measure K to the voters for consideration at the November 3, 2026 General Municipal Election. If approved by a majority of the voters, Measure K would adopt the proposed Charter for the City of Orange, changing the City from a general law city to a charter city pursuant to Article XI of the California Constitution."
- Analysis (p.7): "The proposed Charter would retain the City’s Council-Manager form of government and continue governance by an elected Mayor and City Council. It would establish lifetime term limits of two four-year terms for Mayor and three four-year terms for Council Members."
- Analysis (p.7): "It would establish an Open Space Reserve consisting of City-owned properties designated by the City Council and would require voter approval before property may be removed from the Reserve or used for purposes inconsistent with the Charter."
- Analysis (p.7): "A “No” vote on Measure K rejects the proposed Charter, and the City of Orange would remain a general law city governed by existing state law."
