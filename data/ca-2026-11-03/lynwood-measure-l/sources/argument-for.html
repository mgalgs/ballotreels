ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE L

VOTE YES ON MEASURE L to require visitors staying in Lynwood motels, hotels, and other short-term lodging to pay their fair share for City services they use.

THE LYNWOOD PUBLIC SAFETY/COMMUNITY PROTECTION MEASURE is a 12% transient occupancy tax paid by motel and hotel guests.

This is NOT a tax on Lynwood residents.

Lynwood currently does not collect a transient occupancy tax. People who stay in motels and hotels use our streets, public safety services, and community facilities without paying for them. Voting YES ensures that visitors pay their fair share of service costs, rather than Lynwood residents carrying the entire burden.

Measure L will generate approximately $751,315 annually in locally controlled funding that supports the services residents prioritize.

Measure L provides funding to support public-safety efforts to address crime and keep Lynwood's streets, public spaces, and neighborhoods safe, clean, and welcoming for residents, families, and businesses—as well as visitors.

Criminal activity associated with drugs, gangs, and human trafficking is not welcome. These locally controlled funds support the services and programs needed to protect our local businesses from such criminal activities and provide for a clean, safe, welcoming business district.

VOTE YES on Measure L to:

- Maintain 911 emergency response/sheriff patrols;
- Prevent and address robberies, burglaries, automobile theft, gangs, illegal drugs and human trafficking;
- Repair streets/potholes;
- Keep parks, neighborhoods/public areas clean and safe;
- Address speeding/unsafe driving near schools and neighborhoods; and
- Support youth, afterschool, recreation, and senior programs.

Measure L protects Lynwood residents. Residents temporarily staying in local lodging because their homes are uninhabitable due to fire, flood, earthquake or emergencies would be exempt.

VOTE YES on Measure L, THE LYNWOOD PUBLIC SAFETY/COMMUNITY PROTECTION MEASURE, for safer neighborhoods, better-maintained streets, clean business districts and parks, strong community programs, and a fair contribution from hotel and motel guests who use Lynwood services.
