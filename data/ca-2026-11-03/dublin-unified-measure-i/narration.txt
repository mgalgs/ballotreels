Dublin Unified School District Measure I asks the district's voters whether to renew its $96 parcel tax and add $48 per parcel.
Today, the district levies a $96 parcel tax that voters passed in 2019, which ends on June 30, 2028.
Measure I needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
On July 1, 2027, a tax of $144 per parcel a year would replace the current tax, for nine years.
The rate would rise each year with inflation, and the money could go only to the school purposes the measure lists.
Owners 65 or older, and some people receiving disability benefits, could apply to be exempt.
The impartial analysis says the district estimates the tax would provide about $2.9 million a year.
No argument against Measure I was submitted for this election's Alameda County Composite Voter Information Guide.
Supporters say Measure I keeps classes small, retains strong teachers, and updates funding unchanged since 2008.
A yes vote renews the parcel tax and adds $48 per parcel.
A no vote lets the expiring $96 parcel tax end in June 2028.
