Measure X asks the voters of this city whether to change the tax that businesses pay for a city business license.
The tax falls on businesses, not on residents or consumers who do not run a business.
Today, most businesses pay a tax per worker, a tax per rental unit, and a gross receipts tax capped at $450 a year.
From 2027, the tax would be based on gross receipts, at a rate for each type of business.
Businesses with $100,000 or less in yearly receipts would pay no tax.
The most a business would pay starts at $1,500 a year, rising 10 percent a year until it reaches $25,000.
Marine terminal businesses would pay 3 percent of gross receipts, with no maximum.
The money could pay for any city purpose, and Measure X needs a simple majority.
The city attorney's impartial analysis estimates first-year revenue at about $1.6 million.
Supporters say Measure X replaces an outdated system with a simpler, fairer one, and over 1,800 businesses stop paying.
No argument against Measure X appears among the measure documents that the county Registrar of Voters has posted online.
A yes vote is a vote in favor of the tax.
A no vote is a vote against the tax.
