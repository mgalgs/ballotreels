Measure CR asks the voters of this city whether to tax cannabis businesses that are not stores.
Today, the city taxes cannabis stores at 4 percent of their gross receipts.
The City Council placed Measure CR on the ballot to tax all cannabis businesses at the same rate, for general city purposes.
Growers, manufacturers, distributors and other cannabis businesses would pay 4 percent of their gross receipts, as stores do.
As a general tax, the money would go to the general fund, for any lawful city purpose.
The tax would start in 2027 and last until ended by voters; it needs a majority yes vote to pass.
The ballot question itself projects $1 million a year in added funding; no separate fiscal analysis was gathered.
Supporters say all cannabis businesses would pay, not just some, and the money cannot be seized.
According to the county registrar's official list of measures, no argument against Measure CR was submitted.
A yes vote approves the 4 percent tax on cannabis businesses that are not stores.
A no vote rejects the 4 percent tax on cannabis businesses that are not stores.
