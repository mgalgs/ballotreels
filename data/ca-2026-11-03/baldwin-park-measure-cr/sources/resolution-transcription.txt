RESOLUTION NO. 2026-023

A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF BALDWIN PARK, CALIFORNIA, SUBMITTING A NON-RETAIL CANNABIS BUSINESS TAX MEASURE TO THE ELECTORATE AT THE NOVEMBER 3, 2026 GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION; REITERATING ITS REQUEST THAT THE ELECTION BE CONSOLIDATED WITH OTHER ELECTIONS TO BE HELD ON THAT DATE; AND TAKING CERTAIN OTHER RELATED ACTIONS

WHEREAS, pursuant to Chapter 129 of the Municipal Code the City currently imposes a cannabis retailer tax upon cannabis retailers; and the rate of that tax is 4% of gross receipts; and

WHEREAS, in the interest of good government, in order to provide additional funding for general municipal purposes, and to tax all cannabis businesses at the same rate, the City Council desires to tax non-retail cannabis businesses, such as manufacturers and cultivators, at the same rate as the cannabis retailer tax; and

WHEREAS, all proceeds of the tax will be deposited in the City's general fund, which funds services such as cleaning up trash and graffiti, fixing streets and potholes, crime prevention, 911 emergency response, and providing afterschool and youth employment programs; and

WHEREAS, by its Resolution Nos. 2026-04 and 2026-05 (the "Election Resolutions"), adopted on June 3, 2026, the City Council called a General Municipal Election for November 3, 2026 (the "Election") and requested the Election be consolidated with the statewide general election held on the same date and administered by the Los Angeles County Registrar-Recorder/County Clerk; and

WHEREAS, at the Election the City Council desires to give the voters the opportunity to vote to approve a 4% cannabis business tax on non-retail cannabis businesses; and

WHEREAS, Los Angeles County continues to try to seize County control over available local revenue sources; and

WHEREAS, all money raised by this measure will stay local to help fund essential services such as cleaning up trash and graffiti, fixing streets and potholes, 911 emergency response, and providing afterschool and youth employment programs here in Baldwin Park, and by law can't be taken by the Federal, State or County governments; and

WHEREAS, the City maintains 119 miles of streets, 221 miles of sidewalks, and 30 acres of parks, and seeks to keep its streets, parks and infrastructure in good condition, safe, and clean; and

WHEREAS, fast emergency response times for 911 calls are critical for saving lives, and funding generated by this measure will help ensure that we have enough law enforcement officers to respond quickly to 911 emergencies.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF BALDWIN PARK AS FOLLOWS:

Section 1. Recitals. The City Council hereby finds and determines that the foregoing recitals are correct.

Section 2. Ballot Measure Submitted. The City Council hereby orders, pursuant to Section 9222 of the Elections Code, that the ordinance attached hereto as Exhibit "A" and incorporated herein by reference, be submitted to the voters at the Election. Adoption of the ordinance requires a majority vote of those voting on the matter.

Section 3. Ballot Question. The question submitted by Section 2 of this Resolution shall appear on the ballot as follows:

[Boxed, with YES and NO boxes beside it:]
Good Government Cannabis Reform Measure To fund general city services in Baldwin Park, such as cleaning trash/graffiti, fixing streets/potholes, crime prevention, 911 emergency response, and providing afterschool/youth employment programs, shall the measure applying the City of Baldwin Park's existing 4% cannabis retailing tax rate to non-retail cannabis businesses, providing a projected $1,000,000 annually in additional locally-controlled funding until ended by voters, that can't be taken by the state, with public independent audits be adopted?

Section 4. Consolidation. Pursuant Section 10400 et seq. of the Elections Code, the City Council reiterates its request set forth in the Election Resolutions that the Los Angeles County Board of Supervisors consolidate the Election with any other elections held on the same day in the same territory or in the territory that is in part the same.

Section 5. Conduct of Election. Pursuant to Section 10002 of the Elections Code, the City Council reiterates its request that the Los Angeles County Board of Supervisors permit the Registrar-Recorder/County Clerk to render all services specified by Section 10418 of the Elections Code relating to the election, for which services the City agrees to reimburse the County, in accordance with current County pro-rations and allocation procedures. The Board of Supervisors is authorized to canvass the returns of the Election pursuant to Section 10411 of the Elections Code. In all matters, not otherwise specified in this Resolution, the Election shall be held as set forth in the Election Resolution. Notice of the time and place of the Election is hereby given and the City Clerk is authorized, instructed and directed to give further or additional notice of the election as required by law and to take any other actions required for the conduct of the election.
