## Ballot question (verbatim, from the county measure list)

> Good Government Cannabis Reform Measure To fund general city services in Baldwin Park, such as cleaning trash/graffiti, fixing streets/potholes, crime prevention, 911 emergency response, and providing afterschool/youth employment programs, shall the measure applying the City of Baldwin Park's existing 4% cannabis retailing tax rate to non-retail cannabis businesses, providing a projected $1,000,000 annually in additional locally controlled funding until ended by voters, that can't be taken by the state, with public independent audits be adopted?

Supporters: Daniel Damian, Lakeside Realty & Home Loans; Javier E. Vargas, BP Bus. Assn. Pres; Cecelia Bernal; Martha Escobar, Chicas Graphics
Opponents: None submitted.

Vote threshold: majority vote, 50%+1 (this is a general tax).

## What's present / missing

- **Full text**: present (`full-text.pdf`, and duplicated as Exhibit A inside `resolution.pdf`).
- **Resolution / ballot question / vote threshold**: present (`resolution.pdf` §§2–3).
- **Tax rate statement**: not applicable — this is a tax-rate measure stated directly in the ballot question (4% rate applied to a new class of business), not a bond with a separate Elections Code §9400 tax rate statement.
- **Impartial analysis**: missing — Resolution §7 directs the City Attorney to prepare one, but no copy was posted on baldwinpark.com or located elsewhere. Status: not found/not fetched (per the resolution, filed materials are available for public examination at the City Clerk's Office, but no online copy exists).
- **Argument for**: filed — the county list's "Supporters" (Daniel Damian, Javier E. Vargas, Cecelia Bernal, Martha Escobar) are its filers — but the filed text itself was not located; the city's Measure CR page links only to a blank filing form, not the filed argument. Status: filed (official) but not found/not fetched.
- **Argument against**: none submitted, per the county list (official — not merely unfound).
- **Rebuttals**: none — consistent with no argument against being filed; the resolution (§8) sets a ≤300-word argument limit and ≤250-word rebuttal limit, with an arguments deadline of 2026-08-05 and a rebuttal deadline of 2026-08-17.
- **Fiscal analysis**: no separate fiscal study found; the $1,000,000/yr estimate appears only in the ballot question itself and is repeated in `faq.pdf` and `staff-report.pdf` without underlying methodology.
