ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE CR

(Baldwin Park Cannabis Measure)

Protect Baldwin Park's 911 services WITHOUT raising taxes on residents or property owners--vote YES on CR!

Los Angeles County continues to try to seize control over funding for local communities like ours. Voting yes on CR maintains local control over our own taxpayer dollars for the benefit of our families and residents.

Yes on CR does NOT tax your home or property. It simply updates an existing city ordinance to ensure all cannabis businesses - not just some – are properly paying their fair share for the city services we all expect and deserve!

YES ON CR KEEPS BALDWIN PARK SAFE AND CLEAN. Every dime raised stays local, to keep Baldwin Park safe and healthy. Yes on CR maintains our fast 911 response times and ensures enough law enforcement officers to respond quickly to emergencies. Yes on CR also:

-Cleans up piles of trash and litter on our streets.
-Keeps 119 miles of streets and 221 miles of sidewalks in safe condition.
-Helps keep our 30 acres of parks clean.
-Removes graffiti and blight.
-Keeps youth safe by providing after-school and employment programs.

YES ON CR IS FISCALLY ACCOUNTABLE. By law, NO MONEY can be seized by Los Angeles County, the state or federal government. Yes on CR generates locally-controlled funding, keeping this money in Baldwin Park.

Yes on CR includes tough fiscal accountability protections, including public independent audits and public disclosure of spending.

Again, measure CR does NOT impact your taxes as a Baldwin Park resident –it applies only to cannabis businesses.

Join us! Vote yes on CR to protect Baldwin Park's quality of life and keep our community a special place to live.
