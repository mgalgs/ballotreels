## Facts, with quotes

**Who votes / threshold.** Final list p. 4: "COUNTY MEASURES – (2) LOS ANGELES COUNTY – (2) VOTE PASSAGE – Majority Vote 50%+". Ordinance Section 1: "A special election is hereby called, proclaimed, and ordered to be held on November 3, 2026, for the purpose of voting upon a proposed amendment to the Charter of the County of Los Angeles." Impartial analysis: "If approved by a majority of voters voting on the Measure, this Measure would: …"

**Ballot label** (ordinance Section 2, "The exact form of the measure as it is to appear on the ballot"):
> LOS ANGELES COUNTY CHARTER AMENDMENT REITERATING GOOD FAITH NEGOTIATION OBLIGATIONS AND ESTABLISHING IMPARTIAL BINDING ARBITRATION FOR CERTIFIED PUBLIC SAFETY EMPLOYEE DISPUTES Shall the measure amending the Los Angeles County Charter to prohibit strikes by district attorney investigators, medical examiners, and lifeguards; their supervisors; and nonadministrative civilian employees working in the Fire Department, Sheriff's Department, and the Department of Medical Examiner, reiterate existing legal obligations for good faith negotiations, and establish an impartial binding arbitration process for resolving labor disputes with certified public safety employee organizations representing County employees including firefighters, sheriffs, and the abovementioned employees, as specified in the charter amendment ordinance, be adopted?

**Impartial analysis, key statements** (`m0001053410e.pdf`):
- "Measure A (Measure) is a proposed amendment to the Charter of the County of Los Angeles (County) placed on the ballot by ordinance and resolution of the County Board of Supervisors."
- "Add Section 47.8 to the County Charter."
- "Prohibit strikes by district attorney investigators, medical examiners and lifeguards, and reiterate the existing prohibition on strikes by deputy sheriffs and firefighters."
- "Submission of the unresolved disputes or controversies to a three-member Board of Arbitrators panel for resolution."
- "If no agreement is reached prior to the conclusion of the arbitration hearings, each Party is directed to submit a final offer of settlement on each remaining issue in dispute."
- "After 30 days, unless extended by mutual agreement, the decision becomes public, final, and is binding on the Parties. No further action by the County Board of Supervisors or by the electorate to confirm or approve the decision is permitted or required."
- "Require the Parties to equally share the cost of arbitration."
- "A "NO" vote is a vote to not amend the Charter … and that the labor negotiation process between the County and those labor organizations will remain unchanged."

**Fiscal impact** (`m0001053475e.pdf`, Oscar Valdez, County Auditor-Controller):
- "This measure does not directly affect County revenues."
- "direct administrative costs of arbitration, including arbitrator fees, legal fees, and staff support, which could total tens of thousands to millions of dollars depending on the complexity and duration of the arbitration."
- "we have no basis on which to predict whether arbitrated outcomes pursuant to this measure would result in higher or lower future labor costs than the existing bargaining process."
- "The County's current annual expenditures for compensation (salaries and benefits) for covered public safety employees are approximately $4.1 billion."

**Argument in favor** signers (`m0001053415e.pdf`): Manuel A. Calderon (Firefighter, LA County Firefighters Local #1014); Wendy Ichikawa (Sheet Metal Worker, LA/OC Building Trades Council); David Gaisford (Deputy Sheriff, Association of LA Deputy Sheriffs).
