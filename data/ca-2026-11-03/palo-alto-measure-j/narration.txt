Measure J asks the voters of this city whether to add a 0.5 percent sales tax for general city services.
Measure J is a general tax: the money would go into the general fund, and could pay for anything the city lawfully does.
In June 2026, the City Council adopted advisory guidelines saying it intends to use the money to buy seven acres of a community center from the school district, and modernize it.
The tax would start in April 2027 and last until voters repeal it.
Measure J needs a simple majority of the vote to pass.
The city estimates the tax would raise about $15.6 million a year.
Supporters say it lets the city buy and renovate a center over 70 years old, with failing plumbing and electrical systems.
Opponents say its real purpose is paying the school district $65.5 million for the land, which the measure does not state.
A yes vote approves a 0.5 percent increase to the local sales tax.
A no vote does not approve the increase.
