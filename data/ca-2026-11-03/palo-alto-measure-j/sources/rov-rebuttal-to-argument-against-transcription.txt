[page 2]
Measure J gives us a once-in-a-generation opportunity to secure Cubberley’s future by acquiring the land containing PAUSD’s Cubberley buildings and modernizing the Center.

PAUSD and the City are separate entities with separate budgets. PAUSD determined it doesn’t need its Cubberley buildings for a future school and agreed to sell the City that land. The parties negotiated a sale based on “fair market value” as state law requires. Once assets are exchanged, our City will own the land and will partner with community organizations to co-fund renovations and improvements to the Center.

By law, 100% of Measure J funds stay in Palo Alto and cannot be taken by the State or Federal government. Opponents try to raise doubts about fiscal responsibility, but the facts are clear: these funds are protected for local use and are subject to strong accountability and public oversight. And, because non-residents shop in Palo Alto, visitors will pay 40% of the sales tax.

Measure J will revitalize aging Cubberley, restoring a vibrant and welcoming space to meet current and future needs with functional, affordable, accessible community spaces for childcare, recreation, education, and the arts for people of all backgrounds, ages, and abilities to enjoy.

We can’t rely on the State or Federal government to fix our problems. For a stronger city, we need to act locally. Our community has demanded real improvements to Cubberley for decades. This is our opportunity to make it happen.

The choice is clear: Let’s put community first - vote Yes on J.
