Vote Yes on Measure J — an affordable solution for a better Palo Alto!

The Cubberley Community Center has been a valuable community asset for decades. Yet it is run-down and the City has been unable to invest in its real potential because we haven’t owned the land. For the first time, PAUSD offered to sell the land, enabling Cubberley to become the outstanding community center it can be, benefiting all while we anticipate large housing growth.

Our community brings together all its diverse parts. Affordable, accessible community spaces, childcare, and activities for families and individuals of all ages, cultures, and income levels to enjoy are critical.

The Cubberley Community Center is over 70 years old, has failing plumbing and electrical systems, and doesn’t meet modern disability access or earthquake standards.

Measure J enables the City to buy the land, cost-effectively renovate buildings, and leverage partnerships with organizations willing to invest millions in major upgrades in recreation, performing arts, and programs for all ages and cultures.

Yes on J will:
- Enable purchase of the 7 acres of buildings owned by PAUSD
- Repair electrical, plumbing, and outdated facilities
- Make upgrades to meet earthquake, accessibility and safety standards
- Enhance greenery and outdoor spaces
- Expand recreation, performing arts, skill-building, affordable childcare, outdoor education, and senior and cultural programming

Cost Effective & Local Benefits
- 100% of Measure J funds stay local; cannot be touched by State or Federal Government
- Lower-cost renovations of existing buildings
- Leverage resources of community partners
- 40% of Measure J sales-tax is paid by nonresidents
- PAUSD receives funds for schools
- 100% of the revenue will be spent locally to benefit our community
- Essentials like groceries and prescription medicine are tax-exempt
- Subject to independent financial audits

Join trusted local leaders and vote Yes on Measure J!
