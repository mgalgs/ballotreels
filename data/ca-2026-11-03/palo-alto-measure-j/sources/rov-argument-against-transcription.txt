ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE J

VOTE NO ON THIS DISHONEST MEASURE

This Measure is an attempt to manipulate voters into approving a tax increase the purpose of which the City knows most Palo Altans don't support.

When pressed by reporters, Councilmember Pat Burt acknowledged that the real purpose of the sales tax increase is to enable the City to pay the Palo Alto School District $65.5 million to purchase the land at Cubberley.

Why doesn't the Measure state this? Because, as City Staff has acknowledged, buying the land didn't poll well when they surveyed residents. In other words, this is a bait and switch, driven by polling data.

The two-thirds of surveyed residents who said they opposed the land purchase were right.

Why should taxpayers, who already fund both the City and the School District, pay $65.5 million more to move public money from one to the other?

We don't even know how the $65.5 million price for the property was determined.

It was established behind closed doors. City Hall refuses even to provide an appraisal of the property.

There is no separate cookie jar at City Hall for the tens of millions this tax increase would raise.

The money can be used for anything. Maybe that's developing Cubberley, maybe that's installing a luxe gym on the executive floor of City Hall.

The proposed tax increase is permanent.

Why permanent, when the land purchase is a one-time expense?

And why a regressive sales tax hike which would hit lower-income families hardest?

Both the Daily Post's editor and the Weekly's government watchdog strongly oppose this Measure.

Please reject Measure J.
