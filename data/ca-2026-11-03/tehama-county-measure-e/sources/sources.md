## Facts with quotes

- **Ballot question (Ordinance No. 2158, Section 2 "Form of Ballot Question"):** "COUNTY CHARTER AMENDMENT — Shall the measure amending the Tehama County Charter to establish the annual salary of members of the Board of Supervisors at the California state minimum wage multiplied by 2,080 hours be adopted?" YES / NO.
- **Who votes:** Ordinance §7.B: "All qualified voters in Tehama County shall be permitted to vote in the special election on the Measure."
- **New Charter text (Ordinance §3.A):** "Notwithstanding any ordinance currently in effect, effective January 1, 2027, the annual salary of each member of the Board of Supervisors shall be equal to the State of California hourly minimum wage multiplied by two thousand eighty (2,080) hours. The salary shall automatically increase, without further action, whenever the State of California minimum wage increases. Except as provided herein, the salary established by this section may not be changed except by approval of the voters at a general election."
- **Vote needed:** Ordinance §3.B: "only after approval by a majority (i.e., 50% + 1) of the qualified voters voting in the special election on the Measure." Analysis: "Approval of Measure E requires 50% plus one of the votes cast."
- **Adopted:** "PASSED AND ADOPTED by the Board of Supervisors of the County of Tehama this 4th day of August, 2026"; AYES: Supervisor Hansen, Supervisor Zane, Vice Chair Jones, Chair Walker; NOES: Supervisor Burroughs.
- **Current salary (analysis):** "The County Charter currently sets the salary of each member of the Board of Supervisors at One Thousand Forty-Five dollars ($1,045.00) per month, or Twelve Thousand Five Hundred Forty dollars ($12,540.00) per year. This salary was last increased by the voters on January 1, 1989."
- **Resulting salary (analysis):** "Based on the current California minimum wage of Sixteen dollars ninety cents ($16.90) per hour, this would result in an annual salary of Thirty-Five Thousand One Hundred Fifty-Two ($35,152.00) per each of the five member."
- **Figure conflict to note:** the argument against says the formula would produce "$36,192 annual base salary ... on January 1, 2027—an approximately 188.6% increase". The County Counsel analysis uses $35,152 at the current $16.90 wage. The two use different wage dates; neither document states the January 1, 2027 minimum wage. Not resolved by an official record here.
- **Charter county:** "Tehama County (the "County") is one of fifteen (15) charter counties in the State of California." (analysis)
- **Yes/No (analysis):** "A "yes" vote is a vote in favor of amending the County Charter to set the annual salary of each member of the Board at the California state minimum wage multiplied by two thousand eighty (2,080) hours. A "no" vote is a vote against amending the County Charter, and in favor of retaining the current salary of One Thousand Forty-Five dollars ($1,045.00) per month for each member of the Board." Signed "Margaret Long, Tehama County Counsel".
- **Argument in favor:** signed "s/ Parker R. Hunt, Treasurer/Tax Collector, Tehama County". Claims inflation "has reduced the purchasing power of the supervisor salary by approximately 63 percent" (proponent's claim).
- **Argument against:** signed "s/ Robert Burroughs, Tehama County Supervisor, District 1, Signing in his individual capacity" and "s/ Fred Ehrensvard, Property Manager". Says supervisors also receive "vehicle and cell phone allowances, County-paid medical, dental, vision and life insurance, the County's share of PERS retirement and deferred compensation contributions, committee-service compensation" (opponents' claim).
- **Rebuttal to argument against:** signed "s/ Greg B. Jones, Jr., Supervisor, District 5". Claims the county pays "less than any county in California" (proponent's claim, not verified).
- **Rebuttal to argument in favor:** signed "s/Robert Burroughs, Tehama County Supervisor, District 1, Signing in his individual capacity".
