Measure E asks the voters of this county whether to tie the pay of county supervisors to the state minimum wage.
Today, the county charter pays each supervisor $1,045 a month, or $12,540 a year.
From 2027, the yearly salary would equal the state hourly minimum wage times 2,080 hours.
At today's minimum wage of $16.90 an hour, the impartial analysis puts that at $35,152 a year.
The salary would then rise automatically whenever the state minimum wage rises.
Measure E needs a majority vote to pass.
The documents posted for Measure E do not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Opponents say supervisors also get benefits beyond their base salary, and that future raises would come without another vote.
Supporters say the salary has not changed since 1989, and that pay this low keeps working residents from serving.
A yes vote sets the salary at the state minimum wage times 2,080 hours.
A no vote keeps today's salary of $1,045 a month.
