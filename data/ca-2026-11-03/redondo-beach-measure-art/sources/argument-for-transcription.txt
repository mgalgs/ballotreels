[Page 4]

Measure ART invests in Redondo Beach's future, modernizing outdated zoning to bring new restaurants, shops, and jobs to our commercial corridors, while strictly protecting our neighborhoods. The commercial investments and redevelopment promoted by Measure ART should also generate more revenue for the City's General Fund without taxing residents.

The centerpiece of Measure ART is the Artesia and Aviation Corridors Area Plan. By redesignating aging commercial strips as Neighborhood Commercial, Measure ART removes the barriers that have kept these corridors stuck for decades — opening the door to new shops, restaurants, and gathering places within walking distance of home.

Measure ART requires developers to build responsibly without overdeveloping:

• Buildings capped at 3 stories, with upper floors stepped back from neighboring homes;
• 70% transparent storefronts, creating a pedestrian-friendly environment — no blank walls facing the sidewalk;
• Quality materials, shade structures, and an inviting streetscape; and
• Rooftop dining allowed only under strict noise and design limits.

Measure ART also provides targeted updates in commercial zoning along portions of Pacific Coast Highway (PCH), clearing the way for the beginning of the corridor's long-overdue revitalization; new investment, updated storefronts, and a more inviting PCH for residents and visitors alike.

These zoning changes aren't giveaways to developers. They are guardrails to ensure growth benefits residents first.

Measure ART also modernizes industrial zoning north of Manhattan Beach Boulevard, raising the maximum building intensity so aerospace and industrial employers who anchor Redondo Beach's jobs economy can invest and grow. No project exceeding our current limits is proposed today. This change simply clears an outdated cap and positions Redondo Beach for tomorrow's aerospace jobs.

The City Council completed extensive environmental review before voting unanimously to bring these changes to you. That's not a rubber stamp. It's years of work and analysis, and now it's time to finish the job.

Redondo Beach has wanted to revitalize North Redondo for decades. Measure ART is a major step toward making that happen.

Vote YES on Measure ART.
