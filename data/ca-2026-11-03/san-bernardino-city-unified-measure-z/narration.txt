San Bernardino City Unified School District Measure Z asks the district's voters whether to approve $345 million in school bonds.
The district would borrow the money and repay it from a tax on property in the district.
Measure Z needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would go to listed projects, such as replacing portable classrooms with permanent ones and building a new school to reduce overcrowding.
The district's tax statement estimates a tax of $45 per $100,000 of assessed value, on average and at most, through 2056-2057.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $670 million.
Supporters say Measure Z delivers essential repairs and safety upgrades to aging local schools.
No argument against Measure Z was filed for the San Bernardino County voter guide.
A yes vote is in favor of letting the district issue the bonds.
A no vote is against letting the district issue the bonds.
