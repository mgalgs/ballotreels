## Quoted facts

- **Who votes:** voters of the San Bernardino City Unified School District.
- **Ballot question** (RES_SBCityUSD.pdf): "To repair/upgrade aging classrooms, labs, career technology, school facilities to support college/career readiness in math, science, engineering, arts, skilled trades; fix/replace deteriorating roofs, plumbing, HVAC, electrical; and upgrade schools to meet current health/safety codes and improve disabled access, shall San Bernardino City Unified School District's measure authorizing $345,000,000 in bonds, at legal rates, be adopted, levying approximately $45 per $100,000 assessed value ($22,000,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding, with oversight, audits, and all money locally controlled?"
- **Impartial analysis** (VIG p. 264, County Counsel): "This Measure was placed on the ballot by a resolution adopted by the Board of Education of the San Bernardino City Unified School District (District). A "yes" vote by at least fifty-five percent of the voters voting on this Measure will authorize the District to issue bonds in the amount of up to $345,000,000 to be used solely for the purposes set forth in this ballot Measure."
- **Tax rate statement** (VIG p. 265): "An election will be held in the San Bernardino City Unified School District (the "District") on November 3, 2026, to authorize the sale of up to $345,000,000 in bonds of the District to finance school facilities as described in the measure."
