## Key facts, quoted

- **Ballot question:** "Shall the measure to levy an annual special tax in the amount of $250.00 per year per parcel within the Showcase Ranches Community Services District commencing in FY 2026-2027 and continuing for an unlimited duration, to be used only for road and dam maintenance services and generating an annual revenue of approximately $37,750, to replace the current special tax of $125.00, be adopted?"
- **Analysis** (p. 97): "Measure Q (the "Measure"), if approved by two-thirds of the voters voting thereon, would authorize the levy of an increased special parcel tax at the rate of $250 per parcel per year within the Showcase Ranches Community Services District (the "District"). This Measure was placed on the ballot by the Board of Directors of the District pursuant to Resolution No. 2026-06-1, which was passed at an official meeting of the Board of Directors on June 25, 2026."
- "… used solely for the purpose of road and dam maintenance services within the District. … expected to generate revenue of approximately $37,750 per year. If the Measure is approved, the special parcel tax would replace an existing special tax of $125 per parcel. If the Measure is not approved, the existing special tax of $125 per parcel will remain in effect."
