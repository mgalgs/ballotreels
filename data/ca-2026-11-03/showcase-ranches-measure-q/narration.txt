Measure Q asks the voters of this community services district whether to raise its yearly tax for roads and dams from $125 to $250 per parcel.
Voters in the district approved today's $125 tax in 1984 for road, dam and levee maintenance.
The district's board of directors placed Measure Q on the ballot.
Measure Q needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The new tax would start in the 2026-2027 fiscal year.
An owner of several parcels would pay the new tax on each extra parcel only if a home is built on it.
The money could pay only for road and dam maintenance, and the tax would last until voters change or repeal it.
If Measure Q fails, the $125 tax stays.
The county attorney's impartial analysis expects the tax to raise about $37,750 a year.
No argument against Measure Q was submitted.
No argument for Measure Q was submitted.
A yes vote authorizes the increased tax of $250 per parcel a year.
A no vote is against the increased tax, and the $125 tax stays.
