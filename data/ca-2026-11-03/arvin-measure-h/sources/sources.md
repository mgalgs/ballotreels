- Publisher: City of Arvin
- Title: Special Meeting Agenda of the Arvin City Council, Thursday June 25, 2026 —
  packet pages 1–4 (agenda) and 66–112 (Item 3.A), cut with `qpdf --pages` from
  the 145-page packet
- URL: https://www.arvin.org/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Agenda/_06252026-503
- Item 3.A contents (packet pages): 3.A.1 staff report (66–69); 3.A.2
  resolution calling the election with the measure, Exhibit A ordinance
  (70–82); 3.A.3 resolution on arguments and impartial analysis (83–94); 3.A.4
  rebuttal resolution (95–96); 3.A.5–3.A.7 further resolutions incl.
  consolidation request (97–112).
- Facts:
  - Who votes / question (3.A.2, §2): "the City Council ... does order submitted to
    the voters at the General Municipal Election the following question: To
    protect funding for the City of Arvin's local public safety, police and fire
    departments, 911 emergency response, street and road repairs, parks, youth and
    senior services, and other general city services, shall the measure
    establishing a 1% transactions and use tax in the City of Arvin, providing
    approximately $2,350,000 annually, until ended by voters, with all funds
    staying local, independent audits, and public disclosure, be adopted?"
  - Rate: "a tax is hereby imposed upon all retailers in the incorporated territory
    of the City at the rate 1.0% of the gross receipts" (Ord. §3.36.050)
  - Duration: "The taxes imposed by this Chapter shall remain in effect until
    repealed by the voters of the City of Arvin." (§3.36.160)
  - General tax: "The proceeds from the tax imposed by this Chapter shall be for
    unrestricted general revenue purposes of the City and shall be placed into the
    General Fund of the City." (§3.36.170)
  - Audit: "The independent auditor's report, which shall include an accounting of
    the revenues received and expenditures made from the transactions and use tax,
    will be presented annually to the City Council and made available for public
    review." (§3.36.190)
  - Passage: "approved Ordinance No. ______ by a vote of no less than a majority of
    the votes cast" (§3.36.150)
  - Impartial analysis ordered: "The City Attorney shall prepare an impartial
    analysis of the measure not exceeding 500 words" (3.A.3)

## Ballot label: which wording?
Res. 2026-34 (adopted 5-0) and the June 25 notice match; the July 1 Notice of
Election differs ("local police and fire departments", "audited independently, and
publicly disclosed"). The printed county label is not confirmed (county voter guide
blocked). Treat the Res. 2026-34 text as the adopted question.
