Measure H asks the voters of this city whether to add a second 1 percent local sales tax for general city services.
Today, the city already charges one local sales tax, at 1 percent.
The City Council placed Measure H on the ballot.
Measure H would add another 1 percent, for a local rate of 2 percent in all.
Groceries and prescription medicine would be exempt.
As a general tax, the money would go to the general fund, for any general city purpose.
The tax would last until ended by voters, and needs a majority yes vote to pass.
The city attorney's impartial analysis says the tax would raise about $2.35 million a year.
No argument against Measure H appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
No argument for Measure H appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
A yes vote approves the added 1 percent sales tax.
A no vote is a vote against the added 1 percent sales tax.
