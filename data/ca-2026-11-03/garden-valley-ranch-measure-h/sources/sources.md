## Key facts, quoted

- **Ballot question:** "Shall the measure to levy an annual special tax in an amount of $250.00 with annual adjustments tied to the Consumer Price Index (CPI) not to exceed 4% on each parcel of land within the Garden Valley Ranch Community Service District commencing fiscal year 2026/2027 and continuing for an unlimited duration, to be used only for road improvement and maintenance and generating an annual revenue of approximately $44,000.00, to replace the current special tax of $150.00, be adopted?"
- **Analysis** (p. 66): "Measure H (the "Measure"), if approved by two-thirds of the voters voting thereon, would authorize the levy of an increased special parcel tax at the rate of $250 per parcel per year within the Garden Valley Ranch Estates Community Services District (the "District"). This Measure was placed on the ballot by the Board of Directors of the District pursuant to Resolution No. 01-2026, which was passed at an official meeting of the Board of Directors on May 8, 2026."
- "The amount of the special parcel tax may be adjusted annually in accordance with the Consumer Price Index, not to exceed 4% annually. … expected to generate revenue of approximately $44,000 per year. If the Measure is approved, the special parcel tax would replace an existing special tax of $150 per parcel. If the Measure is not approved, the existing special tax of $150 per parcel will remain in effect."
- Name note: the ballot question says "Garden Valley Ranch Community Service District"; the analysis says "Garden Valley Ranch Estates Community Services District".
