Measure H asks the voters of this community services district whether to raise its yearly road tax from $150 to $250 per parcel.
Voters in the district approved today's $150 tax to maintain and improve its roads.
The district's board of directors placed Measure H on the ballot.
Measure H needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The new tax would apply to each parcel in the district, starting in the 2026-2027 fiscal year.
The tax could rise each year with inflation, by no more than 4 percent.
The money could pay only for road improvements and maintenance, and the tax would last until voters change or repeal it.
If Measure H fails, the $150 tax stays.
The county attorney's impartial analysis expects the tax to raise about $44,000 a year.
No argument against Measure H was submitted, the voter pamphlet of this county says, and it prints no rebuttals.
Supporters say the tax, unchanged since 1997, now pays for only a fraction of the road work it once did.
A yes vote authorizes the increased tax of $250 per parcel a year.
A no vote is against the increased tax, and the $150 tax stays.
