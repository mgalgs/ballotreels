Resolution No. 26-294

[FILED stamp: AUG 13 2026]

BEFORE THE BOARD OF SUPERVISORS

OF THE

COUNTY OF FRESNO, STATE OF CALIFORNIA

IN THE MATTER OF:

CALLING A COUNTYWIDE ELECTION TO BE CONSOLIDATED WITH THE STATEWIDE PRIMARY ELECTION TO BE HELD ON MARCH 7, 2028, FOR THE PURPOSE OF SUBMITTING TO THE ELECTORS OF FRESNO COUNTY AN INITIATIVE MEASURE RELATING TO THE ESTABLISHMENT OF A NEW COUNTYWIDE RETAIL TRANSACTIONS AND USE TAX

RESOLUTION

WHEREAS, on April 14, 2026, Proponents submitted to the Fresno County Clerk/Registrar of Voters a citizens initiative petition ("Petition") signed by not less than 10% of the entire vote cast in Fresno County for all candidates for Governor at the last gubernatorial election preceding the publication of the Proponents' Notice of Intention to Circulate a Petition; and

WHEREAS, the Petition proposes an ordinance, attached hereto as Exhibit A, to create a new retail transactions and use tax to be spent for the purposes specified in the ordinance to benefit the County of Fresno, the incorporated cities in Fresno County, and local transit agencies ("Measure"); and

WHEREAS, on July 8, 2026, pursuant to Elections Code Section 9115, subdivision (b), the Fresno County Clerk/Registrar of Voters completed a full signature verification of the Petition, finding that the Petition is signed by the requisite number of qualified voters to declare the Petition sufficient; and

WHEREAS, on July 14, 2026, pursuant to Elections Code Section 9115, the Fresno County Clerk/Registrar of Voters certified the results of the examination of the Petition to the County of Fresno Board of Supervisors at its regular meeting; and

WHEREAS, on July 14, 2026, pursuant to Elections Code Sections 9111 and 9118, subdivision (c), the County of Fresno Board of Supervisors ordered a report on the Measure at its regular meeting; and

1

[Page 2]
WHEREAS, on August 11, 2026, pursuant to Elections Code Sections 1405, subdivision (a), and 9118, subdivisions (b) and (c), the County of Fresno Board of Supervisors received the report on the Measure and shall submit the Measure, without alteration, to the voters at the next statewide election occurring not less than 88 days after the date of the order of election; and

WHEREAS, the next statewide election occurring not less than 88 days after the date of the order of election will be the Statewide Primary Election held on March 7, 2028; and

WHEREAS, Assembly Bill 1923 (Stats. 2026, ch. 108) ("AB 1923") provides that any Fresno County initiative petition certified as sufficient by the Registrar on or before July 8, 2026, shall be submitted, without alteration, to the voters at the November 3, 2026, Statewide General Election, and further directs the Registrar and this Board "shall perform all acts that they would otherwise perform to place a qualified initiative measure on the ballot"; and

WHEREAS, AB 1923 does not amend or repeal any provision of the Elections Code, and does not provide that it operates notwithstanding the Elections Code or any other law; the timing provisions of Elections Code Sections 1405 and 9118 accordingly remain in full force and applicable to the County of Fresno; and

WHEREAS, the 88th day preceding the November 3, 2026, Statewide General Election was August 7, 2026; AB 1923 was enacted on August 6, 2026; and this Board's first opportunity to order an election on the Measure following enactment occurred fewer than 88 days before November 3, 2026; and

WHEREAS, among the acts this Board and the Registrar "would otherwise perform to place a qualified initiative measure on the ballot" is in compliance with the timing requirements of Elections Code Sections 1405 and 9118; and

WHEREAS, this Board is obligated to comply with, and by this Resolution intends to give full and harmonious effect to, both AB 1923 and the Elections Code; and

WHEREAS, reading AB 1923 and the Elections Code together, the next statewide election at which the Measure may be submitted consistent with the requirements of both enactments is the March 7, 2028, Statewide Primary Election; and

2

[Page 3]
WHEREAS, this Resolution reflects this Board's good faith construction of the statutes applicable to the Measure; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Elections Code Sections 10400 et seq., the County of Fresno Board of Supervisors seeks consolidation of the countywide election on the Measure relating to the proposed ordinance with the March 7, 2028, Statewide Primary Election; and

WHEREAS, under Elections Code Sections 10400 et seq., the County of Fresno Board of Supervisors has the power and authority to order the consolidation of local elections with the Statewide Primary Election; and

WHEREAS, the Proponents have requested that the Measure be designated "Measure S"; and

WHEREAS, approval of a majority of the electors voting upon the Measure isrequired for passage of the Measure.

NOW THEREFORE, be it resolved as follows:

1. The Board hereby calls, orders, and proclaims a countywide election at which the Measure relating to the proposed ordinance shall be submitted to the qualified voters of Fresno County pursuant to Elections Code Sections 1405, subdivision (a), and 9118, subdivisions (b).

2. Under Elections Code Sections 10400 et seq., the Board hereby orders the countywide election for this Measure to be consolidated with the March 7, 2028, Statewide Primary Election and placed on the ballot at that election.

3. The Board acknowledges that the consolidated election will be held and conducted in the manner prescribed in Elections Code Section 10418.

4. The Fresno County Clerk/Registrar of Voters is designated as the elections official for the election, and the Fresno County Clerk/Registrar of Voters is hereby authorized and directed to provide all notices and take all other actions necessary to holding the election, including, but not limited to, providing notices of times within which argumentsfor and against are submitted.

5. The ballot question hereby approved for the election is as follows:

3

[Page 4]
Fresno County Transportation Improvement Act. Shall the measure to impose a new half-cent sales tax to pay for streets and street repairs, busses, bike lanes, and safety improvements; free transit services for seniors, veterans, people with disabilities, and students; initially providing approximately $113 million annually for 30 years, requiring independent oversight and annual audits, be adopted?

Yes ___ No ___

6. The Fresno County Clerk/Registrar of Voters is hereby authorized and directed to cause the complete text of the Measure to be printed as indicated in the attached Exhibit A in the County of Fresno Voter Information Guide, and a copy of the Measure shall be made available to a voter upon request.

7. The Measure shall be designated on the ballot by a letter, as provided in Elections Code Section 13116. Based on the Proponents' request, the Fresno County Board of Supervisors requests that the Fresno County Clerk/Registrar of Voters designate the ballot Measure as Measure "S."

8. Arguments in favor of and in opposition to the Measure and rebuttal arguments shall be permitted and shall be filed with the Fresno County Clerk/Registrar of Voters in accordance with applicable provisions of the Elections Code, including Sections 9161 through 9167. As provided in Elections Code Section 9162, the Fresno County Board of Supervisors or any member of the Board is hereby authorized to act as an author of any ballot argument prepared in connection with the election on the Measure.

9. The Fresno County Clerk/Registrar of Voters shall fix the dates for submission of arguments and rebuttals as provided for in the Elections Code.

10. Pursuant to Elections Code Section 9160, subdivision (b), the County Counsel is directed to prepare an impartial analysis of the Measure.

11. Pursuant to Elections Code Section 9160, subdivision (c), the Auditor-Controller/Treasurer-Tax Collector is directed to prepare a fiscal impact statement which estimates the amount of any increase or decrease in revenues or costs to the County of Fresno if the Measure is adopted.

12. The County Administrative Officer, or his designee, is authorized and directed to work with the Fresno County Clerk/Registrar of Voters and take all steps necessary to place the Measure and any associated materials on the ballot.
