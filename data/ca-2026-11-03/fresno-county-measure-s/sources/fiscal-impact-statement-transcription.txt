COUNTY OF FRESNO
FISCAL IMPACT STATEMENT
FRESNO COUNTY TRANSPORTATION IMPROVEMENT ACT MEASURE

The measure would impose one-half cent sales and use tax on taxable retail sales within Fresno County for a period of 30 years. Based on estimated revenues comparable to the current Measure C, the measure is expected to generate approximately $120 million in revenue during fiscal year 2027-28. Actual annual revenues may vary depending on taxable sales, economic conditions, and other factors affecting sales and use tax collections.

The revenues generated by the measure would be dedicated to transportation-related purposes and allocated pursuant to the measure. Based on estimated first-year revenues of $120 million, approximately 65% ($78.0 million) would be allocated to Neighborhood Street Roads, 25% ($30.0 million) to Public Transportation, 4% ($4.8 million) to Regional Streets, 4% ($4.8 million) to Access & Innovation, 1% ($1.2 million) to Airport purposes, and 1% ($1.2 million) to Administration. A significant portion of the revenues would therefore be available for the improvement, maintenance, rehabilitation, and other transportation-related needs of roads within Fresno County.

The measure provides for Fresno Council of Governments (Fresno COG) to administer the transportation program and associated funds. Accordingly, the County would not be responsible for the administrative costs associated with administering the measure's transportation program, except for any costs or responsibilities specifically assigned to the County under the measure or applicable agreements.

If the measure is not approved, the proposed additional transportation sales tax revenues will not be available. Transportation programs and projects currently supported by Measure C revenues that are expected to continue under the proposed measure could, therefore, require alternative sources of funding. Depending on the availability of other federal, state, local, and transportation funding sources, additional County funding may be required to maintain or fund certain transportation improvements, road projects, or services that otherwise would have been supported by the measure's revenues. The amount of any additional County funding, if required, would depend on future transportation needs, available funding sources, and decisions regarding the prioritization and funding of transportation projects.
