## Facts

**Ballot question.** resolution.pdf, PDF page 4 (lines 1-3):
> "Fresno County Transportation Improvement Act. Shall the measure to impose a new half-cent sales tax to pay for streets and street repairs, busses, bike lanes, and safety improvements; free transit services for seniors, veterans, people with disabilities, and students; initially providing approximately $113 million annually for 30 years, requiring independent oversight and annual audits, be adopted?"

**What it does.** Impartial Analysis by County Counsel (Douglas T. Sloan; filed Sep 2 2026), p. 1:
> "This measure would adopt the Fresno County Transportation Improvement Act, a County of Fresno ordinance imposing a half-cent (0.5%) transactions and use tax (sales tax) for thirty years for specified transportation purposes (Measure). The tax would begin July 1, 2027, when the existing Measure C transportation tax expires, and would end thirty years later unless extended by the voters."

**Allocation.** Same, p. 1:
> "COG would annually allocate the proceeds as follows: 65% to the County and cities to repair, rebuild, and maintain local streets and roads ... 25% to public transportation, with 10% to Clovis Transit, 70% to Fresno Area Express, and 20% to Fresno County Rural Transit Agency. 1% to the Fresno Yosemite International Airport. 4% to regional street, road, and highway projects. 4% first to zero-fare transit for people with disabilities, veterans, seniors, children, and students ... 1% to COG for administration."

**Oversight.** Same, p. 1:
> "An eleven-member Citizens Oversight Committee (Committee) appointed by the COG Policy Board would meet at least quarterly, review expenditures, and oversee annual audits."

**How it got on the ballot; vote needed.** Same, pp. 1-2:
> "The Measure qualified for the ballot by an initiative petition signed by the requisite number of voters. It is being submitted to the voters at the November 3, 2026 election as required by Assembly Bill 1923 (Stats. 2026, ch. 108). Under current law, a citizen initiative imposing a special tax is adopted by a majority vote (more than 50% of votes cast). A "yes" vote approves the tax; a "no" vote rejects it."

**Who votes.** Countywide (all Fresno County voters). The county page lists it as a countywide measure, and it is a County ordinance. Not stated verbatim in a record I read.
