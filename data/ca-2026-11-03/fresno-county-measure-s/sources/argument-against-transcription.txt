Measure S is a NEW, 30 YEAR, $7 BILLION TAX SCAM. It's NOT a continuation of Measure C--it's a public transportation tax disguised as a roads tax. It requires 120 miles of bike lanes be built that won't be used, will eliminate car lanes, and increase traffic congestion. Many of the neighborhood streets and roads that need to be repaired won't be.

There's already $3 BILLION in deferred road repairs needed in the city and county of Fresno. Measure S doesn't commit enough funding to eliminate that backlog and limits our ability to leverage state and federal funds. It's full of promises that will never be kept.

The consequences are real. Mountain communities need adequate roads for wildfire evacuations. Rural communities need safer two-lane roads. Growing cities need roadway capacity, interchanges and infrastructure that support new homes, businesses and jobs.

Unlike Measure C - which helped build, expand, and extend thousands of miles of local roads along with Highways 41, 180, 168 - Measure S is designed to force Fresno County residents out of their cars and ride buses and bicycles to work. Fresno County residents already facing rising gas and food expenses will be further burdened by this new tax measure.

Our economy depends on moving workers, customers, agricultural products and freight efficiently. Transportation dollars should reduce traffic congestion, improve safety and support economic growth - not lock Fresno County into harmful policies that will hurt our community, children and grandchildren for 30 years.

Fresno County voters deserve policies designed around how people actually live, work, and travel. Measure S doesn't prioritize essential infrastructure, doesn't fix roads as needed, provides zero accountability, and will make Fresno County a more dangerous place to travel.

Don't commit Fresno County taxpayers to a tax scam for an entire generation.

Vote NO on Measure S
