VOTE YES on MEASURE S for Better Roads and Safe Streets Throughout Fresno County

We've had enough of politicians saying one thing and doing another. Measure S was created by our communities, for our communities. The money can only be used how our communities say it should - to fix our roads and make it safer, faster, and easier to get around Fresno County.

YES on MEASURE S to Improve Safety and Emergency Response

- Invests in improving road safety to help improve 911 emergency response times and ensure firefighters, ambulances, and police can reach emergencies faster and improves evacuation routes to help families in the event of wildfires.

YES on MEASURE S to Fix Roads in Every Neighborhood

- Keeps local tax dollars here, prioritizing projects residents say matter most: repairing neighborhood streets, fixing dangerous intersections near homes and schools.

YES on MEASURE S to Save Us Money

- Fixing roads now saves us all money. Potholes don't just damage roads - they damage tires, wheels, and suspensions that families have to pay to repair.

- Provides no-cost public transit for Veterans, active-duty military, seniors, people with disabilities, and students.

YES on MEASURE S to Strengthen Our Local Economy

- Every dollar invested creates jobs repairing roads, driving transit, and building new projects right here in our communities that can't be outsourced.

YES on MEASURE S for More Road Repairs, Same Tax Rate, Stronger Accountability

- Measure S is Fresno County's only citizen-led plan to provide every city and town with the funding needed to fix our roads while keeping the current half-cent sales tax rate the same, but now with added transparency, stricter accountability, oversight, and auditing.

Supported by Nurses, Teachers, Small Business Owners, Community Groups, Faith Leaders, and a bipartisan coalition of Mayors, City Councils, and School Boards throughout Fresno County.

Learn More at: VoteYesOnS.com
