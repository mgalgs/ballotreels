Argument Against Measure __

Vote No on Measure X to protect Affordable Homes and support a vibrant downtown small business district in Menlo Park!

Menlo Park is one of the most expensive communities in the country. Teachers, caregivers, firefighters, and emergency responders cannot afford to live here. The next generation deserves the chance to stay near family and friends. Yet, every day, it gets harder to afford a home.

Downtown Menlo Park has too many empty storefronts. Redwood City, San Carlos, and Burlingame have seen a boom in downtown businesses after adding housing and improving parking. New homes bring activity, vitality, and business to downtown. Measure X would prevent Menlo Park from doing this.

If approved, Measure X would block Menlo Park's ability to replace aging surface parking lots with modern garages and add affordable homes above. Measure X inhibits bicycle and pedestrian safety improvements. All told, Measure X would make it harder for families, seniors, and people with disabilities to live and visit downtown.

Measure X is bad policy with no expiration date. Independent analysis finds it likely violates state laws, risking millions in legal fees and years of delay. Taxpayers would foot the bill to defend it, including endless appeals (no matter the merits). It could also put Menlo Park out of compliance with state housing law, costing local control of development (and allowing builder’s remedy projects) city-wide.

If approved, Measure X would:

• Block affordable homes downtown.
• Hinder Menlo Park's efforts to revitalize downtown like neighboring cities have.
• Block much-needed downtown parking improvements.
• Forever inhibit the city's ability to plan for downtown's future.
• Delay even simple downtown projects like installing EV Chargers, by up to two years.
• Complicate compliance with state law.
• Cost Menlo Park taxpayers millions in legal fees.

Join trusted community leaders and organizations and Vote No on Measure X to support a vibrant downtown and create affordable homes within reach for Menlo Park!
