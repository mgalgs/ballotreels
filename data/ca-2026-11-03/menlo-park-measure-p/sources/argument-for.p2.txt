ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE __

Vote YES! on Measure __ to give voters a say before the City Council can permanently convert downtown parking plazas into high-rise developments.

A FLAWED PLAN
Over 3,000 Menlo Park residents signed the Measure X petition because they don’t believe Council’s plan is an effective way to provide the low-income housing we need.

Council presumed that developers, who’d get the plazas for $1/year, could pay to replace 556 spaces. But the developer proposals tell a different story, and Menlo Park could be on the hook for tens of millions to build public garages. Furthermore, the City’s done insufficient analysis on economic impact, traffic, parking, overcrowded classrooms, or fire safety.

Already, 135 downtown businesses publicly oppose the Council's plan, as they’d be unlikely to survive years of high-rise construction followed by a permanent loss of convenient parking.

A SMARTER WAY FORWARD
Most residents aren't aware, but Menlo Park is proving it can deliver housing without this plan. Menlo Park’s own July 2026 housing update reported that housing capacity exceeds state requirements by 30%. Since 2010, Menlo Park has added more than 1,400 homes, nearly 30% more growth than the county average.

And 1,750 more homes are on the way, near downtown, at the former SRI and USGS sites. The problem is that not enough of them are affordable to local workers. We can remedy that by incentivizing the developers to shift units to the very-low-income category. The City’s cost to do that would be far less than giving away public land, building expensive garages, and undermining local businesses.

Menlo Park has many ways to meet its housing goals. The greatest risk is Council pushing forward, without community consent, with a flawed, unfunded plan that could fail us all.

Vote YES! on Measure __. It ensures our voices are heard.
