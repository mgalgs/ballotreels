Regional Transit Measure RTM asks voters across Contra Costa County whether to add a half-cent sales tax for public transit, for 14 years.
In 2025 the Legislature created a transit district covering Contra Costa, Alameda, San Mateo and Santa Clara counties and San Francisco.
Voters placed Measure RTM on the ballot by petition, and it needs a majority of votes across the whole district.
The tax would be half a percent in the four counties and 1 percent in San Francisco.
In Contra Costa County, 58.59 percent of the money raised would go to BART for transit operations.
The impartial analysis estimates the tax would raise about $980 million a year across the five counties.
Supporters say that if it fails, BART, AC Transit, Muni and Caltrain could close stations and cut service, and large agencies must show savings.
Opponents say everybody would pay the tax every day, on most things they buy, and that both BART and AC Transit are grossly mismanaged.
A yes vote is a vote to impose the tax for 14 years.
A no vote is a vote against imposing the tax.
