Measure YV asks the voters of this city whether residents aged 16 and 17 should vote in city and school district elections.
Today, voters must be at least 18 under state law, which the city charter follows.
The state constitution lets charter cities run their own city elections.
Measure YV would let eligible 16- and 17-year-olds vote in city and school board elections.
First, the City Council and the school board must each fund any added election costs and confirm systems are ready.
Including younger voters also must not block combining those elections with county elections.
Measure YV needs a simple majority to pass.
The county Registrar's list of measures does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Opponents say students would become a captive voting bloc, and strangers with political motives would court and contact teens.
Supporters say local decisions shape the lives of teens, and people who vote once eligible stay engaged for life.
A yes vote lowers the voting age to 16 for these elections, once the conditions are met.
A no vote does not lower the voting age.
