REBUTTAL TO ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE YV

Let us address the half-truths and flawed logic in the Argument in Favor.

Working teens pay State and Federal taxes. Culver City's budget is based primarily on Property taxes and Business taxes. This proposal gives children the power to increase your property taxes and pass-thru rent increases.

They point out the proposal failed by 16 votes in 2022. One vote over a tie wins an election, and citizens have already signaled TWICE (2022, 2024) they do NOT support this measure.

The "growing movement" in the US has mostly been limited to school-related votes; not general city council matters.

The research they claim shows increased youth participation and turnout actually shows that, after the initial excitement, they engage no more than older voters do.

So what is the motivation of the three Culver City Council people listed above to repeatedly support lowering the voting age?

Several elections have been decided by fewer than 100 votes in Culver City. Adding ~1,000 16- and 17-year-old voters will instantly make them the deciding voting bloc in future elections.

Clinical studies clearly show 16- and 17-year-olds are highly susceptible to peer pressure and emotional arguments. State education code requires any adult that works with children submit to a criminal background check. If passed, the student vote will empower adults to impose their political will through our children without such protection, while candidates and their supporters lobby your children for votes.
