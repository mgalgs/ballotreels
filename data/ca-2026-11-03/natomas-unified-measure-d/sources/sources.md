## Key facts, quoted

- **Ballot question:** "Natomas Schools Repair, Safety, Education Measure. To continue providing quality education/ career training; repair aging infrastructure/ electrical systems/ classrooms/ leaky roofs; improve school safety/ security/ access for students with disabilities/ safe play areas, by repairing, constructing, acquiring classrooms/ facilities, shall Natomas Unified School District's measure authorizing $300,000,000 in bonds at legal rates; levying $60 per $100,000 of assessed valuation; raising approximately $22,000,000 annually while bonds are outstanding; requiring oversight, disclosure, audits, no money for administrators' salaries, be adopted?"
- **Threshold:** "55% Vote Required"; analysis: "Passage of Measure D requires approval by 55% of the voters voting thereon."
- **Analysis** (box label "Impartial Analysis Prepared by County Counsel"; unsigned on the page): "Measure D was placed on the ballot by the governing board of the Natomas Unified School District ("District"). If approved, Measure D would allow the District to incur bonded indebtedness up to a maximum amount of $300 million dollars." "No funds derived from bond sales may be used for general school operating expenses, including administrator and teacher salaries or pensions". "Measure D requires: 1) the appointment of a citizen's oversight committee; 2) completion of annual independent performance and financial audits; and 3) creation of an account into which proceeds of the bonds shall be deposited." "The District estimates the average annual tax rate levy to fund this bond is $59.38 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. The final fiscal year in which the tax is anticipated to be collected will be 2053-54. The District estimates that the total amount repayable during the life of the bond, including principal and interest, is approximately $600 million dollars."
- **Yes/No:** "A "YES" vote on Measure D means you wish to allow the District to issue bonds in an amount not to exceed $300 million dollars." / "A "NO" vote on Measure D means you do not wish to allow the District to issue the proposed bonds."
- **Tax rate statement** (signed "s/Robyn Castillo, Superintendent, Natomas Unified School District"): average rate "$59.38 per $100,000 of assessed valuation"; final year "2053-54"; highest rate "$60 per $100,000 of assessed valuation … first apply is fiscal year 2027-28"; "total debt service, including the principal and interest, that would be required to be repaid if all the bonds are issued and sold is $599,856,974." It adds: "Voters should note that estimated tax rates are based on the ASSESSED VALUE of taxable property on the County's official tax rolls, not on the property's market value."
- **Argument in favor signers:** Angelique Ashby, California State Senator; Micah Grant, President, Natomas Unified School District; Karina Talamantes, Vice Mayor, City of Sacramento; Lisa Kaplan, Councilmember. Its claim "Measure D will not raise your tax rate" is the authors'; the official tax rate statement gives the estimated rates above.
