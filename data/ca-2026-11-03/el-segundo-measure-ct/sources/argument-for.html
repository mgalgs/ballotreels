ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE CT

In 2022, El Segundo voters approved Measure BT to modernize the City's outdated Business License Tax Ordinance. One section of Measure BT relating to charities intended to set a $150 tax for charitable organizations. This section was overbroadly stated as applying to nonprofit/charitable organizations. An unintended loophole resulted: certain nonprofit entities that do not actually operate for charitable purposes have been able to claim the same flat $150 tax rate meant for local charities, while other businesses across town continue paying their full share.

Measure CT fixes that mistake. It restores the standard Business License Tax on nonprofit organizations that do not serve a charitable purpose, so they pay the same rate as comparable businesses, just as the law required before 2022. At the same time, it goes further for genuine charities. Instead of paying the $150 tax, true charitable organizations in El Segundo will be fully exempt from the Business License Tax, honoring the City's longstanding support for our local charities. These revisions are the only changes proposed to the 2022 voter approved Measure BT.

Measure CT is a general tax and the proceeds will go to the general fund to be used for any lawful City purpose including police and fire protection, parks, capital improvements and other core City services every resident relies on. Correcting this loophole is projected to restore approximately $700,000 annually to the City's general fund.

This is not a new tax on residents. It is a fix that closes a loophole, ensures every business pays its appropriate share, and strengthens support for the charities in El Segundo.
