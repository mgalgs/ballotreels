## Ballot question and supporters/opponents (verbatim, from the county's final measures list)

> Shall an Ordinance amending the City's Business License Tax Ordinance to restore and impose a business license tax on nonprofit organizations that do not serve a charitable purpose, and also to exempt charitable organizations from having to pay a business license tax, thereby indefinitely restoring approximately $700,000 general funds annually, be adopted?
>
> Supporters: Mayor Pimentel; Mayor Pro Tem Baldino; Council Member Boyles; Council Member Giroux; Council Member Keldorf
> Opponents: None submitted.

Vote threshold: Majority Vote 50%+1 (county's final measures list).

- **Argument for**: missing — county list names five Council members as supporters, but no filed argument text was located (same block).
- **Argument against**: not applicable — county list states no argument against was submitted.
- **Rebuttal**: not applicable — no argument against exists to rebut.
- **Resolution placing it on the ballot**: missing — Resolutions No. 5610 and 5611 are referenced by the city's own (blocked) election page but could not be retrieved.
- **Tax rate statement**: not applicable — Measure CT is a business license tax, not a property-tax-secured bond measure.
