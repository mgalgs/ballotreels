CITY ATTORNEY'S IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE CT

If approved, Measure CT would amend the El Segundo Municipal Code to make nonprofit organizations that do not serve a charitable purpose subject to the City’s normal business license tax schedule. The Measure would also exempt charitable organizations from the payment of any business license tax.

In November 2022, the City’s voters approved Measure BT, which amended several provisions of the Municipal Code governing the business license tax. Prior to the approval of Measure BT, Section 4-1-7 of the Municipal Code exempted charitable organizations from the business license tax. Measure BT amended Section 4-1-7 to instead require charitable organizations to pay a flat fee business license tax of $150 per year. Measure BT also amended Section 4-1-7 to require “nonprofit organizations” to pay $150 per year flat fee, even though that category was not defined by Measure BT and had previously not existed within the business license tax ordinance. By including the category “nonprofit organizations” in Section 4-1-7, certain nonprofit business entities that do not operate for charitable purposes, that had previously paid the regularly applicable business license tax, became eligible for the reduced ($150/year) business license tax rate. These “nonprofit organizations” maintain a nonprofit status under the organizational structure, but they do not operate for charitable purposes. Nevertheless, because of the amendment approved by Measure BT, these nonprofit businesses became eligible for the reduced tax rate intended for charitable organizations.

If approved, Measure CT would reverse the City’s unintended consequence of exempting non-charitable organizations from paying business license taxes by deleting the reference to “nonprofit organizations” in Section 4-1-7. At the same time, Measure CT would repeal the $150 annual business license tax for charitable organizations and exempt charitable organizations from having to pay the business license tax entirely.

The El Segundo City Council placed Measure CT on the ballot. It takes a simple majority of the votes cast to pass Measure CT.

A “yes” vote is a vote in favor of exempting charitable organizations from the business license tax entirely and requiring nonprofit organizations that do not operate for a charitable purpose to pay the regularly applicable business license tax pursuant to the El Segundo Municipal Code.

A “no” vote is a vote in favor of maintaining the current business license tax rate of $150/year for both charitable organizations and nonprofit organizations that do not operate for a charitable purpose.
