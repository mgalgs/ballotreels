Measure CT asks the voters of this city whether to change how the business license tax applies to charities and other nonprofits.
The City Council placed Measure CT on the ballot, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
Before 2022, charities paid no business license tax, and nonprofits that do not serve a charitable purpose paid the regular tax.
In 2022, voters approved changes to the tax that set a flat $150 a year for charities and for all nonprofit organizations.
Today, charities and nonprofits that do not serve a charitable purpose both pay the $150 flat rate.
Nonprofits that do not serve a charitable purpose would again pay the regular business license tax.
Charities would no longer pay the $150 tax, and would be fully exempt.
The ballot question says the change would restore about $700,000 a year to the general fund of the city.
Supporters say the measure closes a loophole letting nonprofits without a charitable purpose pay the rate meant for charities, and fully exempts true charities.
The official county list of measures for this election says that no argument against Measure CT was submitted, by any group or person.
A yes vote exempts charities fully, and has other nonprofits pay the regular tax.
A no vote keeps the $150 yearly rate for both.
