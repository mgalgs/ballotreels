## Quoted passages

- Question (p.1): "Shall the measure updating the City of Orange’s existing Transient Occupancy Tax, a tax on persons occupying hotel rooms by increasing the tax rate from 10% to 14% for hotels with 11 or more rooms and applying the tax to online and other travel companies, generating approximately $3,000,000 annually for general City purposes including police, fire and emergency response, parks, and street and infrastructure maintenance, until ended by voters, be adopted?"
- Who placed it / threshold (p.5): "The City Council of the City of Orange is submitting Measure J to the voters for consideration at the November 3, 2026 General Municipal Election. If approved by a majority of the voters, Measure J would amend Chapter 5.16 of the Orange Municipal Code"
- Analysis (p.5): "The measure would increase the TOT rate from 10 percent to 14 percent for hotels with eleven (11) or more accommodations. Hotels with ten (10) or fewer accommodations would continue to be subject to the existing 10 percent tax rate."
- Analysis (p.5): "The ordinance would also update the City’s TOT definitions and apply the tax to the full amount charged to guests for accommodations, including amounts collected by accommodations intermediaries such as online travel or booking companies, as provided in the ordinance."
- Analysis (p.5): "The City estimates that the measure would generate approximately $3 million annually"
