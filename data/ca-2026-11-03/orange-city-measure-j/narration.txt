City of Orange Measure J asks city voters whether to raise the hotel tax from 10 to 14 percent at larger hotels.
Today, hotel guests in Orange pay a 10 percent tax on their stay.
The City Council placed Measure J on the ballot, and it needs a majority to pass.
The rate would rise to 14 percent at hotels with 11 or more rooms, and stay at 10 percent at smaller ones.
The tax would also cover the full amount guests pay, including through online booking companies.
The money would go to the general fund for any city purpose, until voters end the tax.
The city attorney's impartial analysis says the city estimates the change would raise about $3 million a year.
Supporters say residents should not carry the whole cost of serving hotel guests, and Orange stays competitive with neighbors.
No argument against Measure J was submitted, so none is printed in the official Orange County Voter Information Guide.
A yes vote authorizes the city to raise the rate at larger hotels and apply the tax to online booking companies.
A no vote does not authorize those changes.
