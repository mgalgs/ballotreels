Impartial Analysis – Measure S – El Camino Healthcare District – Term Limits

California law permits a district's governing body to propose a measure to limit the number of terms a member of the governing body may serve. Here, the Board of Directors of the El Camino Healthcare District (District) has placed on the ballot a Measure to amend the District Bylaws to limit District Directors to serving four (4) four-year terms. The District currently has no limit on the number of terms a Director may serve.

If a majority of voters approve this Measure, the District Bylaws will be amended to provide that:

• For terms beginning on or after December 1, 2026, no person may serve more than four full terms as a District Director;
• If for any reason a District Director serves a partial term—such as when a Director is appointed to fill a vacancy—that partial term shall not be counted as a term for purposes of the four-term limit; and
• Terms served prior to December 1, 2026, whether full or partial, are not included toward the maximum number of terms.

A "yes" vote is a vote to amend the District Bylaws to prohibit District Directors from serving more than four full terms, for terms beginning on or after December 1, 2026.

A "no" vote is a vote to not amend the District Bylaws.
