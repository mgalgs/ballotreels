Measure S asks the voters of this health care district whether to limit its directors to four four-year terms.
Today, the district has no limit on the number of terms a director may serve.
Measure S needs a majority of the vote to pass.
The district bylaws would say that no one may serve more than four full terms as a director.
Only terms beginning on or after December 1, 2026 would count, and a partial term, such as filling a vacancy, would not.
The impartial analysis does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Supporters say good governance needs fresh perspectives, and term limits open seats to new candidates and keep the board accountable to the community.
No argument against Measure S was filed, so the list of local measures from the county shows only the argument in favor of it.
A yes vote amends the district bylaws to bar directors from serving more than four full terms, counting terms that begin on or after December 1, 2026.
A no vote does not amend the district bylaws.
