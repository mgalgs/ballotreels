## Facts with quotes (Lassen County Counsel's analysis, p. 20 — differs in wording from Trinity's)

- "Proposal by the Shasta-Tehama-Trinity Joint Community College District (the "District") to issue up to $129,630,000 in general obligation bonds provided the measure passes with at least 55% voter approval. The District estimates an average annual levy of $7.76 per $100,000 of assessed value."
- "The District estimates that the total amount repayable during the life of the bonds, including principal and interest, will be approximately $264,800,000. The highest estimated tax rate is $15.29 per $100,000 of assessed value estimated to be levied in fiscal years 2031-32 through 2042-43."
- **Geography:** "serving students, veterans, and residents throughout the District which includes portions of Shasta, Tehama, Trinity, Lassen, Humboldt, and Modoc Counties."
- **Board action:** "on May 13, 2026, by Resolution No. 2025-26-18, the District's Board of Trustees adopted a resolution authorizing and requesting consolidation with other elections to be held on November 3, 2026".
- **Caveat:** "Approval of this measure does not guarantee that all proposed projects to be improved with bond proceeds will be funded beyond the local revenues generated by the measure. The District's proposal for the projects assumes the receipt of matching state funds".
