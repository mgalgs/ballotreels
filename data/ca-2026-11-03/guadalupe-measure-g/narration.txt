Measure G asks the voters of this city whether to tax what cannabis businesses take in.
Measure G would not change the city rules for cannabis businesses, or allow any new ones to open.
Cannabis stores could be taxed up to 8 percent of gross receipts, and other cannabis businesses up to 4 percent.
The city council could set lower rates, but higher ones would need voter approval.
As a general tax, the money could pay for any city purpose, until voters repeal the tax.
Measure G needs a simple majority to pass.
The ballot question estimates the revenue at about $78,000 a year.
Supporters say replacing today's fee with a tax helps local businesses while keeping an important city revenue source.
No argument against Measure G was filed, so the county voter guide prints only the one in favor.
A yes vote approves the cannabis business tax, up to its top rates.
A no vote does not approve the cannabis business tax.
