Tahoe Truckee Unified School District Measure P asks the district's voters whether to renew its $148 school parcel tax with a $40 increase.
Today's $148 parcel tax, approved in 2018, expires in July 2028.
Measure P needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
From July 1, 2027, the tax would be $188 per parcel for nine years, rising with inflation by up to 3 percent a year.
The money could pay for academic programs, career training, the arts, libraries, and counseling and nursing.
The measure estimates the tax would raise about $7 million a year.
Opponents say Measure P raises the parcel tax by $40 with automatic yearly increases, years before the current tax expires.
Supporters say without renewal, over $5 million would be cut, forcing teacher layoffs, and that all funds go to classrooms.
A yes vote is a vote to authorize the special tax.
A no vote is a vote against it, and the 2018 tax would continue under its existing terms.
