Measure R asks the voters of this town whether to continue a parcel tax for paramedic services for four more years.
The tax pays for paramedic services from a regional paramedic authority that the town belongs to.
Today the tax is $103.50 per housing unit, or per 1,500 square feet of other buildings, and it ends in June 2027.
Measure R would raise the tax to up to $107.50 per housing unit, or per 1,000 square feet of other buildings, rising up to $4 a year until June 2031.
The money could pay only for paramedic and emergency medical services from the regional authority, with a yearly report on spending.
Measure R needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The impartial analysis estimates the tax would raise $432,000 a year.
No argument against Measure R was filed, according to the page for the measure from the county, so only the argument in favor appears there.
Supporters say Measure R renews paramedic funding for four years at the current tax rate, with modest yearly increases, and preserves the number of on-duty paramedics.
A yes vote approves the paramedic tax for four more years.
A no vote does not approve the paramedic tax.
