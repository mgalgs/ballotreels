Los Rios Community College District Measure H asks voters in part of the district whether to approve $1.1 billion in college bonds.
The district would borrow the money and repay it from a tax on property in that part of the district.
Measure H needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would pay for college career training facilities, safe drinking water and roof repairs.
The district's tax statement estimates an average tax of $16.55 per $100,000 of assessed value, and at most $20.80, through 2050-2051.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $1.79 billion.
Opponents say the district needs no new buildings now, as half its students study online, leaving campus space unused.
Supporters say Measure H keeps local colleges affordable and fixes leaky roofs and drinking water, all without raising taxes.
A yes vote allows the district to issue up to $1.1 billion in bonds.
A no vote does not allow the district to issue the proposed bonds.
