ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF SPECIAL TAX MEASURE _

VOTE YES to protect lives, maintain and grow our parks and recreation, and keep Orange Cove safe.

Measure _ is a parcel tax that will allow Orange Cove's Police, Parks, and Recreation Services to continue. Currently, the City is facing financial constraints that cause a reduction in parks and recreation services and affect the City's police force. Measure _ will allow Police, Parks, and Recreation in the City to continue at the levels that the community deserves, instead of the bare minimum.

Approval of Measure _ will ensure 100% of funds stay in Orange Cove and can only be spent on our Police, Parks, and Recreation. Measure _ allows for the highest level of public safety which residents expect and deserve and the City will be unable to provide under the current financial constraints. Measure _ also allows the City's parks to be maintained appropriately and for recreation services to be expanded for the City's youth.

Vote YES on Measure _ because every penny remains in Orange Cove to protect residents, parks, and recreation.

Vote YES on Measure _ to prevent cuts in police personnel and equipment that could result in longer police response times and reductions in service. In an emergency, every second matters.

Vote YES on Measure _ because our youth deserve clean parks and expanded recreation services:

Measure _ ensures local control over money raised from Orange Cove residents and guarantees our money is spent exclusively in Orange Cove for our benefit and our protection. This is especially important in protecting Orange Cove's youth and schools.

Measure _ protects our community and keeps our police services top-tier for the residents of Orange Cove.

VOTE YES ON MEASURE _ - KEEP ORANGE COVE SAFE AND PROSPEROUS!
