Argument Against City of Orange Cove Special Parcel Tax Measure

Fresno County

City of Orange Cove

November 3, 2026 Consolidated Statewide General Election

Word Count: 216

Text of Argument Below This Line

==============================================================

How has your cost of living changed in the last few years? What about your neighbors? Before you vote on this sales tax for the City of Orange Cove, please think carefully about what it would mean for our community.

This tax would collect about $460,907 every year from the people of Orange Cove, and it would never end.

It also goes up automatically each year by 3%! FOREVER!

You might feel your share is worth paying because it promises better public services. Public services matter, and wanting to support them shows real care for your community. But there are other ways to help. If you are willing to give money through a tax, would you also be willing to give it through voluntary donations - where you choose the amount and the timing?

Think again about your neighbors. Many have struggled in recent years, and even a $125 parcel tax could make life noticeably harder. That means less money for groceries, saving for college, or paying medical bills after an emergency. We respect those who want to raise money for a good cause. We simply ask that they try voluntary giving first, especially when so many families are already stretched thin.

Let your neighbors decide for themselves which causes they want to support, and vote NO on Measure __.

Join us: https://lpfresno.org/NoOrangeCoveTax
