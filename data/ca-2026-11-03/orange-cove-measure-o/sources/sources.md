The registrar's page posts no separate ordinance file ("Exhibit A") for Measure O. The resolution says the synopsis covers "Ordinance No. 403", to be added as Orange Cove Municipal Code Chapter 3.34.

## Facts

**Ballot question.** Resolution No. 2026-28, Section 2, PDF page 2, boxed under "City of Orange Cove Measure _":
> "To generate approximately $460,907 annually to fund police, recreation, and park maintenance services where 70% of the special tax proceeds shall be used for police services, shall the measure levying $125/unit for single family homes and $140/unit for multifamily, $800/acre for non-residential, and $400/acre for undeveloped parcels, known as Measure _, be adopted without expiration, with 3% annual increase, with all funds generated staying in the City of Orange Cove?"

**Who votes.** Resolution Section 1, PDF page 2:
> "The City Council submits to the voters of the City of Orange Cove at a General Municipal Election to be consolidated with the regularly scheduled November 3, 2026 Statewide General Election, a ballot measure for consideration of an ordinance for establishment of a Special Parcel Tax for Police, Recreation, and Park Maintenance Services"

**Background.** Resolution recitals, PDF page 1:
> "WHEREAS, the ballot measure relating to Ordinance No. 396 to provide for the continued funding of police and fire services was rejected by voters in March 2024; and WHEREAS, the special parcel tax relating to Ordinance No. 396 was sunset in November 2024"

**What it does.** Impartial Analysis (Megan N. Crouch, City of Orange Cove City Attorney; filed Aug 10 2026):
> "Measure _ proposes a parcel tax of $125 a unit on each single-family residential parcel within the City; $140 on each multifamily unit within the City; $800 per acre for each nonresidential parcel; and $400 per acre for each undeveloped parcel. Measure _ will generate approximately $460,907 annually to fund police, park maintenance, and recreation services. Measure _ proposes an annual 3% increase in the parcel tax to account for inflation."
> "Seventy percent of the proposed Measure _ will be used to fund police services. ... The Measure provides for a citizen oversight committee to ensure the funds are spent as intended by the voters."

**Vote needed.** County page heading: "Two-Thirds (2/3) Majority to Pass". The impartial analysis does not state the threshold.
