Measure _ Impartial Analysis

Measure _ proposes to raise revenue for police, park maintenance, and recreation services through a parcel tax. Measure _ has been placed on the ballot by the majority vote of the City of Orange Cove's governing body. Measure _ proposes to continue the police, park maintenance, and recreation services parcel tax without expiration, until repealed by the voters. Measure _ is a proposed parcel tax on each parcel of real property in the City. The tax would be paid in addition to current State and local property taxes and would be collected at the same time and in the same manner as the existing real property taxes. Measure _ proposes a parcel tax of $125 a unit on each single-family residential parcel within the City; $140 on each multifamily unit within the City; $800 per acre for each nonresidential parcel; and $400 per acre for each undeveloped parcel. Measure _ will generate approximately $460,907 annually to fund police, park maintenance, and recreation services. Measure _ proposes an annual 3% increase in the parcel tax to account for inflation.

Measure _ proposes that the parcel tax be used to fund police, park maintenance, and recreation services. Seventy percent of the proposed Measure _ will be used to fund police services. The City is legally bound to use the funds for police, park maintenance, and recreation services. The Measure provides for a citizen oversight committee to ensure the funds are spent as intended by the voters.

Megan N. Crouch
City of Orange Cove City Attorney
