## Ballot question (guide p. 799)

> Shall the measure authorizing the Albany City Council to amend the residential voter requirements for zoning changes to the R-1 zoned areas of the City established by Measure D (enacted by the Albany voters in 1978), without majority neighborhood voter approval, after following noticed public hearing and zoning ordinance amendment procedures prescribed by State Law and the Albany Municipal Code, be adopted?

Transcribed from the guide; line-break hyphens rejoined and the YES/NO box labels omitted.

## Who votes on it

Voters in the City of Albany. The guide section is headed "CITY OF ALBANY MEASURE O", and the Registrar's measure list (<https://alamedacountyca.gov/rov_app/measures/election/260>) files it under that jurisdiction. Vote threshold: see the analysis quotes below.

## Quoted passages

Quotes are from the guide's text layer (decoded; some glyphs in the source fonts lack a Unicode map). Where a quote and the PDF page differ, the PDF page governs.

- p. 799: "If approved by a majority of Albany voters, this Measure would amend the Albany Municipal Code to authorize the City to make zoning changes to land within the R-1 zoned areas from time to time, without majority neighborhood voter approval, after following noticed public hearing and zoning ordinance amendment procedures prescribed by State law and the Albany Municipal Code."
- p. 799: "By placing Measure “O” on the ballot, the City complies with California Elections Code section 9217 which requires a majority of the Albany voters to approve the repeal or amendment of a previously voter-approved measure."
- p. 799: "A “yes” vote on Measure “O” will authorize the City Council to make zoning changes within the R-1 zoned areas without majority neighborhood voter approval[.]"
- p. 799: "A “no” vote on Measure “O” will not authorize the City Council to make zoning changes within the R-1 zoned areas without majority neighborhood voter approval."
- p. 799: "If you desire a copy of the Measure, please call the City Clerk’s Office at 510 528-5710 and a copy will be mailed at no cost to you."
