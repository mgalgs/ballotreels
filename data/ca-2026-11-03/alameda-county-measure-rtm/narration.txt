Regional Transit Measure RTM asks voters across Alameda County whether to add a half-cent sales tax for public transit, for 14 years.
In 2025 the Legislature created a transit district covering Alameda, Contra Costa, San Mateo and Santa Clara counties and San Francisco.
Voters placed Measure RTM on the ballot by petition, and it needs a majority of votes across the whole district.
The tax would be half a percent in the four counties and 1 percent in San Francisco.
In Alameda County, 64.7 percent of the money raised would go to BART for transit operations.
The impartial analysis estimates the tax would raise about $980 million a year across the five counties.
Opponents say the tax would push the sales tax to 11.25 percent in most of the county, and BART and AC Transit are grossly mismanaged.
Supporters say that without the measure, BART could close 15 stations, and AC Transit could cut service and lay off workers, meaning longer waits.
A yes vote is a vote to impose the tax for 14 years.
A no vote is a vote against imposing the tax.
