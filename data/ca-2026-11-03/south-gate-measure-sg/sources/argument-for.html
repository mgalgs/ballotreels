ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE SG

MEASURE SG – UTILITY USERS TAX

South Gate faces ongoing financial challenges as the cost of providing essential City services continues to rise. Employee costs, equipment, supplies, infrastructure maintenance, and other operating expenses have increased, while the City has limited locally controlled revenue available to meet these growing costs.

Contrary to a common misconception, not all taxes paid by South Gate residents remain with the City. Portions of sales and property taxes are distributed among the State, County, school districts, and other public agencies.

South Gate has worked to maintain services by aggressively pursuing grants, reducing expenses, and making difficult budget decisions, including staffing reductions. However, grants are generally restricted to specific purposes and cannot always be used to fund ongoing City services.

The proposed Utility Users Tax (UUT) would establish an 8% tax on specified utility services and is estimated to generate approximately $10.4 million annually. The measure includes an exemption for qualifying low-income seniors, independent audits, public disclosure, and locally controlled revenue.

UUT revenue could be used to support services, including police and 911 response, street and sidewalk maintenance, school crossing guards, parks and recreation programs, youth services, and other General Fund services.

Without additional ongoing revenue, the City may need to consider further reductions to programs, staffing, maintenance, and other services.

The November 3, 2026, election gives South Gate voters the opportunity to decide whether establishing the UUT is the appropriate way to provide additional locally controlled funding for City services.

This measure is supported by representatives of our local youth organizations and local business owners who recognize the importance of maintaining essential City services and investing in the future of our community.

To preserve services, I urge you to vote YES on the SG ballot measure.
