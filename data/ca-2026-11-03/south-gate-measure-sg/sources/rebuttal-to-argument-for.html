REBUTTAL TO ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE SG

Vote NO On Measure SG.

Politicians claim Measure SG will protect essential services. The tax's language tells a different story: the 8% utility users tax is a general tax. Not one dollar is guaranteed for 911 response, streets, crossing guards, parks, or youth programs.

Affordability IS the issue. South Gate families can't afford another tax increase. L.A. County just raised the countywide sales tax, increasing South Gate's rate from 10.75% to 11.25%. Rent, groceries, gasoline, and utility costs keep climbing. Politicians keep finding ways to make South Gate less affordable.

South Gate calls this a "utility tax." It's much more.

Measure SG would make our community less affordable by adding 8% to the cost of essential services, including electricity, natural gas, water, phone, cable television, and all streaming subscription services. Unless defeated, families will pay about $600 more every year. Working families, younger low-income residents, renters, and businesses would all pay more.

Measure SG never expires. City Hall admits employee costs and operating expenses are rising but asks residents to absorb the consequences forever.

Census data places South Gate's median annual household income at $74,571. Nearly one in four employees received more than $100,000/yr. in total pay. At least 25 employees received more than $200,000/yr. in total pay.

City leaders need to control payroll and benefit costs, cut waste, balance the budget, and account for Measure P, the permanent general sales tax promoted to preserve public safety and prevent service cuts.

VOTE NO on Measure SG.
