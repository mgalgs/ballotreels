CITY OF SOUTH GATE – (1)
VOTE PASSAGE – Majority Vote 50%+1
SG     SOUTH GATE FISCAL EMERGENCY STREET MAINTENANCE, YOUTH
       PROGRAMS, PARKS FUNDING PROTECTION MEASURE. Shall an ordinance
       preventing cuts to City of South Gate services including maintaining streets and
       sidewalks; maintaining school crossing guards, parks/ recreation programs, police 911
       and support services, and other General Fund uses by establishing an 8% utility user
       tax, exempting low income seniors, annually providing approximately $10,400,000 until
       ended by voters; requiring independent audits, public disclosure of all spending, local
       control, to benefit South Gate residents, be adopted? Supporters: Gil Hurtado,
       Councilmember; Hector De La Torre, Former Councilmember; South Gate POA; South
       Gate MEA; South Gate AYSO Opponents: California Taxpayers Association; Los
       Angeles County Taxpayers Association.
