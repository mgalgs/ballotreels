## Ballot question and supporters/opponents (verbatim, from the county's final measures list)

> SOUTH GATE FISCAL EMERGENCY STREET MAINTENANCE, YOUTH PROGRAMS, PARKS FUNDING PROTECTION MEASURE. Shall an ordinance preventing cuts to City of South Gate services including maintaining streets and sidewalks; maintaining school crossing guards, parks/recreation programs, police 911 and support services, and other General Fund uses by establishing an 8% utility user tax, exempting low income seniors, annually providing approximately $10,400,000 until ended by voters; requiring independent audits, public disclosure of all spending, local control, to benefit South Gate residents, be adopted?
>
> Supporters: Gil Hurtado, Councilmember; Hector De La Torre, Former Councilmember; South Gate POA; South Gate MEA; South Gate AYSO
>
> Opponents: California Taxpayers Association; Los Angeles County Taxpayers Association.

Vote threshold: Majority, 50%+1 (Resolution 2026-35-CC body text: "requires the approval of a simple majority (50% plus one) of those voting on it"). The resolution placing it on the ballot separately required a 2/3 council vote under Govt. Code §53724(b) — a different, internal requirement that does not change the voters' passage threshold.

## What's present and what's missing

- **Full ordinance text**: present (Exhibit A, within `resolution.pdf`).
- **Resolution placing the measure on the ballot**: present (`resolution.pdf`).
- **Impartial analysis**: missing — directed by Resolution 2026-35-CC §5 (City Attorney Raul F. Salinas), but the document itself was not located anywhere on the City's site.
- **Argument for**: **confirmed filed** — five named supporters in the county's final list, after the filing deadline. The filed text is missing, not found — no index/listing page for filed arguments was found on the City's document server, and guessed filenames all 404'd.
- **Argument against**: **confirmed filed**, despite organized opposition (California Taxpayers Association, LA County Taxpayers Association). The filed text is missing, not found, same as above.
- **Rebuttals**: status unconfirmed either way — California law would allow a rebuttal on each side since both a for and an against argument exist, but no rebuttal text (or confirmation one was filed) was found. `notice-no-more-arguments.pdf` confirms only that a deadline for primary arguments existed, not whether rebuttals followed.
- **Tax rate statement**: not applicable — SG is a general utility users tax, not a property-tax-secured bond measure.
