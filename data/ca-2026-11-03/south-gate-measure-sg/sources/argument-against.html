ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE SG

Vote NO on Measure SG.

South Gate families are already stretched thin by rising rents, groceries, gasoline, and utility bills. L.A. County just raised the countywide sales tax, increasing South Gate's rate from 10.75% to 11.25%.

Residents pay some of the nation's highest gas taxes, plus state income and property taxes. City Hall politicians want another tax.

Measure SG would impose an 8% tax on you for essential services like electricity, natural gas, water, phone, cable, satellite, internet-based calling, and streaming subscriptions, including Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, YouTube Premium, Spotify, ViX, Canela TV, and Estrella TV. That's about $600 per household per year, when families can least afford it.

This tax has no expiration date. It's permanent. It would continue forever unless voters organize and fund another election to repeal it. That's not temporary relief.

Measure SG is a general tax. Politicians say the money will protect police, 911 response, streets, parks, and youth programs. No revenue is legally guaranteed for those purposes. Politicians may spend it on anything.

Politicians have failed to control rising payroll and benefit costs, cut waste, or balance the budget. Before demanding more from working families, renters, seniors, and small businesses, City Hall should account for Measure P funds, a permanent general sales tax intended to preserve public safety and prevent service cuts.

Broad utility taxes hit vulnerable residents hardest. Measure SG's exemptions are too narrow. Some low-income seniors may qualify, but working families, younger low-income residents, renters, and local businesses would still pay higher rates.

South Gate deserves accountability, not another blank check. We can support essential services without approving a permanent tax that lacks guaranteed spending protections.

Send the politicians a clear message: live within its means, protect taxpayers, and prioritize essential services.

VOTE NO on Measure SG.
