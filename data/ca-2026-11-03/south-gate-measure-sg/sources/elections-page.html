FAQs

When are elections held in South Gate?

Elections are held in odd-numbered years and in accordance with established city ordinances. Elections are held in staggered terms, meaning that every 2 years in the odd-numbered years, there are either 2 seats for City Council, 1 seat for City Treasurer, and 1 seat for City Clerk; or 3 seats for City Council up for reelection.

How do I register to vote?

Registration forms are available at the City Clerk’s Office in City Hall (8650 California Avenue). Your local post office and the Los Angeles County Registrar Recorder (12400 E Imperial Highway, Norwalk) also have the forms available. Voter registration is processed by the California Secretary of State's Office.

Register to Vote

How do I find my polling place during an election?

Find your polling place by calling the City Clerk's Office at 323-563-9573, or online.

Find Your Polling Place

What is the deadline to register to vote?

In California the deadline to register to vote for any election is 15 days before Election Day.

What is a provisional ballot?

While provisional voting may be relatively new in some areas of the country, California's provisional voting statutes have been in effect since 1984. First, provisional voting ensures that no properly registered voter is denied their right to cast a ballot if that voter's name is not on the polling place roster due to a clerical, processing, computer, or other error. Second, provisional voting allows elections officials to ensure that no voter votes twice, either intentionally or inadvertently, in a given election. 

What if I want to work at the polls? How do I apply?

Election Workers play an essential role in elections and in our democracy. They are trusted community members who assist the in-person voting experience throughout Los Angeles County.
