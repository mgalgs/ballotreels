REBUTTAL TO ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE SG

Utility companies opposing South Gate's Utility Users Tax under the guise of protecting taxpayers is hypocrisy. These companies collect millions from South Gate customers and are now spending money through a political action committee to convince residents that our community should not invest in essential services.

Consider the source.

Think about it: Utility companies can increase what they charge South Gate families without asking South Gate voters for permission. Yet when our City needs additional revenue to protect essential services, we must go directly to voters and ask for your approval. That is accountability.

Now, utility interests are spending your money to defeat a local funding solution while ignoring the rising costs, placing enormous pressure on our City budget.

South Gate faces a structural deficit of $9.4 million. The City has already made difficult cuts: more than 30 positions eliminated, crossing guards eliminated, overtime reduced, and programs and services cut. These are real cuts affecting services our community depends on.

Measure SG provides locally controlled funding that stays in South Gate and can support police and 911 response, streets, parks, youth and senior programs, and other essential city services.

Yes, Measure SG is a general tax. Budget decisions will be made through a transparent public process, with accountability directly to South Gate residents.

The real question is: Who should determine South Gate's future-our residents or utility companies spending money to defeat a local solution?

South Gate deserves financial stability, safe neighborhoods, maintained streets, and strong community services.

Keep South Gate dollars working for South Gate. Vote YES on Measure SG.

GIL HURTADO
Councilmember
