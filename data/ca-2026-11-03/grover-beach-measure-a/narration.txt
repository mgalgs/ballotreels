Measure A asks the voters of this city whether the city clerk should be elected instead of appointed.
Measure A is a citizen initiative that reached the ballot by petition.
The city clerk keeps the official city records and serves as the elections official for the city.
Today the city clerk is appointed, not elected.
Voters would elect the city clerk to four-year terms.
The city would first hold a special election to fill the office until the next regular city election.
Measure A needs a simple majority to pass.
The impartial analysis does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Supporters say an appointed clerk depends on the city manager for the job, while an elected clerk answers to voters.
Opponents say Measure A shrinks the pool of applicants to local voters, and drops requirements of competence and experience.
A yes vote makes the city clerk an elected office with a four-year term.
A no vote keeps the city clerk an appointed office.
