## Quoted facts

- **Who votes:** Grover Beach voters.
- **Ballot question** (resolution, Section 1): "CITY OF GROVER BEACH INITIATIVE MEASURE A-26 Shall the current appointive Office of the City Clerk be required to be an elected position?"
- **Impartial analysis** (p. 28): "Initiative Measure A-26 asks the voters whether the City of Grover Beach City Clerk shall be an elected position for a four-year term of office." … "On March 14, 2024, a citizen's ballot initiative was proposed to make the City of Grover Beach City Clerk an elected position for a four-year term of office. The initiative petition gathered sufficient signatures for placement on the ballot and was certified by the Deputy City Clerk of the City of San Luis Obispo given the conflict of interest with the currently appointed City Clerk validating signatures for this initiative." … "Since the City's incorporation in 1959, the City Clerk has been an appointed position"
- **Arguments:** for, against, and both rebuttals printed (pp. 29–31).
