ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE SM

Our Schools Are Our Community's Greatest Asset. Vote Yes on Measure SM!

Our public schools educate our children, strengthen our neighborhoods, and protect our home values. That's why we're asking you to vote YES on Measure SM.

Some of our school buildings are more than 70 years old. Roofs leak. Pipes fail. Heating and air conditioning break down on the hottest days of the year. Our students deserve better.

Vote YES on Measure SM to repair and modernize Bellflower Unified School District schools, ensuring students can learn in safe, functional, and up-to-date classrooms.

Measure SM protects taxpayers.

- Every Dollar Stays Local. By law, this money cannot be taken by the State and spent elsewhere.
- Independent Oversight. An independent citizens' oversight committee. Mandatory annual financial and performance audits. Public disclosure of every dollar spent.
- Schools, Not Administrators. No administrator salaries. No pensions. No overhead. Period.

Measure SM improves our children's schools.

- Safety First. Modernize fire alarms and sprinklers, campus security, lighting, safe drinking water, and access for students with disabilities.
- Basic Repairs. Fix deteriorating roofs, plumbing, sewer and gas lines, and outdated electrical systems.
- Room to Learn. Upgrade classrooms, science labs, libraries, arts spaces, and career-technical facilities to prepare students for college, the skilled trades, and good-paying careers.

Measure SM puts local people to work.

The District's Community Workforce Agreement sets a goal that 40% of construction jobs go to qualified local residents, veterans, and pre-apprenticeship graduates. Our tax dollars stay here, working for Bellflower School District families.

Strong schools build strong neighborhoods. They protect property values. They will better prepare every student for college, skilled trades careers, and the modern economy.

Join us. VOTE YES ON MEASURE SM.
