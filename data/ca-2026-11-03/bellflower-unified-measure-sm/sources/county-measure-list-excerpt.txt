BELLFLOWER UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT – (1)
VOTE PASSAGE – 55% of the Votes Cast
SM     With funds that cannot be taken by the State and spent elsewhere, shall Bellflower
       Unified School District's measure to repair/replace deteriorating roofs, plumbing, heating,
       ventilation and electrical systems; upgrade classrooms, labs and career training facilities;
       and, improve access for students with disabilities be adopted, authorizing $135 million in
       bonds levying $49 per $100,000 assessed value ($8 million annually) while outstanding,
       with legal rates, audits, citizen oversight, no funds for administrator salaries and full
       disclosure of all spending? Supporters: Asm. José Solache; Sup. Janice Hahn; Dr.
       Robinson, BUSD Teacher; John Young, BTA Pres.; Mayra Garza, BUSD School Board
       Pres. Opponents: None submitted.
