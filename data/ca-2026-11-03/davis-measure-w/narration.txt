Measure W asks the voters of this city whether to let about 232 acres of farmland become a new neighborhood.
City rules require a vote of the people to turn farmland or open space into urban uses.
The site is now set aside for farming, and the city could annex it if Measure W passes.
The plan allows 1,250 homes, at least 250 of them affordable, plus a park with a gym, trails and shops.
These features could not be changed much later without another vote.
The voter guide for this county does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Supporters say Measure W brings homes young families can afford, and a regional park funded by the developer.
No argument against Measure W was filed by the July deadline, so only an argument in favor appears.
A yes vote supports the general plan changes and the project features.
A no vote opposes the general plan changes and the project features.
