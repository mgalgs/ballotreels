Proposition 43 is about the votes some local taxes need to pass.
A special tax is a local tax used for one specific purpose.
Today, special taxes that local governments propose need two-thirds of the vote.
But court decisions let special taxes that voters propose pass with a majority.
From 2027, Proposition 43 would require two-thirds for voter-proposed special taxes too.
The Legislative Analyst's Office says local tax revenue could be lower than it otherwise would be, by an unknown amount.
Supporters say Proposition 43 restores Proposition 13's two-thirds vote for local special taxes, ending a loophole.
Opponents say Proposition 43 lets one-third of voters block funding for local services a majority supports.
A yes vote means certain taxes would require two-thirds approval by voters.
A no vote means certain taxes could continue to pass with a majority vote.
