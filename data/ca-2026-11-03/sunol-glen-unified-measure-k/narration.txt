Sunol Glen Unified School District Measure K asks the district's voters whether to approve $6.1 million in school bonds.
The district would borrow the money and repay it, with interest, from a tax on property in the district.
Measure K needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would finish the Main Building retrofit, replace plumbing, electrical, sewer and heating systems, and upgrade classrooms and restrooms.
The district's tax statement estimates an average tax of $33.54 per $100,000 of assessed value, and at most $35, through 2058-2059.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $13.8 million.
No argument against Measure K was submitted for this election's Alameda County Composite Voter Information Guide.
Supporters say Measure K finishes the Main Building retrofit and modernizes classrooms at low tax rates.
A yes vote authorizes $6.1 million in bonds, and a tax of about $35 per $100,000 to repay them.
A no vote authorizes neither the bonds nor the tax of about $35 per $100,000 to repay them.
