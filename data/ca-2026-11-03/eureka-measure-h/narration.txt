Measure H asks the voters of this city whether to let the city council set the pay of the mayor and council members.
The city charter sets the mayor's pay at $625 a month, and each council member's at $500.
Those amounts date from 1988, and changing them takes a charter amendment.
Measure H needs a majority of the vote to pass.
Measure H would let the council raise the pay to $1,250 a month for the mayor and $1,000 for each council member, which could take effect in January 2027.
Later yearly changes would follow review by the finance advisory committee of the city.
The official voter guide for this election does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
The voter guide prints no argument for Measure H.
The voter guide prints no argument against Measure H.
A yes vote allows the higher pay, and yearly changes after the committee's review.
A no vote keeps the current pay limits.
