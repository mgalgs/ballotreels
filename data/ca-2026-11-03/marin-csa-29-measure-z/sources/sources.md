Measure Z — County Service Area 29 (Paradise Cay) — maintenance-dredging special parcel tax, increased and continued for 10 years (FY2027-28 to FY2036-37), 2/3 vote
Retrieved 2026-10-30. Publisher of every file: County of Marin, Board of Supervisors (Granicus agenda/minutes portal, marin.granicus.com, view_id=33), unless noted.
Letter "Z" comes from the county's list-of-measures page (see ../../README.md); the BOS documents do not carry the letter.

BALLOT QUESTION (verbatim, Resolution No. 2026-032; heading printed as "COUNTY SERVICE AREA NO. 29 / PARADISE BAY DREDGING SPECIAL TAX MEASURE" — "Paradise Bay" [sic] in the source):
  For ongoing maintenance dredging in the principal waterways of Paradise Cay along with the north and south entry channels to San Francisco Bay, shall the measure for an annual parcel tax in County Service Area No. 29, for $2,200 per lot per year commencing in July 2027 providing an estimated $272,800 and automatically increasing 3% each year thereafter, for a total of ten years, be adopted?
