Measure Z asks the voters of this service area whether to approve a yearly parcel tax for maintenance dredging, for ten years.
The service area is made up of waterfront lots, which already pay a dredging tax that voters approved in 2016.
A county staff report says that tax is $1,900.12 per lot, and it ends after fiscal year 2026-2027.
The county board of supervisors placed Measure Z on the ballot in May 2026; it needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
Starting in July 2027, each lot would pay $2,200 a year, rising 3 percent each year, for a total of ten years.
The tax would pay for ongoing maintenance dredging of the main waterways and the two entry channels to the bay.
The ballot question estimates the tax would provide about $272,800.

A yes vote approves the parcel tax of $2,200 per lot a year, rising 3 percent a year, for ten years.
A no vote rejects the new tax.
