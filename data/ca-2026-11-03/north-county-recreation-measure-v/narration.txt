Measure V asks the voters of this recreation and park district whether to approve a parcel tax of $82 a year.
The district manages neighborhood parks and community facilities, including a community senior center.
Measure V needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
Each property in the district would pay $82 a year.
The tax would continue until the district's voters repeal it.
The money would go to expanding the senior center, clean and safe parks and restrooms, disability access, recreation programs, and staff to maintain the district's properties.
The ballot question says the tax would raise about $300,000 a year.
Supporters say the gym that serves as an emergency shelter needs a new heating and cooling system, and the senior center has outgrown its space.
No argument against Measure V appears among the official documents gathered for this video; the county list of measures links only the argument in favor.
A yes vote is in favor of an annual parcel tax of $82 per parcel, as the measure sets out.
A no vote is against the parcel tax.
