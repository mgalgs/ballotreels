Impartial Analysis of Measure V

A Parcel Tax pursuant to Section 4 of Article XIIIA of the California Constitution and California Government Code sections 50079 et seq. and 50075 et seq., otherwise known as the Parcel Tax Law, authorizes a school district, upon approval by two-thirds of the electorate voting on the measure, to levy a qualified special tax for specified purposes.

The North County Public Recreation District Board (hereafter "the District") has placed this measure on the ballot, proposing a parcel tax be levied in the amount of $82 on each property situated within the district boundaries, to remain in effect until officially ended by district voters. The parcel tax will raise a dedicated, predictable revenue stream.

The Parcel tax funds will be used for such things as: expanding the Community Senior Center; keeping neighborhood parks clean, safe and secure; upgrading district park infrastructure and community space to meet Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) requirements; keeping park restrooms clean; enriching recreational programming, activities, classes and sports leagues for all age groups; and attracting, retaining and supporting qualified staff to maintain district properties.

The highest tax rate to be levied is $82 per parcel, per year. The tax will remain in effect until officially ended by district voters.

A "Yes" vote on this measure is a vote in favor of the issuance of an annual parcel tax of $82 per parcel, as set forth in Measure V.

A "No" vote on this measure is a vote against the issuance of an annual parcel tax of $82 per parcel, as set forth in Measure V.
