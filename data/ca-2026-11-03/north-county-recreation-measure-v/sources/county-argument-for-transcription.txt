Argument in favor of Measure V

Argument for Measure V

Vote YES on Measure V—our parks, seniors, youth, and emergency preparedness cannot wait. North County Recreation and Park District (NCRPD) families depend on safe parks, programs, and facilities ready when disaster strikes. Measure V invests in a stronger community.

Our parks and facilities are essential to our quality of life. Measure V provides local funding to maintain facilities, strengthen emergency preparedness, and expand programs for families and seniors.

Strengthening Disaster Preparedness

When disaster strikes, the NCRPD gym serves as a designated emergency shelter. The gym needs an HVAC replacement estimated at more than $800,000. Measure V will help fund this improvement, so families have safe, climate-controlled shelter when needed most.

Expanding Senior Services

Our Senior Center has outgrown its space. Measure V will help fund a new modular building, giving seniors a place to gather, socialize, stay active, and support their well-being.

Voting YES on Measure V will:
• Expand Senior Center space with a new modular building for activities and socialization.
• Strengthen emergency preparedness by helping fund the gym's $800,000-plus HVAC replacement.
• Maintain clean, safe parks and restrooms for children and families.
• Enhance youth and senior programs that keep residents active and connected.
• Improve disability accessibility throughout District Park facilities.

Measure V costs less than one Starbucks coffee a week, just $82 per parcel per year, about $6.83 per month or $1.58 per week.

For that small weekly investment, Measure V will provide approximately $300,000 each year for parks, senior services, youth programs, accessibility improvements, and emergency preparedness.

The funding stays local to serve the community through NCRPD facilities and programs and is subject to public oversight.

Our seniors need more space. Our emergency shelter needs critical upgrades. Our parks need continued investment. We cannot afford to wait.

Vote YES on Measure V—for our seniors, children, and a safer North County.
