Measure L asks the voters of this road zone whether to replace its $225 yearly road assessment with a $500 tax per parcel.
The county formed the zone in 1989 to pay for road improvements and maintenance.
The county counts 57 registered voters living in the zone.
The Board of Supervisors placed Measure L on the ballot at the request of the zone's advisory committee.
Measure L needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The new tax would apply to every parcel, built on or not, starting in the 2027-2028 fiscal year.
The money could pay only for the roads in the zone, and the tax would last until voters change or repeal it.
If Measure L fails, the $225 assessment stays.
The county attorney's impartial analysis expects the tax to raise about $14,500 a year.
No argument against Measure L was submitted.
No argument for Measure L was submitted.
A yes vote authorizes the tax of $500 per parcel a year.
A no vote is against the tax, and the $225 assessment stays.
