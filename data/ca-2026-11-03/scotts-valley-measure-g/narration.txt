City of Scotts Valley Measure G asks voters in the city whether to raise the hotel tax from 11 to 13 percent.
The hotel tax is paid by hotel and motel guests who stay in the city for fewer than 30 days.
All of the hotel tax goes into the general fund for city services.
Measure G would raise the rate to 13 percent, which is $13 a night on a $100 room.
Measure G is a general tax, so it needs a simple majority to pass.
The measure documents that Santa Cruz County Elections published do not include a cost estimate for the measure.
No argument against Measure G appears among the measure documents that Santa Cruz County Elections has published for the November election.
Supporters say Measure G is paid by visitors, not local families, and keeps all of the money in Scotts Valley.
A yes vote raises the hotel tax from 11 to 13 percent.
A no vote leaves the hotel tax unchanged.
