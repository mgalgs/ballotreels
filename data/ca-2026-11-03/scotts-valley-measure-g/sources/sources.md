## Quoted facts

- **Who votes / threshold:** Notice: "City of Scotts Valley (Majority Voter Approval Required)".
- **Ballot question:** "Shall the City of Scotts Valley adopt an ordinance amending Section 3.24.030 of the Municipal Code raising the transient occupancy tax rate from 11% to 13%?"
- **Impartial analysis (City Attorney Kirsten Powell, signed Aug 12, 2026):**
  - "would amend the Scotts Valley Municipal Code by raising the Transient Occupancy Tax (TOT) rate from the current rate of 11 percent to 13 percent."
  - "The Transient Occupancy Tax is a tax paid by hotel and motel guests who spend fewer than 30 consecutive days in a hotel or motel in the City."
  - "a hotel guest currently pays $11 tax per night on a $100 hotel room. If the ballot measure is adopted, the hotel guest would pay $13".
  - "All of the City's transient occupancy tax revenue is deposited into the City's General Fund ... This ballot measure therefore proposes a "general purpose tax" ... and requires a simple majority vote for adoption."
  - "This ballot measure has been placed on the ballot by the Scotts Valley City Council."
- **Argument in favor** (transcribed from scan, p. 1): "Paid only by visitors, Measure G protects Scotts Valley's parks, roads, public safety, and quality of life." "generating an estimated $300,000 a year for our community." (Signers are on pp. 2–3 of the scan; not transcribed.)
