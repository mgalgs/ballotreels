Argument in Favor of Measure G

Paid only by visitors, Measure G protects Scotts Valley's parks, roads, public safety, and quality of life. Please vote Yes on G.

Scotts Valley is a small town that provides great quality of life — but Sacramento shortchanges us. The City retains only 6.5% of the property taxes generated here, one of the lowest shares in California. That leaves us scrambling every year just to keep our roads paved, our parks maintained, and our police and fire crews ready to respond.

Measure G helps fix this the right way: a 2% increase in our hotel tax, from 11% to 13%. That's just $2 more per night on a $100 room — paid by visitors passing through, not by the families who call Scotts Valley home. Our rates stay in line with neighboring cities, so we remain competitive.

Think about it: visitors drive our roads, enjoy our parks, and rely on our police and fire departments too. It's only fair they help cover the cost — generating an estimated $300,000 a year for our community. With Measure G 100% of funds stay right here in Scotts Valley! Sacramento gets nothing.

This is local money for local priorities, decided by local voters — not politicians in the state capital. It's a small, sensible step that helps preserve the small-town quality of life we all moved here for, without asking neighbors and local families to pay more.

Please join local community leaders in voting YES on Measure G.
