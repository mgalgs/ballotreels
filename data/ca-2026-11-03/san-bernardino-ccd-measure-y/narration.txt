San Bernardino Community College District Measure Y asks the district's voters whether to approve $898 million in college bonds.
The district would borrow the money and repay it from a tax on property in the district.
Measure Y needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would go to listed projects at San Bernardino Valley College and Crafton Hills College, such as career training facilities and asbestos removal.
The district's tax statement estimates a tax of $25 per $100,000 of assessed value each year, through 2058-2059.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $1.9 billion.
No argument against Measure Y was filed for the San Bernardino County voter guide.
Supporters say Measure Y keeps local college and job training affordable and campuses safe.
A yes vote means the district may issue the bonds.
A no vote means the district may not issue the bonds.
