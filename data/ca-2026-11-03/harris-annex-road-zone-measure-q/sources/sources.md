## Facts and the passages that establish them

### Ballot question

S1 PDF p. 75 (same wording in S3 Section 5, p. 3):

> MEASURE Q
> Shall the County of San Diego, beginning Fiscal Year 2027-28 and annually thereafter, levy a special tax of up to $1,000 per developed / $375 per undeveloped parcel within Permanent Road Division No. 1000, Zone No. 9B – Harris Annex, replacing the existing assessment? The tax may be adjusted by up to 5% annually based on the Los Angeles Construction Cost Index or a comparable Southern California index and remain until modified or dissolved by law.
> Tax – YES / Tax – NO

### Vote required

S1 PDF p. 75: "This measure requires 2/3rds Majority of those voting on the measure."

### Who votes on it

S3 Section 5 (p. 2):

> the Board of Supervisors hereby calls and orders an election to be conducted in the Permanent Road Division No. 1000, Zone No. 9B – Harris Annex and be consolidated with the November 3, 2026 Gubernatorial General Election, for the purpose of submitting the proposed Ordinance, attached as Exhibit A and incorporated herein by reference to the registered voters of said PRD Zone.

S3 recital (p. 2): "The Board of Supervisors desires to submit to the registered voters of Permanent Road Division No. 1000, Zone No. 9B – Harris Annex the question of whether a special tax for road maintenance shall be approved and levied within the PRD Zone". Section 6(b): "there shall be a single precinct consisting of the area within the PRD Zone."

In short: the registered voters of Permanent Road Division No. 1000, Zone No. 9B – Harris Annex.

### Who placed it on the ballot

S1 PDF p. 75: "The Board of Supervisors for the County of San Diego placed this Measure on the ballot."

S3 p. 5: "ON MOTION of Supervisor Desmond, seconded by Supervisor Montgomery Steppe, the above Resolution was passed and adopted by the Board of Supervisors, County of San Diego, State of California, on this 24th day of June 2026, by the following vote: AYES: Aguirre, Anderson, Lawson-Remer, Montgomery Steppe, Desmond".

S3 recitals (p. 1): the Board approved formation of "CSA 9B – Harris Annex" on March 20, 1979; on June 21, 2000 it transferred CSA zones to Permanent Road Division No. 1000 and adopted Ordinance No. 9165; "following a community outreach event conducted by the Department of Public Works on July 2, 2025, the Road Committee elected to pursue a special tax assessment within the PRD Zone".

### Impartial analysis

S1 PDF p. 75, "COUNTY COUNSEL IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS / PERMANENT ROAD DIVISION ZONE 9B HARRIS ANNEX SPECIAL TAX MEASURE". Key statements:

> If this Measure is approved by two-thirds of the votes cast, parcels within Permanent Road Division Zone 9B Harris Annex shall pay a maximum parcel tax of $1,000 per parcel for each developed parcel and $375 per parcel for each undeveloped parcel commencing in Fiscal Year 2027-2028. The stated purpose of the special tax is to fund ongoing road maintenance, which includes administrative expenses.

> The special tax will replace the existing assessment imposed in the Permanent Road Division by repealing Ordinance No. 9165. Total estimated revenue derived from the special tax from the 53 developed and 3 undeveloped parcels currently in the Permanent Road Division for the first fiscal year is $54,125.

> A "YES" vote is in favor of authorizing the County of San Diego to replace the existing assessment with a special tax of up to $1,000 per developed parcel and up to $375 per undeveloped parcel to fund ongoing road maintenance within the Permanent Road Division.

### Arguments and rebuttals

S1 PDF p. 75: "NO ARGUMENTS WERE FILED WITH THE REGISTRAR OF VOTERS." No argument for or against and no rebuttals are printed.

Neither is archived; they would likely be in the Board of Supervisors' June 24, 2026 agenda materials (item 06), unconfirmed.

### Tax rate statement or fiscal analysis

No separate statement. The fiscal facts are County Counsel's (above): $1,000 / $375 per parcel, 53 developed and 3 undeveloped parcels, $54,125 in the first year, up to 5% a year adjustment.
