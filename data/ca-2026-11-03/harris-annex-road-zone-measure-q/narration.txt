Harris Annex Road Zone Measure Q asks the zone's voters whether to replace its road assessment with a special tax on each parcel.
Today, the zone's 53 developed and 3 undeveloped parcels pay an assessment set by a county ordinance.
Measure Q needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
Measure Q would replace the assessment with a yearly tax of up to $1,000 per developed parcel and $375 per undeveloped parcel.
The tax would pay for ongoing road maintenance, including administrative costs, starting in the 2027-2028 fiscal year.
The tax could rise by up to 5 percent a year, following a construction cost index.
The impartial analysis estimates the tax would raise $54,125 in its first year.
No argument for Measure Q was filed with the Registrar of Voters.
No argument against Measure Q was filed with the Registrar of Voters.
A yes vote is in favor of replacing the existing assessment with the special tax for road maintenance.
A no vote is against replacing the existing assessment with that special tax.
