Measure D asks the voters of this life support district whether to raise its parcel tax for ambulance and emergency medical services.
The district already levies a parcel tax, which the two counties it serves collect for it.
Measure D needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
Measure D would set the tax at $101 per unit of benefit, based on how each parcel's land is used.
A single-family home counts as three units, a hotel or restaurant as 20, and undeveloped farm or timber land as none.
The rate would rise each July with inflation, and the money could pay only for emergency medical services and the district's operating costs.
The measure question estimates that the tax would raise about $1.97 million a year.
No argument against Measure D was submitted for the county Registrar of Voters' printed voter guide pages on this measure.
Supporters say emergency calls have doubled and cost per call rose over 25 percent, without any parcel tax increase.
A yes vote is a vote in favor of authorizing the special tax.
A no vote is a vote against authorizing the special tax.
