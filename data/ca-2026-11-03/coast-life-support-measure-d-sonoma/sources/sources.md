## Facts and quotes

**Ballot question (p1):** "Shall Measure D (the Coast Life Support District Emergency Medical Services Tax) be adopted? Allows Coast Life Support District to increase the current special parcel tax rate on real property to $ 101 per unit of benefit, for emergency medical services, including ambulance, life support and medical transport purposes and other necessary operating expenses, providing an estimated $1,972,934 annually, until the tax is amended or repealed; authorizes annual increases for inflation; and raises the District's appropriations limit to permit spending of the tax revenue."

**Vote needed:** "2/3 (Two-Thirds) Needed to Pass" (p1); "This measure must be adopted by two thirds (2/3) of the voters." (p2)

**Who votes:** voters of the Coast Life Support District, in Mendocino and Sonoma counties (the analysis is signed by both County Counsels; the argument calls CLSD "a bi-county Special District").

**Impartial analysis (p2), key statements:**
- "If approved, the measure would authorize the District to increase its existing parcel tax to one hundred and one dollars ($101) per unit of benefit. Units of benefit are assessed to parcels of land within the District based on the type of land use". Schedule includes "Single Family Dwelling 3", "Hotels, motels, restaurants, golf courses, theaters 20", undeveloped land 0.
- "...on July 1 of each year following adoption, the special tax rate shall be increased by an amount equal to the annual percentage increase in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U), San Francisco-Oakland-Hayward".
- "The measure also increases the District's appropriations limit by the amount of revenue raised through the increased special tax."
- "This measure was placed on the ballot by the Board of Directors for the Coast Life Support District."
- Dated June 3, 2026; signed Katharine L. Elliott, Mendocino County Counsel (by Matthew Kiedrowski) and Joshua A. Myers, Sonoma County Counsel (by Adam Brand).

**Argument in favor (p2):** signed Andrea Lunsford, Bronwyn Golly (EMS Chief, CLSD), Barbara Burkey, Janis Dolphin, Naomi Schwartz (Member of CLSD BOD).

**Argument against:** "No Argument Was Submitted Against Measure D" (p2).
