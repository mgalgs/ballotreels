## Quoted passages

- Question (p.1): "Shall a measure that directs, to the extent technically and financially feasible, the use of ranked choice voting for City Council Member and Mayoral Elections occurring after January 1, 2028, be adopted?"
- Who placed it (p.5): "The City Council of the City of Irvine has placed a measure before the voters that would amend the Irvine Municipal Code to use ranked choice voting (RCV) for City Council member and mayoral elections after January 1, 2028, if certain technical and financial conditions are satisfied."
- Analysis (p.5): "The City of Irvine (City) currently uses a plurality voting system for City Council member and mayoral elections."
- Analysis (p.5): "The additional cost for an RCV election is not more than 0.23% of the City’s general fund budget for the fiscal year during which the proposed RCV election would be held (RCV Cost Cap). For reference, 0.23% of the City’s fiscal year 2026-2027 general fund budget of approximately $312.1 million is approximately $717,790."
- Analysis (p.5): "The proposed measure requires that RCV ballots allow the ranking of a minimum of five candidates, that write-in candidates be permitted, and that voters receive specific instructions regarding RCV."
- Effect (p.5): "If a majority of voters vote “Yes,” then this measure will take effect ten (10) days after election results are declared by the City Council."
