Proposition G asks San Francisco voters whether to reopen the Upper Great Highway to private vehicles on weekdays.
Today that stretch of road is Sunset Dunes Park, closed to private vehicles at all times.
Proposition G would open the road to private vehicles from Monday morning until Friday evening.
Weekends and holidays would stay closed to private vehicles, and coastal permits would be needed first.
The Controller's Statement estimates one-time costs of about $9.8 million, less than 1 percent of the roughly $7 billion General Fund.
Yearly costs would rise between $500,000 and $1.9 million, also less than 1 percent, while park staffing costs would fall.
Opponents say Proposition G spends nearly $10 million to remove a coastal park whose visits are mostly on weekdays.
Supporters say Proposition G restores a weekday road and a weekend park, because traffic injuries in the Sunset have risen.
If you vote yes, you want to close the park on weekdays to reopen the highway to private vehicles.
If you vote no, you do not want the city to make these changes.
