Argument in Favor of Measure _

YES on 555 Kelly: The fiscally responsible choice

This referendum is not about voters choosing whether or not to build 555 Kelly. This is because 555 Kelly is an irreplaceable part of Half Moon Bay's legally required Housing Element and the State has already sued Half Moon Bay to force compliance with State law, including affordable housing projects like 555 Kelly. Your Yes vote will help to ensure that we do not bankrupt the City with fines, penalties and legal costs before the project is finished.

What is 555 Kelly?
- 555 Kelly provides 40 affordable apartments at no cost to taxpayers for senior farmworkers who have worked in our fields for decades.
- It will include a community center, with a full kitchen, that will be available to the entire community.
- The project has been fully reviewed and approved.

Why your Yes Vote is critical for Half Moon Bay:
- The financial risks are not hypothetical. The State of California has already sued Half Moon Bay for not meeting our affordable housing requirements.
- State law bars cities from rejecting housing like this, even by referendum. Courts can force approval anyway, and fine the city up to $10,000 per unit, per month.
- A No vote could trigger Builders Remedy, which would result in unchecked development being forced on Half Moon Bay, as well as diverting potentially millions in HMB taxpayer money from vital services to pay legal fees, fines and penalties.

The bottom line:
- Under California State law the city must build more affordable housing.
- Half Moon Bay taxpayers will pay nothing to construct, maintain, or manage 555 Kelly.
- It's a chance to give back to our senior farmworkers while being fiscally responsible.
- Your Yes vote can prevent a potentially huge financial loss while helping to solve the city's legal issues instead of making them worse.

Please vote YES on Measure _
