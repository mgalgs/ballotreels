# Measure Q — City of Half Moon Bay (referendum on Ordinance No. 2026-01, 555 Kelly Avenue farmworker senior housing)

- Jurisdiction: City of Half Moon Bay.
- Kind: referendum. Voters decide whether to adopt Ordinance No. 2026-01, which approves agreements with Mercy Housing California 110, L.P. for a 40-unit affordable senior farmworker housing project on City-owned land at 535-555 Kelly Avenue. A "yes" vote adopts the ordinance.
- Who votes: the voters of the City of Half Moon Bay. Resolution, p. 2, Sec. 1: "The City Council hereby approves the submission of Ordinance No. 2026-01, as set forth in Exhibit A, to the voters of the City of Half Moon Bay at the November 3, 2026, general municipal election."
- Vote threshold: majority. Resolution, p. 3, Sec. 4: "the vote requirement for the Measure to pass is a majority (50%+1) of the votes cast."
- Placed by: a citizen referendum petition filed "on behalf of "Let Half Moon Bay Voters Decide"" (p. 1); the City Council submitted the ordinance to the voters by Resolution No. C-2026-63.
- Election: November 3, 2026.

## Ballot question

> Shall Ordinance No. 2026-01, approving agreements with Mercy Housing California 110, L.P. (including a Ground Lease of Real Property for 99 years at a rate of $1/year, which is customary, and Affordable Housing and Property Disposition Agreement) providing for the development/operation of a Senior Affordable 40-Unit Residential Project for Agricultural Workers, which includes 2,653-square foot Community Space, at 535-555 Kelly Avenue in the City of Half Moon Bay, and authorizing other related actions, be adopted?

(resolution-full-text.pdf p. 3, Sec. 2(a); transcribed from the scan.)

## Impartial analysis

"CITY ATTORNEY IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE Q", signed "Denise S. Bazzano, City Attorney, City of Half Moon Bay" (impartial-analysis.pdf p. 2).

- "On May 5, 2026, the Half Moon Bay City Council adopted Ordinance No. 2026-01, approving four agreements between the City of Half Moon Bay ("City") and Mercy Housing California 110, L.P. ("Mercy") ... for an affordable housing development for senior agricultural workers and 2,653 square feet of community serving space ("Project")." (p. 1)
- "Measure Q has been placed on the ballot because the City received a petition signed by the requisite number of voters seeking a referendum on Ordinance No. 2026-01." (p. 1)
- Ground Lease: "This agreement establishes the contractual obligations for leasing the Property and would be for a period of 99 years at a rate of $1 per year." (p. 1)
- "If approved by the voters, Measure Q would adopt Ordinance No. 2026-01 that authorizes the City Manager to execute the Agreements, take actions necessary to implement the Agreements, find the Agreements' approval exempt from the California Environmental Quality Act ("CEQA") section 15332 (Infill Projects), make Government Code section 37364 findings, require the City Manager to review the Ground Lease at least every seven years." (p. 1)
- "A "YES" vote is a vote in favor of Measure Q and in favor of adopting Ordinance 2026-01. A "NO" vote is a vote against Measure Q and against adopting Ordinance 2026-01." (p. 1)
- "Measure Q will be effective only if a majority of voters voting on the Measure vote in its favor." (p. 1)
