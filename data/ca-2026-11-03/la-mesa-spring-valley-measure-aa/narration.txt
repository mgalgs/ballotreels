La Mesa-Spring Valley School District Measure AA asks the district's voters whether to approve $131 million in school bonds.
The district would borrow the money and repay it from a tax on property in the district.
Measure AA needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would repair roofs, plumbing and electrical systems, add air conditioning and shade, and remove asbestos and lead pipes.
The district's tax statement estimates a tax of $21.70 per $100,000 of assessed value, collected through 2056-57.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $258 million.
No argument against Measure AA was filed for the San Diego County voter guide.
Supporters say Measure AA repairs schools over 60 years old, without raising tax rates.
A yes vote is in favor of letting the district sell the bonds.
A no vote is against letting the district sell the bonds.
