## Ballot question and supporters/opponents (verbatim, from the county's final measures list)

> To provide safe, modern neighborhood schools by repairing/upgrading/equipping aging classrooms, science labs, instructional technology, and school facilities that support math, science, engineering, literacy, arts, skilled trades, and college/career readiness, fixing deteriorating roofs, plumbing, electrical systems, and removing asbestos/lead pipes, shall Charter Oak Unified School District's measure authorizing $75,000,000 in bonds at legal rates be adopted, levying $40 per $100,000 assessed value ($5,000,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding, with independent oversight/audits, all money locally controlled?
>
> Supporters: Charter Oak PTA Council; Covina Police Officers Assn.; Charter Oak Education Foundation; Cristian Aguilar, COUSD Board Pres
>
> Opponents: None submitted.

Vote threshold: 55% of the Votes Cast (county list).
