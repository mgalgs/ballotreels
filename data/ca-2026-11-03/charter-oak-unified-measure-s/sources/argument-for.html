ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE S

Great local schools are essential to the high quality of life in the family-focused Charter Oak Unified School District, serving families in the cities of Covina, Glendora, and San Dimas. Measure S is needed to ensure our students continue learning in safe, modern schools that prepare them for success.

Vote YES on Measure S to provide modern classrooms, labs, and instructional technology that support excellence in math, science, engineering, literacy, reading, writing, the arts, and technology.

Vote YES to prepare students for college and high-demand careers, including health sciences, robotics, engineering, and the skilled trades.

Vote YES on Measure S to improve student and teacher safety by replacing outdated fire alarms, emergency communication systems, and security infrastructure.

Many of our schools are more than 60 years old. Aging underground pipes have caused gas leaks, water leaks, and sewage backups. Some campuses still contain asbestos and aging lead plumbing, while older buildings need seismic upgrades. In recent years, roof leaks damaged 45 classrooms, disrupting learning and increasing repair costs.

Vote YES on Measure S to:
Upgrade classrooms, science labs, career technical education facilities, and instructional technology; Repair deteriorating roofs, plumbing, wiring, and underground infrastructure; Improve campus safety and security; Remove asbestos and aging lead plumbing from older schools; Prevent more costly emergency repairs in the future

Measure S Protects Taxpayers. Every dollar stays in Charter Oak Unified schools and cannot be taken by the State or used by other districts.

Independent citizen oversight, annual audits, a detailed project list, and public disclosure of all expenditures are required. By law, no bond funds can be used for administrator salaries or pensions.

Join local parents, teachers, business leaders, and community members. Vote YES on Measure S.
