Measure P asks the voters of this community services district whether to raise its yearly parcel tax for fire protection.
Today, each parcel the tax applies to pays $25 a year for fire protection and prevention services.
Measure P needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
Measure P would raise the tax to $75 a year per parcel, starting in the 2027-2028 fiscal year.
The money would go to a special fund used only for fire protection and prevention, including staff, equipment and vehicles.
The tax would continue unless a later two-thirds vote of the district's voters ends it.
The impartial analysis estimates the increase would bring in about $755,000 more each year.
No argument against Measure P appears among the official documents that the county has posted online for this ballot measure.
No argument for Measure P appears among the official documents that the county has posted online for this ballot measure.
A yes vote is in favor of raising the parcel tax from $25 to $75 a year.
A no vote is against the increase, keeping the existing tax of $25 a year.
