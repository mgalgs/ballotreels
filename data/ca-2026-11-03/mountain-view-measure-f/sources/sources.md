## Ballot question (p. 2)

> Shall the measure to provide locally controlled funding for repairing potholes, streets and sidewalks, improving police, fire, and 911 emergency response services, supporting affordable housing, and adding new parks, and other general government services by authorizing City Council to increase the City of Mountain View's existing Transient Occupancy Tax paid by hotel and short-term rental guests from 10% up to 15%, generating up to $5,200,000 annually until ended by voters, with independent audits, be adopted?

## Full text

Exhibit A ordinance (pp. 5–6), "AN ORDINANCE OF THE PEOPLE OF THE CITY OF MOUNTAIN VIEW AMENDING MOUNTAIN VIEW CITY CODE SECTION 33.2 TO AUTHORIZE CITY COUNCIL TO INCREASE THE TRANSIENT OCCUPANCY TAX". Key lines (p. 5):

> The tax imposed by this chapter shall be ten percent (10%) of the rent charged by the operator.

> Pursuant to authority granted by the voters, the City Council may, by ordinance amending this section, increase or decrease the tax rate established in subsection (b), provided that the tax rate shall not exceed fifteen percent (15%) of the rent charged by the operator.

Recital (p. 5): "the transient occupancy tax rate currently imposed by the City of Mountain View is one of the lowest in the area and has not been updated since 1991".

Resolution p. 4, §12: "The full text of this ballot measure shall not be printed in the County Voter Information Guide."

Adopted 2026-06-23 as Resolution No. 19130 (motion Ramirez/Hicks; roll-call text cut off in the Legistar record).
