Measure F asks the voters of this city whether to let the City Council raise the hotel tax from 10 up to 15 percent.
Hotel and short-term rental guests pay the tax on stays under 30 days, with their room bills.
Voters last raised the rate in 1991, and today it is 10 percent.
The City Council placed Measure F on the ballot unanimously, and it needs a majority to pass.
The money would go to the general fund, for any government purpose, such as streets, emergency services and parks.
The City Attorney's analysis says each 1 percent increase could bring in about $1.1 million more a year.
The ballot question puts the total at up to $5.2 million a year.
No argument against Measure F was filed with the county Registrar of Voters.
Supporters say the tax rate here is lower than in most county cities.
A yes vote lets the City Council raise the rate up to 15 percent.
A no vote rejects the ordinance, and the rate stays at 10 percent.
