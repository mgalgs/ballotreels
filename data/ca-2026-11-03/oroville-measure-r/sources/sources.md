## Facts with quotes

- **Who votes / what it is:** "The Oroville City Council placed Measure R on the ballot. Measure R would amend the Oroville City Charter provisions concerning the City Treasurer." (guide p. 71)
- **Ballot label (guide p. 71):** "City of Oroville – Resolution 9465 – Charter Amendment: Removing City Treasurer Position. Shall the City of Oroville Charter be amended to delete the vacant and no longer needed City Treasurer position, saving the City approximately $65,000 annually? Yes or No"
- **Question as adopted (Resolution 9465, §2A):** "Shall the City of Oroville Charter be amended to delete the vacant and no longer needed City Treasurer position, saving the City approximately $65,000 annually." (the resolution text ends with a period, not a question mark)
- **What it changes:** "Measure R would delete Article IX, Section 7, which establishes the Treasurer and states the Treasurer's duties. It would also delete the sentence in Article VI, Section 1 requiring the Treasurer to be elected and to serve a four-year term, and other references to the elected City Treasurer where needed to give effect to this Measure. Voters would no longer elect a City Treasurer, and the measure would not create an appointed Treasurer position." (guide p. 71)
- **Who does the work instead:** "The City's financial functions would continue to be performed under the remaining Charter, Municipal Code, state law, and City fiscal policies, principally through the appointed Director of Finance and other designated staff." (guide p. 71)
- **Savings:** "The City's estimate is that eliminating the position would save approximately $65,000 annually. Actual savings would depend on the compensation/benefit costs that otherwise would have been incurred for the Treasurer position." (guide p. 72)
- **Vote needed:** "Measure R requires a majority of votes cast on the measure to pass." (guide p. 72); Resolution 9465 §3: "a majority (50%+1) of the votes cast."
- **Yes/No:** "A "yes" vote is a vote to eliminate the City Treasurer position as described above. A "no" vote is a vote to retain the existing Charter provisions." (guide p. 72)
- **Analysis author:** "s/Scott E. Huber, City Attorney, City of Oroville, CA" (guide p. 72)
- **Adopted:** Resolution 9465 "PASSED, APPROVED, AND ADOPTED this 16th day of June, 2026"; ayes listed as seven council members, noes "None".
- **Discrepancy to note:** Resolution 9465 §1 says the election is "for the purpose of submitting four questions", but the posted resolution sets out two (2A, 2B) and its heading §2 says "Four Measures Submitted to Voters". Only R and S appear on the ballot per the county.
