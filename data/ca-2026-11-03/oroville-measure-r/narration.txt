Measure R asks the voters of this city whether to remove the elected City Treasurer from the city charter.
Today voters elect a City Treasurer to a four-year term, to collect city taxes and safeguard city money.
A director of finance, appointed by the City Council, already has many of the same duties.
The City Council placed Measure R on the ballot, and its question calls the position vacant and no longer needed.
Measure R would delete the treasurer from the charter, and create no appointed treasurer instead.
The director of finance and other staff would mainly do the financial work of the city.
Measure R needs a simple majority to pass.
The city attorney's impartial analysis says the city estimates savings of about $65,000 a year, depending on what the position would have cost.
The county voter guide says that no argument for Measure R was filed.
The county voter guide says that no argument against Measure R was filed.
A yes vote is a vote to eliminate the City Treasurer position.
A no vote is a vote to retain the existing charter provisions.
