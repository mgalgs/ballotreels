Measure V asks the voters of this city whether to add a new 1 percent sales tax for city services.
Today, the combined sales tax rate in the city is 8.125 percent, and the city keeps 1.75 percent of it.
Measure V would raise the combined rate to 9.125 percent.
The new tax would be in addition to an existing three-quarter percent city sales tax, which would not change.
The money would go into the city general fund for any lawful purpose, with a citizens oversight committee and independent audits.
The tax would start in April 2027 and last until repealed by voters, and the measure needs a simple majority.
The ballot question estimates Measure V would raise about $28 million a year.
Opponents say the budget shortfall is temporary but the tax is permanent, and the council can spend it however it chooses.
Supporters say a structural budget deficit threatens city services, and visitors who shop in the city will help pay for them.
A yes vote approves the additional 1 percent sales tax.
A no vote rejects the additional 1 percent sales tax.
