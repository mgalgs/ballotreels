Argument in Favor of Measure V

Vote YES on Measure V to protect Vacaville's:

Police, Fire and 911 Emergency Response
Local Road and Street Repairs
Parks and Recreation
Other essential City Services that make Vacaville a great place to live, work, raise a family, and retire.

Like many cities, Vacaville faces a structural budget deficit due to decreased revenues and rising costs which threatens many services our families rely on.

Vacaville has already made difficult cuts, reduced costs, frozen positions, and tightened its budget. But without additional local funding, deeper cuts could affect public safety staffing, emergency response times, road maintenance, parks and recreation, and other essential services vital to the quality of life in Vacaville.

Measure V provides a local solution.

Measure V is estimated to generate approximately $28 million annually. Revenue generated by Measure V will remain under local control and cannot be taken by the State. Visitors and non-residents who shop in Vacaville will help pay for the services they use, reducing the burden on Vacaville residents.

Measure V does not apply to groceries, prescription medications, or services.

Measure V also requires clear transparency, including:

A Citizens' Oversight Committee
Annual Independent Audits
Public Reporting of revenues and expenditures
Fiscal accountability to ensure the public knows how funds are spent

Measure V is NOT about expanding government. It is about protecting what Vacaville residents already value:

Safe Neighborhoods
Fast 911 Response
Well-Maintained Streets and Roads
Fire Protection
Crime Prevention
Parks and Recreation

Vacaville's future should be decided by Vacaville residents.
