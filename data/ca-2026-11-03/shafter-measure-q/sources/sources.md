### `notice-of-election-tut-measure.pdf`
- Publisher: City of Shafter, City Clerk (Yazmina Pallares)
- Title: "Notice of Measure to be Voted On" (archive title "Notice of Election for
  TUT Ballot Measure to be Voted on Nov. 3 2026"), published July 23, 2026
- URL: https://www.shafterca.gov/Archive/ViewFile/Item/1838 (listed at
  https://www.shafterca.gov/Archive/45)
- Facts:
  - Who votes: "will be submitted to the voters of the City of Shafter at the
    General Municipal Election to be held on November 3, 2026"
  - Ballot question: "CITY OF SHAFTER SALES TAX MEASURE. Shall the measure to
    provide local funding for City of Shafter services and facilities, including
    police services, traffic enforcement, street repairs and safety improvements,
    library and recreation services, park maintenance, and other municipal

    revenue remaining in the City of Shafter, be adopted?"

    Wednesday, August 12, 2026 / Deadline for Filing Rebuttal Arguments: 5:00 p.m.,
    Monday, August 17, 2026"

### `agenda-packet-2026-07-07-item6-pp433-451.pdf`
- Publisher: City of Shafter
- Title: July 7, 2026 Regular City Council Meeting Agenda Packet — Management
  Report item 6 "Approving Placement of Ballot Measure - Local Transactions and Use
  (Sales) Tax", packet pages 433–451 of 480 (cut with `qpdf --pages`; the full
  118 MB packet was not kept)
- URL: https://shafterca.api.civicclerk.com/v1/Meetings/GetMeetingFileStream(fileId=588,plainText=false)
  (agenda alone: fileId=587; portal https://shafterca.portal.civicclerk.com/)
- Contents: staff report (pp. 433–435), Resolution No. 3103 (pp. 435–438),
  Exhibit A Ordinance No. 782 (pp. 439–446), Resolution No. 3104 consolidation
  (pp. 447–448), Resolution No. 3105 argument rules (pp. 449–451).
- Facts:
  - Ballot question in Res. 3103 §3: "CITY OF SHAFTER SALES TAX MEASURE. Shall the
    measure to provide local funding for City of Shafter services and facilities,
    including police services, traffic enforcement, street repairs and safety
    improvements, library and recreation services, park maintenance, and other
    municipal services, by enacting a 1% sales tax generating approximately
    $4,000,000 annually until ended by voters, with annual audits, public review,
    and all revenue remaining with the City of Shafter, be adopted?" — note "with
    the City" here vs "in the City" in the clerk's notice. The final printed label
    is not confirmed (county voter guide blocked).
  - General tax: "the one percent (1%) TUT is a general tax, the revenue of which
    will be placed in the City's general fund and will be used to pay for general
    City services"
  - Vote needed to pass: "subject to majority voter approval" (Ord. 782 recital)
  - Rate: "at the rate of one percent (1%) of the gross receipts of any retailer"
    (Ord. 782 §3.16.040)
  - Duration: "The tax imposed by this chapter shall continue until this ordinance
    is repealed."
  - Revenue: "the measure is anticipated to generate approximately $4,000,000
    annually in additional General Fund revenue" (staff report)
  - Council vote needed to place it: "Approving this ballot measure requires
    affirmative votes from four (4) Councilmembers to satisfy the two-thirds (2/3)
    membership requirement." (staff report)
  - Impartial analysis is ordered: "The City Attorney shall prepare an impartial
    analysis of the Measure, not to exceed 500 words" (Res. 3103 §5)
  - The packet holds the resolutions as proposed; July 7 minutes were not posted
    in the portal, so the adoption vote is not confirmed here. The City Clerk's
    July 23 notice shows the measure was placed.
