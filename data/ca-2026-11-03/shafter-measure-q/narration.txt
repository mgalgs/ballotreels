Measure Q asks the voters of this city whether to add a 1 percent sales tax for city services.
The City Council voted unanimously in July to place Measure Q on the ballot.
The tax would add $1 for every $100 spent, on top of current sales taxes.
As a general tax, the money would go to the general fund, for any lawful city program or service.
The revenue would stay in the city, and would not be shared with the state or the county.
The tax would start on April 1, 2027, last until repealed by voters, and needs a majority vote to pass.
The city attorney's impartial analysis estimates about $4 million a year in added local funding.

No argument against Measure Q appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
A yes vote on Measure Q authorizes the 1 percent sales tax.
A no vote on Measure Q does not authorize the 1 percent sales tax.
