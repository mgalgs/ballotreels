NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of the City of Brisbane that the City of Brisbane orders as follows:

Section 1. Ballot Language. The ballot question for the proposed measure shall be as follows:

Shall the measure updating the City's Business License Tax by replacing the current tiered structure with a $50 tax for businesses with annual gross receipts of $25,000 or less and $50 plus $1.00 per $1,000 of gross receipts above $25,000 for most other businesses; establishing a minimum tax base equal to the cost of operations for businesses whose operating costs exceed gross receipts; and raising approximately $1,000,000 annually, until repealed by voters, be adopted?
YES
NO

Section 2. Approval of Ordinance and Placement of Ordinance on Ballot. Pursuant to Elections Code section 9222, the City Council of the City of Brisbane hereby approves the ballot language, and the Ordinance attached hereto as Attachment A, to be placed on the ballot and submitted to the voters of the City at the November 3, 2026 election.
