City of Brisbane Measure X asks the voters of Brisbane whether to replace the tiered business license tax with one formula based on gross receipts.
Today, most businesses pay tiered rates, from $50 for the smallest to $1,300 plus $0.04 per $1,000 for the largest.
The tax is a general tax: its money goes into the General Fund for general city services.
Businesses with gross receipts of $25,000 or less would pay $50, and most others $50 plus $1 per $1,000 above that.
A business whose operating costs exceed its gross receipts would be taxed on its cost of operations.
The impartial analysis says Measure X would end some exemptions, including those for newspapers, nonprofits and veterans.
A preamble of the ordinance says nonprofit exemptions would stay, but the amended code it adopts strikes the nonprofit exemption.
The city attorney's impartial analysis says the city estimates Measure X would raise about $1 million more a year.
Supporters say raising fees unchanged since 1983 makes businesses contribute their share to city services.
No argument against Measure X appears in the published measure documents of San Mateo County.
A yes vote replaces the tiered rates with the new formula.
A no vote keeps the current tax structure in place.
