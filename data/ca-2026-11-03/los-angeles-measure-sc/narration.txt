City of Los Angeles Measure SC asks voters whether to change the City Charter rules on campaign finance for Los Angeles Unified School District board elections.
Today, one section of the City Charter sets the campaign finance rules that school board candidates must follow.
Measure SC would remove the separate school board rules, and make school board candidates follow the rules for candidates for mayor.
Some rules would not apply to school board candidates, including public matching funds and the limits on contributions from city contractors and lobbyists.
The basic contribution limit would stay at $1,000, and the City Council could add other limits or exceptions by ordinance.
The City Administrative Officer says Measure SC is not expected to have a financial impact on the city.
No argument against Measure SC was submitted, so the voter pamphlet from the City Clerk prints only the argument in favor of it.
Supporters say Measure SC simplifies the charter by removing repeated rules, and puts school board candidates under the same framework as mayoral candidates.
A yes vote means you want to remove the repeated language, clarify the rules for school board candidates, and let the City Council make more changes by ordinance.
A no vote means you do not want these changes.
