REBUTTAL TO ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE ES

We agree that exceptional public schools enhance our quality of life, support property values, and prepare the next generation for success.

Support for our schools is not the issue.

Voting NO on this tax will not hurt our schools.

This new tax will not increase school funding.

It's a Santa Monica City tax disguised as a school tax with no City spending restrictions.

Malibu schools benefit from this tax without Malibu residents paying.

You – Santa Monica renters and property owners - will now pay the City's $12 million commitment to the School District. This agreement provides after-school community access and City programming, including the daycare program, at elementary and middle school fields and playgrounds.

If the agreement ends, there is no guarantee that community access and City programming will continue.

There are no restrictions on how the City can use the freed-up $12 million. It can be spent on anything, including a Rose Parade float.

Proponents claim that City funding is unstable, yet the City provided this funding for decades. There's a simple solution – the City should continue its school partnership with another long-term Joint Use Agreement.

Despite declaring fiscal distress last year, the City announced a $9 million surplus. The District averaged a $14 million surplus over the last 5 years and has $84 million in reserves.

Threats of cuts to teachers and programs are fearmongering.

There is no need for this tax.

Vote NO on Measure ES
