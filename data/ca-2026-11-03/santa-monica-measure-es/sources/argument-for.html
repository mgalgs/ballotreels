ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE ES

Great public schools are the foundation of our democracy. Our exceptional public schools benefit the entire community—enhancing our quality of life, supporting strong property values, and preparing the next generation for success.

Today, thanks to outstanding teachers and staff, dedicated, hardworking students, and decades of community support, our local public schools rank among the nation's best.

But that excellence is in jeopardy.

The City of Santa Monica's unprecedented fiscal distress has put $12 million in annual City funding for our public schools at risk. Without a stable, locally controlled funding source, our schools face the loss of teachers and essential educational programs.

That's why thousands of Santa Monica voters signed petitions to place Measure ES on the ballot.

Measure ES replaces uncertain City funding, scheduled to expire next year, with a permanent and stable local funding source that will protect educational excellence for future generations.

Vote YES on Measure ES to:

- Prevent the loss of up to 73 teachers
- Preserve exceptional math, science and humanities instruction while protecting our nationally recognized music and arts programs
- Protect critical programs for early childhood education and disadvantaged students
- Maintain the educational excellence that helps keep Santa Monica a great place to live

By law, every dollar raised by Measure ES stays in the classrooms of our local public schools:

- Not one cent can be taken by the State or federal government
- Funds cannot be used for administrator salaries, facility repairs and improvements, or new construction
- An independent citizens' oversight committee will ensure that all funds are spent exactly as promised

Please join parents, teachers, business leaders, seniors and other community stakeholders by voting YES on Measure ES for EDUCATIONAL EXCELLENCE in our local public schools—for our children's futures!
