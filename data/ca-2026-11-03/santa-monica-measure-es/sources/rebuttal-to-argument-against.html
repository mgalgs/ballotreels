REBUTTAL TO ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE ES

Don't gamble with our children's future: Vote YES on Measure ES.

FACT: Opponents of Measure ES are misrepresenting the facts.

FACT: The City of Santa Monica provides $12 million annually in supplemental funding so that our schools remain among California's best. Today the City faces a serious financial crisis.

FACT: As the City depletes its reserves to maintain essential services, this long-standing school funding is at risk.

FACT: Measure ES provides the only permanent, reliable funding source to protect Santa Monica's public schools.

FACT: Without Measure ES, academic excellence, outstanding school arts and music programs, and the ability to attract and retain exceptional teachers will be jeopardized.

FACT: State law guarantees and preserves community access to school district fields, playgrounds, and other school facilities.

FACT: Thousands of Santa Monica voters signed petitions to place Measure ES on the ballot because they understand that excellent public schools are worth protecting. They strengthen our community and protect property values.

FACT: Seniors and nonprofits are exempt from Measure ES. Homeowners will pay $1.36 per day. For renters, any allowable pass through will be miniscule. This is a small price to pay to preserve one of Santa Monica's greatest assets and protect our children's future in an ever-changing world.

FACT: Funds from Measure ES cannot be taken by the city, state or federal government.

FACT: Measure ES is backed by a broad coalition of Santa Monica teachers, parents, homeowners, renters, business leaders, and community members.

Protect our children, our teachers, and our excellent schools. Vote YES on Measure ES.
