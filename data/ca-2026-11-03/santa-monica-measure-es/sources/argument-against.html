ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE ES

We support excellent schools in Santa Monica. We are against this measure because it is deceptive. Schools don't get more money out of this, the City does. This $495 a year parcel tax is a back-door City tax increase.

Santa Monica's excellent public schools are one of our community's greatest assets. For decades, the City has funded the Master Facilities Use Agreement (MFUA) providing community access to Santa Monica-Malibu Unified School District (SMMUSD) recreational facilities and playgrounds after-school and weekends.

This measure shifts payment from the City to the people of Santa Monica - forever. Without the MFUA, it jeopardizes after-school programs and community access to SMMUSD facilities.

Measure ES does not provide additional funding for SMMUSD.

This tax is not needed. The City deceptively proposed cutting the MFUA payment after declaring "fiscal distress" to add $12 million to its budget of $525 million. This is a permanent solution to a temporary problem that has already gotten better. The City had a $9 million surplus last year.

Housing is less affordable with this $495 a year parcel tax that increases each year, forever. This regressive tax puts a disproportionate burden on lower income residents, targets working families, and can be passed through to renters. The Water Garden pays the same as a condominium.

SMMUSD funding is reliable and growing with large surpluses and reserves. Revenue increased 53% in 10 years to $225 million, including a new $10 million transfer tax, while enrollment decreased 23%. In the last five years, SMMUSD had an average annual surplus of $14 million and reserves of $84 million.

Why would only teachers be at risk? Teachers are only 39% of SMMUSD's 1,262 staff, which has remained largely unchanged while enrollment decreased.

Vote NO on this new tax for the City.

Vote NO on Measure ES
