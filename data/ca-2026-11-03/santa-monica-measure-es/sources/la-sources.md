## Ballot question and supporters/opponents (verbatim, from the county's final measures list)

> MEASURE ES: Shall a measure imposing a City of Santa Monica collected parcel tax of $495 per taxable parcel, adjusted annually for inflation, exempting qualifying seniors, affordable housing, non-profits, religious organizations, and schools, providing continual funding for Santa Monica's public schools, including retaining teachers, assisting disadvantaged students, supporting art, music, science, math, and early childhood education, keeping school facilities clean and safe for student and community use, generating an estimated $12,000,000 annually until repealed by voters, with oversight requirements, be adopted?
>
> Supporters: SM-Malibu Classroom Teachers Assn.; SM Chamber of Commerce; SMM Council of PTAs
>
> Opponents: Northeast Neighbors; Wilshire Montana Neighborhood Coalition; Marc Verville; Karen Melick; Lori Brown

Vote threshold: Majority, 50%+1.

## What's present and what's missing

- **Full text**: present (`full-text.pdf`).
- **Resolution placing the measure on the ballot**: present (`resolution.pdf`).
- **Impartial analysis**: missing. Required by state law for a local ballot measure, but not found — `santamonica.gov`, where the City posts these, returned the same Imperva/Incapsula bot-challenge page on every path tried (including a Wayback Machine capture, which caught the same block). Not confirmed whether one was ever posted; just not fetched.
- **Argument for / argument against**: both sides are named in the county's final list (3 supporters, 5 opponents) as of the list's "Rev. 9/22/26" date, which is after the state-law deadline for filing arguments — so both an argument for and an argument against were almost certainly filed. The actual filed text of either is missing — not found, same `santamonica.gov` block.
- **Rebuttals**: status unconfirmed either way — California law would allow a rebuttal on each side since both a for and an against argument exist, but no rebuttal text (or confirmation one was filed) was found.
- **Tax rate statement**: not applicable — ES is a parcel tax, not a property-tax-secured bond measure, so no tax-rate statement is required.
