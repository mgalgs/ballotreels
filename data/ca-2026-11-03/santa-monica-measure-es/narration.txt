Measure ES asks the voters of this city whether to tax each parcel of property every year to fund the local public schools.
Measure ES is a citizens' initiative: the county verified enough petition signatures to place it on the ballot.
The measure's text says the city now funds the school district partly through an agreement that expires on June 30, 2027.
Each parcel would be taxed $495 a year, rising each year with inflation.
Owners 65 or older living on their parcel, nonprofits, affordable housing, religious groups and schools could be exempt.
All the money would go to the school district, though not for administrator salaries, land or new buildings.
The tax would last until voters repeal it, with an independent audit of the spending each year.
The ballot question estimates the tax would raise about $12 million a year; no fiscal analysis was gathered.
Two neighborhood groups and three individuals filed an argument against Measure ES; its text is not among the documents gathered for this video.
Three groups, including a teachers association, filed an argument for Measure ES; its text is not among the documents gathered for this video.
A yes vote adopts the $495 parcel tax for the public schools.
A no vote rejects the $495 parcel tax for the public schools.
