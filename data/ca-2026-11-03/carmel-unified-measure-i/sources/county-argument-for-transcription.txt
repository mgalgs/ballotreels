Carmel Unified School District
Argument in Favor of I
(300 words)

Our Carmel Unified Schools are at a turning point. Your YES vote on Measure I will ensure schools receive the repairs and updates they need to serve our communities for decades.

Across Carmel by the Sea, Carmel, Carmel Highlands, Carmel Valley, Cachagua, Big Sur, and Pebble Beach, aging schools are beginning to fail in ways that affect students' learning. Now is the time to ensure our schools remain safe, reliable, and worthy of the children they serve.

Many of our schools and facilities are nearly 90 years old. Despite careful maintenance, aging roofs, failing heating and cooling systems, deteriorating water and sewer lines, and outdated electrical infrastructure are increasingly disrupting classrooms and becoming more costly to repair.

Teachers' focus should be instruction, not flooded campuses or unreliable systems. Students deserve safe, comfortable, and dependable schools where every day is dedicated to learning.

The simple truth: the last bond approved by voters to update Carmel Unified Schools was over 20 years ago.

Measure I will help improve campus safety through upgraded access controls, emergency communications, security cameras, and fire alarm systems.

Yes on Measure I for Safer, Upgraded Schools
• Improve student and school safety
• Replace decaying water pipes, roofs, sewer, electrical and HVAC systems
• Improve accessibility for students with disabilities
• Replace aging portables and our oldest buildings with updated, permanent classrooms
• Weatherproof schools to prevent mold and dry rot

Measure I is Transparent and Accountable
• All funds are spent locally and cannot be taken by the State
• Independent citizens' oversight, annual reports and audits ensure funds are used as intended
• Money cannot be used for salaries or pensions

Each generation inherits its schools from the one before it, and strong schools protect property values. We have the opportunity to join together and step up for our schools.

Join us and YES on Measure I.
