Measure F asks the voters of this city whether to add a yearly parcel tax to pay for fire and emergency response.
The city council placed Measure F on the ballot in August, with no votes against.
Each single-family home would pay $95 a year, each apartment unit $65, and commercial and industrial parcels more.
The money would go into a special fund, used only for fire and emergency services.
A committee of five residents would review the spending and report each year.
The tax would have no end date, and needs two-thirds of the votes to pass.
The city attorney's impartial analysis estimates that the tax would raise about $240,000 a year.
Supporters say the department relies almost entirely on volunteers, and Measure F would ensure emergency coverage around the clock.
Opponents say even a $95 parcel tax could make life noticeably harder for families, and this tax would never end.
A yes vote enacts the parcel tax, at $95 a year for each single-family home.
A no vote does not enact the parcel tax.
