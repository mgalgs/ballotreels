## Facts

**Ballot question.** resolution.pdf, Section 4, PDF page 3:
> "To ensure 24/7 response to fires and medical emergencies such as heart attacks, strokes, and car accidents; hire/train firefighters/EMTs; upgrade safety equipment, vehicles/life-saving rescue tools, shall a measure be adopted to enact a $95/year parcel tax for single family homes and specified amounts for multifamily, commercial, agricultural, and industrial parcels, providing approximately $240,000/year until ended by voters; requiring independent oversight; funds used only for Firebaugh Fire Department response?"

**Who votes.** resolution.pdf, Section 5, PDF page 3:
> "The City Council hereby approves the proposed ordinance, in the form thereof, and its submission to the voters of the City at the November 3, 2026 election."

**What it does; vote needed.** Impartial analysis letter from Nicholas M. Matoian, Griswold, LaSalle, Cobb, Dowd & Gin, LLP, City Attorney for the City of Firebaugh, Aug 7 2026 (filed Aug 10 2026), p. 1:
> "The Firebaugh City Council voted to place on the ballot a proposed ordinance to add Chapter 11.5 to the Firebaugh Municipal Code establishing a special parcel tax to pay for local fire and emergency response services. There is no sunset clause; the ordinance will continue indefinitely, until otherwise repealed. If approved by a two-thirds majority of the electorate of the City of Firebaugh, the ordinance would impose a tax in the amount of ninety-five dollars ($95.00) on each single-family residential parcel and agricultural parcel, sixty-five dollars ($65.00) per unit on each multi-family residential parcel, four hundred ninety-five dollars ($495.00) on each commercial parcel, and seven hundred fifty dollars ($750.00) on each industrial parcel."
> "The proposed special parcel tax is estimated to raise two hundred forty thousand dollars ($240,000.00) each year and is designated for local fire and emergency response services only."

County page heading: "Two-Thirds (2/3) Majority to Pass".
