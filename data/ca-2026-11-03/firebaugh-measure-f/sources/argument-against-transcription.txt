Argument Against City of Firebaugh Parcel Tax Measure

Fresno County

City of Firebaugh

November 3, 2026 Consolidated Statewide General Election

Word Count: 286

Text of Argument Below This Line

==============================================================

How has your cost of living changed in the last few years? What about your neighbors? Before you vote on this sales tax for the City of Firebaugh, please think about a few important points:

This tax would collect about $240,000 every year from the people of Firebaugh, and it would never end.

You might feel your share is worth paying because it promises to continue and improve emergency services. Safety services matter, and wanting to support them shows you care about your community. But there are other ways to help. If you are willing to give money through a tax, would you also be willing to give it through voluntary charity instead?

Think again about your neighbors. Many have struggled in recent years, and even a $95 parcel tax could make life noticeably harder. That means less money for groceries, saving for college, or paying medical bills after an emergency.

Home ownership is already too expensive for many, and higher property taxes make it worse. Renters and everyday shoppers feel the impact too because landlords and businesses pass those costs on. Voluntary charity works differently: people and businesses give what they can, when they can.

This tax would stay in place FOREVER, and once a tax is approved, it is difficult to change or remove. Families may face new challenges in the future. Voluntary giving lets people adjust their support as their own finances change.

We respect people who want to raise money for a good cause. We simply ask that they try voluntary giving first, especially when so many families are already facing hard times.

Let your neighbors choose which causes they want to support, and vote NO on Measure F.

Join us: https://lpfresno.org/NoFirebaughTax
