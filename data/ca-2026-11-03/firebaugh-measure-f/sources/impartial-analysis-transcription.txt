Re: City of Firebaugh November 3, 2026 Election
City of Firebaugh Parcel Tax Measure Impartial Analysis

Dear County Clerk:

Below is the impartial analysis from our office as City Attorney for the City of Firebaugh in reference to the City of Firebaugh's Parcel Tax Measure referenced above. Please let us know if you have any questions.

The Firebaugh City Council voted to place on the ballot a proposed ordinance to add Chapter 11.5 to the Firebaugh Municipal Code establishing a special parcel tax to pay for local fire and emergency response services. There is no sunset clause; the ordinance will continue indefinitely, until otherwise repealed. If approved by a two-thirds majority of the electorate of the City of Firebaugh, the ordinance would impose a tax in the amount of ninety-five dollars ($95.00) on each single-family residential parcel and agricultural parcel, sixty-five dollars ($65.00) per unit on each multi-family residential parcel, four hundred ninety-five dollars ($495.00) on each commercial parcel, and seven hundred fifty dollars ($750.00) on each industrial parcel.

Parcels owned by the City, Federal or State government or any other public agency and parcels owned by any public or private entity that are specifically exempted from a parcel tax enacted as a special tax per Federal or State statute or regulation shall be exempt.

The proposed special parcel tax is estimated to raise two hundred forty thousand dollars ($240,000.00) each year and is designated for local fire and emergency response services only. To ensure that the tax revenues are spent only on local fire and emergency response services and for accountability purposes, such tax revenues will be subject to independent oversight, financial audits, and annual public reporting.

Lyman D. Griswold (1914-2000) Michael E. LaSalle (Retired) Steven W. Cobb (1947-1993) Jim D. Lee (Retired) Robert W. Gin (Retired)
