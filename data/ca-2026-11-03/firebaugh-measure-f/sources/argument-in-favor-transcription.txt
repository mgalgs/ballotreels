Ballot Argument for Firebaugh Fire Measure

Vote Yes on Measure __ - Protect Firebaugh's Emergency Fire and Medical Response

When someone suffers a heart attack, stroke, serious accident or house fire, every second matters. Fast emergency response can mean the difference between life and death.

Today, Firebaugh's Fire Department depends almost entirely on volunteer firefighters who often have full-time jobs. That means there isn't always a firefighter at the station ready to respond immediately when an emergency happens.

When someone calls 911, they shouldn't have to wonder whether a firefighter is available.

Measure __ will ensure 24-hour, 7-day a week emergency response coverage, while continuing to support our dedicated volunteer firefighters who put their lives on the line and proudly serve our community.

Measure __ will also help replace aging firefighting and emergency medical equipment, improve firefighter safety, and ensure Firebaugh has the life-saving tools needed to respond to medical emergencies, structure fires, vehicle accidents, wildfires and other disasters.

Over the past several years, emergency calls in Firebaugh have continued to increase while staffing has remained virtually unchanged. As California faces larger wildfires and more frequent emergencies, Firebaugh must be prepared to respond quickly when our families need help most.

Your YES vote on Measure __ will:

- Ensure firefighters are available 24 hours a day to respond to emergencies

- Upgrade aging firefighting, rescue and life-saving emergency medical equipment

- Maintain fast response times for fires, medical emergencies and natural disasters

- Strengthen Firebaugh's ability to protect lives, homes and property

Strict Fiscal Accountability

- Every dollar stays in Firebaugh and may only be used for local fire protection and emergency response.

- Independent community oversight and annual audits make sure funds are spent as promised.

- State government cannot touch these funds.

Protect our families. Protect our homes. Protect Firebaugh's emergency response.

Vote YES on Measure __.
