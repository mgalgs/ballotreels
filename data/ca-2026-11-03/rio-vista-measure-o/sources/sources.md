## Facts and quotes

**Ballot question** (Notice of Measures, 8/5/2026, https://content.solanocounty.gov/sites/default/files/2026-08/Notice_of_Measures.pdf): "Shall the existing three-quarter (3/4%) percent transactions and use tax measure to support the City of Rio Vista's general services, including police, fire, parks, and other unrestricted general fund expenditures, be extended for three years, commencing on April 1, 2027, and which is projected to provide between $1,398,000 to $1,467,000 of revenue annually for the City?"

**Impartial analysis** ("City Attorney's Impartial Analysis of Measure O: Rio Vista Transaction and Use Tax Measure", signed Mona G. Ebrahimi, City Attorney), key statements:
- "The City Council of the City of Rio Vista has placed Measure O on the November 3, 2026 ballot."
- "If approved by Rio Vista voters, Measure O will impose a three-quarter percent (0.75%) transactions and use ("sales") tax for three years."
- "As a "general tax," the revenue generated by Measure O will be deposited in the City's general fund."
- "...because Measure O is a general tax, it will be approved if over a majority of voters (over 50%) vote in favor of it. If adopted, Measure O would generate between $1,398,000 and $1,467,000 annually."
- "If approved, Measure O would become effective April 1, 2027 and remain in effect until April 2030. The tax would expire at the end of the three-year period unless it is reauthorized by a majority of the voters at a future election that occurs prior to the expiration of the tax."

**Who votes:** Rio Vista voters ("If approved by Rio Vista voters").
