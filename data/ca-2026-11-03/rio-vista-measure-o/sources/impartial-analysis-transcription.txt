CITY ATTORNEY'S IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE O:
RIO VISTA TRANSACTION AND USE TAX MEASURE

The City Council of the City of Rio Vista has placed Measure O on the November 3, 2026 ballot. California Revenue and Taxation Code section 7285.9 authorizes the City Council to levy a transactions and use tax by ordinance, provided the ordinance establishing the tax is ultimately approved by the voters.

If approved by Rio Vista voters, Measure O will impose a three-quarter percent (0.75%) transactions and use ("sales") tax for three years.

This tax is administered by the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration. Once established, it is allocated to the city where any goods are delivered or placed into use. As a "general tax," the revenue generated by Measure O will be deposited in the City's general fund.

Because this tax will be a general tax, the City of Rio Vista will be able to use the tax proceeds for any legal governmental purpose, including police, fire, parks, and other unrestricted general fund expenditures. Also, because Measure O is a general tax, it will be approved if over a majority of voters (over 50%) vote in favor of it. If adopted, Measure O would generate between $1,398,000 and $1,467,000 annually.

If approved, Measure O would become effective April 1, 2027 and remain in effect until April 2030. The tax would expire at the end of the three-year period unless it is reauthorized by a majority of the voters at a future election that occurs prior to the expiration of the tax.

A "YES" vote is a vote to approve the 0.75% percent sales tax for a period of three years. A "NO" vote is a vote against the sales tax.

The above statement is an impartial analysis of Measure O. If you desire a copy of the ordinance or measure, please call the elections official's office at (707) 374-6451 and a copy will be mailed at no cost to you.

Mona G. Ebrahimi, City Attorney
