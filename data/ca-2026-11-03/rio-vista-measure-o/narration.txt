Measure O asks the voters of this city whether to extend a 0.75 percent city sales tax for three more years.
Today, the city already collects this sales tax to support general city services.
Measure O would keep the tax from April 1, 2027, to March 31, 2030.
After that, the tax would end unless voters approve it again at a later election.
The money would go to the general fund, for police, fire, parks or any other lawful city purpose.
Measure O needs a simple majority to pass.
The city attorney's impartial analysis says Measure O would raise between $1,398,000 and $1,467,000 a year.
Supporters say the tax pays for paramedic, fire and police services, and since it is an extension, will not increase local taxes.
Opponents say the city has not solved its budget problems, and nothing guarantees the money will improve police, fire or parks.
A yes vote approves the 0.75 percent sales tax for three years.
A no vote is a vote against the sales tax.
