## Contents

- **Ballot question:** "To fund local programs and services – such as maintaining health and human service program levels, enhancing public safety, and supporting emergency preparedness projects – shall the ordinance increasing Yolo County's Transient Occupancy Tax, which is paid only by hotel/lodging/short-term rental guests in unincorporated areas (excluding cities), from 8% to 12% of the total rent charged, providing approximately an additional $250,000 annually until ended by voters, be adopted?"
- **Full text:** "ORDINANCE NO. 1585 — AN ORDINANCE OF THE BOARD OF SUPERVISORS OF THE COUNTY OF YOLO AMENDING TITLE 3, CHAPTER 7 OF THE YOLO COUNTY CODE OF ORDINANCES TO INCREASE THE TRANSIENT OCCUPANCY TAX RATE IN THE UNINCORPORATED AREAS OF THE COUNTY FROM EIGHT PERCENT (8%) TO TWELVE PERCENT (12%) OF THE RENT CHARGED AND OTHER CHANGES".
- **Impartial analysis:** "County Counsel's Impartial Analysis of Measure X", signed "/s/ Philip J. Pogledich, County Counsel, County of Yolo".
- **Argument in favor:** signed by Supervisors Sheila Allen (Chair), Lucas Frerichs, Oscar E. Villegas, Mary Vixie Sandy.
- **Argument against:** "No arguments were filed Against Measure X by the 5:00 p.m. July 24, 2026 deadline."
- **Rebuttals:** "If only an Argument IN FAVOR OF is filed, there is no Rebuttal period." (none)
- **Tax rate statement / fiscal analysis:** none (not a bond).

## Key facts, quoted

- Who votes: "The electors entitled to vote on Measure X are qualified electors residing within Yolo County, including its incorporated and unincorporated areas."
- Threshold: "To pass, a majority (50% + 1) of the votes cast by Yolo County voters must approve the measure." Analysis: "The Measure passes if it is approved by a simple majority of the qualified electors voting on the Measure."
- Where the tax applies: "If approved, Measure X will only increase the TOT charged for short-term lodging stays in the unincorporated areas of the County. TOT rates charged in the incorporated cities of Davis, Woodland, West Sacramento, and Winters would not change."
- Rate and start: "If Measure X is approved, Ordinance No. 1585 would be adopted and the TOT rate would increase to 12%, effective January 1, 2027, on short-term lodging stays of 30 days or less within the unincorporated areas of Yolo County. For example, if a hotel in the unincorporated area of Yolo County charges a guest $100 per night, the TOT charged would increase from $108 per night to $112 per night."
- Who pays: "The TOT is paid by the lodging guest."
- Definitions changed: the ordinance clarifies "rent" as "the total of all consideration charged to the guest for the short-term occupancy, including the room rate, service charges, retail markup charges, commission, processing fees, cleaning fees, resort fees, pet fees, and online booking fees", and that "hotel" "includes short-term rentals, vacation rentals, guest rooms, and camping sites, except to the extent exempted by State law."
- General tax: "Measure X was placed on the ballot by a unanimous vote of the Yolo County Board of Supervisors. TOT general tax revenues generated by Measure X would be placed into the County General Fund and would be available for appropriation through the County's annual budget for any lawful expenditure." (The ordinance's certification lists "RECUSE: BArajas".)
- Yes/No: "A "yes" vote on Measure X approves Ordinance No. 1585 and the proposed increase in the TOT from 8% to 12%." / "A "no" vote on Measure X is a vote against authorizing the proposed changes in Ordinance No. 1585 and the TOT rate would remain at 8%."
