Measure X asks the voters of this county whether to raise the hotel tax outside the cities from 8 to 12 percent.
Voters in the cities vote too, though the tax would apply only outside them.
Lodging guests pay the tax, on stays of up to 30 days.
From January 2027, a $100 night would cost a guest $112 with the tax, not $108.
The money would go to the general fund of the county, for any lawful use, until voters repeal the tax.
The ballot question estimates about $250,000 a year in added revenue.
No argument against Measure X was filed by the July deadline, so only an argument in favor appears.
Supporters say visitors pay the tax, not residents, and that most local governments nearby already charge 12 percent.
A yes vote approves raising the tax from 8 to 12 percent.
A no vote keeps the rate at 8 percent.
