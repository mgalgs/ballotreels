Ramona Municipal Water District Measure P asks the district's voters whether to amend Proposition NN, a limit voters adopted in 1988.
Proposition NN limits debt for major projects to about $3.3 million, a limit that rises each year with consumer prices.
For 38 years, the district has treated that debt limit as a limit on its spending, too.
Measure P would set a separate $10 million limit on spending for new projects that take on no debt.
Grants and donations would no longer count toward either limit.
The San Diego County Voter Information Guide does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Supporters say construction costs have risen faster than inflation, and holding elections before grant contracts has cost the district grants.
No argument against Measure P was filed with the Registrar of Voters for the San Diego County Voter Information Guide.
A yes vote means grants and donations would be exempt, and a $10 million spending limit would be set.
A no vote means Proposition NN would remain unchanged, with no spending limit specified.
