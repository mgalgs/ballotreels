## Facts and the passages that establish them

### Ballot question

S1 PDF p. 75 (same wording in S3 Section 4, p. 3):

> MEASURE P
> AMENDMENT OF PROPOSITION 'NN' TO MODERNIZE THE ORDINANCE. Shall the voters of the Ramona Municipal Water District adopt a measure amending Proposition 'NN' by (1) exempting grant funding and donations from the calculation of authorized expenditure limit; (2) increasing the expenditure limit for new projects from $1,000,000 to $10,000,000 where no debt is incurred; and (3) establishing annual adjustments to the expenditure limit based on the San Diego Area Consumer Price Index beginning January 1, 2028?

### Vote required

S1 PDF p. 75: "This measure requires Simple Majority (50%+1) of those voting on the measure." S3 Section 6: "the vote requirement for the Ordinance to pass is a majority (50% +1) of the votes cast."

### Who votes on it

The ballot question itself: "Shall the voters of the Ramona Municipal Water District adopt a measure". S3, Section 3 (p. 2): the Board "calls and orders a general district election to be held in the Ramona Municipal Water District, on Tuesday, November 3, 2026"; Section 7 (p. 3): "the submission of said ordinance to the voters of the District at the November 3, 2026, general district election".

In short: the voters of the Ramona Municipal Water District.

### Who placed it on the ballot

S1 PDF p. 75: "The Board of Directors for the Ramona Municipal Water District ("District") placed this measure on the ballot."

S3 pp. 4-5: "ADOPTED, SIGNED AND APPROVED at a regular meeting of the Board of Directors of Ramona Municipal Water District held on the 21st day of July 2026, by the following vote: AYES: Hickle, Norman, Piva, Zoria; NOES: None; ABSENT: Lynch; ABSTAIN: None". Signed Jim Hickle, President; attested Princess Norman, Secretary.

### Impartial analysis

S1 PDF p. 75, "DISTRICT COUNSEL IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS", signed "Steven E. Boehmer, District General Counsel". Key statements:

> If approved by a majority of the votes cast, this measure will amend Proposition "NN," which established Ordinance No. 108 (Chapter 99.04 of the District's Regulatory Code). Because Proposition NN was a citizens' initiative, it can only be repealed or amended by a majority vote.

> However, Proposition NN imposed an "authorized debt limit," rather than an "authorized expenditure limit." The initial limit was set at $1,000,000 but allowed for annual adjustments based on the San Diego Area Consumer Price Index (CPI) for all Urban Consumers. Based on those adjustments, the current limit is approximately $3,300,000. For the past 38 years, the District has treated the debt limit as an expenditure limit.

> A "yes" vote means the following: (1) grant funding and donations would be exempt from expenditure and debt limit calculations; (2) an "expenditure limit," will be established in the initial amount of $10,000,000, with CPI adjustments beginning January 1, 2028; and (3) debt for major projects will be limited to the amounts initially established by Proposition NN as adjusted annually since 1989, currently approximately $3,300,000.

> A "no" vote means that Proposition NN would remain unchanged with no expenditure limit specified and the current debt limit of approximately $3,300,000 as adjusted annually since 1989 would remain in place.

S3 recital (p. 1): Prop. NN was approved "on November 8, 1988". S3 recital (p. 1) also gives "approximately $5,000,000 for water storage reservoirs" as the current adjusted debt limit for that category.

### Arguments and rebuttals

- **Argument in favor**, S1 PDF p. 75. First line: "In 1988, Ramona Municipal Water District voters adopted Proposition NN which prohibits the District from spending more than $1 million for new capital projects, property acquisitions and/or any improvements to real property unless first approved by voters." Signers: Princess Norman (RMWD Director – Division 5 Board Secretary); Jim Piva (RMWD Director – Division 3 Board Vice-President).
- **Argument against**: none. S1 PDF p. 75: "No Argument Against Measure P was filed with the Registrar of Voter's Office."
- **Rebuttals**: none printed.

### Full text

**The guide does not print the full text.** S3 Section 5 (p. 3): "The District does not request the Registrar of Voters to print the entire text of the Ordinance in the voter information materials." S1 instead says: "If you desire a copy of the ordinance or measure, please call the district elections official's office at 858-505-7260".

The full text is S3 Exhibit A, pp. 6-10, "Full Text of Ballot Measure / Amendment to the District's Legislative Code / AMENDMENT TO CHAPTER 99.04 OF THE RAMONA MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICT LEGISLATIVE CODE TO ACKNOWLEDGE VOTER AMENDMENTS TO PROPOSITION NN". Additions are shown underlined in red and deletions struck through (for example "AND AMENDED ON NOVEMBER 3, 2026 BY MEASURE __"); read the scan, there is no text layer.
