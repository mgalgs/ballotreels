Measure M asks the voters of this road zone whether to raise its yearly road tax from $150 to $375 per parcel.
The county formed the zone in 1985 to pay for road improvements and maintenance.
The county counts 490 registered voters living in the zone.
The Board of Supervisors placed Measure M on the ballot at the request of the zone's advisory committee.
Measure M needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The new tax would apply to every parcel, built on or not, starting in the 2027-2028 fiscal year.
The tax could be adjusted each year with a construction cost index.
The money could pay only for the roads in the zone, and the tax would last until voters change or repeal it.
If Measure M fails, the $150 tax stays.
The county attorney's impartial analysis expects the tax to raise about $99,000 a year.
No argument against Measure M was submitted, the voter pamphlet of this county says, and it prints no rebuttals.
Supporters say no other public funds can pay for the zone's roads, and there is no reserve for emergency repairs.
A yes vote is a vote to increase the tax to $375 per parcel a year.
A no vote is a vote against the increase, and the $150 tax stays.
