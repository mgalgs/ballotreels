[Page 1]

I've consistently opposed raising taxes on the people of Fresno County, and I don't believe government should ask working families to pay more simply because it wants more revenue. That's why I view this proposal differently.

A Transient Occupancy Tax is paid primarily by visitors staying in hotels, motels, and short-term rentals for fewer than 30 days - not by Fresno County residents. Our families already pay property taxes, sales taxes, and other local taxes to support County services. This proposal asks visitors who benefit from those same services to help cover a small share of the cost.

When people visit Fresno County, they use our roads, rely on our public safety and emergency response systems, and enjoy the services our residents help fund. It's only fair that they contribute while they're here instead of leaving the entire bill to local taxpayers.

Nearly every county in California already has a Transient Occupancy Tax. Fresno County is one of the few that does not. Adopting a TOT simply brings us in line with what has become the statewide standard.

At the proposed 12% rate, the measure is expected to generate about $4.5 million each year to support essential County responsibilities, including public safety, wildfire preparedness, emergency response, 911 services, road maintenance, and other core services. It also helps reduce pressure on the County's General Fund, protecting local taxpayers from bearing those costs alone.

I remain committed to keeping taxes low for Fresno County residents. I support this proposal because it is a reasonable, fiscally responsible way to have visitors - not our residents - help pay for the services they use while visiting our community.

The undersigned proponent(s) or author(s) of the primary argument in favor of the Transient Occupancy Tax ballot proposition A at the countywide special election to be consolidated with the statewide general election for the County of Fresno to be held on November 3, 2026 hereby state that this argument is true and correct to the best of his knowledge and belief.
