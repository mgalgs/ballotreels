## Facts

**Ballot question.** County of Fresno page "Measure A: Short-Term Lodging Transient Occupancy Tax":
> "Measure A Ballot Question: "Fresno County Hotel Tax. Shall the measure to establish a tax paid only by guests staying in overnight short-term lodging in the unincorporated area of Fresno County at the rate of 12% of the rent for the stay, estimated to raise $4,500,000 annually, until changed or repealed, in general revenue for the County of Fresno, which provides public safety, wildfire/disaster response, 911, road maintenance, behavioral and public health, water infrastructure, parks, and other public services, be adopted?""
(Not yet checked against the wording in `resolution.pdf`.)

**Who placed it; who pays.** Impartial Analysis by County Counsel (Douglas T. Sloan, County Counsel; Katie Doerr, Assistant County Counsel; filed Jul 21 2026):
> "The Fresno County Board of Supervisors placed Measure __, also known as the "Hotel Tax," on the ballot ... If approved by the voters, this measure will impose a transient occupancy tax paid by guests staying 30 days or less in short term lodging in the unincorporated area of Fresno County. ... The amount of the tax is 12% of the rent for the stay."

**Revenue, start date, kind of tax.** Same analysis:
> "The measure is estimated to generate additional revenue of approximately $4.55 million annually. If the measure passes, the tax will be effective January 1, 2027, and will remain in effect until ended by voters. This tax is a "general tax," and the County may use the tax revenue for any governmental purpose ..."

Note: the ballot question says "$4,500,000 annually" and "until changed or repealed"; the analysis says "approximately $4.55 million" and "until ended by voters".

**Vote needed.** Same analysis:
> "A majority vote (more than 50% of votes cast) is required to pass the measure."

**Who votes.** The measure is countywide: all Fresno County voters vote on it, although the tax applies only in the unincorporated area. The analysis gives the area the tax applies to, not who votes. The county page groups it with the countywide measures, and Board-placed county measures go to all county voters. Not stated verbatim in a record I read.

**Referral.** County page: "Measure A was referred to the ballot by a unanimous vote of the Fresno County Board of Supervisors on June 30, 2026."
