Argument Against Fresno County Transient Occupancy Tax Measure

Fresno County

November 3, 2026 Consolidated Statewide General Election

Word Count: 289

Text of Argument Below This Line

==============================================================

How has your cost of living fared over the last few years? What about that of your neighbors? Rather than cast a vote in favor of this new tax for the Fresno County we ask you to consider the following:

This tax will impose $4,500,000 every year on visitors and residents of Fresno County with no end date. Perhaps you think your share of that burden is well worth paying, given the promise of continuing services in the county. We agree that road and safety services are important, and if you feel that this is a cause worth giving money towards, we applaud your generosity and your desire to contribute to your community. But we ask you to consider other options to fund these projects; if you are willing to contribute to these in the form of a tax, would you be willing to do so through voluntary charity instead?

Think again of your neighbors: many of them have struggled the past few years, and a whopping 12% tax undoubtedly represents a substantial burden on their cost of living. The county residents that operate short-term rentals will end up shouldering the costs when they can't raise their rates sufficiently. That's less money available to put food on the table, save for their children's higher education, or even pay those expensive medical bills after the paramedics have left. We applaud the community spirit of those who wish to raise money for a worthy cause; we simply ask that they first turn towards voluntary means to do so, especially given the difficult financial times that many find themselves in.

Allow your neighbors to decide for themselves which causes they choose to fund, and vote NO on Measure _.

Join us: https://lpfresno.org/NoHotelTax
