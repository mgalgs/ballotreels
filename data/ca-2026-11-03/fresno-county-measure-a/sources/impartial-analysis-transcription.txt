IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS BY COUNTY COUNSEL

MEASURE __

The Fresno County Board of Supervisors placed Measure __, also known as the "Hotel Tax," on the ballot, as authorized by the California Constitution and state law. If approved by the voters, this measure will impose a transient occupancy tax paid by guests staying 30 days or less in short term lodging in the unincorporated area of Fresno County. The tax is paid by the person who stays in a hotel, motel, short term rental, or other lodging, and remitted to the County by the person operating the lodging. The amount of the tax is 12% of the rent for the stay. The measure contains exclusions for certain types of lodging.

The measure obligates lodging operators to collect and remit the tax, keep certain records, and provide information to the Tax Collector. It imposes penalties upon operators for nonpayment and late payment, and provides procedures to dispute the tax.

The measure is estimated to generate additional revenue of approximately $4.55 million annually. If the measure passes, the tax will be effective January 1, 2027, and will remain in effect until ended by voters. This tax is a "general tax," and the County may use the tax revenue for any governmental purpose, including public safety, wildfire and disaster response, 911, road maintenance, behavioral and public health, water infrastructure, parks, and other public services. Funding from the measure is subject to the same standards of public disclosure as all County spending, including annual audits by an external auditor.

A majority vote (more than 50% of votes cast) is required to pass the measure. A "Yes" vote supports the passage of the measure; a "No" vote opposes passage of the measure.
