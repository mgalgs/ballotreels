City of Orange Measure I asks city voters whether to add a 1 percent sales tax for 13 years.
The City Council placed Measure I on the ballot, and it needs a majority of the vote to pass.
The tax would apply to most retail sales, but not most groceries or prescription medicines.
The money would go to the general fund, for any lawful city purpose, such as public safety, streets and parks.
An oversight committee and a yearly independent audit would check the spending.
The tax would end after 13 years, unless voters end, change or extend it sooner.
The city attorney's impartial analysis says the city estimates the tax would raise about $37 million a year.
Opponents say Measure I is a scheme to cover overspending by the city, and averages $800 per household a year.
Supporters say the city cut 10 percent of staff first, and nearly two-thirds of its sales taxes are paid by nonresidents.
A yes vote authorizes the city to establish the 1 percent sales tax for 13 years.
A no vote does not authorize the city to establish the tax.
