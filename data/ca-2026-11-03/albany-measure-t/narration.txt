City of Albany Measure T asks Albany voters whether to create a new parcel tax to maintain and improve street trees and streetlights.
The City Council placed Measure T on the ballot in July, and it needs two-thirds of Albany voters to pass.
Starting in July 2027, property owners would pay a yearly tax by the square foot of land, rising with inflation by up to 3 percent a year.
Only the first 500,000 square feet of any parcel would be taxed.
Very low-income residents would pay nothing, low-income residents half, and leaseholders on exempt land would pay too.
The money would go to a fund for street trees and streetlighting, with a yearly report on how it is spent.
The City Attorney's analysis estimates the tax would raise $775,000 a year.
Supporters say failing decades-old lamps and too few street trees need dedicated, reliable funding that the city does not have.
The Alameda County Composite Voter Information Guide says that no argument against Measure T was submitted for this election.
A yes vote approves the new parcel tax for street trees and streetlighting.
A no vote does not approve the new parcel tax for street trees and streetlighting.
