## Facts with quotes

- **Ballot summary (p. 39):** "This Measure would increase the existing library special tax from $28 to $50 per parcel per year for habitable parcels within Susanville city limits. Funds would support library materials, facility operations, technology, educational programs, and staffing. The tax would continue annually until repealed. An annual report would be required. Approval requires a two-thirds vote."
- **Analysis (p. 35):** "Measure Z proposes increasing the District's existing special parcel tax from Twenty-Eight dollars ($28.00) to Fifty dollars ($50.00) per year for each habitable parcel and each living unit of multiple living unit parcels within the City Limits (each, a "Parcel") of the City of Susanville (the "City")." / "The existing special tax of Twenty-Eight dollars ($28.00) per Parcel has not been increased in thirty-two (32) years."
- **Start / revenue:** "If approved, the increased annual tax of Fifty dollars ($50.00) per Parcel would take effect July 1, 2027" / "expected to generate approximately One Hundred Ninety-Four Thousand Six Hundred dollars ($194,600.00) in annual revenue."
- **No exemptions:** "This measure provides no exemptions."
- **Vote needed:** "Approval requires at least two-thirds of votes cast, as required by Article XIIIC, Section 2(d) of the California Constitution. If the measure fails, the existing annual tax of Twenty-Eight dollars ($28.00) per Parcel remains in effect."
- **Tax rate statement (p. 39):** "The special tax approved by this measure is a flat parcel tax of $50.00 per year per habitable parcel or unit"; "The tax is not subject to a sunset date"; "This measure authorizes a special tax, not a bond issuance. No debt service will be incurred."
