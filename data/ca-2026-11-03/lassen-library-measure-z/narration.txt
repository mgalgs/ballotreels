Measure Z asks the voters of this library district whether to raise the library parcel tax from $28 to $50 a year.
The district runs the public library, and already charges a special tax of $28 a year.
The tax falls on each habitable parcel or living unit inside the city limits.
Measure Z needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The tax would rise to $50 in July 2027, with no exemptions, until voters or the district board repeal it.
The money could pay only for library services, such as books, technology, programs and staff.
The district's tax rate statement estimates that the $50 tax would raise about $194,600 a year.
Supporters say the rate is unchanged in over three decades while costs rose sharply, and the library runs a deficit.
No argument against Measure Z was submitted, so the county voter guide prints only the argument in favor of it.
A yes vote supports raising the tax to $50 a year for each parcel.
A no vote opposes the increase and keeps the $28 tax.
