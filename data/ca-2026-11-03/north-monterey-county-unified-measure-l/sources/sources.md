## Quoted facts

- **Ballot question** (Resolution, Exhibit B-1 "Ballot Measure Statement"): "To repair and upgrade older schools to meet current health/safety codes; remove asbestos/lead pipes; fix deteriorating roofs, plumbing, dry rot, HVAC and electrical systems; improve school safety/security; and upgrade aging classrooms including science, technology, and skilled trades facilities; shall North Monterey County Unified School District's measure authorizing $40,000,000 in bonds at legal rates be adopted, levying $32 per $100,000 assessed value ($2,800,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding, with citizen oversight, independent audits and all money locally controlled?"
- **Letter / placement** (district page): "On June 25, 2026, the NMCUSD Governing Board voted to place Measure L, a $40 million bond measure, on the November 3, 2026 ballot".
- **Threshold** (resolution): bonds under Proposition 39 "to be approved by fifty-five percent (55%) of the voters".
- **Repayment** (resolution): "The tax levied to repay the Bonds is estimated to average $2,800,000 annually in all years bonds are outstanding anticipated to be collected through fiscal year 2053/54 at a rate of approximately $0.032 per $100 / $32.00 per $100,000 of assessed value".
- **Existing bonds** (district page): "Homeowners currently pay $38.54 per $100,000 of assessed valuation toward the 2002 and 2013 bonds."
