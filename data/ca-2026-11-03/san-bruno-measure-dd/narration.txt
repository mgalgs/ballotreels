City of San Bruno Measure DD asks voters in the city whether to ban safe and sane fireworks.
Today, a 2005 ordinance approved by city voters allows limited sale and use of safe and sane fireworks.
The City Council placed Measure DD on the ballot, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
Measure DD would replace the 2005 ordinance with a ban on selling, using or setting off safe and sane fireworks.
The published measure documents of San Mateo County do not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Supporters say much of the city lies in high fire hazard zones, where even state-approved fireworks carry fire risks.
Opponents say the ban ends the largest yearly fundraiser of many local nonprofits, and does not stop illegal fireworks.
A yes vote on Measure DD prohibits the limited sale and use allowed today.
A no vote on Measure DD continues the limited sale and use allowed today.
