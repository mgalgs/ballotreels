ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE ___

Vote NO to Protect San Bruno’s Nonprofits, Community Services, and Public Safety

Every Fourth of July, San Bruno’s nonprofit organizations raise funds that stay right here in our community. These local volunteers support youth sports, veterans’ programs, scholarships, camps, school activities, community projects, and families in need. For many of these organizations, this is their largest fundraiser of the year.

For years, these nonprofit organizations have done more than support community programs. They have also helped fund San Bruno’s Fourth of July public safety efforts, contributing tens of thousands of dollars annually toward police services, fire prevention, public education, inspections, enforcement, and cleanup activities.

Everyone agrees that illegal fireworks are dangerous and should be stopped. But Measure ___ does not stop illegal fireworks. Illegal fireworks are already illegal. Criminals who ignore today’s laws will not obey tomorrow’s ban. Instead, Measure ___ eliminates one of San Bruno’s most successful nonprofit fundraising programs while the City continues to face the growing challenge of illegal fireworks without the long-standing financial support provided by local nonprofit organizations.

For decades, San Bruno nonprofits have partnered with the City to strengthen our community and support Fourth of July public safety efforts. Measure ___ ends that partnership.

Measure ___ does not stop illegal fireworks. It eliminates funding for local nonprofits and public safety efforts that will still be needed to protect San Bruno neighborhoods.

[PDF page 4]

Protect our local nonprofits. Protect the community services they provide and their support for San Bruno’s public safety efforts. Focus enforcement where it belongs: on illegal fireworks.

Vote NO on Measure ___.
