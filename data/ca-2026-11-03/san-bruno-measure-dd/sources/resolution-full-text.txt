RESOLUTION NO. 2026 - 077

A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF SAN BRUNO SUBMITTING TO THE VOTERS AT THE NOVEMBER 3, 2026 GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION A MEASURE THAT PROPOSES ADOPTION OF AN ORDINANCE TO PROHIBIT THE SALE, USE, OR DISCHARGE OF SAFE AND SANE FIREWORKS, REQUESTING THAT SUCH ELECTION BE CONSOLIDATED WITH THE STATEWIDE GENERAL ELECTION HELD ON THAT DATE, TAKING CERTAIN OTHER ACTIONS, AND FINDING SUCH ACTION EXEMPT FROM CEQA

WHEREAS, by prior resolution, the City Council called a general municipal election (the “Election”) for November 3, 2026, to be consolidated with the statewide general election to be held on that same date;

WHEREAS, in 2005, the voters approved a “Safe and Sane Fireworks Ordinance,” codified as Chapter 6.50 of the San Bruno Municipal Code, to allow the continued sale, use, and discharge of safe and sane fireworks;

WHEREAS, the State Fireworks Law, being California Health and Safety Code sections 12500 through 12728, permits each city to regulate or prohibit the sale, use or discharge of “safe and sane fireworks” within its jurisdiction;

WHEREAS, San Bruno and Pacifica are the only cities in San Mateo County that permit the sale of “Safe and Sane” fireworks,

WHEREAS, the State Fire Marshal released updated Fire Hazard Severity Zone maps in 2025, and much of San Bruno is now designated as a high fire hazard severity zone, heightening the risk associated with the discharge of consumer fireworks;

WHEREAS, the City Council has determined that the sale, use and discharge of safe and sane fireworks presents a risk of fires and the generation of smoke and air emissions, noise, and litter, which may adversely affect the environment;

WHEREAS, the City Council has determined that the sale, use, and discharge of safe and sane fireworks presents a risk of harm to persons and property; and

WHEREAS, in order to more fully protect the health and safety of persons and property within the City of San Bruno, the City Council desires to have the matter of an ordinance that would prohibit the sale, use, and discharge of safe and sane fireworks be presented to the voters on the November 3, 2026 ballot.

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF SAN BRUNO, CALIFORNIA, AS FOLLOWS:

1. Pursuant to Election Code Sections 9222 and 10201, the City Council directs that the ordinance attached hereto as Exhibit “A” and incorporated herein by reference (the “Measure”) be submitted to the qualified voters of the City of San Bruno at the Election.

2. The question shall appear on the ballot as follows:

[PDF page 2]

Shall the City of San Bruno Safe and Sane Fireworks Ordinance be repealed to prohibit the sale, use, and discharge of safe and sane fireworks in the City of San Bruno?
Yes
No

3. The Measure shall only take effect if at least a majority of those voting on the question vote in favor of it at the Election.

4. The full text of the Measure, attached to this Resolution as Exhibit A, being the proposed ordinance repealing the City of San Bruno Safe and Sane Fireworks Ordinance, shall be completely printed in the voter pamphlet.

5. The City Council hereby directs the City Clerk to transmit a copy of the Measure to the City Attorney. The City Attorney shall prepare an impartial analysis of the Measure, not to exceed 500 words in length, showing the effect of the Measure on the existing law and the operation of the measure, and transmit such impartial analysis to the City Clerk no later than August 18, 2026 by 5:00 p.m.

6. Pursuant to Elections Code Section 10403, the City Council requests that the San Mateo County Board of Supervisors consent to consolidation of the election for the Measure with the general election to be conducted by the San Mateo County Registrar of voters on November 3, 2026. The election shall be held in all respects as if there were only one election, and only one form of ballot shall be used. The election will be held and conducted in accordance with the provisions of law regulating a special election consolidated with a statewide election. The City will bear all costs customarily charged by the County to a city in connection with a ballot question submitted at a municipal election that is consolidated with an election conducted by the County.

7. The City Council directs the City Clerk to file a certified copy of this Resolution with the Registrar of Voters of San Mateo County.

8. Notice of the time and place of holding of the election is given and the City Clerk is authorized, instructed, and directed to give further or additional notice of the election in the time, form, and manner as required by law.

9. The Board of Supervisors is authorized to canvass the returns of the Election pursuant to Section 10411 of the Elections Code.

10. Pursuant to Section 10002 of the Elections Code, the Board of Supervisors is requested to permit the County Clerk to render all services specified by Section 10418 of the Elections Code relating to the election, for which services the City agrees to reimburse the County, in accordance with current County pro-rations and allocation procedures.

11. The City Clerk shall file a certified copy of this Resolution with the Clerk of the County Board of Supervisors.

[PDF page 3]

12. The City Clerk is hereby authorized to do all things necessary and proper to implement the provisions of this Resolution.

13. This Measure is exempt from the California Environmental Quality Act pursuant to Section 15061(b)(3) because it can be seen with certainty that there is no possibility that this ballot measure will have a significant effect on the environment. To the extent this Measure results in changes to the sale, use or discharge of safe and sane fireworks in the City, such effects would not result in a substantial adverse change in the environment, and may reduce fire risk, air emissions, noise, and litter associated with safe and sane fireworks.

EXHIBIT A

Proposed Ordinance

AN ORDINANCE OF THE PEOPLE OF THE CITY OF SAN BRUNO, CALIFORNIA TO REPEAL THE SAFE AND SANE FIREWORKS ORDINANCE CODIFIED IN CHAPTER 6.50 OF THE SAN BRUNO MUNICIPAL CODE AND REPLACING SUCH ORDINANCE WITH A BAN ON THE SALE, USE, OR DISCHARGE OF SAFE AND SANE FIREWORKS IN THE CITY OF SAN BRUNO

THE PEOPLE OF CITY OF SAN BRUNO ORDAIN AS FOLLOWS:

SECTION 1. The purpose of this Ordinance is to prohibit the sale, use or discharge of safe and sane fireworks in the City of San Bruno by repealing the Safe and Sane Fireworks Ordinance, an initiative measure adopted by the voters in 2005 to allow the continued sale, use, or discharge of safe and sane fireworks in the City of San Bruno, and replacing such ordinance with a ban on the sale, use, or discharge of safe and sane fireworks in the City of San Bruno.

SECTION 2. Chapter 6.50 of the San Bruno Municipal Code is hereby amended and restated in its entirety, to read as follows:

“Section 6.50.010. Sale, use, and discharge of safe and sane fireworks prohibited. The sale, use, or discharge of safe and sane fireworks is prohibited. The term “safe and sane fireworks” shall have the meaning ascribed to it in Section 12529 of the California Health and Safety Code, or such other law as amended from time to time.”

SECTION 3. If any provision, clause, sentence or paragraph of this Ordinance or the application thereof to any person or circumstance shall be held invalid, such invalidity shall not affect the other provisions of this Ordinance which can be given effect without the invalid provisions or application, and to this end the provision of this Ordinance are declared to be severable.

SECTION 4. This ordinance shall take effect 10 days after the City Council declares the results of the vote at the November 3, 2026, election.

Approved by the People of the City of San Bruno at the General Municipal Election Held on November 3, 2026.

Rico E. Medina
Mayor
