# Measure DD — City of San Bruno — Ordinance banning "safe and sane" fireworks (repeals the 2005 fireworks ordinance)

- **Jurisdiction:** City of San Bruno.
- **Kind:** Council-placed ordinance that amends and restates Municipal Code Chapter 6.50 (the voter-approved 2005 "Safe and Sane Fireworks Ordinance") to prohibit the sale, use and discharge of safe and sane fireworks. Placed by City Council Resolution No. 2026-077.
- **Who votes:** San Bruno voters. Resolution Sec. 1, PDF p. 1: the Council "directs that the ordinance attached hereto as Exhibit "A" ... (the "Measure") be submitted to the qualified voters of the City of San Bruno at the Election."
- **Threshold:** simple majority. Resolution Sec. 3, PDF p. 2: "The Measure shall only take effect if at least a majority of those voting on the question vote in favor of it at the Election."

## Ballot question (verbatim)

Resolution Sec. 2, PDF p. 2 (scan; the lead-in "The question shall appear on the ballot as follows:" is at the foot of p. 1):

> Shall the City of San Bruno Safe and Sane Fireworks Ordinance be repealed to prohibit the sale, use, and discharge of safe and sane fireworks in the City of San Bruno?

## Full text

- **Resolution No. 2026-077**, City Council of the City of San Bruno, PDF p. 3: "I hereby certify that foregoing Resolution No. 2026 - 077 was introduced and adopted by the San Bruno City Council at a regular meeting on July 28, 2026, by the following vote: AYES: Councilmembers: Hamilton, M. Medina, Salazar, Alvarez, Mayor R. Medina NOES: Councilmembers: None ABSENT: Councilmembers: None". Signed Lupita Huerta, City Clerk. Filed with the Chief Elections Officer "JUL 30 2026" (stamp, p. 1).
- Recitals, PDF p. 1: "in 2005, the voters approved a "Safe and Sane Fireworks Ordinance," codified as Chapter 6.50 of the San Bruno Municipal Code"; "San Bruno and Pacifica are the only cities in San Mateo County that permit the sale of "Safe and Sane" fireworks"; "the State Fire Marshal released updated Fire Hazard Severity Zone maps in 2025, and much of San Bruno is now designated as a high fire hazard severity zone".
- Sec. 4, PDF p. 2: the full text "shall be completely printed in the voter pamphlet."
- **Exhibit A**, PDF p. 4, Sec. 2: new "Section 6.50.010. Sale, use, and discharge of safe and sane fireworks prohibited. The sale, use, or discharge of safe and sane fireworks is prohibited." Sec. 4: "This ordinance shall take effect 10 days after the City Council declares the results of the vote at the November 3, 2026, election."

## Impartial analysis

`impartial-analysis.pdf`, p. 1: "CITY ATTORNEY IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE DD", signed "/s/ Trisha Ortiz, City Attorney for the City of San Bruno".

- "Measure DD asks the voters of the City of San Bruno whether they want to repeal the City of San Bruno Safe and Sane Fireworks Ordinance to prohibit the sale, use, and discharge of safe and sane fireworks in the City of San Bruno."
- "Measure DD was placed on the ballot by the San Bruno City Council. This measure, if approved by the voters, adopts an ordinance to amend Chapter 6.50 of the San Bruno Municipal Code to prohibit the sale, use, and discharge of safe and sane fireworks in the City of San Bruno."
- "In 2005, the voters of the City of San Bruno approved an initiative measure adopting an ordinance to add Chapter 6.50 to the San Bruno Municipal Code. Chapter 6.50 permits the limited sale, use, and discharge of "safe and sane fireworks,""
- "Measure DD will become effective only if approved by a majority of the voters voting on the measure. A "yes" vote on Measure DD is a vote to prohibit the sale, use and discharge of safe and sane fireworks in the City of San Bruno. A "no" vote on Measure DD is a vote to continue the limited sale, use and discharge of safe and sane fireworks in the City of San Bruno, as provided by the current Chapter 6.50 of the San Bruno Municipal Code."

2. Sandy Alvarez — "Vice Mayor"

4. Marty Medina — "Councilmember"
5. Michael Salazar — "Councilmember"

Key text (p. 3):
- "In recent years, fireworks related incidents have resulted in between 5 and 11 fires annually, including exterior and structural fires. One incident caused an estimated $200,000 in damage to a single home."
- "San Bruno and Pacifica are now the only cities in San Mateo County that still permit "Safe and Sane" fireworks."
- "This measure simply asks voters whether the City should repeal the existing ordinance and prohibit "Safe and Sane" fireworks. It is a practical, safety-focused decision."

4. Mike Yip (form: "Michael Yip") — "Fundraising Coordinator/Board Member, SanBruno Soccer"

**Rebuttal to argument in favor** (`rebuttal-to-argument-for.pdf`, written by opponents). Form p. 1: "Rebuttal to Argument in Favor of Measure DD", signed by the same individuals as the primary argument, contact "KC Jenkins". Signers printed under the text (p. 3): the same five as the argument against (Daimhin Kirwan, Commander, San Bruno Police Association; Robert Luttrell, Vice President, Parent Boosters of 72; Jennifer Berliner, Fundraising Director, San Bruno Youth Baseball; Mike Yip, Fundraising Coordinator/Board Member, SanBruno Soccer; Stephan Marshall, San Bruno Resident).

Key text (pp. 2-3, "Vote NO on Measure DD — Protect Public Safety and Nonprofit Funding"):
- "Supporters of Measure DD cite "fireworks-related" incidents, but they do not tell you how many involved legal, State Fire Marshal-approved Safe and Sane fireworks and how many involved illegal aerial fireworks and explosives."
- "Recent City reports show that fireworks-related calls and citations have substantially declined."
- "Don't mistake banning something legal for solving the problem of illegal fireworks."

**Rebuttal to argument against** (`rebuttal-to-argument-against.pdf`, written by the Council). Form p. 1: "Rebuttal to Argument Against Measure DD", "Signed by Same Individual(s) Selected for the Voter Information Pamphlet for the Primary Argument", contact "Michael Salazar"; received AUG 18 2026. The signer form (p. 2) is blank because the same signers as the argument in favor apply (the five Council members above).

Key text (p. 3):
- "Since voters approved Safe and Sane fireworks in 2005, conditions in San Bruno have changed. The city now experiences heightened fire risk in designated hazard areas, and public safety agencies report increased seasonal hazards."
- "The community must weigh both the longstanding fundraising benefits to local nonprofits and the evolving fire-safety considerations that have emerged over time."
