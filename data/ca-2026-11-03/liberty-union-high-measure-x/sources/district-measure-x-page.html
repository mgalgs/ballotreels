On July 22, 2026, the Liberty Union High School Board of Trustees voted unanimously to authorize an election for a school bond to be held on November 3, 2026. 

The District has been evaluating its facility needs and has undertaken a thorough Facilities Master Planning process. Facilities continue to need upgrades and improvements to provide students and staff with modern educational facilities and programs supporting future success. 

The proposed school facilities bond, Measure X, would provide locally controlled funding of $165 million to improve and modernize our schools while helping preserve these important community assets for future generations, with an estimated tax rate of $20 per $100,000 of assessed value during the term of the bonds.

KEY PROJECTS PLANNED FOR OUR SCHOOLS

 


LIBERTY HIGH SCHOOL 

  • Replace the aging N-Wing classroom building with modern instructional facilities 
  • Replace the aging H & K-Wing classroom buildings with modern instructional facilities 
  • Modernize the Liberty High School Library to support research, collaboration, technology, and student learning 
  • Upgrade and modernize aging science classrooms and laboratories

FREEDOM HIGH SCHOOL 

  • Replace 21 aging portable classrooms with permanent, modern learning spaces 
  • Construct a new Career Technical Education Center supporting Agriculture, Law Enforcement, Fire Science, and Public Safety programs 
  • Upgrade science laboratories and hands-on learning environments 

 

HERITAGE HIGH SCHOOL 

  • Construct a new six-classroom instructional building 
  • Build an auxiliary gymnasium to support athletics, physical education, and student activities 
  • Upgrade core infrastructure and instructional space to meet current and future student needs

 

DISTRICT-WIDE IMPROVEMENTS 
 Improve campus safety and security systems   Improve accessibility and facility efficiency
Upgrade roofs, plumbing, electrical, heating, and ventilation systems  Remove hazardous materials where needed 
Enhance technology infrastructure  Support modern classrooms and career readiness programs

 

 

 MAXIMIZING LOCAL DOLLARS 
If approved by voters, the measure could help Liberty Union High School District qualify for approximately $35 million in state matching funds, bringing additional resources to local schools that might otherwise go to other districts. 

 

 


REMEMBER TO VOTE ON ELECTION DAY  
Election Day: Tuesday, November 3, 2026   |   Learn more at www.luhsd.net/measure-x
