Measure X asks the voters of this school district whether to approve $165 million in bonds to repair and upgrade school facilities.
The district proposed the bonds by resolution, and the measure needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money could pay for projects such as roofs, plumbing, heating and electrical systems, removing asbestos and lead pipes, and science labs.
A citizens' oversight committee and yearly performance and financial audits would check the spending.
Approval does not guarantee funding beyond the bond money, and some projects may count on state matching funds that are not assured.
The district's tax rate statement estimates a tax of $20 per $100,000 of assessed value, both on average and at its highest, through 2057-2058.
The statement puts the total to repay, with interest, at about $350 million.
Opponents say Measure X comes on top of two existing bonds still being repaid, and its project list is not specific.
Supporters say many facilities are over 60 years old and need critical repairs, and that oversight and audits are required.
A yes vote is in favor of authorizing the bonds.
A no vote is against authorizing the bonds.
