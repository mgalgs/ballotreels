San Mateo County Measure L asks county voters whether the county charter should make long-term resilience to weather events a county priority.
San Mateo County is one of 15 California counties with a charter, which its voters first approved in 1932.
The preamble at the start of the charter does not address resilience to weather events today.
Measure L would add a paragraph making long-term resilience against fire, flood, wind, rain, heat, drought and sea level rise a county priority.
The paragraph says that work would be done together with cities, districts and other public agencies.
The measure would not change any other part of the charter.
The Board of Supervisors placed Measure L on the ballot, and it needs a majority of the vote to pass.
The published measure documents of San Mateo County do not include a cost estimate for the measure.
No argument for Measure L appears in the published measure documents of San Mateo County.
No argument against Measure L appears in the published measure documents of San Mateo County.
A yes vote means the charter preamble would be amended to add this priority.
A no vote means the charter preamble would not be amended to add it.
