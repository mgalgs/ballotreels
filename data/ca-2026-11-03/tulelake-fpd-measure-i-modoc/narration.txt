Measure I asks the voters of this fire protection district whether to raise the district's yearly parcel tax for fire protection.
In 1987, the district's voters approved a yearly tax of $16 for each vacant parcel, $25 for each improved parcel and $50 for each business.
Measure I needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
Measure I would raise those amounts to $50, $100 and $150, starting in the 2027-2028 fiscal year.
The money would go to a special fund for fire, rescue, emergency medical and ambulance services.
The tax would continue until the district's voters repeal it.
The ballot question estimates the tax would raise about $106,250 a year.
No argument against Measure I appears among the official documents that either county has posted online for this ballot measure.
Supporters say living costs rose over 177 percent while the tax stayed the same, putting services and fire insurance at risk.
A yes vote adopts the ordinance raising the parcel tax to the new amounts.
A no vote means the ordinance raising the parcel tax does not take effect.
