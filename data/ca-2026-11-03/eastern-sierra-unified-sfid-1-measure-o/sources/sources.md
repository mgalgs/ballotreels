## Quoted facts

- Ballot question: "To construct and equip classrooms and career technical education (CTE) facilities at Coleville High School and Lee Vining High School for job skills/career readiness in engineering, science, automotive technology, welding, carpentry, agricultural mechanics and other skilled trades; and improve school safety/security systems, shall the measure be adopted for School Facilities Improvement District No. 1 of the Eastern Sierra Unified School District authorizing $6,000,000 in bonds at legal rates, levying approximately $19 per $100,000 assessed value ($390,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding, with citizen oversight, audits, and all money staying local?"
- Who votes: analysis, "Measure O would authorize the Eastern Sierra Unified School District (“the District”) to issue and sell general obligation bonds within School Facilities Improvement District No. 1 of the District, which encompasses Coleville High School and Lee Vining High School."
- Origin: "This measure was placed on the ballot by the Board of Trustees of the Eastern Sierra Unified School District".
- Vote needed: "Passage of Measure O requires approval by at least 55% of the voters voting on the measure".
- Use limits: "Bond proceeds cannot be used for teacher or administrator salaries, pensions, benefits, or other district operating expenses."
- Oversight: "The measure requires the Board of Trustees to establish an independent Citizens’ Oversight Committee, within 60 days after the election results are entered in the Board’s minutes".
- Tax rate statement: average "$19 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. It is currently expected that the tax will be collected until fiscal year 2054-2055."; highest "$19 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. This rate is projected to apply in each year that bonds are outstanding."; total debt service "approximately $11,300,000."
- Argument in favor signers: "Deborah Bush, Local Business Owner; Richard Malekos, Chairman, Antelope Valley Senior Center; John Peters, Local Business Owner; Brianna Brown, President, Bridgeport Chamber of Commerce; Jeremy Ross, President, June Lake Loop Chamber of Commerce".
