Measure T asks the voters of this city whether to add a second half percent sales tax.
Today, the city already charges a half percent sales tax of its own, set to end in 2047.
Measure T would add another half percent, for a total city rate of 1 percent.
The new tax would last until voters repeal it.
Groceries and prescription medicine would not be taxed.
As a general tax, the money would go into the city general fund, for any city purpose.
Measure T needs a simple majority to pass.
The city attorney's impartial analysis estimates about $8.4 million a year in added revenue.
No argument for Measure T is printed in its posted pages.
No argument against Measure T is printed in its posted pages.
A yes vote approves the added half percent sales tax.
A no vote rejects the added sales tax.
