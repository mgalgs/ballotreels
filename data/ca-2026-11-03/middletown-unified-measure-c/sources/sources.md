## Facts and quotes

**Ballot question (p1):** "To improve the quality of education; repair/replace leaky roofs; construct/improve science, technology, engineering, arts, mathematics, agriculture, and career technical education facilities; and replace outdated heating, ventilation and air-conditioning systems; shall Middletown Unified School District's measure authorizing $38,000,000 of bonds, at legal rates, and levying approximately $49 per $100,000 of assessed value ($2,155,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding, be adopted, with annual audits, independent citizens' oversight, NO money for administrator salaries and all money spent locally?"

**Who votes / vote needed (full text, p1):** "By approval of this school improvement bond measure by at least 55% of the voters of the Middletown Unified School District (the "District") voting on the measure, the District shall be authorized to issue and sell bonds of up to $38,000,000".

**Impartial analysis (p5)** (signed Lloyd C. Guintivano, County Counsel, County of Lake), key statements:
- "Measure "C", a Middletown Unified School District ("District") bond measure, seeks voter approval to authorize the District to issue general obligation bonds in an aggregate principal amount not to exceed $38,000,000".
- "Both the School Bond Law and the General Obligation Bond Law authorize the governing board of a school district to issue and sell general obligation bonds ... if approved by at least 55 percent of the voters voting on the measure".
- Safeguards include an independent citizens' oversight committee to ensure "that no funds are used for any teacher or administrative salaries or other school operating expenses."

**Tax rate statement (p5, "Exhibit B — Tax Information Statement")** (dated August 5, 2026, Jeff Crane, Superintendent): average "$47.33 per $100,000 of assessed valuation", final year "fiscal year 2061-62"; highest "$49.00 per $100,000 of assessed valuation in fiscal year 2026-27"; total debt service "approximately $77,562,917".

**Argument in favor (p6):** signed Zoi Bracisco (School Board President), Jose Simon III (Chairman Middletown Rancheria), Kurtis Woodard (Business Owner/Citizens Bond Oversight Committee).

**Argument against:** "NO ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE C WAS SUBMITTED" (p6).
