Measure P asks the voters of this city whether to tax hotel stays at 10 percent of the rent.
The City Council placed Measure P on the ballot in May 2026.
Guests would pay 10 percent of their rent for hotel stays of 30 days or less.
Measure P is a general tax, which the ballot question says would fund city services, programs, economic development and capital improvements.
The ballot question says the tax would remain until repealed by the voters.
The measure also lets the City Council repeal the tax without a vote.
It needs a majority of the vote to pass.
The ballot question estimates that the tax would raise about $250,000 a year for each hotel.
No argument for Measure P appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
No argument against Measure P appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
A yes vote on Measure P adopts the 10 percent hotel tax.
A no vote on Measure P does not adopt the 10 percent hotel tax.
