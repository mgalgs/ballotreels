## Ballot question (draft resolution, p. 2)

> Shall Section 606 of the City Charter be amended to provide that a person appointed to fill a vacancy on the City Council shall serve until a successor is elected at the next General Municipal Election (held in November of even-numbered years), instead of at the earliest statewide election, thereby avoiding the cost of a separate election to fill the vacancy and the possibility of two City Council elections in the same year?

("in substantially the following form"; not confirmed against the printed ballot.)

## Full text

Exhibit A (pp. 6–8) restates §606 (Vacancies). The change is in §606(d), the appointee's term; the text extracts with underline and strikeout merged, so quote it from the PDF, not from extracted text. Unchanged context, p. 7, §606(c): "Within sixty days of the date the office is officially declared vacant, the City Council shall ... elect to fill the vacancy by appointment or by calling an election." §606(g) (p. 8) keeps the limit that the Council "shall not make an appointment to fill a vacancy if the appointment would result in more than two Council members or one Councilmember and the Mayor currently serving on the Council as appointees".

Adoption: adopted with measures G and H on 2026-06-23 (Legistar matter 26-0622).
