Rebuttal to Argument Against Measure ☒

[Pursuant to §9285: Maximum 250 words]

American democracy is under attack as big money, special interests, and sensationalized media undermine our national elections. At times like these, local governments must work to strengthen democracy and increase voter turnout. That’s why passing Measure I is so important.

Sunnyvale Charter Section 606.d states that appointees “shall hold office until the next General Municipal Election or Special Municipal Election consolidated with the next statewide election." Depending on timing, a vacant seat can go up for BOTH the primary AND general elections. A vacancy triggered a double-election in 2016 and nearly triggered one in 2022.

This is a problem. We need as many residents voting for City Council as possible. Consolidating council races with low-turnout primary and special elections, when voters don’t expect them, depresses turnout and weakens local democracy.

Running for office costs time, money, and stress. Having to run two campaigns in a year will deter qualified candidates, while having to double-fundraise will favor those with deep pockets or wealthy backers. We already have trouble attracting candidates: since 2022, half of our City Council races have gone uncontested. Why make that problem worse?

The opposition worries about longer appointed terms, but Measure I only adds months at most. They cite outdated reports and problems that Sunnyvale addressed years ago. They note mayoral elections can create vacancies, but Measure I doesn’t affect this.

The opposition’s arguments simply don’t make sense, and double elections don’t either.

Protect local democracy and maximize turnout. Learn more at www.YES-on-GHI.com, and vote YES on Measure I.
