Measure Q asks the voters of this school district whether to levy a parcel tax of $148 a parcel for eight years.
Measure Q needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The tax would be adjusted each year for inflation, and continuing it would be subject to voter approval.
It names core programs in math, science, reading, writing and arts, keeping qualified teachers, and manageable class sizes.
It also includes senior exemptions.
The ballot question says the tax would provide $1 million a year in locally controlled funding.
Supporters say Measure Q helps pay competitive teacher salaries and prevent further deep cuts, and that great schools protect property values.
No argument against Measure Q was filed, so the list of local measures from the county shows only the argument for it.
A yes vote levies the parcel tax of $148 a parcel for eight years.
A no vote does not levy the tax.
