# Measure Q — Cambrian School District — Parcel tax, $148 per parcel for 8 years

- **Letter:** Q, confirmed by the district's page "Measure Q - 2026 Parcel Tax".
- **Jurisdiction:** Cambrian School District.
- **Kind:** parcel tax (qualified special tax), placed by the Board of Trustees.
- **Who votes:** district page: "All registered voters within CSD boundaries are eligible to vote on Measure Q on the November 3, 2026 ballot."
- **Threshold:** district page: "Measure Q requires two-thirds (66.7%) voter approval to pass."
- **Status:** the district page says "On June 18, 2026, the Board voted to place Measure Q, a local school parcel" tax on the ballot. The board resolution was not found (board agendas are on cambrianpublic.ic-board.com; not tried in depth).

## Ballot question (district page, under "Measure Q - Ballot Question")

> To protect the quality of education in our local elementary and middle schools, provide funding for core academic programs in math, science, engineering, technology, reading, writing and arts, attract/retain highly qualified teachers, and maintain manageable class sizes, shall Cambrian School District's measure be adopted, levying $148/parcel for 8 years, its continuance subject to voter approval, with senior exemptions, annual inflation adjustments, providing $1,000,000 annually in locally controlled funding that can't be taken by the State?

From the district's web page, not from the resolution or the printed ballot.
