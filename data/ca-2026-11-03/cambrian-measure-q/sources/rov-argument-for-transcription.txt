[Original page 14: the argument]

Cambrian School District

ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE ___

VOTE YES on _ to protect the outstanding quality of education that children attending our local TK-8 schools receive from the Cambrian School District (CSD). When our schools are strong, Cambrian is strong.

Our Neighborhood schools – For over 160 years, Cambrian schools have been the bedrock of our community – consistently rank among the top 10% in the state. However, they are at risk. It's not an expense problem; it's a revenue problem. The state's faulty funding formula under funds the Cambrian School District. In fact, we're among the lowest funded in the state.

The result: over the last 2 years, CSD schools have suffered millions in cuts and our teacher salaries have fallen behind neighboring districts.

Cambrian School District has already made difficult decisions; including consolidating from six schools to five, saving $1 million annually which goes back to support teaching and classroom instruction. Measure _ helps provide competitive teacher salaries, prevent further deep cuts to vital instructional programs, library services, academic and mental health services, and avoid future teacher layoffs.

Measure _ will:
• Attract and Retain highly qualified teachers
• Continue high-quality programs in math, science, engineering, and technology
• Continue high-quality programs in reading, writing, language arts, music, visual and performing arts
• Maintain manageable class sizes
• Provide counseling and academic intervention for students who need additional support

Measure _ includes a SENIOR CITIZEN EXEMPTION.

REQUIRED Taxpayer protections:
• ALL funds STAY LOCAL to support Cambrian elementary/middle school students
• NO funds are allowed for administrators' salaries
• The State CANNOT take this funding away
• Independent and mandatory audits ensure funds are spent properly

Even if you don't have school-age children, supporting Measure _ is a wise investment. Great schools protect property values and our quality of life. That's why parents, teachers, business and civic leaders, and residents from every Cambrian neighborhood SUPPORT Measure _.
