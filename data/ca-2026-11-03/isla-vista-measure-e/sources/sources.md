## Quoted facts

- **Who votes:** voters of the Isla Vista Community Services District. Two-thirds needed (impartial analysis below).
- **Ballot question** (sample ballot, p. 33): "Increased Isla Vista Municipal Infrastructure and Services. To fund local improvements to housing services; community based public safety; transportation, parking, and public transit; clean and safe public spaces, including lighting and sidewalks; and community facilities and events, shall voters authorize a Utility Users' Tax of 7% on methane and 6% on trash, sewage, water, and electric, authorizing establishment of exemptions, generating approximately $1,200,000 additional annually, until repealed by voters?"
- **Impartial analysis** (p. 18, County Counsel, dated August 7, 2026): "This Measure was placed on the ballot by the Board of Directors of the Isla Vista Community Services District (District), which is the governing board of the District. If approved by two-thirds of the votes cast by voters voting on the proposition, this Measure authorizes the District to impose a new special tax in the form of a Utility User Tax (UUT) at the rate of 7% of service charges for the utility of methane, and of 6% of the service charges for the utilities of water, electricity, sewer, and garbage disposal services. The UUT shall not apply to any utility provided by a telecommunications provider. This proposed tax is in addition to the District's existing 8% utility user tax that was approved by the voters in 2018."
