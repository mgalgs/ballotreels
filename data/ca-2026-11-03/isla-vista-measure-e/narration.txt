Measure E asks the voters of this community services district whether to add a tax on gas, water, electric, sewer and trash bills.
Voters approved the district's current 8 percent utility users' tax in 2018.
Measure E needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
Measure E would add 7 percent on gas, and 6 percent on water, electric, sewer and trash, on top of the current tax.
The money could fund housing services, public safety, transit, public spaces, and community events, until voters repeal the tax.
The ballot question estimates the tax would raise about $1.2 million a year.
The official voter guide for this election says no argument against Measure E was filed.
Supporters say Measure E funds buses, street repairs, public safety and cleanup, all controlled locally.
A yes vote authorizes the new tax.
A no vote rejects the new tax, and the current tax stays.
