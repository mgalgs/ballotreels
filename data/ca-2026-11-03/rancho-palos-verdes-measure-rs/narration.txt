Measure RS asks the voters of this city whether to raise the hotel and motel guest tax from 10 to 13 percent.
Today, guests staying 30 days or less at a hotel or motel pay this tax, at 10 percent of the rent.
The City Council placed Measure RS on the ballot by a unanimous vote.
Measure RS would raise the rate to 13 percent.
The money would go to the general fund, for general city services such as public safety and wildfire mitigation.
The higher rate would last until ended by voters, and needs a majority yes vote to pass.
The city attorney's impartial analysis says the increase would add $2.2 million a year to the general fund.
The official county list of measures, which names who filed each argument, says no argument against Measure RS was submitted.
Supporters say Measure RS would help prepare for wildfires and landslides, and only overnight hotel and motel visitors pay it.
A yes vote approves raising the hotel and motel guest tax from 10 to 13 percent.
A no vote disapproves of raising the hotel and motel guest tax at this time.
