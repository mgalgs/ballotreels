ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE RS

Protect Rancho Palos Verdes from natural disasters such as landslides, land movement, earthquakes and wildfires –Vote YES on RS.

Rancho Palos Verdes has already experienced how quickly a natural disaster can threaten our homes, roads, and essential services. We cannot eliminate every risk, but we can make sure our community is ready to respond, recover, and remain safe.

Over four years, RPV has to spend more than $72,000,000 in taxpayer dollars responding to the devastating impacts of landslides. Yes on RS allows our City to prepare for and respond effectively and decisively to wildfires, landslides, land movement and other natural disasters.

Measure RS is the fiscally responsible plan to protect our community WITHOUT RAISING TAXES on residents. It is only paid by overnight hotel and motel visitors, so they pay their fair share to protect our community – all while keeping RPV's hotel and resort rates lower than those of area competitors.

Many people don't know that there were more than 300 reported burglaries, robberies, and thefts --including over 70 residential burglaries – in Rancho Palos Verdes in the last year alone. Yes on RS maintains public safety patrols and 911 response times, and helps prevent property crimes, home invasions, and vehicle thefts.

Again, measure RS keeps your families, local businesses, and properties safe WITHOUT RAISING TAXES on residents.

Every dollar generated by measure RS remains under local control and by law must be used for the benefit of Rancho Palos Verdes residents. The State and Los Angeles County cannot seize these funds.

Measure RS includes strong taxpayer safeguards:

Public disclosure of all spending
Strict fiscal accountability and transparency requirements
Independent annual financial and performance audits

Join a broad coalition of public safety leaders, small business owners, a unanimous City Council, and community leaders: vote yes on RS.
