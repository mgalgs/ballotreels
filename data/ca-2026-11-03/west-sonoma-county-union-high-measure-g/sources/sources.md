## Facts and quotes

**Ballot question (p1):** "To continue supporting academic excellence for West Sonoma County Union students without increasing current tax rates; protect music, art, dance, shop, culinary and other career technical programs; retain teachers and staff; and keep class sizes low, shall West Sonoma County Union High School District's measure be adopted, renewing its existing parcel tax for eight years at the same rate of $79 per parcel, raising approximately $1,950,000 annually with exemptions including seniors and all funds staying local?"

**Vote needed:** "2/3 (Two-Thirds) Needed to Pass" (p1); "California law authorizes school districts to levy a special tax provided two-thirds of voters voting on the measure approve the tax." (p2)

**Who votes:** voters of the West Sonoma County Union High School District (tax applies "for each parcel of land within the District's boundaries", p2).

**Impartial analysis (p2), key statements:**
- "Measure G proposes to continue a special tax of $79 annually per parcel for each parcel of land within the District's boundaries. The tax would begin on July 1, 2028, and would remain in effect for eight (8) years."
- Exemptions "upon application to the District ... (i) one or more persons who are aged 65 years or older and own and occupy the parcel as a principal residence; or (ii) persons who receive Supplemental Security Income for a disability, regardless of age, and own and occupy the parcel as a principal residence."
- "If approved, Measure G would raise approximately $1,950,000 annually."
- Signed Joshua A. Myers, County Counsel, by Adam Brand, Assistant County Counsel.

**Argument in favor (p2):** signed Sean McDonagh (Chief of Police, Sebastopol Police Dept.), Dorothy Rodella (VP & Branch Manager Exchange Bank), Loretta Castleberry (President of Analy Alumni Assn.), Herman G. Hernandez (Trustee Area 5, Sonoma County Office of Education), Diane Hughes (Business Manager, West County Transportation).

**Argument against:** "No Argument Was Submitted Against Measure G" (p2).
