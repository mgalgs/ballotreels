Measure G asks the voters of this high school district whether to continue an education parcel tax of $79 a year.
A parcel tax charges the same amount on each parcel of land in the district, collected with regular property taxes.
Measure G needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The continued tax would begin in July 2028 and last eight years.
The money could pay only for the purposes the measure lists, such as retaining teachers, running academic and arts programs, updating technology and maintaining class size.
The impartial analysis estimates that Measure G would raise about $1.95 million a year.
Supporters say Measure G keeps a stable source of local funds against uncertain state funding, without raising the current rate.
No argument against Measure G was submitted for the county Registrar of Voters' printed voter guide pages on this measure.
A yes vote will authorize the district to continue the education parcel tax.
A no vote will not authorize the district to continue the tax.
