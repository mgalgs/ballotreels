Measure B asks the voters of this county whether to add a one-half percent sales tax for wildfire preparedness, watersheds and open space.
Measure B is a citizens' initiative, placed on the ballot by the Board of Supervisors on June 16, after its petition signatures were certified.
The tax would run from April 2027 to March 2045.
Half the money would go to the regional park and open space district, and half would be distributed by the Board of Supervisors.
The money would pay for wildfire preparedness, such as fuel clearing and fuel breaks, and for protecting watersheds and open space.
A committee of nine residents would review the spending each year, and its findings would go into the county auditor-controller's annual audits.
The initiative's text says the tax needs a majority of the votes cast.
The ballot question estimates the tax would raise $23 million a year.
The resolution says no fiscal impact statement by the county auditor is required.

A yes vote adopts the one-half cent sales tax for 18 years.
A no vote does not adopt the tax.
