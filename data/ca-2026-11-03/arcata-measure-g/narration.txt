Measure G asks the voters of this city whether to extend a 0.75 percent local sales tax until voters repeal it.
Voters approved the tax in 2008 for 20 years, and it expires on March 31, 2029.
If Measure G passes, the tax would continue past that date, and the total sales tax rate in the city would stay at 10.25 percent.
As a general tax, the money goes into the city general fund, for any lawful city program, service or improvement.
Measure G needs a majority of the vote to pass.
The city attorney's impartial analysis estimates that the tax provides about $2.8 million a year.
The county voter guide prints only the argument in favor of Measure G, and none against it.
Supporters say the tax paid for trail, street and sidewalk projects, and is not a new tax.
A yes vote extends the 0.75 percent sales tax until voters repeal it.
A no vote does not authorize extending the sales tax.
