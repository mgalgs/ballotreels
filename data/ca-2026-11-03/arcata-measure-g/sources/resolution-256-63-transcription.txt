RESOLUTION NO. 256-63

A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF ARCATA CALLING FOR THE PLACEMENT OF A GENERAL TAX MEASURE ON THE BALLOT FOR THE NOVEMBER 3, 2026, GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION FOR QUALIFIED VOTERS TO APPROVE AN ORDINANCE TO EXTEND THE TERMS OF A GENERAL TRANSACTIONS AND USE TAX AT THE RATE OF THREE-FOURTHS OF ONE CENT (3/4¢); AND DIRECTING THE CITY ATTORNEY TO PREPARE AN IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF THE MEASURE

[Page 3]

ARCATA SALES TAX MEASURE

Shall the measure to continue existing general government funding to maintain essential services including public safety staffing; filling potholes; maintaining and repairing City streets, sidewalks, multi-use trails, and bicycle paths; and expanding multi-modal circulation, by renewing and extending the voter-approved 3/4 cent sales tax until ended by voters, with all funds staying in Arcata, providing approximately $2.8 million annually, and requiring audited spending reports and citizens' oversight, be adopted?
YES
NO

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, the City Council hereby approves the Ordinance in substantially the form attached hereto as Exhibit "A" and approves submission thereof to the voters at the General Municipal Election on November 3, 2026.

SECTION 2. That the method of tax collection and all aspects of the application and operation of any tax levied pursuant to the adoption of the Ordinance attached hereto in Exhibit "A" shall be as provided in such Ordinance and/or as authorized in the Ordinance to be conducted and determined by the City Council.

SECTION 3. That the vote requirement for the ballot measure to pass is a majority (50% + 1) of the votes cast at the November 3, 2026, General Municipal Election.

SECTION 4. That the City Clerk is hereby authorized and directed to make any changes to the text of the proposition or this Resolution, including its exhibits, as required to conform to any requirements of law.

SECTION 5. Pursuant to Elections Code section 9280, the City Council hereby directs the City Clerk to transmit a copy of the Measure to the City Attorney. The City Attorney shall prepare an impartial analysis of the Measure, not to exceed 500 words in length, showing the effect of the Measure on existing law and the operation of the Measure, and transmit such impartial analysis to the City Clerk not later than the deadline for submittal of primary argument for or against the Measure.

SECTION 6. The City Clerk shall, not later than the 88th day prior to the General Municipal Election to be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, file with the Board of Supervisors and the Office of Elections of the County of Humboldt, a certified copy of this Resolution.
