## Facts with quotes

- **Ballot question (Resolution 256-63 §1):** "ARCATA SALES TAX MEASURE — Shall the measure to continue existing general government funding to maintain essential services including public safety staffing; filling potholes; maintaining and repairing City streets, sidewalks, multi-use trails, and bicycle paths; and expanding multi-modal circulation, by renewing and extending the voter-approved 3/4 cent sales tax until ended by voters, with all funds staying in Arcata, providing approximately $2.8 million annually, and requiring audited spending reports and citizens' oversight, be adopted?"
- **Vote needed:** Resolution §3: "the vote requirement for the ballot measure to pass is a majority (50% + 1) of the votes cast". Analysis: "If approved by a majority of City voters (50% + 1), this measure would extend the existing three-fourths of one cent (3/4¢) transactions and use tax within the City until rescinded by voters."
- **Who placed it:** "On June 17, 2026, the City Council of the City of Arcata ("City"), acting on its own motion, not by voter petition, voted to place Measure G on the ballot" (guide p. 19).
- **History / expiry:** "On November 4, 2008, City voters approved a three-fourths of one cent (3/4¢) transactions and use tax for a twenty (20) year term." / "The three-fourths of one cent (3/4¢) transactions and use tax expires on March 31, 2029."
- **Rate effect:** "The City's current sales tax rate is 10.250%. If the measure passes, the sales tax rate would remain 10.250%"
- **Revenue:** "It is estimated that extending the three-fourths of one cent (3/4¢) transactions and use tax will provide 2.8 million dollars ($2,800,000) in annual local funding."
- **General tax:** "This sales tax is a "general tax", meaning that revenues raised from the tax go into the City's general fund to pay for any lawful City program, service, or improvement."
- **Oversight:** "The measure would require the existing Transactions and Use Tax Oversight Committee to conduct an annual review of all expenditure of revenues generated by the sales tax."
- **Yes/No:** "A "yes" vote on Measure G will authorize extending the three-fourths of one cent (3/4¢) sales tax until rescinded by voters. A "no" vote on Measure G will not authorize extending the three-fourths of one cent (3/4¢) sales tax until rescinded by voters."
- **Argument in favor signers (guide p. 20):** Wendy Starr; Jeff Nagan; Charles Powell; Stephanie Lane; Colin Fiske. Its figures ("$39 million generated", "an additional $35 million in grants") are the proponents' claims.
