## Facts and quotes

**Ballot question** (Solano Notice of Measures, 8/5/2026, https://content.solanocounty.gov/sites/default/files/2026-08/Notice_of_Measures.pdf): "To improve the quality of education; repair or replace leaky roofs; modernize/renovate outdated classrooms, restrooms and school facilities; and replace deteriorating plumbing and sewer systems; shall Winters Joint Unified School District's measure authorizing $29,900,000 in bonds, at legal interest rates, and levying approximately $49 per $100,000 of assessed valuation (raising $1,600,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding, be adopted, with a Board-appointed citizens' oversight committee and annual independent audits to assure proper expenditure of funds?"

**Impartial analysis** ("Winters Joint Unified School District School Bonds — Measure B, Impartial Analysis by Yolo County Counsel", dated July 13, 2026, Philip J. Pogledich, County Counsel; Cedric Hopkins, Senior Deputy), key statements:
- **Who votes:** "This measure was placed on the ballot by the Board of Trustees of the Winters Joint Unified School District, which lies within Yolo and Solano Counties. If approved, it would authorize the District to issue and sell general obligation bonds in an amount not to exceed $29,900,000."
- "By law, bond proceeds may not be used for teacher or administrator salaries, pensions, or other school operating expenses."
- "The District's Tax Rate Statement estimates the average annual tax rate required to fund the bonds at $46.00 per $100,000 of assessed valuation, and the highest tax rate at $49.00 per $100,000 of assessed valuation, first occurring in fiscal year 2027–28. The final fiscal year the tax is anticipated to be collected is 2061–62. The best estimate of the total debt service (principal and interest) if all bonds are issued and sold is $58,600,000."
- **Vote needed:** "This measure requires approval by 55% of the voters voting on it."
