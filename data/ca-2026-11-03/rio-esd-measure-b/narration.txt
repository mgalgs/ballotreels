Measure B asks the voters of this school district whether to approve $78 million in bonds for school repairs, safety and new buildings.
The board of trustees ordered this election on June 24, 2026, and the county added it to the November 3 ballot.
Measure B needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would modernize classrooms and restrooms, replace leaky roofs, and build libraries and classrooms.
The measure's text says every school would get some money, with priority for older schools that have not had major improvements.
The bond money could not pay teacher or administrator salaries, or other school operating costs.
A citizens' oversight committee and yearly independent audits would check how the money is spent.
The tax rate statement of the district estimates an average tax of $28.21 per $100,000 of assessed value, and at most $30, through 2060-2061.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, from a tax on property in the district, at $158,188,000.

A yes vote authorizes the district to issue up to $78 million in bonds.
A no vote does not authorize the bonds.
