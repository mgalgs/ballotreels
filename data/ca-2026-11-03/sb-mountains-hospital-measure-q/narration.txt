San Bernardino Mountains Community Hospital District Measure Q asks the district's voters whether to approve $61 million in hospital bonds.
The district would borrow the money and repay it from a tax on property in the district.
Measure Q needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The money would go to listed projects, such as a new emergency department and acute care wing, and earthquake safety upgrades at the hospital.
The district's tax statement estimates an average tax of $23.37 per $100,000 of assessed value, and at most $25, through 2055-2056.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $106 million.
No argument against Measure Q was filed for the San Bernardino County voter guide.
Supporters say Measure Q helps meet state earthquake rules and keep emergency services open.
A yes vote means the district may issue the bonds.
A no vote means the district may not issue the bonds.
