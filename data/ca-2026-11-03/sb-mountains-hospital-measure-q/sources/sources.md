## Quoted facts

- **Who votes:** voters of the San Bernardino Mountains Community Hospital District.
- **Ballot question** (RES_SBMCHD.pdf, Exhibit A): "To repair and upgrade 75-year-old Mountains Community Hospital including: fixing deteriorating roofs, plumbing, electrical, and safety systems; upgrading/expanding emergency department, medical technology, and treatments; and maintaining local access to life-saving emergency medical care for accidents, heart attacks, strokes, and other emergencies, shall San Bernardino Mountains Community Hospital District's measure be adopted authorizing $61,000,000 in bonds at legal rates, levying $25 per $100,000 assessed value ($3,500,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding, with independent oversight and all money locally-controlled?"
- **Impartial analysis** (VIG p. 250, County Counsel): "This Measure was placed on the ballot by a resolution adopted by the Board of Directors of the San Bernardino Mountains Community Hospital District (District). A "yes" vote by at least two-thirds of the voters voting on this Measure will authorize the District to issue bonds in the amount of up to $61,000,000 to be used solely for the purposes set forth in this ballot Measure."
- **Tax rate statement** (VIG p. 251): "An election will be held in the San Bernardino Mountains Community Hospital District (the "District") on November 3, 2026, to authorize the sale of up to $61,000,000 in bonds of the District for the specific hospital-related facilities projects listed in the Bond Project List"
