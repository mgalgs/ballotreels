San Mateo County Community College District Measure V asks the district's voters whether to approve $848 million in college bonds.
The district would borrow the money and repay it from a tax on property in the district.
Measure V needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would go to listed projects at the three colleges, such as pipes, roofs, safety systems and computer labs.
The district's tax statement estimates an average tax of $10.50 per $100,000 of assessed value, and at most $18, through 2048-2049.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $1.35 billion.
Supporters say Measure V repairs campus buildings over 50 years old and keeps college affordable, all without raising taxes.
Opponents say this is the district's fifth bond request since 2001, and a new bond means decades more taxes.
A yes vote would let the district issue the bonds.
A no vote would prevent the district from issuing the bonds.
