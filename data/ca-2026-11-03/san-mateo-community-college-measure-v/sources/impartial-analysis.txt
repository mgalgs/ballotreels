IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE [TBD]

The California Constitution and Education Code authorize the San Mateo County Community College District (District) to issue bonds for specified purposes if approved at an election by 55% of those voting on issuance of the bonds. [29]

The District Board of Trustees proposes this Measure, which would authorize the District to issue bonds in a principal amount not to exceed $848 million. The bonds will have an interest rate not exceeding the legal maximum and will be repaid within the time permitted by law. The Tax Rate Statement printed in this pamphlet contains the District's best estimates of tax rates required to service the bond debt during the life of the bonds, which is anticipated to last until June 2049. The District's best estimate of the highest annual tax rate levy to fund this bond is $18 per $100,000 ($0.018 per $100) of assessed valuation. The District estimates that the total amount repayable during the life of the bond, including principal and interest, will be approximately $1.35 billion. [127]

The California Constitution requires the listing of specific projects to be funded from the bond revenue and certification that the District Board of Trustees has evaluated safety, class size reduction, enrollment growth, and information technology needs in the development of that list. [39]

The Bond Project List can be found in the full text of the Measure, and includes without limitation:

- Upgrade and improve infrastructure, including physical plants, HVAC equipment, piping, and electrical systems;
- Repair or replace pipes, plumbing, sewer and gas lines, storm drains, heating, cooling, ventilation, electrical, and other utility systems;
- Replace, repair, or upgrade aging roofs, flooring, windows, paint, and related building systems;
- Install, replace, update, or improve safety and security systems;
- Replace or upgrade information technology, communications systems, and related equipment;
- Construct, repair, or improve roadways, walkways, parking lots, and related site improvements;
- Replace or upgrade computer labs and classroom technology, equipment, and other student support systems and instructional technology.

[109]

The Measure authorizes modernization, renovation, expansion, acquisition, construction/reconstruction, rehabilitation, and/or replacement of facilities Districtwide, including future District sites. The Bond Project List does not imply a specific prioritization among the projects, and should be reviewed for further details. [38]

[Page 2]

No proceeds from the bonds shall be used for teacher or administrator salaries or other school operating expenses, except to reimburse the District for the cost of staff time spent working on the bond projects. [35]

State law requires that the District take certain steps to account for the proceeds from the bonds. Accordingly, the District will appoint an independent citizens' oversight committee, establish a special account for deposit of bond proceeds, and conduct annual independent performance and financial audits to ensure that bond funds are spent only for the purposes listed in the Bond Project List and for no other purposes. [64]

A "yes" vote on this Measure would authorize the District to issue bonds in a principal amount not to exceed $848 million for the purposes listed in the Bond Project List. [29]

A "no" vote would prevent the District from issuing the bonds. [11]

This Measure passes if at least 55% of those voting on the Measure vote "yes." [15]
