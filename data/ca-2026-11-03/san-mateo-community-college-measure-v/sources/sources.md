# Measure V — San Mateo County Community College District (general obligation bond, $848,000,000)

- **Jurisdiction:** San Mateo County Community College District (College of San Mateo, Cañada College, Skyline College).
- **Kind:** General obligation bond, $848,000,000 (Article XIIIA § 1(b)(3), Proposition 39 bond).
- **Who votes:** the voters of the District. Resolution Section 2 (p. 2): "The Board hereby orders an election and submits to the electors of the District the question of whether general obligation bonds of the District shall be issued and sold in a maximum principal amount of $848,000,000". Section 3 (p. 2): "such bond election shall be held solely within the boundaries of the District."
- **Vote threshold:** 55%. Recital (p. 1): the District may issue bonds "upon a two-thirds vote of the Board ... by a 55% vote of the electorate". Impartial analysis (p. 2): "This Measure passes if at least 55% of those voting on the Measure vote “yes.”"
- **Placed by:** the Board of Trustees, Resolution No. 26-25.
- **Election:** November 3, 2026.

## Ballot question

> Without increasing current tax rates and to protect affordable higher education at College of San Mateo, Cañada College, and Skyline College by repairing/constructing science, engineering, math, technology, skilled trades, and career-training classrooms that prepare students for in-demand careers; and repairing roofs, plumbing, electrical, shall San Mateo County Community College District's measure authorizing $848,000,000 in bonds at legal rates be adopted, levying approximately $18 per $100,000 assessed value ($75,000,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding, with independent audits, oversight, and all money locally controlled?

(resolution-full-text.pdf p. 14, Appendix B "Abbreviated Form of Bond Measure"; transcribed from the scan. The Full Text of the Measure, Appendix A, begins p. 7.)

## Full text (resolution)

- Resolution No. 26-25, Board of Trustees of the San Mateo County Community College District, "ORDERING AN ELECTION TO AUTHORIZE THE ISSUANCE OF GENERAL OBLIGATION BONDS" (p. 1). Filed with the Chief Elections Officer AUG 03 2026 (stamp, p. 1).
- Adopted "by the following vote, on July 29, 2026" (p. 6). AYES (handwritten, p. 6): Wayne Lee, Richard Holober, Lisa Petrides, John Pimentel, Michael Guingona. NOES, ABSENT, ABSTAIN: blank.
- Recital (p. 1): the Board "has obtained reasonable and informed projections of assessed property valuations ... and does not expect a new bond measure to increase current tax rates in the District".
- Section 6 (p. 2): proceeds "will be used only for the purposes specified in Section 1(b)(3) of Article XIII A ... and not for any other purpose, including teacher and administrator salaries and other operating expenses."
- Section 7 (p. 3): if 55% approve, the Board shall "conduct an annual, independent performance audit", "conduct an annual, independent financial audit", and "establish and appoint members to an independent citizens' oversight committee".
- Section 8 (p. 3), statement required on the sample ballot: "Approval of this measure does not guarantee that the proposed project or projects that are the subject of bonds under this measure will be funded beyond the local revenues generated by this measure."
- The project list is in Appendix A (pp. 7-13, not transcribed here).

## Impartial analysis

Heading as printed: "IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE [TBD]" (p. 1). **No author name or office is printed** on either page. (In San Mateo County, analyses of district measures are normally by County Counsel, but this file does not say so.)

- p. 1: "The District Board of Trustees proposes this Measure, which would authorize the District to issue bonds in a principal amount not to exceed $848 million."
- p. 1: "The District's best estimate of the highest annual tax rate levy to fund this bond is $18 per $100,000 ($0.018 per $100) of assessed valuation. The District estimates that the total amount repayable during the life of the bond, including principal and interest, will be approximately $1.35 billion."
- p. 1: the bond debt "is anticipated to last until June 2049."
- p. 2: "No proceeds from the bonds shall be used for teacher or administrator salaries or other school operating expenses, except to reimburse the District for the cost of staff time spent working on the bond projects."
- p. 2: "A “yes” vote on this Measure would authorize the District to issue bonds in a principal amount not to exceed $848 million for the purposes listed in the Bond Project List." / "A “no” vote would prevent the District from issuing the bonds."

## Tax rate statement / fiscal

Appendix C, "TAX RATE STATEMENT", resolution-full-text.pdf pp. 15-16 (C-1, C-2), signed by the Chancellor (name redacted):
- "The best estimate of the average annual tax rate that would be required to fund this bond issue over the entire duration of the bond debt service ... is $10.50 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. It is currently expected that the tax will be collected until fiscal year 2048-49." (p. 15)
- "The best estimate of the highest tax rate that would be required to fund this bond issue ... is $18.00 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. This rate is projected to apply beginning in fiscal year 2045-46." (p. 15)
- "The best estimate of total debt service, including principal and interest, that would be required to be repaid if all the bonds are issued and sold will be approximately $1,350,000,000." (p. 15)
- "Due to the scheduled amortization of outstanding bonds that will occur prior to the time the first levy is expected pursuant to this measure, the District expects that such levy will not increase the 2025-26 tax rate of $18 per $100,000 levied." (p. 15, underlined in original)

## Notes

- The ballot question's "approximately $18 per $100,000" matches the statement's *highest* rate; the *average* rate is $10.50 per $100,000.
- The impartial analysis prints no author and still says "MEASURE [TBD]".
- Signer order and titles are as on the filed forms; the printed pamphlet (per-voter, not checked) may differ.
