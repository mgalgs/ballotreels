OFFICE OF THE CHIEF ELECTIONS OFFICER
SAN MATEO COUNTY
AUG 10 2026

Argument Against Measure ___

Measure __ is San Mateo County Community College District's fifth request for more bond debt since 2001.

Taxpayers have already given them more than $1,100,000,000; now the District wants another $848,000,000!

Where did all that money go?

BROKEN PROMISES!

Voters were promised past bond funds would modernize classrooms, repair aging facilities, and prepare students for high-demand jobs.

Yet bond money funded costly projects never identified on the ballot, including penthouse-style administrative offices at CSM ($87M), private event rental facilities at Skyline ($40M), extravagant membership health clubs at CSM ($58M) & Cañada ($117M), and faculty housing debt ($46M).

BALLOT MISINFORMATION!

The current ballot language hoodwinks voters again!

Voters must read the fine print to discover the REAL plans:

-Yet another health and wellness club at Skyline

-Two very costly Fine Arts Centers at Cañada & Skyline

-Likely demolition of CSM's Library and Gym (two of the campus's remaining historic core buildings)

Are these the "science, engineering, math, technology, skilled trades, and career-training classrooms that prepare students for in-demand careers" promised on the ballot?

[Page 8]

THEY HAVE ENOUGH MONEY!

Why costly new construction when enrollment is not increasing and online learning is growing?

Move over, military-industrial complex.

The construction unions-consultants complex has infiltrated school districts statewide.

These special interests profit from demolition and costly new construction rather than repair and modernization of existing facilities.

MAKE NO MISTAKE: THIS IS A TAX INCREASE!

The District claims your tax rate won't increase. That's misleading.

A new bond means new debt and decades of additional taxes.

Think of your mortgage or car loan. If you added 30 years of payments but kept the monthly payment the same, would you say it cost you nothing?

Of course not.

More years of taxes means more taxes paid. Period.

Vote NO on Measure __!
