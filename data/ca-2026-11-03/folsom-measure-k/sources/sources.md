## Key facts, quoted

- **Ballot question:** "Shall the measure increasing the campaign contribution limit from $150 to $500 per donor per election on campaigns for Folsom City Council be adopted?"
- **Analysis:** "City Attorney's Impartial Analysis of Measure K", signed "s/Sari Myers Dierking".
- **History:** "In 1994, Folsom voters passed an initiative measure establishing a $150 per person per election limit on donations to candidates for City Council. This rule has not been updated since 1994."
- **Placed by / threshold / effect:** "On July 14, 2026, the City Council voted to place Measure K on the November ballot. If passed by a majority of Folsom voters (50% + 1), Measure K would amend the Folsom Municipal Code to increase the campaign contribution limit applicable to candidates for Folsom City Council from $150 per person per election to $500 per person per election."
- **Scope:** "Measure K does not apply outside the City of Folsom, and it does not apply to any City election other than an election for Folsom City Council. For example, Measure K does not apply to ballot measures."
- **Yes/No:** "A "yes" vote on Measure K will authorize an increase …" / "A "no" vote … The $150 per person per election campaign contribution limit will remain in place."
