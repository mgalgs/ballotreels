City of Folsom Measure K asks voters in the city whether to raise the limit on donations to City Council candidates from $150 to $500.
In 1994, Folsom voters set a limit of $150 per person, per election, on donations to council candidates.
The limit has not been updated since 1994.
The City Council placed Measure K on the ballot, and it needs a simple majority.
Measure K would raise the limit to $500 per person, per election.
Measure K would apply only to Folsom City Council elections, not to ballot measures.
The Sacramento County Current Measure Information page does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
No argument against Measure K was filed with Sacramento County Voter Registration and Elections.
Supporters say raising the limit lets residents support candidates directly, leveling the playing field.
A yes vote authorizes raising the limit from $150 to $500 per person, per election.
A no vote keeps the $150 limit in place.
