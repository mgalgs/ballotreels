City of Berkeley Measure Y asks voters in the city whether to add a parcel tax for arts grants.
Voters signed a petition to put Measure Y on the ballot, and it needs a majority vote to pass.
Property owners would pay a yearly tax on each square foot of buildings, from 2027, for 12 years.
Three-quarters of the money would go to performing arts groups, and the rest to artists, building projects, festivals and running the program.
The City Attorney's impartial analysis says the city estimates the tax would raise $6.3 million in its first year, and $84.8 million in all.
Supporters say Measure Y preserves the nonprofit theater, music, dance and arts venues that keep Berkeley an arts destination.
Opponents say arts groups that weathered the pandemic are now financially stable, and the tax rewards failing business strategies.
A yes vote means the parcel tax for arts grants would be created.
A no vote means the parcel tax would not be created.
