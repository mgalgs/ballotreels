Galt Joint Union High School District Measure G asks the district's voters whether to approve $43 million in high school bonds.
The district would borrow the money and repay it from a tax on property in the district.
Measure G needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would upgrade classrooms to train students in trades like welding, construction and plumbing, repair leaky roofs, and remove asbestos and mold.
The district's tax statement estimates an average tax of $29.62 per $100,000 of assessed value, and at most $30, through 2055-2056.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $94 million.
No argument against Measure G was filed with Sacramento County Voter Registration and Elections.
Supporters say Measure G expands trades training with local funds the state cannot take.
A yes vote allows the district to issue up to $43 million in bonds.
A no vote does not allow the district to issue the proposed bonds.
