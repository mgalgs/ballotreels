Santee School District Measure BB asks the district's voters whether to approve $30 million in school bonds.
The district would borrow the money and repay it from a tax on property in the district.
Measure BB needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would repair leaky roofs, plumbing and sewer systems, update heating and air conditioning, and build classrooms.
The district's tax statement estimates an average tax of $12.62 per $100,000 of assessed value, and at most $19.14, through 2049-2050.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $49.7 million.
No argument against Measure BB was filed for the San Diego County voter guide.
Supporters say Measure BB funds essential repairs under local control without raising tax rates.
A yes vote is in favor of letting the district sell the bonds.
A no vote is against letting the district sell the bonds.
