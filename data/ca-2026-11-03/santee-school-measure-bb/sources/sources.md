## Facts and the passages that establish them

### Ballot question

S1 PDF p. 86:

> MEASURE BB
> With funds that cannot be taken by the State and spent elsewhere, and no increase in tax rates, shall Santee Elementary School District's measure:
> • repairing leaky roofs, plumbing, sewer systems
> • updating inefficient heating, ventilation, air-conditioning systems
> • building multipurpose instruction classrooms
> • repairing aging schools, classrooms
> be adopted authorizing $30 million of bonds with legal rates, average levies below $13 per $100,000 of assessed valuation ($2.2 million annually) while outstanding, audits, independent oversight, and full public disclosure of all spending?
> Bonds – Yes / Bonds – No

Name as printed: the question says "Santee Elementary School District"; the guide heading, the impartial analysis, the tax rate statement and the resolution all say "Santee School District".

### Vote required

S1 PDF p. 86: "This measure requires 55% of those voting on the measure."

### Who votes on it

S3, Resolution 2526-24, Section 1 (PDF p. 4):

> hereby requests the San Diego County Registrar of Voters to conduct an election under the provisions of Proposition 39 and the Act and submit to the electors of the District the question of whether bonds of the District in the aggregate principal amount of $30,000,000 (the "Bonds") shall be issued and sold

Section 3: "the purpose of the election shall be for the voters in the District to vote on a proposition". S1 PDF p. 86 (Tax Rate Statement): "An election will be held in the Santee School District (the "District") on November 3, 2026".

In short: the electors (voters) of the Santee School District.

### Who placed it on the ballot

S1 PDF p. 86: "The Board of Education for the Santee School District ("District") placed this measure on the ballot."

S3 PDF p. 7 (clerk's certificate): Resolution No. 2526-24 "was duly adopted by the Board of Education of the Santee School District at the meeting thereof held on the 16th day of June, 2026", AYES 4, NOES 1, ABSENT 0, ABSTENTIONS 0 (handwritten); signed Dr. Tim Glover, Clerk.

### Impartial analysis

S1 PDF p. 86, "COUNTY COUNSEL IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS". Key statements:

> If approved by 55% of the votes cast, this measure will authorize the District to issue and sell general obligation bonds in an amount not to exceed $30,000,000.

> The District estimates the average tax rate would be $12.62 for every $100,000 of assessed valuation, and the highest tax rate would be approximately $19.14 for every $100,000 of assessed valuation. The tax is anticipated to be collected through fiscal year 2049-2050.

> The estimated total debt service, including principal and interest, required to be repaid if the bonds are issued and sold is approximately $49,700,000.

### Tax rate statement

S1 PDF p. 86, "SANTEE SCHOOL DISTRICT GENERAL OBLIGATION BONDS TAX RATE STATEMENT", "By: /s/ Dr. Kristin Baranski, Superintendent":

> 1. The best estimate of the average annual tax rate ... is $12.62 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. The final fiscal year in which the tax is anticipated to be collected is 2049-50.
> 2. The best estimate of the highest tax rate ... is $19.14 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. It is estimated that such rate would be levied starting in fiscal year 2030-31.
> 3. The best estimate of total debt service ... will be approximately $49,700,000.

Note: the question promises "average levies below $13" and "no increase in tax rates"; the statement's highest rate is $19.14. Both as printed; the record does not reconcile them further.

### Arguments and rebuttals

- **Argument in favor**, S1 PDF p. 87. First line: "Santee's elementary schools are among our community's most valuable assets." Signers: Barbara Ryan (Santee School District Board Member); Linda Vail (Former Independent Oversight Committee Chair); Michelle Corderman (District Teacher of the Year); Candace Schmitthenner (Santee Parent/Volunteer); Mark Kersey (President and CEO of San Diego County Taxpayers Association).
- **Argument against**: none. S1 PDF p. 87: "NO ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE BB WAS FILED WITH THE OFFICE OF THE REGISTRAR OF VOTERS".
- **Rebuttals**: none printed.

S1 PDF pp. 87-89, "FULL TEXT OF MEASURE BB". It opens: "The following is the full proposition presented to the voters by the Santee School District (the "District")." It ends (p. 89) with the State Matching Funds statement: "Approval of Measure BB does not guarantee that the proposed project or projects in the Santee School District that are the subject of bonds under Measure BB will be funded beyond the local revenues generated by Measure BB."
