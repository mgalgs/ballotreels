## Facts, with quotes

- **Question:** "Shall the City Charter be amended to increase the minimum budget allocated to the Department of Recreation and Parks, phased in over ten years until it is double the current minimum budget allocation, with exceptions for fiscal emergencies?"
- **Who votes / threshold:** City of Los Angeles voters; RR/CC list p. 12 "Majority Vote 50%+1". Impartial Summary: "This measure will become effective if approved by a majority of voters."
- **Impartial Summary:** "The percentage would increase from 0.0325% to 0.065% of the assessed value of all property as assessed for City taxes, phased in over ten years. This measure would not create a new tax or generate additional City revenue." "Consequently, the amount of General Fund revenue available for other City services will be reduced."
- **Financial Impact:** "Assuming average property value growth, RAP's funding increases from $303 million to $995 million by 2036-37" "Because this measure does not raise new revenues, the City must reallocate resources from other services, including public safety, public works, and homeless services to meet the increased funding mandate."
