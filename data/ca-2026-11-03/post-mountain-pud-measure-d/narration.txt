Measure D asks the voters of this utility district whether to approve a $100 yearly tax per parcel for road maintenance.
The district's ordinance says the district is responsible for about 27 miles of local roads.
It says the commission that oversees local districts began steps to dissolve it in 2024, then gave it through 2026 to find steady road funding.
Measure D needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The money could pay only for road work, such as grading, snow removal, culverts, erosion control and emergency repairs.
The tax would last until voters repeal it or the district stops levying it.
The impartial analysis says the district expects the tax to raise about $100,000 a year.
No argument for Measure D was submitted, the county voter guide says.
No argument against Measure D was submitted, the county voter guide says.

A no vote is against authorizing the special tax.
