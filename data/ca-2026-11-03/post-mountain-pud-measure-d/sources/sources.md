## Facts with quotes

- **Ballot question (guide p. 18):** "To maintain local road maintenance authority and provide dedicated funding for road maintenance and related services, shall a measure imposing a $100 annual special tax per parcel, raising approximately $100,000 annually until repealed by voters with all funds spent locally, be adopted pursuant to Post Mountain PUD Ordinance No. 2026-01?" — "This measure is subject to approval by two-thirds of the votes voting on the measure to pass."
- **What it does (p. 19):** "If approved, D would impose an annual special tax of one hundred ($100.00) dollars on each lot or parcel within the District, as identified by an Assessor's Parcel Number assigned by the Trinity County Assessor."
- **Uses:** "Road grading and surface maintenance; Snow removal; Culvert maintenance and drainage improvements; Erosion control and sediment reduction; Emergency road repair; and Equipment necessary to perform road maintenance services."
- **Revenue:** "The District estimates that the special tax would raise approximately one hundred thousand ($100,000.00) dollars annually."
- **Duration:** "The tax would continue until repealed by the voters, until the District no longer provides the services and the lien is canceled in accordance with law or until the District Board ceases to levy the tax."
- **Yes/No:** "A "yes" vote is a vote in favor of authorizing the one hundred ($100.00) dollar annual special tax per parcel for road maintenance and related services within the District. A "no" vote is a vote against authorizing the special tax."
- **Context in the ordinance (p. 20, district's recitals):** Trinity LAFCo "adopted Resolution 2024-03, a Resolution of Intent to Initiate Dissolution of the Post Mountain Public Utility District"; on "November 4, 2025, Trinity LAFCo adopted Resolution No. 2025-07, extending the remediation period through December 31, 2026"; the district is "responsible for approximately 27 miles of local roads"; a 2024 rate study "estimated that the cost of basic annual road maintenance is approximately $180,500 per year".
- **Precincts:** guide footer on p. 19: "BT 4 - CP 5, 10".
