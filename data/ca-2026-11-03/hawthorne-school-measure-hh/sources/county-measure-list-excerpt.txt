HAWTHORNE SCHOOL DISTRICT – (1)
VOTE PASSAGE – 55% of the Votes Cast
HH     HAWTHORNE SCHOOL DISTRICT SCHOOL REPAIR/IMPROVEMENT MEASURE.
       To repair aging classrooms, ensure student health, safety and achievement and
       update technology, upgrade aging plumbing, electrical, lighting, heating/ventilation,
       safety/security systems, shall this Hawthorne School District measure be adopted to
       repair, construct, acquire classrooms, facilities, sites/equipment, and issue
       $110,000,000 in bonds, at legal rates, levy on average $30 per $100,000 assessed
       value (averaging $5,240,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding, require
       independent audits/oversight, all money for local schools? Supporters: HSD Board of
       Trustees; Cristina Chiappe; Sergio R. Mortara; Vicente Bravo; Luciano A. Aguilar; Dr.
       Eugene M. Krank Opponents: None submitted.
