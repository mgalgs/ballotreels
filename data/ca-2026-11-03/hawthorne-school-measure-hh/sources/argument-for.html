ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE HH

Vote YES for HH - repair and modernize local schools in the Hawthorne School District to give all our students the facilities and tools necessary for success.

In order for our students to thrive and succeed, they need access to high-quality learning environments that allow for engagement in rigorous and relevant learning experiences that reflect the demands and possibilities of today's world.

Specifically, Measure HH will:

- Create more inclusive and welcoming learning environments
- Enhance campus safety and security
- Modernize outdated classrooms, restrooms, and school facilities
- Provide well-maintained and efficient learning environments by repairing deteriorating plumbing and replacing outdated heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems
- Increase student access to current instructional technology and network security

Fiscal accountability is required by law:

- All funds raised by Measure HH benefit local Hawthorne School District students-not a penny can be taken by the state or other school districts
- An independent Citizens' Oversight Committee ensures funds are spent as authorized by voters
- No funds can be used for administrator salaries

A YES vote for Measure HH is a vote for the future of our students!
