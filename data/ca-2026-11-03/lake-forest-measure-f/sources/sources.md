## Quoted passages

- Question (p.1): "Shall the measure limiting city council members to two consecutive terms in office with a mandatory four-year out of office period before being eligible to serve for another two consecutive terms in office, be adopted?"
- Who placed it / threshold (p.2): "Measure F was placed on the ballot by the Lake Forest City Council." … "Measure F requires simple majority approval of the voters to pass."
- Current law (p.2): "Lake Forest Municipal Code Section 2.04.080 currently prohibits any person from serving as a Lake Forest City Council Member or Mayor for more than two four-year terms. The City’s current term limits have been in place since the passage of Measure S in 2018."
- Analysis (p.2): "After the required four-year break, a person may again serve up to two four-year terms."
- Full text (p.2): "There is no limit to the number of times a person may serve two consecutive terms with the requisite four year break in service."
- Yes/No (p.2, as printed): "A “No” vote on Measure F will not approve the Measure and will prohibit the City Council members or Mayor from serving more than two four-year terms."
