Lake Forest Measure F asks the voters of Lake Forest whether to change the term limits for City Council members.
Today, no one may serve on the City Council, or as mayor, for more than two four-year terms.
Measure F would allow two terms in a row, then require a break of at least four years.
After each break, a person could serve two more terms, with no limit on how many times.
Members serving now would get no added terms in a row, but could serve again after a four-year break.
The Orange County Voter Information Guide does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
No argument in favor of Measure F was submitted to the Orange County Voter Information Guide.
Opponents say Measure F replaces the lifetime limit voters passed in 2018, turning limits into careers.
A yes vote means no more than two four-year terms in a row without a four-year break.
A no vote keeps the limit of two four-year terms on council members and the mayor.
