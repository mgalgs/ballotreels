ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE UP

Vote YES on Measure UP – protect affordable higher education and job training without increasing current tax rates.

For more than 100 years, Pasadena City College has helped local students move UP – earning degrees, transferring to four-year universities, learning job skills, and building successful careers.

The cost of universities keeps going UP. Each year Pasadena City College provides nearly 30,000 local students an affordable path to higher education, career training, and economic opportunity. PCC trains the nurses, firefighters, and skilled workers that keep us healthy, safe, and fuel our economy.

Meanwhile, many Pasadena City College classrooms, labs, job training facilities, and essential infrastructure are 60 to 90 years old. Delaying repairs will only cost more the longer we wait.

Measure UP will:

- Repair aging roofs, plumbing, electrical systems and classrooms

- Update science labs and classrooms that help students transfer to four-year universities while saving families thousands

- Expand career training for nurses, healthcare professionals, firefighters, paramedics, construction workers, welders, plumbers, and other skilled trades on which our community depends

- Improve earthquake safety, campus security, and accessibility for people with disabilities

Measure UP protects taxpayers:

- Does NOT increase tax rates. It continues the current tax rate.

- Keeps every dollar local. No funds can be taken by the state or federal government.

- Cannot be used for administrator salaries or pensions.

- Requires an independent oversight committee, annual financial audits, public disclosure, and a legally binding project list so taxpayers know how every dollar is spent.

Measure UP is about rising student opportunities – not rising tax rates.

It helps students move UP to college, UP to better careers, and UP to brighter futures.

Measure UP reflects responsible planning: fixing aging facilities before costs rise, training the skilled workforce we need, and preserving affordable higher education without increasing current tax rates.

Please vote YES on Measure UP
