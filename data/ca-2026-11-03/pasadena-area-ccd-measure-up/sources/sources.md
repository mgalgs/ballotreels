# Pasadena Area Community College District — Measure UP

Sources gathered 2026-10-30.

county-measure-list-excerpt.txt: the district's lines of the Los Angeles County
Registrar-Recorder/County Clerk's "Measures Appearing on the Ballot", November 3, 2026,
https://content.lavote.gov/docs/rrcc/documents/final-measures-appearing-on-the-ballot-9-22-2026-v-2.pdf (text extracted from the PDF). It prints the vote needed (55%), the ballot
question, and who filed arguments ("Opponents: None submitted.").

The county's Interactive Sample Ballot, https://isb.lavote.gov/parsed/4348/contests/bmc10935.json,
prints four documents for Measure UP; each is saved as its English HTML with a header naming
the source:

- impartial-analysis.html: the impartial analysis.
- full-text.html: the measure's full text (the bond measure and its project list).
- tax-rate-statement.html: the District's tax rate statement.
- argument-for.html: the argument in favor. It ends with the county's statement that no
  argument against was submitted.

The district has no boundary in the Census school district files (it is a community
college district), so the contest is placed under Los Angeles County with a `voters` line.
