RESOLUTION NO. ____

A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF OXNARD, CALIFORNIA ORDERING AN ELECTION TO BE HELD ON NOVEMBER 3, 2026 TO CONSIDER THE ADOPTION OF A MEASURE ENTITLED “CITY OF OXNARD PUBLIC SAFETY, FINANCIAL STABILITY MEASURE (NO TAX RATE INCREASE)” AND REQUESTING THE COUNTY CLERK TO CONDUCT THE ELECTION, AND AUTHORIZING THE BOARD OF SUPERVISORS TO CANVASS THE RETURNS

WHEREAS, on November 4, 2008, the People of the City of Oxnard adopted the “City of Oxnard Vital Services Transactions (Sales) and Use Tax Ordinance”, commonly referred to as “Measure O”; and

WHEREAS, Measure O has provided funding for core community programs and services within the City; and

WHEREAS, the City of Oxnard is committed to maintaining current programs and services without increasing existing taxes; and

WHEREAS, without this measure, Oxnard would lose approximately $20 million per year for core services including public safety, 911 and fire services, senior support, homelessness programs, and clean and safe public spaces essential to the City’s long-term financial stability; and

WHEREAS, State and County funding cuts have jeopardized services that Oxnard’s growing senior population depends on for health and wellness programs and hot, nutritious meals; and

WHEREAS, continued local funding will sustain after-school, summer, and gang prevention programs that keep thousands of Oxnard youth safe, supervised, and out of trouble; and

WHEREAS, over 70% of Fire Department calls involve medical emergencies, and cuts to local funding would compromise the firefighters, paramedics, and equipment needed for 911 rapid response to save lives; and

WHEREAS, without this measure, Oxnard would lose millions of dollars which public safety programs rely on, forcing some funding cuts to police and fire departments, which may result in longer 911 response times; and

WHEREAS, this measure will not increase current tax rates, and maintains Measure O’s existing requirements that every dollar be used in Oxnard, with full public spending disclosure, independent annual audits, and citizen oversight of all spending; and

WHEREAS, the City Council desires to submit a measure entitled “City of Oxnard Public Safety, Financial Stability Measure (No Tax Rate Increase)” to voters of the City at an election to be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, and to be consolidated with any other election to be held on that date; and

ORDINANCE NO. ________

AN UNCODIFIED ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF OXNARD, CALIFORNIA ADOPTING THE “CITY OF OXNARD PUBLIC SAFETY, FINANCIAL STABILITY MEASURE (NO TAX RATE INCREASE)”, WHICH REMOVES THE SUNSET PROVISION OF MEASURE O (CITY OF OXNARD VITAL SERVICES TRANSACTIONS (SALES) AND USE TAX ORDINANCE) SO THAT MEASURE O’S EXISTING, VOTER-APPROVED SALES TAX SHALL CONTINUE UNTIL ENDED BY THE VOTERS, WITH OVERSIGHT, INDEPENDENT AUDITS, PUBLIC SPENDING DISCLOSURE, AND WITH ALL FUNDS FOR OXNARD

WHEREAS, on November 4, 2008, the People of the City of Oxnard adopted the “City of Oxnard Vital Services Transactions (Sales) and Use Tax Ordinance”, commonly referred to as “Measure O”; and

WHEREAS, Measure O has provided funding for core community programs and services within the City; and

WHEREAS, the City of Oxnard is committed to maintaining current programs and services without increasing existing taxes; and

WHEREAS, Measure O has an existing sunset date of March 31, 2029; and

WHEREAS, without this measure, Oxnard would lose approximately $20 million per year for core services including public safety, 911 and fire services, senior support, homelessness programs, and clean and safe public spaces essential to the City’s long-term financial stability; and

WHEREAS, State and County funding cuts have jeopardized services that Oxnard’s growing senior population depends on for health and wellness programs and hot, nutritious meals; and

WHEREAS, continued local funding will sustain after-school, summer, and gang prevention programs that keep thousands of Oxnard youth safe, supervised, and out of trouble; and

WHEREAS, over 70% of Fire Department calls involve medical emergencies, and cuts to local funding would compromise the firefighters, paramedics, and equipment needed for 911 rapid response to save lives; and

WHEREAS, without this measure, Oxnard would lose millions of dollars which public safety programs rely on, forcing some funding cuts to police and fire departments, which may result in longer 911 response times; and

WHEREAS, this measure will not increase current tax rates, and maintains Measure O’s existing requirements that every dollar be used in Oxnard, with full public spending disclosure, independent annual audits, and citizen oversight of all spending; and

WHEREAS, the People of the City of Oxnard wish to amend the provisions of Measure O to remove its twenty-year sunset date so that Measure O’s existing, voter-approved sales tax will continue until ended by the voters.

NOW, THEREFORE, the People of the City of Oxnard do ordain as follows:

Section 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as the “City of Oxnard Public Safety, Financial Stability Measure (No Tax Rate Increase)”. The City of Oxnard shall be referred to as “City”. This Ordinance shall be applicable in the incorporated territory of the City.

Section 2. PURPOSE. This Ordinance is adopted to amend the provisions of the “City of Oxnard Vital Services Transactions (Sales) and Use Tax Ordinance” (Ordinance No. 2779, commonly known as Measure O) to remove its sunset provisions so that Measure O’s existing, voter-approved ½¢ sales tax continues until ended by voters.

Section 3. REPEAL OF PART 4 OF ORDINANCE NO. 2779. Part 4 of Ordinance No. 2779 entitled “TWENTY-YEAR SUNSET” is hereby repealed. Except for the repeal of Part 4 of Ordinance No. 2779, all remaining provisions of Ordinance No. 2779 shall remain in full force and effect until ended by the voters.

Section 4. ENJOINING COLLECTION PROHIBITED. No injunction or writ of mandate or other legal or equitable process shall issue in any suit, action, or proceeding in any court against the State or the City, or against any officer of the State or the City, to prevent or enjoin the collection under Ordinance No. 2779 (which this Ordinance amends) or Part 1.6 of Division 2 of the Revenue and Taxation Code, of any tax or any amount of tax required to be collected pursuant to Ordinance No. 2779.

Section 5. SEVERABILITY. If any section, subsection, sentence, clause or phrase of this Ordinance is for any reason held to be invalid, such decision shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions of this Ordinance. The People of the City of Oxnard hereby declare that they would have adopted this Ordinance and each section, subsection, sentence, clause and phrase thereof, irrespective of the fact that any one or more sections, subsections, sentences, clauses or phrases be declared invalid, and intend that the invalid portions should be severed and the balance of the Ordinance enforced.
