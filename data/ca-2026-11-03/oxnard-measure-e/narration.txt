Measure E asks the voters of this city whether to remove the end date of an existing half-cent sales tax.
Voters approved this half-cent sales tax, known as Measure O, in 2008.
Under its twenty-year sunset, the tax is set to end in March 2029.
The City Council placed Measure E on the ballot in June.
Measure E would repeal the sunset, so the tax would continue at the same rate, under its existing rules, until ended by voters.
The ballot question lists uses such as public safety, 911 response, street repair and homelessness programs.
Measure E needs a majority of the vote to pass.
The ballot question says the tax would provide $20 million a year.
No argument for Measure E appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
No argument against Measure E appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
A yes vote removes the end date, so the tax continues until ended by voters.
A no vote keeps the end date, so the tax ends in March 2029.
