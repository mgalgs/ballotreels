Belmont is a city of 27,000, sharing a 75-year-old community center originally built when the population was 5,000. We have no reliable place to go when disaster strikes. Barrett Community Center's roofs leak, its systems fail, and it doesn't meet today's earthquake, fire, or accessibility standards. Every year we wait, repairs cost more and deliver less.

Measure ___ replaces Barrett with a resilient, durable community center built for the city Belmont is today. It funds a facility with backup power and real emergency capacity, something the city and current buildings lack. When wildfires, floods, earthquakes, or extended power outages strike, residents will have a place to shelter, charge devices, and get help.

More than 1,700 Belmont youth already rely on Barrett's programs every week. A modern facility adds capacity for seniors, space for physical fitness, expanded childcare and after-school enrichment programs, and reliable meeting rooms for local organizations who are underserved today.

Every dollar is protected. Revenue is earmarked for a dedicated special fund for the community center. It cannot fund unrelated city expenses, nor can it be taken by the state or federal government. Annual audits are required. Seniors 65 and older who own and live in their homes are eligible for exemption.

Measure ___'s dedicated local funding also positions Belmont to compete for state and county matching grants, so residents aren't covering costs alone.

We're Belmont neighbors, not politicians. We've done the homework, held the meetings, and heard your voices. This measure is on your ballot because Belmont residents gathered the signatures to put it here. The decision is yours.

Belmont deserves a community center that meets its needs now and in the future. Let's invest in ourselves and build it together.

Vote YES on Measure ___.
