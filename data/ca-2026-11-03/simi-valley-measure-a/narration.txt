Measure A asks the voters of this city whether to raise the tax on hotel stays from 10 percent to 12 percent.
Today, guests at hotels and other short-term lodging in the city pay a 10 percent tax on their rent.
The City Council placed Measure A on the ballot in June.
Guests would pay 12 percent of the rent instead, until voters end the tax.
It is a general tax; the ballot question lists uses such as emergency response, police, and streets.
Measure A needs a majority of the vote to pass.
The ballot question says the increase would provide about $400,000 a year.
No argument against Measure A appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
No argument for Measure A appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
A yes vote raises the tax on hotel stays to 12 percent.
A no vote keeps it at 10 percent.
