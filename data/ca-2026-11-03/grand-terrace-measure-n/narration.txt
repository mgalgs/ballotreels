City of Grand Terrace Measure N asks Grand Terrace voters whether to add a 1 percent sales tax for general city services.
Measure N needs a majority of the vote to pass.
The tax would apply to most retail sales in the city, but not to groceries or prescription medicine.
The money would go to the city general fund, for any city purpose, such as law enforcement.
An advisory committee would review the spending and the yearly audits.
The tax would stay until voters repeal it.
The city attorney's impartial analysis estimates the tax would raise about $1,200,000 a year.
No argument for Measure N was submitted to appear in the county voter guide.
No argument against Measure N was submitted to appear in the county voter guide.
A yes vote is a vote to approve the 1 percent sales tax.
A no vote is a vote against the 1 percent sales tax.
