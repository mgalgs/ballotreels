## Quoted facts

- **Ballot question and letter** (city page): "CITY MEASURE R — Preserving General Services Measure. Shall the measure, to provide funding for the City of Pacific Grove services, such as keeping streets, sidewalks, beaches, parks, playgrounds and public facilities safe, clean, and well-maintained; repairing/replacing aging infrastructure; fixing potholes and streets; and providing fire protection, paramedic, crime prevention, and 9-1-1 response by establishing a 0.375% transactions and use ("sales") tax, providing approximately $1,000,000 annually for 10 years, with independent audits, and all money locally controlled, be adopted?"
- **Who votes:** "a general election will be held in the City of Pacific Grove on the 3 day of November 2026 to submit to the electorate a ballot measure".
- **Arguments/analysis process:** "primary arguments (300 words) in favor of or against the above measure may be submitted in writing to the City Clerk's Office ... on or before 12:00 P.M. on August 13, 2026"; "The City Attorney shall prepare an impartial analysis (500 words) of the measure ... on or before 12:00 P.M. on August 13, 2026."
