November 3, 2026 General Election and Ballot Measures 

The next City of Pacific Grove Municipal Election will be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026.

Municipal elections are conducted in November of even-numbered years.

UPCOMING SEATS AVAILABLE FOR NOMINATION

 Mayor:  2-year term
One Councilmember Seat for District 1:  4-year term
One Councilmember Seat for District 3:  4-year term
One Councilmember Seat for District 5:  4-year term
One At-Large Councilmember Seats: 2-year term

BALLOT MEASURE

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that a general election will be held in the City of Pacific Grove on the 3rd day of November 2026 to submit to the electorate a ballot measure with the following ballot measure question:

CITY MEASURE R
Preserving General Services Measure. Shall the measure, to provide funding for the City of Pacific Grove services, such as keeping streets, sidewalks, beaches, parks, playgrounds and public facilities safe, clean, and well-maintained; repairing/replacing aging infrastructure; fixing potholes and streets; and providing fire protection, paramedic, crime prevention, and 9-1-1 response by establishing a 0.375% transactions and use (“sales”) tax, providing approximately $1,000,000 annually for 10 years, with independent audits, and all money locally controlled, be adopted?”
 
Yes (_____)
No  (_____)

 

NOTICE IS FURTHER GIVEN that primary arguments (300 words) in favor of or against the above measure may be submitted in writing to the City Clerk’s Office, 300 Forest Avenue, Pacific Grove, CA 93950 for printing and distribution to the voters, pursuant to provisions of the California Elections Code on or before 12:00 P.M. on August 13, 2026.  Rebuttal arguments (250 words) by authors of said primary arguments may be filed with the City Clerk’s Office in like manner by 12:00 P.M., on or before August 20, 2026. 

The City Attorney shall prepare an impartial analysis (500 words) of the measure pursuant to Elections Code Section 9280 on or before 12:00 P.M. on August 13, 2026.

Printed arguments submitted to voters shall be titled either “Argument in Favor of Measure  R” or “Argument Against Measure R,” and “Rebuttal to Argument in Favor of Measure R” or Rebuttal to Argument Against Measure R” respectively. 

All arguments concerning the above measure must be accompanied by the required form entitled “Argument Signature Statement” a copy of which may be obtained below or from the City Clerk’s Office.  This form must be signed by each author and proponent, if different, from the argument.   

No primary argument shall exceed 300 words and no rebuttal argument shall exceed 250 words in length.  One argument for and one rebuttal thereto if any, on the measure will be selected for printing and distribution to the voters.  No more than five signatures shall appear with any argument. 

The authors of the primary arguments in favor of or against such measures may prepare and submit rebuttal arguments not exceeding 250 words or may authorize in writing any other person or persons to prepare, submit, or sign the rebuttal. See form below.

NOTICE IS FURTHER GIVEN that there shall be a 10-calendar day examination period for public review of said arguments.  During this time, any registered voter of the election jurisdiction, or the elections official, may seek a writ of mandate or an injunction requiring any or all the materials to be amended or deleted.  The review period for primary arguments in favor of or against ballot measures shall commence on August 14, 2026 and shall end at on August 24, 2026.  The review period for rebuttals to primary arguments in favor of or against ballot measures shall commence at on August 21, 2026 and on August 31, 2026. 

NOTICE IS FURTHER GIVEN that voted Precinct, Vote by Mail and Provisional Ballots for the Election, held on November 3, 2026, shall be tallied at the location given below:

Monterey County Elections Office
1441 Schilling Place-North
Salinas, CA  93901


The polls will be open between the hours of 7:00 A.M. and 8:00 P.M. on November 3, 2026.   

Sandra Kandell,
City Clerk/Elections Official
City of Pacific Grove
