## Quoted facts

- **Ballot question** (resolution draft, Section V, "the 75-word ballot question shall read as follows"): "Shall Ordinance No. 2687, which repeals the Residential Rental Registration Ordinance, the Rent Stabilization Ordinance, the Tenant Protection and Just Cause Eviction Ordinance, and the Tenant Anti-Harassment Ordinance, be adopted?"
- **Threshold** (Section VI): "The vote requirement for the measure to pass is a simple majority (50% + 1) of the votes cast."
- **How it got on the ballot** (resolution recitals):
  - "on June 3, 2025, the City Council adopted an ordinance to repeal four rent-related ordinances ... ("Ordinance No. 2687"): Ordinance No. 2663 (Residential Rental Registration Program), Ordinance No. 2681 (Rent Stabilization), Ordinance No. 2682 (Tenant Protection and Just Cause Eviction), and Ordinance No. 2683 (Tenant Anti-Harassment)".
  - ""Protect Salinas Renters 2026" submitted a referendum of Ordinance No. 2687 to the City Clerk"; "On August 15, 2025, the Monterey County Registrar of Voters issued a Certificate of Signature Verification".
  - "on September 9, 2025, the City Council considered the Referendum Petition ... and decided to submit Ordinance No. 2687 to the voters of Salinas at the next general municipal election on November 3, 2026".
- **Impartial analysis (City Attorney, council draft):**
  - "The City Council of the City of Salinas has placed this measure on the November 3, 2026, ballot, as the result of a referendum on the City Council's adoption of Ordinance No. 2687".
  - Ordinance 2663 (adopted April 4, 2023) "requires that all residential rental properties in Salinas ... be registered with the City".
  - Ordinance 2681 (Sept 24, 2024) "established limits on annual rent increases for controlled rental units"; 2682 "established local criteria for just cause evictions beyond what State law currently provides ... and relocation assistance in the amount of three months' rent"; 2683 prohibits "harassment of tenants by landlords".
  - "Ordinance No. 2687 ... would repeal all four of the Rent Ordinances, thereby eliminating local rental registration requirements, local annual rent increase limits, local requirements for evictions from rental units, and local protections for tenants from harassment by landlords."
  - "A "YES" vote is to vote in favor of the measure and to repeal the four Rent Ordinances. A "NO" vote is to vote against the measure and to keep the four Rent Ordinances in effect."
