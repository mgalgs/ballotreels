Measure H asks the voters of this city whether to adopt an ordinance that repeals four city ordinances on rental housing.
In June 2025, the City Council adopted the ordinance by a vote of 5 to 2.
A referendum petition signed by more than 10 percent of the registered voters in the city was then filed against the ordinance.
The City Council then chose to submit the ordinance to the voters.
The four ordinances cover rental registration, rent stabilization, tenant protection and just cause eviction, and tenant anti-harassment.
Measure H would repeal all four ordinances in their entirety.
Measure H needs a simple majority to pass.
The ballot question and the City Council resolution do not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Any argument against Measure H is filed with the city clerk, and is not online.
Any argument for Measure H is filed with the city clerk, and is not online.
A yes vote adopts the ordinance, repealing the four rental housing ordinances.
A no vote does not adopt the ordinance that would repeal them.
