Los Alamitos Measure G asks the voters of Los Alamitos whether to raise the hotel tax from 8 percent to 12 percent.
Today, hotel guests in Los Alamitos pay an 8 percent tax on room rent, for stays of 30 days or less.
That tax brings about $600,000 a year into the general fund, which pays for general city services.
Measure G would raise the rate to 12 percent.
The ballot question says the higher rate would bring in about $300,000 more a year.
Supporters say visitors pay this tax, not residents, and the state cannot take the money away.
No argument against Measure G was submitted to appear in the Orange County Voter Information Guide.
A yes vote is a vote to increase the hotel tax rate from 8 percent to 12 percent.
A no vote is a vote to keep the current hotel tax rate at 8 percent.
