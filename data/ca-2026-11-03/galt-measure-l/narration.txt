City of Galt Measure L asks voters in the city whether the City Treasurer and City Clerk should be appointed instead of elected.
Today, both offices are elected, and each officeholder must live and be registered to vote in Galt.
No minimum training or experience is required for either elected office.
The Galt City Council put the question to voters under state law, and it needs a simple majority.
If Measure L passes, both offices would become appointed after the 2028 general election.
Appointees would serve at the pleasure of the City Council, which could set education and training requirements and review their performance each year.
Appointees would not be subject to the state residency requirements.
The Sacramento County Current Measure Information page does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Supporters say most California cities appoint both offices, letting Galt hire professionals on merit.
No argument against Measure L was filed with Sacramento County Voter Registration and Elections.
A yes vote approves making the offices of City Treasurer and City Clerk both appointive.
A no vote disapproves of making the offices appointive, and they would remain elected.
