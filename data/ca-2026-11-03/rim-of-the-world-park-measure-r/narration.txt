Rim of the World Recreation and Park District Measure R asks the district's voters whether to raise its yearly parcel tax.
Property owners in the district now pay a special tax of $22 a parcel each year, on their property tax bill.
Measure R would raise the tax to $37 a parcel from July 2027, then by 1 percent a year until voters repeal it.
The money could be used only for the district's parks, recreation programs and staff, and emergency repairs.
Measure R needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The ballot question estimates the increase would bring in about $510,000 in its first year.
No argument against Measure R was filed for the San Bernardino County voter guide.
Supporters say Measure R sustains parks and programs as insurance and repair costs rise.
A yes vote raises the tax to $37 a parcel, with the yearly 1 percent increase.
A no vote keeps the tax at $22 a parcel, as it is now.
