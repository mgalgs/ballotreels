## Quoted facts

- Who votes / vote needed: notice, "an election will be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, within the District ... This measure will become effective only if approved by 55% of those voters voting on the measure."
- Ballot question: "Student Safety and Classroom Modernization Measure. To provide updated science/ technology/ engineering/ math classrooms and multipurpose spaces; improve campus and classroom security; update/ repair student restrooms/ plumbing; and upgrade student drop-off, pick-up, parking, shade, play and athletic areas; shall Sylvan Union School District’s measure be adopted authorizing $70,000,000 in bonds at legal rates, $25 per $100,000 assessed value, providing approximately $5,045,000 annually while bonds are outstanding, with audits, independent citizen oversight and all funds locally controlled?"
- Analysis: "The Board of Trustees of the Sylvan Union Schol District (District) placed this Measure on the ballot to authorize the issuance and selling of bonds of up to $70 million in aggregate principal amount" (typo "Schol" as printed).
- Analysis: "the estimated average annual tax ... would be $25 per $100,000 ... The estimated maximum tax rate is $25.00 per $100,000 ... with a levy starting in fiscal year 2027-2028 through 2058-2059. The District estimates that the total amount repayable ... will be approximately $148,328,230."
- Tax rate statement: average "$25 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. The final fiscal year in which the tax is anticipated to be collected is 2058-59" (printed "205859"); highest "$25 per $100,000 of assessed valuation first occurring in fiscal year 2027-28"; total debt service "$148,328,230".
