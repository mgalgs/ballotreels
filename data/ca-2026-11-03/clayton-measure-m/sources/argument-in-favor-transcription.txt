ARGUMENT IN FAVOR MEASURE M

Measure M is an investment in Clayton’s quality of life designed to protect our city’s future.

Clayton will soon be facing a long-term structural deficit of $1.18 million due to rising costs of delivering essential services.

This deficit means that without action, the City will struggle to sustain our current police services, repair our streets and sidewalks, preserve parks and recreation programs, and maintain aging infrastructure.

Measure M offers a long-term solution to our deficit through a 1% sales tax increase. Projections estimate this will generate $1 million annually. This tax increase will ensure the City has the resources to continue delivering the quality services residents depend on.

Since Clayton’s founding, City leaders have always fought to avoid tax increases.

Before proposing Measure M, the City exhausted every other available means of generating new revenue. This included updating fees, reorganizing staff, and reducing service contracts. Those measures have helped, but are simply not enough to close our deficit.

The City does not propose this lightly. Measure M would be our first sales tax increase in the City’s sixty-two year history, which is why Clayton’s sales tax rate is among the lowest in the County.

Measure M is a local decision designed to meet local priorities. Should residents choose to approve Measure M, all of the resulting funds would remain accountable to residents and under the control of the City, not the County or State. That means 100% of all revenues generated stay in Clayton.

Clayton is a special community defined by safe neighborhoods, well-maintained parks, and a high quality of life.

By acting now, we can protect the services that make Clayton a desirable place to live, work, and raise a family while avoiding more difficult and expensive choices later.
