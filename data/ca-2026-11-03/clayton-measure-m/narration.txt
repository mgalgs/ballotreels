City of Clayton Measure M asks the voters of Clayton whether to add a 1 percent sales tax to pay for city services.
The City Council voted unanimously in June to place Measure M on the ballot.
The tax would add $1 for every $100 spent in Clayton, from April 2027 until voters repeal it.
Measure M is a general tax: its money can pay for any lawful program or service of the city.
The city attorney's impartial analysis estimates Measure M would raise about $1 million a year.
Supporters say that without it, the city will struggle to sustain police, streets and parks, and that all of the money stays in Clayton.
Opponents say Measure M would spike the sales tax rate to 9.75 percent, punishing working families and seniors, and is a permanent tax hike.
A yes vote authorizes the 1 percent sales tax.
A no vote does not authorize the 1 percent sales tax.
