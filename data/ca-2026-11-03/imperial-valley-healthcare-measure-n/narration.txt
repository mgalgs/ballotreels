Measure N asks the voters of this health care district whether to approve a $111 yearly tax on each parcel of property.
A 2023 state law created the district, which covers the whole county.
The district runs one of the two hospitals in the county, and agreed in 2025 to take over the other.
A 2024 state law requires the board to put a permanent funding source before voters by this election.
The tax would start in July 2027 and last until repealed.
The money could pay for emergency care, hospital operations, doctors and nurses, and earthquake safety upgrades.
Measure N needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The measure's text estimates about $8.9 million a year in revenue.
No argument against Measure N is printed in its posted pages.
No argument for Measure N is printed in its posted pages.
A yes vote authorizes the district to levy the $111 parcel tax.
A no vote does not authorize the tax.
