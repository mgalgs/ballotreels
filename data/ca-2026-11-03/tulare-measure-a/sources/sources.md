# City of Tulare — Measure A ("City of Tulare Services Measure")

Nothing archived. Checked 2026-10-28.

Everything about this measure so far comes from finders (WebSearch summaries of the city page; CalTax "Preliminary Overview of 2026 Local Tax Elections", Oct 2, 2026; news). None of it is confirmed by an official record:

- 1-cent increase to the City's existing transactions and use tax, from 0.5% to 1.5%; general tax; majority vote; about $16 million a year; Council voted June 2, 2026 to place it.
