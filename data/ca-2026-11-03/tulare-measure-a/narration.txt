Measure A asks the voters of this city whether to raise the sales tax by 1 percent to pay for general city services.
The City Council placed Measure A on the ballot in June 2026.
The tax would add 1 percent to the existing sales tax in the city.
Measure A is a general tax for city services, which the ballot question lists as including police, fire, roads and parks.
The ballot question also calls for independent audits and public disclosure of spending.
The tax would last until ended by voters, and needs a majority of the vote to pass.
The ballot question estimates that Measure A would raise about $16 million a year.
No argument for Measure A appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
No argument against Measure A appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
A yes vote on Measure A adopts the 1 percent sales tax increase.
A no vote on Measure A does not adopt the 1 percent sales tax increase.
