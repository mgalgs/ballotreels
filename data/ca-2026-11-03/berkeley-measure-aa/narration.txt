City of Berkeley Measure AA asks voters in the city whether to double its tax on sugary drinks.
Today, distributors of sugar-sweetened drinks in Berkeley pay a general tax on each fluid ounce.
Voters signed a petition to put Measure AA on the ballot, and it needs a majority vote to pass.
A special tax at twice that rate would replace the general tax, adjusted for inflation from 2028.
The money would go to a special fund for public health, nutrition and drinking water programs.
The City Attorney's impartial analysis estimates $2.2 million a year, about $1 million more than the current tax raises.
Opponents say doubling the tax makes soda drinkers pay more to support city-funded outreach programs with no accountability or proof of health outcomes.
Supporters say the tax funds school gardens, nutrition education and health programs, and the higher rate keeps funding in step with rising costs.
A yes vote replaces the general tax with the higher special tax.
A no vote keeps the current general tax.
