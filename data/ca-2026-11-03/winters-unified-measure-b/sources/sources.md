## Key facts, quoted

- **Ballot question:** "To improve the quality of education; repair or replace leaky roofs; modernize/renovate outdated classrooms, restrooms and school facilities; and replace deteriorating plumbing and sewer systems; shall Winters Joint Unified School District's measure authorizing $29,900,000 in bonds, at legal interest rates, and levying approximately $49 per $100,000 of assessed valuation (raising $1,600,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding, be adopted, with a Board-appointed citizens' oversight committee and annual independent audits to assure proper expenditure of funds?"
- **Threshold:** "To pass, it is required that 55% of the votes cast by Winters Joint Unified School District voters must approve the bond measure." Analysis: "This measure requires approval by 55% of the voters voting on it."
- **Placed by / amount:** "This measure was placed on the ballot by the Board of Trustees of the Winters Joint Unified School District, which lies within Yolo and Solano Counties. If approved, it would authorize the District to issue and sell general obligation bonds in an amount not to exceed $29,900,000."
- **Rates (analysis summarising the tax rate statement):** "The District's Tax Rate Statement estimates the average annual tax rate required to fund the bonds at $46.00 per $100,000 of assessed valuation, and the highest tax rate at $49.00 per $100,000 of assessed valuation, first occurring in fiscal year 2027–28. The final fiscal year the tax is anticipated to be collected is 2061–62. The best estimate of the total debt service (principal and interest) if all bonds are issued and sold is $58,600,000."
- **Yes/No:** "A "YES" vote is a vote to authorize the District to issue up to $29,900,000 in general obligation bonds and to levy the property tax necessary to repay them." / "A "NO" vote is a vote against authorizing the bonds and the associated property tax."
- **Analysis signed:** "Dated: July 13, 2026 — PHILIP J. POGLEDICH, COUNTY COUNSEL — By /s/ Cedric Hopkins, CEDRIC HOPKINS, SENIOR DEPUTY".
- **Tax rate statement** signed "Winters Joint Unified School District — By: /s/ Rody Boonchouy, Rody Boonchouy, Ed.D., Superintendent". Figures as above.
- **Argument in favor signers:** Keith W. Fridae, Former Mayor City of Winters; Robert Craig McNamara, President, Sierra Orchards; Cecilia M. Aguiar-Curry, Assemblymember Dist 4; Danielle Smith, Former PTA President; Sandra T. Vickrey, Local Realtor.
- **Argument against:** "No arguments were filed AGAINST Measure B by the 5:00 p.m. July 24, 2026 deadline." Rebuttals: "If only an Argument IN FAVOR OF is filed, there is no Rebuttal period."
