Argument in Favor of Measure Z

For more than 40 years, CALM Zoo has been a cherished part of Kern County, where countless children get hands-on experience with California's native wildlife for the first time. But CALM Zoo was built entirely by volunteers and now much of the infrastructure needs repair and maintenance and many of the animal exhibits are out of date and too small.

Measure Z helps CALM Zoo reach its next chapter by implementing a Master Plan to help the Zoo better care for animals and visitors: repairing and improving to animal exhibits, protecting them from extreme heat, improving wildfire/disaster preparedness, repairing roofs, electric and sewer systems, removing asbestos, mold and lead paint.

Yes on Measure Z will expand exhibits so animals have more room to roam in environments that mimic their natural habitats, improve veterinary facilities, conservation efforts, and educational opportunities, and experiences for guests.

CALM Zoo is one of few affordable educational experiences left for Kern County families. Measure Z keeps entrance fees low and continues free/discounted educational field trips for schoolchildren throughout Kern County.

By law, every dollar must remain dedicated to CALM Zoo improvements and cannot be taken or diverted to other purposes. Public spending disclosure, independent oversight and audits ensure accountability and transparency.

The cost: just one penny for every eight dollars spent.

The Zoo's animals depend on our community's continued support to receive the care they deserve. Measure Z ensures the Zoo can meet the basic needs of its animals—engaging habitats, exercise, nutritious food, heating, and cooling—while upgrading facilities for lifelong care and expanding programs to rescue and rehabilitate injured animals.
