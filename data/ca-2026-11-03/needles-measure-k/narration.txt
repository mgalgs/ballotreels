City of Needles Measure K asks Needles voters whether to raise the tax on hotel and other short stays to 14 percent.
Guests pay this tax on stays of 30 days or less at hotels, motels, RV parks and short-term rentals.
Today, the rate is 10 percent of the rent.
Measure K would raise the rate to 12 percent in 2027, 13 percent in 2029, and 14 percent in 2030.
The money could pay for any city purpose, until voters end the tax.
Measure K needs a majority of the vote to pass.
The impartial analysis says the city expects about $200,000 a year at first, and about $400,000 from 2030.
Supporters say Measure K asks visitors to contribute more toward Needles, and keeps the money for local services.
No argument against Measure K was filed, so the county voter guide prints only the argument of supporters.
A yes vote is in favor of raising the tax to 14 percent in phases.
A no vote is against the measure, keeping the tax at 10 percent.
