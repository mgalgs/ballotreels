City of San Mateo Measure H asks voters in the city whether to add a quarter-cent sales tax.
Today, the city already charges a quarter-cent sales tax, set to expire in 2048.
The City Council placed Measure H on the ballot, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
The quarter-cent sales tax the city charges would rise to a half cent, until voters repeal it.
Measure H would be a general tax, so the money could pay for any city purpose.
The City Attorney's impartial analysis estimates the tax would raise about $7 million a year.
Opponents say living costs are already too high, and the tax has no end date and leaves the budget deficit unsolved.
Supporters say Measure H keeps police, fire and street repair going, and without it, much deeper service cuts will follow.
A yes vote approves Measure H and its quarter-cent sales tax.
A no vote rejects Measure H and its quarter-cent sales tax.
