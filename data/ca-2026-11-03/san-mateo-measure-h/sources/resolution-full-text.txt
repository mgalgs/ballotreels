CITY OF SAN MATEO
RESOLUTION NO. 84 (2026)

SUBMITTING TO THE VOTERS A MEASURE TO INCREASE THE GENERAL TRANSACTIONS AND USE (SALES) TAX RATE BY AN ADDITIONAL ONE-QUARTER PERCENT (0.25%) UNTIL ENDED BY VOTERS, THEREBY INCREASING THE TOTAL TRANSACTIONS AND USE TAX RATE TO ONE HALF PERCENT (0.50%), TO BE ADMINISTERED BY THE CALIFORNIA DEPARTMENT OF TAX AND FEE ADMINISTRATION, TO BE VOTED ON AT THE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION ON TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 3, 2026

WHEREAS, the City of San Mateo is committed to maintaining the high quality of life and essential municipal services that support the health, safety, and welfare of this community; and

WHEREAS, the City recently commissioned two independent community surveys to better understand community priorities, which found that residents are highly satisfied with the overall quality of life in San Mateo and support continued investment in essential City services and infrastructure; and

WHEREAS, despite maintaining balanced budgets and implementing a Financial Sustainability Plan that includes expenditure reductions, improved cost recovery, pursuit of grant opportunities, economic development strategies, and revenue enhancements, the City continues to face a long-term structural budget deficit because the cost of providing essential services is increasing at a higher rate than revenues; and

WHEREAS, the City faces significant long-term fiscal challenges as the cost of providing essential City services continues to outpace revenue growth, compounded by the loss of state and federal funding and increasing costs to maintain and replace aging infrastructure and public facilities; and

WHEREAS, the City of San Mateo has over $350 million in unfunded capital improvements and infrastructure needs, affecting the City's ability to maintain and improve city streets, sidewalks, parks, public facilities, stormwater infrastructure, and other critical public assets; and

WHEREAS, additional locally controlled revenue will provide the City with a stable funding source to help maintain essential City services and address critical infrastructure needs while ensuring that funds generated in San Mateo remain under local control and cannot be redirected by the State; and

WHEREAS, San Mateo Municipal Code (SMMC) Chapter 3.50 currently imposes a Transactions and Use Tax of one quarter percent (0.25%) that expires on March 31, 2048; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Revenue and Taxation Code Sections 7251.1, the combined rate of all transaction and use taxes imposed by the City and county is capped at 2%, there is remaining capacity within the local transactions and use tax cap of one quarter percent (0.25%), and the City is authorized to impose the tax contingent on voter approval; and

WHEREAS, the San Mateo City Council has determined to submit to the voters a measure to approve new SMMC Chapter 3.51 that would increase the Transactions and Use Tax rate by an additional one quarter percent (0.25%) until ended by voters, thereby increasing the total Transactions and Use Tax rate to one half percent (0.50%); and

WHEREAS, if the proposed additional Transaction and Use Tax is approved by the voters, is estimated to generate approximately $7 million annually in locally controlled funding for general

[PDF page 2]

governmental purposes; and

WHEREAS, revenues generated by the measure could be used to support City services and infrastructure, including maintaining neighborhood streets, sidewalks, parks, recreation facilities, and public spaces; repairing potholes; supporting police, fire, paramedic, crime prevention, and emergency response services; improving traffic safety; and other general governmental purposes consistent with community priorities.

NOW, THEREFORE, THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF SAN MATEO, CALIFORNIA, RESOLVES AS FOLLOWS:

1. Pursuant to California Constitution Article XIII C, Section 2(b), Government Code Sections 53723 and 53724, Revenue and Taxation Code Section 7285.9, and Elections Code Section 9222, the City Council hereby calls for an election at which it shall submit to the qualified voters of the city a measure, that if approved by the majority of voters voting in the election, would adopt and impose a transactions and use (sales) tax of 0.25%. The election shall be held in the city on November 3, 2026.

2. The City Council hereby approves the Ordinance authorizing the levying of the transactions and use tax for general purposes as set forth in Exhibit A attached hereto and its submittal to the qualified voters of the city during the November 3, 2026 election in accordance with Government Code Sections 53723, 53724(b) and Revenue and Taxation Code Section 7285.9. The Ordinance specifies that (i) the rate of the sales tax shall be one quarter percent (0.25%) of the gross receipts of any retailer from the sale of all tangible personal property sold at retail in the city or otherwise within the base of the transactions and use tax, (ii) the rate of the use tax shall be one quarter percent (0.25%) of the sales price of such tangible personal property, and (iii) that upon the Operative Date of the Ordinance, the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration shall collect the tax from retailers that are subject to the tax and remit the funds to the City.

3. The City Council approves the proposed ordinance set forth in Exhibit A, "AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF SAN MATEO ADOPTING CHAPTER 3.51 OF THE SAN MATEO MUNICIPAL CODE TO INCREASE THE GENERAL TRANSACTIONS AND USE (SALES) TAX RATE BY AN ADDITIONAL ONE-QUARTER PERCENT (0.25%) UNTIL ENDED BY VOTERS, THEREBY INCREASING THE TOTAL TRANSACTIONS AND USE TAX RATE TO ONE HALF PERCENT (0.50%), TO BE ADMINISTERED BY THE CALIFORNIA DEPARTMENT OF TAX AND FEE ADMINISTRATION" attached hereto and incorporated in this Resolution by this reference, for submission to the voters of the City, and orders that the Ballots for the election shall be in the form provided by law, and that the ballot question shall appear on the ballot in the following form and shall be submitted to the voters of the City:

To provide funding for San Mateo city services, such as keeping local streets, sidewalks, parks, infrastructure, and community facilities safe, clean, and well-maintained, fixing potholes, providing fire protection, paramedic, crime prevention, and 9-1-1 emergency response, and improvements to traffic safety/traffic management, shall the City of San Mateo's ordinance establishing a one-quarter cent sales tax be adopted, providing 7 million dollars annually for general government use until ended by voters, with independent audits and all funds locally-controlled?

4. The City Council hereby finds and determines that the transactions and use (sales) taxes imposed pursuant to the Ordinance is for general municipal purposes and accordingly the taxes shall be approved upon a majority vote of the qualified voters of the City of San Mateo voting in the election on the Ordinance.

5. In accordance with Elections Code 10403, the City Council hereby calls for an election regarding the above measure and directs that such election be consolidated with all city, county, and statewide elections occurring on November 3, 2026. The election shall be held and conducted in the manner prescribed by Elections Code section 10418. In all particulars not recited in this resolution, the election shall be held and conducted as provided by law for holding municipal elections.

6. The City Clerk is directed to forward the proposed measure to the City Attorney for preparation of an impartial analysis in accordance with Section 9280 of the Elections Code.

7. Arguments for and against the proposition may be submitted to the qualified voters of the City in accordance with sections 9282 through 9287 of the California Elections Code. The deadline date for submitting ballot arguments for or against the proposition shall be set by the City Clerk. Proposed arguments shall not exceed 300 words and shall be submitted to the Office of the City Clerk. The deadline for submitting rebuttal arguments shall be set by the City Clerk. Proposed rebuttal arguments shall not exceed 250 words and shall be submitted to the office of the City Clerk. The provisions of Section 9285(a) of the California Elections Code shall apply to the submittal of rebuttal arguments.

8. The City Council authorizes two members of that body, Deputy Mayor Nicole Fernandez and Council Member Cwirko-Godycki, to file written arguments in favor of the proposition, and to change the argument until and including the date fixed by the City Clerk after which no arguments for or against the proposition may be submitted to the City Clerk. Any argument filed for or against said proposition not exceeding 300 words shall be filed with the City Clerk, signed, with the printed name(s) and signature(s) of the author(s) submitting it, or if filed on behalf of an organization, the name of the organization, and the printed name and signature of at least one of its principal officers who is the author of the argument.

9. In accordance with Section 12111 of the Elections Code, the City Clerk is hereby authorized and directed to cause notice of the election on the measure to be published. The City Clerk is hereby directed to file a certified copy of this resolution with the Board of Supervisors and the County elections official, and is hereby authorized and directed to take all steps necessary to place the measure on the ballot and to cause the ordinance or measure to be printed.

10. The City and other City officers are directed to do all things necessary to meet the requirements of law for the November 3, 2026, municipal election.

11. The City Manager and City Attorney are jointly authorized to make non-substantive changes to the Ordinance in order to implement changes requested by the State of California, including the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration.
