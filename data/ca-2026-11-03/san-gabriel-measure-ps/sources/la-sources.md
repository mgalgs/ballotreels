## Ballot question and supporters/opponents (verbatim, from the county's final measures list)

> SAN GABRIEL PUBLIC SAFETY/ LOCAL CONTROL To protect San Gabriel's financial stability; maintain medical 911/police response, fire services; prevent crime; protect drinking water sources; repair streets/potholes; keep public areas safe/clean; maintain safe schools; prepare for/respond to earthquakes, fire/other disasters; retain/attract qualified firefighters; for other general services; shall a measure establishing a 1¢ sales tax providing approximately $7,100,000 annually until ended by voters, requiring audits, spending disclosure, funds locally controlled, be adopted?
>
> Supporters: P. Apparis SG Firefighters Assn President; T. Guliani SG Police Assn President
> Opponents: None submitted.

Vote threshold: Majority Vote 50%+1 (county's final measures list).
