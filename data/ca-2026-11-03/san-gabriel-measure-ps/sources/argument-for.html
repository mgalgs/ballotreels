ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE PS

Maintain San Gabriel's health and safety! Vote yes on PS.

Many people don't know that 60% of the time after one medical 911 call is made, another one comes in just 5 minutes later. San Gabriel only has one ambulance to respond to 911 calls. The city is forced to rely on neighboring cities to provide a second ambulance when there's a second call – delaying response times that save lives.

Yes on PS ensures San Gabriel has enough lifesaving equipment: a second ambulance, more firefighters, and paramedics to address our residents' medical emergency needs and the ability to respond to more than one emergency at the same time.

Health and Safety
The number of homeless people in San Gabriel increased by 60% in the last year alone. Yes on PS helps people with mental illnesses, veterans, elderly, and families find the housing, treatment, and services they need to get off the streets.

Yes on PS also:
-Protects local drinking water sources
-Prevents crime/property theft
-Maintains fire services

Fiscal Accountability

Yes on PS stops Los Angeles County bureaucrats from seizing San Gabriel's dollars. Measure PS maintains local control over our money, for the services we need. By law, every penny raised by measure PS must be spent in San Gabriel. No money can be taken by the County, Sacramento, or Washington D.C.

Here's what measure PS doesn't do:
Measure PS isn't a tax on your home or property.
Measure PS doesn't apply to food purchased as groceries or prescription medication.

Join a unanimous mayor and council, public safety leaders, local businesses, and your neighbors: vote yes on PS. For more information about measure PS, visit: www.SanGabrielCity.com
