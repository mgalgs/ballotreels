Measure PS asks the voters of this city whether to add a 1 percent sales tax for general city services.
The City Council placed Measure PS on the ballot, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
The tax would add 1 percent to the price of most goods sold in the city, paid by residents and visitors alike.
Groceries and prescription medicine would not be taxed.
The money would go into the general fund for any lawful city purpose, and that fund is audited every year.
The tax would have no end date, unless voters repeal it at a later election.
The ballot question estimates about $7.1 million a year, and the impartial analysis estimates about $7 million a year.
Supporters say the city has only one ambulance, and the measure would pay for a second, more firefighters and paramedics, all spent locally.
The official county list of measures for this election says that no argument against Measure PS was submitted, by any group or person.
A yes vote approves the 1 percent sales tax.
A no vote is a vote against the 1 percent sales tax.
