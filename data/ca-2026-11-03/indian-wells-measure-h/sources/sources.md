## Quoted passages

- Question (p.22): "CLARIFYING NONPROFIT EXEMPTION FROM CITY ADMISSIONS TAX. To safeguard tax-exemption benefits for residents and visitors who purchase tickets for events sponsored by charitable, religious, public service, and political organizations, shall the voters approve updates to the City's admissions-tax ordinance?"
- Background (p.30): "In 2005, Indian Wells voters approved a City ballot measure, which is commonly referred to as “Measure H”. Measure H enacted a City general admissions tax at a maximum rate of 10 percent (10%) on the admission charge to an event, as defined in Measure H."
- Who placed it (p.30): "On July 2, 2026, the City Council placed Measure “H” on the November 3, 2026, General Municipal Election ballot. The purpose of Measure “H” is to update the Municipal Code by amending and clarifying the types of admissions charges exempted from the City’s admissions tax."
- Analysis (p.30): exempt entities include "Organizations organized and operated exclusively for charitable, religious, scientific, literary, or educational purposes and that have been determined by the United States Internal Revenue Service to be exempt from federal taxation under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code"

Note: the 2026 measure reuses the letter "H", the same letter as the 2005 admissions-tax measure it amends.
