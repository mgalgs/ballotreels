City of Indian Wells Measure H asks voters in the city whether to update which events are exempt from the city admissions tax.
In 2005, city voters approved a tax of up to 10 percent on admission charges to events.
Today, events by nonprofit groups such as country clubs, social clubs, tennis clubs, government agencies and schools are exempt.
Any change to the 2005 measure needs a majority of city voters, so the City Council placed Measure H on the ballot.
Measure H would replace that list: only events run by government agencies, tax-exempt political groups, or federally tax-exempt charities, religious and educational groups would be exempt.
Events at a hotel operating legally in the city would also be exempt.
Charges paid by event sponsors would stay exempt.
The Riverside County Voter Information Guide does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Supporters say the tax was never meant for charitable, religious, political or hotel events; Measure H fixes that.
No argument against Measure H was filed, and none is printed in the Riverside County Voter Information Guide.
A yes vote will approve the updated exemptions from the admissions tax.
A no vote will not approve the updated exemptions from the admissions tax.
