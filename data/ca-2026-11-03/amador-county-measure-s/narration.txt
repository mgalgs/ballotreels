Measure S asks the voters of this county whether to bring back a vehicle registration fee that pays to remove abandoned vehicles.
Since 1991, a county authority has removed abandoned and wrecked vehicles from public and private property, cities included.
Its only funding was a yearly fee on each vehicle registered in the county, which had to be renewed every 10 years.
Voters renewed the fee in 2012, then turned it down in 2022.
Measure S would bring the fee back for 10 years: $1 a vehicle, and $3 for certain commercial vehicles.
The money could pay only for removing abandoned vehicles, and the State Controller would review the program every year.
Measure S needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The ballot question estimates the fee would raise about $55,000 a year.
No argument against Measure S was submitted: the county ballot question page lists its opponents as none submitted.
Supporters say abandoned vehicles cause blight and hazards, and without the fee, the program's reserves would run out.
A yes vote brings back the fee for 10 years.
A no vote means the fee is not collected.
