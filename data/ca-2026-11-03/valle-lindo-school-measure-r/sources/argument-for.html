ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE R

Our schools are some of the most important assets in our community and should be our number one priority. Quality schools improve student achievement, increase property values, prepare children for a productive future, and create greater neighborhood safety. Although our teachers and staff do a great job educating local students, many classrooms and school facilities in the Valle Lindo School District are outdated and inadequate to provide students with the school facilities they need to succeed.

Established in 1863, Valle Lindo School District is the third oldest district in the State of California. While our schools have been well maintained over the years, they are old. The average age is 45 years, and Dean L. Shively Middle School, our oldest, was first built in 1954! Aging classrooms and facilities must be upgraded, since many do not meet 21st-century educational, safety, or technological standards. Measure R would allow the District to improve our neighborhood schools and the quality of education provided to local children.

If passed, Measure R would provide funding to make critical facility improvements at Dean Shively Middle School and New Temple Elementary School including:

Replacing old heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems

Making health, safety and handicapped accessibility (ADA) improvements

Upgrading inadequate electrical systems

Modernizing and upgrading outdated classrooms, restrooms, and school facilities

Repairing or replacing leaky roofs

Measure R makes financial sense and protects taxpayers.

By law, spending must be reviewed and annually audited by an independent citizens' oversight committee.

All bond funds must be spent locally and cannot be taken by the State.

Funds are required to be spent only on schools, not for administrator or teacher salaries.

Measure R upgrades and renovates old and inadequate classrooms, improves the education of local children, and maintains the quality of our community. That's something we can all support. Please join us and VOTE YES ON MEASURE R!
