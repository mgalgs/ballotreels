## Ballot question

From "Att 7 BALLOT QUESTION REVISED", section "BALLOT QUESTION - REVISED (REDLINED)":

> Shall the proposed ordinance, prohibiting residential real property rental rate increases for some rental units that exceed sixty percent of the percentage increase in the Consumer Price Index increase or three percent, whichever is lower, defining a base rent for affected units as the rent in effect on July 1, 2025, establishing a process for consideration of additional rent increases, requiring City administration estimated to cost approximately $1,080,000 to $2,360,000 annually, funded by annual rental property owner fees, limiting the grounds for evicting tenants of some rental units, and enacting additional housing regulations, be adopted?

The same attachment also gives an "INITIAL DRAFT" without the cost clause. **Which version was adopted and printed is not confirmed** (the redlining itself is lost in text extraction).

## Key statements (City Attorney's ballot title and summary, petition stage)

- "TITLE: An Initiative to Impose Rent Control and to Establish Just-Cause Eviction Criteria (including mobilehomes)"
- "This Measure would further cap rents for covered housing at the agreed rent as of July 1, 2025 for units occupied on that date, or the agreed rent when a tenant first occupies a unit after that date. This "Base Rent" can only be increased by an "Annual Allowable Increase" of 60 percent of the Consumer Price Index, but not less than 0 percent nor more than 3 percent, or by a Landlord’s petition to the City"
- "The Measure would repeal the City’s existing Tenant Relocation Assistance Ordinance and Rent Registry Ordinance"
- "The City would impose a rental housing fee on landlords to fund the program, initially, $120 / year for units not entirely or partially exempt"

Related: Legistar matter 26-470 (Council 2026-09-21, "ORD 2026-010") adopted a separate City "Rent Review Program effective January 1, 2027"; it is not part of Measure S.
