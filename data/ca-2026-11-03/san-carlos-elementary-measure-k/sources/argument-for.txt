San Carlos School District
2026 Parcel Tax Measure – on the November 3, 2026 Ballot
Argument In Favor of Measure _

VOTE YES on Measure _ to protect our teachers and protect our property values. Seniors 65+ can apply for an exemption.

San Carlos is a destination community with high-performing public schools. Demand for strong schools is what keeps our property values strong. In 2020, our community generously approved a parcel tax to fund teacher positions. When it expires, $3 million will disappear from our district's budget, forcing teacher layoffs and threatening cuts to programs in reading, writing, math, technology, arts, and more.

Measure _ is NOT a brand-new tax. It simply renews our existing school funding with a modest increase that preserves San Carlos School District's ability to fund and retain 24 teaching positions.

VOTING YES means:
- Retaining our excellent teachers who make San Carlos schools great
- Maintaining lower class sizes for all learners
- Preserving high-quality educational programs for San Carlos students in reading, math, science, the arts, and more

Safeguards for Seniors and Taxpayers are built in
- Senior homeowners 65+ are eligible for an exemption.
- BY LAW these funds cannot be taken by the State; every dollar stays in San Carlos.
- No funds are allowed for administrators' salaries.
- Renters do not pay this tax.

Join San Carlos parents, teachers, neighbors, and local business and community leaders in voting Yes on Measure _. Whether or not you have school-age children, supporting quality education is a wise investment that protects the well-being of our community and protects your property values.

VOTE YES on _. www.yesforscsd.org
