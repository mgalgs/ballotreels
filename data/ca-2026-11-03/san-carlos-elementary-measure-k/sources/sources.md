# Measure K — San Carlos School District (parcel tax, $489/parcel, 8 years)

- **Jurisdiction:** San Carlos School District.
- **Kind:** Qualified special tax (parcel tax), "San Carlos School District Local Education Funding Measure of 2026"; replaces the 2020 Measure N parcel tax.
- **Who votes:** the voters of the District. Section 3 (resolution p. 3): the Board "requests the Registrar of Voters of the County (the "Registrar of Voters") to submit to the voters of the District on November 3, 2026, the following ballot measure". Exhibit A (p. 8): notice "to the qualified electors of the San Carlos School District".
- **Vote threshold:** two-thirds. Section 5 (p. 5): "the above measure shall become effective upon the affirmative vote of at least two-thirds of those voters voting on the proposition." Impartial analysis p. 2: "This Measure passes if at least two-thirds of those voting on the Measure vote "yes"."
- **Placed by:** Board of Education, Resolution No. 11:25/26, adopted April 2, 2026. The election is called by the County Superintendent of Schools on the Board's order (Section 2, p. 2).
- **Election:** November 3, 2026.

## Ballot question

> To protect outstanding educational programs, hands-on science, math, technology, arts, reading and writing, attract and retain high-quality teachers, maintain reasonable class sizes, and sustain property values, shall the San Carlos School District's measure be adopted to replace its existing parcel tax at a $489 per parcel rate (raising approximately $4,400,000 annually) for 8 years, with annual cost-of-living adjustments, citizen oversight, senior exemptions, all funds spent locally and no funds used for administrator salaries?

(resolution-full-text.pdf p. 5, Section 4 "Abbreviation of Measure", checked against the page image; the same wording is repeated in the Exhibit A form of notice, p. 8. The resolution prints it in quotation marks, removed here; a handwritten "(70)" word count is in the margin.)

## Full text (resolution)

- Resolution No. 11:25/26, "RESOLUTION OF THE BOARD OF EDUCATION OF THE SAN CARLOS SCHOOL DISTRICT, COUNTY OF SAN MATEO, STATE OF CALIFORNIA, ORDERING AN EDUCATION PARCEL TAX ELECTION" (p. 1; stamped "Approved on APR 02 2026 Board Agenda").
- Adopted: "PASSED AND ADOPTED this day, April 2, 2026, by the following vote: AYES: 4 NOES: 0 ABSTAIN: 0 ABSENT: 1" (p. 7; numbers handwritten). The clerk's certificate (p. 9) records the same vote at the April 2, 2026 regular meeting. Signatures redacted by ACRE.
- Recital (p. 2): "Measure N is set to expire in June 2028 and the loss of this revenue would be significant".
- Recital (p. 2): "local parcel tax funding provides $3.029 million to the District each year and is budgeted to account for approximately 5.57% of the District's total revenue in fiscal year 2025-26".
- Measure, "Purposes" (p. 3): proceeds may be used for "(a) attracting and retaining qualified and experienced teachers; (b) maintaining small class sizes; (c) maintaining quality science, technology, math, reading and writing instruction; (d) maintaining hands-on science, math and technology programs, (e) maintaining school libraries; and (f) providing art, music and other well-rounded educational programs, and not for any other purpose, including administrators' salaries or pensions."
- Measure, accountability (p. 4): "The Board of Education shall establish an independent citizens' oversight committee".
- Measure (p. 5): "If this Measure is approved by voters, the District's existing education and local funding parcel tax approved by the voters of the District in 2020 (Measure N) shall cease to be collected as of July 1, 2027."
- Section 6(b) (p. 6) asks the Registrar to "reprint the measure in its entirety ... in the voter information pamphlet".

## Impartial analysis

Headed "IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE [TBD]". **No author's name or office is printed** on either page, so the author is unconfirmed from this file. Bracketed numbers are per-paragraph word counts in the original.

- p. 1: "This Measure, if approved by voters, would authorize a new parcel tax in the amount of $489 per parcel per year, for a period of up to eight years, commencing July 1, 2027. The amount of the tax is subject to annual adjustment based on the Bay Area Consumer Price Index, and is estimated to raise approximately $4,400,000 annually."
- p. 1: "The District's existing parcel tax (Measure N) will cease to be collected as of July 1, 2027."
- p. 1: "Any person who is 65 years of age or older as of July 1 of the tax year, and who owns and occupies a parcel within the District, may apply for an exemption from the tax."
- p. 1: "The Measure does not authorize use of tax proceeds for administrators' salaries or pensions."
- p. 1: "A "yes" vote on this Measure would impose on property within the District an annual parcel tax of $489, subject to annual adjustment based on the Bay Area Consumer Price Index, for up to eight years beginning July 1, 2027, for the purposes listed above."
- p. 2: "A "no" vote on this Measure would not allow the parcel tax to be levied."

## Tax rate, exemptions, duration (from the measure in the resolution)

- Rate and duration (p. 3, "Terms"): "Upon approval of two-thirds of those voting on this measure (this "Measure"), the San Carlos School District (the "District") shall be authorized to levy a qualified special tax (the "Education Parcel Tax") at the amount of $489 per year on each parcel of taxable real property in the District, adjusted by the Bay Area Consumer Price Index each year, for no more than eight (8) years, commencing July 1, 2027."
- Exemptions (p. 3): "any parcel owned and occupied by the following taxpayers shall be exempt from the Education Parcel Tax upon proper application to the District: (a) persons who are 65 years of age or older as of July 1 of the tax year; (b) persons receiving Supplemental Security Income for a disability, regardless of age; and (c) persons receiving Social Security Disability Insurance benefits, regardless of age, whose yearly income does not exceed 250 percent of the 2012 federal poverty guidelines issued by the United States Department of Health and Human Services."
- Automatic carry-over (p. 3): owners of owner-occupied single-family parcels "currently exempted from the District's existing education and local funding parcel tax approved by the voters of the District in 2020 (Measure N) shall automatically be exempted from this Measure without having to file a new application."
- Parcel defined (p. 4): "any unit of real property in the District which receives a separate tax bill for ad valorem property taxes from the Tax Collector's Office."
- Appropriations limit (p. 4): "The District's annual appropriations limit shall be raised in each year the Education Parcel Tax is authorized to be levied in the amount of the funds raised by the Education Parcel Tax in such year."
- Revenue: "raising approximately $4,400,000 annually" (ballot question). No separate fiscal statement was found among ACRE's files.
