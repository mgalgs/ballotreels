San Carlos School District Measure K asks the district's voters whether to replace its existing parcel tax with a tax of $489 per parcel.
Today, the district levies a $334.60 parcel tax that voters passed in 2020, which ends in June 2028.
Measure K needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
From July 1, 2027, the new tax would replace the 2020 tax for up to eight years, rising each year with inflation.
The money could pay for teachers, small classes, science, libraries and the arts, but not administrators' pay.
The impartial analysis says the tax is estimated to raise about $4.4 million a year.
No argument against Measure K appears in the published measure documents of San Mateo County for this election.
Supporters say Measure K is not a brand-new tax; without it, $3 million would disappear, forcing teacher layoffs.
A yes vote would impose the $489 parcel tax, adjusted each year, for up to eight years.
A no vote would not allow the new parcel tax to be levied.
