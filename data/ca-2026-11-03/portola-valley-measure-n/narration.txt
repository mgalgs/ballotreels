Town of Portola Valley Measure N asks the voters of Portola Valley whether to adopt a town charter and a higher tax on property sales.
In California, a general law town must follow state law, while a charter town may set its own rules on municipal affairs.
Portola Valley is a general law town, and taxes property sales at the most state law allows it: 27.5 cents per $500.
The charter would give the town its own powers over local taxes, and would not change existing local laws.
The transfer tax would become $10 per $1,000 of the price, and the buyer and seller can agree who pays.
The Town Council could lower or end the tax, but raising it would need another vote of the people.
The impartial analysis of the town attorney says the tax would raise about $2 million a year, for any town purpose.
Opponents say only the few residents selling each year would pay, $45,000 and up on an average home sale.
Supporters say Measure N gives Portola Valley the resources to be self-reliant, and prevents a county takeover of local zoning.
A yes vote adopts the charter and authorizes the transfer tax.
A no vote is a vote against both the charter and the tax.
