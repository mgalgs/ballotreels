Section 2. Election Called. Pursuant to the requirements of the laws of the State of California, the Town Council hereby calls an election to be held in the Town of Portola Valley, California, on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, at which it shall submit to the voters of Portola Valley a measure that, if approved by a majority vote, would make Portola Valley a Charter Town and adopt a real property transfer tax.

Section 3. Ballot Question. The Town Council, pursuant to its right and authority, does hereby submit for adoption by the voters of Portola Valley at the General Municipal Election of November 3, 2026, the following question:

[page 4]
Town of Portola Valley Autonomy Measure. Shall the measure maintaining Portola Valley’s independent status be adopted, providing: wildfire prevention/vegetation management; 911 emergency response/police patrols; local zoning/planning services; storm drain repair; and general government services; by adopting a charter for the Town of Portola Valley solely for, and establishing a real property transfer tax of $10 per $1,000, paid by buyers or sellers of property, until ended by voters, providing approximately $2,000,000 in locally controlled funding annually, with independent oversight?
