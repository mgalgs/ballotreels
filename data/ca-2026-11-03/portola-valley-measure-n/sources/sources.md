# Measure N — Town of Portola Valley (town charter + real property transfer tax, general tax)

- Jurisdiction: Town of Portola Valley.
- Kind: one measure that both adopts a town charter (making Portola Valley a charter town) and enacts a real property transfer tax of $10 per $1,000 (general tax). Ballot label: "Town of Portola Valley Autonomy Measure".
- Who votes: the voters of the Town of Portola Valley. Resolution, p. 3, Sec. 2: "the Town Council hereby calls an election to be held in the Town of Portola Valley, California, on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, at which it shall submit to the voters of Portola Valley a measure that, if approved by a majority vote, would make Portola Valley a Charter Town and adopt a real property transfer tax."
- Vote threshold: simple majority. Resolution, p. 4, Sec. 5: "The Measure shall not take effect unless and until it is approved by a vote of at least a simple majority (50% plus 1) of affirmative votes of the voters voting on the question at the election. If the measure is approved, both the Charter and the Real Property Transfer Tax shall be approved."
- Placed by: the Town Council, Resolution No. 3010-2026.
- Election: November 3, 2026.

## Ballot question

> Town of Portola Valley Autonomy Measure. Shall the measure maintaining Portola Valley's independent status be adopted, providing: wildfire prevention/vegetation management; 911 emergency response/police patrols; local zoning/planning services; storm drain repair; and general government services; by adopting a charter for the Town of Portola Valley solely for, and establishing a real property transfer tax of $10 per $1,000, paid by buyers or sellers of property, until ended by voters, providing approximately $2,000,000 in locally controlled funding annually, with independent oversight?

(resolution-full-text.pdf p. 4, Sec. 3; lead-in at the bottom of p. 3; transcribed from the scan and re-checked at 150 dpi.) "solely for, and establishing" is printed exactly so. Sec. 4 (p. 4) lets the Town Manager and Town Attorney make "minor non-substantive revisions to the ballot question, as deemed necessary by the County Elections Office", so the printed ballot may differ.

## Full text (resolution / ordinance)

- Resolution No. 3010-2026, "A Resolution of the Town Council of the Town of Portola Valley Submitting to the Voters of Portola Valley a Measure Both to Adopt a Town Charter and to Authorize a Real Property Transfer Tax at the General Municipal Election to Be Held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026 as Called by Resolution 3001-2026" (p. 1). Filed with the Chief Elections Officer JUL 23 2026 (stamp, p. 1).
- Adopted: "PASSED AND ADOPTED on July 22, 2026 by the following vote: AYES: WOLTER, HASKO, FLYNN, HUFTY, TAYLOR NOES: NONE ABSTAIN: NONE ABSENT: NONE" (p. 6). Signed Craig Taylor, Mayor; attest Veronica Dao, Town Clerk.
- Recitals, p. 1: the Town "has imposed a real property transfer tax ... since 1967 at the state-authorized rate of 27.5¢ per $500 of the real estate sales price"; "as a general law town, Portola Valley does not currently have the legal authority to enact a real property transfer tax beyond that already imposed".
- Recital, p. 2: "should the Town lose its independent status, zoning decisions would be made by the County".
- Sec. 5, p. 4: the Charter and Ordinance "shall be completely printed in the voter pamphlet".
- Charter (Exhibit A), Sec. 100, p. 7: "the Town may impose a tax on the conveyance of real property, based upon the price paid for the real property ("real property transfer tax")." Sec. 101: "Except as provided in this Charter with respect to the power of the Town over local taxation, the powers of the Town shall otherwise be constrained by, subject to, and governed by the general laws of the State".
- Ordinance No. 2026-456 (Exhibit B), Sec. 3.12.020, p. 9: tax "at the rate of $10.00 for each one thousand dollars ($1,000.00) of the value of consideration ... paid for a transfer of real property" (replacing "twenty-seven and one-half cents for each five hundred dollars", struck through).
- Sec. 3.12.030, pp. 9-10: "The transferor and transferee of real property subject to the tax shall be jointly and severally liable for payment of this tax to the Town and may apportion the tax among themselves."

## Impartial analysis

"TOWN OF PORTOLA VALLEY MEASURE N TOWN ATTORNEY'S IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS", signed "Catherine Engberg, Town Attorney, Town of Portola Valley" (impartial-analysis.pdf p. 1; the scan carries handwritten word counts, total 472).

- "The Portola Valley Town Council placed Measure N on the ballot to ask Portola Valley voters to approve both a Town Charter and a tax on the sale of real property ("RPTT")."
- "The Charter itself would not change existing local laws, like the Municipal Code."
- "The Town currently imposes a tax on the transfer of real property at the rate of twenty-seven and one-half cents ($0.275) per five-hundred dollars ($500) of the sales price of property, which is the maximum tax rate permitted under state law for general law towns."
- "Measure N would authorize the Town to levy the RPTT at the rate of ten dollars ($10) per one thousand dollars ($1,000) of consideration paid for real property. ... The buyer and seller can negotiate responsibility for payment of the RPTT."
- "The RPTT would be a general tax because the revenue would be deposited into the Town's general fund and could be used for any legitimate Town purpose. If approved, the Measure would generate approximately $2 million of additional revenue annually."
- "A "Yes" vote is a vote to adopt the Charter and authorize the RPTT. A "No" vote is a vote against the Charter and RPTT. Measure N would be approved if it received a simple majority of "Yes" votes."

## Tax rate statement / fiscal

General tax, not a bond; no tax rate statement is among ACRE's files. From Ordinance No. 2026-456 (resolution-full-text.pdf):
- Rate (Sec. 3.12.020, p. 9): "$10.00 for each one thousand dollars ($1,000.00) of the value of consideration" — applies "when the consideration or value of the interest or property conveyed ... exceeds one hundred dollars".
- Exemptions: Secs. 3.12.040 onward (pp. 10-12) carry the existing exemptions (e.g., "Any tax imposed pursuant to this chapter shall not apply to any instrument in writing given to secure a debt."; public agencies acquiring title; bankruptcy/reorganization; SEC orders; marital dissolution).
- Council powers (Sec. 3.12.130, pp. 12-13): may amend "in any manner that does not increase the tax rates", including "(A) Repeal or reduce the tax." and "(D) Create exemptions from the tax."
- Oversight (Sec. 2, p. 13): "The Town of Portola Valley Finance and Audit Committee ... shall review the audit".
- Effective (Sec. 5, p. 13): "10 days after the certification of its approval by the voters". Duration: "until ended by voters" (ballot question). Revenue: "approximately $2,000,000 ... annually" (question); "approximately $2 million of additional revenue annually" (impartial analysis).
