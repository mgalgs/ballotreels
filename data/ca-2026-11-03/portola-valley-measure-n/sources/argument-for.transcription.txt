ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE “___” (Town of Portola Valley Autonomy Measure)

PROTECT PORTOLA VALLEY’S TOWN AUTONOMY: Vote YES on “___”.
It’s more important than ever that Portola Valley has the resources to be self-reliant in these uncertain times.

YES on “___”:
- Financially stabilizes Portola Valley
- Maintains OUR Local Autonomy
- Maintains Emergency Reserves

If Portola Valley were to lose our Town status, we would lose local control over important decisions that affect our community, including zoning and land use. Instead of decisions being made in our Town these decisions could be made by County.

YES on “___”: prevents a County takeover of our local zoning and planning services.

EMERGENCY RESPONSE & PREPAREDNESS

YES on “___” ensures Portola Valley is prepared for natural disasters such as wildfires, earthquakes, flooding or landslides with prevention measures, evacuation planning and emergency response communication systems.

YES on “___”: Maintains wildfire prevention services including vegetation and brush management and clearance programs reducing the chance of wildfires.

YES on “___”:
- Maintains 911 emergency response times and police patrols
- Repairs deteriorating storm drains preventing flooding and water pollution
- Provide community resilence

LOCAL CONTROL OVER PORTOLA VALLEY’S FUNDING
By law, YES on “___” requires every dime be used for Town services—none can be taken by the County, State or Federal Government. Yes on “___” gives you—the Portola Valley taxpayer -- LOCAL CONTROL.

MEASURE “___” ACCOUNTABILITY
Yes on “___” includes strict accountability provisions, including public spending reports, community oversight and annual independent financial audits. Additionally, all funds go ONLY to Portola Valley, and none can be taken by the state.

We must take action now if we want to save our Town. Join Portola Valley firefighters, local business owners, a unanimous City Council and your neighbors and vote YES on “___”.
