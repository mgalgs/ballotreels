Argument in Favor of Measure "A"
Le Grand Union High School District
School Improvement Funding Measure

Yes on Measure "A"! Let's protect our investment and continue improving Le Grand high school – without increasing prior voter-approved taxes.

Our high schools are among our community's most vital assets. In recent years, Le Grand high school has received acknowledgement for increasing test scores, college-going rates and career opportunities. On top of driving student achievement, our high schools support strong property values and foster community safety.

Le Grand high school has benefited greatly from past community support. Past voter-approved funding went to deal with most basic needs. To show, today we have our distinguished Career Technical Education program, which prepares students for jobs in agriculture, business, and healthcare fields. The funds were used responsibly and stretched as far as possible. Now it's time to complete the next phase of essential improvements.

Why Measure "A" is Urgently Needed:

- Old infrastructure: Le Grand High first opened in 1909. Old and inadequate infrastructure (roofs, electrical, HVAC, plumbing and sewer systems) must be revamped.
- Aging classrooms: Many classrooms require repairs and upgrades to meet today's safety and educational standards.
- Outdated sports/ recreation facilities: Used by our students and entire community, our sports facilities need upgrading to ensure accessibility and functionality.

Measure "A" is a smart investment:

- Does not increase tax burden: Tax rates remain at similar levels previously approved by voters.
- Leverages State funding: Makes District eligible for $5.8M in state matching grants.
- 100% local funds: Dollars stay local, benefiting our students and community.
- Strong accountability: By law, funds may be spent only on voter-approved projects and will be audited annual through independent citizen oversight.

Without this investment, our aging facilities will continue to deteriorate, resulting in more costly emergency repairs in the future. Measure "A" ensures our students have safe, quality facilities for years to come.

Join us – VOTE YES ON MEASURE "A"!

/s/ Jeannie Giampaoli, Board President, Le Grand UHSD
/s/ Vickie Banaga, Le Grand resident
