Measure A asks the voters of this school district whether to approve $9,100,000 in school bonds.
The district would sell the bonds and repay them from a tax on property in the district.
Measure A needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The ballot question lists projects such as modernizing classrooms and restrooms, replacing leaky roofs, and upgrading electrical and plumbing systems.
The impartial analysis, citing the district's tax rate statement, estimates the highest rate at $25.25 a year per $100,000 of assessed value.
The analysis estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $21 million, with the tax ending in 2057-2058.
No argument against Measure A appears among the official documents gathered for this video; only the argument in favor does.
Supporters say the high school opened in 1909, its roofs and plumbing must be revamped, and tax rates would stay similar.
A yes vote is in favor of authorizing the district to issue and sell up to $9,100,000 in bonds.
A no vote is against authorizing the district to issue and sell the bonds.
