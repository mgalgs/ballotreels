RESOLUTION NO. 2026-12

A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF FORTUNA ORDERING THE SUBMISSION TO THE QUALIFIED ELECTORS OF THE CITY OF FORTUNA A CERTAIN MEASURE RELATING TO A PROPOSED ORDINANCE AMENDING CHAPTER 3.13 OF TITLE 3 OF THE FORTUNA MUNICIPAL CODE TO AUTHORIZE AN ADDITIONAL CITY TRANSACTIONS AND USE TAX FOR GENERAL PURPOSES AT THE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION TO BE HELD ON TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 3, 2026, AS CALLED AND PROPOSED TO THE QUALIFIED ELECTORS BY RESOLUTION; DIRECTING THE CITY ATTORNEY TO PREPARE AN IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF THE MEASURE; SETTING THE DATES FOR ARGUMENTS ON THE MEASURE; AND REQUESTING THE BOARD OF SUPERVISORS OF THE COUNTY OF HUMBOLDT TO CONSOLIDATE SAID ELECTION PURSUANT TO SECTION 10403 OF THE ELECTIONS CODE

[Page 2]

NOW, THEREFORE, THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF FORTUNA DOES RESOLVE, DECLARE, DETERMINE AND ORDER AS FOLLOWS:

SECTION 1. The City Council, pursuant to its right and authority, does order submitted to the voters at the General Municipal Election of November 3, 2026, the following question:

Fortuna Essential Services Funding Measure
Shall the measure to maintain essential City services, such as protecting public safety; increasing and restoring police officers; repairing aging/ deteriorating streets and potholes; providing programs for seniors and youth; and keeping parks clean and safe, by authorizing a ¾-cent sales tax in the City of Fortuna, generating $1,800,000 annually for general government use until ended by voters, requiring independent audits, community oversight, and all funds staying in Fortuna, be adopted?
Yes
No

SECTION 2. The proposed measure submitted to the voters is attached as Exhibit A. The City Council hereby approves the ordinance by a two-thirds vote of the City Council, the form thereof, and its submission to the voters of the City at the November 3, 2026 General Municipal Election, as required by Revenue and Taxation Code section 7285.9. The ordinance authorizes an additional transactions tax at the rate of three-quarters of one percent (0.75%) commencing April 1, 2027, and an additional use tax at the rate of three-quarters of one percent (0.75%) commencing April 1, 2027. The additional taxes shall be administered by the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration. The ordinance further provides that the transactions and use taxes previously approved by the voters shall remain in effect through March 31, 2033, unless sooner repealed in accordance with law. From April 1, 2027, through March 31, 2033, the combined rate of the existing and additional transactions taxes, and the combined rate of the existing and additional use taxes, shall be one and one-half percent (1.5%).
