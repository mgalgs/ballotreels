## Facts with quotes

- **Ballot question (Resolution 2026-12 §1):** "Fortuna Essential Services Funding Measure — Shall the measure to maintain essential City services, such as protecting public safety; increasing and restoring police officers; repairing aging/ deteriorating streets and potholes; providing programs for seniors and youth; and keeping parks clean and safe, by authorizing a ¾-cent sales tax in the City of Fortuna, generating $1,800,000 annually for general government use until ended by voters, requiring independent audits, community oversight, and all funds staying in Fortuna, be adopted?"
- **Proposal (guide p. 13):** "Measure D asks the voters whether the City of Fortuna should authorize an additional three-quarters of one percent (¾-cent) transactions and use tax, commonly known as a "sales tax," beginning April 1, 2027. The City currently imposes a separate ¾-cent transactions and use tax that is scheduled to expire on March 31, 2033. If Measure D is approved, the combined City transactions and use tax rate will be 1.5% from April 1, 2027, through March 31, 2033."
- **History:** "Fortuna voters approved the existing ¾-cent tax as Measure E in 2016. In 2020, voters approved Measure G, which extended that tax through March 31, 2033."
- **Duration:** "The additional ¾-cent tax authorized by Measure D would continue after that date unless repealed by the voters or reduced or repealed by the City Council to the extent permitted by law."
- **Use / revenue:** "Measure D is a general tax. Revenue generated by the measure may be used for any legitimate City governmental purpose ... The measure is estimated to generate approximately $1.8 million annually. Revenues are subject to independent audit and community oversight."
- **Vote needed:** "Measure D requires approval by a majority of the voters voting on the measure."
- **Yes/No:** "A "Yes" vote is a vote to approve the additional ¾-cent transactions and use tax. A "No" vote is a vote against the additional ¾-cent transactions and use tax."
- **Argument in favor (guide p. 14):** signed "/s/ Mike Johnson, Mayor of Fortuna".
