Measure D asks the voters of this city whether to add a second 0.75 percent local sales tax.
Today, the city already has a 0.75 percent sales tax, set to end on March 31, 2033.
Measure D would add another 0.75 percent from April 1, 2027, for a combined city rate of 1.5 percent until the first tax ends.
The added tax would continue after 2033, unless repealed by voters, or reduced or repealed by the council as the law allows.
As a general tax, the money may be used for any legitimate city purpose.
Measure D needs a majority of the vote to pass.
The city attorney's impartial analysis estimates that Measure D would bring in about $1.8 million a year.
The county voter guide prints only the argument in favor of Measure D, and none against it.
Supporters say the tax gives stable local funding for city services, and the state cannot take it.
A yes vote approves the added 0.75 percent sales tax.
A no vote is a vote against the added 0.75 percent sales tax.
