RESOLUTION NO. 2025/26-38
OF THE BOARD OF EDUCATION OF THE
HACIENDA LA PUENTE UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT
PROPOSING A LIMITATION ON THE NUMBER OF TERMS
SERVED BY MEMBERS OF THE GOVERNING BOARD

WHEREAS, a majority of the Board of Education of the Hacienda La Puente Unified School District ("Board") believes that periodic leadership turnover on the governing board promotes accountability, encourages civic participation, and provides additional community members with the opportunity to serve; and

WHEREAS, Education Code section 35107(c) authorizes the governing board of a school district to adopt a proposal to limit the number of terms a member of the governing board may serve, subject to approval by the voters of the school district; and

WHEREAS, Education Code section 35107(c) further provides that any proposal to limit the number of terms served by a member of a school district governing board shall apply prospectively only and shall not become operative unless submitted to the electors of the school district at a regularly scheduled election and approved by a majority of the votes cast; and

WHEREAS, members of the Board serve four-year terms of office pursuant to law; and

WHEREAS, the Board desires to propose a limitation providing that no person may serve more than two (2) lifetime four-year terms as a member of the Board; and

WHEREAS, the Board intends to adopt a lifetime service limit of two (2) consecutive four-year terms of members of the Governing Board of the Hacienda La Puente Unified School District, and no person may serve more than two (2) full four-year terms on the Board regardless of the trustee area represented. Any full four-year term served on or after December 11, 2026 shall count toward the term limit, consistent with the prospective application required by law; and

WHEREAS, the Board finds that submitting this proposal to the voters will allow the electorate of the District to determine whether such term limits should be imposed.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Board of Education ("Board") hereby determines, finds, and resolves as follows:

SECTION 1. The foregoing recitals are true and correct and are incorporated herein by reference.

SECTION 2. Submission of Term Limits Proposal to Voters. Pursuant to Education Code section 35107(c), the Board hereby orders that a proposal to limit the number of terms a member of the Board may serve be submitted to the electors of the Hacienda La Puente Unified School District at the next regularly scheduled election authorized by law.

--- Page 2 ---

SECTION 3. Proposed Term Limit. If approved by the voters, the proposal shall provide that no person may serve more than two (2) consecutive full four-year terms as a member of the Board, regardless of the trustee area represented. Any full four-year term served on or after December 11, 2026 shall count toward the term limit. The limitation shall apply prospectively only, as required by law.

SECTION 4. Ballot Measure and Ballot Language. The Board hereby directs that the ballot measure submitted to the voters shall be presented in substantially the following form:

BALLOT QUESTION
Shall members of the Board of Education of the Hacienda La Puente Unified School District be limited to serving no more than two (2) consecutive four-year terms during their lifetime regardless of the trustee area represented?
YES ____ NO ____

SECTION 5. The Superintendent or designee is authorized and directed to take all actions necessary and appropriate to submit the measure to the voters, including coordination with the Los Angeles County Registrar-Recorder/County Clerk and the preparation and filing of all required election materials.

SECTION 6. The Board finds that this Resolution and the proposed ballot measure are not a project within the meaning of the California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA) because they relate to organizational or administrative activities of government that will not result in direct or indirect physical changes in the environment.

SECTION 7. If any provision of this Resolution or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, such invalidity shall not affect the other provisions or applications of the Resolution, which can be given effect without the invalid provision or application.

SECTION 8. If approved by the voters, the term limits would be effective on January 1, 2027.

SECTION 9. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon its adoption.

I hereby certify that the foregoing Resolution was duly adopted at a meeting of the Board of Education of the Hacienda La Puente Unified School District, at a meeting thereof duly held on the 11th day of May, 2026, by a majority vote of its present members.
