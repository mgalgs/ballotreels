## Ballot question (verbatim, from the county measure list)

> Shall members of the Board of Education of the Hacienda La Puente Unified School District be limited to serving no more than two (2) four-year terms during their lifetime regardless of the trustee area represented?

Supporters: None submitted.
Opponents: None submitted.

Vote threshold: majority vote, 50%+1.

## What's present / missing

- **Resolution / substance of the measure**: present (`resolution.pdf`). Resolution recitals cite Education Code §35107(c) as authority, state the limitation applies prospectively only ("any full four-year term served on or after December 11, 2026 shall count toward the term limit"), and that it requires majority-vote approval — consistent with the county list's "two (2) four-year terms... lifetime... regardless of the trustee area represented" and 50%+1 threshold. The resolution predates the county's final ballot-question wording, so its own language does not match the ballot question word-for-word, though it matches in substance.
- **Full text**: not applicable beyond the resolution itself — there is no separate ordinance or charter text for this kind of measure.
- **Tax rate statement / fiscal analysis**: not applicable — not a tax or bond measure.
- **Impartial analysis**: missing — not located (district/county counsel). Status: not found/not fetched.
- **Arguments for/against, rebuttals**: none submitted, per the county list (official — not merely unfound). The resolution itself has no argument exhibits attached, consistent with this.
- **Staff report**: no document beyond the resolution's own "WHEREAS" recitals was located; `hlpschools.org`'s BoardDocs portal (http://www.boarddocs.com/ca/hlpusd/board.nsf/public) was identified as the likely home for a March 12, 2026 "Term Limits Presentation" but was not fetched (follow-on work, not pursued for this staging pass).
