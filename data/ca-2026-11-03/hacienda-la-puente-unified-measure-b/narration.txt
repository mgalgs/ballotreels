Measure B asks the voters of this school district whether to limit school board members to two four-year terms in a lifetime.
School board members serve four-year terms, and state law lets a board ask its voters to limit how many terms members may serve.
Three of the five board members voted to place Measure B on the ballot, and two voted against.
The resolution says the majority believes periodic turnover on the board promotes accountability and gives more people a chance to serve.
No one could serve more than two four-year terms on the board in a lifetime, whichever part of the district they represent.
Only full terms served from November 3, 2026 onward would count toward the limit.
Measure B needs a majority yes vote to pass.
