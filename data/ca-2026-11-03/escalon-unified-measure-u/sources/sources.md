## Quoted facts

- Who votes: tax rate statement, "An election will be held in the Escalon Unified School District (the “District”) on November 3, 2026, to authorize the sale of up to $26,000,000 in general obligation bonds."
- Ballot question: "To provide safe, modern schools by upgrading/repairing aging classrooms, science labs, career technology and school facilities to support college/career readiness in math, science, technology, arts, engineering, and skilled trades; and fixing deteriorating roofs, plumbing, HVAC, and electrical systems; shall Escalon Unified School District’s measure authorizing $26,000,000 in bonds at legal rates be adopted, levying approximately $18 per $100,000 assessed value ($1,700,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding, with independent audits, citizen oversight and all money locally controlled?"
- Vote needed: "Passage of Measure U requires approval by fifty-five percent (55%) of voters voting thereon."
- Analysis: "Approval of Measure U would allow the Escalon Unified School District (the “District”) to incur bond indebtedness up to a maximum principal amount of $26,000,000.00."
- Analysis: "No funds derived from the bond sales may be used for general school operating expenses, including teacher or District administrators’ salaries".
- Analysis: "Measure U would require the District to: (1) establish an independent citizens’ oversight committee; (2) conduct annual independent performance and financial audits; and (3) direct the funds to be deposited into a special account."
- Tax rate statement: average "is $18 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. It is currently expected that the tax will be collected until fiscal year 2057-58."; highest "is $18 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. This rate is projected to apply beginning in fiscal year 2027-28."; total debt service "will be approximately $61,000,000."
- Argument in favor signers: "Marlene M. Sousa, Life long Resident; Susan Gentry, Retired Educator/ Escalon Resident; Cecilia Nelson, Life long Resident/ Business owner; Joshua Eichhorn, Escalon Resident/ Business Owner; Richard Thompson, Escalon Resident".
