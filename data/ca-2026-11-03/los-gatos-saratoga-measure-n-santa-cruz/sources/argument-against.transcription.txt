Argument Against
Los Gatos-Saratoga Joint Union High School District $321,000,000 Bond, Measure __

When school boards ask voters permission to go into debt with bond like Measure _, they admit that every project they are currently spending your tax dollars on, now, is more important than the projects listed in this ballot measure.

Budgets reflect priorities. Los Gatos-Saratoga Joint Union High School District is saying every educational dollar spent today is going to something they consider a higher priority than "fixing leaky roofs" and "keeping schools clean and well-maintained" and "technology".

Do you agree?

The $99,000,000 bond voters approved in 2014 listed "replacing leaky and aging roofs" that apparently need replacing again!

Did you ever take out a 25-30 loan to buy a personal computer? No? But that is what the District did in 2014 with BOND money to "update technology". Nuts, right?

Education Data Partnership (ED-DATA.ORG) shows declining enrollment of 3,018 students in the district, which means the bond could exceed $106,361 per student -- NOT counting interest expense.

When you buy a home, truth in lending laws require that you be told the real total cost of buying that home. For example:

Borrow $321,000,000; pay 3% interest for 30 years: payments are $9,630,000 per year equals $288,900,000 interest plus principal $321,000,000 for a lifetime total cost of $561,750,000. Over a half BILLION dollars...of YOUR money!

Consumer protection laws should apply to bond issues. As taxpayers, we deserve to know the full truth about Measure __. We don't even know when this debt will begin, let alone what the market interest rate will be, when it does.

Just say no to bond debt.

Question: will this bond measure fix declining English proficiency? NO!

Question: will this bond measure fix declining math proficiency? NO!

Question: will this bond measure fix declining enrollment? NO!

Stop rewarding bad behavior. Vote NO tax increase!
