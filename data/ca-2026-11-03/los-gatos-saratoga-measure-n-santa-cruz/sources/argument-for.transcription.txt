Argument in Favor of Measure __

Vote Yes on __ to repair our high schools, maintain our excellent reputation, and protect local property values.

Los Gatos High School and Saratoga High School are central to our community's identity. Our high schools are among the best in the state and nation and are the #1 driver of local property values. They are why many of us move here and stay.
Yet some high school buildings are between 65, 75, and even 100 years old, and in desperate need of repairs and updates. Roofs leak, plumbing fails and outdated energy systems are inefficient. If we wait, repair costs will go up, and our aging high schools will require even more costly maintenance.
Additionally, many classrooms and labs don't meet the modern standards necessary to maintain our prestigious academics. Award-winning programs like our robotics team are doing more with less, but the need is dire. Upgrading our schools will allow us to continue offering the best academic programs and ensure that the most qualified teachers will want to continue teaching here.
Your YES vote on Measure __ will help avoid expensive, temporary fixes, and help our high schools qualify for millions in state matching funds.

Vote Yes on __ to:
- Repair leaky roofs
- Replace failing plumbing, electrical, heating, and cooling systems
- Update failing school restrooms
- Improve school safety
- Provide up-to-date instructional tools for teachers
- Upgrade science, technology, arts, math, robotics, and career technical education classrooms and labs

Strict Accountability Keeps Measure __ Local:
- Every penny stays local — nothing can be taken by the State or Federal governments
- Annual independent audits and citizens' oversight ensure funds are spent as promised
- Measure __ can't fund administrator salaries

Essential repairs and upgrades can't wait. Help protect the high-quality education our community expects and our students deserve. Vote Yes on _!
