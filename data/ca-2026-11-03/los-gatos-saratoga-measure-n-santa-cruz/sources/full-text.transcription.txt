FULL TEXT BALLOT PROPOSITION OF THE LOS GATOS-SARATOGA UNION HIGH SCHOOL DISTRICT BOND MEASURE ELECTION NOVEMBER 3, 2026

The following is the full proposition presented to the voters by the Los Gatos-Saratoga Union High School District.

"To maintain quality academic facilities at Los Gatos High School and Saratoga High School by replacing leaky roofs and failing restrooms, plumbing, electrical, heating/ cooling systems; improving school safety; updating, repairing and equipping schools including science, technology, engineering, arts, math, robotics, and career/ college readiness classrooms/ labs; shall Los Gatos-Saratoga Union High School District's measure be adopted, authorizing $321,000,000 in bonds at legal rates, levying approximately $30 per $100,000 assessed value ($23,000,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding, with oversight, annual audits, and local control?"

PROJECT LIST

The Board of Trustees of the Los Gatos-Saratoga Union High School District is committed to maintaining high quality academics and programs at Los Gatos High School and Saratoga High School by repairing and upgrading aging school facilities and providing safe, secure, modern classrooms, labs and technology needed to support high quality instruction in math, science, engineering, technology, and other skilled trades. To that end, the Board evaluated the District's urgent and critical facility needs, including safety issues, class size reduction, basic repairs, computer and information technology in developing the types of projects to be funded by this measure as described below.

In approving this Project List, the Board of Trustees determines that the District must:

(i) Replace leaky roofs and improve school safety; and
(ii) Provide up-to-date instructional tools for teachers; and
(iii) Replace failing plumbing, electrical, heating and cooling systems; and
(iv) Adhere to specific fiscal accountability safeguards such as:
(a) Every penny must be spent on local high schools and cannot be spent on administrator salaries;
(b) Prohibit the State from taking any of the funds raised;
(c) Require annual independent financial and performance audits; and
(d) Require the review of all expenditures by an independent citizens' oversight committee and public disclosure of all spending.
