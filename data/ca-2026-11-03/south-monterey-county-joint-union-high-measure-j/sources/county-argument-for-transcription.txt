Argument in Favor of Measure J

Vote YES on Measure J to create two locally governed, community-based unified school districts—Greenfield Unified School District and King City Joint Unified School District.

This proposal gives both communities the opportunity to provide students with a seamless educational experience from TK to grade 12. Instead of transitioning between separate elementary and high school districts, students in King City and Greenfield will benefit from one continuous educational pathway. Teachers and administrators can work across all grade levels to align curriculum, strengthen student support, preserve and expand successful programs, and build pathways to college, careers and life beginning in the earliest grades. Successful programs can become part of a single TK-12 vision allowing students to build on what they learn through graduation.

This proposal is the result of years of careful study and received unanimous approval from both the Monterey County Committee on School District Organization and the California State Board of Education. The iBank debt that once prevented unification has been fully repaid. Today, there are no remaining financial barriers. Both new unified districts will begin on solid financial footing, with assets and liabilities divided fairly according to law.

A "YES" vote means stronger local governance, improved educational alignment, more efficient administration, and greater accountability to parents in King City and Greenfield. This measure keeps local schools under local leadership while preserving each community's identity and allowing educators to focus on what matters most: student success.

This is a once-in-a-generation opportunity to strengthen public education in South Monterey County. Please join us in supporting our students, our families, and the future of both King City and Greenfield.

Vote YES on Measure J.

Authors:
The Governing Board of the South Monterey County Joint Union High School District
The Governing Board of the Greenfield Union School District
