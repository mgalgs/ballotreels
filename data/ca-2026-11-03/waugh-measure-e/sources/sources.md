## Facts and quotes

**Ballot question (p1):** "To improve the quality of education; replace heating, ventilation and air-conditioning systems; modernize outdated classrooms, restrooms and school facilities; and make health, safety and security improvements, shall Waugh School District's measure authorizing $16,000,000 in bonds, at legal interest rates, and levying approximately $30 per $100,000 of assessed valuation (raising $917,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding, be adopted, with a board appointed citizens' oversight committee and annual independent audits to assure proper expenditure of funds?"

**Vote needed:** "55% Needed to Pass" (p1).

**Who votes:** voters of the Waugh School District ("If the bond measure is approved by at least 55% of the voters of the District voting on the bond measure", tax rate statement, p2).

**Impartial analysis (p2), key statements:**
- "The Waugh School District ("District") Board of Trustees ("Board") has placed on the ballot the question whether to issue $16,000,000 in bonds for such purposes."
- "As required by state law, Measure E prohibits using bond proceeds for teacher or administrator salaries or other operating expenses."
- "Bonds will be repaid with funds raised from an increase in property taxes based upon the assessed value of taxable property in the District through fiscal year 2060-61. The "Tax Rate Statement" ... estimates the highest tax rate necessary to fund the bonds is $30 per $100,000 of assessed value."
- "If all bonds are sold, the total debt services, including principal and interest, is estimated at $32,000,000."
- Signed Joshua A. Myers, County Counsel, by Adam Brand, Assistant County Counsel.

**Tax rate statement (p2):** average "$28.65 per $100,000 of assessed valuation", final year "fiscal year 2060-61"; highest "$30.00 per $100,000 of assessed valuation in fiscal year 2027-28"; total debt service "approximately $32,000,000". Dated June 9, 2026; signed Mike Gardner, Superintendent.

**Argument in favor (p3):** signed John Shribbs (Petaluma City Councilmember), Vanessa Chung (Waugh District Parent), Dr. Sabina Gupta Sud (Pediatric Dentist), Kristine Weeks (Waugh School Board), Jarrett Baglietto (Waugh School Board).

**Argument against:** "No Argument Was Submitted Against Measure E" (p3).
