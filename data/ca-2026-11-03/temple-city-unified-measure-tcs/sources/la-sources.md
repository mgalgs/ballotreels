## Ballot question and supporters/opponents (verbatim, from the county's final measures list)

> WITHOUT INCREASING EXISTING TAX RATES, to upgrade safety/security and HVAC systems; remove asbestos/lead pipes; repair/upgrade/equip aging classrooms, science labs, school/career technology facilities to support college/career readiness in math, science, technology, arts, medical science, skilled trades; shall Temple City Unified School District's measure be adopted authorizing $146,000,000 in bonds at legal rates, levying $43 per $100,000 assessed value (averaging $8,710,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding, with independent citizen oversight, audits, all money locally controlled?
>
> Supporters: William Man, TC Mayor; Ryan Liu, PCC Trustee; TC Schools Foundation; Jerry Jambazian; Tim Huang, Chinese Amer. Assn Pres.
>
> Opponents: None submitted.

Vote threshold: 55% of the Votes Cast (county list).
