ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE TCS

Vote YES on TCS to provide safe, updated Temple City Unified schools – without increasing taxes!

If we want kids to succeed in college and careers, they must be skilled in the use of today's technology and have advanced courses in science, math, engineering, and technology.

While our schools are among the best in the State academically, many facilities were built more than 70 years ago, when asbestos and lead pipes were standard construction materials. Roofs, plumbing, and electrical systems are aging, and classrooms need updated heating, air conditioning, and ventilation.

Whether or not you have children in the schools, high performing schools lead to higher property values. Voting YES on TCS helps our community stay strong.

Plus, all funds raised by Measure TCS stay right here—and cannot be taken by the State.

Vote YES on TCS: Safe and Updated Schools Without Increasing Taxes
- Repair deteriorating roofs, plumbing, sewer, and electrical systems
- Update classrooms and labs to support high quality instruction in math, science, arts, engineering, and technology
- Remove hazardous materials such as asbestos and lead pipes
- Improve student safety and bring classrooms up to current health, safety, and accessibility standards
- Provide modern career technical facilities so students are prepared for college and in-demand careers

Measure TCS Includes Strict Taxpayer Accountability
- All funds will stay here in neighborhood schools and cannot be taken by the State
- Independent citizens' oversight and mandatory independent annual audits are required
- No funds can be used for administrators' salaries or pensions

Measure TCS will not increase taxes; it simply renews our community commitment to excellent public schools, ensuring our schools stay competitive with other top performing districts in our region.

Join local teachers, parents, and community leaders in voting YES on TCS!
