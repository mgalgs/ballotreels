## Facts and quotes

- Measure letter and label (notice p. 3): "MEASURE U (APPOINTIVE CITY TREASURER)".
- Ballot question (notice p. 3): "Shall the office of the City Treasurer be Appointive?" (YES / NO).
- Statement printed with the question (notice p. 3): "The City Treasurer shall no longer be an elective official in the City of Livingston. From and after November 2028, or upon earlier vacancy in the office of City Treasurer, the City Treasurer shall be appointed by the City Council. The City Treasurer so appointed shall hold office at the pleasure of the City Council and, notwithstanding California Government Code section 36502 to the contrary, is not required to be a resident or elector in the City. The City Council may by Ordinance vest in the City Manager its authority to appoint the City Treasurer."
- Spanish (notice p. 4): "MEDIDA U (TESORERO MUNICIPAL POR DESIGNACIÓN) … ¿Debería el cargo de Tesorero Municipal ser de designación?"
- Placed on the ballot (agenda Jan 21, 2026, consent item 5): "Resolution Calling For A Municipal Election To Be Consolidated With The Statewide General Election On November 3, 2026, Seeking Voter Approval To Convert The Office Of City Treasurer To An Appointed Office, Setting Dates For The Submission Of Arguments In Favor Of Or Against The Ballot Measure, And Requesting The Assistance Of The County Of Merced In Connection With That Election."
- The office is also on the ballot (notice p. 1): "For City Treasurer … Short Term of Two Years … NO CANDIDATES".
