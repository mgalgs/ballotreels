Measure HP asks the voters of this city whether to limit council members to two terms in a lifetime.
Today, city law limits council members to three terms.
But someone who sits out one election cycle starts counting again, and can serve up to three more terms.
The City Council placed Measure HP on the ballot.
Measure HP would end that reset, so no one could serve more than two terms in a lifetime.
A partial term would count as a full term only if it ran more than two years.
Moving to another council district would not reset the count.
Terms served before November 3, 2026, would not count toward the limit.
Measure HP needs a majority yes vote to pass.
The city attorney's impartial analysis does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
The official county list of measures, which names who filed each argument, says no argument against Measure HP was submitted.
Supporters say the current rotating system creates career politicians, and that a lifetime limit opens the council to new voices.
A yes vote sets a two-term lifetime limit and ends the reset.
A no vote keeps the current three-term limit, with the reset.
