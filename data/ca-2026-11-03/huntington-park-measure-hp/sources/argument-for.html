ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE HP

VOTE YES on Measure HP to protect the integrity of our local democracy, bring accountability to City Hall, and ensure fresh perspectives on the Huntington Park City Council.

Our current system is broken. Right now, Huntington Park has a provision that allows politicians to serve three terms, step aside for just one election cycle, and then run all over again. This rotating system creates career politicians and prevents new leaders from stepping up to serve our community.

Measure HP fixes this by establishing a strict, absolute two-term lifetime limit for City Council members.

A YES vote on Measure HP will:

- End Career Politicians: Once a council member serves two terms, they are done for life. No more sitting out an election just to reset the clock.
- Encourage New Leadership: Lifetime limits guarantee opportunities for new voices, new ideas, and regular citizens to represent our neighborhoods.
- Protect Fair Representation: These rules apply to everyone equally, even as we transition to by-district elections. Moving from one district to another will not allow a politician to bypass the limit.

Importantly, this change is fair and legally compliant. Per state law, the countdown starts from zero for everyone at the November 2026 election, ensuring an equal playing field. It is time to bring true accountability to Huntington Park. Let's close the revolving door at City Hall and open up our government to the community.

Join a unanimous City Council, local residents, and community leaders in voting for honest government, structural reform, and true representation.
Vote YES on Measure HP!
