City of Colton Measure O asks Colton voters whether the city treasurer should be appointed instead of elected.
Today Colton voters elect the city treasurer to a four-year term, and the current term ends in November 2028.
State law lets Colton have an elected or an appointed treasurer, but a switch to appointed needs a majority of voters.
If Measure O passes, the City Council would appoint the next treasurer when the current term ends.
The council would set the education and training requirements, and review the treasurer's performance.
The San Bernardino County Voter Information Guide does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Supporters say Measure O ensures a qualified professional treasurer, screened for experience, education and a background check.
No argument against Measure O was submitted, so the county voter guide prints only the supporters' argument.
A yes vote makes the treasurer an appointed office, with no treasurer election in 2028 unless voters change it again.
A no vote keeps the treasurer an elected office, up for election again in November 2028.
