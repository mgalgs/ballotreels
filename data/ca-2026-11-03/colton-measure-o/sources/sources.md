## Quoted facts

- **Who votes:** Colton voters. Impartial analysis: "Measure "O" asks Colton voters whether the position of Colton City Treasurer should be an appointed position or remain an elected one."
- **Ballot question** (RES_CityofColton.pdf, Section 3): "Shall the office of Colton City Treasurer be appointive?"
- **Impartial analysis** (VIG p. 241, City Attorney): "On March 3, 2026, the City Council placed Measure "O" on the November 3, 2026 General Municipal Election ballot." … "If Measure "O" passes, the City Treasurer would be appointed by the Colton City Council and no longer elected by Colton voters. If Colton voters approve Measure "O", the City Council will appoint the next City Treasurer when the term of the current incumbent expires in November, 2028." … "a transition from an elected City Treasurer to an appointed City Treasurer must be approved by a majority of the City's voters voting at an election."
