               Argument in Favor of Measure O                                         Argument Against Measure O
 Strong neighborhood schools are one of our community's most valuable
 assets and deserve to be a top priority. Good schools raise student
 achievement, support local property values, prepare young people for
 successful futures, and contribute to safer neighborhoods. Our teachers
 and staff do excellent work, yet many classrooms and facilities in the Old
 Adobe Union School District have aged beyond what today's students
 need to learn and thrive.
 While the District has maintained its facilities responsibly, older
 classrooms and buildings need updating because many no longer meet
 modern educational and technology standards. Measure O would let the
 District upgrade local schools and strengthen the education students
 receive. Investing now lets us meet current safety, technology, and
 instructional standards while improving our community.
 If approved by voters, Measure O will fund essential facility
 improvements at local elementary schools, including: Repairing and
 replacing leaky roofs, making health, safety and security improvements       No Argument Was Submitted Against Measure O
 throughout the District, making accessibility (ADA) improvements,
 replacing outdated heating, ventilation and cooling systems to increase
 energy-efficiency, and modernizing outdated classrooms, restrooms,
 and school facilities, including multi-purpose rooms.
 Old Adobe Union School District is proud to have award-winning schools.
 Measure O protects academic facilities so our excellent instruction and
 performance standards can be maintained.
 Measure O is fiscally responsible and includes strong taxpayer
 protections. All funds stay local. The State Cannot take this funding
 away. Taxpayer protections are Required. No funds can be spent on
 administrators' salaries or pensions. Independent Citizens' Oversight
 and mandatory audits ensure funds are spent properly. For more
 information go to www.oldadobe.org.
 Measure O repairs and modernizes outdated, inadequate classrooms,
 improves local students' education, and protects the character of our
 community. It is something we can all stand behind. Please join us and
 Vote Yes On Measure O!
