## Facts and quotes

**Ballot question (p1):** "To improve the quality of education; repair/replace leaky roofs; make health, safety and security improvements; replace outdated HVAC systems; make accessibility (ADA) improvements, modernize classrooms, restrooms and school facilities, including multi-purpose rooms; shall Old Adobe Union School District's measure be adopted authorizing $48,000,000 of bonds at legal rates, generating on average $2,800,000 annually while bonds are outstanding at approximate rates of $25 per $100,000 assessed value, with annual audits, oversight, no money for salaries, all money staying local?"

**Vote needed:** "55% Needed to Pass" (p1); "...provided 55 percent of voters who vote on the measure approve the sale of the bonds." (p2)

**Who votes:** voters of the Old Adobe Union School District ("Bonds will be repaid from property taxes levied on real property within the District", p2).

**Impartial analysis (p2), key statements:**
- "As required by law, Measure O prohibits using bond proceeds for teacher or administrator salaries or other operating expenses."
- "The District's Tax Rate Statement ... estimates the highest tax rate necessary to fund the bonds is $25 per $100,000 of assessed value. Those tax levies are projections, and could go up or down".
- "If all bonds are sold, the total debt service (including principal and interest) will be $97,000,000. The tax will raise approximately $2,800,000 annually."
- "Those taxes would be in addition to current property taxes on real property within the District."
- Signed Joshua A. Myers, County Counsel, by Ethan Pawson, Deputy County Counsel.

**Tax rate statement (p2):** average "$24.15 per $100,000 of assessed valuation ... collected until fiscal year 2059-60"; highest "$25.00 per $100,000 ... projected to apply in fiscal years 2027-28 through 2055-56"; total debt service "approximately $97,000,000". Signed Michele Gochberg and Cindy Friberg, Co-Superintendents.

**Argument in favor (p3):** signed Kimberly Strickland (Attorney, PEF Board Member), Anna Dietrich (Parent, OAUSD Trustee), Renee McKenna (Parent, Petaluma Valley Rotary), Peter J. Walsh (Parent, OAUSD Trustee).

**Argument against:** "No Argument Was Submitted Against Measure O" (p3).
