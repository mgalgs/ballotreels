 E    PROPOSED COUNTY CHARTER AMENDMENT. COUNTY OF LOS ANGELES ETHICS
      COMMISSION AND COMMUNITY INVESTMENT BUDGET ALLOCATION CHARTER

      greater independence and structure for the County of Los Angeles Ethics Commission,
      Office of Ethics Compliance, and Ethics Compliance Officer, including appointment

      the County's commitment to community investment and alternatives to incarceration, as
      specified in the charter amendment ordinance, be adopted? Supporters: League of
      Women Voters of LA County; CA Clean Money; Supervisor Lindsey Horvath; CA
      Common Cause; AAPI Equity Alliance Opponents: None submitted.
