## Facts, with quotes

**Who votes / threshold.** Ordinance Section 1: "a special election is hereby called, proclaimed, and ordered to be consolidated with the statewide general election to be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, for the purpose of submitting to the eligible registered voters of the County of Los Angeles a proposed amendment to the Charter of the County of Los Angeles." Ordinance Section 2: "This measure (E) will become effective only if submitted to the voters at the election held on November 3, 2026, and only after approval by a majority of the qualified voters voting in the election on the issues."

**Ballot label** (ordinance Section 2):
> PROPOSED COUNTY CHARTER AMENDMENT. COUNTY OF LOS ANGELES ETHICS COMMISSION AND COMMUNITY INVESTMENT CHARTER AMENDMENT. Shall the measure, amending the Los Angeles County Charter to provide greater independence and structure for the County of Los Angeles Ethics Commission, Office of Ethics Compliance, and Ethics Compliance Officer, including appointment processes, qualifications, term, duties, and responsibilities, and to continue uninterrupted the County's commitment to community investment and alternatives to incarceration, as specified in the charter amendment ordinance, be adopted?

The RR/CC final list (p. 4) prints the title slightly differently: "COUNTY OF LOS ANGELES ETHICS COMMISSION AND COMMUNITY INVESTMENT BUDGET ALLOCATION CHARTER AMENDMENT", and "duties and responsibilities" without the comma. Which wording is on the printed ballot is not confirmed.

**Impartial analysis, key statements** (`m0001095910e.pdf`):
- "Measure E (Measure) is a proposed amendment to the Charter of the County of Los Angeles (Charter) placed on the ballot by ordinance and resolution of the County of Los Angeles (County) Board of Supervisors."
- "Amend Article III-D of the Charter, by repealing Sections 11.68 and 11.70 and adding Sections 11.67.00, 11.67.50, 11.68.00, 11.68.50, 11.69.00, 11.69.50, and 11.70.00, to provide greater independence and structure for the County Ethics Commission (Commission) and the Office of Ethics Compliance"
- "Establishing annual funding requirements for the Commission and the Office of Ethics Compliance that provide at least $14.3 million dollars for the Commission's and Office of Ethics Compliance's annual operating budget starting with the 2027-2028 fiscal year"
- "Requiring both the County Board of Supervisors and the County Executive to continue to allocate ten percent (10%) of the County's locally generated unrestricted revenues for direct community investment and alternatives to incarceration; and - Providing that the aforementioned allocation is not subject to veto by the County Executive."

**Full text, selected** (`m0001095940e.pdf`): "The Commission shall have seven (7) members." "Pay a monetary penalty to the General Fund of the County of up to fifteen thousand dollars ($15,000) for each violation, adjusted annually … or three (3) times the amount which the person failed to report properly or unlawfully contributed, expended, gave, or received, whichever is greater."

**Fiscal impact** (`m0001095975e.pdf`, Oscar Valdez, County Auditor-Controller):
- "This continues an existing budget allocation and does not alter current County revenues or expenditures." (about the 10% allocation)
- "This measure requires the County Board of Supervisors (Board) to appropriate a minimum of $14.3 million for the Ethics Commission's and Office of Ethics Compliance's annual operating budget, beginning in Fiscal Year 2027-28."
- "The measure does not identify a funding/revenue source for these expenditures."

**Argument in favor** signers: Fatima Malik (League of Women Voters of LA County); Trent Lange (CA Clean Money); Lindsey P. Horvath (LA County Supervisor); Darius Kemp (CA Common Cause); Alton Wang (AAPI Equity Alliance).
