## Quoted passages

- Question (p.22): "Shall Ordinance No.1618, amending the land uses for property located South of I-10 and the Union Pacific Railroad and West of Sunset Avenue allowing General Commercial, Industrial, Open Space — Parks and Resources land uses on the property and pre-zoning territory within the City's Sphere of Influence located South of the North Boundary of Western Avenue and West of Sunset Avenue for General Commercial, Industrial, Open Space — Parks and Resources land uses, be adopted?"
- What it is / who placed it (p.43): "Measure U is a referendum regarding City of Banning Ordinance No. 1618 which was adopted by the Banning City Council on April 14, 2026, (the “Ordinance”)." … "The Measure was placed on the ballot by a referendum petition signed by the requisite number of voters."
- Effect (p.43): "If Measure U is approved, the Ordinance will become effective. If the Measure U is not approved, the Ordinance will not become effective."
- Analysis (p.43): "If the Ordinance is approved by the voters the owner of the property, NP Banning Industrial, LLC will be able to build a project that includes General Commercial (GC), Industrial (I), Open Space - Parks (OS-P), and Open Space - Resources (OS-R) land uses pursuant to the Sunset Crossroads Specific Plan adopted pursuant to Resolution 2026-29."
- Threshold (p.43): "A “Yes” vote by a majority of those voting on Measure U means Ordinance No. 1618 will become effective."
