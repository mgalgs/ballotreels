City of Banning Measure U asks voters in the city whether to approve a zoning change for land west of Sunset Avenue.
The City Council adopted the zoning change in April 2026.
Voters then signed a referendum petition, so the change takes effect only if voters approve it.
Today the land is zoned for homes, general commercial use, parks and open space.
Measure U would zone it for general commercial, industrial, park and open space uses, under the Sunset Crossroads plan.
The owner of the property could then build a project with those uses.
The Riverside County guide does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Supporters say the project would create more than 3,700 permanent jobs, and would bring new shopping and dining closer to home.
Opponents say nearly 5 million square feet of warehouses means more trucks, pollution and traffic, and Banning can grow without them.
A yes vote means Ordinance 1618 will take effect.
A no vote means Ordinance 1618 will not take effect, and the zoning stays as it is.
