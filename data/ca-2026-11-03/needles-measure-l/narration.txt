City of Needles Measure L asks Needles voters whether the city may borrow up to $11,200,000 through bonds for streets, parks and other public works.
The bonds would be repaid by a property tax on property in the city.
Measure L needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The money could repair streets, potholes and sidewalks, improve parks and public buildings, and help revitalize downtown Main Street.
Yearly independent audits would check that the money goes only to these projects.
The tax rate statement estimates the tax at $60 a year per $100,000 of assessed value.
The statement puts the total to be repaid, with interest, at about $25.16 million.
No argument against Measure L was filed, so the county voter guide prints only the argument of supporters.
Supporters say Measure L repairs streets and potholes, with taxpayer protections: no money for salaries, and yearly audits.
A yes vote would authorize the city to sell up to $11,200,000 in bonds for these repairs.
A no vote would not authorize the bonds.
