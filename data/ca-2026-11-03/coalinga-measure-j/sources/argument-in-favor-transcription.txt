ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE J

Vote YES on Measure J to protect locally controlled funding for the essential services and community improvements Coalinga residents rely upon.

Measure J is not a new tax. It continues an important source of locally generated revenue approved by Coalinga voters. Every dollar raised by Measure J stays in Coalinga and cannot be taken by the State or used by another community.

Measure J has helped fund additional police officers and firefighters, public-safety vehicles, fire engines and the City's Emergency Operations Center. It has also supported community investments, including the new Splash Pad on Fifth Street and the planned Food Court on Elm Avenue between Fourth and Fifth streets.

Continuing Measure J will help Coalinga:

• Maintain police, fire and emergency-response services
• Recruit and retain qualified public-safety personnel
• Replace aging police, fire and emergency vehicles
• Maintain streets, parks and public facilities
• Invest in projects that strengthen our local economy and quality of life

Measure J benefits everyone who lives, works, shops or operates a business in Coalinga. Visitors and travelers also contribute when they make taxable purchases within the city, helping reduce the burden on local residents.

Strong public safety, dependable City services and well-maintained community facilities are essential to Coalinga's future. They help protect our families, support local businesses and make our community a better place to live and work.

Measure J keeps local dollars under local control and allows Coalinga to continue investing in the priorities that matter most to our community.

Please join Coalinga's business and public-safety leaders in voting YES on Measure J this November.
