## Facts

**Ballot question.** City Council Resolution No. 4337, Section 4 "Form of Measure", PDF page 2:
> "Shall the measure for The City of Coalinga that seeks to extend its general-purpose, one-cent sales tax, Measure J, which is expected to generate $2,000,000 annually, representing approximately 25% of the City's General Fund revenues, with annual citizen review and independent audits to April 1, 2039 be adopted?"

**Synopsis.** Same, Section 7, PDF page 2:
> "The proposed tax measure renews the previously voter-approved 1% sales tax (one cent for every dollar spent) within the City of Coalinga which generates $2,000,000 annually. Adoption of this renewal does not increase the sale tax above current levels. ... The renewal will extend the expiration of the sales tax to April 1, 2039. The tax measure was placed on the ballot by the Coalinga City Council."

**Impartial analysis.** Letter from Mario U. Zamora, Griswold, LaSalle, Cobb, Dowd & Gin, LLP, City Attorney for the City of Coalinga, Aug 17 2026 (filed Aug 18 2026):
> "The proposed tax measure extends the length of the current sales tax within the City of Coalinga of 1% (one cent for every dollar spent) to 2039. Funds generated by the revenue will continue to be allocated to the General Fund, where the residents voice their preference of spending to the City Council. Funds may continue to be used for any general fund purpose. The renewal will extend the expiration of the sales tax to April 1, 2039. The tax measure was placed on the ballot by the Coalinga City Council."

**Who votes.** Voters of the City of Coalinga. Resolution Section 7: "the following measure is to be voted on at the special municipal election to be held in the City of Coalinga".

**Vote needed.** County page heading: "Majority (50%+1) Required to Pass".
