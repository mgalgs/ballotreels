Argument Against City of Coalinga Sales Tax Renewal Measure

Fresno County

City of Coalinga

November 3, 2026 Consolidated Statewide General Election

Word Count: 294

Text of Argument Below This Line

================================================================

How has your cost of living changed in the last few years? What about your neighbors? Before you vote on this higher sales tax for the City of Coalinga, please think about a few important points:

This tax will collect about $2,000,000 every year from the people of Coalinga until 2039.

You might feel your share is worth paying because it promises to continue and improve emergency services. Safety services matter, and wanting to support them shows you care about your community. But we ask you to think about other ways to fund these projects. If you are willing to give up your money through a tax, would you also be willing to give it through voluntary charity instead?

Think again of your neighbors: many have struggled in recent years, and even a 1% sales tax can be a heavy burden. That means less money for groceries, for college, or even paying medical bills after an emergency.

A sales tax treats every shopper the same, no matter their income. A parent working two jobs pays the same extra tax on basic items as someone with far more resources. Voluntary charity works differently: people give what they can, when they can.

This tax would stay in place for many years, and once a tax is approved, it is rarely easy to change or remove. Families may face new challenges in the future. Voluntary giving lets people adjust their support as their own finances change.

We respect people who want to raise money for a good cause. We simply ask that they try voluntary giving first, especially when so many families are already facing hard times.

Let your neighbors choose which causes they want to support, and vote NO on Measure J.

Join us: https://lpfresno.org/NoCoalingaTax
