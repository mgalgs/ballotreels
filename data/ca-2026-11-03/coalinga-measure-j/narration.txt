Measure J asks the voters of this city whether to extend its 1 percent sales tax for ten more years.
Voters approved the 1 percent sales tax in 2018, and it is set to expire on April 1, 2029.
Measure J would keep the rate at 1 percent, and move the end date to April 1, 2039.
The money would still go to the general fund of the city, for any general purpose.
A citizens advisory committee would review the spending plans each year, with an annual independent audit.
Measure J needs a simple majority of the votes to pass.
The ballot question says the tax brings in about $2 million a year, about 25 percent of the general fund revenues of the city.
Supporters say Measure J is not a new tax, and it has helped pay for more police officers, firefighters and fire engines.
Opponents say even a 1 percent sales tax can be a heavy burden on families, and voluntary giving should come first.
A yes vote extends the 1 percent sales tax to April 1, 2039.
A no vote leaves the tax set to expire on April 1, 2029.
