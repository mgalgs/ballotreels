City of Oceanside Measure R asks voters in the city whether to change charter rules on pay and labor agreements for public works.
The city charter, approved by voters in 2010, generally exempts Oceanside from state public works contracting laws.
The City Council placed Measure R on the ballot, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
Measure R would write the state's prevailing wage requirements for public works into the charter.
It would also allow, but not require, the City Council to use project labor agreements on city projects.
Project labor agreements are pre-hire agreements with unions that set the terms of work on a project.
The San Diego County guide does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Opponents say the city already qualifies for state funding, and that project labor agreements raise costs and exclude local contractors.
Supporters say Measure R aligns the charter with state laws, so Oceanside stays eligible for its share of state infrastructure money.
A yes vote favors amending the Oceanside City Charter.
A no vote opposes amending the Oceanside City Charter.
