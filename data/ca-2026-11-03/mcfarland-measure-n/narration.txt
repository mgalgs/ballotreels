Measure N asks the voters of this city whether to adopt a city charter.
The state constitution lets a city adopt a charter for its own governance, by a majority vote of its voters.
The City Council proposed the charter, and appointed an advisory committee to help draft it.
The council held two public hearings on it, in March and April 2026.
The ballot question says the charter would provide local control over municipal affairs, while staying subject to state and federal law.
Measure N needs a majority vote to pass.
The county elections page for this election does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Supporters say the charter lets local officials accountable to local voters decide local affairs, rather than relying only on one-size-fits-all state laws.
Opponents say the charter gives the city government too much authority, while it weakens competitive bidding, taxpayer protections and public accountability.
A yes vote adopts the proposed city charter.
A no vote does not adopt the proposed city charter.
