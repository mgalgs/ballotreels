- Publisher: City of McFarland (City Clerk Erika De La Cruz)
- Title: Resolution No. 2026-75, "Amending Resolution No. 2026-50 to Correct the
  Election Date for the Submission of a Proposed City Charter to the Voters at the
  Tuesday, November 3, 2026 Statewide General Election", adopted June 10, 2026
- URL: https://media-002-us.cdn.govstack.com/mcfarlandcity-us/media/miad3kkc/resolution-no-2026-75-amending-resolutions-no-2026-50-to-correct-the-election-date-for-the-submission-of-a-proposed-city-chart.pdf
  (listed at https://www.mcfarlandcity.org/council-administration/city-clerk/charter-city/charter-city-agendas-notices-resolutions/)
- Facts:
  - Ballot question (§3): "Shall the City of McFarland adopt a City Charter that
    would provide local control over municipal affairs while remaining subject to
    state and federal law?"
  - Who votes (§2): "orders the submission of the proposed City Charter to the
    voters of the City of McFarland at the Tuesday, November 3, 2026 statewide
    general election."
  - Placed by the council: "Government Code section 34458 authorizes the
    legislative body of a city, on its own motion, to propose the adoption of a
    city charter"
  - Hearings: "Public Hearing No. 1: March 11, 2026 / Public Hearing No. 2: April
    22, 2026"
  - Vote: Ayon, Cano, Pérez, Gutierrez aye; Gonzalez absent; "PASSED AND ADOPTED
    at a regular meeting ... on June 10, 2026"
