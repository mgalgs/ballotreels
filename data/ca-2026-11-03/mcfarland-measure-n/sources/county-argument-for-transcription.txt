Argument in Favor of Measure N
Adopt the McFarland City Charter
A YES vote on Measure N gives McFarland greater local control over its future while keeping all the protections and services residents expect from their city government.
The proposed City Charter serves as McFarland's local constitution. It allows decisions about local municipal affairs to be made by local elected officials accountable to McFarland voters rather than relying solely on one-size-fits-all state laws designed for cities across California.
A charter city remains subject to state and federal laws on matters of statewide concern, including public safety, environmental protection, labor laws, and constitutional rights. However, the charter provides additional flexibility in areas such as municipal contracting, election administration, governance, and other local affairs where local solutions often work best.
The proposed charter was developed through a transparent process that included citizen advisory committee meetings, public hearings, and opportunities for community input. It reflects McFarland's values, priorities, and commitment to responsible local government.
Adopting a charter can help the City respond more effectively to local needs, pursue economic development opportunities, improve efficiency, and provide greater certainty in municipal operations. Most importantly, it keeps decision-making closer to the people most affected by those decisions: the residents of McFarland.
This measure does not create a new government. It does not eliminate voter rights. It does not affect existing constitutional protections. Instead, it provides McFarland with greater authority to manage its own municipal affairs while remaining fully accountable to local voters.
A YES vote is a vote for local control, local accountability, and local decision-making.
Vote YES on Measure N.
Submitted by:
Mayor Saul Ayon
Vice Mayor Ricardo Cano
Coach Jim White
