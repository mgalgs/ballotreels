Measure O asks the voters of this school district whether to replace their parcel tax with a tax of $310 a parcel for seven years.
In 2022, the district's voters approved a parcel tax of $214.10 a parcel, adjusted each year, for seven years.
Measure O needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The 2022 tax would stop, and the new tax of $310 a parcel would run each year from July 1, 2027, for seven years.
The money could pay only for listed purposes, such as reading, math and science, special education, and pay for teachers and staff.
Owners aged 65 or older who live on their parcel could apply for an exemption, and so could some residents with disabilities.
The ballot question says the tax would raise $6.5 million a year.
Supporters say Measure O protects the teachers, services and programs already at work in schools, and its funds cannot be taken by the state.
No argument against Measure O was filed, so the list of local measures from the county shows only the argument in favor of it.
A yes vote replaces the existing parcel tax with the new $310 tax for seven years, beginning July 1, 2027.
A no vote does not replace the tax.
