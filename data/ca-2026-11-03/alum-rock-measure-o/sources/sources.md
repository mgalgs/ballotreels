# Measure O — Alum Rock Union Elementary School District — Education parcel tax, $310 per parcel for seven years (replaces Measure I)

- **Letter:** O, confirmed by the district's news post "Alum Rock November 3, 2026 Ballot Measures" ("Measure O Parcel Tax - Educational Programs & Services") and its Measure O FAQ.
- **Jurisdiction:** Alum Rock Union Elementary School District.
- **Kind:** qualified special tax (parcel tax), placed by the Board of Trustees.
- **Who votes:** voters of the District.
- **Threshold:** two-thirds. Resolution p. 1: the Law "authorizes a school district, upon approval by two-thirds (2/3) of the electorate voting on the measure, to levy a qualified special tax"; p. 7: "Subject to two-thirds approval of the voters".
- **Status: agenda version.** The archived Resolution No. 1-26/27 is the "FINAL FOR AGENDA" file posted under the July 16, 2026 board meeting. Its signature block (p. 6) says it was adopted "on July 16, 2026" but the vote lines (AYES/NOES/ABSENT/ABSTAIN) are blank and it is unsigned. Adoption is consistent with the district's later news post naming Measure O, but the signed resolution was not found.

## Ballot question (resolution p. 11, Exhibit B "ABBREVIATED FORM OF MEASURE", "*For Ballot Label. Limited to 75 words or less.")

> With funds that cannot be taken by State or Federal Government officials, shall Alum Rock Union Elementary School District's measure to protect services for students with disabilities; improve reading, writing, math and science programs; attract and retain highly qualified teachers; and support students experiencing housing instability be adopted, by replacing the current tax at $310 per parcel for seven years, while raising $6.5 million annually with senior exemptions, and public disclosure of all spending?

From the board's agenda version; not confirmed against the printed ballot.

## Full text (Exhibit A, pp. 7–10)

> ... qualified special tax at the rate of $310 per parcel on all assessor's parcels, replacing the current ... (p. 7)

> It is intent of the voters that the tax be levied for a period of seven ... (p. 7; the tax "shall expire on June 30, 2034")

Senior exemption (p. 8): "An exemption shall be available to persons who are 65 years or older and own and occupy as a principal residence a parcel subject to the education parcel tax." The same page also provides an SSI disability exemption.

Replacement (resolution p. 5, §15): "the educational parcel tax shall replace the Measure I Parcel Tax during the remaining term thereof, and the District shall cease to levy and collect the Measure I Parcel Tax commencing with fiscal year 2027-28." The recital (p. 1) says Measure I was approved "at an election held on June 7, 2022" at "$214.10 per parc[el]".

FAQ (informational): "If approved by at least two-thirds of votes cast, Measure O will replace the district's existing parcel tax. It will raise approximately $6.5 million annually for seven years".
