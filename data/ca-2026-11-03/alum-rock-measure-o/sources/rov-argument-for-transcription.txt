[Original page 2]

Alum Rock Union School District
Argument in Favor of Measure O
Academic Programs and Student Support Measure

Alum Rock's elementary schools are where children learn, grow, and receive the support to reach their full potential. Our schools help students build strong academic foundations, support student well-being, and provide opportunities for every child to succeed.

Measure O is a local, dedicated funding source that replaces the current parcel tax approved by voters in 2022. It protects the critical academic programs, services, and people our students depend on every day.

Your YES vote on Measure O will:

• Protect services and programs that students with disabilities rely on every day.
• Provide academic support services to improve reading, writing, math and science comprehension so every student develops the fundamentals for success.
• Provide competitive salaries and benefits for teachers to retain and recruit strong educators
• Recruit and retain strong teachers, staff and counselors who are dedicated to helping our students succeed.
• Support English Language Learner programs.
• Improve science, technology, engineering, arts and mathematics programs.
• Provide programs and services for foster youth students and those experiencing housing instability to help students stay on track.

Measure O includes strict taxpayer protections:

• Measure O funds cannot be taken by State or Federal government officials and spent elsewhere.
• Public disclosure of all spending is required, ensuring responsible use of funds.
• No funds for administrators' salaries, pensions, or benefits
• Homeowners age 65+ can apply for an exemption.
• Funds will directly support programs and services for local students.

Your YES vote on Measure O protects the teachers, services and programs already at work in our schools. Every child deserves a strong start. Don't let budget cuts take away dedicated teachers, vital services, and academic programs that students rely on every day.

Join teachers, parents, guardians, grandparents, neighbors, business owners, and community leaders in voting YES on Measure O!
