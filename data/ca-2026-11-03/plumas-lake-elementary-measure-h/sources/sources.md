## Key facts, quoted

- **Ballot question** (p. 1): "To construct/furnish a gymnasium, an essential component of the new Plumas Lake Junior High School, all prior components of which have been funded from builder fees and State funds and not from any tax burden on District property owners, shall the Plumas Lake Elementary School District issue $9,000,000 in bonds at legal rates, annually levying approximately $12 per $100,000 of assessed valuation, generating approximately $426,000 annually while bonds are outstanding, with strict accountability including audits and public spending disclosures?"
- **Threshold** (analysis): "The Resolution requires approval by at least 55% of the registered voters voting on the Resolution for passage." Placed by: "The Resolution was placed on the ballot by the District Board of Trustees."
- **Analysis** (p. 3, signed "Janet Bender, Yuba County Counsel"): "Resolution 25-17 (the "Resolution") seeks voter approval for the Plumas Lake Elementary School District (the "District") to issue and sell bonds in the maximum principal amount of up to $9 million at interest rates not to exceed the legal limits." Projects "include the construction and furnishing of a new gymnasium for Plumas Lake Junior High School." "The Resolution would require that the District will conduct annual audits and establish an independent citizens' oversight committee". "The tax will be levied at an estimated annual tax rate of $12 per $100,000 of assessed valuation, generating approximately $426,000 annually while bonds are outstanding."
- **Tax rate statement** (p. 4, "Dated: May 16th, 2026", signed "/s/ Jeff D. Roberts, Superintendent, Plumas Lake Elementary School District"): average "$12.54 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. The final fiscal year in which the tax is anticipated to be collected is 2056-57."; highest "$19 per $100,000 of assessed valuation … levied in fiscal year 2028-29 through 2032-33"; total debt service "approximately $17.5 million".
- **Argument in favor:** "No Argument in Favor of Measure H Filed".
- **Argument against** signers: Johanna Lassaga, Chair, Yuba County Republican Central Committee; John Barnes, PLESD School Board Trustee; Lance Christensen, Yuba County Resident. Its statements (e.g. voters rejected "three separate $18 million bond measures in November 2024") are the authors'; not checked against official results.
- **Rebuttals:** none printed (with no argument in favor, there is nothing to rebut on one side; no rebuttal to the argument against appears).
- The document notes: "Arguments and rebuttals are the opinions of the authors. Arguments are printed exactly as submitted."
