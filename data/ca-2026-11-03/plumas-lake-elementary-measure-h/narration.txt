Measure H asks the voters of this school district whether to approve $9,000,000 in bonds to build a gymnasium.
The district would sell the bonds and repay them from a tax on property in the district.
Measure H needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would build and furnish a new gymnasium at the district's new junior high school, with the utilities and disabled access it needs.
The bond money could not pay teacher or administrator salaries or other operating costs.
The district's tax rate statement estimates $12.54 a year per $100,000 of assessed value, on average, through 2056-2057.
The statement estimates the highest rate at $19 per $100,000, from 2028-2029 through 2032-2033.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $17.5 million.
Opponents say voters repeatedly rejected district bonds, and the district already found another way to build a smaller school without a bond.
No argument in favor of Measure H was filed, so the measure pages from county elections print only the argument against the measure.
A yes vote authorizes the district to issue $9,000,000 in bonds for the gymnasium.
A no vote does not authorize the district to issue the bonds.
