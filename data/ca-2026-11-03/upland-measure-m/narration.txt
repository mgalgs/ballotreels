City of Upland Measure M asks Upland voters whether to change how the city taxes businesses.
Upland last updated its business license tax in 1994.
Today the tax is capped at $864 for most businesses taking in over $1.4 million.
Measure M would base the tax mainly on gross receipts.
Businesses taking in under $100,000 a year would pay a flat $75.
Above that, the rate would depend on the kind of business, with a cap of $28,000 a year.
The new rates would start in 2028 and last until voters end them.
The city attorney's impartial analysis estimates $3.3 million a year, $2.1 million more than the current tax.
Supporters say Measure M makes the largest businesses pay their share, and lowers taxes for most small businesses.
Opponents say Measure M triples the business tax, and that businesses will pass the cost on to consumers.
A yes vote approves this change to the business license tax.
A no vote does not approve this change to the business license tax.
