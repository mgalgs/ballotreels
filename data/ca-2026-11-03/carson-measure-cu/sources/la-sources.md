## County measure list entry

From `county-measure-list-excerpt.txt` (LA County RR/CC final measures list):

> CU Carson City Government Accountability, Charter Update, No Tax Increase Measure. Shall the measure amending the Carson City Charter require campaign contribution transparency; make the City Charter consistent with State law on referring ballot measures for voter approval; update term limits to increase voter options; update City Treasurer duties; clarify and clean up inconsistent or outdated language, be adopted?
>
> Supporters: Lula Davis-Holmes, Mayor; Cedric Hicks, Mayor Pro Tem; Jawane Hilton, Councilmember; Arleen Rojas, Councilmember; Darrow's New Orleans Grill.
> Opponents: None submitted.

Vote threshold: Majority 50%+1.

## What's present vs. missing

- **Impartial analysis**: present (`impartial-analysis.pdf`).
- **Argument in favor**: **missing — not found/not fetched.** The county list names five supporters, so an argument was presumably filed, but neither `resolution.pdf` nor `arguments-filing-resolution.pdf` contains the actual argument text, and it was not found as a separate posted document on the city's page. The council agenda-packet staff report rendered as an HTML viewer rather than a downloadable PDF, so it was not saved either.
- **Argument against**: **missing — not filed.** The RR/CC list states "Opponents: None submitted," so no argument against exists to find.
- **Rebuttal to argument for / rebuttal to argument against**: **not applicable** — with no opposing argument filed, there is nothing to rebut on either side.
- **Full text**: present — the charter sections being amended (§301, §305, §306, §319, §320, §502, §907) are detailed in both the resolution and the impartial analysis.
- **Tax rate statement / fiscal**: **not applicable.** The measure explicitly imposes no tax increase; it is a governance/charter-cleanup measure.
