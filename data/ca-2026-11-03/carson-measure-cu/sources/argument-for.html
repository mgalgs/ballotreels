ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE CU

VOTE YES ON MEASURE CU, THE GOVERNMENT ACCOUNTABILITY, CHARTER UPDATE, NO TAX INCREASE MEASURE TO SUPPORT GOOD GOVERNMENT FOR THE COMMUNITY OF CARSON.

The City of Carson is forward-thinking and looking toward what is best for its residents. As a charter city, it is important that the City keep up with the evolution of resident needs and evolve your government by providing an opportunity for the voters to update our City Charter. The Government Accountability, Charter Update, No Tax Increase Measure will make necessary changes to modernize our government.

Measure CU will update the Charter and provide the following benefits:

- Reinforces campaign disclosure requirements
- Updates contracting practices to be more efficient
- Updates term limits to let voters choose who they want on city council
- Better empowers voters to decide the fate of special taxes
- Prioritizes professional investment of taxpayer resources

Business people, community volunteers and elected leaders agree that Measure CU delivers much needed updates to the Carson City Charter.

MEASURE CU DOES NOT CREATE A TAX. It is about governance and empowering our voters.

Carson must always strive to be efficient and follow best practices. That means we need to revise our City's Charter from time to time to make that possible. Now is that time.

Measure CU improves government efficiency.
Measure CU increases transparency.
Measure CU increases the power of voters.
Measure CU protects our city investments.

Vote YES on Measure CU for a brighter future for Carson.
