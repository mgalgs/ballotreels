San Mateo County Measure J asks county voters whether to give the Board of Supervisors more time to fill a vacant elected county office.
Today, when an elected county office other than a seat on the board falls vacant, the board has 30 days to appoint someone or call a special election.
If the board does not act in time, the county elections officer must call a special election.
No public meeting is required before the board appoints someone.
Measure J would extend the deadline from 30 days to 60 days.
Before any appointment, the board would hold at least one public meeting for comment on the appointment and the candidates.
The Board of Supervisors placed Measure J on the ballot, and it needs a majority of the vote to pass.
The published measure documents of San Mateo County do not include a cost estimate for the measure.
No argument against Measure J appears in the published measure documents of San Mateo County.
No argument for Measure J appears in the published measure documents of San Mateo County.
A yes vote means the charter would be amended to allow 60 days and require a public meeting.
A no vote means the charter would not be amended, and the deadline stays 30 days.
