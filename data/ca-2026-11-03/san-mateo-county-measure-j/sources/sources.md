# Measure J — County of San Mateo — Charter amendment (Sec. 415: filling vacancies)

- **Jurisdiction:** County of San Mateo (countywide).
- **Kind:** County Charter amendment, placed by the Board of Supervisors (Resolution No. 081882 and Ordinance No. 4920). It is the "Third Measure" of four in that resolution (L, AA, J, U).
- **Who votes:** all county voters. Resolution Sec. 9(b), PDF p. 8: "All qualified voters in San Mateo County shall be permitted to vote in the special election on the Charter amendments set forth in the Measures."
- **Threshold:** simple majority. Resolution Sec. 6, PDF p. 8: each Measure "will become effective only if submitted to the voters at the Countywide special election on November 3, 2026, and only after approval by a majority (i.e., 50% + 1) of the qualified voters voting in the special election."

## Ballot question (verbatim)

Resolution Sec. 4(a), PDF p. 5 (repeated in Ordinance Sec. 8(a), PDF p. 17), under the heading "COUNTY CHARTER AMENDMENT":

> Shall the San Mateo County Charter be amended to extend the period in which the County Board of Supervisors is required to either call a special election or make an appointment in the event of a vacancy in an elected office other than the Board of Supervisors from 30 days to 60 days, and to require the Board to conduct at least one public meeting to receive public comment prior to any appointment?

## Full text

- **Resolution No. 081882**, Board of Supervisors, County of San Mateo. PDF p. 11: "Regularly passed and adopted this 9th day of June, 2026". AYES: "JACKIE SPEIER", "NOELIA CORZO", "RAY MUELLER", "LISA GAUTHIER", "DAVID J. CANEPA". NOES: "NONE".
- **Ordinance No. 4920**, calling the election. PDF p. 21: "Regularly passed and adopted this 9th day of June, 2026"; same five AYES, NOES "NONE". Introduced "at its regular meeting on May 19, 2026" (resolution recital, PDF p. 2).
- **What it changes** (Resolution Sec. 4(b), PDF p. 5): the Measure "would amend and replace in its entirety the first paragraph of Section 415 of Article IV of the Charter". New text, PDF pp. 5-6, includes: "If a vacancy occurs in an elective office other than the Board of Supervisors, the Board shall within 60 days of the effective date of the vacancy, either make an appointment or order the calling of a special election to fill the vacancy. In the event that the Board elects to make an appointment, the Board shall hold at least one noticed, public meeting for the purpose of receiving public comment regarding the appointment and qualifications of potential candidates." and "If the Board does not make an appointment or call a special election within 60 days, the county officer responsible for conducting county elections shall immediately order a special election to be called to fill the vacancy."
- Sec. 6, PDF p. 8: "Each Measure may be voted on separately without prejudice to the other Measures".
- Sec. 9(g), PDF p. 9: "The County Attorney's Office shall prepare the impartial analysis of the Measures."

## Impartial analysis

`impartial-analysis.pdf`, p. 1. Headed "IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE [TBD]". **No author or signature is printed** on it; the resolution assigns it to the County Attorney's Office. It ends "(500 word limit. 374 words)".

- "Approval of Measure [TBD] ("Measure") would amend Section 415 of Article IV of the Charter to extend, from 30 days to 60 days, the period in which the Board must either make an appointment or call a special election to fill a vacancy in an elective office other than the Board, and to require a public meeting before any appointment is made."
- "Section 415 currently requires the Board, within 30 days of a vacancy occurring in an elective office other than a Member of the Board, to either make an appointment or order a special election to fill the vacancy."
- "Section 415 does not currently require a public meeting before the Board makes an appointment."
- "If the Board does not make an appointment or call a special election within 60 days, the county officer responsible for conducting elections would still be required to immediately order a special election, as under current law."
- "The Measure would not change or otherwise affect the other provisions of Section 415 of the Charter, including the timing and conduct of any special election called to fill a vacancy, and the procedures governing an elected officer's resignation."
- "Measure requires approval by a majority of the qualified voters voting in the election." (printed without "This" at the start)
