## Quoted facts

- **Who votes:** City of Ontario voters. Impartial analysis: "If approved by a majority of Ontario voters, this Measure would authorize a one-half percent (1/2%) retail transactions and use (sales) tax within the City of Ontario."
- **Ballot question** (RES_Ontario.pdf, Section 3): "To maintain fire, paramedic, police, 911 response, anti-gang programs; respond to property crimes, thefts, burglaries; clean and maintain public areas; repair streets/potholes; address homelessness; increase access to affordable housing for seniors/families/individuals; retain/attract local businesses; for general City services; shall the measure establishing a half-cent sales tax providing approximately $32,500,000 annually until ended by voters; requiring audits, spending disclosure, funds locally controlled, be adopted?"
- **Impartial analysis** (VIG p. 206, City Attorney): "On August 7, 2026, the City Council voted to place Measure "H" on the November 3, 2026 General Municipal Election ballot." … "A one-half percent (1/2%) rate equates to an extra 50¢ per $100 purchased. It is estimated that Measure "H" will provide an additional $32,500,000 in annual local funding" … "This tax would be a "general tax," meaning that revenues raised from the tax would go into the City's general fund to pay for any lawful City program, improvement, or service."
- **Arguments:** the guide prints an argument in favor, an argument against, and a rebuttal to each (VIG pp. 206–209).
