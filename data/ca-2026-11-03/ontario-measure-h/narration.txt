City of Ontario Measure H asks Ontario voters whether to add a half-cent sales tax.
The City Council placed Measure H on the ballot, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
Shoppers in Ontario would pay an extra 50 cents for every $100 of purchases.
The tax would start in April 2027 and last until voters repeal it.
Measure H is a general tax: the money would go into the general fund, for any lawful city service.
The city attorney's impartial analysis estimates the tax would raise $32.5 million a year.
Opponents say Measure H brings Ontario the highest sales tax in San Bernardino County, and another blank check.
Supporters say Measure H keeps fire, paramedic and emergency services going, and fixes the local streets and potholes.
A yes vote authorizes the half-cent sales tax.
A no vote does not authorize the half-cent sales tax.
