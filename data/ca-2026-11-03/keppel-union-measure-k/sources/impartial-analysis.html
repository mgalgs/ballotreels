IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE K

By Dawyn R. Harrison, County Counsel

This measure was placed on the ballot by the governing board (Board) of the Keppel Union School District (District). If approved by at least 55 percent of the votes cast on the measure, this measure will authorize the District to issue and sell up to $27,000,000 in general obligation bonds. The sale of these bonds by the District represents a debt of the District.

Proceeds from the sale of bonds authorized by this measure may only be used by the District for the construction, reconstruction, rehabilitation, or replacement of school facilities, including the furnishing and equipping of school facilities, and the acquisition or lease of school facilities. A complete list of the projects and allowed expenditures is included in the full text of the measure. Bond proceeds may not be used for teacher and administrator salaries and other operating expenses.

If the measure is approved, the Board will be required to establish an independent Citizens' Oversight Committee and have independent performance and financial audits conducted annually to ensure that bond proceeds are spent only for projects identified in the measure. The Board will deposit bond proceeds into a separate account, to be held by the Los Angeles County Treasurer and Tax Collector, as required by the Education Code. Approval of the measure does not guarantee that projects described in the measure will be funded beyond local revenues generated by the measure. The District's proposal for certain projects may assume receipt of matching State funds subject to appropriation by the Legislature or approval of a statewide bond measure.

According to the District's Tax Rate Statement, the best estimate of the average annual tax rate required to fund the bonds is $30.00 per $100,000 of assessed value. The best estimate of the highest tax rate required to fund the bonds is $30.00 per $100,000 of assessed value. The final fiscal year in which the tax is anticipated to be collected is fiscal year 2065-66. The best estimate of the total debt service, including principal and interest, required to be repaid if all bonds are issued and sold is $67,800,000. The bonds will not exceed the maximum interest rate as allowed by law.
