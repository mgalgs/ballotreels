Measure Y asks the voters of this city whether to add a 1 percent sales tax for general city services, with no end date.
The tax would be paid on top of current sales taxes, and collected the same way.
The money would go to the general fund of the city, for any lawful city service, and would not be shared with other governments.
Measure Y needs a simple majority to pass.
The city attorney's impartial analysis estimates about $16 million a year in added revenue.
Supporters say police and fire calls have surged, and Measure Y would improve emergency response and repair roads.
No argument against Measure Y was filed by the July deadline, so only an argument in favor appears.
A yes vote authorizes the 1 percent sales tax.
A no vote does not authorize the sales tax.
