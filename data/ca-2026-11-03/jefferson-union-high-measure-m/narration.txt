Jefferson Union High School District Measure M asks the district's voters whether to replace its 2018 parcel tax with a tax of $98 per parcel.
Today, the district levies a $58 parcel tax that voters passed in 2018.
Measure M needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
Starting in fiscal year 2027-2028, a $98 tax per parcel would replace the $58 tax for ten years.
The money could pay for teachers, counselors, academic programs and career training, but not administrators' pay.
The impartial analysis says the tax is estimated to raise about $3.4 million a year.
Supporters say Measure M helps keep qualified teachers, with local money the state or federal government cannot take.
Opponents say Measure M is no simple replacement but a tax hike, and the community needs fiscal discipline.
A yes vote would impose the $98 parcel tax for ten years.
A no vote would not allow the new parcel tax to be levied.
