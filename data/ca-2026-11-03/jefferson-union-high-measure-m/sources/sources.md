# Measure M — Jefferson Union High School District (parcel tax, $98 per parcel, 10 years)

- **Jurisdiction:** Jefferson Union High School District (Jefferson, Oceana, Shasta, Terra Nova, Thornton and Westmoor High Schools).
- **Kind:** Qualified special tax (education parcel tax), $98 per parcel for ten years from fiscal year 2027-28; replaces the 2018 Measure Y parcel tax ($58 per parcel).
- **Who votes:** the voters of the District. Resolution Section 1 (p. 1-2): "The Board hereby orders an election and submits to the electors of the District the question of whether an education parcel tax shall be levied". Section 2 (p. 2): "the election shall be held solely within the boundaries of the District".
- **Vote threshold:** two-thirds. Recital (p. 1): the Law "authorizes a school district, upon approval by two-thirds (2/3) of the electorate voting on the measure, to levy a qualified special tax". Impartial analysis: "This Measure passes if at least two-thirds of those voting on the Measure vote “yes”."
- **Placed by:** the Board of Trustees, Resolution No. 2025-2026/19.
- **Election:** November 3, 2026.

## Ballot question

> With funds that cannot be taken by the State or Federal Government and spent elsewhere, shall Jefferson Union High School District's measure to directly support teachers and academic programs; expand / enhance career training and college preparedness classes; and hire and retain qualified teachers and counselors at Jefferson High School, Oceana High School, Shasta High School, Terra Nova High School, Thornton High School, and Westmoor High School be adopted, replacing the 2018 parcel tax at $98 per parcel for ten years (raising $3.4 million annually) with full public disclosure of all spending?

(resolution-full-text.pdf p. 5, Exhibit A "Introduction", printed inside quotation marks; repeated in Exhibit B "Ballot Measure Abbreviated Form", p. 8. From the OCR layer, checked against the line breaks.)

## Full text (resolution)

- Resolution No. 2025-2026/19, Board of Trustees, "RESOLUTION ORDERING AN ELECTION FOR THE PURPOSE OF SUBMITTING TO THE QUALIFIED ELECTORS OF THE DISTRICT THE QUESTION OF LEVYING AN EDUCATION PARCEL TAX" (p. 1; the OCR reads "LEWING").
- Adopted "by the following vote, on June 2, 2026" (p. 9). AYES (handwritten, p. 9; my reading): Sherrett Walker, Rosie Tejada, Andrew Lie, Jerome Gallegos, Lascundra Gutter (last name reading uncertain). NOES, ABSENT, ABSTAIN: blank.
- Recital (p. 1): Measure Y (November 6, 2018) authorized "an educational parcel tax in the amount of $58 per parcel for 10 years, commencing with fiscal year 2019-20".
- Section 5 (p. 2): the tax "shall be levied in an equal amount against each parcel of taxable real property in the District in the amount of $98 per parcel for ten years, commencing with fiscal year 2027-28."
- Section 15 (p. 4): if approved, the new tax "shall replace the Measure Y Parcel Tax during the remaining term thereof, and the District shall cease to levy and collect the Measure Y Parcel Tax commencing with fiscal year 2027-28."
- Exhibit A, Purposes (p. 5): "Under no circumstances shall any of the proceeds of the education parcel tax be used for administrators' salaries, benefits or pensions."
- Section 7(b) (p. 3): "the Board shall appoint a Citizens' Oversight Committee".

## Impartial analysis

Heading as printed: "IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE [TBD]". **No author name or office is printed.** (The PDF's metadata lists "Wendy Wong" as author; that is file metadata, not a printed byline.)

- "The District currently has in place a parcel tax of $58 per parcel (Measure Y) that this Measure would replace; if this Measure is approved, the District will cease to levy and collect the Measure Y parcel tax commencing with fiscal year 2027-28."
- "This Measure, if approved by voters, would impose an annual tax of $98 per parcel for ten years, commencing with fiscal year 2027-28, and is estimated to raise approximately $3,400,000 annually."
- "Any person 65 years of age or older who owns and occupies a parcel as a principal residence may apply to the District for an exemption from the tax."
- "The Measure provides that under no circumstances shall any proceeds of the parcel tax be used for administrators' salaries, benefits, or pensions."
- "A “yes” vote on this Measure would impose on property within the District an annual parcel tax of $98 for ten years, commencing with fiscal year 2027-28, for the purposes listed above." / "A “no” vote on this Measure would not allow the parcel tax to be levied."

## Tax rate statement / fiscal

Parcel tax, so no tax rate statement. From the resolution:
- Rate and duration: "$98 per parcel for ten years, commencing with fiscal year 2027-28" (Section 5, p. 2; Exhibit A p. 5).
- Parcel: "any unit of land in the District that receives a separate tax bill from the San Mateo County Tax Collector" (Section 5, p. 2).
- Exemptions (Section 6, p. 2; Exhibit A pp. 5-6): "All property that would otherwise be exempt from property taxes shall also be exempt from the education parcel tax"; "An exemption shall be available to persons who are 65 years or older and own and occupy as a principal residence a parcel". Exhibit A adds that owners "who have already qualified for a senior citizen exemption under the District's current parcel tax measure will be automatically qualified".
- Revenue: "raising $3.4 million annually" (ballot question); "approximately $3,400,000 annually" (impartial analysis).
- No cost-of-living escalator appears in the resolution.
