Jefferson Union High School District

Argument in Favor of Measure _.

Our local public high schools—Jefferson, Oceana, Shasta, Terra Nova, Thornton and Westmoor High Schools—are vital to our community's prosperity and enhance the value of our homes and businesses.

Your Yes vote of Measure __ will replace the District's existing parcel tax and provide local funding that cannot be taken by the State or Federal government and spent elsewhere.

Measure __ comes with critical taxpayer protections including:

- Transparency and Accountability: Public disclosure of all spending plus independent citizen oversight and third-party audits.
- Focused Spending: Funds may be used only for the specific purposes approved by voters—supporting teachers, classrooms and academic programs. No funds may be spent on administrator salaries, pensions or benefits.
- Senior Citizen Exemption: Homeowners age 65 and over can apply for an exemption.

Within these strict limits, Measure __ supports:

- PEOPLE: Attracting and retaining qualified teachers, counselors and staff who provide consistent instruction, guidance and support.
- PATHWAYS: Expanding and enhancing college-preparation, career training and Career Technical Education classes that connect learning with real-world skills and opportunities.
- PROGRAMS: Protecting strong academic programs and classes that prepare students to succeed after graduation.
- COMMUNITIES: High-quality schools increase the value of our homes and businesses.

Vote YES on Measure _ to replace the current parcel tax in order to attract and retain qualified, dedicated teachers.

Strong local high schools benefit the entire community and help protect local property values. Measure __ helps keep dedicated educators, and prepare graduates for college, careers and the future.

Join parents, teachers, graduates, seniors and community leaders in voting YES on Measure _.
