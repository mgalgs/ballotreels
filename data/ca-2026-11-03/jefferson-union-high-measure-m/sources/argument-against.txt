Argument Against Measure

As a Pacifica resident and public school parent, I know firsthand how dedicated our teachers, principals, and staff are. They do remarkable work every single day. I strongly support public education, but continuously increasing property taxes without structural reform is not the right solution.

Local property owners are already carrying a heavy financial burden. Since 2012, you have approved $399.29 million in Jefferson Union High School bonds across Measures E (2012), J (2014), J (2020) and Z (2024). On typical tax bills, bond repayments alone add significant costs. It shows as "Jefferson UHSD Bond" costing me $1,144.23 this past year. Please check your own tax bill to see what you are already paying.

On top of bond debts, parcel taxes keep compounding. In 2024 Measure Z added a new $95 annual parcel tax. 2018 Measure Y currently costs $58 annually. There is also a Maintenance Assessment District tax that adds another $10 annually.

Measure is presented as a simple "replacement" for 2018 Measure Y, but it actually raises the tax from $58 to $98 per year - a tax hike on top of recent new taxes. Meanwhile, the ballot language is practically copy-pasted from 2018 and offers no new accountability standards, no structural changes, and no fresh ideas. It is just higher costs for local residents.

Taxpayers support our schools, but our community needs fiscal discipline and better leadership, not endless tax increases. Vote NO on Measure
