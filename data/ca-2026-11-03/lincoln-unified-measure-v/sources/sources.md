## Quoted facts

- Who votes: ROV notice, "THE FOLLOWING MEASURE SHALL BE PUT TO A VOTE FOR THE QUALIFIED VOTERS IN LINCOLN UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT."
- Ballot question (ROV notice: "The following is the abbreviated form of the bond measure:"): "Lincoln Unified School District Facilities and Safety Measure. To improve the quality of education; make safety/security improvements; provide classrooms and labs for job training and career readiness; replace roofs and HVAC systems; restrooms and facilities improvements; shall Lincoln Unified School District’s measure authorizing $79,000,000 of bonds be adopted at legal rates, estimated levies averaging $39.07 per $100,000 assessed value ($4,471,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding, with citizens’ oversight, audits, no money for salaries and funding only for LUSD schools?"
- Vote needed: "Passage of Measure V requires approval by fifty-five percent (55%) of voters voting thereon."
- Analysis: "Approval of Measure V would allow the Lincoln Unified School District (the “District”) to incur bond indebtedness up to a maximum principal amount of $79,000,000.00."
- Analysis: "Measure V would require the District to: (1) establish an independent citizens’ oversight committee; (2) conduct annual independent performance and financial audits; and (3) direct the funds to be deposited into a special account."
- Tax rate statement: average "$39.07 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. It is currently expected that the tax will be collected until fiscal year 2061-62."; highest "$39.50 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. This rate is projected to apply to fiscal years 2027-28 through 2058-59"; total debt service "approximately $156,500,000."
