Measure O asks the voters of this city whether to let the existing dairy keep operating.
Measure O is a citizen initiative, placed on the ballot by a petition with 632 valid signatures, where 524 were needed.
Today, city law prohibits concentrated animal feeding operations, and gives existing ones a period to phase out.
Measure O would keep the existing definition, which sorts them as large, medium or small by the number and type of animals confined and their potential to affect water quality.
Measure O would let existing operations keep running, and end the phase-out period.
New operations would still be prohibited everywhere in the city.
Existing operations would have to certify each year that they meet state and federal requirements.
Measure O needs a majority vote to pass.
The county elections page for this election does not include a cost estimate for the measure.

A yes vote lets existing operations keep running, ends the phase-out period, and requires a yearly certification.
A no vote keeps the current rules, which prohibit these operations and phase out existing ones.
