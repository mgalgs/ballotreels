PASADENA MEASURE PFD

Pasadena voters will decide this November on the Pasadena 911 Firefighter/Paramedic Emergency Medical/Wildfire Preparedness/Response Measure, a locally controlled funding measure that would provide dedicated revenue for specified fire and emergency-response purposes.

The measure would provide funding that may be used for fire station improvements, emergency response equipment, wildfire preparedness and brush clearance. It would help maintain fire, paramedic and 911 response; prepare for and respond to wildfires, earthquakes and medical emergencies; recruit and retain well-trained firefighters; and maintain lifesaving equipment and fire-alert communication.

WHAT THE MEASURE FUNDS

  • Earthquake retrofit of Station 33 and Station 37
  • Construct new fire station training center for firefighters and paramedics, modern equipment, firefighter training, and paramedic response capacity
  • Wildfire preparedness and brush and debris clearance

MEASURE AT A GLANCE

  • No annual cost-of-living increase
  • Sunset: Expires after 14 years
  • Qualified low-income senior property owners exempted
  • Requires independent audits and public disclosure of spending
  • Funds restricted to local fire services only
  • Annual tax rate: 19¢ per square foot of improved property

WHAT IS IT GOING TO COST?

  • Residential property example (1,600 sq ft): approximately $304 per year (about $25.33 per month)
  • The measure would apply uniformly to all properties — including residential, commercial, and other property types. The cost for any property is based on its building square footage/improved property
  • Find your building square footage/improved property at LA County Assessor -
    Property Search Portal: Assessor.LAcounty.gov/Homeowners/Property-search

If your neighborhood association, homeowners’ group, business organization, service club, faith‑based organization, or other community group would like to host an informational meeting, City staff and Fire Chief Chad Augustin are available to meet with you. The presentation will provide factual information about the Fire Department’s current and future operational needs, outline the contents of Measure PFD, and address questions from participants.
