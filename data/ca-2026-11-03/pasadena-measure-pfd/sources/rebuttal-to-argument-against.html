REBUTTAL TO ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE PFD

The opponents don't dispute the danger and offer no alternative to keep us safe.

"Demand a better plan" really means wait. Wait while stations close. Wait while a 911 call goes unanswered. Wait for the next earthquake to endanger our families and our first responders. Wait for the next Eaton Fire.

Pasadena has fewer firefighters than we did 75 years ago, while emergency calls have increased more than tenfold. Several stations are over 60 years old. Two fail earthquake safety standards and could be condemned. We need to act now. Two years from now could be too late.

Opponents are falsely combining this measure with unrelated measures to claim that it is five times more expensive to Pasadena homeowners than it actually is. The reality: a family in a starter home would pay less than $25 per month.

Opponents' claim that accountability "won't work" ignores what the measure requires: independent annual audits, citizens' oversight, and public disclosure of every dollar. This money can be spent only on local fire protection and emergency medical response. It cannot be diverted by politicians or taken by the county, state, or federal government.

Firefighters, scientists, and earthquake and wildfire experts support Measure PFD because they understand the growing fire, earthquake, and disaster risks that an underfunded fire department poses to residents' lives and property .

Pasadena can't wait. Join firefighters, paramedics, earthquake and wildfire experts, and small business owners: Vote YES on Measure PFD.
