## County measure list entry

From `county-measure-list-excerpt.txt` (LA County RR/CC final measures list):

> PFD City of Pasadena 911 Firefighter/Paramedic Emergency Medical/Wildfire Preparedness/Response Measure. To help maintain fire, paramedic, 911 response; prepare for/respond to wildfires/earthquakes/medical emergencies; recruit/retain well-trained firefighters; maintain lifesaving equipment/fire alert communication; shall City of Pasadena's measure annually levy 19¢ per square foot of improved property, with low-income senior exemption, providing approximately $22,100,000 annually, expiring after 14 years, requiring accountability, independent audits, oversight, public spending disclosure; all funds must be used to support local fire protection/emergency medical response services, be adopted?
>
> Supporters: Pasadena Firefighters Association; Victor M. Gordo, Pasadena Mayor; Gale Kohl, Owner, Gale's Restaurant; Giancarlo DiMassa, MD
> Opponents: Pasadena Foothill Association of Realtors.

Vote threshold: 2/3 of votes cast (confirmed in Resolution No. 10203 §6 and the Notice of Election).

## What's present vs. missing

- **Impartial analysis**: present (`impartial-analysis.pdf`).
- **Argument in favor**: **missing — not found/not fetched.** The county list names four supporters, so an argument was presumably filed, but the filed text was not located as a standalone document; `election-notice.pdf` gives only the filing deadline, not the text.
- **Argument against**: **missing — not found/not fetched.** The county list names one opponent (Pasadena Foothill Association of Realtors), so an argument against exists, but its text was not located either.
- **Rebuttal to argument for / rebuttal to argument against**: **missing — not found/not fetched.** Both an argument for and against exist per the county list, so rebuttals to each are plausible, but neither was located as a standalone document.
- **Full text**: present, inside `resolution.pdf` — Exhibit A, the ordinance adding Pasadena Municipal Code Chapter 4.110.
- **Tax rate statement / fiscal**: the ~$22,100,000/year estimate is confirmed in the ballot question itself and in `fact-sheet.pdf`/`staff-report.pdf`. No standalone Prop-13-style tax rate statement was found; Measure PFD is a flat parcel tax rather than a bond, so that document type may not apply in the same way.
