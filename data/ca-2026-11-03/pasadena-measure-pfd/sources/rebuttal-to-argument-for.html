REBUTTAL TO ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE PFD

Vote NO on Measure PFD.

Pasadena's firefighters and paramedics deserve strong support.

Emergency services protect the entire community. Measure PFD represents the WRONG solution: a 14-year, 19-cent-per-square-foot tax expected to collect $22.1 million annually.

L.A. County raised the sales tax in the June 2026 election. This proposed tax makes Pasadena even less affordable.

Measure PFD would hit Pasadena's forgotten middle hardest: retirees on fixed incomes, young families struggling to pay their mortgages, and longtime homeowners whose insurance, utilities, groceries, and property taxes are rising faster than their paychecks. These residents earn too much to qualify for assistance but too little to absorb another tax increase. Politicians talk about affordability but pass policies and taxes that make Pasadena less affordable.

Only qualifying low-income seniors receive an exemption. Apartment properties, small housing providers, businesses, and other owners receive no comparable protection.

Proponents cite the Eaton Fire to push an inequitable tax. County and state fire investigators determined that electrical arcing from utility equipment caused the disaster. Emergency medical calls, not building fires, account for most Fire Department responses.

Measure PFD guarantees tax collections; it does not guarantee completed station repairs, faster response times, successful recruitment, or fewer overlapping emergencies. Annual audits merely track spending after the money leaves taxpayers' pockets.

Supporting firefighters does not require approving every tax. Pasadena can strengthen emergency services through a fairer plan that shares costs among everyone who benefits, establishes measurable performance standards, and protects existing fire funding.

Vote NO on Measure PFD

RIAN BARRETT
Chief Executive Officer, Pasadena Foothill Association of Realtors

MATTHEW KLINK
