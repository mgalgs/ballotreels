## Facts and the passages that establish them

### Ballot question

S1 PDF p. 80:

> MEASURE S
> To provide locally-controlled funding for Oceanside services and infrastructure, such as keeping streets, sidewalks, parks, and public facilities safe, clean, and well-maintained; fixing potholes; improving traffic; restoring/protecting local beaches; modernizing public safety/emergency facilities to improve 911 emergency response/community safety; and repairing aging infrastructure, shall City of Oceanside's ordinance establishing a 1/2 cent sales tax be adopted, providing $20,000,000 annually for general government use for 10 years, with independent audits and citizen oversight?

### Vote required

S1 PDF p. 80: "This measure requires Simple Majority (50%+1) of those voting on the measure."

### Who votes on it

S1 PDF p. 81 (ordinance recital):

> the City has the authority to enact a local sales tax for general purposes with the approval of the majority of voters in the City voting in an election on the issue

S1 PDF p. 81 (Sec. 34.7.2(a)): the tax "shall be operative if a majority vote of the electors voting on the measure vote to approve the imposition of the tax". S1 PDF p. 84 (Section 3): "certification of its approval by a simple majority of the voters voting on the question at the consolidated November 3, 2026 general election".

In short: the voters of the City of Oceanside.

### Who placed it on the ballot

S1 PDF p. 80: "Measure "S" was placed on the ballot by the Oceanside City Council". S1 PDF p. 84:

> INTRODUCED at a special meeting of the City Council of the City of Oceanside, California, held on the 24th day of June, 2026, and, thereafter,
> PASSED AND ADOPTED at a regular meeting of the City Council of the City of Oceanside California, held on the 5th day of August, 2026, by the following vote:
> AYES: Sanchez, Joyce, Figueroa, Robinson
> NAYS:
> ABSENT: Weiss

### Impartial analysis

S1 PDF p. 80, "CITY ATTORNEY IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS", "Dated: August 14, 2026", "T. Steven Burke, Jr., City Attorney". Key statements:

> If approved, the tax would commence on or after April 1, 2027 and would remain in place for ten years.

> The current sales tax rate in Oceanside is 8.25%. If Measure S is approved, the sales tax rate would increase to 8.75%.

> The City estimates that Measure S, if adopted, will generate approximately $20 million annually. These revenues are authorized to be used for any lawful municipal purpose.

> Because the revenues raised by Measure S can be used for any general governmental purpose, a majority of voters must approve Measure S for the tax to go into effect.

> Measure S requires a Citizens' Oversight Committee ("COC") to be responsible for annually making recommendations to the City Council on a spending plan for the revenues generated by Measure S.

> A "yes" vote on Measure S favors increasing the sales tax by one-half of one percent for ten years.
> A "no" vote on Measure S opposes an increase in the sales tax by one-half of one percent for ten years.

### Arguments and rebuttals

- **Argument in favor**: **none printed.** S1 has no argument in favor of Measure S and, unlike the school measures, no printed notice that none was filed. I searched the full text of S1 for "favor of Measure S" and found nothing.
- **Argument against**, S1 PDF p. 80. Headline: "The CITY wants MORE TAXES! Vote NO on Measure S". It says: "We just approved a sales tax two years ago." Signers: Steven Burrell (Senior Advocate); Rocky Chavez (Former State Assembly Member); Jeordie Fellner (Retired Oceanside School Teacher, Community Advocate); Michele L. Cyr (Licensed Clinical Social Worker); Karie Lasko (Former Small Business Owner, Alternate Manufactured Home Fair Practices Commissioner).
- **Rebuttals**: none printed.

### Full text

S1 PDF pp. 81-84, "FULL TEXT OF MEASURE S / ORDINANCE NO. 26-OR0505-1", "AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF OCEANSIDE ADDING CHAPTER 34, ARTICLE II, DIVISION 3, SECTIONS 34.7.1 THROUGH 34.7.17 TO THE OCEANSIDE CITY CODE TO ESTABLISH A TEMPORARY ONE-HALF CENT GENERAL TRANSACTIONS AND USE (SALES) TAX...". Key provisions:

- Sec. 34.7.4(a): "a tax is hereby imposed upon all retailers in the incorporated territory of the City at the rate of one-half of one percent (0.5%)".
- Sec. 34.7.5: "The authority to levy the tax imposed by this Ordinance shall expire on April 1, 2037."
- Sec. 34.7.16(d) (p. 84) refers to a "Measure X Citizens Oversight Committee" that may share members with the new COC. (Measure X is not otherwise identified in S1.)

### Tax rate statement or fiscal analysis

No separate tax rate statement (that is a bond-measure requirement). The fiscal facts are the City Attorney's: rate 8.25% to 8.75%, about $20 million a year.
