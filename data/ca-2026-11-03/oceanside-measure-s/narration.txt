City of Oceanside Measure S asks voters in the city whether to add a half-cent sales tax for 10 years.
Today, the sales tax rate in Oceanside is 8.25 percent.
The City Council placed Measure S on the ballot, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
The rate would rise to 8.75 percent, starting no earlier than April 2027, for 10 years.
The money could pay for any lawful city purpose.
A citizens' oversight committee would recommend a spending plan each year and review an annual independent audit.
The City Attorney's impartial analysis says the city estimates the tax would raise about $20 million a year.
No argument in favor of Measure S appears in the San Diego County Voter Information Guide for this election.
Opponents say voters just approved a sales tax two years ago, and this tax could be spent on anything.
A yes vote favors raising the sales tax by one-half of one percent for 10 years.
A no vote opposes raising the sales tax by one-half of one percent for 10 years.
