## Quoted facts

- **Who votes:** City of Chino voters. Impartial analysis: "If approved by a majority of voters voting on the measure, it would adopt an ordinance adding Chapter 3.44 to the Chino Municipal Code and repealing related existing business license tax provisions."
- **Ballot question** (RES_CityofChino.pdf, Section 4): "Without taxing residents, and to support general city services such as 911/police response; protect local drinking water sources; retain/attract local businesses; shall the measure be adopted to reform City of Chino's outdated business license ordinance with rates of $0.60 - $1.50 per $1,000 of gross receipts for larger businesses based on business category; and only $25 for smaller businesses (under $75,000), generating approximately $4,800,000 annually until ended by voters; with audits, spending disclosure, funds locally controlled?"
- **Impartial analysis** (VIG p. 197, City Attorney): "The measure would replace portions of the existing business license tax structure with a gross receipts-based tax." … "For businesses with $75,000 or less in gross receipts, the tax would be $25. For gross receipts above $75,000, the measure would impose the following maximum tax rates on gross receipts above $75,000: General/retail businesses: $0.60 per $1,000 Contractors: $1.00 per $1,000 Services: $1.00 per $1,000 Professional/advanced businesses: $1.50 per $1,000 Real property/rental businesses: $1.50 per $1,000"
