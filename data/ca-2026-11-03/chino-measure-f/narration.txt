City of Chino Measure F asks Chino voters whether to change how the city taxes businesses.
Chino already charges a business license tax to businesses operating in the city.
Measure F would replace parts of that tax with a tax on gross receipts.
Businesses with $75,000 or less in gross receipts would pay $25.
Above that, top rates would run from 60 cents to $1.50 per $1,000, by kind of business.
The council could lower the tax, but not raise it without another vote.
The money would go to the general fund, for any lawful city purpose.
The ballot question says the tax would raise about $4.8 million a year.
Supporters say Measure F has larger businesses contribute fairly, protects small businesses, and does not tax residents.
No argument against Measure F was submitted, so the county voter guide prints only the supporters' argument.
A yes vote approves the business license tax ordinance.
A no vote is against approving the business license tax ordinance.
