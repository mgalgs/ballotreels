Measure H asks the voters of this city whether to let the council set the most the city manager may approve to settle a legal claim.
Today a state law requires council approval of any settlement of a claim against the city above $50,000.
The Legislature set that limit in 1989, and only a charter amendment can change it for the city.
Measure H would let the council set the limit by ordinance, and raise or lower it later without another charter amendment.
In August 2026, the council adopted an ordinance setting the limit at $250,000 if Measure H passes.
Settlements above the limit would still need council approval, in a closed session as today.
The city attorney's impartial analysis does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
No argument against Measure H was filed with the Registrar of Voters for this county.
Supporters say a $50,000 cap set nearly 40 years ago now catches even minor claims.
A yes vote lets the council set the settlement limit by ordinance.
A no vote keeps the limit in state law, now $50,000.
