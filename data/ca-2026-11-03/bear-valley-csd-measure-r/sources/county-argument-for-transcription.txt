Bear Valley CSD Citizen Initiative Arguments

Argument In Favor Of Measure ____

REPEAL OF WATER RATE INCREASES ENACTED BY RESOLUTION NO. 26-29 OF THE BEAR VALLEY COMMUNITY SERVICES DISTRICT BOARD OF DIRECTORS ON MARCH 26, 2026.

Voting YES repeals the near doubling of water rates over the next four years, as enacted by the Bear Valley CSD Board.

The Bear Valley Community Services District Board of Directors voted to nearly DOUBLE WATER RATES within four years, which began April 2026 and includes annual increases through 2030. These outrageously high and destructive increases threaten home values and the ability of families, seniors and fixed income residents to continue to live in Bear Valley.

Let's not forget large increases to solid waste (trash), sewer customers, a new manure fee, a junk mailbox fee, and a major road assessment increase—all put forth by Bear Valley CSD inside of one year!

The Board of Directors claims only 18 percent of water customers submitted a protest against the massive water rate increase. That's true. No ballot was supplied and customers had to write their own letters of protest or seek out protest letters created by organizers. HERE, all voters are supplied with a ballot.

The Board claims revenue will be reduced by $10M by this Measure, but that's false. The Board will immediately put forth a new water rate increase just like they did when sewer rates were rejected. We can only hope the Board respects the will of the voters and come up with a more reasonable increase—just like they did for sewer rates.

Voting YES on this Measure instructs the Board of Directors to respect the finances of residents and accord great deference to affordability when enacting rate, fee and tax increases.

/S/ Kevin Rice [street address left out]
/S/ David Licher [street address left out]
