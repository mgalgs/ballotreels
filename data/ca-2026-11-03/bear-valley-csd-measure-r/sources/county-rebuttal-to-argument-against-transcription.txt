Rebuttal to Argument Against Measure R

Arguments of the Bear Valley CSD fall flat. Measure R does prohibit a rate increase; Measure R simply repeals the current rates, forcing the Board to pursue a more respectful rate increase. Measure R directs the Board to operate the District with sorely lacking prudence and cost-consciousness.

The base rate for water has more than TRIPLED since 2018. Where did that money go? Nearly doubling rates again is destructive to residents and home values. Numerous fixed-income residents cannot afford hundreds of dollars in monthly water bills.

One very elderly widower signed the petition for Measure R and related he just can't afford higher bills, but then conceded his plan: He probably won't live until 2030 when the water rate increases reach the maximum.

Numerous financially distressed residents will similarly not be able to afford water bills which will escalate in 2030 to over six times what they were in 2018. Bear Valley residents need actual results from the BVCSD, not higher bills.

The water rate increases are so onerous the Homeowners Association is suing, contending the rates were illegally calculated by including charges not related to supplying non-potable and recycled water. Residents will now have to pay lawyers for BOTH the CSD (through taxes) and the HOA (through dues). Measure R will provide an end to this litigation and force BVCSD to come up with a legal rate structure.

Measure R is a real solution. Yes on R.
