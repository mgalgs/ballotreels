Measure R asks the voters of this community services district whether to repeal the water rate increases its board adopted in March 2026.
Measure R is a citizens' initiative, placed on the ballot by a petition signed by at least 10 percent of the district's registered voters.
The board then ordered it put to the voters without changes.
On March 26, 2026, the district's board adopted updated water rates for the properties it serves.
Measure R would repeal the March 2026 rate increases in full.
Measure R needs a majority of the vote to pass.
The impartial analysis does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Supporters say the board voted to nearly double water rates within four years, threatening home values and residents' ability to stay.
Opponents say repeal would cut the revenue needed for essential projects on a complex water system more than 50 years old.
A yes vote repeals the water rate increases the board adopted in March 2026.
A no vote keeps the water rates the board adopted in March 2026 in place.
