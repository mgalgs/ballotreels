City of Adelanto Measure P asks Adelanto voters whether to add a 1 percent sales tax to fund city services.
Measure P needs a majority of the vote to pass.
Stores in the city would charge 1 percent more on taxable sales from April 1, 2027, or one cent on each dollar.
The money would go to the city general fund, for any city purpose, until voters end the tax.
An independent accountant would audit the money every year.
The city attorney's impartial analysis estimates the tax would raise about $3,000,000 a year.
Supporters say Measure P speeds 911 response, with one fire station for 55 square miles, and fixes potholes.
No argument against Measure P was filed, so the county voter guide prints only the argument of supporters.
A yes vote adopts the 1 percent tax for city services.
A no vote means the tax is not adopted.
