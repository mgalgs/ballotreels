## Quoted facts

- **Who votes:** City of Adelanto voters (municipal election consolidated with the Statewide General Election, RES Section 3).
- **Ballot question** (RES_CityofAdelanto.pdf, Section 3.C): "Measure _____—Transactions and Use Tax Shall the measure to provide funding for Adelanto city services, such as paving streets and fixing potholes, fire protection, paramedic, and 911 response; law enforcement and crime prevention; keeping public areas/parks safe/clean; addressing homelessness; and repairing aging storm drains, pipes, bridges, and public areas by establishing a 1% transactions and use tax, providing approximately $3,000,000 annually until ended by voters, requiring audits, public spending disclosure, all money controlled locally, be adopted?"
- **Impartial analysis** (VIG p. 244, City Attorney): "This ballot measure establishes a local transactions and use tax in the City of Adelanto. The ballot measure amends the Adelanto Municipal Code to add Chapter 3.70 to include the tax. It is estimated that the tax will raise approximately $3,000,000 for the City of Adelanto. All funds raised would go to the City's General Fund and could be used for any legitimate municipal purpose" … "The sales tax is imposed on all retailers within the City of Adelanto at the rate of 1% (one percent) of the gross receipts of the retailer from the sale of all tangible personal property sold within the City."
