Measure T asks the voters of this city whether to add a half-cent sales tax for general city services.
The city council placed it on the ballot to help make up for the loss of Measure C, a county transportation sales tax set to expire in June 2027.
Today, the city charges its own 1 percent sales tax.
Measure T would raise it to 1.5 percent, starting on July 1, 2027.
Groceries and prescription medicine would be exempt.
The ballot question says the tax runs until voters stop it; the measure's text sets 30 years, or less if a county tax replaces Measure C.
Measure T needs a simple majority to pass.
The city attorney's impartial analysis says the tax would raise about $300,000 a year.
Opponents say even a half-percent sales tax is a heavy burden for struggling neighbors, and those raising money should try voluntary giving first.
No argument for Measure T was filed with the county registrar of voters, so the published measure documents include only the opponents' argument.
A yes vote adopts the half-cent sales tax.
A no vote does not adopt the half-cent sales tax.
