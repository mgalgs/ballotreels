## Facts

**Ballot question.** resolution.pdf, Section 4 "Ballot Measure", PDF page 2 ("in substantially the following form"):
> "To fund general City services, such as local police, fire protection, emergency response, repair streets and roads, improve aging infrastructure, maintain parks and public facilities, and other general City services, shall the measure, establishing a one-half cent (½¢) transactions and use tax providing approximately $300,000.00 annually until ended by voters and public reporting consistent with State law as required, be adopted?"

**Who votes; vote needed.** resolution.pdf, Sections 3 and 5, PDF page 2:
> "A General Municipal Election shall be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, for the purpose of submitting the Ordinance to the qualified electors of the City."
> "The vote requirement for the Measure to pass is a majority (50% + 1) of the votes cast."

**What it does.** City Attorney's Impartial Analysis of Measure T (Roy C. Santos, City Attorney; filed Aug 25 2026):
> "The City of Fowler ("City") currently imposes a 1% transactions and use tax ... The Measure, if approved by voters, adds a new Chapter 11 to Title 3 of the Fowler Municipal Code which establishes a new transactions and use tax at the rate of 0.5% such that the total combined transactions and use tax rate is 1.5%."
> "If approved, the Measure will generate approximately $300,000 annually. This tax is a general tax and revenue generated by the tax would be placed in the City's general fund, are not restricted, and may be used for any City general fund purpose."
