ORDINANCE NO. 2026-07

AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF FOWLER, ADDING A NEW CHAPTER 11 TO TITLE 3 OF THE FOWLER MUNICIPAL CODE WHICH ESTABLISHES A NEW TRANSACTIONS AND USE (SALES) TAX AT THE RATE OF 0.5% (1/2 ¢)

WHEREAS, pursuant to Article XIIIC of the California Constitution, Section 53720 et seq. of the California Government Code, the City has the authority to levy a general tax, including, but not limited to, increasing an existing general tax; and

WHEREAS, Article XIIIC, Section 2, of the California Constitution requires that the election at which a general tax increase is submitted to the electorate shall be consolidated with a regularly scheduled general election for members of the governing body of the local government, except in cases of emergency declared by a unanimous vote of the governing body; and

WHEREAS, Part 1.6 (commencing with Section 7251) of Division 2 of the California Revenue and Taxation Code, and Section 7285.9 of the California Revenue and Taxation Code, authorizes a city to adopt a transactions and use tax ordinance, which shall be operative if a majority of the electors voting on the measure vote to approve the imposition of the tax at an election called for that purpose; and

WHEREAS, Revenue and Taxation Code Section 7285.9 requires that any ballot measure/ordinance establishing or increasing a transactions and use tax must be approved by a 2/3 vote of all members of the City Council and by a majority vote of the qualified voters of the City voting on the ballot measure; and

WHEREAS, by Resolution No. 2854, the City Council approved this Ordinance by a 2/3 vote of all its members; and

WHEREAS, Tuesday, November 3, 2026, is the date of the general municipal election, which has been consolidated with the statewide general election occurring on the same date, at which members of the City Council will be elected;

WHEREAS, The City Council of the City of Fowler has ordered submission of a ballot measure to the voters at the November 3, 2026, General Municipal Election proposing a one-half of one percent (0.50%) Transactions and Use Tax for general municipal purposes.

NOW, THEREFORE, THE PEOPLE OF THE CITY OF FOWLER, CALIFORNIA, DO HEREBY ORDAIN AS FOLLOWS:

Section 1. Recitals. The recitals set forth above are true and correct and incorporated herein by this reference.

Section 3-11.12. Amendments.
All amendments subsequent to the effective date of this Chapter to Part 1 of Division 2 of the Revenue and Taxation Code relating to sales and use taxes and which are not inconsistent with Part 1.6 and Part 1.7 of Division 2 of the Revenue and Taxation Code, and all amendments to Part 1.6 and Part 1.7 of Division 2 of the Revenue and Taxation Code, shall automatically become a part of this Chapter, provided however, that no such amendment shall operate so as to affect the rate of tax imposed by this Chapter.

Section 3-11.13. Enjoining Collection Forbidden.
No injunction or writ of mandate or other legal or equitable process shall issue in any suit, action or proceeding in any court against the State or the City, or against any officer of the State or the City, to prevent or enjoin the collection under this Chapter, or Part 1.6 of Division 2 of the Revenue and Taxation Code, of any tax or any amount of tax required to be collected.

Section 3-11.14. Severability.
If any provision of this Chapter or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the Chapter and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.

Section 3-11.15. Effective Date.
Pursuant to Elections Code Section 9217, this chapter shall be considered adopted on the date that the City Council declares that the voters of the City of Fowler have approved the ordinance enacting this Chapter by a vote of no less than a majority of votes cast by the electors voting at the election held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, and shall go into effect on July 1, 2027.

Section 3-11.16. Termination and/or Suspension Date.
(a) The tax imposed by this chapter shall continue in effect for thirty (30) years unless and until an ordinance extending the levy of the tax is approved by a majority of the qualified voters of the City voting on the question at an election. The City Council shall not extend the tax without such voter approval, except as required by state or federal law. Following certification of voter approval of such extension, the City shall notify the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration and complete all actions

[Page 8]

necessary to extend administration and collection of the tax.

(b) Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, the tax, or portion thereof, imposed by this chapter shall be suspended, during any period in which imposition of the tax, or portion thereof, would cause the combined rate of transactions and use taxes applicable within the City to exceed the limitation established by Revenue and Taxation Code section 7251.1. The City Manager shall consult with the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration ("CDTFA") to determine the earliest date on which the tax, or portion thereof, may lawfully become operative or resume collection. Any commencement, suspension, or resumption of collection shall occur on the first day of the calendar quarter and in accordance with applicable law and CDTFA administrative requirements.

(c) Further, the tax imposed by this Chapter shall automatically terminate on the first day of the first calendar quarter commencing more than 110 days after all of the following have occurred:

(1) The voters of Fresno County have approved a countywide transaction and use tax measure intended to succeed or replace Measure C at the November 3, 2026, General Election, or at any subsequent election authorized by law; and

(2) The California Department of Tax and Fee Administration has established the operative date for administration and collection of the successor countywide transactions and use tax; and

(3) The City Council has determined by resolution, following a duly noticed public meeting, that the successor countywide transactions and use tax is intended to succeed or replace Measure C, and related municipal purposes intended to be funded by this Chapter.

Following adoption of the resolution described above, the City shall notify the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration and complete all actions necessary to terminate administration and collection of the tax imposed by this Chapter.

(d) Termination of this Chapter shall not affect any tax liability incurred before the operative date of termination or the authority of the City or the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration to audit, collect, refund, or otherwise administer taxes attributable to any period before the operative date of termination.

Section 3-11.17 Oversight Committee
Should the tax established by this Chapter be approved, the City Council, in compliance with the requirements of the Fowler Municipal Code and City policies and/or procedures, may enact an Oversight Committee.

Section 3-11.18 Amendment by City Council
The City Council has the right and authority to amend this chapter, to further its purposes and intent (including but not limited to amendment for more efficient administration as determined by the City Council), in any manner that does not increase a tax rate or otherwise constitute a tax increase for which voter approval is required by Article XIII C of the California Constitution, pursuant to Elections Code Section 9217.

Section 3. Council Authority to Amend. The City Council has the right and authority to amend this Ordinance, to further its purposes and intent (including but not limited to amendment for more efficient administration as determined by the City Council), in any manner that does not increase a tax rate, or otherwise constitute a tax increase for which voter approval is required by Article XIII C of the California Constitution, pursuant to Elections Code Section 9217.

Section 4. Environmental Compliance. The City finds the adoption of this Ordinance is not subject to the California Environmental Quality Act ("CEQA"), because the enactment of this Ordinance: (1) does not constitute a "project" as defined by CEQA pursuant to California Code of Regulations, Title 14, Sections 15060(c)(3) and 15378; (2) will not result in a direct or reasonably foreseeable indirect physical change in the environment pursuant to California Code of Regulations, Title 14, Section 15060(c)(2); and (3) is covered by the general rule that CEQA applies only to projects which have the potential for causing a significant effect on the environment pursuant to California Code of Regulations, Title 14, Section 15061(b)(3). This Ordinance establishes a transactions and use tax, provides for the creation of City funding mechanisms and City fiscal activities, includes organizational or administrative activities of government which will not result in direct or indirect physical changes to the environment, and does not involve any commitment to any project which may result in a potentially significant physical impact on the environment.

Section 5. Severability. Should any section, subsection, paragraph, sentence, clause, or phrase of this Ordinance be declared unconstitutional or invalid for any reason, such declaration shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions of this Ordinance.

Section 6. Effective Date. Pursuant to Elections Code Section 9217, this Ordinance shall be considered adopted on the date that the City Council declares that the voters of the City of Fowler have approved this Ordinance by a vote of no less than a majority of votes cast by the electors voting at the election held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, and shall go into effect on July 1, 2027.

Section 7. Execution. The Mayor, or his designee, shall sign this Ordinance and the City Clerk shall attest and certify to the passage and adoption of this Ordinance if a majority of the voters voting in the City's general municipal election on November 3, 2026, approve it.

Section 8. Publication. The City Clerk shall cause this Ordinance to be published in the manner required by law.

PASSED, APPROVED AND ADOPTED BY THE PEOPLE OF THE CITY OF FOWLER at the General Municipal Election held on November 3, 2026.
