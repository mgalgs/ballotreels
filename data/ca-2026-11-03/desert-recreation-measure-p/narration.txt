Desert Recreation District Measure P asks the district's voters whether to approve a yearly tax on property for parks and recreation services.
The district provides parks, recreation facilities and community programs for residents of all ages.
Measure P needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
Each year, parcels with buildings valued over $50,000 would pay $0.05 per square foot of building.
Parcels of low-income seniors, and parcels already exempt from property taxes, would be exempt.
The money would go to park repairs, safe drinking water, cooling centers, senior services, youth programs and addressing homelessness.
The ballot question estimates the tax would raise $11.3 million a year, until voters repeal it.
No argument against Measure P was filed for this year's County Voter Information Guide.
Supporters say Measure P avoids cuts to youth and senior programs and repairs parks.
A yes vote authorizes the district to establish the parcel tax.
A no vote rejects the district establishing the parcel tax.
