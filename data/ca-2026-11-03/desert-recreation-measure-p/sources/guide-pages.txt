AN ORDINANCE OF THE PEOPLE OF DESERT RECREATION DISTRICT, CALIFORNIA, ENACTING A SPECIAL PARCEL TAX
UPON PROPERTY WITHIN THE DISTRICT TO FUND LOCAL SAFE PARKS AND RECREATION SERVICES
The People of the Desert Recreation District do ordain as follows:
WHEREAS, the Desert Recreation District (District) provides parks, recreation facilities, community programs, and related
services throughout its service area covering 1,887 miles across the greater Coachella Valley; and
WHEREAS, The District is responsible for maintaining facilities and programs that serve residents of all ages and abilities and
provide opportunities for recreation, community engagement, relief from extreme temperatures, and access to essential community
services; and
WHEREAS, the District has placed a priority on preserving opportunities for children to run, play and experience nature to improve
their health and well-being, and keep our children and teens off the streets, safe, and out of trouble; and
WHEREAS, the District has identified long-term funding needs associated with aging parks and recreation facilities and the
continuation of community services including, repairs to water, gas, and sewer systems at District parks and recreation facilities; removal
of asbestos and mold; facility safety improvements; maintenance of cooling centers; services for vulnerable seniors; youth gang and
drug prevention programs; and efforts addressing homelessness and its impacts on District facilities and programs; and
WHEREAS, the District has worked hard to balance its budget each year and to maintain services to residents even while rising
costs and the aging facilities have impacted the District’s budget, creating a structural deficit; and
WHEREAS, without a stable funding source, the District will be forced to make significant cuts to programs our children and seniors
depend on, reduce critical park and recreation facilities maintenance, and reduce the community services available to residents; and
WHEREAS, the District’s Board of Directors has reviewed all options, and received information about a potential funding measure,
with strict accountability requirements, that would provide locally controlled funding to help fund services, programs and maintain aging
recreation facilities.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE PEOPLE OF THE DESERT RECREATION DISTRICT, AS FOLLOWS:
SECTION 1. Authority, Purpose and Intent. Pursuant to the authority of Section 5789.1 of the California Public Resources Code and
Article 3.5 of Chapter 1 of part 1 of Division 1 of Title 5 of the Government Code, commencing with Section 50075, and other applicable
law, there is hereby levied and assessed a special parcel tax by the Desert Recreation District for each fiscal year. It is the sole purpose
and intent of this ordinance to impose a special parcel tax for the specific purpose of constructing, maintaining and improving safe,
clean parks and recreation services for the community, its youth, and senior citizens.

IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE “P”
Safe Parks and Youth/Senior Recreation
The purpose of Measure P is to enable voters to authorize or reject the
Desert Recreation District (“District”) the establishment of a special
parcel tax upon properties within the District. The Board of Directors of
the District, by adopting Resolution No. 26-25 (“Resolution”), elected to
call an election to place this Measure on the ballot.
Measure P would establish an annual special parcel tax to be levied at the
rate of 5 cents ($0.05) per square foot of structural improvements on all
parcels with an improvement value greater than $50,000 per the Riverside
County Assessor. Parcels subject to exemption are low-income seniorowned parcels and parcels currently exempt from ad valorem property
taxes as determined by the Riverside County Assessor. Money raised by
the special parcel tax will be expended on maintaining safe drinking water,
address homelessness, repair aging gas/sewer lines, remove asbestos/

vulnerable seniors, and youth gang/drug prevention programs.
Measure P provides for the Finance Director of the District to cause a
report to be prepared by an independent auditor to be ﬁled with the Board
of Directors of the District no later than August 30th of each year. The
report shall state: (a) the amount of parcel taxes collected and expended in
such year; and (b) the status of any projects or description of any programs
and services funded from proceeds of the parcel taxes.
For this Measure to be approved, two-thirds (2/3) of qualiﬁed voters within
the District who vote on the Measure must vote yes.
A “YES” vote on Measure “P” is a vote to authorize the District to establish
a special parcel tax to provide ﬁnancing of park projects.
A “NO” VOTE on Measure “P” is a vote to reject the District from
establishing a special parcel tax to provide ﬁnancing of park projects.

ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE “P”
Vote YES on Measure P to protect parks and recreation areas in
the Coachella Valley!
Every day, Desert Recreation District parks, pools, recreation centers,
and community spaces provide safe places for children to play, families
to gather, seniors to stay active, and residents to improve their health
and well-being.
However, decades of use have left a mark on our recreation facilities.
Bathrooms are crumbling, pipes leak, and outdated electrical systems
can’t keep up. As our region continues to experience hotter summers,
recreation centers also provide spaces where seniors can stay active
and ﬁnd relief during periods of extreme heat.
Without an additional source of funding, Desert Recreation District
will be forced to make signiﬁcant cuts to programs our children
and seniors rely on.
Measure P funds basic repairs so our parks and recreation facilities don’t
fall into disrepair and become more expensive to ﬁx. Well-maintained
community centers and parks help keep our neighborhoods desirable
and our home values strong.
Measure P maintains safe, youth and sports programs that keep them
active, engaged, off the streets and out of trouble.
