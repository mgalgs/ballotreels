## Quoted passages

- Question (p.22): "LOCAL PARK REPAIR/SAFETY AND SENIOR/YOUTH SERVICES MEASURE. To maintain safe drinking water, address homelessness, repair aging gas/sewer lines, remove asbestos/mold at parks/recreation facilities; maintain cooling centers, services for vulnerable seniors, youth gang/drug prevention programs; shall Desert Recreation District's measure be adopted levying 5¢ per building square foot annually, providing $11,300,000 annually until ended by voters, adjusting the appropriations limit; requiring spending disclosure, audits, all funds staying local; exempting low-income seniors?"
- Who placed it (p.47): "The Board of Directors of the District, by adopting Resolution No. 26-25 (“Resolution”), elected to call an election to place this Measure on the ballot."
- Analysis (p.47): "Measure P would establish an annual special parcel tax to be levied at the rate of 5 cents ($0.05) per square foot of structural improvements on all parcels with an improvement value greater than $50,000 per the Riverside [County …]" (sentence continues on the page).
- Full text (p.45): "the Desert Recreation District (District) provides parks, recreation facilities, community programs, and services throughout its service area covering 1,887 miles across the greater Coachella Valley"

Geography: Desert Recreation District has no Census boundary. Official boundary map: Riverside LAFCO, <https://lafco.org/wp-content/uploads/documents/special-district-service-boundary-and-soi-maps/Desert_RPD.pdf> (listed on <https://www.lafco.org/resources/special-districts/>; not downloaded).
