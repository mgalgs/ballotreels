Measure R asks the voters of this school district whether to approve $2.8 million in school bonds.
The district would sell the bonds and repay them from a tax on property in the district.
Measure R needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would go to classrooms, a gymnasium for school and community use, restrooms, and heating, ventilation and electrical systems.
Bond money could not pay salaries, and a citizens' oversight committee and yearly audits would check the spending.
The district's tax statement estimates $24.86 per $100,000 of assessed value each year, through 2056-2057.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $6.35 million.
No argument against Measure R is printed in its posted pages.
No argument for Measure R is printed in its posted pages.
A yes vote authorizes the district to issue the bonds.
A no vote does not authorize the district to issue the bonds.
