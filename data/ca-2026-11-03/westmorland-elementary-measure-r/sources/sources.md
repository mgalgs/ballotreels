**Small discrepancy:** the tax rate statement gives "$24.86 per $100,000"; the ballot question and impartial analysis round it to $25.

## Quoted facts

- **Ballot question:** "To improve athletic facilities at Westmorland Elementary School; repair restrooms; upgrade outdated heating, ventilation, and electrical systems; repair, construct, acquire classrooms, sites and equipment, shall the Westmorland Union Elementary School District's measure be adopted to issue $2,800,000 of bonds at legal rates, levy approximately $25/$100,000 of assessed value, generating approximately $218,799 annually while bonds are outstanding, with annual audits, independent citizens' oversight, NO money for salaries, all money staying local?"
- **Tax rate statement:** average annual rate "$24.86 per $100,000 (2.5¢ per $100) of assessed valuation. It is currently expected that the tax will be collected until fiscal year 2056-2057." Total debt service "$6,345,171."
- **Impartial analysis (County Counsel):** "This measure was placed on the ballot by the governing board of Westmorland Union Elementary School District ... if approved by 55% of votes cast on the measure, will authorize the District to issue and sell up to $2,800,000 in general obligation bonds." "The final fiscal year in which the tax is anticipated to be collected is fiscal year 2056-2057." Total debt service "$6,345,171."
