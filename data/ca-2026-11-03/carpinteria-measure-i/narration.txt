Measure I asks the voters of this city whether to raise the sales tax by a quarter percent.
Today, the total sales tax in the city is 9 percent, including a 1.25 percent city tax.
Measure I would raise the city tax to 1.5 percent, and the total to 9.25 percent.
The higher rate would start around April 2027 and last until voters repeal it.
As a general tax, the city council could spend the money on any lawful purpose.
Measure I needs a simple majority to pass.
The city attorney's impartial analysis projects about $700,000 a year in added general fund revenue.
Supporters say without Measure I, deeper cuts are unavoidable, and it is critical for street and pothole repairs.
No argument against Measure I was filed, so the county voter guide prints only the one in favor.
A yes vote authorizes the quarter-percent sales tax.
A no vote does not authorize the quarter-percent sales tax.
