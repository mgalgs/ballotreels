## Quoted facts

- **Who votes:** Carpinteria voters. Impartial analysis (p. 16): "If approved by a majority of Carpinteria voters, this Measure would increase the City's transactions and use tax rate by one-quarter of one percent (0.25%), from 1.25% to 1.50%, which would increase the total combined sales tax rate charged on taxable transactions within the City from 9.00% to 9.25%."
- **Ballot question** (sample ballot, p. 23): "Carpinteria Public Safety, Roads, City Services Measure. To help repair streets/potholes/sidewalks; better prepare for/respond to wildfires/floods, other emergencies/natural disasters; keep public areas/parks safe/clean; help maintain sheriff's service/response, property crime/theft prevention efforts; address homelessness; support youth/senior programs; other general government purposes; shall City of Carpinteria's measure be adopted levying a 1/4¢ sales tax, providing approximately $700,000 annually until ended by voters, requiring audits, spending disclosure, funds locally controlled?"
- **Impartial analysis** (p. 16): "On June 22, 2026, the City Council voted to place Measure I2026, also known as the Carpinteria Public Safety, City Services Measure, on the November 3, 2026 general municipal election ballot." … "The proposed transactions and use tax measure is a general tax placed on the ballot by the City Council of the City of Carpinteria. Measure I2026 is projected to generate approximately $700,000 in additional annual general fund revenue, which may be used for any lawful governmental purpose at the discretion of the City Council."
