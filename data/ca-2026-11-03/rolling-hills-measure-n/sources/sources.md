## Key facts, quoted

- **Ballot question:** "Shall the measure authorizing the Rolling Hills Community Services District to levy a special tax in the amount of $250 per parcel annually, subject to an annual inflation adjustment not to exceed 3%, on all parcels within the District, except the Sierramonte subdivision, generating $87,250 annually, until ended by voters, and increasing the District's appropriations limit, to maintain District infrastructure and services, including fire mitigation, roads, drainage, lighting, landscaping, and parks, be approved?"
- **Analysis** (p. 86): "Measure N (the "Measure"), if approved by two-thirds of the voters voting thereon, would authorize the levy of a special parcel tax at the rate of $250 per parcel per year within Zone No. 1 of the Rolling Hills Community Services District (the "District"). This Measure was placed on the ballot by the Board of Directors of the District pursuant to Resolution No. 26-04, which was passed at an official meeting of the Board of Directors on June 23, 2026."
- "The proposed special parcel tax would be levied only within Zone No. 1, which includes all territory within the jurisdictional boundaries of the District except for the area commonly referred to as the Sierramonte subdivision." Uses: "maintaining and improving existing District facilities and services provided within Zone No. 1, including but not limited to fire mitigation, roads, drainage, lighting, landscaping, and parks." "The amount of the special parcel tax may be adjusted annually in accordance with the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers, not to exceed 3% annually." "… expected to generate revenue of approximately $87,250 per year. This Measure will have no effect on the existing $200 annual assessment within the District."
- Who votes: voters in Zone No. 1 — i.e. the District minus the Sierramonte subdivision (inferred from "levied only within Zone No. 1"; the analysis does not state the voting area separately).
