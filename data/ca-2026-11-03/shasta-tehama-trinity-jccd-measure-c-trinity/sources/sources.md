## Facts with quotes

- **Ballot question (guide p. 11):** "To improve college facilities serving students, veterans, and residents; renovate job training spaces including welding, public safety, agriculture and nursing; make accessibility improvements for the disabled; and acquire, construct, and renovate classrooms/ buildings; shall Shasta-Tehama-Trinity Joint Community College District's measure be adopted authorizing $129,630,000 of bonds at legal rates, estimated levies averaging $7.76 per $100,000 assessed value (raising $7,800,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding with annual audits, independent citizens' oversight committee, and all funds remaining local?" Choices: "Bonds - Yes" / "Bonds - No".
- **Who votes:** "The electors who will be entitled to vote on the measure are those qualified electors who reside within the boundaries of the District." (guide p. 12). The tax rate statement names the district as "of the counties of Shasta, Tehama, Trinity, Lassen, Humboldt and Modoc, California" (p. 17).
- **Vote needed / amount:** "If fifty-five percent (55%) of the qualified electors voting on Measure C vote for approval, the District's Board of Trustees would be authorized to issue bonds in the principal amount not to exceed $129,630,000" (p. 12).
- **Use limits:** "The proceeds will not be used for any purpose not identified in the measure, including teacher or administrator salaries. The District's Board of Trustees shall establish a citizen's oversight committee" (p. 12).
- **Tax rate statement (p. 17):** average "$7.76 per $100,000 of assessed value. The final fiscal year in which the tax is anticipated to be collected is 2060-61"; highest "$15.29 per $100,000 of assessed value ... levied in fiscal years 2031-32 through 2042-43"; total debt service "approximately $264,800,000". Signed by Dr. Frank Nigro, Superintendent/President (per the analysis: "According to the tax rate statement submitted by Dr. Frank Nigro, Superintendent/President of the District").
- **Argument in favor signers (p. 13):** Todd Jones, President & CEO Redding Chamber of Commerce; Jill Cox, Business Owner, Trinity County; Michelle Hickok, Shasta College Board President; Joe Wyse, Shasta College Superintendent/President-Retired; Judy Flores, Shasta County Superintendent of Schools-Retired. Its claim "Approving Measure C will improve our campuses without increasing tax rates" is the proponents' statement.
- **Board action (Lassen guide analysis, for reference):** "on May 13, 2026, by Resolution No. 2025-26-18, the District's Board of Trustees adopted a resolution authorizing and requesting consolidation" — https://www.lassencounty.gov/DocumentCenter/View/3261/Full-VIG-Nov-26-Gen-WEB-PDF?bidId= (PDF p. 20).
