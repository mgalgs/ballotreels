Argument in Favor of Measure Q

Measure Q. Qualiﬁcations Over Politics.

Vote YES on Measure Q to ensure Fairﬁeld’s City Clerk is selected for qualiﬁcations,
experience, and professional competence—NOT campaign politics.

The City Clerk is the keeper of Fairﬁeld’s public record. Every agenda, ordinance,
resolution, meeting minute, public notice, election ﬁling, campaign disclosure, and official
City action depends on the Clerk’s Office being accurate, impartial, transparent, and
professionally managed.

This is not a policymaking position. The City Clerk does not set taxes, approve
development, adopt budgets, or make laws. The Clerk administers elections, maintains
public records, protects access to government information, and ensures official actions
are properly noticed, recorded, preserved, and available to the public.

Today, these responsibilities require specialized knowledge of election law, the Brown
Act, the Public Records Act, the Political Reform Act, conﬂict-of-interest rules, records
retention, agenda procedures, cybersecurity, and modern information systems.

Yet an elected candidate generally needs NO professional certiﬁcation, relevant
experience, or specialized training.

Measure Q would allow Fairﬁeld to recruit broadly and select the best-qualiﬁed
professional based on:

   •   Education
   •   Experience
   •   Integrity
   •   Management ability
   •   Knowledge of elections, records, and open-government laws

Measure Q also strengthens accountability.

An appointed City Clerk can be evaluated regularly, held to clear performance standards,
and replaced if the office is not meeting Fairﬁeld’s expectations. Voters will continue
electing the City Council—the officials who make policy and remain directly accountable
to the public.

More than two-thirds of California cities already appoint their city clerks because this
work has become technical, demanding, and essential to honest, effective government.
Measure Q does not weaken transparency. It protects transparency by placing this
important office in the hands of a qualiﬁed, neutral professional accountable for results.

For professionalism, accountability, public access, and good government, vote YES on
Measure Q. Fairﬁeld deserves nothing less from this vital public office.
