REBUTTAL TO THE ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE Q

Vote YES on Measure Q to ensure Fairﬁeld’s City Clerk is selected based on qualiﬁcations, experience,
and professional skills-not campaign politics.

The City Clerk runs elections, maintains public records, protects access to government information, and
ensures o"icial actions are properly announced, recorded, preserved, and accessible to the public.

Currently, a candidate for City Clerk only needs to be at least 18 years old and a registered Fairﬁeld voter.
If only one person runs, they are automatically elected to a four-year term, regardless of their
professional qualiﬁcations or experience.

Measure Q would allow Fairﬁeld to select the most qualiﬁed City Clerk based on certiﬁcation, education,
experience, integrity, management ability, and knowledge of elections, records, and open-government
laws.

Approximately 80% of California cities appoint their City Clerks. Cities similar in size to Fairﬁeld-including
Concord, Vallejo, Richmond, Berkeley, Clovis, Simi Valley, and Thousand Oaks-have chosen appointment
to keep politics out of this important professional role.

Measure Q means a more professional, accountable process focused on qualiﬁcations and performance
for Fairﬁeld residents.

Please join your neighbors in voting YES on Measure Q.
