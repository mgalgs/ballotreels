## Facts and quotes

**Ballot question** (Notice of Measures, 8/5/2026, https://content.solanocounty.gov/sites/default/files/2026-08/Notice_of_Measures.pdf): "Shall the Office of City Clerk be appointive?"

**Who votes / vote needed (analysis):** voters of the City of Fairfield. "This Measure was placed on the ballot by the Fairfield City Council and requires the approval of a majority of voters."

**Impartial analysis** (signed David Lim, City Attorney, City of Fairfield), key statements:
- "The proposed Measure seeks to convert the position of Fairfield City Clerk from an elective to an appointive office."
- "This Measure allows the City Council to delegate its appointment authority to the City Manager."
- "This Measure will have no impact upon the current term of the incumbent City Clerk. The incumbent City Clerk was last elected in 2024 and would be permitted to stay in office until the end of her current term in 2028."
- "An appointed City Clerk would not be required to be a resident or elector of the City, and will receive compensation in a fixed amount established by the City Council."
- "A "yes" vote on this Measure amends Chapter 2 of the City Municipal Code in order to make the City Clerk position appointive ... A "no" vote on this Measure means the City Clerk position will remain elected."

**Argument in favor:** signed "s/ Karen L Reese, City Clerk" and "s/ Kevin O'Rrourke [sic], Retired City Manager".
