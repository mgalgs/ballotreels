CITY OF FAIRFIELD

RESOLUTION NO. 2026-158

WHEREAS, a city clerk oversees vital municipal functions pursuant to Government Code section 40801 et seq., such as maintaining official records, administering oaths and affirmations, taking and certifying affidavits and depositions pertaining to city business, keeping meeting minutes, recording, publishing, and certifying city ordinances, and additional duties as prescribed by ordinance or other state laws, including the administration of elections as a neutral elections official under the Political Reform Act and ensuring compliance with state transparency laws such as the Ralph M. Brown Act (Brown Act) and the Public Records Act (PRA); and

WHEREAS, the position of the Fairfield City Clerk is currently an elected office; and
