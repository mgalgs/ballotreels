Argument Against City of Sanger Transient Occupancy Tax Measure

Fresno County

City of Sanger

November 3, 2026 Consolidated Statewide General Election

Word Count: 282

Text of Argument Below This Line

==============================================================

How has your cost of living changed in the last few years? What about your neighbors? Before you vote on this higher tax for the City of Sanger, please think about a few important points:

This tax will collect about $55,037 every year on visitors and residents of Sanger, and it would never end.

You might feel your share of that burden is worth paying because it promises to continue and improve services in the county. Safety services matter, and wanting to support them shows you care about your community. But we ask you to think about other ways to fund these projects. If you are willing to give up your money through a tax, would you also be willing to give it through voluntary charity instead?

Think again of your neighbors: many of them have struggled the past few years, and a whopping 12% tax undoubtedly represents a substantial burden on their cost of living. The Sanger residents that operate short-term rentals will end up shouldering the costs when they can't raise their rates sufficiently. That means less money for groceries, for college, or even paying medical bills after an emergency.

This tax would stay in place FOREVER, and once a tax is approved, it is hard to change or remove. Families may face new challenges in the future. Voluntary giving lets people adjust their support as their own finances change.

We respect people who want to raise money for a good cause. We simply ask that they try voluntary giving first, especially when so many families are already facing hard times.

Let your neighbors choose which causes they want to support, and vote NO on Measure B.

Join us: https://lpfresno.org/NoSangerHotelTax
