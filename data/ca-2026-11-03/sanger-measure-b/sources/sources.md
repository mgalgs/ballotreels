## Facts

**Ballot question.** resolution.pdf, Section 4 "Ballot Question", PDF page 3:
> "Shall the measure increasing the City of Sanger transient occupancy tax on hotel, motel, short-term rental, and similar lodging guests staying 30 days or less, generally not paid by Sanger residents, to 12%, until ended by voters, providing approximately $55,037 annually for general City services and deposited into the City's general fund, be adopted?"

**Vote needed / general tax.** resolution.pdf, Sections 5-6, PDF page 3:
> "The measure shall be deemed approved if it receives the affirmative vote of a majority of the voters voting on the measure." ... "The measure is submitted as a general tax measure."

**What it does.** City Attorney's Impartial Analysis of Measure B (Shannon Chaffin, Aleshire & Wynder, LLP, City Attorney; dated Aug 24 2026):
> "The City of Sanger currently imposes a transient occupancy tax at a rate of 4% of the rent charged for stays of 30 consecutive days or less. This measure would increase the tax rate to 12% and amend and restate the City's transient occupancy tax ordinance to apply to most short term lodging (30 days or less)."
> "The measure is a general tax. Its proceeds would be deposited in the City's general fund and could be used for any lawful municipal purpose. The tax would continue until ended by the voters."
> "A "yes" vote is a vote to increase the transient occupancy tax rate from 4% to 12%, expand and modernize its application to short-term rentals and online platforms, and adopt the amended ordinance."

**Who votes.** Voters of the City of Sanger (a city measure at a General Municipal Election; resolution Section 7 refers to "the General Municipal Election called by this Resolution").
