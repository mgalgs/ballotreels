Measure DE asks the voters of this city whether to make the mayor an office elected by voters citywide.
The city charter lets the City Council set, by ordinance, how the mayor is chosen.
Council members are elected by district.
The City Council proposed this ordinance and placed it before voters.
Starting with the November 2028 election, voters citywide would elect a mayor every four years, for a four-year term.
The mayor would be a voting member of the City Council and would run its meetings.
The council districts and the council-manager form of government would stay as they are.
Measure DE needs a majority yes vote to pass.
Neither the ordinance nor the official list of measures includes a cost estimate for the measure.
The official county list of measures, which names who filed each argument, says no argument against Measure DE was submitted.
The official county list of measures, which names who filed each argument, says no argument for Measure DE was submitted.
A yes vote adopts the ordinance to have voters elect the mayor citywide.
A no vote rejects the ordinance to have voters elect the mayor citywide.
