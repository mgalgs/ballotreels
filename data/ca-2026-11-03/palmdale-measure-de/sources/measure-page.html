Candidate Statements 

Election Results

The City’s next General Municipal Election is Nov. 3, 2026.

Welcome to the City’s election information webpage. Here, you will find information about upcoming and past elections, campaign contributions to candidates and elected officials, and the election process.

Regular elections are consolidated with statewide elections in November of even-numbered years to fill expired City Council terms. The City Clerk is the local elections official and coordinates with the County of Los Angeles to conduct municipal elections. It is the City Clerk’s duty to qualify candidates for the ballot and to provide guidance in meeting their legal responsibilities before, during and after an election.

The 2026 General Municipal Election for the City of Palmdale will be for the following:

  • One Councilmember for District 3, four-year term
  • One Councilmember for District 4, four-year term
  • One Councilmember for District 5, four-year term

GETTING STARTED INFORMATION

Registered voters residing in the City of Palmdale please be advised that the Nomination documents for the upcoming election shall be available no earlier than Monday, July 13, 2026, and no later than Friday, Aug. 7, 2026 at 5 p.m., pursuant to Elections Code Section 10220, 10224 & 10407, at the City of Palmdale, City Clerk’s Office, located at 38300 Sierra Highway, Suite C, Palmdale, California 93550, between the hours of 7:30 a.m. and 6 p.m.  Nomination documents must be filed at the Palmdale City Clerk’s Office no later than 5 p.m. on Friday, Aug. 7, 2026.

Although the City Clerk’s Office is normally closed for business on Fridays, along with all other City offices, it will be open on Friday, Aug. 7, 2026, between the hours of 7:30 a.m. and 5 p.m. for the sole purpose of issuing and accepting candidates’ nomination papers for the aforementioned Consolidated General Municipal Election. 

Pursuant to Elections Code Section 10225, if nomination documents for an incumbent are not filed by 5 p.m. on Friday, Aug. 7, 2026, the nomination period shall be extended until 5 p.m. on Wednesday, Aug. 12, 2026. Furthermore, if the number of persons who have been nominated to a particular office or category of offices elected at large does not meet the number to be filled, such office(s) may be filled by appointment in the manner prescribed by Elections Code Section 10229, provided any and all statutorily mandated conditions for making such an appointment are first met.

IMPORTANT DATES

  • July 13 - Aug. 7, 2026: Nomination Period. 
  • Aug. 7, 2026: Last day for candidates to file nomination documents.
  • Aug. 12, 2026: Nomination Extension Period. If the incumbent for a district does not file, only that district’s nomination period is extended by 5 days.
  • Sept. 7 - Oct. 20, 2026: Period to accept write-in candidates.
  • Oct. 19, 2026: Last day to register to vote for this election.
  • Nov. 3, 2026: Election Day.
  • Update: Incumbent Andrea Alarcón in District 5 did not file. The nomination period for District 5 is extended to 5 p.m. on Wednesday, Aug. 12, 2026.
