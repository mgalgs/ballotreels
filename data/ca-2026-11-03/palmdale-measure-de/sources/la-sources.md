## Ballot question and supporters/opponents (verbatim, from the county's final measures list)

> Shall the ordinance establishing the office of Mayor as an at-large, elected position beginning with the November 2028 general municipal election be adopted?
>
> Supporters: None submitted.
>
> Opponents: None submitted.

Vote threshold: Majority Vote 50%+1.

## What's present and what's missing

- **Full ordinance text**: present (`resolution.pdf`, Exhibit A, Ordinance No. 1669, pp. 5-7 of 7).
- **Resolution placing the measure on the ballot**: present (Resolution No. CC 2026-047).
- **Impartial analysis**: missing — not located; the July 7, 2026 council item that placed this on the ballot would have had a staff report/directed one, but neither was found.
- **Arguments for/against and rebuttals**: not located; consistent with the Notice of Measure's statement that the argument deadline (Aug 14, 2026) passed with no filings found anywhere, and the county list shows "Supporters: None submitted. Opponents: None submitted."
- Two unrelated documents (an impartial analysis for a different, 2024 measure "PD"; a 2010 pawn-shop ordinance) were found during the search and discarded, not staged.
