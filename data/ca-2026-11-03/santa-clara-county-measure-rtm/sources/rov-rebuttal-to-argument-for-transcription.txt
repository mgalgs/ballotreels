REBUTTAL TO ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE RTM (Regional Transit Measure)
For submittal to Santa Clara County ROV

[argument starts here]

Stop this Gigantic New Sales Tax!

This $14,000,000,000 Regional Transit Measure SALES TAX makes Bay Area life LESS AFFORDABLE.

Our cost of living is too high already. This tax would worsen it:

- Gas, groceries, rent, utility prices keep rising.

- We’d ALL pay this tax DAILY, on most purchases.

- Sales tax hikes add costs, aggravating the inflation problem.

- Necessities are taxed: prepared food, clothing, pet food, over-the-counter medicines.

- Our low-income neighbors, seniors, students, the disabled, will suffer terribly.

Santa Clara County’s SALES TAX rate will rise to at least 10.25%.

- Imagine forfeiting at least $1.02 tax for every $10.00 of prepared food — $102.50 for $1,000 smartphones!

Don’t trust BART or VTA with more of your money!

- They’re grossly mismanaged:

  In June, Santa Clara County’s Civil Grand Jury found VTA’s management of BART’s $13,000,000,000 extension faces huge cost overruns, and suffers from weak board oversight.

  VTA has a plan to divert our money to “bailout” that fiasco.

- Our sales taxes for VTA already total more than $1 Billion/year.

  VTA hands tens of millions to BART.

  They want more!

- Since 2019, the major transit agencies’ ridership fell 51%, yet expenses climbed 28%!

- BART and VTA gave pay/benefits above $250,000 to 539 and 123 employees, respectively, in 2024. This TAX picks our pockets to fill theirs.

- “Oversight”? Impotent.

- Instead of demanding another BILLION dollars/year from us, transit agencies must better use existing taxpayer money.

  Fixes before funding!

  Don’t award them more money until they stop their wasteful spending.

Sales taxes EXCEEDING 10.2% throughout Santa Clara County? Outrageous!

Vote NO!
