The page carries no printed heading and no printed signer block; it is the rebuttal text only.

It’s undeniable - severe Caltrain and BART station closures and service cuts would affect all of us in Santa Clara County - worsening traffic and straining transportation options for hundreds of thousands of Bay Area residents who need to get to work or medical appointments.

Please consider the FACTS and don’t be misled by half-truths and exaggerations:

FACT: Before supporting the Regional Transit Measure, business groups and local leaders ensured the measure included strong accountability and oversight requirements - BART and other large agencies must show cost savings to continue to receive dedicated operating funding.

FACT: Based on detailed financial analysis and projections, it’s clear that failure of this measure could result in major station closures and service cuts for both Caltrain and BART. Caltrain could be forced to close stations, reduce to hourly service, and stop late night and weekend service entirely. BART could close 15 stations and reduce service by 70%.

FACT: Without this measure, commutes would be longer and more unpredictable as people who depend on transit are forced onto already-crowded streets, roads and highways.

FACT: taxpayers from across Santa Clara County support the Regional Transit Measure, along with stakeholders who don’t always agree- business, labor, environmental, and community groups.

FACT: The Regional Transit Measure invests money directly into Santa Clara County communities, providing funding for transit improvements and road repaving including pothole repairs.

FACT: Don’t be misled- the Regional Transit Measure does NOT tax rent, utilities, prescription medicines, or most groceries.

Preserve our Bay Area quality of life for future generations - www.connectbayarea.com
