The Regional Transit Measure protects the public transit that people in our region depend on and invests in practical local transportation improvements to help our communities get around. It includes strong accountability and oversight that requires large transit agencies to show cost savings to continue to receive dedicated operating funding.

Today, Bay Area transit faces a turning point. Without new funding from the Regional Transit Measure, we will experience significant and deep cuts to public transit service -- setting us back decades.

Caltrain, BART and VTA connect people to jobs, events, and activities across Silicon Valley and the Bay Area. Failure of this measure could result in major station closures and service cuts for both Caltrain and BART. Caltrain could be forced to close stations, reduce frequency, and stop late night and weekend service entirely. BART could close 15 stations and reduce service by 70%.

The consequences would impact everyone. Thousands of people would be forced onto our already congested roads and freeways every day with more cars flooding Highways 101, 280, 87, and 85, worsening air pollution, making traffic jams start earlier and last longer, resulting in more time behind the wheel and less time home with our families.

The Regional Transit Measure also invests money directly into Santa Clara County communities, making local transit more reliable and efficient, repairing potholes, reducing traffic, and improving safety. And it doesn’t tax rent, utilities, prescription medicines, or most groceries.

This measure is the right way to reduce traffic, support our local economy, and ensure families, seniors, people with disabilities, students, and workers have an affordable way to get where they need to go.

Please join us - vote Yes on the Regional Transit Measure. www.connectbayarea.com
