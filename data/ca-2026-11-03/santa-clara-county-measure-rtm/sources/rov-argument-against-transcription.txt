Argument Against Regional Transit Measure: Demand Fixes Before Funding

- This proposed five-county sales tax hike (Regional Transit Measure or RTM) is a $14 billion-dollar bailout for a broken system. Oversight is toothless. Let's demand accountability and structural reform instead of forcing taxpayers to shoulder another heavy burden.

- This measure expects working families to pay for ongoing mismanagement. Grand juries in Alameda, Contra Costa and Santa Clara have found major flaws in transit financial management and oversight. Since 2019 ridership at the major transit agencies has fallen 51%, yet expenses have risen 28%. Sales taxes grow with inflation, so why has Transit outspent its revenues?

- Sales taxes are regressive, hitting lower-income residents the hardest. California’s are the nation’s highest, exceeding the 9.25% sales-tax limit. RTM will push our county sales tax to over 10%. While we tighten our belts, agencies want blank checks without addressing their own administrative bloat.

- We need Fixes Before Funding, not higher taxes to sustain the inefficient status quo.

- Proponents are exploiting a loophole to bypass standard taxpayer protections, attempting to pass this massive tax hike with a mere 50%+1 majority instead of the traditional two-thirds requirement.

- Furthermore, this tax allows continuation of capital-heavy mega-projects. Major transit projects are routinely years late, way over budget, yet deliver less than 20% of the promised ridership. Instead of propping up outdated transit modes, agencies must adapt to modern commuting realities and reallocate existing capital funds to cover shortfalls.

- Santa Clara residents will pay the largest share of these regressive taxes because we have the largest population. And yet most of the tax dollars will be spent in the other four counties!

- That is not fair!!!

- Don't reward financial negligence.

- Don't let RTM treat you like an ATM.

- Fix Bay Area Rapid Transit by rejecting this tax and demand fiscal discipline.

- Learn more at: NoNewTransitTax.com

- Vote NO on the Measure __.
