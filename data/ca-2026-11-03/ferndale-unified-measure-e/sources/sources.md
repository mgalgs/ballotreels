## Facts with quotes

- **Ballot question (packet p. 12, Appendix B):** "To prepare students for careers and college and qualify for state matching grants by: constructing/ modernizing Career Technology Education classrooms, labs and related facilities; and making related improvements for health, safety and security; shall Ferndale Unified School District's measure be adopted authorizing $7,000,000 of bonds at legal rates, generating on average $483,000 annually while bonds are outstanding at approximate rates of $49.70 per $100,000 assessed value, with annual audits, oversight, no money for salaries, all money staying local?"
- **Vote needed:** "Measure E is a bond measure placed on the ballot by the Ferndale Unified School District, which would allow the district to issue bonds in the amount of up to $7 million to pay for specific school facilities projects. Measure E must be approved by a 55 percent vote." (guide p. 15)
- **Projects:** "Measure E complies with this requirement, listing projects related to creating and upgrading facilities for career technical education."
- **Rate:** "The maximum tax allowed by law for a unified school district is $60 per $100,000 of assessed property value. As stated in the ballot question and the tax rate statement included with the ballot materials, the district estimates that the average tax levy necessary to pay for the debt obligation would be $49.70 per $100,000 of assessed property value."
- **Total debt service:** "The bonds would allow the district to raise $7 million. The district estimates that total debt service, including principal and interest, required to be paid if all bonds are issued and sold, would be $15.4 million." (guide p. 16)
- **Accountability:** "By law, the district must "conduct an annual, independent performance audit to ensure that the funds have been expended only on the specific projects listed." This audit is then provided to a citizen's oversight committee".
- **Board vote:** resolution p. 6: "adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Ferndale Unified School District of Humboldt County ... on June 9, 2026"; AYES 4, NOES 0, ABSENT 1.
- **Argument in favor signers (guide p. 16):** William G. Becker, Retired Business Owner; Jim Walker, Dairyman; Alexa Alexandre, Agriculture Teacher; Luke McCanless, Dairyman; Bruce Titus, Business Owner.
