## Facts and quotes

**Ballot question (p1):** "Without raising taxes, to improve and protect Sonoma County's regional and neighborhood parks; safeguard water supplies, streams, rivers; reduce future wildfire risk; preserve fish and wildlife habitat; conserve natural areas for future generations; expand walking, hiking, and biking trails; shall Sonoma County continue a one-eighth-cent special transaction and use tax (sales tax) countywide until ended by voters, providing approximately $15.5 million annually, with community oversight, public disclosure, and annual audits?"

**Vote needed (p1):** "2/3 Needed to Pass". Analysis (p2): "The tax proposed by the ordinance will become effective only if approved by a two-thirds majority of those voting on the measure."

**Who votes:** countywide. Ordinance §1 (p4): "This Ordinance shall be applicable in the incorporated and unincorporated territory of the County."

**Impartial analysis (p2), key statements:**
- "Measure H asks voters whether the County should extend a one-eighth cent special sales tax countywide, until repealed by the voters, to fund improvements and maintenance for Sonoma County Regional and Neighborhood Parks and local city parks."
- "If approved, Measure H would extend a special tax that will provide approximately $15,500,000 annually. As a special tax, funds may be used only for the specific purposes set forth in the full text of the Measure"
- "The extension of the tax would begin on April 1, 2029, and remain in effect until repealed by the voters."
- "The Board of Supervisors unanimously voted to place this measure on the ballot."
- Signed: Joshua A. Myers, County Counsel, by Adam Brand, Assistant County Counsel.

**Fiscal impact statement (p2):** "This measure would extend the one-eighth of one cent per dollar (0.125%) transactions and use (sales) tax in Sonoma County for regional and city parks first approved by voters as Measure M in November 2018 and set to expire on March 31, 2029." "...the measure is estimated to generate approximately $17.1 million in the first full fiscal year (2029-30)." Allocation: "One-third or 33.3 percent will be distributed to incorporated cities ... the remaining two-thirds or 66.7 percent will be dedicated to three distinct program categories administered by Sonoma County Regional Parks". Signed Erick Roeser, Auditor-Controller-Treasurer-Tax Collector.

Note: the question and analysis say ~$15.5 million annually; the fiscal impact statement estimates $17.1 million in FY 2029-30. Both figures are as printed.

**Argument in favor (p3):** signed Fire Safe Sonoma (Ben Nicholls), Mike McGuire (State Senator), Sonoma County Farm Trails (Carmen Snyder), Lisa G. Carreño, Sonoma County Regional Parks Foundation (Melissa Kelley).

**Argument against:** "No Argument Was Submitted Against Measure H" (p3).
