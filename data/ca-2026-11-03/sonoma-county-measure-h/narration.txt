County of Sonoma Measure H asks voters across the county whether to continue a sales tax for parks.
Voters first approved the tax, one-eighth of 1 percent, in 2018, and it expires on March 31, 2029.
As a special tax, its money may be spent only on the purposes the measure lists, and it needs two-thirds of the vote.
Measure H would extend the tax from April 1, 2029, until voters repeal it.
One-third of the money would go to city parks, and two-thirds to regional parks, trails and open space.
The ballot question says about $15.5 million a year; the County Auditor estimates $17.1 million in the first full year, 2029-2030.
No argument against Measure H was submitted for the Sonoma County voter guide, so no opposing view appears beside it.
Supporters say Measure H renews funding that protects parks, reduces wildfire risk and keeps water clean, all without raising taxes.
A yes vote will authorize the county to extend the sales tax.
A no vote will prohibit the county from extending the sales tax.
