## Quoted passages

- Question (p.22): "CANYON LAKE PUBLIC SAFETY AND EMERGENCY RESPONSE PROTECTION MEASURE; Shall the measure establishing a 1¢ transactions and use tax, estimated to generate $1,140,000.00 annually until repealed by voters, and requiring the proceeds and specified existing EMS subscription-fee, property-tax fire, and utility-users-tax revenues to be deposited in a restricted fund used only for Canyon Lake police, fire, paramedic, and emergency-response staffing, compensation, equipment, and operations, with annual independent audits, be adopted?"
- Who placed it / threshold (p.44): "The Canyon Lake Public Safety and Emergency Response Protection Measure (Measure “E”) was placed on the ballot by a petition signed by the requisite number of voters. If approved by a majority of Canyon Lake voters voting on Measure E, the Measure would authorize a one percent (1%) special transactions and use (sales) tax and establish restrictions governing the receipt and expenditure of specified City revenues."
- Analysis (p.44): "It is estimated that Measure “E” will provide an additional $1,140,000 in annual local funding. Because the revenues are restricted to specified purposes, the Measure would impose a special tax."
- Analysis (p.44): "All revenues from the new tax would be deposited into the Public Safety Fund and could not be commingled with the City’s General Fund. The Measure also requires specified existing City revenues to be deposited into the Fund, including EMS Subscription Program revenues, property-tax revenues received from Riverside County for fire protection, the City’s Utility Users Tax dedicated to police, fire, and paramedic services, and related investment earnings."
