Measure S asks the voters of this city whether to add a 0.75 percent sales tax to pay for general city services.
The City Council placed Measure S on the ballot in June 2026.
The tax would add 0.75 percent to the sales tax in the city, on top of any existing local sales tax.
Measure S is a general tax for city services, which the ballot question lists as including police, firefighters, streets and parks.
The ballot question also calls for public disclosure of spending and audits.
The tax would last until ended by voters, and needs a majority of the vote to pass.
The ballot question estimates that Measure S would raise about $3.4 million a year.

No argument for Measure S appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
A yes vote on Measure S adopts the 0.75 percent sales tax.
A no vote on Measure S does not adopt the 0.75 percent sales tax.
