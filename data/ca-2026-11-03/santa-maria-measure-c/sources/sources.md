## Quoted facts

- **Who votes:** Santa Maria voters. Impartial analysis (p. 26): "The Santa Maria City Council has placed Measure C2026 on the ballot to ask the voters of Santa Maria if the City should enact a local one-half (1/2) cent sales tax."
- **Ballot question** (sample ballot, p. 37): "CITY OF SANTA MARIA GENERAL SERVICES. Maintain and enhance community services including parks and recreation, the aquatic center, library, youth programs, and roads and streets, to improve quality of life; shall the measure be approved adopting an ordinance implementing Measure C2026 at the one-half cent rate until ended by voters, providing $13,000,000 annually that cannot be taken by Sacramento, requiring oversight, independent audits, with all funds used locally."
- **Impartial analysis** (p. 26): "The City currently collects a local one (1) cent sales tax under the previously adopted Measure U." … "The proposed increased local sales tax would continue until repealed by the voters of the City. As a general local tax approved by the voters of Santa Maria, Measure C2026's revenue must be placed in the City's general fund and used for general governmental purposes. It is estimated that Measure C2026 will provide an additional $13 million in annual local funding" … "A "Yes" vote is a vote to approve the local one-half (1/2) cent sales tax. A "No" vote is a vote against the local one-half (1/2) cent sales tax."
