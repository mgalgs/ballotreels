Measure C asks the voters of this city whether to add a 0.5 percent local sales tax.
Today, the city already collects a 1 percent local sales tax under an earlier measure.
Measure C would add half a percent more, collected the same way, until voters repeal it.
As a general tax, the money would go to the city general fund, for general government purposes.
Measure C needs a simple majority to pass.
The city attorney's impartial analysis estimates that Measure C would bring in an added $13 million a year.
No argument against Measure C was filed, so the county voter guide prints only the one in favor.
Supporters say Measure C keeps youth out of gangs, and keeps parks and the aquatic center open year-round.
A yes vote approves the half-percent local sales tax.
A no vote is a vote against the half-percent local sales tax.
