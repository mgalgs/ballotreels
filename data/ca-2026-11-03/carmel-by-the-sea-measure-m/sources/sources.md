## Quoted facts

- **Ballot question** (Resolution 2026-042, §3; OCR artefacts like "12o/o" = 12%, "$t,900,000" = $1,900,000 corrected here): "To upgrade streets/sidewalks, address beach erosion, enhance wildfire preparedness, maintain City trees, and fund other general government purposes in City of Carmel-by-the-Sea, shall the measure establishing a new 2% transient occupancy tax, paid by lodging guests, in addition to the existing 10% transient occupancy tax, increasing the total tax rate to 12%, with the new 2% tax generating approximately $1,900,000 annually, and the total 12% tax generating $11,700,000 annually, until ended by voters, be adopted?"
- **What it does** (§2): "establishes a new 2% transient occupancy tax that is in addition to the existing 10% transient occupancy tax so the total tax rate is 12% ... and will be effective until ended by voters".
- **Threshold** (§5): "The vote requirement for this Measure to pass shall be a majority of those casting ballots on the Measure (50% plus 1)."
- **Adoption:** "PASSED AND ADOPTED ... this 2nd day of June, 2026 ... AYES: Councilmembers Baron, Buder, Delves, Dramov, and Mayor Byrne NOES: None".
