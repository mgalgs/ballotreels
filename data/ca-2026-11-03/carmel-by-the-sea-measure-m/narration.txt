Measure M asks the voters of this city whether to add a new 2 percent tax on lodging stays, on top of the existing 10 percent tax.
All five members of the City Council voted to place Measure M on the ballot.
Lodging guests pay a tax of 10 percent of the rent charged.
Measure M would add a new 2 percent tax, for a total of 12 percent of the rent.
As a general tax, the money from the new tax would go to the general fund, for any general city purpose.
The new tax would last until ended by voters, and needs a simple majority to pass.
The ballot question says the new tax would raise about $1.9 million a year, and the total tax about $11.7 million.
Any argument for Measure M is filed with the city clerk, and is not online.
Any argument against Measure M is filed with the city clerk, and is not online.
A yes vote adopts the new 2 percent tax on lodging stays.
A no vote does not adopt the new 2 percent tax on lodging stays.
