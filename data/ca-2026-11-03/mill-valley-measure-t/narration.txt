Measure T asks the voters of this city whether to renew a special tax for fire suppression and road repair, for 10 years.
A city staff report says the current service tax expires in mid-2027.
The tax would be $303 a year for each single-family home, rising 2 percent each year.
The staff report says that rate is no increase over what taxpayers pay now.
Homeowners over 65 who live in their homes, with incomes at or below the median income scale of a county housing authority, would not pay.
Until the tax ends in 2037, its money could go only to fire suppression, including vegetation and tree work, and to street repair.
The same vote would set a legal spending limit for the new tax district, equal to what the tax collects each year.
Measure T needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The ballot question says the tax would raise an average of $1.9 million a year over 10 years.

No argument for Measure T appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
A yes vote renews the service tax for 10 years and sets the district's spending limit.
A no vote does not renew the service tax, which expires in mid-2027.
