City of Folsom Measure I asks voters in the city whether the City Council, not the city manager, should appoint the City Attorney.
Today, the city manager appoints and removes the City Attorney, Folsom's chief legal officer.
The City Council placed Measure I on the ballot, and it needs a simple majority.
Measure I would let the City Council appoint, suspend or remove the City Attorney.
The City Attorney would get a public hearing before removal, and could not be removed within 90 days of a new council member taking office.
The Sacramento County Current Measure Information page does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Supporters say most California councils appoint their city attorney, keeping accountability with elected officials.
No argument against Measure I was filed with Sacramento County Voter Registration and Elections.
A yes vote authorizes the charter amendment letting the City Council appoint, suspend or remove the City Attorney.
A no vote does not authorize that charter amendment.
