## Key facts, quoted

- **Ballot question:** "Shall the measure amending the Adopted Charter of the City of Folsom to have the City Council appoint and direct the City Attorney and to make related operational changes, be adopted?"
- **Analysis heading:** "City Clerk's / Elections Official's Impartial Analysis of Measure "I"" — signed "s/Christa Freemantle, City Clerk/Elections Official". (The page's box label says "Prepared by City Counsel".)
- **Current law:** "The City Charter currently provides that the City Attorney is a department head who may be appointed and removed by the Folsom City Manager."
- **Placed by / threshold:** "On July 14, 2026, the City Council placed Measure "I" on the November 3, 2026 General Municipal Election ballot. If passed by a majority of Folsom voters (50%+1), Measure "I" would amend the City Charter to provide that the City Council, and not the City Manager, shall have the power to appoint, suspend or remove the City Attorney."
- **Conditions:** "Measure "I" expressly requires the City Attorney to be a member of the California State Bar and chosen solely on the basis of administrative qualifications." "The City Attorney has the right to a public hearing prior to being removed or suspended … only after 10 days' published notice". It "prohibits the City Council from removing, suspending, requesting the resignation of, or reducing the duties, salary or benefits of the City Attorney during a period of 90 days immediately following the installation of any newly elected or newly appointed City Councilmember."
- **Yes/No:** "A "yes" vote on Measure "I" will authorize a Charter amendment to give the City Council the authority to appoint, suspend or remove the City Attorney, subject to the procedural and timing rules above." / "A "no" vote on Measure "I" will not authorize a Charter amendment …"
- **Full text:** "ORDINANCE NO. 1371 — AN ORDINANCE OF THE PEOPLE OF THE CITY OF FOLSOM AMENDING SECTION 4.03, "CITY ATTORNEY," OF THE CITY CHARTER …"
- **Argument in favor:** signed "s/Justin Raithel, Mayor, City of Folsom". It states (author's claim, not the county's): "City councils appoint the city attorney in 469 of California's 482 incorporated cities."
- **Not filed:** "***NO REBUTTAL TO ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE I WAS FILED***", "***NO ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE I WAS FILED***", "***NO REBUTTAL TO ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE I WAS FILED***".
