ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE Q

Vote YES on Measure Q to preserve Diamond Bar's quality of life, protect local resources, and prevent crime.

Since choosing local control in 1989, Diamond Bar residents have enjoyed safe neighborhoods, clean streets, well-maintained parks and infrastructure, and quality public services that build community. But new challenges threaten our way of life. Unfunded mandates from the state and the rising costs of services and infrastructure maintenance have led to a structural budget deficit that could reach the millions of dollars if the City does not continue to cut services and programs. All the while, Sacramento and the County take more and more of what is rightfully ours.

WE MUST ACT NOW TO PROTECT OUR COMMUNITY'S FUTURE.

A YES vote provides sustainable local resources to:

- Maintain Sheriff's patrols to prevent property crime
- Maintain 911 response
- Prepare for wildfires and other emergencies
- Maintain streets and sidewalks and repair potholes
- Repair local parks, facilities, and public infrastructure
- Repair parkways and medians
- Address homelessness

A YES vote ensures your tax dollars are only invested locally. For too long, residents have seen their tax dollars taken by Sacramento and the County with little to no local return. And if we don't act now, there's nothing stopping them from taking more. Measure Q rights these wrongs by keeping every dollar local and accountable to you with transparent public audits, public participation in the budgeting process, and direct fiscal oversight. NOT ONE PENNY GOES TO SACRAMENTO OR THE COUNTY.

Invest in your community and join local leaders and your neighbors in voting YES on Measure Q.

JODY ROBERTO

DEVANG MEHTA
