Newport Beach Measure H asks the voters of Newport Beach whether to amend the General Plan's housing rules for five areas.
Voters placed Measure H on the ballot by signing a petition.
State law requires Newport Beach to plan enough sites for its share of the region's housing needs, at each income level.
Today, the housing policy in the General Plan allows up to 8,174 homes in five areas.
Measure H would lower that limit to 2,900 homes: 2,160 for low incomes or below, and 740 for moderate incomes.
Only a later vote of the people could change that part of the General Plan.
Measure H does not approve any specific building project.
The Orange County Voter Information Guide does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Opponents say Measure H identifies no funding for subsidies or infrastructure, and risks financial penalties under housing law.
Supporters say Measure H reverses the council's approval of 8,174 homes, with a plan meeting state housing law.
A yes vote is a vote in favor of amending the General Plan.
A no vote is a vote against amending the General Plan.
