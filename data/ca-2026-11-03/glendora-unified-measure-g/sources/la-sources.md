## Ballot question (verbatim, from the county measure list)

> Glendora Unified School District Classroom Repair Extension Measure. To repair or replace deteriorating roofs, plumbing, HVAC, electrical systems; remove asbestos and lead pipes; upgrade classrooms, labs, facilities to support college/career readiness in math, science, engineering, technology and skilled trades; shall Glendora Unified School District's measure be adopted, authorizing $86,000,000 in bonds at legal rates (without increasing current tax rates), levying approximately $33 per $100,000 assessed value ($5,000,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding, with independent oversight/audits, and funds locally controlled?

Supporters: Art Ludwick; Glendora Ed Foundation; Dr. Cliff Hamlow, Fmr Mayor, Glendora Teachers Assoc.; Mendell Thompson, City Council
Opponents: Mel Bennett

Vote threshold: 55% of the votes cast (Prop 39 school bond).

Note: the earlier research pass's `sources.md` flagged an opponent ("We Are Glendora" / Mel Benett) as unconfirmed secondhand reporting. The county's official final measures list — grepped directly for this task — in fact lists "Opponents: Mel Bennett" (note the double-t spelling), so an argument against this measure **was** officially filed; only its text was not located.
