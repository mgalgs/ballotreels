REBUTTAL TO ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE G

Don't be misled. Measure G is a fiscally responsible plan to repair aging Glendora schools WITHOUT INCREASING TAX RATES.

The FACTS:

- By law, school bond tax rates cannot be capped. This is true for every bond measure – not unique to Measure G. Glendora Unified School District uses conservative projections to show that extending the $33 per $100,000 we currently pay supports Measure G without increasing tax rates. GUSD voters have approved only two school bonds in over 30 years – the last one over 20 years ago. As the 2000 bond is retired, Measure G extends the current rate to repair and upgrade aging schools.

- GUSD is responsibly using the Whitcomb property. This surplus property is being exchanged to provide a revenue stream for daily school operations like teaching, academic programs, and school maintenance. These dollars can patch an otherwise sound roof or repair an HVAC unit. They cannot replace deteriorating roofs, plumbing, electrical, heating and cooling systems across many schools.

- State matching funds are never guaranteed. School districts apply once they have required local matching funds. Measure G allows Glendora to compete for over $50,000,000 in matching funds our residents already help fund through state taxes. Without Measure G, those dollars improve schools elsewhere.

The opposition doesn't dispute that 70-year-old schools need repairs – waiting only makes them more expensive.

Measure G requires a specific project list, public disclosure, independent oversight, and annual audits. Funds stay local and cannot be spent on salaries/benefits.

Trusted Glendora leaders agree: YES on G to repair Glendora schools WITHOUT INCREASING TAX RATES.
