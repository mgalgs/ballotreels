GLENDORA UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT – (1)
VOTE PASSAGE – 55% of the Votes Cast
G      Glendora Unified School District Classroom Repair Extension Measure. To repair or
       replace deteriorating roofs, plumbing, HVAC, electrical systems; remove asbestos and
       lead pipes; upgrade classrooms, labs, facilities to support college/career readiness in
       math, science, engineering, technology and skilled trades; shall Glendora Unified School
       District's measure be adopted, authorizing $86,000,000 in bonds at legal rates (without
       increasing current tax rates), levying approximately $33 per $100,000 assessed value
       ($5,000,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding, with independent oversight/audits,
       and funds locally controlled? Supporters: Art Ludwick; Glendora Ed Foundation; Dr.
       Cliff Hamlow, Fmr Mayor, Glendora Teachers Assoc.; Mendell Thompson, City Council
       Opponents: Mel Bennett
