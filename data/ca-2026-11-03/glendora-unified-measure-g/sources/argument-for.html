ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE G

Vote YES on G to repair aging Glendora schools WITHOUT INCREASING TAX RATES.

Glendora schools have a long tradition of providing excellent education to local students that helps them succeed in college and careers. However, many classrooms and school facilities are nearly 70 years old. Aging pipes, roofs, plumbing, sewer lines, air conditioning, and electrical systems are failing now, causing gas leaks, water leaks, sewage backups, roof leaks, and other problems that will only become more expensive if we wait.

Measure G repairs and upgrades our aging schools without increasing the current tax rate. It simply extends the voter-approved rate already in place without an increase.

This fiscally responsible plan also helps our schools qualify for $50,000,000 or more in state matching funds that will otherwise go to schools in other communities, not Glendora.

Voting YES on G will:
- Repair deteriorating roofs, plumbing, sewer, electrical, and HVAC systems
- Remove hazardous materials, including asbestos and lead pipes
- Update classrooms, labs, and technology for science, math, engineering, and career training
- Improve student safety and campus security, including alarms, cameras, locks, fencing, and emergency systems

Measure G includes strict fiscal accountability protections and local control:
- A detailed project list and required public disclosure of all spending
- All funds stay local and cannot be taken by the state or federal governments
- Independent oversight and annual audits are required
- No funds may be used for administrator or employee salaries or benefits

Strong schools protect property values, support our local economy, help attract and retain quality teachers and families, and improve quality of life for everyone, whether or not you have children in school.

Join parents, grandparents, teachers, school leaders, business leaders, and neighbors who value safe, excellent local schools and fiscal responsibility - vote YES on G to repair Glendora schools WITHOUT INCREASING TAX RATES.
