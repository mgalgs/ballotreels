REBUTTAL TO ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE G

Proponents cite failing systems, yet GUSD's own state-required 2025 SARCs rated every campus's Overall Facility Rate "Good." Despite Public Records Act requests filed June 2 and August 11, 2026, the District has produced no facility assessment or documentation supporting the scope or cost of the repairs claimed.

Measure G would be one of three GUSD bonds on properties.

Measure G would authorize a new $86 million bond - with a total 30-year obligation of approximately $161,348,832 through 2056-2057.

Meanwhile, the District is in escrow to exchange Whitcomb for $28,450,000 in income-producing property, throwing off roughly $1.4 million a year - revenue that arrives regardless of this bond, yet the District has never analyzed whether it reduces the need for one.

"Without increasing tax rates" is a projection, not a guarantee. The District's own materials call the rate estimates "informational only" and "not maximum amounts or limitations."

The existing bond is scheduled to retire in August 2027. Voting NO lets that reduction happen; voting YES replaces it with a new 30-year obligation.

Before a $161 million, 30-year obligation, taxpayers deserve assessments and an accounting of unused resources already on hand. The District has offered neither. Vote NO on Measure G.

MEL BENNET
Founder We Are Glendora (WAG)
