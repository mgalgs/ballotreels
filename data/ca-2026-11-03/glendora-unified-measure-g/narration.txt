Measure G asks the voters of this school district whether to approve $86 million in school bonds.
Property owners here already pay a tax for school bonds voters approved in 2000: $33 per $100,000 of assessed value in 2025-2026.
Measure G needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The district expects the new bonds would not raise that $33 rate, but the tax would be collected for more years.
The measure says the district's expectation is not a legal limit on the tax rate.
The bond money could pay for roof, plumbing and heating repairs, removing asbestos and lead pipes, and upgrading classrooms and labs.
The district's tax rate statement estimates the tax for these bonds at $33 per $100,000 of assessed value, through 2056-2057.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $161.3 million.
Opponents filed an argument against Measure G; its text is not among the documents gathered here.
Supporters filed an argument for Measure G; its text is not among the documents gathered here.
A yes vote approves the $86 million in school bonds.
A no vote rejects the $86 million in school bonds.
