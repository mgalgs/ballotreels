## Quoted facts

- **Who votes:** voters of the Isla Vista Community Services District. Majority needed (impartial analysis below).
- **Ballot question** (sample ballot, p. 33): "Establishment of an Appropriations Limit. The appropriations limit on spending the proceeds of taxes imposed by the Isla Vista Community Services District may be established by the electors of the District. In succeeding years, the appropriations limit will be equal to the prior year's limit, subject only to specified adjustments in the Government Code; shall voters establish an appropriations limit of $3,500,000/fiscal year subject only to certain statutory adjustments, until voters decide to change it?"
- **Impartial analysis** (p. 25, County Counsel, dated August 7, 2026): "This Measure was placed on the ballot by the Board of Directors of the Isla Vista Community Services District (District), which is the governing board of the District. If approved by a majority of the votes cast by voters voting on the proposition, this Measure establishes an appropriations limit of the District of $3,500,000 per fiscal year. This appropriations limit is subject to adjustment each year pursuant to the procedures in California Government Code section 7900, et seq."
- **Full text** (p. 27): "A RESOLUTION OF THE PEOPLE OF THE ISLA VISTA COMMUNITY SERVICES DISTRICT ESTABLISHING THE INITIAL APPROPRIATIONS LIMIT FOR THE DISTRICT PURSUANT TO ARTICLE XIII B § 4 OF THE CALIFORNIA CONSTITUTION"
