Measure F asks the voters of this community services district to set a yearly limit on the district's spending of tax money.
The state constitution requires units of government, including this district, to have such a spending limit.
Voters may set the first limit, and the district's board then adjusts it each year for inflation and population.
Measure F needs a majority of the vote to pass.
Measure F would set the limit at $3.5 million each fiscal year, adjusted under state law, until voters change it.
The official voter guide for this election does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
The voter guide says no argument against Measure F was filed.
The voter guide says no argument for Measure F was filed.
A yes vote sets the yearly limit at $3.5 million.
A no vote does not set that limit.
