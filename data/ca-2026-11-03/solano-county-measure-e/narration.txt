Measure E asks the voters of this county whether to raise and widen a business tax on energy facilities and data centers.
Today, commercial wind turbines in the unincorporated parts of the county pay a business license tax on the power they generate.
Measure E would raise the wind turbine rate, and add new taxes on commercial solar, battery storage, natural gas extraction and data centers.
Starting in 2028, the rates would be adjusted each year for inflation, but could not go down.
The money would go into the county general fund for any lawful purpose, with no end date, and the measure needs a simple majority.
The impartial analysis says county staff estimate Measure E would raise about $353,000 more each year.
Opponents say higher business taxes raise everyday prices for residents, and would discourage clean energy investment that creates local jobs.
Supporters say the wind rate has not changed since 1994, and new energy facilities should help pay for county services.
A yes vote approves the changes to the county business license tax.
A no vote keeps the current business license tax, without the new categories or the inflation adjustments.
