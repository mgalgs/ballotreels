## Facts with quotes (guide page numbers are PDF pages of the full guide)

- **Ballot label (p. 58):** "Without increasing current tax rates, to repair and replace leaky roofs and windows; construct facilities for career technical education; upgrade inadequate electrical and air-conditioning systems; make health, safety and security improvements and repair classrooms, restrooms and school facilities, shall Oroville City Elementary School District's measure authorizing $16,000,000 of bonds at legal rates be adopted, with average levies of $16 per $100,000 assessed value (approximately $1,000,00 annually), while bonds are outstanding, citizens' oversight, annual audits, and all money staying local? Bond – YES or Bond – NO" — note "$1,000,00" is printed that way.
- **Vote needed:** "Pursuant to Section 18 of Article XVI and Section 1 of Article XIIIA of the California Constitution, this measure will become effective upon the affirmative vote of at least 55% of the qualified electors voting on this measure." (p. 58)
- **Amount:** "seeks voter approval to authorize the District to issue $16 million of bonds at legal rates." (p. 58)
- **Use limits:** "Proceeds may not be used for any other purpose such as teacher salaries and other operating expenses." (p. 58)
- **Tax rate:** "The best estimate of the tax rate required to be levied to fund the bonds is $16.28 per $100,000 of assessed valuation and the bonds issued pursuant to this measure are not expected to increase the current tax rate in the District. The final fiscal year in which it is anticipated that the tax will be collected is 2059-60." (p. 59)
- **Total debt service:** "approximately $33,635,000." (p. 59; also tax statement item 3, p. 69)
- **Tax statement, highest rate:** "The best estimate of the highest tax rate that would be required to fund this bond issue ... is $16.28 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. This rate is projected to apply in each fiscal year that bonds are outstanding." (p. 69)
- **No increase claim (district's statement):** "the District expects that such levy will not increase the cumulative fiscal year 2025-26 tax rate of $16.28 per $100,000." (p. 69) Signed "s/Spencer Holtom, Superintendent - Oroville City Elementary School District" (p. 70).
- **Oversight:** "The Board of Trustees ("Board") will appoint a citizens' oversight committee to ensure that the bond proceeds are spent for the projects listed in the measure. The Board will also conduct annual, independent performance and financial audits." (p. 59)
- **Who placed it:** "This measure is placed on the ballot by the governing board of the District." Analysis signed "s/Brad J. Stephens, Butte County Counsel" (p. 59)
- **Argument in favor signers (p. 60):** Janet Goodson (Oroville City Council Member), Vince Xiong (OCESD Educator), Betty Pendergraft (OCESD Parent Leader and Employee), Vanessa Hernandez-Felix (Ophir PTO President; Local Business Owner), Charles Tracy (Retired Educator; Local Business Owner). Its claim "Measure Q will give the Oroville City Elementary School District access to over $11 million in State matching funds" is the proponents' statement, not the analysis.
- **Argument against (p. 61):** "No argument against was filed."
