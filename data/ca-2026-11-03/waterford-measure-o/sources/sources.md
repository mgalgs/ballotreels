## Quoted facts

- Authority: analysis, "Pursuant to Government Code section 34871, the City Council may submit to the electors the question of whether the City Council of the City of Waterford be elected by districts in four districts with the Mayor elected at-large."
- Origin: "On April 16, 2026, the City Council of the City of Waterford approved the submittal of the question to the qualified electors of the City of Waterford of whether the City Council of the City of Waterford be elected by districts in four districts with the Mayor being at-large and directed that the measure be placed on the November 3, 2026 ballot."
- Effect: "The Ballot Measure, if approved, would be effective immediately and the next election of the City Council of the City of Waterford would be by districts and the Mayor at-large."
- Yes: "Change how City Council members are elected from “at large” to “by district” with district boundaries based on the proposed Council District Map"; "Only those voters within the City Council district would be allowed to elect the council member for that particular district".
- No: "Not change the current method of electing city council members. Each council member would continue to be elected “at large” by the voters citywide."
- Full text recital: "the City reviewed the election and Census data, the City has only one or two precincts in each election, and the City was unable to identify the presence of racially polarized voting."
