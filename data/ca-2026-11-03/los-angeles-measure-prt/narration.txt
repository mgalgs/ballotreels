City of Los Angeles Measure PRT asks voters in the city whether to change charter rules for the airports, the harbor, and the Department of Water and Power.
Today, the City Council can approve leases of up to 50 years for the airports and the water and power department, and up to 66 years for the harbor.
Measure PRT would allow leases of up to 66 years for the airports and the water and power department as well.
Of the seven airport commissioners, three would have to live near Los Angeles International Airport and two near Van Nuys Airport, up from one each.
The harbor would set aside part of its operating income each year for waterfront public access projects, with the harbor board deciding how much.
The City Administrative Officer says the harbor already sets aside 10 percent of its operating income, about $28.6 million a year, and calls the other changes cost-neutral.
No argument against Measure PRT was submitted, so the voter pamphlet from the City Clerk prints only the argument in favor of it.
Supporters say Measure PRT gives neighbors of the airports more representation on the airport board, and keeps effects on local jobs in port decisions.
A yes vote means you want the charter amended to make these changes.
A no vote means you do not want the charter amended.
