City of San Bruno Measure S asks voters in the city whether to repeal a 1977 zoning law requiring voter approval for some buildings.
Today, city voters must approve buildings over three stories or fifty feet tall, denser housing, and multistory parking structures.
The City Council placed Measure S on the ballot, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
Measure S would end the citywide vote, leaving such projects to city staff, the planning commission or the City Council.
The published measure documents of San Mateo County do not include a cost estimate for the measure.
No argument against Measure S appears in the published measure documents of San Mateo County.
Supporters say state housing laws overrule the 1977 law, and repeal would attract tax-paying development.
A yes vote on Measure S removes the voter approval requirements.
A no vote on Measure S keeps the voter approval requirements.
