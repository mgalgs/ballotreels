Argument in Favor of Measure "V"

Vote YES on Measure "V" – Investing in the Future of Alview-Dairyland Schools. Our schools are among our community's most vital assets. Quality local schools drive student achievement, prepare children for productive futures, increase property values, and foster a safer, more connected community. For generations, local families have trusted Alview-Dairyland schools with their children's education.

While our dedicated teachers and staff do an outstanding job, our facilities are now over 100 years old and falling behind. This upcoming election on November 3rd, local voters have a historic opportunity to support Measure "V" and invest in the long-term health of our schools.

Why Measure "V" is Urgently Needed:

• A New Community Gymnasium: Alview-Dairyland is one of the few districts in the entire state without a school gym. Measure "V" will fund a new gymnasium for both student and community use.
• Critical Repairs: Funding will address essential infrastructure needs, including repairing or replacing aging roofs, outdated electrical, HVAC, plumbing, and sewer systems.
• 21st-Century Classrooms: Although well-maintained, our classrooms must be updated to meet modern educational and safety standards.
• A Historic First: Unlike other districts that request funding every 8-to-10 years, this is the very first funding measure in Alview-Dairyland's history.

Taxpayer Protections:

• Full Accountability: By law, all funds must be spent on facility improvements — not administrative salaries or operations.
• Independent Oversight: An independent oversight committee will audit spending to guarantee funds are used as authorized by voters.
• 100% Local Funds: Every dollar raised stays right here to benefit our students and community.

Without this investment, our facilities will continue to decline, leading to much higher emergency repair costs down the road. By investing in our schools today, we secure a safe, modern, and thriving educational environment for generations to come.

Join your neighbors – VOTE YES ON MEASURE "V"!
