## Facts

**Ballot question.** Registrar's legal notice, July 24, 2026:
> "Alview-Dairyland Union School District, Bond Measure: To improve the quality of education; build a gymnasium at Dairyland for school and community use; upgrade classrooms; repair roofs, windows, electrical, HVAC and plumbing; and make safety/security improvements; shall Alview-Dairyland Union School District's measure be adopted to issue $7,300,000 of bonds at legal rates, for the first time in District history, with average rates of $28.25 per $100,000 assessed value ($454,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding, audits, oversight, NO money for salaries, all money staying local?"
(Matches resolution Appendix B, p. 12, as seen at low resolution. Not compared word by word.)

**Vote needed; what it does.** Impartial Analysis of Measure "V" (Regina A. Garza, Madera County Counsel):
> "The Measure was placed on the ballot by the Board of Trustees of the Alview-Dairyland School District ("District"). If approved, the Measure would authorize the District to issue $7,300,000 in general obligation bonds to fund educational facility projects for the District's schools. ... To pass, the Measure must be approved by fifty-five (55%) of voters who vote in the election."
> "The District's best estimate of the highest tax rate to be collected to repay the bonds is $30.00 per $100,000 of assessed value of property within the District. The District's best estimate of the total amount, including interest, that will be required to repay the bonds is $16,088,166."

**Who votes.** Same analysis: "If the Measure is not approved by fifty-five percent (55%) of the qualified District electors voting on the Measure ..." Voters of the Alview-Dairyland Union School District vote on it.

**Tax rate statement.** Resolution Appendix C, PDF p. 13:
> "1. The best estimate of the average annual tax rate that would be required to fund this bond issue over the entire duration of the bond debt service ... is $28.25 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. It is currently expected that the tax will be collected until fiscal year 2060-61."
> "2. The best estimate of the highest tax rate ... is $30.00 per $100,000 of assessed valuation."
> "3. The best estimate of total debt service, including principal and interest ... will be approximately $16,088,166."
