Measure A asks the voters of this road zone whether to raise its yearly road tax from $300 to $450 per parcel.
The county formed the zone in 1986 to pay for road improvements and maintenance.
The county counts 103 registered voters living in the zone.
The Board of Supervisors placed Measure A on the ballot at the request of the zone's advisory committee.
Measure A needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The new tax would apply to every parcel, built on or not, starting in the 2027-2028 fiscal year.
The money could pay only for the roads in the zone, and the tax would last until voters change or repeal it.
If Measure A fails, the $300 tax stays.
The county attorney's impartial analysis expects the tax to raise about $33,300 a year.
No argument for Measure A was submitted.
No argument against Measure A was submitted.
A yes vote authorizes the increased tax of $450 per parcel a year.
A no vote is against the increased tax, and the $300 tax stays.
