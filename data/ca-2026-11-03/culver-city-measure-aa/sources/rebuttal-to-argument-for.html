REBUTTAL TO ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE AA

Measure AA is a well-intentioned, but terribly-timed effort.

We are in the midst of an affordability crisis. AND we just voted for a huge amount of money for our schools in 2024.

We here in Culver City have always been EXTREMELY generous to our schools and city. But enough is enough.

Measure AA = Always Asking (for more money).

Voters have already supported TEN tax hikes in Culver City since 2014. AlwaysAsking.org, lists them all. This website will also help you figure out how much you will pay if this Always Asking Measure passes. Typical homeowners can expect to see $500-$1000 of new taxes. Larger buildings can be charged up to $15,000 per parcel each year.

Ultimately, we can't control the price of our groceries. We can't control the price of our gas. We can't control all the greedy corporations trying to take our money.

However, here in California, we CAN control our tax increases with our vote. So this Nov. 3, Vote NO on Measure AA.

It's time to say NO to those "Always Asking" for more tax hikes.

JOHN DEREVLANY
Long-Time Homeowner
