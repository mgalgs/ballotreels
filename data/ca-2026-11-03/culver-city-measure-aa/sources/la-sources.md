## County measure list excerpt

See `county-measure-list-excerpt.txt`, cut from the LA County RR/CC's official "FINAL — GENERAL ELECTION November 3, 2026 — Measures Appearing on the Ballot" (Rev. 9/22/26):

> **Shall the measure, to strengthen academic excellence, attract and retain teachers, support music/arts/athletics/STEM programs, instructional aides, and for other designated purposes, by imposing a special tax on all real estate parcels, including commercial use, of $0.48/building square foot, with senior exemptions, independent citizen/auditor oversight, subject to a tax cap, for 10 years, generating approximately $18,000,000 annually in local funds for Culver City public schools that the state cannot take be adopted?**
>
> Supporters: Darrel Menthe; Ross Piro; Culver City Federation of Teachers; CCUSD Association of Classified Employees; Ji Young Denick
> Opponents: John Derevlany

Vote threshold: Majority Vote 50%+1.
