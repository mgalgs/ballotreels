ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE AA

Culver City residents strongly support public education, but ENOUGH IS ENOUGH.

Nearly every year, we're asked to vote for ANOTHER tax increase. This will be one of the biggest yet: about $800 per household (based on the city's average home size of 1618 sf).

We just voted for a $189 parcel tax for our schools in Nov. 2024, and a $358 million bond measure in March 2024. Those two votes alone added nearly $1,000 a year to my tax bill. I'm sure plenty of homeowners are paying even more.

Plus, there were votes to increase our sales tax in 2025, 2020, and 2018, as well as a $99 a year parcel tax for clean water in 2016. Culver City now has one of the highest sales tax rates in the nation.

I hear a lot of gripes about "affordability" these days. So here's a helpful tip for everyone: STOP VOTING TO RAISE OUR TAXES!

Do our schools need money? Sure, that's why you voted for all the bonds and parcel taxes in 2024 (as well as other parcel taxes in 2018 and 2010). Do we really need yet another school tax just two years after the last one? Plenty of neighboring school districts achieve higher academic ratings and student outcomes without forcing residents to pay ANY extra taxes at all.

So this November, vote for affordability. Vote for common sense. Vote NO on Measure AA.

JOHN DEREVLANY
26-Year Resident and Homeowner
