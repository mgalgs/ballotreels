ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE AA

Support Culver City Schools!

Measure AA was put on the ballot by an all-volunteer group of Culver City parents, residents, and teachers who worked together all year to craft the measure and gather enough signatures to place it on the ballot. Thanks to thousands of you who signed the petition, we finally have the opportunity to put local dollars into Culver City schools that cannot be taken by the State. The final step on our journey together is to vote YES on Measure AA.

Our tagline is "Strong schools, strong homes." We believe that strong public schools mean stronger communities and better home values. "Strong schools" means having the resources to attract and retain the best teachers and support staff.

We need Measure AA because Culver City receives the lowest funding allocation per student possible from the State of California. Our kids deserve full supply closets and no economic barriers to participation in sports, STEM, arts, extracurriculars, and music.

Measure AA ensures responsible spending. Measure AA establishes committee of parents and residents, not chosen by the school board, who will write a plan for our money each year before a single dollar is budgeted or spent. We can create new programs and strengthen existing programs. This is the community's choice, and our community's voice.

The Measure AA "sliding scale" mechanism ensures tax fairness. For the first time in Culver City history, commercial buildings and movie studios will be asked to pay their fair share.

Measure AA provides an exemption for homes owned by seniors and ADUs where seniors live.

This measure is supported by CCUSD teachers and staff. Every member of the CCUSD Board of Education signed the petition.

Measure AA will provide Culver City families with the great public schools we deserve.

Vote YES on Measure AA!
