REBUTTAL TO ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE AA

Great public schools require more than bare minimum state funding. There's no way around it. CCUSD is in danger of being taken over by the State if the district cannot put together a long-term financial solution that maintains reserves and avoids critical layoffs. The layoffs CCUSD has already been forced to make are unacceptable. Our children deserve better!

Contrary to the claims of the opposition, Culver City voters have not yet supported public schools with significant local dollars at the level other communities do. The only parcel tax that exists is $189 per parcel, whether you're a movie studio or a homeowner. In 2024, we voted to renew the existing tax at the same rate without even adjusting for inflation. A school bond can only be used for construction, not classroom services.

By contrast, Measure AA is a fair, progressive measure. Commercial properties will pay more of their fair share for the first time, and all the money can go to classroom services.

What the Opposition fails to appreciate is that the public schools are one of the most important contributors to home values. Strong schools = strong homes. Research indicates that well-funded public schools increase home values by 5%-10% long term. This is an investment in our kids, our community, and our homes. Seniors are exempt from this measure, but still get the property value boost!

Measure AA will be our long-term solution for underfunded schools.

Vote YES on Measure AA!
