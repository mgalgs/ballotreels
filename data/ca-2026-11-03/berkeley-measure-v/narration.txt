City of Berkeley Measure V asks voters in the city whether to add a 0.5 percent sales tax.
The City Council placed Measure V on the ballot, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
The total sales tax rate in Berkeley would rise to 10.75 percent, from April 2027 until voters repeal it.
The money would go into the general fund, and could be spent on any city purpose.
The City Attorney's impartial analysis says the city estimates the tax will raise between $9 million and $10 million a year.
Opponents say the permanent sales tax is regressive, and would fall hardest on students, low-income households, seniors and working families.
Supporters say Measure V would prevent forced cuts to fire and police, prevent a fire station closure, and maintain emergency response.
A yes vote is a vote in favor of the tax.
A no vote is a vote against the tax.
