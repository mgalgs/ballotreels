Measure W asks the voters of this school district whether to approve $6.9 million in school bonds.
The district would sell the bonds and repay them from a tax on property in the district.
Measure W needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money could build an athletic complex for school and community use, and upgrade playgrounds, tracks and baseball fields.
Bond money could also improve classrooms and restrooms, repair roofs, plumbing, heating and cooling, and improve safety and security systems.
Bond money could not pay teacher or administrator salaries, and a citizens' oversight committee and yearly audits would check the spending.
The district's tax statement estimates $30 per $100,000 of assessed value each year, through 2059-2060, the same rate every year.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $15.1 million.
