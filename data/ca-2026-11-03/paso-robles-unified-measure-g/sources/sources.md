## Quoted facts

- **Who votes:** voters of the Paso Robles Joint Unified School District. Notice: "the following measure will be submitted to the voters of the Paso Robles Joint Unified School District: MEASURE G-26".
- **Ballot question** (Notice): "With funds that cannot be taken by the State and spent elsewhere, shall Paso Robles Joint Unified School District's measure to renovate and expand classrooms and career training facilities; and repair leaky roofs and inefficient heating and air-conditioning be adopted authorizing $205 million of bonds with legal rates, average levies below $50 per $100,000 of assessed value (raising $12.2 million annually) while outstanding, audits, taxpayer oversight, no funds for administrator salaries, pensions or benefits, and full disclosure of all spending?"
- **Impartial analysis** (VIG p. 39): "Under the California Constitution, school districts may issue bonds if approved by at least 55 percent of voters within the boundaries of the district who are voting on a measure to authorize the issuance of bonds." … "The Board of Trustees of the Paso Robles Joint Unified School District ("the District") has placed Measure G-26 on the ballot, proposing the sale of bonds in an amount not to exceed $205 million at interest rates not to exceed legal limits."
- **Tax rate statement** (VIG p. 40): "An election will be held in Paso Robles Joint Unified School District (the "District") on November 3, 2026, for the purp[ose] …" (see PDF).
- **Arguments:** for, against, rebuttal to the argument against; "THERE WAS NO REBUTTAL TO THE ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE G-26 FILED".
