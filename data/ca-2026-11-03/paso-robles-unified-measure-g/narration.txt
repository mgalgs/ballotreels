Measure G asks the voters of this school district whether to approve $205 million in school bonds.
The district would sell the bonds and repay them with a yearly tax on property in the district.
Measure G needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money could pay only for facilities for the district's students in grades 9 through 12, such as renovating classrooms and career training facilities and repairing leaky roofs.
The district's tax rate statement estimates an average tax of $49.59 per $100,000 of assessed value through 2057-2058.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $378 million.
Opponents say the district should focus on academic performance first, as serious facility shortfalls still remain after an earlier bond.
Supporters say the state cannot take the money, which would update career training classrooms and repair leaky roofs and plumbing.
A yes vote is in favor of issuing $205 million in bonds for the measure's purposes.
A no vote is against issuing the bonds.
