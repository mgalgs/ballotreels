ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE ___

Vote YES on Measure ___.

Today, there is no limit on how long councilmembers can serve on the City Council . Someone can serve for decades.

This measure limits Council members to three consecutive four-year terms, twelve years of service, after which they must step away for two years before seeking a seat again. It is not a ban. It is a pause that reopens every seat to the community.

Term limits bring new voices, new ideas, and new energy into City Hall. They widen the path for residents who have never seen a realistic opening to run. They ensure that experience is renewed rather than merely accumulated, and that our government reflects the East Palo Alto of today.

Measure ___ is written to be fair. It applies going forward only. No current Council member’s past service is counted against them, and no one is removed from a seat they hold today.

Six Cities across San Mateo County already have term limits. The San Mateo County Board of Supervisors is limited to three terms and twelve years, exactly what this measure asks of us. The legislators who represent us in Sacramento, the Governor, and the President are all term-limited.

Twelve years is a full and generous opportunity to serve. After that, our neighbors deserve a real chance to choose someone new, and the best ideas should have to earn support all over again.

A vote for Measure ___ is a vote for accountability, for competition, and for a City Council that stays close to the people it serves.

Please join us. Vote YES on Measure ___.
