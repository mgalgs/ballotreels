# Measure CC — City of East Palo Alto (ordinance: City Council term limits)

- **Jurisdiction:** City of East Palo Alto.
- **Kind:** Council-placed ordinance amending the Municipal Code (adds Article IV, "Term Limits", Sec. 2.08.190, to Chapter 2.08 of Title 2). It is **not** a charter amendment, although ACRE's link title for the resolution calls it one (see Notes).
- **Who votes:** the voters of the City of East Palo Alto. Resolution No. 30-2026, Sec. 1 (resolution-full-text.pdf p. 1): the election is called "for the purpose of submitting the measure to a vote of the qualified voters of the City of East Palo Alto".
- **Vote threshold:** majority. Sec. 3 (p. 2): "To be effective, a majority of the City Council must vote to place the measure on the ballot and a majority of the voters voting on the measure must vote in the affirmative."
- **Placed by:** the City Council.
- **Election:** November 3, 2026.

## Ballot question

> Shall the measure providing that no person shall be eligible to serve for more than three (3) consecutive full terms on the City Council be adopted, with service of partial terms exceeding two years qualifying as a full term?

(resolution-full-text.pdf p. 2, Sec. 2; read from the page image.)

## Full text (resolution and ordinance)

- **Resolution No. 30-2026**, City Council of the City of East Palo Alto, "PASSED AND ADOPTED this 12th day of March 2026". AYES: Abrica, Barragan, Dinan, Lincoln, Romero. NOES, ABSENT, ABSTAIN: blank. Signed Webster Lincoln, Mayor; James Colin, City Clerk; approved as to form, John D. Lê (p. 3).
- Recital (p. 1): "at its duly noticed regular meeting held on March 3, 2026, the City Council determined to submit a proposed measure regarding City Council term limits to the voters of the City pursuant to Elections Code Section 9222 and Government Code Section 36502".
- **Exhibit A, Ordinance No. [blank]**, "adding Article IV to Chapter 2.08 of Title 2 of the East Palo Alto Municipal Code regarding term limits for councilmembers" (p. 4). New Sec. 2.08.190 (p. 4): "No person shall be eligible to serve as a member of the City Council for more than three consecutive four-year terms. If a person serves a partial term that exceeds two years, that partial term shall be considered a full term for purposes of this section. The section shall apply only to terms that begin after the effective date of this ordinance."
- Ordinance Sec. 2 (p. 4): "This Ordinance shall take effect as provided in Section 9217 of the Elections Code of the State of California."

## Impartial analysis

"CITY ATTORNEY'S IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE __", signed John D. Lê, City Attorney; filed August 18, 2026 (impartial-analysis.pdf p. 1).

- "Measure __ would amend the East Palo Alto Municipal Code to establish term limits for City Councilmembers."
- "Currently, the Municipal Code does not limit how long an individual may serve on the City Council. This measure would limit individuals to a maximum of three (3) consecutive four-year terms, or a total of twelve (12) years. A partial term of more than two (2) years would count as a full term."
- "The limitation would apply only to terms that begin after the ordinance takes effect, which under Elections Code Section 9217, would be in December 2026. Any terms served by an individual on the City Council before that date would not count towards the term limit."
- "This measure was placed on the ballot by the City Council of the City of East Palo Alto. It requires a majority vote to pass."
- "Voting "Yes" means adopting these term limits for City Councilmembers. Voting "No" means City Councilmembers would not be subject to term limits."

## Notes

- **Not a charter amendment.** ACRE's link title for the resolution is "City of East Palo Alto Charter Amendment Measure", but the resolution's title says "A MEASURE TO AMEND THE MUNICIPAL CODE TO ESTABLISH TERM LIMITS FOR CITY COUNCIL POSITIONS" (p. 1), the ordinance amends Municipal Code Chapter 2.08 (p. 4), and the impartial analysis says the measure "would amend the East Palo Alto Municipal Code". The resolution cites Government Code Section 36502 (p. 1).
- The argument in favor says Councilmembers "must step away for two years before seeking a seat again". The ordinance text limits "three consecutive four-year terms" and does not itself state a two-year break.
- The page-3 footer of the resolution reads "Resolution No. 32-202"; pp. 1-2 read "Resolution No. 30-2026", as does the title. Reported as printed.
- The impartial analysis and the primary arguments say "Measure __"; the rebuttal says "Measure CC". The printed guide presumably substitutes "CC"; unconfirmed, since the per-voter sample ballot pamphlet was not checked.
