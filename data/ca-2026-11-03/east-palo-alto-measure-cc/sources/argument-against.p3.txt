Ballot Argument Against Term Limits—Measure___

East Palo Alto already has term limits. It’s called voting out your city representatives when they are not meeting the community’s needs. Vote No on Measure___.

We already have that opportunity every two years in November. EPA voters have on numerous occasions not reelected councilmembers, selecting other candidates instead. Don’t be fooled into voting for a law limiting the people’s choice. Vote No on Measure___.

San Mateo County has 20 cities. There’s a sound reason why only 6 cities have term limits and 14 cities, a supermajority, do not. East Palo Altans disadvantage ourselves with term limits, handicapping our seasoned elected representatives from holding leadership positions on vital regional bodies. It takes time to be known and supported countywide by electeds. This measure gets in the way of that. Vote No on Measure_.

EPA is a small city that punches above its weight precisely because our representatives are more likely able to hold regional positions. Let’s keep it that way

If you don’t like an elected official, just vote them out of office. Don’t limit the people’s ability to vote for someone with proven and effective experience and who they trust is doing a good job. That is what this measure will do if passed. It hurts EPA. Vote No on Measure___.

/s/ Javanni Brown; Ravneel Chaudhary; Luis Rosas
