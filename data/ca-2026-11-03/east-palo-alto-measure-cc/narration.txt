East Palo Alto Measure CC asks city voters whether to limit City Council members to three consecutive full terms.
Today, the city code does not limit how long someone may serve on the City Council.
The City Council placed Measure CC on the ballot, and it needs a majority to pass.
Measure CC would allow at most three consecutive four-year terms, or 12 years.
A partial term of more than two years would count as a full term.
Only terms beginning after it takes effect, in December 2026, would count, so earlier service would not.
The published measure documents of San Mateo County do not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Opponents say voters already limit terms by voting members out, and limits keep seasoned members from regional leadership posts.
Supporters say term limits bring new voices and new ideas into city hall, and the limit applies going forward only.
A yes vote adopts these term limits for City Council members.
A no vote means City Council members would not be subject to term limits.
