## Facts and quotes

**Ballot question** (`ballot-question.html`): "St. Helena City Services Measure. To maintain/improve St. Helena's long-term finances and maintain 911 fire/public safety response, St. Helena's infrastructure including repairing roads/potholes/sidewalks; reducing local wildfire risk by managing brush/other fuels; maintain parks; supporting local businesses; for other general city services; shall the measure be adopted establishing a ½¢ locally controlled sales tax, generating approximately $2,000,000 annually until ended by voters, requiring independent audits, public spending disclosure, all funds benefiting St. Helena residents?"

**Impartial analysis** (`impartial-analysis.html`, "City Attorney's Impartial Analysis of Measure S, 1/2% Transactions and Use (Sales) Tax", /s/ Ethan Walsh, City Attorney), key statements:
- "On July 7, 2026, the City Council voted to place Measure S on the November 3, 2026, General Municipal Election ballot."
- **Who votes / vote needed:** "If approved by a majority of St. Helena voters, this Measure would authorize a one-half percent (1/2%) retail transactions and use (sales) tax within the City of St. Helena."
- "It is estimated that Measure S will provide an additional $2,000,000 in annual local funding ... This tax would be a "general tax," meaning that revenues raised from the tax would go into the City's general fund to pay for any lawful City program, improvement, or service."
- "If approved, the tax would remain in effect until repealed by voters at a subsequent election."
- "All revenues raised by the tax would remain in the City and would not be shared with the State, County, or any other agency."

**Index page:** "Type of Measure: General tax"; "Approval Required: Majority vote".
