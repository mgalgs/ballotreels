City of St. Helena Measure S asks St. Helena voters whether to add a one-half percent sales tax for city services.
The City Council voted in July to place Measure S on the ballot.
The tax would be paid on top of current sales taxes, and collected the same way.
The money would go into the general fund of the city, for any lawful program, improvement or service.
All of the money would stay in St. Helena, not shared with the state or the county.
The tax would last until voters repeal it, and needs a simple majority to pass.
The city attorney's impartial analysis estimates Measure S would raise about $2 million a year.
No argument for Measure S appears among the measure documents that the City of St. Helena posted.
No argument against Measure S appears among the measure documents that the City of St. Helena posted.
A yes vote authorizes the one-half percent sales tax.
A no vote does not authorize the one-half percent sales tax.
