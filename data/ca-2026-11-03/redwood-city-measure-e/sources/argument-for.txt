Argument in favor of Measure __

Mom and pop landlords, homeowners, and longtime Redwood City renters all agree: Rent control will keep Redwood City affordable and create stability for our community.

Corporate landlords and real estate trusts have bought up 87% of multifamily housing in Redwood City, and rents have skyrocketed. Our children can't find housing here. Our teachers, city employees, and essential workers live far away and commute for hours.

Residents are getting rent hikes of $350 a month, which may be higher once the temporary 10% state rent cap expires. Families are forced out by unjust evictions, intimidation, or health problems from neglected repairs. Seniors are at risk of homelessness.

With Measure __, Redwood City will join 30+ cities around California that have enacted fair rent control and meaningful renter protections, including Mountain View and East Palo Alto.

Measure __ will:
• Keep costs stable by allowing predictable rent increases of 60% of inflation or 5%, whichever is lower.
• Guarantee landlords a fair return on their investment—if costs increase, a landlord can petition the city for a larger rent increase.
• Only allow evictions if there is a "just cause" (e.g. failure to pay rent, breach of lease, owner moving into the unit).
• Protect against landlord use of intimidation and lack of repairs to push people out of their homes.
• Prevent homelessness through higher relocation assistance for no-fault evictions.

Measure __ will be funded by a small fee on landlords—not paid for by homeowners, renters, nor the city's general fund.

Measure __ was drafted by a diverse group of Redwood City residents who've been working for a decade to keep families and seniors in their homes, so our community can be safe and strong. Over 7,000 Redwood City voters signed petitions to get Measure __ on the ballot.

Join all of us, your neighbors, and vote YES on Measure __ to keep Redwood City affordable!
