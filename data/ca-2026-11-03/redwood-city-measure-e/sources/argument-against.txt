ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE __

The Redwood City Council voted 6-0 to oppose Measure _.

Measure _ was not prepared by the City Council or City staff. The measure repeals strong tenant protection measures adopted by the Redwood City Council that exceed State laws protecting renters.

Redwood City is committed to protecting renters. Many of us are renters ourselves, and our families have lived with the instability this measure claims to address.

But good intentions don't guarantee good policy. If approved by voters, Measure _ will mean less affordable housing, higher prices for renters, and new costs to the City likely impacting essential city programs and services.

An independent, City-commissioned analysis found this measure would cost Redwood City up to $11 million per year and require up to 18 new full-time employees to administer. The initiative prohibits the City from using General Fund dollars, but the math does not work.

Cuts to critical neighborhood services or new taxes will be needed to fund ongoing implementation. What is worse, this initiative targets non-profit affordable housing projects, single-family homes and in-law units with onerous regulations and fees.

Nonprofit housing providers would face new fees, unsustainable rent restrictions, and tightening operating budgets. This will drain funds needed for repairs, resident services, and long-term preservation of the affordable housing this measure claims to protect.

As a citizens' initiative, any future changes to the ordinance will require a costly city-wide election.

Redwood City is a housing leader: creating housing near transit, strong Tenant Protections, and millions awarded every year towards affordable housing.

This initiative will undo our progress, put the City at financial risk, reduce housing choice and affordability, target homeowners, and halt future affordable housing projects in our community.

Redwood City cannot afford Measure _.

Vote NO on Measure _.
