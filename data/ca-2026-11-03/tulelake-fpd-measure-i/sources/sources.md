## Facts with quotes

- **Ballot question (Resolution 2026-4, p. 2; same in Siskiyou's Notice of Election):** "Shall an ordinance of the Tulelake Multi-County Fire Protection District imposing an annual special parcel tax to maintain and improve fire protection and prevention services in the amount of $50 per parcel for unimproved parcels, $100 per parcel for improved parcels, and $150 for commercial parcels for each business occupancy, estimated to raise approximately $106,250 annually, which shall continue until repealed by the voters, be adopted?"
- **Two counties:** the ordinance refers to "other charges and taxes fixed and collected by Siskiyou County and Modoc County on behalf of the District" (p. 5) and to "The records of the Siskiyou County As[sessor]" (p. 4).
- **Vote needed:** Ordinance §14: "This Ordinance shall be adopted and become effective only if approved by two-t[hirds]" of voters (p. 6, text layer is imperfect).
- **Argument in favor (signed John Prosser, President, and Jeffrey Boyd, Secretary, Tulelake Multi-County Fire Protection District, dated 8-12-26):** "35 years ago the voters of the Tulelake Multi County Fire Protection District voted in favor of a special tax for fire protection services. The Cost of Living Index has risen by 177.35% since then but the District's parcel tax has remained the same." / "our volunteers responded to 302 calls in 2025, the majority of these being medical calls."
