Measure HB asks the voters of this city whether to add a half percent sales tax for general city services.
Four of the five City Council members voted to place Measure HB on the ballot.
Under the state constitution, a new general city tax must be approved by a majority of voters.
A general tax goes into the general fund, and may be spent on any lawful city purpose.
Measure HB would add a half percent tax on retail sales in the city, with no end date unless voters repeal it.
The ballot question lists public safety, streets, storm drains and parks among its uses, but the money could fund any city purpose.
The city would report the revenue each year, and a citizens' committee would review the reports and audits.
The impartial analysis says the measure estimates about $2 million a year, with actual revenue depending on taxable sales.
Supporters say the county is seeking higher charges for fire and lifeguard services, and every dollar the tax raises would stay local.
Opponents say the tax would put the sales tax here above neighboring cities, and the city balanced this year's budget without it.
A yes vote adopts the half percent sales tax.
A no vote rejects the half percent sales tax.
