REBUTTAL TO ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE HB

The argument against Measure HB is riddled with inaccuracies.

Opponents claim this tax won't fix the City's structural deficit. Wrong — the revenue closes the gap, full stop. And every dollar stays local, with a community oversight committee and independent annual audits to ensure accountability.

Hermosa Beach faces large new outside charges from LA County for fire and lifeguard services. The County wants to raise our fire services contract by 30% a year — nearly $2 million more. It also wants the City to pay for lifeguard services and beach maintenance — $4 million in new charges. Opponents say these bills can be ignored. Wrong. The County already successfully charges for lifeguard services in Santa Monica and Cabrillo Beach. Hermosa Beach owns its beach; our neighbors do not.

Opponents also claim West Hollywood's sales tax is on an escalator. Not true — after this October, it reaches 11%, because voters there approved a fixed 0.75% increase in 2021 to keep money in their city.

We must pass Measure HB before the County places its own sales tax measure on the ballot, maxing out what state law allows. Manhattan Beach saw this happening and voted to increase their sales tax and keep tax money in the city, not let the county take it. Failure to pass this increase we're left cutting public safety staffing while infrastructure repairs grind to a halt.

Keep our money local, with real oversight — Vote YES on Measure HB.
