## Ballot question and supporters/opponents (verbatim, from the county's final measures list)

> Shall the measure to adopt a one-half percent (0.5%) local transactions and use (sales) tax providing approximately two million dollars ($2,000,000) annually until ended by voters (with citizens' oversight, independent audits, public reporting, and all funds remaining under local control) to maintain public safety services; repair and maintain streets, sidewalks, storm drains, parks, public facilities, and other infrastructure; address long-term fiscal sustainability; and support other general governmental purposes be adopted?
>
> Supporters: Michael Keegan and Rob Saemann, HB Councilmembers; Maggie Bove-LaMonica, School Boardmember; Richard Koenig; Annie Seawright
>
> Opponents: Dean Francois, HB Councilmember; George Barks, HB Ex Mayor; Mike Sribney, Brian Sheil & Richard O'Reilly, HB Small Biz Owners

Vote threshold: Majority, 50%+1, per Elections Code §9217 (a 2/3 council vote was needed only to place the measure on the ballot, per Resolution §6 — a different, internal requirement that does not change the voters' passage threshold).

## What's present and what's missing

- **Full ordinance text**: present (Exhibit A, "Hermosa Beach General Transactions and Use Tax Ordinance," adding Chapter 3.26 to Title 3 of the Municipal Code — within `resolution.pdf`).
- **Resolution placing the measure on the ballot**: present (`resolution.pdf`). It also designates Mayor Pro Tem Michael Keegan and Council Member Rob Saemann to submit the argument in favor on behalf of the Council (§5(d)) — consistent with the county list's named supporters.
- **Impartial analysis**: missing — not found. `www.hermosabeach.gov` returned HTTP 403 (Akamai bot-protection block) to every automated request, including the City Clerk's "Ballot Measure HB" page where this would be posted.
- **Argument for**: **confirmed filed** — the resolution itself designates the authors (Keegan, Saemann), matching the county list's named supporters. The filed text itself is missing, not found, same `hermosabeach.gov` block.
- **Argument against**: both sides are named in the county's final list (four named opponents), so an argument against was almost certainly filed too. The filed text is missing, not found, same block.
- **Rebuttals**: status unconfirmed either way — California law would allow a rebuttal on each side since both a for and an against argument exist, but no rebuttal text (or confirmation one was filed) was found.
- **Tax rate statement**: not applicable — HB is a general sales tax, not a property-tax-secured bond measure.
