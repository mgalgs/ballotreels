REBUTTAL TO ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE HB

Don't be misled by sales pitch. Proponents own filing calls Measure HB "half percent tax (0.05%)". That's mathematically wrong; half percent is 0.5%.

On October 1, LA County Measure ER raises Hermosa sales tax rate from 9.75% to 10.25%. If HB passes, Hermosa rises to 10.75%, higher than Redondo and El Segundo.

It's deceiving saying increased dollars stay in Hermosa Beach. Naturally it does - they're new increases over existing taxes.

They say over 50% is paid by visitors. That's false. $80,000 cars pay $800 more. Higher taxes on hotels and short-term rentals WOULD be paid by visitors. City Hall didn't put that on the ballot.

Tax brings in $2 million a year. It won't save the Pier exceeding $50 million or fix the structural deficit. It's permanent, oversight is advisory only, and can be spent anywhere.

City Hall claims "cost-cutting mode" yet proposes new commissions, PR consultants, and overtime. Look what City Hall did with residential parking - instead of small fix it was revamped, creating red tape and costing tax dollars.

City Hall paid consultants to promote tax increase, then tried to pay consultant $50,000 more to sell it.

The $4 million lifeguard contract doesn't have to cost that. Neighboring cities pay nothing.

We balanced this year's budget without this tax.

The real problem is bringing in more revenue, not bringing in revenue on the backs of the working class most affected by Measure HB.

Citizens rejected this in 2022. Citizens rejected it by a bigger margin in 2024. Citizens should reject it a third time.

DEAN FRANCOIS
