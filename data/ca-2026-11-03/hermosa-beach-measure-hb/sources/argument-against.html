ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE HB

On October 1st, LA County raises sales tax to 10.25%. If Measure HB passes, Hermosa Beach goes to 10.75%.

If you buy online shipped to Hermosa Beach - electronics, clothes, pet food - you pay Hermosa sales tax. You buy a car new or used, you pay where you live.

An $80,000 car: $7,800 tax today. With HB: $8,600. That's $800 more on one purchase.

You'll pay more than Redondo Beach and El Segundo. They stay at 10.25%. We'll be 10.75%. If you own a shop on Pier Ave or Hermosa Ave, why shop here when you can save next door or online?

Tax is permanent. No plan, no end date, oversight is advisory only, can be spent anywhere.

It doesn't fix the deficit. City Hall paid consultants to say voters would raise taxes instead of increasing revenue/cut spending. Then attempted $50,000 contract to sell it, so-far blocked. City Hall warning 2027-2028 deficit yet balanced this year's budget without tax by cutting higher-paid positions and adding revenue-neutral fees. We can streamline.

LA County lifeguard contract at $3-5 million doesn't have to cost that. Neighboring cities pay nothing. Negotiate it, bid it, or bring it in-house. Instead, City Hall wants YOU to pay.

City Hall revamped residential parking instead of a fix, creating red tape and higher costs.

We could've put a hotel tax and higher tax on short-term rentals on this ballot - paid by visitors. That didn't happen. West Hollywood passed an escalator - if County raises sales tax, the quarter-cent returns to West Hollywood. Hermosa Beach could've done that.

Voters said no in 2022 and by a bigger margin in 2024. Nothing has changed except we proved we can cut costs.

The Howard Jarvis Taxpayers Association said sales taxes are "unreasonable and unfairly harsh on those least able to afford it."

Vote No on HB
