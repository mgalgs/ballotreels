ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE HB

Vote YES to Protect Hermosa Beach's Essential Infrastructure and Services

Hermosa Beach is at a crossroads. The rising cost of maintaining our city and the county's growing charges for services we've long depended on threaten the quality of life that makes our community special.

Measure HB, a half percent (0.05%) sales tax increase assure that we maintain our public safety services, repair our streets, sidewalks, parks and our Hermosa Beach Pier. Manhattan Beach took advantage of this in 2024 and now is spending that money locally in Manhattan Beach.

Los Angeles County is requesting a major increase in the cost of our contracted County Fire Department Services. Additionally, the County is imposing new charges of an additional 4 million dollars for Lifeguard Services and Beach Maintenance.

The Hermosa Beach Pier—the heart of our city and a landmark treasured by residents and visitors alike—is deteriorating and must be replaced at a cost exceeding $50 million. Money will be needed to keep this landmark open.

Beyond the Pier, our City Yard and City Hall are outdated and in need of renovation to serve our community efficiently. Hermosa Beach also faces a long list of unfunded capital improvement projects—critical repairs and upgrades that have been deferred for years.

Every dollar raised by Measure HB stays right here in Hermosa Beach. Unlike county or state funding, this money cannot be taken away by Sacramento or redirected elsewhere.

Because this is a sales tax, the cost is shared by everyone who pays sales tax (over 50% spent by visitors).

We are in a cost cutting mode at City Hall. We have eliminated positions and re-organized our workforce. Doing nothing is not a plan. Measure HB is a fiscally responsible way to protect what we love about Hermosa Beach.

Join us in keeping Hermosa Beach safe, beautiful, and financially secure.

Vote YES on Measure HB
