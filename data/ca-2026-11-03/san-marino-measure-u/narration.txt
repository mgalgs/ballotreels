Measure U asks the voters of this city whether to extend the utility users tax with no end date, and raise its rate from 5 to 6 percent.
The tax is charged on telecommunications, video, electricity, gas and water service.
Today the rate is 5 percent, and the tax is set to end on March 31, 2027.
The City Council placed Measure U on the ballot.
Measure U would raise the rate to 6 percent.
Measure U would remove the end date, so the tax would last until ended by voters.
As a general tax, the money would go to the general fund, for the usual expenses of the city.
Measure U needs a majority yes vote to pass.
The city attorney's impartial analysis says the city estimates about $2.62 million a year in revenue.
The official county list of measures, which names who filed each argument, says that no argument against Measure U was submitted.
Supporters say the tax has funded police, fire and other services for decades, and renewing it avoids further cuts to core services.
A yes vote extends the utility users tax at 6 percent, until ended by voters.
A no vote rejects the extension, and the tax would end on March 31, 2027.
