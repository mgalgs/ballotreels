## Quoted facts

- Ballot question: "LONE PINE SPECIAL EMERGENCY RESPONSE SERVICES TAX REINSTATEMENT. Shall the measure reinstating the Lone Pine Fire Protection District's previously approved $100 parcel tax, originally adopted in 2014 and expired December 31, 2025, be adopted to continue funding Lone Pine Fire Protection District Special Emergency Response Services, generating approximately $80,000 annually for a period of ten years, with all funds remaining under local control?"
- Analysis: "This measure would reinstate a special parcel tax within the Lone Pine Fire Protection District (the “District”). The District previously adopted a $100 per-parcel tax in 2014 that expired on December 31, 2025."
- Term and rate: "If approved, this measure would authorize the District to levy a $100 tax annually on each taxable parcel of real property within the District beginning July 1, 2027 and ending June 30, 2037."
- Uses: "obtaining and maintaining emergency medical and ambulance services; purchasing and maintaining emergency response equipment; paying salaries, benefits, and training costs for emergency response personnel; other related activities."
- Vote needed: "This measure would take effect only if approved by at least two-thirds (2/3) of the voters voting on Measure Z."
- Full text, revenue: "The Tax is expected to generate approximately $80,000 annually, depending on the number of taxable parcels within the District."
- Full text, account: "All revenues from the Tax shall be deposited into a special account entitled “Lone Pine Fire-Ambulance.”"
