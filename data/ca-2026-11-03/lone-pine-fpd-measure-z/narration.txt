Measure Z asks the voters of this fire protection district whether to bring back a $100 yearly parcel tax for emergency response services.
The district adopted a $100 parcel tax in 2014, and that tax expired on December 31, 2025.
Measure Z needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
Measure Z would charge $100 a year on each taxable parcel in the district, from July 1, 2027, through June 30, 2037.
The money could pay only for emergency services, such as ambulance service, emergency equipment, and pay and training for responders.
Property owned by federal or state government agencies would not pay the tax.
The measure's full text estimates the tax would raise about $80,000 a year, depending on the number of taxable parcels.
Supporters say local emergency medical services face growing demands, aging equipment and rising costs, and Measure Z gives them dedicated local funding.
No argument against Measure Z was submitted, so the county voter guide prints only the argument in favor of the measure.
A yes vote is a vote to approve the tax on property in the district.
A no vote is a vote against the tax on property in the district.
