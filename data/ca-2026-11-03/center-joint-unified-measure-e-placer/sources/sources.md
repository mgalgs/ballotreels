## Consistency check against Sacramento's copy

The Placer impartial analysis (ME1) matches Sacramento's text: "If approved, Measure E would allow the District to incur bonded indebtedness up to a maximum amount of $197 million dollars." "The District estimates the average annual tax rate levy to fund this bond is $54.94 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. The final fiscal year in which the tax is anticipated to be collected will be 2068-69. The District estimates that the total amount repayable during the life of the bond, including principal and interest, is approximately $517 million dollars." "Passage of Measure E requires approval by 55% of the voters voting thereon."

Both counties print an argument in favor and no argument against; no rebuttals.
