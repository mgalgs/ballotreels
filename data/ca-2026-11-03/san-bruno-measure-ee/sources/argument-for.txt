Argument in Favor of Measure ___

San Bruno is facing an unprecedented fiscal challenge. State actions threaten more than $20 million every year in revenue that funds the essential services our community relies on. At the same time, our Business License Tax, unchanged since 1999, no longer reflects today's economy, treats every business exactly the same regardless of impact, and charges lower marginal rates as businesses earn more. This outdated, regressive structure leaves San Bruno far behind neighboring cities and unable to sustainably fund basic services.

This measure is not a tax on residents. It is a modernization and simplification of our Business License Tax so it is fair, equitable, and aligned with real-world business activity. It replaces the obsolete 1999 formula with clear, reasonable rates based on business type and gross receipts, ensuring that large enterprises contribute appropriately while protecting small businesses by preserving the low $75 base rate.

Importantly, this measure does not tax affordable housing providers, who are exempt under state law. It also ensures that homeowners renting out rooms or ADUs on their primary residential property will continue to pay no fee.

By updating the tax schedule, San Bruno will generate approximately $4 million annually—critical funding to maintain rapid 911 emergency response, fix potholes and city streets, keep library programs strong, continue investing in programs at our Senior Center, and preserve parks and open spaces. These are core services that our residents depend on.

This measure is accountable, transparent, and includes annual independent audits.

Vote YES to protect essential services and keep San Bruno strong. Vote YES on Measure ___.
