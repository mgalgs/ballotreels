RESOLUTION NO. 2026 - 079

A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF SAN BRUNO SUBMITTING TO THE VOTERS AT THE NOVEMBER 3, 2026 GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION, A MEASURE TO MODERNIZE AND SIMPLIFY THE BUSINESS LICENSE TAX, REQUESTING THAT SUCH ELECTION BE CONSOLIDATED WITH THE STATEWIDE GENERAL ELECTION HELD ON THAT DATE AND TAKING CERTAIN OTHER ACTIONS

WHEREAS, a business license tax is imposed on businesses in the City of San Bruno (the "City") to ensure that businesses pay their fair share for use of the City's roads, public safety and other City services; and

WHEREAS, the tax was last adjusted 26 years ago, in 1999, and it provides a complicated methodology, with varied rate structures for dozens of different types of businesses that places an unfair burden on small businesses and does not ensure that larger businesses pay a fair share for the City services and infrastructure they utilize;

WHEREAS, the City proposes to modernize and simplify the business license tax, and to increase some business license tax rates; and

WHEREAS, the proposed changes are designed to attract, retain, and encourage business growth and job creation, and increase some rates, while simplifying tax oversight and compliance; and

WHEREAS, the business license tax is a general tax -- a locally controlled revenue source deposited in the general fund to be used for any City purposes, including essential services such as street repair, neighborhood safety, emergency response, and enhanced fire protection; and

WHEREAS, the proposed business license tax is estimated to raise approximately $4,000,000 annually for the City; and

WHEREAS, by prior resolution, the City Council called a general municipal election (the "Election") for November 3, 2026, to be consolidated with the statewide general election to be held on that same date; and

WHEREAS, following study sessions on June 23, 2026 and July 14, 2026, the City Council directed staff to bring back to the City Council for approval a business license tax modernization and simplification ballot measure to be placed on November 3, 2026 ballot.

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF SAN BRUNO, CALIFORNIA, AS FOLLOWS:

1. Pursuant to Government Code Sections 53724 and Election Code Section 9222, the City Council directs that the ordinance attached hereto as Exhibit "A" and incorporated herein by reference (the "Measure") be submitted to the qualified voters of the City of San Bruno at the Election.

2. The question shall appear on the ballot as follows:

San Bruno Essential Services Protection Measure. This Measure is a general tax measure and must be approved by a majority of the votes cast in order to be adopted.

3. The full text of the Measure, attached to this Resolution as Exhibit A, being the proposed ordinance to modernize and simplify the business license tax, shall be completely printed in the voter pamphlet.

4. The City Council hereby directs the City Clerk to transmit a copy of the Measure to the City Attorney. The City Attorney shall prepare an impartial analysis of the Measure, not to exceed 500 words in length, showing the effect of the Measure on the existing law and the operation of the Measure, and transmit such impartial analysis to the City Clerk no later than August 18, 2026 by 5:00 p.m.

5. Pursuant to Elections Code Section 10403, the City Council requests that the San Mateo County Board of Supervisors consent to consolidation of the Election with the general election to be conducted by the San Mateo County Registrar of voters on November 3, 2026. The election shall be held in all respects as if there were only one election, and only one form of ballot shall be used. The election will be held and conducted in accordance with the provisions of law regulating a special election consolidated with a statewide election. The City will bear all costs customarily charged by the County to a city in connection with a ballot question submitted at a municipal election that is consolidated with an election conducted by the County.

6. The City Council directs the City Clerk to file a certified copy of this Resolution with the Registrar of Voters of San Mateo County.

7. Notice of the time and place of holding of the election is given and the City Clerk is authorized, instructed, and directed to give further or additional notice of the election in the time, form, and manner as required by law.

8. The Board of Supervisors is authorized to canvass the returns of the Election pursuant to Section 10411 of the Elections Code.

9. Pursuant to Section 10002 of the Elections Code, the Board of Supervisors is requested to permit the County Clerk to render all services specified by Section 10418 of the Elections Code relating to the election, for which services the City agrees to reimburse the County, in accordance with current County pro-rations and allocation procedures.

10. The City Clerk shall file a certified copy of this Resolution with the County Clerk.

11. The City Council hereby finds and determines that this action is not a project subject to the California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA) pursuant to Section 15378(b)(4) of the CEQA Guidelines because a measure to update the business license tax constitutes a government funding mechanism for municipal services and does not commit the City to any specific project which may result in a potentially significant physical impact on the environment. The City Council further finds and determines that even if the action were deemed to constitute a project under CEQA, it is exempt from CEQA pursuant to Section 15273(a) of the CEQA Guidelines because it establishes a charge that funds operating expenses incurred by the City for ongoing municipal service activities, including but not limited to road maintenance, repair, and improvement, public safety services, maintaining library programs, maintaining parks and open space and fire suppression activities. The business license tax revisions will increase revenues to allow for the continued funding of City services. The City Council additionally finds that the action is exempt pursuant to Section 15061(b)(3) of the CEQA Guidelines because it can be seen with certainty that there is no possibility that the action may have a significant effect on the environment.
