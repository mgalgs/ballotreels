# Measure EE — City of San Bruno — Business license tax (general tax), "San Bruno Essential Services Protection Measure"

- **Jurisdiction:** City of San Bruno.
- **Kind:** ordinance amending Municipal Code Chapters 3.04, 3.08, 3.12 and 3.16 to restructure the business license tax on gross receipts; general tax, until ended by voters. Placed by City Council Resolution No. 2026-079.
- **Who votes:** San Bruno voters. Resolution Sec. 1, PDF p. 1: the Council "directs that the ordinance attached hereto as Exhibit "A" ... (the "Measure") be submitted to the qualified voters of the City of San Bruno at the Election."
- **Threshold:** simple majority. Resolution, PDF p. 2, under the ballot question: "This Measure is a general tax measure and must be approved by a majority of the votes cast in order to be adopted."

## Ballot question (verbatim)

Resolution Sec. 2, PDF p. 2 (scan; lead-in at the foot of p. 1):

> San Bruno Essential Services Protection Measure. To protect essential services such as: fixing potholes/city streets; providing rapid response to 9-1-1 emergency calls; maintaining library programs for children, teens, adults/seniors; maintaining parks/open space; and general city services, shall the measure be adopted simplifying/modernizing San Bruno's business license ordinance, making rates more equitable/equal, updating the tax rate schedule as described in the ordinance, until ended by voters, raising $4,000,000 annually, with annual independent audits?

## Impartial analysis

`impartial-analysis.pdf`: "CITY ATTORNEY IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE EE", signed "/s/ Trisha Ortiz, City Attorney for the City of San Bruno" (p. 2).

- "Measure EE asks the voters of the City of San Bruno whether they want to amend the City's business license tax structure and rates so that every business pays a revised tax based on gross receipts, with two exceptions" (p. 1)
- "Measure EE, if approved by the voters, simplifies the current business license tax structure by establishing a tax of $75 for gross receipts of $75,000 or less for all businesses, and by establishing tax rates per $1,000 of gross receipts in excess of $75,000 depending on the business classification" — "$1.00 for "General/Retail,"", "$2.00 for "Professional and Advanced,"", "$1.50 for "Real Property and Rental Activities,"", "$1.50 for "Services,"", "$2.00 for "Contractors,"" (p. 1)
- "The revised business license tax structure proposed by Measure EE reduces taxes for some businesses and increases taxes for others. Measure EE does not change the business license tax for cardrooms, which is based on the number of authorized tables, and airport parking facilities, which is 8.0% of gross receipts." (p. 1)
- "The City estimated that Measure EE would raise approximately $4,000,000 per year for use in the City. Measure EE provides that the tax will be in effect until ended by the voters." (p. 1)
- "The business license tax is a general tax, which means the revenue can be used for all general governmental purposes." Proposition 218 "requires Measure EE to be approved by a majority of the voters voting on the Measure." (pp. 1-2)
- "A "yes" vote on Measure EE is a vote to amend the City's business license tax ordinance to revise the business license tax rates and structure. A "no" vote on Measure EE is a vote to not amend the City's business license ordinance and maintain the current business license tax rates and structure." (p. 2)

## Tax rate statement / fiscal

No tax rate statement (not required for a business license tax). Fiscal figures in the official files: "approximately $4,000,000 annually" (resolution recital, p. 1, and ballot question); "approximately $4,000,000 per year" (impartial analysis, p. 1); and the rate schedule summarized above. The argument in favor's "more than $20 million every year" in threatened state revenue does not appear in the resolution or the impartial analysis.
