City of San Bruno Measure EE asks voters in the city whether to revise the business license tax.
Today, the tax depends on things like office space, employees or vehicles.
The City Council placed Measure EE on the ballot, and it needs a simple majority.
Most businesses would pay $75 on their first $75,000 in receipts, then $1 to $2 per $1,000 more, by type.
Taxes would go down for some businesses and up for others.
Measure EE would be a general tax, lasting until voters repeal it.
The City Attorney's impartial analysis reports a city estimate of about $4 million a year.
Opponents say businesses would pass the tax on in higher prices and lower wages, and some may relocate.
Supporters say Measure EE updates an outdated tax so big businesses pay fairly and city services stay funded.
A yes vote revises the business license tax rates and structure.
A no vote keeps the current business license tax rates and structure.
