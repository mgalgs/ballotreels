YES on Measure D gives Los Altos residents the right to vote on permanent changes to public downtown parking plazas.

YES establishes an ordinance that requires voter approval before the City of Los Altos sells, trades, leases, conveys, declares “surplus land,” or otherwise permanently changes the current use of the city-owned downtown parking plazas.

The ordinance is housing friendly. Voter approval is not required for state-mandated affordable housing on City-designated Parking Plazas 7 and 8.

The City will continue to maintain, improve, landscape, and use the parking plazas for parking, festivals, parades, farmers markets, and other community events.

The City can propose any project for voter consideration, including a park, parking garage, and performing arts center.

Our 10 city-owned parking plazas are valuable public assets: Convenient, tree-lined parking supports local businesses, strengthens the City's sales tax base, and provides easy access for residents, employees, and visitors. Before changing the fundamental nature of Downtown, the community, not any one city council, should have a say.

Vote YES for the Downtown Parking Plazas Ordinance. Your downtown. Your vote.

Yeslosaltos.org
