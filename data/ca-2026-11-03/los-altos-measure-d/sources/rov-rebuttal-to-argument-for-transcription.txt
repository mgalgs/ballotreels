Measure D is Dangerous and Deceptive.

D is Deceptive: It doesn’t protect parking. When Plazas 7 and 8 build state-mandated housing, we lose up to 266 spaces — and the City can’t replace this parking without a costly citywide election.

D is Dangerous: These restrictions jeopardize our state-certified Housing Element. If Los Altos can’t rezone to HCD’s satisfaction, we risk falling back under Builders Remedy, losing local zoning control for months and opening the door to projects vastly out-of-scale, as has happened in Palo Alto and Menlo Park.

D is Damaging: This vaguely-worded measure invites lawsuits that waste millions of taxpayer dollars. YIMBY Law has already warned the City that they will sue Los Altos if this passes, and the state Attorney General is aggressively enforcing housing law against neighboring cities.

D is Dubious: Measure D gives its own proponents special legal protection to sue or appeal on the City’s behalf — at our expense — with zero accountability. You can’t vote them out.

D is Disingenuous: It claims to give us a vote we already have. We already vote for City Council, who weigh housing, safety, traffic, and budgets year-round. Measure D trades our ability to hold council responsible for zoning with a biannual vote on downtown construction proposals.

D is Destructive: A citywide vote for every change discourages investment and could freeze our 70-year-old asphalt lots into a museum of increasingly empty storefronts.

Join the League of Women Voters and Los Altos Village Association: Vote no on D.
