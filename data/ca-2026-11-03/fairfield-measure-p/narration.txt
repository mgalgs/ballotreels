Measure P asks the voters of this city whether to extend the city sales tax and raise it by 1 percent.
Today, the city has a 1 percent sales tax that is set to expire in March 2033.
Measure P would make the city tax 2 percent, and keep it in place until ended by voters.
The total sales tax rate in the city would go from 8.375 percent to 9.375 percent, with groceries and prescriptions still exempt.
The money could be spent on any general city purpose, and the measure needs a simple majority.
The city attorney's impartial analysis says the city estimates Measure P would raise about $24 million a year in new revenue.
Opponents say Measure P removes the end date, so the tax could continue forever, and the council can spend it on anything.
Supporters say Measure P has protected police, fire and street repair for years, and the cost of city services has risen sharply.
A yes vote adopts the sales tax ordinance.
A no vote does not adopt the sales tax ordinance.
