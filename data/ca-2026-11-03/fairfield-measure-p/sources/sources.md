## Facts and quotes

**Ballot question** (Notice of Measures, 8/5/2026, https://content.solanocounty.gov/sites/default/files/2026-08/Notice_of_Measures.pdf): "To provide and protect funding for Fairfield's services, such as police, fire, 911 emergency response, neighborhood safety, street and sidewalk repair, homelessness prevention and response, and general governmental use, shall an ordinance be adopted to extend the City's sales tax and increase the rate by up to one-cent per dollar until ended by voters, adding approximately $24 million annually, with independent citizen oversight, annual audits, public spending reports, and all funds locally controlled?"

**Who votes / vote needed (analysis):** voters of the City of Fairfield. "Article XIIIC of the California Constitution, commonly known as Proposition 218, requires that the proposed general tax be approved by a majority of the voters voting on the Measure."

**Impartial analysis** (signed David G. Lim, Fairfield City Attorney), key statements:
- "Measure P would increase the City's existing sales tax rate by 1%, or 1 cent, on every dollar of taxable sales of goods in the City ... Measure P would also extend the City's existing 1% transactions and use tax until ended by the voters."
- "The Measure would be a general tax, which means the tax revenue could be spent for all general governmental purposes. The City estimated that this Measure will raise approximately $24 million per year in new local revenue".
- "The total sales tax rate paid in Fairfield ... is currently 8.375%. If the Measure is approved by the voters, the total sales tax rate paid in the City would be 9.375%, subject to any changes in state law."
- "Measure P was placed on the ballot by a unanimous vote of the City Council."

**Argument in favor:** signed "s/M. Dae Spering, G.M. Down Town Theater" and "s/Sean Quin, Retired City Manger" [sic].

**Argument against:** signed "Solano County Taxpayers Association s/Michael Nolan, President". Says the revenue goes where "they can be spent on any purpose the City Council chooses."
