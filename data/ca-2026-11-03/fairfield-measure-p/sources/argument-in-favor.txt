Argument in Favor of Measure P

Measure P. Protecting What Works.

Vote YES on Measure P to protect Fairﬁeld’s essential services, including:

   •   Maintaining police patrols and crime prevention
   •   Protecting ﬁre, paramedic, and 911 emergency response
   •   Repairing streets, roads, and potholes
   •   Keeping parks, youth, and senior programs available
   •   Addressing homelessness and keeping public spaces safe and clean
   •   Maintaining ﬁscal stability and local control

For more than a decade, Measure P has provided locally controlled funding that stays in
Fairﬁeld and cannot be taken by the State. Measure P helped protect neighborhood police
patrols, maintain ﬁre and emergency medical response, and repair local streets and roads.

But the cost of providing essential city services has increased dramatically. Public safety
costs more. Street repairs cost more. Emergency response, equipment, fuel, insurance,
and basic city operations all cost more.

Without renewing and updating Measure P, Fairﬁeld will face difficult choices that could
affect the services residents rely on every day.

Measure P includes strict accountability requirements, including:

   •   Annual Audits
   •   Public Disclosure
   •   Independent Citizen Oversight to ensure funds are spent as promised.

Every dollar must be used for Fairﬁeld services and Fairﬁeld priorities. Funds cannot be
touched by the State.

Measure P is not a tax on your home or property. It helps maintain essential services
through a local funding source shared by residents, visitors, and others who shop in
Fairﬁeld and use city services.

Fairﬁeld residents deserve safe neighborhoods, reliable emergency response, well-
maintained streets, and a city that can plan responsibly for the future.

Please join public safety leaders, senior citizens, local businesses, and neighborhood
leaders in voting YES on Measure P.
