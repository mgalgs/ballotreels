Measure D asks the voters of this school district whether to approve $288 million in school bonds.
The district would repay the bonds from a yearly tax on property in the district.
Measure D needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would upgrade classrooms and career training buildings, replace leaky roofs and old heating and cooling, and improve safety.
The district's tax rate statement estimates an average tax of $58.55 per $100,000 of assessed value, and at most $60, until 2062-2063.
Supporters say Measure D renovates schools without raising tax rates, and qualifies them for $82 million in state matching funds.
Opponents say falling enrollment leaves schools with room to spare, and claims that tax rates will not rise deserve careful scrutiny.
A yes vote would authorize up to $288 million in bonds, repaid by a property tax.
A no vote is a vote against authorizing the bonds.
