## Key facts, quoted

- **Ballot question** (MD1): "To upgrade, renovate, modernize and expand classrooms, schools, and career technical education buildings; repair/ replace leaky roofs and outdated heating/ cooling systems; and make safety/ security improvements with no projected tax rate increase, shall Rocklin Unified School District's measure authorizing $288,000,000 of bonds at legal rates be adopted, generating on average $15,800,000 annually while bonds are outstanding at approximate rates of $59 per $100,000 assessed value, with audits, citizens' oversight, no money for salaries and funds staying local?"
- **Placed by / threshold:** "This measure was placed on the ballot by the governing board of the District through adoption of Resolution No. 25-26-43 on June 10, 2026. Approval by 55% of the voters voting on the measure is required for its adoption."
- **Analysis:** "This measure would authorize the sale of general obligation bonds by the Rocklin Unified School District (the "District") in the aggregate principal amount of $288,000,000." "The project list includes potential for improvements at Rocklin, Victory, and Whitney high schools, Spring View and Granite Oaks middle schools, the Rocklin Alternative Education Center, and other school facilities." "The District's estimate of the highest tax rate that would be required to fund this bond issuance is $60 per $100,000 of assessed valuation and is estimated to be collected until fiscal year 2062-2063." Signed "Clayton T. Cook, County Counsel".
- **Yes/No:** "A "YES" vote on this Measure would authorize the Rocklin Unified School District to borrow money through the sale of bonds in an amount not exceeding $288,000,000, to be repaid from revenues generated by an annual tax levied on taxable property within the District." / "A "NO" vote on this Measure is a vote against authorizing the District to issue the proposed general obligation bonds."
- **Tax rate statement** (MD8, "PREPARED BY THE DISTRICT"): average "$58.55 per $100,000 of assessed valuation … collected until fiscal year 2062-63"; highest "$60 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. This rate is projected to apply beginning in fiscal year 2027-28"; total debt service "approximately $657,200,000".
- **Argument in favor signers** (MD2): Paul Ruhkala, 60-Year Rocklin Resident; Nicole Smith, Rocklin Unified Parent; Davis Stewart, Retired Rocklin Principal; Julie Hupp, President, Rocklin School Board; Scott Yuill, Former Rocklin Mayor.
- **Rebuttal to argument in favor** and **argument against** (MD2–MD3): William J McEnroe.
