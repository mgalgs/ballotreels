City of Porterville Two Charter Amendments Measure Information

NOTICE OF ELECTION FOR TWO CHARTER AMENDMENTS AND NOTICE OF DATE FIXED FOR SUBMITTING ARGUMENTS FOR THE CITY OF PORTERVILLE.

 

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN to the qualified electors of the City of Porterville, located in the County of Tulare, State of California, that, in accordance with law, an election will be held on Tuesday, the 3RD  day of November, 2026, to submit for voter approval or rejection the following measures, each to be presented on the individual ballot as follows:

 

CITY OF PORTERVILLE CHARTER AMENDMENT

Shall the City Charter be amended to authorize the City Council to determine whether public works, maintenance, repair, improvement, and construction activities are performed by City forces, through contracts, or through agreements with other public agencies, and to authorize the City to establish purchasing and contracting procedures?

      YES                                                 NO

 

CITY OF PORTERVILLE CHARTER AMENDMENT

Shall the City Charter be amended to require that vacancies on the City Council be filled by appointment of the City Council when less than one year remains in the unexpired term, and by a special election when one year or more remains in the unexpired term?

     YES                                               NO

 

Each measure will be adopted if approved by a majority of the electorate voting on that Charter Amendment.

 

Arguments for or against these Charter Amendments may be filed with the Tulare County Registrar of Voters, 5300 W. Tulare Ave., Suite 105, Visalia, CA 93277, no later than August 17, 2026. The elections official has determined this date to be reasonable, allowing sufficient time to prepare and print the arguments. Arguments may not exceed 300 words in length.

 

Pursuant to Sections 9281, 9282, and 9287 of the Elections Code, one argument for and one argument against each measure will be selected for printing and distribution to voters with the sample ballot.

 

The elections official will also provide an opportunity for rebuttal arguments, not to exceed 250 words, to be filed no later than August 27, 2026, in accordance with Section 9285 of the Elections Code.

 

For more information on filing arguments, please contact the Elections Office at (559) 839-2100.

DATED:  July 30, 2026

Michelle Baldwin,

Registrar of Voters

 

By:                                                                                                         

Pauline Ramirez, Deputy Election Clerk
