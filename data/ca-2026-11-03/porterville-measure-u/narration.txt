Measure U asks the voters of this city whether to change the city charter's rules for public works and purchasing.
The City Council placed Measure U on the ballot in July 2026.
Today the charter says spending over $5,000 must be contracted to the lowest responsible bidder after public notice.
The council can do the work another way only by a four-fifths vote, after rejecting the bids or in a disaster.
Measure U would delete those rules from the charter.
Instead, the city could do public works with its own workers, without having to contract them out, or contract with other public agencies.
The city could also set its own rules for buying goods, property and services.
Measure U needs a majority of the vote to pass.
Neither the notice of election nor the resolution includes a cost estimate for Measure U.
No argument for Measure U appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
No argument against Measure U appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
A yes vote on Measure U changes the charter's rules for public works and purchasing.
A no vote on Measure U keeps the charter's current rules for public works and purchasing.
