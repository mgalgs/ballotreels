## Facts and quotes

**Ballot question (p1):** "Without increasing tax rates, to improve facilities to train students for careers in nursing/firefighting/first responders, building trades, agriculture, auto mechanics; attract/retain high quality faculty, prepare students for universities; repair leaky roofs; repair, construct, and acquire classrooms/sites/facilities/equipment, shall the Sonoma County Junior College District measure be adopted authorizing $830,000,000 in bonds at legal rates, levying $25 per $100,000 of assessed valuation ($55,300,000 annually), while bonds are outstanding, and with oversight/audits/local control?"

**Vote needed:** "55% Needed to Pass" (p1); analysis: "California law allows school districts to issue bonds ... if 55% of the voters who vote on the measure approve the sale of the bonds."

**Who votes (p2):** "The Sonoma County Junior College District ("District") Board of Trustees ("Board") has placed on the ballot in Sonoma, Marin, and Mendocino Counties the question of whether to issue bonds in the amount of $830,000,000".

**Impartial analysis (p2), key statements:**
- "As required by state law, the measure prohibits using bond proceeds for teacher or administrator salaries or other operating expenses."
- "Inclusion of a project on the Project List does not guarantee it will be funded or completed."
- "Bonds will be repaid with funds raised from an increase in property taxes based upon the assessed value of taxable property in the District anticipated to be through fiscal year 2058-59. The "Tax Rate Statement" ... estimates the highest tax rate necessary to fund the bonds is $25 per $100,000 of assessed value. Those tax levies are projections, and could go up or down".
- "If all bonds are sold, the total debt services, including principal and interest, is estimated at $1,760,000,000."
- Signed: Joshua A. Myers, Sonoma County Counsel (by Adam Brand); Katharine Elliott, Interim Mendocino County Counsel (by Brina A. Blanton); Brandon Halter, Marin County Counsel.

**Tax rate statement (p2):** average annual rate "$22.00 per $100,000 (2.2¢ per $100) of assessed valuation", collected "until fiscal year 2058-59"; highest rate "$25.00 per $100,000 ... first occurring in fiscal year 2041-42"; total debt service "approximately $1.76 billion". Signed Angélica Garcia, Ed.D., Superintendent/President.
