## Quoted passages

- Question (p.1): "Shall an ordinance to amend the Laguna Beach Municipal Code by enacting a limit on consecutive years of service by members of the City Council to a combined total of two four-year consecutive terms, with the ability to again serve as a Councilmember after a lapse of service of at least two years, be adopted?"
- Who placed it (p.3): "Measure E was placed on the ballot by a citizens’ initiative petition. On December 9, 2025, the City Council voted to place Measure E on the November 3, 2026 General Municipal Election, in compliance with the California Elections Code."
- Threshold (p.3): "If approved by a majority of voters (50% +1), Measure E would add Section 2.04.080 to the Laguna Beach Municipal Code to establish term limits on City Council service."
- Current law (p.3): "Currently, the City of Laguna Beach does not have term limits on elected City Council office."
- Analysis (p.3): "Measure E will only apply to City Council terms starting as of the November 3, 2026 General Municipal Election. State law does not allow local term limits to apply retroactively to prior terms."
