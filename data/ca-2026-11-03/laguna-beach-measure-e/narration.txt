Laguna Beach Measure E asks the voters of Laguna Beach whether to set term limits for City Council members.
Measure E was placed on the ballot by a citizens' initiative petition.
Today, Laguna Beach elects five City Council members to four-year terms, with no term limits.
Measure E would limit members to two four-year terms in a row.
After a break of two years, a person could serve two more terms.
Only terms starting with the November 2026 election would count toward the limit.
The Orange County Voter Information Guide does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Supporters say incumbents hold big advantages, and term limits level the playing field, bringing fresh ideas.
Opponents say elections already hold members accountable, and limits force out experienced officials voters still trust.
A yes vote approves term limits for the City Council.
A no vote means Laguna Beach will continue to have no council term limits.
