Measure H asks the voters of this county whether an employment contract may set how the County Executive Officer can be removed.
The County Executive Officer is the chief administrator of the county, appointed by the Board of Supervisors.
Today, the officer serves at the board's pleasure, and can be removed by a vote of three supervisors.
The Board of Supervisors placed Measure H on the ballot, and it needs a majority to pass.
Measure H would keep the three-vote rule, unless the officer's employment contract with the board says otherwise.
Measure H sets no specific alternative, and does not require the board to sign such a contract.
The board would still appoint the officer and review the officer's work.
The Measure H voter guide pages do not include a cost estimate for the measure.
No argument for Measure H was submitted.
No argument against Measure H was submitted.
A yes vote lets an employment contract set the terms for removing the County Executive Officer.
A no vote keeps removal by a vote of three supervisors, with no contract able to change it.
