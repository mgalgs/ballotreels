## Key facts, quoted

- Who votes / threshold: "This measure was placed on the ballot by the Board of Supervisors of the County of Placer through adoption of Ordinance 6382-B. Approval by a majority of voters voting on this measure is required for its adoption."
- Current law: "The CEO serves at the pleasure of the Board and may be removed by an affirmative vote of three members of the Board."
- Change: "Measure H would amend Section 502(a) to provide that the CEO could be removed by an affirmative vote of three members of the Board, unless otherwise specified in an employment contract between the Board and the CEO."
- Limits: "The measure does not establish a specific alternative procedure for removal or require that the Board enter into an employment contract containing alternative removal provisions." "Measure H would not change the CEO's role as the County's chief administrative officer, the requirement that the CEO be appointed by the Board of Supervisors, or the requirement that the CEO's performance be evaluated by the Board from time to time."
- Yes/No: "A "YES" vote on Measure H is a vote to amend the Placer County Charter to allow an employment contract between the Board of Supervisors and the County Executive Officer to establish provisions governing removal of the County Executive Officer." / "A "NO" vote on Measure H is a vote to leave the current Charter language unchanged, under which the County Executive Officer may be removed by an affirmative vote of three members of the Board of Supervisors, and the Charter would not authorize an employment contract to provide otherwise."
