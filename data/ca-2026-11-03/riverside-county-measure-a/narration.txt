Riverside County Measure A asks voters across the county whether to continue the existing half-cent sales tax for transportation.
Voters first approved the tax in 1988, to pay for highways, local roads and public transit.
In 2002, voters extended it for 30 more years, so it is now set to expire in 2039.
The Riverside County Transportation Commission placed Measure A on the ballot, and it needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The tax would continue at the same rate after 2039, until voters repeal it.
A spending plan splits the money among highways, local roads and public transit.
An independent taxpayers oversight committee would review a yearly audit.
The ballot question estimates the tax would provide about $280 million a year.
Opponents say drivers still face gridlock after decades of this tax, and voters should not make it open-ended.
Supporters say Measure A would fix potholes, repair aging bridges and speed up traffic, all without raising taxes.
A yes vote extends the half-cent tax at the same rate until voters repeal it.
A no vote rejects the extension, and the tax stays set to expire in 2039.
