## Quoted passages

- Question (p.22): "Riverside County Traffic Relief, Pothole/Road Repair Investment Renewal Without raising tax rates, shall the measure to reduce congestion; repair potholes; complete freeway projects; keep roads in good condition; help improve safety; support local jobs; reinforce aging bridges, highways/roads from flooding/earthquakes; keep senior/student/disabled/veteran bus fares low be adopted, continuing the existing ½¢ voter-approved transportation sales tax providing approximately $280,000,000 annually until ended by voters, requiring audits, spending disclosure, local control?"
- Who placed it (p.40): "The Riverside County Transportation Commission (“Commission”), by adopting Resolution No. 26-011 (“Resolution”), elected to call an election to place this Measure on the ballot."
- Threshold (p.40): "For this Measure to be approved, at least two-thirds (2/3) of qualified voters who vote on the Measure must vote yes."
- History (p.40): "Measure A was initially approved by voters in 1988 and authorized a levy of retail transactions and use tax. In 2002, Measure A was extended for an additional thirty years by voters. The existing Measure A is set to expire in 2039."
- Analysis (p.40): "The Special Sales Tax would continue to be allocated among three funding categories: (1) Highways and Regional Corridors; (2) Local Streets and Roads; and (3) Public Transportation within Riverside County."
- Analysis (p.40): "Measure A provides for the Commission to conduct an annual independent financial audit of the proceeds of the Special Sales Tax. An independent taxpayers oversight committee has been established and shall review the annual audit of the Commission."
- Expenditure Plan (p.29): "There is no increase to the existing sales tax rate."
