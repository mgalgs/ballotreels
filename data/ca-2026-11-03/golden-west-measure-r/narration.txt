Measure R asks the voters of this community services district whether to raise its yearly road tax from $120 to $300 per parcel.
Voters in the district approved today's $120 road tax in 1984, and the tax has not been raised since.
The district's board of directors placed Measure R on the ballot.
Measure R needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The new tax would apply to each parcel in the district, starting in the 2027-2028 fiscal year.
The tax could be adjusted each year with a construction cost index.
The money could pay only for road improvements and maintenance, and the tax would last until voters change or repeal it.
If Measure R fails, the $120 tax stays.
The county attorney's impartial analysis expects the tax to raise about $135,300 a year.
Supporters say repaving costs have climbed fast, and the higher tax would finish the next big project sooner, for less.
No argument against Measure R was submitted, the voter pamphlet of this county says, and it prints no rebuttals.
A yes vote authorizes the increased tax of $300 per parcel a year.
A no vote is against the increased tax, and the $120 tax stays.
