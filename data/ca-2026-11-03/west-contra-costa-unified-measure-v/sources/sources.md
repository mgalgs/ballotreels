## Facts from the District page (official)

- "West Contra Costa voters first passed a 7.2 cents-per-square-foot parcel tax in 2004 to avoid classroom-level budget cuts. This local tax in support of local schools was reapproved by voters in 2012 and 2016. It has never been increased by the District and is not a new tax."
- "Both WCCUSD district schools and charter schools benefit from the parcel tax, which brings in $10.2 million annually. Exemptions are in place for seniors and for those with disabilities."

## Ballot question — UNCONFIRMED

Not found on an official source. Finder text (cocotax.org; not a source):

> Shall the measure supporting local quality education in West Contra Costa Unified School District, protecting core academics, science, music, arts, and career technical programs that prepare students for the changing economy; attracting and retaining qualified teachers; and maintaining mental health services and counselors to support student success; by renewing the parcel tax at the existing rate of 7.2¢/building square foot, raising approximately $10,238,737 annually for nine years, with senior exemptions, independent citizen oversight, audits, and local control, be adopted?
