Measure V asks the voters of this school district whether to renew its parcel tax, a yearly tax on each square foot of building, for nine years.
The district's voters first approved the tax in 2004, then again in 2012 and 2016, and today's tax ends in June 2027.
The renewed tax would run from July 2027 to June 2036, and it needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
A parcel with a 2,000-square-foot building would pay $144 a year; owners 65 or older, and some with disabilities, could apply for an exemption.
The money could pay only for listed purposes, such as core academics, teachers and counseling, under an oversight committee.
The ballot question says the tax would raise about $10.2 million a year.
Opponents say the district does not have a revenue problem, and that its residents already pay the highest school-bond tax in the county.
Supporters say this renews an existing tax rather than adding one, and that without it, schools face cuts to programs students rely on.
A yes vote authorizes the parcel tax for nine years.
A no vote is against authorizing the parcel tax for nine years.
