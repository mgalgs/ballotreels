a. UPDATE ON THE 2025 AND 2026 CITY COUNCIL PRIORITIES AND APPROVAL TO SURVEY AND/OR CONDUCT OUTREACH WITH THE COMMUNITY TO INFORM FUTURE COUNCIL PRIORITIES

Assistant to the City Manager Joe Carozza introduced the item. He reported that the report provides an update to the City Council on progress made toward the five strategic priorities established for calendar years 2025 and 2026: Economic Development and Downtown Vitality; Environmental Sustainability and Climate Action; General Plan Update; Park and Recreation Facilities; and Public Safety and Social Wellness. Staff continue to implement the scopes of work adopted by Council in early 2025, and the report highlights notable accomplishments and significant progress. He reported that the City Council is requested to approve staff to survey and/or conduct outreach with the community to inform future City Council priorities.

Each of the priorities were discussed further:

Economic Development Manager Mike Nimon discussed economic development and downtown vitality.

Sustainability Manager Candice Rankin-Mumby discussed environmental sustainability and climate action.

Community Development Director Erika Vandenbrande reported on the General Plan update.

Public Works Director Rich Payne discussed park and recreation facilities.

Chief of Police Ryan Hibbs discussed public safety and social wellness.

No public comments were received.

No formal action was taken. The City Council received the 2025 and 2026 City Council Priorities Update and approved surveying and/or conducting outreach with the community to inform future Council priorities.

b. FISCAL YEAR 2027 PROPOSED BUDGET AMENDMENTS

Administrative Services Director Kirsten LaCasse provided the staff presentation. She reported that every two years the City Council adopts a budget that provides the spending plan to govern city operations. On June 17, 2025, the City Council adopted the Fiscal Year 2025-26 (FY26) & Fiscal Year 2026-27 (FY27) Operating Budget. Throughout the two-year budget cycle, staff actively monitor revenue and expenditures, review major budget trends, and propose changes when necessary. This report outlined proposed changes to the FY27 budget, which begins on July 1, 2026.

No public comments were received.

Motion by Mayor Pro Tem Francois, second by DeVinney, carried unanimously, to SUPPORT the proposed amendments to the FY27 Adopted Operating Budget presented (the item will return at the June 2, 2026 City Council meeting for formal adoption).

c. CONSIDERATION OF A RESOLUTION SUBMITTING A MEASURE TO THE VOTERS OF WALNUT CREEK FOR THE NOVEMBER 3, 2026 GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION TRANSITIONING THE CITY TREASURER POSITION FROM ELECTED TO APPOINTED, INCLUDING SPECIFYING PROCEDURES AND PROCESSES RELATED TO THE BALLOT ARGUMENTS

Assistant to the City Manager Joe Carozza provided the staff presentation.  He reported that the office of City Treasurer is currently an elected position as established by the Walnut Creek Municipal Code and authorized under California Government Code. The City Council is asked to consider a resolution submitting a measure to the voters of Walnut Creek for the November 3, 2026 general municipal election transitioning the City Treasurer position from an elected to an appointed office and specifying procedures and processes related to the ballot arguments. He reported that the election of a City Treasurer to a new four-year term is also scheduled to appear on the same ballot.

No public comments were received.

Motion by Mayor Wilk, second by Mayor Pro Tem Francois, carried unanimously, to ADOPT RESOLUTION NO. 26-31 submitting a measure to the voters of Walnut Creek for the November 3, 2026 general municipal election to determine whether the position of City Treasurer should be changed from elected to appointive; requesting that the Contra Costa County Board of Supervisors consent to the consolidation of this election with the statewide general election to be held on the same date; directing the City Attorney to prepare an impartial analysis; and authorizing Mayor Kevin Wilk and Mayor Pro Tem Matt Francois to submit a written argument in favor of the proposed measure, as well as a rebuttal argument on behalf of the City Council, if so needed.

YEAS: Darling, DeVinney, Silva, Mayor Pro Tem Francois, Mayor Wilk.
