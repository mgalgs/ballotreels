## Quoted facts

- **Who votes:** voters of the Upland Unified School District.
- **Ballot question** (RES_UplandUSD.pdf, Exhibit A): "To improve the quality of education by modernizing, constructing, upgrading and equipping classrooms, restrooms and school facilities; improve career technical classrooms/labs; replace roofs; make safety/security improvements; and renovate/replace electrical, plumbing and sewer systems; shall Upland Unified School District's measure be adopted, authorizing $236,700,000 of bonds at legal rates, levying approximately $47.50 per $100,000 assessed value (raising $14,200,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding, with audits, independent oversight, NO money for salaries, and all money staying local?"
- **Impartial analysis** (VIG p. 168, County Counsel): "This Measure was placed on the ballot by a resolution adopted by the Board of Education of the Upland Unified School District (District). A "yes" vote by at least fifty-five percent of the voters voting on this Measure will authorize the District to issue bonds in the amount of $236,700,000 to be used solely for the purposes set forth in this ballot Measure. A "no" vote means that the District will not be authorized to issue and sell the $236,700,000 in bonds set forth in the Measure." … "The bonded debt will be a general obligation of the District and will be financed by taxes levied annually on taxable property located within the District."
- **Tax rate statement** (VIG p. 171): "An election will be held in Upland Unified School District (the "District") on November 3, 2026 for the purpose of submitting to the electors of the District the question of incurring bonded indebtedness of the District in the principal amount of $236,700,000."
- **Arguments:** for, against, and both rebuttals printed (VIG pp. 168–171).
