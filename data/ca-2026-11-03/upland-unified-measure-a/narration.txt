Upland Unified School District Measure A asks the district's voters whether to approve $236.7 million in school bonds.
The district would borrow the money and repay it from a tax on property in the district.
Measure A needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would go to listed types of projects, such as modernizing classrooms, job training labs and school security.
The district's tax statement estimates an average tax of $47.46 per $100,000 of assessed value, and at most $49, through 2062-2063.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $511 million.
Supporters say Measure A would repair aging schools, protect students and prepare our children for the future.
Opponents say taxpayers still pay for an earlier bond, and the district should operate within its budget.
A yes vote means the district may issue the bonds.
A no vote means the district may not issue the bonds.
