Measure C asks the voters of this city whether to tax cannabis businesses, if the city ever allows them.
Commercial cannabis sales are banned in the city today, and Measure C would not allow them.
The city council would have to adopt a separate law before any cannabis business could open.
The council could set the tax at up to 10 percent of what a cannabis business takes in.
As a general tax, the money could pay for any city purpose, until voters repeal the tax.
Measure C needs a simple majority to pass.
City staff estimate the tax could bring in about $500,000 a year, if it were set at 6 percent.
Supporters say cannabis tax money now goes to other communities, and this would keep it here to help pay for police and roads.
Opponents say enforcing cannabis rules often costs more than the tax brings in, and the measure opens a path to cannabis shops.
A yes vote authorizes the city to impose a cannabis business tax of up to 10 percent.
A no vote does not authorize the tax.
