## Quoted facts

- **Who votes:** Arroyo Grande voters. Impartial analysis (p. 27): "If approved by a majority of Arroyo Grande voters, this Measure would authorize a maximum ten percent (10%) Cannabis Business Tax on gross receipts received by any commercial cannabis businesses engaging in business in the City, lawfully or unlawfully, excluding industrial hemp businesses."
- **Ballot question** (Resolution No. 2026-047, Section 2; transcribed from the scanned page image): "Shall the measure to maintain Arroyo Grande City services, including public safety, road maintenance, infrastructure repair, recreational programs, and other general City services by enacting a tax of up to 10% of gross receipts on cannabis businesses operating in the city if such businesses are permitted, estimated to generate approximately $500,000 annually, with spending oversight and until ended by voters, be adopted?"
- **Impartial analysis** (p. 27, City Attorney): "On June 9, 2026 the City Council unanimously voted to place Measure "C-26" on the November 3, 2026 general municipal election ballot. By placing Measure "C-26" on the ballot, the City complies with Article XIII C of the California Constitution (Proposition 218), which requires a majority of the voters to approve an ordinance that establishes a local general tax." … "This measure only enacts a tax on commercial cannabis businesses should they be permitted by the City in the future. Commercial cannabis sales are currently prohibited in Arroyo Grande"
- **Arguments:** the guide prints an argument in favor, an argument against, and a rebuttal to each (pp. 28–30).
