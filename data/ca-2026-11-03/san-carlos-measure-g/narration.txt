San Carlos Measure G asks city voters whether to add a half-cent sales tax for 14 years.
Today, the sales tax rate in San Carlos is 9.375 percent.
The City Council placed Measure G on the ballot, and it needs a majority to pass.
Measure G would add a 0.5 percent sales tax, for a total rate of 9.875 percent.
Groceries, prescription medicines and diapers would stay exempt.
The tax would run from April 1, 2027, until March 31, 2041, unless voters extend it.
The money would go to the general fund, for any lawful city purpose.
The impartial analysis says the city estimates $6 million a year in added revenue.
Opponents say the half cent boosts city sales tax revenue by 50 percent, and a general tax is a blank check.
Supporters say the tax would fix potholes, streets and storm drains, and the money stays in San Carlos, with audits.
A yes vote is a vote in favor of the half-cent sales tax.
A no vote is a vote against the half-cent sales tax.
