Draft Ballot Argument in Favor of Measure ___ ["Draft Ballot" struck through by hand]

Vote YES on Measure __ for a safe, strong San Carlos

San Carlos is known as the City of Good Living because generations of residents have invested in our safe streets, clean parks, reliable local services, and unmatched quality of life. But those basics do not maintain themselves and San Carlos faces sharp increases in costs to fund public safety, maintain streets and parks, and provide quality services to residents.

This half cent sales tax provides locally controlled funding to fix potholes, repair aging streets and sidewalks, improve storm drains, maintain parks and community facilities, support emergency response, and keep San Carlos neighborhoods safe and well cared for.

Families and local businesses are already facing higher costs, and voters will be asked to consider several measures on November’s ballot. That is exactly why Measure __ deserves careful consideration. It provides local money for local needs. The funds generated will stay in San Carlos, cannot be taken by the State or County, and are subject to independent audits and public disclosure.

Putting off basic maintenance does not save money. Cracked sidewalks, failing sewers, aging park amenities and infrastructure continue to require our attention. This Measure will ensure San Carlos address everyday problems before they become bigger and even more costly.

Endorsed by local leaders, Labor, Fire, San Carlos business owners, and public safety professionals, Measure __ is not about expanding government. It’s about investing in the quality of life we enjoy, the streets we drive, the sidewalks we travel with our kids, the parks where families gather, and the emergency services we count on when minutes matter.

Join us in supporting this sensible, locally-controlled solution to maintaining a safe, strong San Carlos for generations to come. www.YesforSanCarlos.com.
