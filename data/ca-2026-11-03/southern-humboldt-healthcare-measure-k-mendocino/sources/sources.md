## Facts and quotes

**Ballot question** (file 2, p10, "Exhibit B — Ballot Question/Abbreviated Form of Ballot Measure"): "To replace 77 year-old Jerold Phelps Community Hospital with a modern hospital to provide life-saving emergency care for victims of accidents, heart attacks, strokes, and other emergencies, improve Emergency Room facilities, operating rooms, provide advanced medical equipment/technology and life flight services; and qualify for matching funds shall Southern Humboldt Healthcare District's measure authorizing $25,000,000 in bonds be adopted, levying 8 cents per $100 assessed value (raising $2,000,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding, with independent citizen oversight and all funds staying local?" (Choices: "BONDS – YES" / "BONDS – NO".) This is the district's adopted form; not confirmed against the printed ballot.

**Lead county** (file 2, p1, Resolution 26:06): "WHEREAS, the District is primarily located in Humboldt County and partially in Mendocino County (together, the "Counties")".

**Vote needed / who votes** (full text, p1): "By approval of this measure (the "Measure") by at least two-thirds of the qualified voters voting, the District shall be authorized to issue and sell bonds in the aggregate principal amount of $25,000,000". Full text, introduction: the District "is a major provider of health care services to communities in Southern Humboldt County and Mendocino County."

**Impartial analysis** (p3, "Measure K - Impartial Analysis, Humboldt County Counsel", "Submitted via Humboldt County Counsel"), key statements:
- "Measure K is a bond measure placed on the ballot by the Southern Humboldt Community Healthcare District."
- "These include a new hospital facility, an upgraded emergency room, flight and heliport facilities to accommodate expedited life flight air transportation, meeting upcoming seismic requirements, and complying with the Americans with Disabilities Act."
- "The measure would authorize the district to sell bonds in the amount of $25 million and levy a property tax to fund the repayment of the bonds. The district estimates that the average annual tax necessary to fund the repayment would be $8 per $100,000 of assessed property value, and that the total cost of debt service, including principal and interest, would be approximately $50.4 million. The measure requires a two-thirds vote to pass."
- "One of the intended uses of the funds by the district is the acquisition of medical equipment. This likely does not fall within the constitutional exemption for the acquisition and improvement of real property."

**Discrepancy, as printed:** the Mendocino guide's copy of the analysis says "$8 per $100,000"; the Humboldt County copy of the same analysis (file 2, p12) says "the average annual tax necessary to fund the repayment would be $80 per $100,000 of assessed property value"; the Tax Rate Statement (p2 of file 1) says "8¢ per $100 ($80 per $100,000) of assessed valuation" and the ballot question says "8 cents per $100". So $80 per $100,000 is supported by three official texts; the Mendocino guide's "$8" appears to be a misprint, but that is my inference, not an official statement.

**Tax rate statement** (p2): average and highest rate "8¢ per $100 ($80 per $100,000) of assessed valuation"; "the final fiscal year in which the tax will be collected is fiscal year 2052-53"; total debt service "approximately $50.4 million".

**Argument in favor** (p3): signed Yvonne Hendrix (Director, Southern Humboldt Chamber of Commerce), Seth Ayers (Chief, Redway Fire Protection District), Carolyn Hino-Bourassa, Christina Huff, Shawn Masterson.

**Argument against:** "NO ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE K WAS SUBMITTED." (p3)
