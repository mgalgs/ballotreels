Measure K asks the voters of this health care district whether to approve $25 million in bonds for hospital projects.
The district would sell the bonds and repay them from a property tax in the district.
Measure K needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The money could pay for a new hospital, an upgraded emergency room, a helicopter landing site and earthquake safety work.
The impartial analysis says buying medical equipment, one planned use, likely falls outside the constitutional exception that allows this bond tax.
The district's tax rate statement estimates $80 per $100,000 of assessed value a year, through 2052-2053.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $50.4 million.
No argument against Measure K was submitted for the printed county voter guide pages on this health care district measure.
Supporters say state law requires replacing the hospital within 3 years or closing it, and Measure K funds a modern hospital.
A yes vote adopts the measure authorizing $25 million in bonds.
A no vote does not adopt the measure authorizing the bonds.
