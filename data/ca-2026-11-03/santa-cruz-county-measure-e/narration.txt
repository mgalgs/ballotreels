County of Santa Cruz Measure E asks county voters whether to add a half-cent sales tax for five years.
The board of supervisors placed Measure E on the ballot, declaring the county in fiscal distress from cuts in federal and state funding.
Today, the sales tax is 9.5 percent outside the cities, and up to 9.75 percent within them.
Measure E would raise each rate by half a percent, from April 2027 through April 2032.
The county auditor-controller estimates Measure E would add about $27 million a year to the general fund, about 3 percent of its $844 million this year.
Supporters say Measure E keeps hospitals and emergency rooms open, and partly replaces federal funding the county has lost.
Opponents say Measure E is a regressive tax that hits low-income workers, and the county could spend it on anything.
A yes vote approves a half-cent sales tax increase for five years.
A no vote keeps the sales tax rate as it is now.
