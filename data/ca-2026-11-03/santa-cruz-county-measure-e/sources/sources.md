## Quoted facts

- **Who votes / threshold:** Notice: "County of Santa Cruz (Majority Voter Approval Required)".
- **Ballot question:** "To keep local hospitals/emergency rooms open, protect ambulance response, sustain access to urgent healthcare, including reproductive and mental health care, support local food/housing assistance and other essential services, and partially restore critical lost federal funding, shall the County of Santa Cruz be authorized to collect a supplemental half-cent (0.5%) general sales tax for 5 years, providing $27,000,000 annually in locally controlled funds?"
- **Impartial analysis (County Counsel Jason M. Heath):**
  - "The Santa Cruz County Board of Supervisors ("the Board") placed Measure "E" on the ballot. It did so in connection with a resolution declaring the County to be in a state of fiscal distress due to recent reductions in federal and related state funding".
  - "temporarily increase the County's transactions and use tax ("sales tax") by one-half percent throughout the County, including cities within the County. The tax would be in place for five years, taking effect on April 1, 2027, and continuing through April 1, 2032."
  - "the County estimates that it will receive approximately $6,750,000 in Fiscal Year 2026-27 and approximately $27,000,000 in future fiscal years thereafter. Any revenues raised from Measure "E" will be placed in the County's General Fund and may be used for any lawful government purpose."
  - Rate table: unincorporated 9.5% → 10%; City of Santa Cruz 9.75% → 10.25%; Watsonville 9.75% → 10.25%; Capitola 9.25% → 9.75%; Scotts Valley 9.75% → 10.25%.
  - "Certain types of items are excluded from local sales taxes, such as many food and grocery items, prescription medicine, certain medical devices, diapers, and hygiene products."
- **Fiscal impact (Laura Bowers):** "would impose a supplemental one-half percent (0.5%) sales tax on taxable sales throughout Santa Cruz County for five years. The additional revenue would be deposited into the County General Fund for general County purposes." "would annually generate approximately $27 million in additional revenue".
- **Argument for** (transcribed from scan): "Vote YES on Measure E to protect emergency medical care and essential services throughout Santa Cruz County." Signers: Dr. Joe Gallagher (Pajaro Valley Health Care District board), Anita Aguirre (Santa Cruz Community Health CEO), Chief Mark Correira (Scotts Valley Fire Protection District), Tricia Wynne (Second Harvest Food Bank board emeritus), Elaine Johnson (Housing Santa Cruz County).
- **Argument against:** "We strongly urge a NO vote on Measure E. Regressive sales taxes are the most punitive type of tax ..." Signers: Becky Steinbruner, Gail Harlamoff, Linda Santos, Michael Lelieur, Dave Michaels.
- **Rebuttal to argument for:** "All of the money will go to the County General Fund, with no restrictions whatsoever on how it is spent." Signers: Becky Steinbruner, Dave Michaels, Michael Lelieur, Patrice Edwards, Gail Harlamoff.
- **Rebuttal to argument against:** "Measure E expires automatically after five years ... Measure E would not apply to key necessities like groceries or prescription medicines. It adds just 50 cents to a $100 eligible purchase." Signers: Dr. Faris Sabbah, Kristen Brown, Jennifer Merchant, Sam Altis, Donna Young.
