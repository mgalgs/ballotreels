Measure FF — Upper Bay Permanent Road Division (Bay Road, unincorporated Mill Valley) — road improvement and maintenance special parcel tax, 2/3 vote
Retrieved 2026-10-30. Publisher of every file: County of Marin, Board of Supervisors (Granicus agenda/minutes portal, marin.granicus.com, view_id=33).
Letter "FF" comes from the county's list-of-measures page (see ../../README.md); the BOS documents do not carry the letter.
Scale: the division is 10 improved parcels + 1 unimproved parcel fronting the non-county-maintained section of Bay Road (staff report).

BALLOT QUESTION (verbatim, Resolution No. 2026-071, para. 8; headed "MEASURE SUBMITTED TO VOTE OF VOTERS OF UPPER BAY PERMANENT ROAD DIVISION FOR APPROVAL OF SPECIAL TAX AND LOAN AND ITS REPAYMENT"; "to fun improvements" [sic] in the source):
  Shall the measure be adopted authorizing a special tax on parcels within the Division of $4,350 per improved parcel per year and $2,175 per unimproved parcel per year for 10 years, beginning in fiscal year 2027-28, resulting in a total assessment of approximately $456,750 each year, to fun improvements; thereafter, beginning in fiscal year 2037-2038, the special tax shall be $455 per year for each year until repealed, to fund ongoing maintenance, with a provision for an annual adjustment effective July 1 every year in accordance with the change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers San Francisco—Oakland—Hayward for the previous calendar year.
CAUTION: the figure "$456,750" is inconsistent across the county's own documents — the resolution's recital and the petition call it the total cost/total revenue; the question says "each year"; the notice says "per year". (11 parcels x ~$4,350 is ~$45,000/yr, so $456,750 is presumably the 10-year total.) Report the wording as printed; flag the inconsistency.
