Upper Bay Permanent Road Division

Shall the measure be adopted authorizing a special tax on parcels within the Division of $4,350 per improved parcel and $2,175 per unimproved parcel per year for 10 years, beginning Fiscal Year 2027-28, totaling approximately $456,750, to fund improvements; thereafter, authorizing a special tax of $455 per parcel per year for ongoing maintenance, adjusted annually effective July 1 every year in accordance with the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers San Francisco—Oakland—Hayward for the previous calendar year.

YES NO

Votes required to pass: 2/3 voter approval.

Impartial Analysis

Impartial analysis by County Counsel of Measure FF

County of Marin
Upper Bay Permanent Road Division

Measure F

This Measure was placed on the ballot following submission of a qualifying petition containing the number of signatures required by law.

The Upper Bay Permanent Road Division was formed pursuant to Streets and Highway Code §§ 1160-1197 on June 23, 2026 through a petition signed by a majority of property owners within the Upper Bay Permanent Road Division. The petition proposed to levy a special tax upon improved and unimproved parcels within the Division. If this Measure is approved by two-thirds of the votes cast on this special tax proposal, the Upper Bay Permanent Road Division will levy a special tax beginning the first full fiscal year after the effective date.

During the first ten years of the special tax, the rate of the special tax shall be $4,350 per improved parcel per year and $2,175 per unimproved parcel per year. Thereafter, the rate of the special tax shall be $455 per parcel per year. After the tenth year, the rate of the special tax shall be adjusted on July 1 of each year based upon the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers, San Francisco-Oakland-Hayward for the previous calendar year. The special tax will remain in effect until repealed. The Upper Bay Permanent Road Division estimates revenue from the special tax will be approximately $456,750 annually.

The revenue raised by this special tax must be placed in a special account and can only be used for the purposes stated in this Measure. The stated purposes for the funds raised by this special tax are for improvements, and then later for maintenance, of the road within the Upper Bay Permanent Road Division.
