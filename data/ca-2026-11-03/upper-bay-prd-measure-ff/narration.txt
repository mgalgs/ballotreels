Measure FF asks the voters of this road division whether to approve a yearly parcel tax to repave and maintain a private road.
The county does not maintain the road, and its upkeep falls to the owners of the parcels along it.
The county board of supervisors formed the division in June 2026 and placed Measure FF on the ballot in July; it needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
For 10 years from fiscal year 2027-2028, each improved parcel would pay $4,350 a year, and the unimproved parcel $2,175, for improvements.
After that, each parcel would pay $455 a year for ongoing maintenance, adjusted every year for consumer prices, until repealed.
The money would repave and maintain the road, from the end of the county-maintained section to the cul-de-sac at its south end.
If Measure FF passes, the division could borrow from the county against the tax, which would repay the loan and its costs.
