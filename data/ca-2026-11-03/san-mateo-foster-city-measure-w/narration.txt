San Mateo-Foster City School District Measure W asks the district's voters whether to approve $498 million in school bonds.
The district would borrow the money and repay it from a tax on property in the district.
Measure W needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would go to listed projects, such as shop and science classrooms, roofs, windows, fire alarms, and housing for teachers and staff.
The district's tax statement estimates a tax of $29 per $100,000 of assessed value, on average and at most, through 2054-2055.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $900 million.
Opponents say Measure W means 30 years of debt and will not fix declining test scores or enrollment.
Supporters say Measure W repairs 60-year-old schools and houses teachers who struggle with the high cost of living.
A yes vote would let the district issue the bonds.
A no vote would prevent the district from issuing the bonds.
