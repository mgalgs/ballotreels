# Measure W — San Mateo-Foster City School District (general obligation bond, $498,000,000)

- **Jurisdiction:** San Mateo-Foster City School District (elementary and middle schools, San Mateo and Foster City).
- **Kind:** School facilities general obligation bond (Proposition 39), $498,000,000.
- **Who votes:** the voters of the District. Resolution Section 1 (p. 2) asks the Registrar "to conduct an election under the provisions of Proposition 39 and the Act and submit to the electors of the District the question of whether bonds of the District in the aggregate principal amount of $498,000,000 (the "Bonds") shall be issued and sold". Section 3 (p. 3): "the purpose of the election shall be for the voters in the District to vote on a proposition".
- **Vote threshold:** 55%. Recital, p. 1: Proposition 39 "reduced the voter threshold for *ad valorem* property tax levies used to pay for debt service on bonded indebtedness to 55% of the votes cast on a school district general obligation bond measure". Impartial analysis, p. 2: "This Measure passes if at least 55% of those voting on the Measure vote "yes.""
- **Placed by:** Board of Trustees, Resolution No. 06/25-26.
- **Election:** November 3, 2026.

## Ballot question

> To improve elementary and middle school classrooms for skilled trades, science, technology, engineering, and math; replace leaky roofs, old windows, fire alarms and emergency communications systems; provide affordable housing to attract/retain quality teachers/staff; acquire, construct, repair facilities, equipment, sites; shall San Mateo-Foster City School District's measure be adopted, authorizing $498,000,000 in bonds at legal interest rates, levying $29 per $100,000 assessed value, averaging $35,000,000 annually while bonds are outstanding, with audits, independent oversight, and funds used locally?

(resolution-full-text.pdf p. 6, Exhibit A; read from the scan. Answer labels "Bonds – Yes" / "Bonds – No".)

## Full text (resolution)

- Resolution No. 06/25-26, "RESOLUTION OF THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF SAN MATEO-FOSTER CITY SCHOOL DISTRICT ORDERING AN ELECTION, AND ESTABLISHING SPECIFICATIONS OF THE ELECTION ORDER" (p. 1).
- Adopted: "ADOPTED, SIGNED AND APPROVED this 25th day of June, 2026." (p. 4). Secretary's certificate (p. 5): AYES 5; NOES, ABSENT, ABSTENTIONS blank. ACRE redacted the signatures.
- Section 2 (p. 3): "That the date of the election shall be November 3, 2026."
- Section 3(a) (p. 3): "that the proceeds of the sale of the Bonds shall be used only for the purposes set forth in the ballot measure and not for any other purpose, including teacher or administrator salaries or other school operating expenses".
- Section 3(c)-(e) (p. 3): annual independent performance and financial audits, and "the Board shall appoint a Citizens' Oversight Committee in compliance with Education Code Section 15278 no later than 60 days after the Board enters the election results in its minutes".
- Section 8 (p. 4): "The maximum rate of interest on any Bond shall not exceed the maximum rate allowed by Education Code Sections 15140 to 15143, as modified by Government Code Section 53531."
- Recital (p. 1): the measure "would allow the District to update classrooms and labs for science, technology, engineering, and math, restore shop classes and career technical education with modern equipment, update 25-year old fire alarms and emergency communication systems, provide affordable housing to help attract and retain high-quality teachers and staff, and improve safety, security, and accessibility across schools".
- The project list is in Exhibit B (pp. 7-10); not transcribed here.

## Impartial analysis

Headed "IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE [TBD]". **No author's name or office is printed** on either page, so the author is unconfirmed from this file. Bracketed numbers are per-paragraph word counts in the original.

- p. 1: "The Board of Trustees of the San Mateo-Foster City School District (District) proposes this Measure, which would authorize the District to issue bonds in a principal amount not to exceed $498 million."
- p. 1: "The District's best estimate of the average annual tax rate levy to fund this bond is $29.00 per $100,000 ($0.029/$100) of assessed valuation. The District estimates that the total amount repayable during the life of the bond, including principal and interest, will be approximately $900 million."
- p. 1: the bond debt "is anticipated to last until fiscal year 2054-55."
- p. 1: "No proceeds from the bonds shall be used for teacher or administrator salaries or other school operating expenses, except to reimburse the District for the cost of staff time spent working on the bond projects themselves."
- p. 1: "A "yes" vote on this Measure would authorize the District to issue bonds in a principal amount not to exceed $498 million for the purposes listed in the Bond Project List."
- p. 2: "A "no" vote would prevent the District from issuing the bonds." / "This Measure passes if at least 55% of those voting on the Measure vote "yes.""

## Tax rate statement (resolution-full-text.pdf p. 11)

"TAX RATE STATEMENT CONCERNING MEASURE [blank box]", signed Diego R. Ochoa, Superintendent, San Mateo-Foster City School District.

- "The best estimate of the average annual tax rate that would be required to be levied to fund this bond issue over the entire duration of the bond debt service, based on estimated assessed valuations available at the time of filing of this statement, is $29.00 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. It is currently expected that the tax will be collected until fiscal year 2054-55."
- "The best estimate of the highest tax rate that would be required to be levied to fund this bond issue, for each year that bonds are outstanding, based on estimated assessed valuations available at the time of filing this statement, is $29.00 per $100,000 of assessed valuation, which is projected to be the same in every fiscal year the bonds remain outstanding."
- "The best estimate of total debt service, including principal and interest, that would be required to be repaid if all the bonds are issued and sold will be approximately $900 million."
