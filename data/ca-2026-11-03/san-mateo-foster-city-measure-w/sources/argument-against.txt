San Mateo-Foster City School District $498M Bond Measure

The San Mateo-Foster City School District is asking you to incur bond debt of $498 million. That's a debt you owe for the next 30 plus years.

Have they earned it?

The stats:

- Loss of ADA enrollment from 2020-21 school year = 649 students.
- Percentage of students behind grade level in English = 47.26%
- Percentage of students behind grade level in math = 50.57%
- Chronic absenteeism data = 16.2%
- Teachers' salaries of $82,574 (2020-21) rose to $104,943 (2024-25)
  - An Increase = $22,369

Teacher's get pay raises while students test scores decline!

Education Data Partnership (ED-DATA.ORG) shows enrollment of 9,708 students. That's bond debt exceeding $51,297 per student -- NOT counting interest expense which usually doubles the cost. Does that do anything to help students? Clearly no.

When you buy a home, truth in lending laws require that you be told the real total cost of buying that home. For example:

- Borrowing $498,000,000; paying 3% interest for 30 years would incur interest expenses of approximately $257,000,000 plus principal $498,000,000 for a lifetime total cost of $755,000,000....of YOUR money!

Consumer protection laws should apply to bond issues. As taxpayers, we deserve to know the full truth about Measure M. We don't even know when this debt will begin, let alone what the market interest rate will be, when it does.

Just say no to bond debt.

Question: will this bond measure fix declining English proficiency? NO!

Question: will this bond measure fix declining math proficiency? NO!

Question: will this bond measure fix declining enrollment? NO!

Question: will this bond measure fix chronic absenteeism data? NO!

Stop rewarding bad behavior. Vote NO tax increase!
