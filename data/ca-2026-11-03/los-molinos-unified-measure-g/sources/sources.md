## Facts with quotes

- **Ballot label (Exhibit A):** "To improve local schools; modernize/upgrade old classrooms, restrooms and school facilities; improve career technical education classrooms and labs; make safety and security improvements; construct a new elementary gymnasium; and construct new classrooms, shall Los Molinos Unified School District issue $9,800,000 of bonds at legal rates, generating on average $608,000 annually while bonds are outstanding at a rate of $59 per $100,000 assessed value, with annual audits, independent citizens' oversight, NO money for salaries and all money staying local?" Bonds – Yes / Bonds – No.
- **Resolution §1:** an election "shall be held within the District on November 3, 2026, for the purpose of submitting to the registered voters of the District the full ballot proposition contained in Exhibit B". Adopted "this 18th day of June, 2026" — AYES 5, NAYS 0, ABSENT 2; signed Barbara Morgan, Board President.
- **Schools (analysis):** "The schools contained within the District include Los Molinos Elementary, Vina Elementary, and Los Molinos High School."
- **Vote, rate, revenue (analysis):** "the District will seek to levy, upon a 55% vote, an ad valorem tax ... approximately fifty-nine ($59.00) dollars per hundred thousand ($100,000.00) dollars of assessed valuation ... generating on average approximately six hundred and eight thousand ($608,000.00) dollars annually while bonds are outstanding."
- **Total debt service / final year:** "twenty million six hundred sixty-seven thousand and one hundred eighteen ($20,667,118.00) dollars. The final fiscal year in which the tax is anticipated to be collected is 2059-60."
- **Use limits:** "No bond proceeds may be used for teacher or administrator salaries, pensions, or other school operating expenses."
