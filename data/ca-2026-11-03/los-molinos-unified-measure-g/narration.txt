Measure G asks the voters of this school district whether to approve $9.8 million in school bonds.
The district would borrow the money and repay it from a tax on property in the district.
Measure G needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would upgrade old classrooms and restrooms, improve career training labs, add safety measures, and build new classrooms and an elementary gym.
The district's tax rate statement estimates an average tax of $58.97 per $100,000 of assessed value, and at most $60, through 2059-2060.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $20.7 million.
No argument against Measure G appears among the filings that the county elections office has posted for this election.
Supporters say the schools average 76 years old, and that Measure G qualifies the district for state matching funds.
A yes vote approves selling the bonds, to be repaid by a tax on property.
A no vote does not approve selling the bonds.
