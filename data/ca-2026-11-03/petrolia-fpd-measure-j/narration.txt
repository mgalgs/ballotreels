Measure J asks the voters of this fire protection district whether to approve a yearly parcel tax for fire and emergency services.
The tax would be collected on the property tax bill, like other property taxes.
Measure J needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
A parcel with a home or business would pay $95 a year, and land that could be built on but is not, $75.
An owner of adjoining parcels could apply to pay $160 a year for the group.
The rates could rise each year with inflation.
Owners with very low incomes and active volunteer firefighters could apply to be exempt.
The ballot question estimates the tax would raise $21,181 a year.
No argument against Measure J was filed, so the county voter guide prints only the argument in favor of it.
Supporters say Measure J provides the funding needed to continue a proposed reorganization of three fire services into one district.
A yes vote adopts the annual special tax.
A no vote rejects the annual special tax.
