Page 2, sections 4 and 5:

4. Ballot Question. The ballot question to be submitted to the voters shall read substantially as follows:

To provide a stable, locally controlled source of funding to maintain and improve fire protection and emergency response services and to support long-term fire service sustainability in the region, shall Ordinance No. 2026-01 of the Petrolia Fire Protection District establishing an annual special tax of $75 per taxable unimproved parcel, $95 per taxable improved parcel, and $160 per qualifying contiguous parcel group, raising an estimated $21,181 annually unless repealed by the voters, be adopted?

YES

NO

5. Special Tax Ordinance. The ordinance authorizing the special tax to be approved by the voters pursuant to this Resolution is as set forth in Exhibit A hereto. The Board of Directors hereby approves the ordinance, and form thereof, and its submission to the voters of the District at the November 3, 2026 election, subject to the approval of two-thirds of the voters voting on the measure at the election called by the adoption of this resolution. The full text of the ordinance shall be included in the voters' pamphlet. The Ordinance establishes the special tax, including the applicable tax rates, authorized exemptions, annual adjustment provisions, and collection procedures.
