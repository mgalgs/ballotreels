## Facts with quotes

- **Ballot question (Resolution 2026-0723 §4):** "To provide a stable, locally controlled source of funding to maintain and improve fire protection and emergency response services and to support long-term fire service sustainability in the region, shall Ordinance No. 2026-01 of the Petrolia Fire Protection District establishing an annual special tax of $75 per taxable unimproved parcel, $95 per taxable improved parcel, and $160 per qualifying contiguous parcel group, raising an estimated $21,181 annually unless repealed by the voters, be adopted?"
- **Vote needed (guide p. 24):** "As a "special tax," Measure J must be approved by two-thirds of the voters voting in the election".
- **Rates:** "Parcels with one or more structures which may require fire suppression services, such as a home or business, would be taxed $95 per year. Parcels that have the potential to be developed, but are not, would be taxed $75 per year. Contiguous parcels under identical common ownership, regardless of whether they contain structures, would be taxed $160 dollars per year. This requires an approved application from the property owner."
- **Inflation:** "These taxes may be adjusted for inflation each year based on the percentage change in the Consumer Price Index."
- **Exemptions:** hardship waiver for HUD "very low" or "extremely low" income; "Measure J also allows active volunteer firefighters in good standing with the district to apply for and receive an exemption from the tax."
- **Collection:** "The taxes would be placed on the secured tax roll and collected by the Humboldt County Tax Collector in the same manner as other property taxes."
- **Resolution §5:** "subject to the approval of two-thirds of the voters voting on the measure".
- **Argument in favor signers (guide p. 25):** Sara Hanover and Jeff Skehen (Petrolia FPD Commissioners); Chris Gilda (Petrolia FPD Fire Chief); Tanner Speas (Telegraph Ridge FPD Fire Chief); Lars Samuels (Honeydew Volunteer Fire Company Fire Chief).
