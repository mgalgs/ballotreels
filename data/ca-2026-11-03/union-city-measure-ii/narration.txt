City of Union City Measure II asks voters in the city whether to extend the utility users tax for nine more years, at its current 5 percent rate.
Today, Union City taxes phone, video, electricity and gas services at 5 percent of what each provider charges.
Voters first approved the tax in 2020, and it ends after December 31, 2028.
Seniors 70 and older and low-income residents can apply to be exempt.
Measure II would keep the tax at 5 percent until December 31, 2037.
As a general tax, its money could go to any legitimate city purpose, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
The City Attorney's impartial analysis says Measure II would raise about $7 million a year.
Opponents, in a rebuttal in the guide, say the tax adds 5 percent to utility bills, with no guarantee for police or streets.
Supporters say Measure II renews funding at the current rate without raising taxes, preventing deep cuts to the services that keep residents safe.
A yes vote approves extending the utility users tax for nine years at the current rate.
A no vote does not extend the tax, so it would end after December 31, 2028.
