## Facts and the passages that establish them

### Ballot question

S1 PDF p. 81:

> MEASURE H
> With no projected increase in current tax rates and to repair/ replace deteriorating roofs, plumbing, dry rot, termite damage, electrical; upgrade aging schools to meet current health/ safety standards; and upgrade classrooms to support student achievement in math, science, technology, reading, writing, shall Jamul-Dulzura Union School District's measure be adopted authorizing $18,000,000 in bonds at legal rates, levying $30 per $100,000 assessed value (averaging $1,300,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding, with citizen oversight/ audits, all money locally controlled?
> Bonds—Yes / Bonds—No

### Vote required

S1 PDF p. 81: "This measure requires 55% of those voting on the measure."

### Who votes on it

S3, Resolution, Section 2 (PDF p. 5): "The Board hereby orders an election and submits to the electors of the District the question of whether general obligation bonds of the District shall be issued and sold in the maximum principal amount of $18,000,000". Section 3: "such bond election shall be held solely within the boundaries of the District." Section 4: "The purpose of the election shall be for the voters in the District to vote on a bond measure". Cover letter (PDF p. 1): "The election is to occur in the entire territory of the District."

S1 PDF p. 82 (full text): "By approval of this measure by at least 55 percent of the registered voters voting on the measure, the Jamul-Dulzura Union School District (the "District") of San Diego County (the "County") will be authorized to issue and sell general obligation bonds in the aggregate principal of up to $18,000,000".

In short: the electors (registered voters) of the Jamul-Dulzura Union School District.

### Who placed it on the ballot

S1 PDF p. 81: "The Board of Education for the Jamul-Dulzura Union School District ("District") placed this measure on the ballot."

S3 PDF p. 9: "The foregoing Resolution was adopted by the Board of Education of the Jamul-Dulzura Union School District of San Diego County ... by the following vote, on July 9, 2026." AYES 5, NOES 0, ABSENT 0, ABSTAIN 0; "Motion carried 5-0".

Recorded as printed: the cover letter says the resolution orders "a general obligation bond election for November 5, 2024"; the resolution itself (Section 3) says "The date of the election shall be November 3, 2026".

### Impartial analysis

S1 PDF p. 81, "COUNTY COUNSEL IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS". Key statements:

> If approved by 55% of the votes cast, this measure will authorize the District to issue and sell general obligation bonds in an amount not to exceed $18,000,000.

> The District estimates the average tax rate would be $30 for every $100,000 of assessed valuation, and the highest tax rate would be approximately $30 for every $100,000 of assessed valuation. The tax is anticipated to be collected through fiscal year 2062-2063.

> The estimated total debt service, including principal and interest, required to be repaid if the bonds are issued and sold is approximately $43,170,000.

### Tax rate statement

S1 PDF p. 81, "TAX RATE STATEMENT REGARDING PROPOSED JAMUL-DULZURA UNION SCHOOL DISTRICT GENERAL OBLIGATION BONDS", "/s/ Liz Bystedt, Superintendent":

> 1. ... is $30 per $100,000. It is currently expected that the final fiscal year in which it is anticipated that the tax will be collected is 2062-63.
> 2. ... is $30 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. This highest rate is projected to apply in fiscal years 2030-31 through 2062-63.
> 3. ... the total debt service ... is approximately $43,170,000.

S3 (resolution p. 6, PDF p. 8) gives the District's existing bond tax rate: "($60.17 per $100,000 of assessed value)" on the 2025-26 roll, and says the District expects the new bonds "will not increase current tax rates in the aggregate".

### Arguments and rebuttals

- **Argument in favor**, S1 PDF p. 82. First line: "Vote YES on Measure H to repair and upgrade classrooms and school facilities in the Jamul-Dulzura Union School District without increasing existing tax rates." It says "Out of 61 roofs across our schools, 58 are leaking and need replacement". Signers: Dianne Jacob (Former San Diego County Supervisor); Nadine Bennett (Former Superintendent, Jamul-Dulzura Union School District); Kyle Harrell (President, Jamul Soccer; JDUSD Parent); Ami Murofushi (Local Youth Sports Leader; JDUSD Parent); Chris Manzuk (Director of Land Management for the Endangered Habitat Conservancy).
- **Argument against**: none. S1 PDF p. 82: "No Argument Against Measure H was filed with the Office of the Registrar of Voters".
- **Rebuttals**: none printed.
