Jamul-Dulzura Union School District Measure H asks the district's voters whether to approve $18 million in school bonds.
The bonds would be repaid, with interest, by a yearly tax on the assessed value of property in the district.
Measure H needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would repair roofs, plumbing and electrical systems, and upgrade aging classrooms.
The impartial analysis says approval does not guarantee that the listed projects will be funded by the bond sales.
The district's tax rate statement estimates $30 a year per $100,000 of assessed value, through fiscal year 2062-2063.
The statement puts the total repaid, with interest, at about $43.2 million.
No argument against Measure H was filed with the Registrar of Voters for the San Diego County Voter Information Guide.
Supporters say Measure H fixes leaking roofs, failing pipes and termite damage at district schools without increasing existing tax rates.
A yes vote is in favor of authorizing the district to issue and sell the $18 million in bonds.
A no vote is against authorizing the district to issue and sell the bonds.
