Measure RR asks the voters of this city whether to bar evictions for unpaid rent when a tenant owes less than one month of rent.
The City Council says evictions over rent debts of hundreds of dollars or less may be legal today.
The charter's eviction rules cover most rentals, but generally not single-family homes or condominiums.
The rent board recommended the change, which the Council says would protect tenants from unnecessary displacement.
An eviction for unpaid rent would be allowed only when the tenant owes more than one month of rent, at a set rate.
That rate is the average market rent the federal housing department sets each year for local ZIP codes, for a unit of that size.
The eviction notice would have to state the number of bedrooms and that amount.
The official list of measures from the county does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
The mayor, a rent board member, a council member and two others filed an argument for Measure RR; its text was not gathered.
The official county list of measures for this election says that no argument against Measure RR was submitted, by any group or person.
A yes vote sets the one-month minimum before an eviction for unpaid rent.
A no vote leaves the eviction rules in the charter as they are.
