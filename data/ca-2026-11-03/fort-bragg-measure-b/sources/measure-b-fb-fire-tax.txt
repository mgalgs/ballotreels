             ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE B
   Vote YES on Measure B to help keep Fort Bragg’s volunteer
firefighters equipped to protect our community.
 The Fort Bragg Fire Department is built on the dedication of
volunteer firefighters who respond at all hours to fires, medical
emergencies, rescues, traffic collisions, and natural disasters. These
men and women generously give their time to protect our families,
homes, businesses, and visitors. They deserve safe, modern, and
reliable equipment to do their jobs.
 For more than 40 years, Fort Bragg voters have repeatedly
approved a dedicated Fire Equipment Acquisition Tax to help replace
aging fire engines and emergency response equipment. Voters
approved the measure in 1983, 1994, 2004, and 2014, each time by
the required two-thirds majority.
  The current measure simply renews that long-standing local
investment.
 Every dollar stays in Fort Bragg and, by law, can only be used to
purchase fire apparatus and equipment for the Fort Bragg Fire
Department. Funds cannot be used for salaries, pensions, or other
City programs.
 The measure also includes strong accountability:
 ● Funds are deposited into a separate account.
 ● The City Treasurer provides annual public reports.                      NO ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE B WAS SUBMITTED.
 ● The measure expires after 10 years unless renewed by voters.
  By voting YES, you are helping ensure that our volunteer
firefighters have the equipment they need to continue protecting our
community safely and effectively.
 Join Fort Bragg Fire Department Volunteers in voting YES on
Measure B.
