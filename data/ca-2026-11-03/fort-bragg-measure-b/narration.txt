Measure B asks the voters of this city whether to renew a yearly parcel tax that pays for fire equipment.
Voters last approved the tax in 2014, at $22 per parcel a year, and that approval has now expired.
Measure B would set the tax at $50 per parcel a year, for 10 years, starting with the 2027-2028 tax year.
The money could be spent only on buying fire equipment for the city fire department.
Measure B needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The city attorney's impartial analysis says the city estimates the tax would raise about $123,900 a year.
Supporters say volunteer firefighters deserve safe, modern equipment, and every dollar stays local, only for fire equipment.
No argument against Measure B was submitted for the printed voter guide pages on this city measure.
A yes vote approves the ordinance continuing the fire equipment tax for ten years.
A no vote rejects the ordinance continuing the fire equipment tax for ten years.
