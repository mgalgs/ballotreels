EL RANCHO UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT – (1)
VOTE PASSAGE – 55% of the Votes Cast
ED     With funds that cannot be taken by the State or Federal government, shall El Rancho
       Unified School District's measure to update inefficient heating and air-conditioning
       systems; replace aging plumbing and roofs; improve school safety; and modernize and
       equip classrooms and facilities be adopted, authorizing $118 million in bonds with legal
       rates, average annual levies below $55 per $100,000 of assessed valuation ($7.9 million
       annually while outstanding), annual audits, no administrator salaries, independent
       oversight, and disclosure of all spending? Supporters: Bob Archuleta; Marco Zapian;
       Paul Camacho; Guadalupe Diaz; Raul Leos Sr. Opponents: None submitted.
