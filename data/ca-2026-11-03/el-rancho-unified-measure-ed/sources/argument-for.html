ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE ED

Our public schools are our community's most valuable asset. They educate our children, prepare them for successful careers, enhance home values, and support local businesses.

Protecting that investment means keeping our schools safe and functional today, before necessary repairs become costly emergencies. Vote YES on Measure ED to give El Rancho students safe, modern schools with complete public disclosure of every dollar spent.

Measure ED funds:

Health, Safety & Critical Repairs: Replacing inefficient heating and air-conditioning systems, leaky roofs, and aging plumbing; upgrading fire alarm, emergency communication, and campus security systems.

Accessibility & Special Education: Ensuring ADA-accessible classrooms, restrooms, and playgrounds, and upgrading special education classrooms and facilities.

Campus & Learning Environments: Rehabilitating aging school grounds, repairing physical education facilities, and modernizing classrooms districtwide.

Student Wellness: Expanding mental health and wellness spaces for students and families.

Career & Technical Education: Expanding career and technical education classrooms.

Technology Infrastructure: Updating Wi-Fi, computer networks, and technology infrastructure districtwide.

Arts & Performing Arts: Upgrading music and performing arts facilities.

Early Learning: Adding classrooms and facilities for preschool through kindergarten.

Measure ED also requires strict taxpayer protections:

Full Transparency: Complete public disclosure of all spending.

Guaranteed Local Investment: By law, funds cannot be taken by the state or federal government and spent elsewhere.

Independent Oversight: A citizens' bond oversight committee and independent auditor review every dollar spent.

No Funds for Salaries: Bond funds cannot be used for administrators' salaries, pensions, or benefits.

An investment in our schools is an investment in our community's future. Vote YES on Measure ED to give El Rancho students safe, functional schools and the resources to succeed, while safeguarding every taxpayer dollar. Join parents, teachers, neighbors, and community leaders in voting YES.
