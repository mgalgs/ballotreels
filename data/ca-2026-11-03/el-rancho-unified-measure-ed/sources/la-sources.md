## Ballot question and supporters/opponents (verbatim, from the county's final measures list)

> With funds that cannot be taken by the State or Federal government, shall El Rancho Unified School District's measure to update inefficient heating and air-conditioning systems; replace aging plumbing and roofs; improve school safety; and modernize and equip classrooms and facilities be adopted, authorizing $118 million in bonds with legal rates, average annual levies below $55 per $100,000 of assessed valuation ($7.9 million annually while outstanding), annual audits, no administrator salaries, independent oversight, and disclosure of all spending?
>
> Supporters: Bob Archuleta; Marco Zapian; Paul Camacho; Guadalupe Diaz; Raul Leos Sr.
>
> Opponents: None submitted.

Vote threshold: 55% of the Votes Cast (county list).
