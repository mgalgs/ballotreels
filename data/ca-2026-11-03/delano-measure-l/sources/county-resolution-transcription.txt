RESOLUTION NO. 2025 - 43

A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF DELANO, CALIFORNIA, ORDERING THE SUBMISSION TO THE QUALIFIED ELECTORS OF THE CITY OF THREE MEASURES RELATING TO ESTABLISHING AN ELECTIVE OFFICE OF MAYOR TO BE INCLUDED AT THE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION TO BE HELD ON TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 3, 2026 AND GIVING NOTICE OF THE SAME

WHEREAS, a General Municipal Election will be held in the City of Delano ("City"), California, on Tuesday, November 3, 2026; and

WHEREAS, on June 2, 2025, the City Council adopted a resolution calling and giving notice of the holding of a General Municipal Election to be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026; and

WHEREAS, on June 2, 2025, the City Council adopted a resolution requesting the Board of Supervisors of the County of Kern to render specified services for the General Municipal Election to be held on November 3, 2026; and

WHEREAS, the City Council desires to submit to the voters at the election three questions relating to the Elective Office of Mayor.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the City Council of the City of Delano, California, does authorize and determine as follows:

SECTION 1. The City Council hereby orders that the following questions be submitted to the voters in the City of Delano, California, on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, and that the three (3) measures are to appear on the ballot as follows:

[Ballot question box]
ELECTIVE OFFICE OF MAYOR
Shall the electors elect a mayor and four city council members? A "Yes" vote means the citizens directly elect the mayor; a "No" vote continues the practice that the council chooses one of its number to preside as Mayor on an annual basis.

SECTION 2. The ballots to be used at the election shall be in the form and content as required by law.

SECTION 3. The proposed measures submitted to the voters are attached as Exhibit "A" and Exhibit "B," which are incorporated by reference

SECTION 4. The vote requirement for the measures to pass is a majority of 50% +1 of the votes cast.

SECTION 5. The polls for the election shall open at 7:00 a.m. on the day of the election and shall remain open continuously from that time until 8:00 p.m. of the same day when the polls shall be closed, except as provided in Elections Code section 14401.

SECTION 6. In all particulars not recited in this Resolution, the election shall be held and conducted as provided by law for holding municipal elections.

SECTION 7. Notice of the time and place of holding the election is hereby given and the City Clerk is hereby authorized, instructed and directed to give further or additional notice of the election in the time, form, and manner required by law.

SECTION 8. The City Clerk is directed to file a certified copy of this Resolution with the Kern County Registrar of Voters.

PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of the City of Delano at a special meeting held on the 2nd day of June, 2025, by the following vote:

ORDINANCE NO. 2026-

AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF DELANO, CALIFORNIA, AMENDING SECTION 2.04.050 (PRESIDING OFFICER ELECTION AND DUTIES) OF CHAPTER 2.04 (CITY COUNCIL) OF THE DELANO MUNICIPAL CODE TO ESTABLISH THE OFFICE OF AN ELECTED MAYOR WITH A TWO YEAR TERM

WHEREAS, California Government Code section 34900 permits a city council to submit to the voters the question of whether voters shall elect a mayor and four city council members, and whether the mayor shall serve a two-year or four-year term; and

WHEREAS, the Delano City Council is currently organized under the default state law organization for general law cities in which the City Council selects one of its members to serve as Mayor pursuant to Government Code section 36801; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Election Code section 9222, Government Code section 36502, and laws relating to general law cities, the City Council submitted ballot measures to the voters on November 3, 2026, amending the Delano Municipal Code to establish the office of an elected Mayor for a term of either two or four years; and

WHEREAS, on November 3, 2026, a majority of the voters voted in favor of Measure "____" which proposed the following to the voters: "MEASURE _____: ELECTIVE OFFICE OF MAYOR. Shall the electors elect a mayor and four city council members? A "Yes" vote means the citizens directly elect the mayor; a "No" vote continues the practice that the council chooses one of its number to preside as Mayor on an annual basis."; and

WHEREAS, on November 3, 2026, a majority of the voters voted in favor of a two-year term under Measure "____" which proposed the length of the term for elective office of mayor; and

WHEREAS, at the next general municipal election held in the city, in November 2028, one of the offices of city councilmember, to be filled at the election, shall be designated as the office of mayor, to be filled at the election pursuant to Government Code section 34902.

NOW THEREFORE, THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF DELANO, CALIFONIA, DOES HEREBY ORDAIN AS FOLLOWS:
