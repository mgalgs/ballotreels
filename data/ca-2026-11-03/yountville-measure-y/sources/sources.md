## Facts and quotes

**Ballot question** (staff report, "Proposed Ballot Measure – Increase in Appropriations (GANN) Limit"): "To fully utilize TOT and sales tax generated from visitors for public services, parks and recreation, police, and fire emergency programs, shall the appropriations limit set by Article 13B of the California Constitution be increased by $3,000,000 plus 1/4 of TOT revenues for the prior fiscal year, for each year for a four year period commencing FY 2027/2028? By approving this appropriation limit, no existing tax is increased, and no new taxes are created."

Caveat: this is the wording in the staff report recommending the resolution, which the Town's page labels "Staff report with official ballot language". The adopted resolution and the printed ballot were not checked.

**Impartial analysis** ("Town of Yountville, Impartial Analysis of Measure Y, Increase of Appropriation Limits – Gann Limit", signed Gary B. Bell, Yountville Town Attorney, dated 8/4/2026), key statements:
- "This measure was placed on the ballot by the Yountville Town Council and would authorize the Town to continue appropriating the proceeds of taxes already approved. This measure does not approve or impose any new taxes or increase any existing taxes."
- "This measure increases the Town's annual appropriations limit by $3,000,000 plus one-fourth (1/4) of the transient occupancy tax (TOT) revenues for the prior fiscal year, for each year for a four year period commencing with fiscal year 2027/2028."
- **Vote needed:** "This measure requires simple majority (50% + 1) voter approval to pass. If less than a majority of votes cast at the election are in favor, the measure will not pass and the Town's annual appropriations limit will be maintained subject to annual adjustments under state law."
- Background: "If the Town receives tax revenues in excess of its appropriations limit over a period of two fiscal years, it must return those excess tax revenues by a revision of tax rates or fee schedules within the next two subsequent fiscal years."

**Who votes:** Yountville voters (Town page: "Who decides: Yountville voters").

**Argument in favor:** Town page: "The Yountville Town Council submitted an Argument in Favor of Measure Y ... The argument was signed by all five members of the Town Council."
