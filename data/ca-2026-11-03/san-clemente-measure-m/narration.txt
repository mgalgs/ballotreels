San Clemente Measure M asks the voters of San Clemente whether to add a 1 percent sales tax for beach and fire protection.
Voters placed Measure M on the ballot by signing a petition.
Today, the sales tax in San Clemente is 7.75 percent.
Measure M would raise it to 8.75 percent for 10 years.
Half the money would go to a beach fund, with at least 70 percent of that spent on adding sand and controlling erosion.
The other half would go to a fund for fire protection and wildfire prevention.
The impartial analysis says the city estimates the tax would raise about $15 million a year.
Supporters say Measure M would restore beaches and help prevent wildfires, with every dollar legally restricted to those two uses.
Opponents say Measure M would make the city the highest-taxed in South Orange County, and fire money could replace existing funding.
A yes vote is a vote in favor of the tax.
A no vote is a vote against the tax.
