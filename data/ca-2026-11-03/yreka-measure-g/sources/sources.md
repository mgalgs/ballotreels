## Facts with quotes

- **Ballot question:** "MEASURE G To provide funding (all funds used for local services) for infrastructure and general City services, including, but not limited to, public safety, park and recreation programming, community events, and local economic development, shall the City increase the transient occupancy tax (hotel tax) rate from 10% to 12% on July 1, 2027, with an estimated increase of $245,000.00 generated annually from the resulting tax and will remain in effect until repealed or amended?"
- **Who votes:** "The following question will be submitted to the qualified voters of the City of Yreka".
- Vote threshold: not stated in the notice; not confirmed.
