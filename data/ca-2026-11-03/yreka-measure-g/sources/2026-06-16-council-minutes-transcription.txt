11. CITY BUSINESS:
Human Resources Director and City Attorney
a. Agenda Title: Discussion/Possible action on Approving a Resolution Proposing an Increase of the Uniform Transient Occupancy Tax (Hotel Tax) of the City of Yreka to be submitted to the voters for approval.

Recommended City Council Action: Motion to Approve Resolution 2026-22 of the City Council of the City of Yreka (1) on its own motion, the submission to the Electors of the City of Yreka at the November General Election, a Ballot Measure proposal to Amend Sections 3.12.030 and add Section 3.12.140 of the City of Yreka [page 4:] Municipal Code to Increase the Uniform Transient Occupancy Tax (Hotel Tax) from ten percent (10%) to Twelve Percent (12%) (2) Directing the City Attorney to Prepare an Impartial Analysis of the Measure; and (3) City Council Authorizes Councilmember Clair and Councilmember McCoy to Author and Submit a Ballot Measure Argument in Favor of the Ballot Measure.

Motion to Approve Resolution 2026-22 of the City Council of the City of Yreka (1) on its own motion, the submission to the Electors of the City of Yreka at the November General Election, a Ballot Measure proposal to Amend Sections 3.12.030 and add Section 3.12.140 of the City of Yreka Municipal Code to Increase the Uniform Transient Occupancy Tax (Hotel Tax) from ten percent (10%) to Twelve Percent (12%) (2) Directing the City Attorney to Prepare an Impartial Analysis of the Measure; and (3) City Council Authorizes Councilmember Clair and Councilmember McCoy to Author and Submit a Ballot Measure Argument in Favor of the Ballot Measure.
