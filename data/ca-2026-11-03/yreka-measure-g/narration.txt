Measure G asks the voters of this city whether to raise the hotel tax from 10 percent to 12 percent of the rent.
The City Council placed Measure G on the ballot in June 2026.
Today, guests of hotels, campgrounds and RV parks in the city pay a tax of 10 percent of the rent.
The rate would rise to 12 percent on July 1, 2027, and stay until repealed or amended.
It is a general tax, paid into the general fund for city services.
Without a new vote, the council could clarify the tax or change its exemptions, but not raise the rate above 12 percent.
Measure G needs a majority of the vote to pass.
The ballot question estimates that Measure G would raise about $245,000 more a year.
No argument against Measure G appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
No argument for Measure G appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
A yes vote on Measure G raises the hotel tax from 10 percent to 12 percent.
A no vote on Measure G keeps the hotel tax at 10 percent.
