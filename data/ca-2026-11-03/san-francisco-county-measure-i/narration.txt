Proposition I asks San Francisco voters whether to reserve half of the transfer tax on property sales over $10 million for affordable housing.
Today the city taxes those sales at 5.5 or 6 percent, and the money can be spent on any public purpose.
Proposition I would split that tax into two equal halves, so the total rate stays the same.
Money from the new tax could be spent only on affordable housing, including social housing and help for tenants.
The Controller's Statement says about $120 million a year would move out of the General Fund, about 2 percent of its roughly $7 billion.
A new exemption for some recently built apartment buildings would lower total revenue by about $1 million a year, less than 1 percent.
Supporters say voters passed this tax in 2020 for affordable housing, yet none has reached housing in two years.
Opponents say Proposition I starves the General Fund of transfer tax money, forcing city service cuts in lean years.
If you vote yes, you want to dedicate half of this transfer tax to affordable housing.
If you vote no, you do not want to make these changes.
