City of Pacifica Measure BB asks voters in the city whether to add a 1 percent sales tax.
The City Council placed Measure BB on the ballot, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
Measure BB would raise the sales tax rate by 1 percent, until voters repeal it.
Measure BB would be a general tax, so the money could pay for any city purpose.
The City Attorney's impartial analysis reports a city estimate of about $5 million a year in tax revenue.
Supporters say Measure BB protects police, fire and 911 services, and that years of cost cuts have hit their limit.
Opponents say Measure BB would give Pacifica the highest sales tax in northern California, a tax that never expires.
A yes vote is a vote to approve the tax.
A no vote is a vote against the tax.
