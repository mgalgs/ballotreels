(Argument in Favor)
PROTECT PACIFICA'S 911 EMERGENCY SERVICES: Vote YES on "X".
Nearly 1,000 Pacifica residents participated in the Pacifica Discussion 2026 and agree there's nothing more important than Pacifica's public safety services.
YES on "X":
- Maintains emergency medical response times
- Prevents the elimination of firefighter positions
- Maintains police services
- Funds emergency response services
Firefighters who protect us have no space to decontaminate and store personal protection equipment when returning from fires – increasing risk of exposure to carcinogens. YES on "X" funds the facilities to reduce their risk of exposure to hazardous materials.

MAINTAINING PACIFICA'S INFRASTRUCTURE
Pacifica has a backlog of $84 million in needed street and road repairs and maintenance needs, and these repairs become even more expensive, pushing the backlog to $123 million in five years.
YES on "X":
- Paves streets in the worst conditions
- Maintains storm drains to prevent flooding
- Keeps parks, beaches and public spaces safe and maintained

PACIFICA'S FUTURE
City staff worked diligently for years to cut costs but there is a limit, and we have reached it. Further cuts would seriously jeopardize our security, safety, and overall quality of life, and push our city closer to bankruptcy. YES on "X" protects Pacifica's long-term financial stability.

MEASURE "X" ACCOUNTABILITY
Yes on "X" includes strict accountability provisions, including public spending reports, community oversight and annual independent financial audits. Additionally, all funds go ONLY to Pacifica, and none can be taken by the state.

Here's what Measure "X" WON'T do:
- Measure "X" is NOT a tax on your home or property
- Measure "X" DOES NOT APPLY to food purchased as groceries or prescription medications.

Pacifica is blessed with beautiful beaches and open spaces, and this is a major reason why most of us live here. Join Pacifica firefighters, police, local business owners, a unanimous City Council and your neighbors and vote YES on "X".
