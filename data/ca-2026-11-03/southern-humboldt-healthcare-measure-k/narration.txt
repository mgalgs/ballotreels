Measure K asks the voters of this health care district whether to approve $25 million in bonds for a new hospital.
The district would sell the bonds and repay them with a property tax.
Measure K needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The money would pay for projects such as a new hospital, an upgraded emergency room and a heliport.
The impartial analysis says medical equipment, one planned use, likely falls outside the legal exception allowing this tax.
The tax rate statement estimates $80 a year per $100,000 of assessed value, through 2052-2053.
The total to repay, with interest, would be about $50.4 million.
Supporters say the hospital, built 77 years ago in a converted home, must be replaced within 3 years or close.
No argument against Measure K was filed, so the county voter guide prints only the argument in favor of it.
A yes vote approves up to $25 million in bonds.
A no vote rejects the bonds.
