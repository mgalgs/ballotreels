## Facts with quotes

- **Ballot question (packet p. 10):** "To replace 77 year-old Jerold Phelps Community Hospital with a modern hospital to provide life-saving emergency care for victims of accidents, heart attacks, strokes, and other emergencies, improve Emergency Room facilities, operating rooms, provide advanced medical equipment/technology and life flight services; and qualify for matching funds shall Southern Humboldt Healthcare District's measure authorizing $25,000,000 in bonds be adopted, levying 8 cents per $100 assessed value (raising $2,000,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding, with independent citizen oversight and all funds staying local?"
- **Multi-county:** resolution §2 asks "the Board of Supervisors of each of the Counties" to consolidate, and refers to "the Clerk of the Board of both of the Counties". The packet pages I read do not name the second county; not confirmed here.
- **Amount, rate, total (guide p. 26):** "The measure would authorize the district to sell bonds in the amount of $25 million and levy a property tax to fund the repayment of the bonds. The district estimates that the average annual tax necessary to fund the repayment would be $80 per $100,000 of assessed property value, and that the total cost of debt service, including principal and interest, would be approximately $50.4 million. The measure requires a two-thirds vote to pass."
- **Projects:** "These include a new hospital facility, an upgraded emergency room, flight and heliport facilities to accommodate expedited life flight air transportation, meeting upcoming seismic requirements, and complying with the Americans with Disabilities Act."
- **County Counsel caution (verbatim):** "One of the intended uses of the funds by the district is the acquisition of medical equipment. This likely does not fall within the constitutional exemption for the acquisition and improvement of real property."
- **Compliance:** "Measure K complies with these requirements." (Gov. Code 53410 purpose statement, account, annual report)
- **Argument in favor signers (guide p. 27):** Yvonne Hendrix, Director, Southern Humboldt Chamber of Commerce; Seth Ayers, Chief, Redway Fire Protection District; Carolyn Hino-Bourassa, Local Pharmacist, Volunteer & Taxpayer; Christina Huff, Non-Profit Board Member & Local Taxpayer; Shawn Masterson, Business Owner & Community Volunteer.
