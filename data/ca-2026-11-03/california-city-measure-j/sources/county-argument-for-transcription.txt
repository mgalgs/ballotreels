ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE J

A YES vote on Measure J will allow visitors to California City to contribute a greater share toward the cost of the public services they use while visiting our community.

The Transient Occupancy Tax (TOT), commonly known as the "hotel tax," is paid by guests staying fewer than 30 days in hotels, motels, inns, and short-term rentals. The tax is collected by lodging operators and remitted to the City. Residents are not subject to this tax unless they choose to stay in local lodging.

California City's Transient Occupancy Tax rate has remained at 6% since 2006, making it one of the lowest rates in Kern County and well below the rate charged by most California cities. Measure J would update the City's hotel tax to a rate more consistent with surrounding communities and statewide standards.

Revenue generated by Measure J will remain local and be deposited into the City's General Fund, where it may be used to support essential municipal services such as public safety, street and roadway maintenance, parks, infrastructure, emergency response, code enforcement, and other general governmental services as determined through the City's annual public budget process.

As visitors continue to utilize California City's roads, parks, public safety services, and other community amenities, it is reasonable that they contribute toward the costs of maintaining those services.

Measure J is a tax requiring a simple majority vote for approval. It does not create a special tax, nor does it dedicate funds to a single purpose.

A YES on Measure J will help ensure that visitors contribute their fair share while keeping locally generated revenue in California City to support the services our community relies upon.

Vote YES on Measure J

/s/ Marquette Hawkins, Mayor

/s/ James Creighton, Mayor Pro Tem
