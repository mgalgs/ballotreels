Measure G asks the voters of this city whether to renew an existing 1 percent sales tax until ended by voters.
Voters approved the tax in 2014, to last 15 years and end in 2030 unless voters changed it.
The tax is 1 percent of the price of goods sold in the city.
Measure G would keep the rate at 1 percent and remove the 2030 end date, so the tax lasts until voters end or change it.
The money would support general city services, and a committee of members of the public would review and report on its use.
Measure G needs a simple majority to pass.
The ballot question says the tax provides about $30 million a year.
Any argument against Measure G is filed with the city clerk, and is not online.
Any argument for Measure G is filed with the city clerk, and is not online.
A yes vote renews the 1 percent sales tax until ended by voters.
A no vote does not renew the tax, which would end in 2030.
