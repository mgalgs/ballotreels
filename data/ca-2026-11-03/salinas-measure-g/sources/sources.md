## Quoted facts

- **Ballot question** (resolution, Section IV): "Without raising taxes, shall the City of Salinas renew Measure G maintaining the existing 1¢ local sales tax providing approximately $30,000,000 annually until ended by the voters to continue funding street and sidewalk repairs and other long-term infrastructure projects, parks, youth and senior programs, police, fire, and emergency response, homeless reduction, and other general city services, with independent citizen oversight, annual audits, and all funds dedicated to and remaining in Salinas?"
- **Who votes:** "submit to the qualified voters of the City, a measure that, if approved, would impose a 1.0% general transactions and use tax".
- **History:** "in 2014, the voters of Salinas voted to approve a general transactions and use tax in the amount of one cent ... designated in the 2014 general election as "Measure G""; "a transactions and use tax to be in effect for fifteen years and to sunset in 2030 unless amended by the voters."
- **Impartial analysis (City Attorney, council draft):**
  - "ask the voters to approve an ordinance that would renew, until ended by a majority of the voters of the city, an existing one percent (1.0%) transactions and use tax".
  - "this ballot measure would renew and continue a "general tax" and not a "special tax.""
  - "The measure also provides for the continuation of annual audits and an independent oversight committee appointed by the Mayor and City Council."
  - "A "NO" vote is a vote to not renew the existing one cent transaction and use tax, meaning the tax would expire in 2030."
  - "The ballot measure would be approved if it receives a simple majority (over 50%) of "YES" votes."
