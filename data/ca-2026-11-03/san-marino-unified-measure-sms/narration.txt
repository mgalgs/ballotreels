Measure SMS asks the voters of this school district whether to combine and renew two parcel taxes for six years.
Today, the district levies two parcel taxes: $1,084 a year through June 2027, and $415 a year through June 2031.
The district's governing board placed Measure SMS on the ballot, and it needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
Measure SMS would levy one $1,499 tax on each parcel from July 2027 to June 2033, rising each year by up to 3 percent.
Owners 65 or older, and some with disabilities, could apply for an exemption on the home they live in.
The money could pay for teachers and counselors, current programs, class sizes, library media specialists and technology.
The measure's full text estimates the tax would raise $6.1 million a year, varying from year to year.
The official list of measures from the county, which names who filed each argument, says no argument against Measure SMS was submitted.
Supporters say that without the measure the district would cut about 10 percent of its budget, and every dollar stays local.
A yes vote replaces the two taxes with one $1,499 parcel tax for six years.
A no vote keeps the $415 tax through June 2031, while the $1,084 tax ends in June 2027.
