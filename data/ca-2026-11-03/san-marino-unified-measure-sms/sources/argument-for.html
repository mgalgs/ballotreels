ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE SMS

Vote YES on Measure SMS to renew excellence in San Marino schools!

San Marino Unified School District is one of the top school districts in the State, with students earning state-level honors in, math, writing, music, and debate.

Since 1991, voter-approved parcel tax funding has kept our schools among California's best and helped protect local property values. That funding is set to expire unless renewed by voters.

Measure SMS is Not a Tax Increase – It would simply combine two existing voter-approved parcel taxes (Measure E and Measure R) into one streamlined renewal, protecting the stable, locally controlled funding our schools depend on.

Without Measure SMS the District will have to cut approximately 10% of its annual budget – leading to dozens of teachers and school staff layoffs, cuts to academic programs and increases in class sizes.

Your YES vote on Measure SMS will:
- Retain and attract high-quality teachers and staff
- Support advanced programs in math, science, engineering, and technology
- Support mental and behavioral health programs
- Protect hands-on learning programs
- Maintain honors and advanced placement courses
- Prepare students to be competitive in college and career

Measure SMS Includes Fiscal Accountability and Local Control
Every dollar stays local and supports only San Marino students; No funds can be taken by the State or other districts; Public disclosure of all spending ensures funds are used as promised; Senior citizens age 65+ remain exempt and do not need to reapply

By combining two measures into one, Measure SMS will also save our school district hundreds of thousands of dollars charged by the County to administer separate elections – money that can instead go directly to the classroom.

Join local teachers, parents, and community leaders in voting YES on Measure SMS to maintain excellent schools without increasing tax rates!
