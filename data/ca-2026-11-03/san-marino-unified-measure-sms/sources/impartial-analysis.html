IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE SMS

By Dawyn R. Harrison, County Counsel

This measure was placed on the ballot by the governing board (Board) of the San Marino Unified School District (District). If approved by two-thirds (2/3) of the votes cast on the measure, this measure would authorize the District to combine and extend two existing special parcel taxes, known as Measure E and Measure R, into one special parcel tax of $1,499 per parcel of taxable real property (Tax).

If approved, the Tax will be levied annually for six years, beginning July 1, 2027, and ending June 30, 2033. Beginning July 1, 2028, the Tax would increase each year by a cost-of-living adjustment equal to the lesser of three percent or the annual average percentage change in the Los Angeles Metropolitan Statistical Area Consumer Price Index.

The measure defines a "Parcel of Taxable Real Property" (Parcel) as any parcel of land that lies wholly or partially within the District and that receives a separate tax bill for ad valorem property taxes from the Los Angeles County Treasurer and Tax Collector (County Tax Collector). Parcels that are otherwise exempt from ad valorem property taxes in any year would also be exempt from the Tax. Certain contiguous parcels under identical ownership and used solely for owner-occupied, single-family residential purposes may be treated as a single parcel under District procedures. The Tax would be collected by the County Tax Collector, with unpaid taxes to bear interest at the same rate applicable to unpaid ad valorem property taxes.

As stated in the full text of the Measure, proceeds from the Tax shall be used only for specified purposes, including to:

- Retain and attract the best qualified teachers and counselors and ensure the continuation of teaching positions;

- Maintain existing educational programs at current levels;

- Maintain class size;

- Maintain district-wide school library media specialists; and

- Maintain and fund technology systems and personnel, as well as academic and college counseling positions.

Tax proceeds must be deposited into a separate account, with an annual written report to be prepared by the District detailing the collection and expenditure of the proceeds.

If this measure is approved, the existing Measure R tax of $415 per year would no longer be collected on or after July 1, 2027. If this measure is not approved, the Measure R tax will continue to be collected until June 30, 2031. This measure would not affect the existing Measure E tax, which is scheduled to continue at $1,084 per parcel through June 30, 2027.

An exemption may be applied for with the District on any Parcel owned by one or more persons who occupy such parcel as their principal residence and are: (1) 65 years of age or older on or before to June 30 of the preceding fiscal year; (2) receives Supplemental Security Income for a disability regardless of age; or (3) receives Social Security Disability Insurance benefits regardless of age and whose yearly income does not exceed 250 percent of the 2012 federal poverty guidelines. Exemptions granted under prior qualified special taxes will not require re-approval. The District's decisions regarding the Tax, including exemptions, would be final and binding.
