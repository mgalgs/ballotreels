SAN MARINO UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT – (1)
VOTE PASSAGE – 2/3 Votes (66.6% of Votes)
SMS    San Marino Unified School District Quality Education Measure Without
       increasing the existing rates and to protect/maintain quality education programs,
       classroom instruction, prevent class size increases or teaching position reductions,
       attract/retain high-quality teachers/employees, support advanced programs in
       math, science, engineering, technology, shall San Marino Unified School
       District's measure to combine and renew their existing parcel taxes for 6 years at
       $1,499 (combined) with senior exemptions, raising approximately $6,100,000
       annually, annual adjustments, requiring spending disclosure be adopted?
       Supporters: Mandy Saber, President, San Marino Council of PTAs; Karla Domier;
       Janice Lee; Paul Callahan; Dr. Peter Koh, OD Opponents: None submitted.
