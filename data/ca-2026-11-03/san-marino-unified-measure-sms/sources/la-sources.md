## Ballot question and supporters/opponents (verbatim, from the county's final measures list)

> San Marino Unified School District Quality Education Measure Without increasing the existing rates and to protect/maintain quality education programs, classroom instruction, prevent class size increases or teaching position reductions, attract/retain high-quality teachers/employees, support advanced programs in math, science, engineering, technology, shall San Marino Unified School District's measure to combine and renew their existing parcel taxes for 6 years at $1,499 (combined) with senior exemptions, raising approximately $6,100,000 annually, annual adjustments, requiring spending disclosure be adopted?
>
> Supporters: Mandy Saber, President, San Marino Council of PTAs; Karla Domier; Janice Lee; Paul Callahan; Dr. Peter Koh, OD
>
> Opponents: None submitted.

Vote threshold: 2/3 Votes (66.6% of Votes) — district-placed parcel tax (county list).

## What's present and what's missing

- **Impartial analysis**: present.
- **Full measure text**: present.
- **Resolution placing the measure on the ballot**: present (Resolution #17, scanned — the OCR-less scan means its exact text could not be independently transcribed here, but the board action and parcel-tax terms are legible from the page images).
- **Tax rate statement**: not present and likely does not apply — this is a parcel-tax renewal (flat per-parcel rate), not a new ad valorem bond levy, which is the category that triggers an Elections Code §9401 tax rate statement. (Las Virgenes Measure LV, also a parcel-tax renewal in this same batch, has no tax rate statement in its ISB feed either.)
- **Argument for**: not found. Five named supporters are listed in the county's final measures list, but the filed argument text was not located in the inputs for this dir.
- **Argument against / rebuttals**: none — county list states "Opponents: None submitted," so no argument against and no rebuttals exist.
