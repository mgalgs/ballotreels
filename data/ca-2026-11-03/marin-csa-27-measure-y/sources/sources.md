Measure Y — County Service Area 27 (Ross Valley Paramedic Authority) — RVPA paramedic special parcel tax continuation, 4 years (FY2027-28 to FY2030-31), 2/3 vote
Retrieved 2026-10-30. Publisher of every file: County of Marin, Board of Supervisors (Granicus agenda/minutes portal, marin.granicus.com, view_id=33).
Letter "Y" comes from the county's list-of-measures page (see ../../README.md); none of these BOS documents carry the letter (Exhibit A says "[letter designation to be assigned by Marin County Registrar of Voters]").

BALLOT QUESTION (verbatim, Resolution No. 2026-066, Section 3):
  To maintain rapid 9-1-1 emergency response times and preserve the number of on-duty paramedics ready to respond to accidents/medical emergencies, shall the Ross Valley Paramedic Authority measure be adopted continuing for four years the paramedic services special tax of up to $107.50/residential living unit or 1,000 square feet of developed nonresidential property, adjusted up to $4/ year, generating approximately $84,000 annually, with annual audits, public spending disclosure and all funds for local paramedic services?
(Same wording as Larkspur S / San Anselmo V except the revenue figure: $84,000 for CSA 27. Exhibit B's notice-of-election version is worded slightly differently — "Shall a measure to provide funding to maintain ... shall the Ross Valley Paramedic Authority measure be adopted ..." — use Section 3.)
