Measure Y asks the voters of this service area whether to continue a parcel tax for paramedic services for four more years.
The tax pays for paramedic care from the regional paramedic authority, which eight local governments formed in 1982.
This year the tax is $103.50 per housing unit, or per 1,500 square feet of other buildings, and it ends in June 2027.
Measure Y would allow up to $107.50 per housing unit or 1,000 square feet of other buildings, rising up to $4 a year.
Each year the county board confirms the rate, and the yearly increase is not automatic.
The money would go only to paramedic and emergency medical services, kept in a separate fund.
Measure Y needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The ballot question estimates the tax would raise about $84,000 a year.
The county elections office says on its page for this measure that no argument against Measure Y was filed.
Supporters say Measure Y keeps 9-1-1 response rapid and paramedics on duty, at the current rate with modest increases.
A yes vote continues the paramedic tax for four more years.
A no vote does not continue the paramedic tax.
