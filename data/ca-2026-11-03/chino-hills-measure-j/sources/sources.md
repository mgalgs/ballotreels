## Quoted facts

- **Who votes:** City of Chino Hills voters. RES: "the City Council places a proposition ("Ordinance") on the ballot for a previously-called general municipal election to be held in the City of Chino Hills on Tuesday, November 3, 2026."
- **Ballot question** (RES_ChinoHills.pdf, Section 2): "Chino Hills Services Measure: Shall the measure protecting Chino Hills' financial stability and maintaining City services including public safety response times; reducing wildfire risk; preparing for/responding to wildfires/natural disasters; helping prevent crime, thefts, burglaries; keeping public areas safe/clean; protecting Clean Water Act resources; preserving/maintaining open space; supporting other city services by establishing a 1¢ sales tax, providing approximately $11,500,000 annually until ended by voters; with audits, spending disclosure, all funds used locally, be adopted?"
- **Impartial analysis** (VIG p. 222, City Attorney): "If approved, Measure J would authorize a one percent (1% - one cent on every dollar) transactions and use (sales) tax within the City of Chino Hills. It is estimated that Measure J will provide an additional $11,500,000.00 annually to the City's general fund" … "This tax would be a "general tax," meaning that revenues raised from the tax would be deposited into the City's general fund and could be used for any lawful City purpose." … "California Revenue and Taxation Code section 7285.9 authorizes the City to levy a general transactions and use (sales) tax so long as the tax is approved by a majority of the voters voting in an election on the issue."
- **Arguments:** for, against, and both rebuttals printed (VIG pp. 222–224).
