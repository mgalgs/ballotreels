City of Chino Hills Measure J asks Chino Hills voters whether to add a one-cent sales tax.
Today the sales tax in Chino Hills is 7.75 percent, and the city gets 1 percent.
Measure J would add 1 percent more, one cent on every dollar.
The tax would last until voters repeal it, and the council could suspend it at any time.
The money would go to the general fund, for any lawful city purpose.
The City Council placed Measure J on the ballot, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
The city attorney's impartial analysis estimates $11.5 million a year, about 18 percent of general fund spending this year.
Supporters say Measure J keeps police patrols and wildfire prevention, and has visitors help pay for services.
Opponents say Chino Hills stays safe without this tax, with new water savings and millions in grants.
A yes vote is a vote in favor of the one-cent sales tax.
A no vote is a vote against the one-cent sales tax.
