Measure G asks this community college district's voters whether to approve $624 million in college bonds.
The district would borrow the money and repay it from a tax on property in the district.
Measure G needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would expand career training classrooms, remove asbestos and mold, and reduce wildfire threats at the colleges.
The district's tax statement estimates an average tax of $24.67 per $100,000 of assessed value, and at most $24.98, through 2058-2059.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $1.25 billion.
Supporters say Measure G funds urgent repairs and fire safety upgrades without raising taxes.
No argument against Measure G was filed for the San Diego County voter guide.
A yes vote is in favor of letting the district sell the bonds.
A no vote is against letting the district sell the bonds.
