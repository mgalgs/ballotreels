# Measure RTM ("Regional Transit Measure") — Public Transit Revenue Measure District — Transactions and use (sales) tax, citizen initiative

- **Jurisdiction:** Public Transit Revenue Measure District (PTRMD): the counties of Alameda, Contra Costa, San Mateo and Santa Clara, and the City and County of San Francisco. Resolution recital, PDF p. 2: the District has "jurisdiction extending throughout the territorial boundaries of the Counties of Alameda, Contra Costa, San Mateo, and Santa Clara, and the City and County of San Francisco".
- **Kind:** citizen initiative ordinance, the "Connect Bay Area Transit Initiative", imposing a retail transactions and use tax of 0.5% in San Mateo County (1% in San Francisco) for 14 years. Ballot designation (PDF p. 7): "Regional Transit Measure".
- **Who votes:** voters in all five counties. Resolution, PDF p. 8: the District requests the Boards of Supervisors "to submit the Initiative Measure ... to the voters of each aforementioned county (and city and county) comprising the District".
- **Threshold:** simple majority, district-wide. Resolution, PDF p. 9: "if a simple majority of voters, assessed based on the cumulative vote within the entire district, and not separately within each county comprising the district, vote in favor of the adoption of the Initiative Measure, the Initiative Measure shall take effect in the District". (The initiative's Sec. 19, PDF p. 26, calls the taxes "special taxes"; the opponents' argument disputes the 50% + 1 threshold — see below.)

## Ballot question (verbatim)

Resolution, PDF p. 8:

> To prevent major service cuts to BART and other transit, avoid increased traffic, and reduce pollution by: Preserving BART, Caltrain, VTA, SamTrans, AC Transit, Muni, other transit for everyone, including workers, students, seniors, persons with disabilities; Supporting transit safety, cleanliness, affordability, reliability; Repairing targeted roads/potholes; Requiring financial transparency, oversight, accountability; shall the measure enacting a 0.5% (Alameda, Contra Costa, San Mateo, Santa Clara counties), and 1% (San Francisco) sales tax for 14 years generating approximately $980,000,000 annually, be adopted?

## Full text

- **Public Transit Revenue Measure District Resolution No. 2**, PDF pp. 2-10. PDF p. 10: "The above resolution was entered into by the Public Transit Revenue Measure District at a duly called and noticed meeting held in San Francisco, California on July 24, 2026". No vote tally is printed. Signature block for the Chair (OCR reads "Sue Noack"). Filed with San Mateo County's Chief Elections Officer in July 2026 (stamp, p. 1; day partly illegible in the OCR).
- **How it qualified** (PDF pp. 3-6): proponents filed notice "on January 16, 2026"; the petition needed "187,119, which is 5% of 3,742,378"; the District Elections Official found "an estimated value of 235,503 valid signatures", exceeding "the 110% threshold (205,831)". San Mateo County's registrar counted "22,727 signatures" (p. 4).
- **The tax** (initiative Sec. 5, PDF p. 16): "0.5 percent in the Counties of Alameda, Contra Costa, San Mateo, and Santa Clara" and "1 percent in the City and County of San Francisco".
- **Duration** (Sec. 15(c), PDF p. 25): the taxes "shall be imposed beginning on the operative date of this ordinance for a period of 14 years from that date." Operative "on the first day of the calendar quarter commencing more than 110 days after the election" (Sec. 15(b)).
- **San Mateo County money** (Sec. 12, PDF pp. 22-24): of revenue generated in San Mateo County, "26.64 percent" to BART, "7.40 percent" to Muni, "24.07 percent" to Caltrain, and "36.64 percent ... to the San Mateo County Transit District for public transit expenses and roadway repavement projects on roads served by fixed-route transit", with smaller shares for administration, fare programs, accessibility and wayfinding.
- **Oversight** (Sec. 13, PDF p. 24): "within six months of the effective date of this ordinance the district shall establish an independent oversight committee".

## Impartial analysis

`impartial-analysis.pdf`. Title (p. 1): "Public Transit Revenue Measure District's Legal Counsel Impartial Analysis of Regional Transit Measure". Signed (p. 2): "s/ Kathleen Kane", "Legal Counsel for the Public Transit Revenue Measure District".

- "This measure was placed on the ballot by voter initiative, signed by the requisite number of voters within the Public Transit Revenue Measure District ("District")." (p. 1)
- "This measure would impose a tax of 0.5% in the counties of Alameda, Contra Costa, San Mateo, and Santa Clara and 1.0% in the City and County of San Francisco." (p. 1)
- "The tax would be in effect for 14 years and would not count toward the statutory limit on combined local sales tax rates." (p. 1)
- "It is estimated that the tax would raise approximately $980 million per year." (p. 1)
- "The tax would become effective if approved by a majority of those voting on the measure throughout the entire District." (p. 2)
- "A "yes" vote is a vote to impose the tax described above for 14 years. A "no" vote is a vote against imposing the tax." (p. 2)
