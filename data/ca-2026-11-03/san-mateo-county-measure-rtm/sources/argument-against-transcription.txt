ARGUMENT AGAINST REGIONAL TRANSIT MEASURE

• The five-county Regional Transit Measure’s (RTM’s) tax increase is a $14 billion-dollar bailout for a broken system with irresponsible management. Promised oversight is toothless. We must demand accountability and structural reform, not ask taxpayers to shoulder yet another heavy new tax burden. DEMAND FIXES BEFORE NEW FUNDING!

• RTM expects working families to pay for ongoing mismanagement. Grand Juries in Alameda County, Contra Costa County, and Santa Clara County have found major transit-management and financial-oversight flaws. Since 2019, ridership at the major transit agencies has fallen 51%, yet expenses have risen 28%. Sales taxes grow with inflation, so why has Transit outspent even its new revenues?

• Sales taxes are regressive, hitting lower-income residents the hardest. California’s rates, often exceeding an effective 9.25% statutory limit, are among America’s highest. RTM would raise sales taxes in 12 San Mateo County cities above 10%. While families tighten belts, transit agencies want blank checks without addressing their administrative bloat.

• We need Fixes Before New Funding, not higher taxes to sustain the inefficient status quo.

• RTM exploits a loophole to bypass statutory taxpayer protections, attempting to pass this new tax hike with a mere 50% +1 majority instead of the traditional two-thirds requirement.

• Furthermore, this tax underwrites continuation of capital-intensive mega-projects. These are routinely years late, way over budget, yet deliver less than 20% of the promised ridership. Instead of propping up outdated transit modes, agencies must adapt to modern commuting realities and reallocate existing capital funds to cover shortfalls.

• This regional tax also extracts local dollars to subsidize inefficiencies elsewhere. Most new RTM taxes from San Mateo County would go to fund transit agencies serving other counties, like San Francisco.

• Don’t reward budgetary negligence. Reform Bay Area Transit by rejecting this tax and forcing fiscal discipline.
