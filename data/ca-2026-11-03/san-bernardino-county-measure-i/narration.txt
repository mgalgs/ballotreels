San Bernardino County Measure I asks county voters whether to extend the half-cent sales tax for transportation.
Voters approved the current half-cent transportation sales tax in 2004, and it ends in March 2040.
Measure I would keep the tax going after 2040, until voters repeal it.
The money could pay only for streets, roads, highways, freeways and public transit.
An oversight committee would keep checking that the money follows the spending plan.
Measure I needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The ballot question says the tax would provide about $250 million a year.
No argument against Measure I was submitted, so the county voter guide prints only the supporters' argument.
Supporters say Measure I continues today's tax without raising it, and helps compete for outside highway funds.
A yes vote lets the tax continue after March 2040, until voters repeal it.
A no vote means the tax would not be extended, and would end in March 2040.
