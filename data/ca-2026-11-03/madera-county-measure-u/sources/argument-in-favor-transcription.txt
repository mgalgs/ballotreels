[Page 1]

Measure U would raise the Transient Occupancy Tax (TOT) or "hotel tax" in Madera County from 9% to 11%.

This is the hotel tax paid by tourists and visitors who stay in our hotels, motels, AirBnBs, and other vacation rentals. Madera County residents do not pay this tax unless they stay in a local hotel, motel, or vacation rental.

The Madera County Board of Supervisors approved placing Measure U on the ballot after authorizing a needs assessment to understand how to serve County residents amid growth, confront the pressure placed on the County budget by Sacramento's unfunded mandates, and increases in the cost of delivering essential services – including public safety – to residents.

One option to address a portion of the needed funding is to increase TOT.

Visitors use our streets, roads, and emergency services as they enjoy all that Madera County has to offer, and we welcome them to experience the Gateway to Yosemite.

Raising the TOT represents a fair approach to recover the increased costs associated with visitor stays in our County, without burdening residents.

If Measure U is approved, Madera County will still have a lower TOT rate than any other county bordering Yosemite National Park.

A 2% increase in TOT will bring in an estimated $1.78 million annually to the County budget, assisting the County in:

• Recruiting and retaining Sheriff's deputies, firefighters, and prosecutors.
• Reducing the average response times for emergency medical services when Madera County residents call 9-1-1.
• Improving access to County services, especially in Eastern Madera County where visitors increase demand on services.

Along with funding key improvements in public safety and essential services, Measure U revenues stay local and cannot be taken away by the State.

Vote YES on Measure U to deliver a safer Madera County without raising taxes on residents.

*Arguments in support of or in opposition to the proposed laws are the opinions of the authors.
