## Facts

**Ballot question.** Legal notice dated July 24, 2026 (Rebecca Martinez, County Clerk-Recorder & Registrar of Voters, by Stephen Serrano):
> "County Measure – Transient Occupancy Tax (TOT Increase): Shall Madera County's existing transient occupancy tax paid only by hotel/short term rental guests be increased by two percent (2%), providing approximately $1,780,000 annually until ended by voters, requiring independent audits, and all funds will be used locally to enhance 911 emergency response; maintain law enforcement; repair damaged roads; protect water quality; retain and attract jobs and businesses; and other general County services?"

**Who votes.** Same notice: "NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN to the qualified electors of Madera County, California, that on Tuesday, November 3, 2026 ... there will be submitted to the voters the following measure". The tax itself applies only in the unincorporated area (see the analysis).

**What it does; vote needed.** Impartial Analysis by County Counsel (Regina A. Garza, Madera County Counsel; filed Jul 15 2026), titled "Measure U to Increase Madera County's Transient Occupancy Tax Rate from 9% to 11%":
> "Under the California Constitution and related state laws, local governments may levy a general tax if it is submitted to and approved by at least a majority of the voters within its jurisdiction."
> "Measure U, if approved, would amend Section 3.20.030 of the Madera County Code increasing the Transient Occupancy Tax ("TOT") levied in the unincorporated areas of Madera County from 9% to 11%."
> "The TOT increase proposed by Measure U is a general tax, meaning that the revenue generated by the tax would be deposited into the Madera County general fund and may be used for any general governmental purpose."
> "Currently, TOT generates approximately eight million dollars ($8,000,000.00) in general fund revenues for Madera County. Measure U, if passed by a majority of voters, would generate approximately one million seven hundred eighty thousand dollars ($1,780,000.00) in additional general fund revenue annually."
> "This Measure is placed on the ballot by the Board of Supervisors of Madera County."
