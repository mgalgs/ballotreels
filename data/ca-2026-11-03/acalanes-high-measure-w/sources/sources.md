## Ballot question

From the District's "Measure W Resolution" web page (the "by:" items appear there as a bulleted list):

> Shall the measure supporting high-quality education in local high schools by: Attracting/retaining highly qualified teachers; continuing advanced academic programs in science, technology, engineering, math, and arts that prepare students for success in college and careers; maintaining manageable class sizes; with Acalanes Union High School District levying a $168 parcel tax for eight years, with senior exemptions, citizens’ oversight and independent audits, providing $6,000,000 annually in local school funding that cannot be taken by the State, be adopted?

## Who votes on it

- Resolution p. 2, Section 1: "The Board hereby orders an election and submits to the electors of the District the question of whether an education parcel tax shall be levied and collected in the District"
- Resolution p. 2, Section 2: "the election shall be held solely within the boundaries of the District."
- Resolution p. 1: the Parcel Tax Law authorizes "a school district, upon approval by two-thirds of the electorate voting on the measure, to levy a qualified special tax"
- District web page (measure text): "By approval of this proposition (the “Measure”) by at least two-thirds of the registered voters voting on the proposition, the District will be authorized to levy an education parcel tax of $168 per parcel for eight years"

## Key statements (District documents, not an impartial analysis)

- Resolution p. 1: "the District, comprised of Acalanes High School, Campolindo High School, Las Lomas High School, Miramonte High School, and Acalanes Center for Independent Study"
- Measure text (web page): "the Acalanes Union High School District proposes to levy and collect a qualified special parcel tax for a period of eight years, beginning in fiscal year 2027-28, in the amount of $168 per parcel"
- Measure text: "the parcel tax revenues will be used only for lawful educational purposes and shall not be used for administrators’ salaries or benefits."
