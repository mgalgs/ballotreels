Measure B asks the voters of this city whether to add a 1 percent sales tax for general city services.
The City Council placed Measure B on the ballot by a vote of 6 to 1.
The total sales tax rate in the city would rise from 8.25 percent to 9.25 percent.
As a general tax, the money could be spent for any general city purpose.
The tax would last until ended by voters, and needs a simple majority to pass.
The city attorney's impartial analysis says the city estimates about $18 million a year in new revenue.
Supporters say Measure B keeps a paramedic or medical responder on every emergency call, and repairs potholes and streets.
Opponents say Measure B is a forever tax on families already struggling, with no guarantee for parks or street repair.
A yes vote on Measure B adopts the sales tax ordinance.
A no vote on Measure B does not adopt the sales tax ordinance.
