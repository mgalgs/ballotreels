RESOLUTION NO. _________

A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF MERCED, CALIFORNIA, APPROVING AN ORDINANCE AND ORDERING THAT A MEASURE BE SUBMITTED TO THE VOTERS OF THE CITY AT THE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION TO BE HELD ON NOVEMBER 3, 2026 TO CONSIDER A LOCAL 1¢ (1%) GENERAL TRANSACTIONS AND USE TAX TO BE ADMINISTERED BY THE CALIFORNIA DEPARTMENT OF TAX AND FEE ADMINISTRATION

WHEREAS, the City strives to maintain general municipal services for Merced residents, businesses, and visitors at the levels they expect and deserve; and

WHEREAS, the City Council has been informed by staff that the City needs to increase revenues to fund vital City programs and services; and

WHEREAS, the City of Merced's Fire Staffing is set to be reduced on March 2027 by 27% due to lack of funding; and

WHEREAS, according to the 2022 Pavement Condition Index (PCI) Study, the overall condition of the City of Merced's roads is rated as "Poor" requiring a significant capital investment; and

WHEREAS, the City of Merced's Police staffing remains below 2009 levels and has not kept pace with the City's population growth; and

WHEREAS, designated park sites remain undeveloped, and safety concerns at existing parks, including gang activity and homeless encampments, require an influx of capital and personnel resources; and

WHEREAS, this measure has strict accountability requirements, including oversight by an independent citizens' transparency commission, public disclosure of all spending, and independent annual audits to ensure funds are spent as promised; and

WHEREAS, additional sources of local revenue will help the City maintain local control over its revenue and promote fiscal sustainability; and

[Page 2]

WHEREAS, if approved by the voters, the revenue from this measure will be subject to transparency and accountability provisions to ensure that all funds will remain in Merced for the local general services on which local residents and businesses rely, and that none of the new funding can be taken by the Federal or State governments; and

WHEREAS, the City proposes to submit to the voters of the City an Ordinance providing for the levy of a local transactions and use tax.

NOW, THEREFORE, THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF MERCED DOES HEREBY RESOLVE, DETERMINE, FIND AND ORDER AS FOLLOWS:

SECTION 1. The facts set forth in the recitals of this Resolution are true and correct and establish the factual basis for the City Council's adoption of this Resolution.

SECTION 2. Section 7285.9 of the California Revenue and Taxation Code authorizes any city to levy, increase, or extend a transactions and use tax for general purposes at a rate of 0.125 percent, or a multiple thereof, subject to approval by a two-thirds vote of all of the members of the City Council and by a majority of the qualified voters of the City voting in an election on the issue.

SECTION 3. By Resolution No. 2026-32, adopted on June 15, 2026, the City Council has previously called and given notice of the General Municipal Election to be held in the City on November 3, 2026, and to be consolidated with the Statewide General Election conducted on the same date. Pursuant to California Elections Code Sections 9222 and 10201, the City Council hereby orders that at the said election, a Vital Services Protection Ordinance be submitted to the voters for consideration. The full text of the City of Merced Vital Services Protection Ordinance is attached to this Resolution as Exhibit A and incorporated herein by reference. The measure to be submitted to the voters shall appear and be printed on the ballot as follows:

[Page 3]

[A boxed table: a header row, then the question, with a YES box and a NO box at its right]

City of Merced Vital Services Protection Measure

Shall the measure to maintain local quality of life/ general city services, such as 911 police/ emergency medical/ natural disaster response; responding quickly to neighborhood fires/ retaining firefighters; gang prevention; addressing homelessness; fixing roads/potholes; and keeping parks safe/clean, by establishing a 1¢ sales tax, providing approximately $18,000,000 annually until ended by voters, with public disclosure, independent audits, citizen oversight, and all funds for Merced, be adopted?

[YES box] [NO box]

SECTION 4. The City Council, by at least 2/3 vote, hereby approves the proposed City of Merced Vital Services Protection Ordinance, attached to this Resolution as Exhibit A, to be submitted to the voters and the full text to be published in the November 3, 2026 General Municipal Election Voters Information Guide is SECTION 1 of Exhibit "A". The proposed measure is a general tax, as defined in Article XIIIC of the California Constitution, at a rate of 1% on transactions and uses in the City as defined in the Ordinance, to be collected by the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration until repealed by the voters. The Ordinance shall not take effect unless and until approved by a vote of at least a majority of voters voting on the question at the election.

SECTION 5. The City Attorney is hereby directed to prepare an impartial analysis of the measure pursuant to Elections Code Section 9280.

SECTION 6. The City Council directs that arguments for and against the measure may be filed in accordance with applicable law, and that Councilmember[s] [name(s)] are authorized to file an argument in favor of the measure.

SECTION 7. In all particulars not recited in this Resolution, the election shall be held and conducted as provided by law for holding municipal elections. All persons qualified and registered to vote in the City of Merced as of the date of such election shall be entitled to vote.

SECTION 8. Notice of the time and place of holding the General Municipal Election is hereby given and the City Clerk is authorized, instructed, and directed to give further or additional notice of the election, in

[Page 4]

the time, form and manner as required by law, and to publish a synopsis of the measure as required by law.

SECTION 9. The City Clerk is hereby directed to submit complete certified copies of this Resolution to the Merced County Registrar of Voters Office and to the Merced County Board of Supervisors by August 7th, 2026.

SECTION 10. The Mayor shall sign and the City Clerk shall attest to the adoption of this Resolution.
