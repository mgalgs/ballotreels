## Facts and quotes

- Ballot question (resolution p. 3, Section 3, boxed ballot text):
  > City of Merced Vital Services Protection Measure — Shall the measure to maintain local quality of life/ general city services, such as 911 police/ emergency medical/ natural disaster response; responding quickly to neighborhood fires/ retaining firefighters; gang prevention; addressing homelessness; fixing roads/potholes; and keeping parks safe/clean, by establishing a 1¢ sales tax, providing approximately $18,000,000 annually until ended by voters, with public disclosure, independent audits, citizen oversight, and all funds for Merced, be adopted?
- Kind and vote threshold (resolution p. 3, Section 4): "The proposed measure is a general tax, as defined in Article XIIIC of the California Constitution, at a rate of 1% on transactions and uses in the City as defined in the Ordinance, to be collected by the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration until repealed by the voters. The Ordinance shall not take effect unless and until approved by a vote of at least a majority of voters voting on the question at the election."
- Council vote needed to place it (resolution p. 2, Section 2): Revenue and Taxation Code §7285.9 allows the tax "subject to approval by a two-thirds vote of all of the members of the City Council and by a majority of the qualified voters of the City voting in an election on the issue." Section 4: "The City Council, by at least 2/3 vote, hereby approves the proposed City of Merced Vital Services Protection Ordinance."
- Election called (resolution p. 2, Section 3): "By Resolution No. 2026-32, adopted on June 15, 2026, the City Council has previously called and given notice of the General Municipal Election to be held in the City on November 3, 2026."
- Full text: resolution Section 4 says "the full text to be published in the November 3, 2026 General Municipal Election Voters Information Guide is SECTION 1 of Exhibit 'A'." Exhibit A is in the same PDF (resolution ends on p. 5 with an unsigned Mayor/Clerk block and the City Attorney's approval as to form dated 7-29-2026; Exhibit A follows on pp. 6–12, not transcribed).
- Agenda item (Aug 3, 2026 agenda, K.1): "Approving Resolution 2026-52, a Resolution of the City Council of the City of Merced, California, Approving an Ordinance and Ordering that a Measure be Submitted to the Voters of the City at the General Municipal Election to be held on November 3, 2026, to Consider a Local 1¢ (1%) General Transactions and Use Tax … B. Approving the form and ballot question".
- Adoption: NOT confirmed. Legistar records no action for 26-641, and Aug 3 minutes are not posted (event minutes status "Draft"). CalTax (finder) lists the measure as on the ballot.
