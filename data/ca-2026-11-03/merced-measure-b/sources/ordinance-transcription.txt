ORDINANCE NO._________

AN ORDINANCE ADOPTED BY THE VOTERS OF THE CITY OF MERCED IMPOSING A 1% TRANSACTIONS AND USE TAX TO BE ADMINISTERED BY THE CALIFORNIA DEPARTMENT OF TAX AND FEE ADMINISTRATION

THE VOTERS OF THE CITY OF MERCED DO HEREBY ORDAIN AS FOLLOWS:

Section 1. Chapter 3.39 is hereby added to Title 3 of the Merced Municipal Code to read as follows:

"Chapter 3.39 - TRANSACTIONS AND USE TAX

3.39.010. SHORT TITLE. This ordinance shall be known as the City of Merced Vital Services Protection Ordinance. The City of Merced hereinafter shall be called "City." This ordinance shall be applicable in the incorporated territory of the City.

3.39.020. OPERATIVE DATE. "Operative Date" means the first day of the first calendar quarter commencing more than 110 days after the adoption of this ordinance. For purposes of Revenue and Taxation Code section 7265, this ordinance shall be considered adopted on the date of the election in which it is approved by the qualified voters of the City.

3.39.030. PURPOSE. This ordinance is adopted to achieve the following, among other purposes, and directs that the provisions hereof be interpreted in order to accomplish those purposes:

A. To impose a retail transactions and use tax in accordance with the provisions of Part 1.6 (commencing with Section 7251) of Division 2 of the Revenue and Taxation Code and Section 7285.9 of Part 1.7 of Division 2 which authorizes the City to adopt this tax ordinance which shall be operative if a majority of the electors voting on the measure vote to approve the imposition of the tax at an election called for that purpose.

B. To adopt a retail transaction and use tax ordinance that incorporates provisions identical to those of the Sales and Use Tax Law of the State of California insofar as those provisions are not inconsistent with the requirements and limitations contained in Part 1.6 of Division 2 of the Revenue and Taxation Code.

3.39.050. TRANSACTIONS TAX RATE. For the privilege of selling tangible personal property at retail, a tax is hereby imposed upon all retailers in the incorporated territory of the City at the rate of 1% of the gross receipts of any retailer from the sale of all tangible personal property sold at retail in said territory on and after the operative date of this ordinance.

[...]

3.39.070. USE TAX RATE. An excise tax is hereby imposed on the storage, use or other consumption in the City of tangible personal property purchased from any retailer on and after the operative date of this ordinance for storage, use or other consumption in said territory at the rate of 1% of the sales price of the property. The sales price shall include delivery charges when such charges are subject to state sales or use tax regardless of the place to which delivery is made.

Section 2. SEVERABILITY. If any provision of this ordinance or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the ordinance and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.

Section 3. AMENDMENTS. Pursuant to Elections Code Section 9217, this ordinance may be amended by the City Council without a vote of the people, except that, as required by Article XIII C of the California Constitution, voter approval is required for any amendment that would increase the rate of any tax levied pursuant to this Ordinance.

Section 4. EFFECTIVE DATE AND EXPIRATION. This ordinance relates to the levying and collecting of the City's transactions and use taxes and shall take effect immediately upon approval by the voters and shall remain in effect until repealed by the voters of the City of Merced.

APPROVED for placement on the ballot by at least a 2/3 vote of the Merced City Council at a meeting held on August 3, 2026, pursuant to Government Code Section 53724 and Revenue and Taxation Code Section 7285.9, and

PASSED AND ADOPTED by the voters of the City of Merced, State of California, at the General Municipal Election held on November 3, 2026.
