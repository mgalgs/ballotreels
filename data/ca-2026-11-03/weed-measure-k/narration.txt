Measure K asks the voters of this city whether to add a 1.5 percent sales tax for general city services.
The City Council placed Measure K on the ballot in July 2026.
The city says the combined sales tax rate is 7.5 percent today, and would be 9 percent if Measure K passes.
The tax would be 1.5 percent of taxable sales in the city, on top of existing state and local sales taxes.
Purchases not subject to sales tax, such as prescription drugs and most food, would not be taxed.
It is a general tax: the money would go into the general fund, for any lawful city purpose the council decides.
The tax has no end date, and would stay until amended or repealed by the voters.
Measure K needs a majority of the vote to pass.
The ballot question estimates that Measure K would raise about $1,700,000 a year.

No argument for Measure K appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
A yes vote on Measure K imposes the 1.5 percent sales tax.
A no vote on Measure K does not impose the tax.
