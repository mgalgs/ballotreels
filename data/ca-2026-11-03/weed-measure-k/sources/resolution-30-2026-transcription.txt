RESOLUTION NO. 30-2026

A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF WEED PLACING A MEASURE REGARDING A TRANSACTIONS AND USE TAX ON THE BALLOT FOR THE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION CALLED FOR NOVEMBER 3, 2026

WHEREAS, the measure to be submitted to the voters, if approved by a majority (50% + 1) thereof, would impose a retail transactions and use tax at the rate of 1.5%, proceeds of which may be used for general revenue purposes; and

Page 2:

SECTION 2. The City Council hereby submits the Ordinance to the voters at the Election, and it orders the following question to appear on the ballot for the Election:

Shall a measure maintaining 911 emergency services, fire protection and wildfire prevention, emergency medical response; 24-hour police patrol/protection, building safety and inspection; streets, roads, sidewalks and City infrastructure; snow removal, and other general City services, long-term financial stability, by establishing a 1.5% sales tax providing approximately $1,700,000 annually until repealed by voters, subject to regular audits, be adopted?    YES [ ]    NO [ ]

This question requires the approval of a majority of qualified electors casing votes.

SECTION 3. The City Council hereby approves the Ordinance, the form thereof, and its submission to the voters of the City at the November 3, 2026 election, as required by Revenue and Taxation Code Section 7285.9. [...] The Ordinance specifies that the rate of the transactions tax shall be increased by one and one half percent (1.5%) of the gross receipts of any retailer from the sale of all tangible personal property sold at retail in the City; and it specifies that the rate of the use tax shall be increased by one and one half percent (1.5%) of the sales price of tangible personal property stored, used, or otherwise consumed in the City. The Ordinance has no expiration date, meaning that the tax shall remain in effect until repealed by the voters in the future.

The State Board of Equalization shall collect the tax from retailers subject to the tax and remit the funds to the City.
