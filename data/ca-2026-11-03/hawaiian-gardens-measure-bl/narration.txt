Measure BL asks the voters of this city whether to update and simplify the tax that businesses pay for a city license.
The City Council voted unanimously in July to place Measure BL on the ballot, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
Today, the tax uses flat rates, employee counts or gross receipts across more than 30 kinds of business, a structure last updated in 1993.
Contractors would pay a flat $175 a year.
Most other businesses would pay $50 a year, plus a set amount for each $1,000 of gross receipts above $30,000.
Qualifying nonprofit groups, utilities, emergency aid services and licensed card clubs would stay exempt.
The new rates would start on January 1, 2027, and last until voters repeal them.
The ballot question says the tax would generate about $563,810, and the impartial analysis estimates $450,000 a year.
Supporters say the measure replaces 34 confusing categories with simple rates, cutting red tape for small businesses, and all the money stays local.
The official county list of measures for this election says that no argument against Measure BL was submitted, by any group or person.
A yes vote approves the changes to the business license tax.
A no vote does not approve the changes to the business license tax.
