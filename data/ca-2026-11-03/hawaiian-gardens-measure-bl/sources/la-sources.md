## Ballot question and supporters/opponents (verbatim, from the county's final measures list)

> Shall the measure to help fund general City services, such as public safety, street operations and maintenance, and parks and recreation services, by updating and simplifying the City's business license tax to levy a maximum annual $50 flat rate plus $0.95 per $1,000 of gross receipts above $30,000 for all businesses other than contractors, and a $175 flat rate for contractors, generating approximately $563,810 until ended by voters, all funds being local, be adopted?
>
> Supporters: Victor Farfan; Maria Teresa Del Rio
> Opponents: None submitted.

Vote threshold: Majority Vote 50%+1 (county's final measures list).
