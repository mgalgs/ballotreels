ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE BL

Vote YES to Modernize Hawaiian Gardens Business License Taxes

Our city's business license structure hasn't been updated since 1993. Voting YES replaces 34 confusing categories with a streamlined, single gross receipts and flat contractor rate—cutting red tape for small businesses.

This update generates an estimated $400,000 annually in locally controlled revenue to maintain vital municipal services:

- Public Safety & Emergency Response

- Youth Recreation & After-School Programs

- Senior Programs & Services

All funds stay 100% in Hawaiian Gardens to protect our community's quality of life.

Vote YES for efficiency, public safety, and vibrant community programs.

VICTOR FARFAN
City Council Member
