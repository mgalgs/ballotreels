Measure S asks the voters of this county whether to add a three-eighths of 1 percent sales tax for 30 years, for hospitals and fire protection.
Measure S is a citizen initiative, placed on the ballot by petition, and needs a majority of the vote to pass.
In the first year, half the money would go to local hospitals, 45 percent to fire agencies, and 5 percent to wildfire prevention nonprofits.
After the first year, the money would first repay hospital debt for updated facilities, with the rest to nonprofits and fire agencies.
Only hospitals in the county with at least 100 licensed acute care beds on January 1, 2025, qualify.
A citizens' oversight committee and yearly independent audits would check the spending.
The ballot question says the tax would provide about $13 million a year.
Supporters say Measure S keeps aging emergency rooms open and protects rapid emergency response, and visitors also pay their share.
Opponents say Measure S is a sales tax increase taxpayers cannot afford, and a sweetheart deal for one private hospital.
A yes vote authorizes the sales tax.
A no vote is a vote against the sales tax.
