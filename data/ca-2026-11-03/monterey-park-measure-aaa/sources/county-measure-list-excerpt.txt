CITY OF MONTEREY PARK – (2)
VOTE PASSAGE – Majority Vote 50%+1
AAA     Shall the ordinance maintaining City of Monterey Park's services like fire, police, 911
        response; keeping public areas safe/clean; helping prevent property crimes/human
        trafficking in Monterey Park; repairing streets/potholes; for other city services; by
        increasing the transient occupancy tax paid only by hotel/motel guests from 13% to 14%,
        and short-term rental guests, from 13% to 16%, providing approximately $600,000
        annually until ended by voters; requiring audits, spending disclosure, local control over all
        funds, be adopted? Supporters: Henry Lo, Mayor; Jose Sanchez, Pro Tem; Thomas
        Wong, Council Member; Vinh Ngo, Council Member; Elizabeth Yang, Council Member
        Opponents: None submitted.
