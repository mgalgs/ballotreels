Measure AAA asks the voters of this city whether to raise the tax on hotel, motel and short-term rental stays.
Today, guests who stay 30 days or less at a hotel, motel or short-term rental pay a 13 percent tax, added to their bill.
The tax is estimated to bring in $4.5 million in the 2026-2027 budget year.
The City Council placed Measure AAA on the ballot.
Measure AAA would raise the tax to 14 percent for hotel and motel guests.
For short-term rental guests, the tax would rise to 16 percent.
The city could use the added money for any general government purpose.
Measure AAA needs a majority yes vote to pass.
The city attorney's impartial analysis says public documents estimate about $600,000 more a year.
The official county list of measures, which names who filed each argument, says no argument against Measure AAA was submitted.
Supporters say the measure funds city services without raising taxes on residents or businesses, since only visitors pay it.
A yes vote raises the tax to 14 percent for hotels and 16 percent for short-term rentals.
A no vote keeps the tax at 13 percent for both.
