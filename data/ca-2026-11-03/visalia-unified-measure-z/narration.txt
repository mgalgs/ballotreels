Measure Z asks the voters of this school district whether to approve $158 million in school bonds.
The district would sell the bonds and repay them from a tax on property in the district.
Measure Z needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money could remove asbestos and lead pipes, and repair or replace roofs, plumbing, and heating, cooling and electrical systems.
Bond money could also replace portable classrooms with permanent ones, and upgrade labs and career technical facilities.
Bond money could not pay teacher or administrator salaries, and a citizens' oversight committee and yearly audits would check the spending.
The district's tax statement estimates an average of $39.72 per $100,000 of assessed value each year, through 2051-2052.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $313 million.
