BACKGROUND SUMMARY 

Over the past three years, the Board has conducted a comprehensive long-range facilities planning process, including adoption of the Long-Range Facilities Master Plan, facility assessments, enrollment and capacity analyses, funding reviews, and community engagement which included feedback and perspectives from hundreds of teachers, staff, parents, and community.

 

Staff has worked with the District's financial and legal advisors to develop a proposed Project List that addresses the local school priorities identified by our community, financing plan, and Resolution to place a General Obligation Bond measure on the November 3, 2026, General Election ballot. The proposed Project List addresses the District's highest-priority facility needs, including modernization of aging schools, replacement of deteriorated building systems and portable classrooms, improvements to student safety and security, technology infrastructure, and other capital improvements identified through the facility assessment process. The proposed projects also position the District to maximize future State School Facility Program matching funds.

 

If approved by voters, bond proceeds will be used solely for the identified projects and will be subject to all Proposition 39 accountability requirements, including independent audits and oversight by an independent Citizens' Bond Oversight Committee.

FINANCIAL IMPACT 

The Resolution would call a bond election asking District voters to authorize the issuance of $158,000,000, levying 4 cents per $100 assessed value, raising approximately $12 million annually while bonds are outstanding.
