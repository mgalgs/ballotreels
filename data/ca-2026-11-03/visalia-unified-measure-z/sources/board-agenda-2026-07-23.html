     During the course of normal business, there may be times that VUSD staff will need to revise the Board meeting packet Item Enclosures, and will do so contemporaneously with their availability prior to the meeting. Those revisions will be posted on the day of the Board meeting at approximately 12 p.m. At times, there may be a need for additional revised Item Enclosures after 12 p.m. Revised Board meeting packet enclosures will be identified on the Board meeting agenda as "Revised Item Enclosure".

   In compliance with the Americans with Disabilities Act, for those requiring special assistance to access the Board meeting room, to access written documents being discussed at the Board meeting, or to otherwise participate at Board meetings, please contact the Superintendent's Office at (559) 931-8102. Notification at least 48 hours prior to the meeting will enable the District to ensure accessibility to the Board meeting and to provide any required accommodations, auxiliary aids, or services.

     Any documents distributed to Board members regarding open session agenda items shall be made available for public inspection in the Superintendent's Office, located at 5000 W. Cypress Avenue, Visalia, CA 93277 during normal business hours.

     Members of the public may address the Board on any agenda item when the item comes to the Board for consideration. At regular meetings of the Board, members of the public may also address the Board regarding non-agenda items that are nonetheless within the Board's jurisdiction during the general public comment portion of the agenda. Pursuant to Board Bylaw 9323, the Board will limit individual comments to no more than 3 minutes and individual topics to 20 minutes.

    It is recommended that members of the public who wish to participate in any part of public comment fill out a "public comment" form (green form) at the Board meeting before the item comes up on the agenda. Those completing the form will be called up by agenda item to provide their comments in the order they are received, should time allow. The District reserves the right to not hear comments, or portions of comments, that violate meeting guidelines. Spanish translation is available.

     The link to the Regular Board meeting live webcast may be found on the VUSD website at: live at https://www.vusd.org/boe/board-meetings.  Each School Board Meeting live webcast will begin at the scheduled start time on the date of the meeting and end at the conclusion of the meeting.  The auditory recordings are used to assist in compiling the meeting.

     During the meeting, many of the Board members will be speaking on various subjects. It should be noted that each Board member expresses only his/her opinion and not the opinion of the entire Board.

Notice: Education Code Section 49073.2(a) Notwithstanding Section 49073. a local educational agency shall not include the director information or the personal information of a pupil or of a parent or guardian of a pupil has provided a written request to the secretary or clerk of the governing body to exclude his or her personal information or the name of his or her minor child from the minutes of a meeting of the governing body.
