## Facts and quoted passages

- **Ballot question** — from the district's Community Presentation, slide headed "Full Ballot Measure" (p. 16). This is a district publication, **not** the board resolution or the county ballot; the resolution itself could not be fetched (see below). Treat as provisional until checked against the resolution or the county guide:
  > To provide safe, modern schools by removing asbestos/lead pipes; continuing to provide safe drinking water; fixing deteriorating roofs, plumbing, HVAC, electrical systems; and repairing/ upgrading aging classrooms, labs, career technology and facilities to support student achievement and college/career readiness; Shall Visalia Unified School District's measure authorizing $158,000,000 in bonds at legal rates be adopted, levying 4 cents per $100 assessed value ($12,000,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding, with independent audits/oversight and all money locally controlled?
- **Measure letter:** the presentation and https://engage.vusd.org/measure-z say "Measure Z" ("Voters will see Measure Z on the last page of the November 3, 2026 ballot."). This is a district statement, not the county's letter assignment.
- **Placed on the ballot / vote:** minutes of July 23, 2026 (p. 11): "On a motion by Trustee Naylor, seconded by Trustee Oto, the Board voted to approve the Long Range Facilities Plan Resolution to Order an Election for a School Bond Measure on November 3, 2026. Motion passed unanimously. Vote: Ayes: Belt, DeJonge, Gaebe, Gamoian, Naylor, Oto, Villegas Noes: None".
- **Amount and rate:** item cover, Financial Impact: "The Resolution would call a bond election asking District voters to authorize the issuance of $158,000,000, levying 4 cents per $100 assessed value, raising approximately $12 million annually while bonds are outstanding." Minutes (p. 10): "ask homeowners to pay four cents per $100 of assessed home value, or $120 annually for a $300,000 home".
- **Accountability:** item cover: "If approved by voters, bond proceeds will be used solely for the identified projects and will be subject to all Proposition 39 accountability requirements, including independent audits and oversight by an independent Citizens' Bond Oversight Committee."
- **Vote threshold:** not stated in the files I have. Prop 39 school bonds need 55%; confirm in the resolution or county guide before stating it.
- **Who votes:** voters of the Visalia Unified School District (minutes: "voters would decide on November 3, 2026"). Boundary: the district's Census unified school district area.
- **Project list:** one-pager: "Proposed projects reflect cost-inflation and does not take into consideration state-matching bonds. That is to say, this project list reflects what is possible with $158M."
