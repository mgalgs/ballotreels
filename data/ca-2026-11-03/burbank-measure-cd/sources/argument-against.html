ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE CD

VOTE NO ON MEASURE CD.

Burbank's longstanding at-large election system gives EACH VOTER A VOICE IN EVERY COUNCIL SEAT. Measure CD, the By-District Council Elections Measure, would end that.

Today, Burbank voters vote every two years for two or three open Council seats. Under Measure CD, voters would VOTE ONLY ONCE EVERY FOUR YEARS FOR ONLY ONE COUNCIL MEMBER from their district. Residents would no longer have a vote on the other four Council members who make decisions that affect the entire city.

As your City Council, we know Burbank's challenges do not start and stop at district lines. Public safety, traffic, housing, parks, infrastructure, and responsible budgeting need citywide solutions.

Our current system requires every Council member to earn support from voters throughout Burbank and remain accountable to residents in every neighborhood. By-district elections could narrow that accountability and encourage Council members to prioritize individual districts over the needs of the whole city.

Measure CD would replace citywide representation with five separate districts and REDUCE YOUR VOTE FROM FIVE COUNCIL SEATS TO ONE.

Join your City Council in protecting every voter's citywide voice. VOTE NO ON MEASURE CD.
