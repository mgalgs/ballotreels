## Ballot question and supporters/opponents (verbatim, from the county's final measures list)

> BY-DISTRICT COUNCIL ELECTIONS MEASURE CD: Shall the Ordinance be adopted adding Chapter 3 to Title 2 of the Municipal Code changing the City's electoral system for City Council elections from at-large to by-district elections in the districts reflected in Map 130?
>
> Supporters: None submitted.
> Opponents: Burbank City Council.

Vote threshold: Majority Vote 50%+1 (county's final measures list).

- **Tax rate statement**: not applicable — Measure CD is an electoral-system change, not a tax or bond measure.
