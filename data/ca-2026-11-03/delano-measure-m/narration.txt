Measure M asks the voters of this city whether an elected mayor should serve a four-year term.
The City Council put three mayor questions on this ballot: K on electing the mayor, and L and M on a two-year or a four-year term.
Four of the five council members voted to place them, in June 2025.
Today, the council chooses one of its own members to preside as mayor each year.
The four-year ordinance would give an elected mayor a four-year term, and each of four council members a four-year term.
That ordinance is written to follow a majority vote both for an elected mayor and for a four-year term.
The first mayoral election would be in November 2028.
Each of the three measures needs a majority vote to pass.
The county elections page for this election does not include a cost estimate for the measure.

No argument against Measure M appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
A yes vote on Measure M answers yes to a four-year term for the mayor.
A no vote on Measure M answers no to a four-year term for the mayor.
