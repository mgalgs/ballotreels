City of Clayton Measure N asks the voters of Clayton whether to extend and raise the special tax for trails and landscaping.
Since 2007, the tax has paid for trails, roadway landscaping and weed clearing on open space, and it ends in June 2027.
Measure N would extend the tax to June 2037 and raise it from $312.84 to $354.45 a year for each home parcel, or each acre of other property.
After that, the City Council could raise the rate with inflation, up to 3 percent a year.
Measure N needs two-thirds of the votes cast to pass.
The impartial analysis does not include an estimate of how much the tax would raise.
Supporters say the general fund cannot adequately pay for landscaping and trails, and the tax supports fire mitigation in open spaces.
Opponents say the tax would rise with inflation, and that the ballot question lists benefits, not costs, so it is not neutral.
A yes vote approves the extended parcel tax at the new rate.
A no vote does not approve the parcel tax.
