## Ballot question

From `notice-of-measures.pdf`, p. 1:

> CITYWIDE LANDSCAPE MAINTENANCE EXTENSION AND ALTERATION OF RATE OF EXISTING SPECIAL TAX. Shall the measure to fund on-going trail system operations and maintenance, roadway landscaping, open space weed abatement, and related services of the Clayton Community Facility District No. 2007-1 (Citywide Landscape Maintenance) by extending the special tax for ten years and amending its rate and methodology to $354.45/year per residential parcel and $354.45/year per non-residential acre, including annual inflationary adjustments, subject to citizen’s committee oversight, be approved?

## Who votes on it

- Notice, p. 1: "NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN to the qualified electors of the City of Clayton … that in accordance with law, an election will be held on Tuesday, the 3rd day of November, 2026"
- Impartial analysis: "Measure N requires the approval of two-thirds (2/3) of Clayton voters voting on the Measure to pass."

## Impartial analysis — key statements (signed /s/ Malathy Subramanian; text extracted from the .doc with `strings`, so a few quote marks are missing)

- "the CFD currently levies the tax at the annual rate of $312.84 per residential parcel and $312.84 per acre, or fraction thereof, for non-residential parcels. The tax started collection on July 1, 2007 and will expire on June 30, 2027, unless extended by voters."
- "After conducting a noticed public hearing on July 7, 2026, the City Council unanimously voted to place Measure N on the November 3, 2026 General Municipal Election ballot. It proposes to extend the CFD parcel tax for ten (10) years, with a new expiration date of June 30, 2037. It also proposes to increase the annual parcel tax rate, starting July 1, 2027, to $354.45 per residential parcel and $354.45 per acre, or fraction thereof, for non-residential parcels."
- "The Measure allows the City Council to increase the tax rate, starting July 1, 2028 and in each fiscal year thereafter by the applicable Consumer Price Index, with a 3% annual cap"
- "Measure N does not change the purposes of the parcel tax."
