City of Lake Elsinore Measure T asks voters in the city whether the City Treasurer should be appointed instead of elected.
The City Treasurer has custody of the public funds of the city.
Today, voters elect the treasurer every four years.
State law lets the voters of each city choose an elected or an appointed treasurer.
The City Council placed Measure T on the ballot, and it needs a majority of the vote to pass.
The office would become appointed when the term of the current treasurer ends.
The city manager would appoint the treasurer: the manager, the finance director, or another government finance professional.
The Riverside County guide does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
No argument against Measure T was filed, so the county guide prints only the argument from the measure's supporters.
Supporters say the job has no minimum qualifications today, and Measure T would ensure a qualified treasurer and save money.
A yes vote will change the City Treasurer to an appointed position when the current term ends.
A no vote will leave the City Treasurer an elected office, with the next election in 2028.
