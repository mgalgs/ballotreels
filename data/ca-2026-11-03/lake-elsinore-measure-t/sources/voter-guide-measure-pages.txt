                                                    MEASURE “T” - CITY OF LAKE ELSINORE
                                                               ORDINANCE NO. 2026-
         AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF LAKE ELSINORE, CALIFORNIA, AMENDING LAKE ELSINORE
         MUNICIPAL CODE CHAPTER 2.42 (CITY TREASURER) OF TITLE 2 (ADMINISTRATION AND PERSONNEL) TO MAKE THE
         OFFICE OF CITY TREASURER AN APPOINTIVE OFFICE
      Whereas, Government Code Section 36508 and Elections Code Section 9222 authorize the City Council to submit to the electors the
question of whether the elective City Treasurer shall be an appointive office and, if a majority of voters voting on the measure approve it, the office
shall become appointive; and

    Whereas, an appointive City Treasurer will be required to possess relevant experience in government finance and a professional financial
background, training, skills, and experience necessary to fulfill the important responsibilities of the office of City Treasurer; and

     Whereas, on May 12, 2026, pursuant to State law, the City Council adopted Resolution No. 2026-XX, submitting this ordinance to the voters
at the General Municipal Election on Tuesday. November 3, 2026; and

     Whereas, all other legal prerequisites to the adoption of this ordinance have occurred.

     NOW THEREFORE, THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF LAKE ELSINORE DOES ORDAIN AS FOLLOWS:
    Section 1.      Subject to the approval of a majority of the voters of the City of Lake Elsinore at the scheduled election so designated by the City
Council in a separate resolution placing this proposal on the ballot for such election, Chapter 2.42 of Title 2 of the Lake Elsinore Municipal Code is
hereby amended to read as follows:
      2.42.010      Office established.
      As of the November 5, 2028 Municipal Election or the earlier vacancy of the elected City Treasurer position, the office of the City Treasurer
      shall cease to be elected as provided by law and shall become an appointive office. Pursuant to California Government Code Section 36510,
      the City Treasurer shall be appointed by the City Manager. The City Manager may self-appoint to serve as City Treasurer, appoint the Director
      of Finance, or appoint another professional with relevant experience in government finance and a professional financial background, training,
      skills, and experience necessary to serve as City Treasurer.
      2.42.020      Elective office.
      The City Treasurer shall be elected from the City at large at a general municipal election.
      2.42.030      Term of office.
      The City Treasurer shall serve for a term of four years and until his successor is elected and qualified.
      2.42.050      Eligibility.
      No person shall be eligible to hold the office of City Treasurer unless such person, at the time of assuming office, shall be a qualified elector
      of the City and shall have been a resident of the City for at least the minimum period provided by law next preceding the date of such person’s
      election or appointment.
      2.42.060      Vacancies.
      A vacancy in the office of City Treasurer shall be filled by appointment by the City Council. Such appointee shall hold office until the next
      succeeding general municipal election and until his successor is elected and qualified.
      2.42.070      Failure to fill vacancy.
      In the event that the City Council shall fail to fill a vacancy by appointment within 30 days after such office has become vacant, the City Council
      shall forthwith call a special election to fill such vacancy; provided, that if such vacancy occurs within 120 days prior to a general municipal
      election, such vacancy shall be filled at the general municipal election.
      2.42.080      Powers and duties.
      The City Treasurer shall be the custodian of all public funds belonging to or under the control of the City or of any office, department or agency of
      the City and shall perform such duties as may be prescribed from time to time by the City Council consistent with this chapter and applicable law.
      2.42.020      Bond Requirements
      Pursuant to California Government Code Section 36518, the City Treasurer shall furnish a corporate surety bond to be approved by the
      City Council in the same amount as that required of the Director of Finance and City Clerk, and it shall be conditioned upon the satisfactory

      Section 2. Validity. If any portion of this Ordinance is declared invalid by a court of law or other legal body with applicable authority, the
invalidity shall not affect or prohibit the force and effect of any other provision or application of the Ordinance that is not deemed invalid. The voters
of the City hereby declare that they would have circulated for qualification and/or voted for the adoption of this section, and each portion thereof,
regardless of the fact that any portion of the initiative may be subsequently deemed invalid.
     Section 3.       Supersedence. To the fullest extent allowed by law, the provisions of this Ordinance shall prevail over, and supersede, all other
provisions of the Municipal Code and any ordinances, resolutions, or administrative policies of the City of Lake Elsinore which are in conflict with any
provision of this Ordinance.
     Section 4.     Amendments. This section shall not be repealed or amended except by a measure approved by a majority of the electors voting
on the issue at a General Municipal Election, or at a special election called for that purpose.
    Section 5.        CEQA. This Ordinance is exempt from the California Environmental Quality Act in accordance with Section 15061(b) (3) there is
no possibility that the activity in question may have a significant effect on the environment.
      Section 6.      Severability. If any provision of this Ordinance or its application is held invalid by a court of competent jurisdiction, such invalidity
shall not affect other provisions, sections, or applications of the Ordinance which can be given effect without the invalid provision or application, and
to this end, each phrase, section, sentence, or word is declared to be severable.
     Section 7.     Effective Date. This Ordinance shall take effect only if approved by a majority of the eligible voters of the City of Lake Elsinore
voting at a General Municipal election to be held on November 3, 2026, and shall take effect ten (10) days after the City Council has certified the
results of the General Municipal election by resolution.
     Section 8.     Attestation. The Mayor is hereby authorized to attest to the adoption of this Ordinance by the People voting thereon on November
3, 2026, by signing where indicated below.
    Section 9.       Certification. The City Clerk shall certify to the passage of this Ordinance and shall cause a synopsis of the same to be published
according to law.
     PASSED, APPROVED, AND ADOPTED by the People of the City of Lake Elsinore on the 3rd day of November 2026.
