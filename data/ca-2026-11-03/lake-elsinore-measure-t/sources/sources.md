## Quoted passages

- Question (p.22): "Shall the office of City Treasurer be appointive?"
- Current law (p.45): "The City Treasurer in the City of Lake Elsinore is currently elected every 4 years. As an elected office, the only requirements for the City Treasurer are to be at least 18 years of age, a registered voter, and a resident of the City."
- Who placed it (p.45): "Lake Elsinore City Council placed Measure T on the ballot to give voters the choice to officially change from an elected City Treasurer to an appointed one."
- Effect (p.45): "A “YES” vote on Measure T will change the office of the City Treasurer to an appointed position beginning on the expiration of the existing City Treasurer’s term."
- Effect (p.45): "A “NO” vote on Measure T will leave the City Treasurer as an elected office with the next City Treasurer election in 2028."
- Full text (p.43): "Whereas, Government Code Section 36508 and Elections Code Section 9222 authorize the City Council to submit to the electors the question of whether the elective City Treasurer shall be an appointive office"
