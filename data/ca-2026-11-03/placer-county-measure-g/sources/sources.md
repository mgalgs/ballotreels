## Key facts, quoted

- Who votes / threshold: "This measure was placed on the ballot by the Board of Supervisors of the County of Placer through adoption of Ordinance 6381-B. Approval by a majority of voters voting on this measure is required for its adoption." (countywide — "If a majority of the electorate of Placer County approves the above question")
- Current law: "Under the current Placer County Charter, a vacancy on the Board of Supervisors must be filled by a unanimous vote of the remaining members. If the remaining Board members fail to fill the vacancy within 30 days of its occurrence, the authority to appoint a replacement transfers to the Governor of California."
- Change 1: "the period during which the remaining members of the Board of Supervisors can fill the vacancy by unanimous vote would double from 30 days to 60 days."
- Change 2: "If the vacancy occurs 130 days or more before the next scheduled statewide direct primary election, the Board's appointee will only serve temporarily. The vacancy will be submitted to the voters at that upcoming primary election, allowing the voters to elect a representative to serve out the remaining unexpired term."
- Yes/No: "A "YES" vote on Measure G is a vote to amend the Placer County Charter to extend the period for appointment by the Board of Supervisors from 30 days to 60 days and to establish procedures for an election to fill the remainder of an unexpired term under specified circumstances." / "A "NO" vote on Measure G is a vote to leave the current Charter language unchanged, including the 30-day period for appointment by the Board of Supervisors and the existing procedure for appointment by the Governor if the Board does not fill the vacancy within that period."
