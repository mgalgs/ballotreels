                      PROPOSAL TO AMEND PLACER COUNTY CHARTER SECTION 206

Shall the measure amending Article II, Section 206 of the Placer County Charter to extend the deadline for filling
a vacancy on the Board of Supervisors from 30 days to 60 days, providing additional time for the Board to make
an appointment before the Governor fills the vacancy, and requiring vacancies occurring 130 days or more before
a statewide direct primary election be filled temporarily by appointment and placed on the ballot at that election,
be adopted?

                                       PROPOSED CHARTER TEXT REVISION
If a majority of the electorate of Placer County approves
the above question, then Article II, Section 206 of the
Placer County Charter shall be amended as follows:
Sec. 206 Vacancies.
If a vacancy occurs on the Board of Supervisors, it shall be
filled by the unanimous vote of the remaining members,
and if they shall fail to make such appointment within 30
60 days of the occurrence of any such vacancy, then such
vacancy shall be filled by the Governor; provided that any
appointment under this section shall be of a person who
for at least 30 days prior to his their appointment has been
a resident of the supervisorial district in which the vacancy
exists. If the appointment is made one hundred thirty (130)
days or more before the next statewide direct primary
election, the vacancy shall be filled temporarily by
appointment, but the office shall be placed on the ballot at
the statewide direct primary election, and the person
elected shall serve the remainder of the unexpired term. If
the appointment is made less than one hundred thirty (130)
days prior to the next statewide direct primary election, the
appointee shall serve the remainder of the unexpired term.
