Measure G asks the voters of this county whether to change how an empty seat on the Board of Supervisors is filled.
Today, the other supervisors must agree unanimously on a replacement within 30 days.
If they do not, the Governor of California appoints one.
The Board of Supervisors placed Measure G on the ballot, and it needs a majority to pass.
Measure G would give the board 60 days instead of 30.
If the seat opens at least 130 days before the next statewide primary, the appointee would serve only until voters elect someone at that primary.
If it opens later, the appointee would serve the rest of the term.
The Measure G voter guide pages do not include a cost estimate for the measure.
No argument for Measure G was submitted.
No argument against Measure G was submitted.
A yes vote gives the board 60 days and sets up an election for the rest of a term in some cases.
A no vote keeps the 30-day period and the appointment by the Governor.
