Measure C asks the voters of this city whether to amend the city charter to update its rules for public works contracts.
Today the charter requires council approval for public works contracts over $1,000, and awards every contract to the lowest responsible bidder.
Those rules were enacted in 1952 and have not changed since.
Measure C would raise the threshold for council approval to $250,000, and the city manager would approve smaller contracts.
Most contracts would still go to the lowest responsive and responsible bidder, with exceptions for best value contracting such as design-build.
In design-build, one contract goes to a single design and construction team, chosen as the most qualified bidder.
The impartial analysis projects savings in staff time, as smaller contracts would no longer go to the council, and possibly from best value contracting.
Supporters say the outdated charter allows only one contracting method, which often delays complex projects.
No argument against Measure C was filed with the Registrar of Voters for this county.
A yes vote replaces the charter's public works rules with the updated rules.
A no vote keeps the existing rules without amendment.
