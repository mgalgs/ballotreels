City of Rancho Mirage Measure R asks voters in the city whether to add a one-cent sales tax.
The City Council voted unanimously to place Measure R on the ballot, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
The 1 percent tax would be added to current sales taxes and would stay until voters repeal it.
Measure R is a general tax, so its money would go into the general fund of the city for any lawful purpose.
An independent firm would audit the tax money and its spending every year.
The City Attorney's impartial analysis says the city estimates the tax could raise about $7.5 million a year.
No argument against Measure R was filed, and none is in the Riverside County Voter Information Guide.
Supporters say costs outpace revenues, and tourists and visitors pay an estimated 75 percent of sales tax.
A yes vote is a vote in favor of adopting the 1 percent sales tax.
A no vote is a vote against adopting the 1 percent sales tax.
