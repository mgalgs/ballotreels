## Quoted passages

- Question (p.22): "RANCHO MIRAGE PUBLIC SAFETY, ROADS, CITY SERVICES MEASURE. Shall the measure maintaining Rancho Mirage’s long-term financial stability, City services such as 9-1-1 emergency medical/fire response, fire protection, Sheriff’s patrols, crime prevention/response programs; keeping public areas safe/clean; maintaining streets, roads, parks, libraries, fire stations; retaining/attracting local businesses, other services, by establishing a 1¢ sales tax generating approximately $7,500,000 (seven point five million dollars) annually until ended by voters, requiring public spending disclosure, funds for Rancho Mirage only, be adopted?"
- Who placed it (p.30): "On July 16, 2026, the City Council of the City of Rancho Mirage unanimously voted to place Measure R (the “Measure”) on the ballot for the November 3, 2026, General Election, in compliance with Article XIII C (Proposition 218) of the California Constitution."
- Threshold / effect (p.30): "If approved by a majority of the voters, the Measure would adopt an ordinance adding Chapter 3.22 to Title 3 of the Rancho Mirage Municipal Code establishing a one percent (1%) transactions and use tax (commonly referred to as a “sales tax”) within the City of Rancho Mirage (the “City”) and would remain in effect indefinitely unless and until ended by the voters of Rancho Mirage at a future election."
- Analysis (p.30): "The City estimates that the Measure could generate approximately $7,500,000 annually in additional revenue."
- Analysis (p.30): "This tax would be a “general tax”, meaning that revenues generated from the tax would be deposited into the City’s General Fund and may be used for any lawful governmental purpose as determined by the City."
