      CITY OF PARAMOUNT – (1)
      VOTE PASSAGE – 2/3 Votes (66.6% of Votes)
P      City of Paramount Public Safety, Environment, Streets and Business Improvement
       Measure Without taxing residential property owners or renters, shall the ordinance to
       fund services for Paramount 911 response/public safety; reducing crime; responding to
       business burglaries; addressing street racing; monitoring/improving water/air quality;
       repairing streets/potholes; supporting businesses by annually levying between 8¢ and
       13¢ per square foot on industrial/commercial properties only, with annual adjustments up
       to 3%, raising approximately $4,000,000 annually until ended by voters, requiring audits,
       spending disclosure, local control, be adopted? Supporters: Brenda Olmos, Mayor;
       Robert Luna, Sheriff; Coalition for Clean Air; J. Perez, Business Owner; MUSA
       nonprofit Opponents: None submitted.
