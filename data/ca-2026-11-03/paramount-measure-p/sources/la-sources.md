## County measure list entry

From `county-measure-list-excerpt.txt` (LA County RR/CC final measures list):

> P City of Paramount Public Safety, Environment, Streets and Business Improvement Measure Without taxing residential property owners or renters, shall the ordinance to fund services for Paramount 911 response/public safety; reducing crime; responding to business burglaries; addressing street racing; monitoring/improving water/air quality; repairing streets/potholes; supporting businesses by annually levying between 8¢ and 13¢ per square foot on industrial/commercial properties only, with annual adjustments up to 3%, raising approximately $4,000,000 annually until ended by voters, requiring audits, spending disclosure, local control, be adopted?
>
> Supporters: Brenda Olmos, Mayor; Robert Luna, Sheriff; Coalition for Clean Air; J. Perez, Business Owner; MUSA nonprofit
> Opponents: None submitted.

Vote threshold: **2/3** (confirmed by the public-hearing notice's citation of Govt. Code §50077 — this is a special tax dedicated to specific purposes, not a general-fund tax, which resolves the "unusual 2/3 threshold" otherwise implied by the county list's framing as "Without taxing residential property...").

## What's present vs. missing

- **Impartial analysis**: present (`impartial-analysis.pdf`).
- **Argument in favor**: **missing — not found/not fetched.** The county list names five supporters, so an argument was presumably filed (the City Clerk's deadline for arguments was August 14, 2026, 5:00pm, per `elections-page.html`), but it was not found on the elections page or the property-parcel-tax page.
- **Argument against**: **missing — not filed.** The RR/CC list states "Opponents: None submitted," so no argument against exists to find.
- **Rebuttal to argument for / rebuttal to argument against**: **not applicable/not found.** With no opposing argument filed, there is nothing to rebut; no rebuttal document was located for the favor side either.
- **Full text**: **missing — not found.** The public-hearing notice states "a copy of the complete resolution and proposed ordinance are available in the City Clerk's office," but no web-published copy of the adopted resolution or ordinance was found as a standalone document.
- **Tax rate statement / fiscal**: present (`fiscal.pdf`), though it is an informational mailer rather than a formal tax-rate-statement document; it covers the rate structure, phase-in schedule, and revenue estimate.
