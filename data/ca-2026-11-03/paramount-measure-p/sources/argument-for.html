ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE P

Vote YES on Measure P the City of Paramount Public Safety, Environment, Streets and Business Improvement Measure to fund our streets, cops and critical community services. Paramount faces rising costs to maintain essential services and repair local streets damaged by heavy truck traffic.

Currently, many commercial/industrial properties do not generate local sales taxes, despite causing wear and tear on our streets and driving calls for police service for trespassing and illegal dumping. This measure provides a pathway for these properties to pay their fair share.

This measure DOES NOT TAX RENTERS OR HOMEOWNERS. It applies only to commercial, industrial, and vacant business land. Because the tax is based on property size, a typical small business property will pay less than $100 annually, while massive corporate distribution centers will pay their appropriate share. To ensure a predictable tax for business properties, the tax includes a gradual three-year phase-in and a 3% inflation cap to prevent runaway increases.

This measure will help fund Paramount's top priorities:

- Protecting Our Environment: Funding advanced air quality monitoring and rapid illegal dumping cleanups to ensure environmental justice and monitor potential industrial polluters.
- Supporting Small Businesses: Providing critical local grants for security cameras, ADA compliance, and facade improvements for our small business owners in the city.
- Supporting Public Safety: Maintaining rapid 911 emergency response times and deploying two dedicated security patrol cars into commercial and industrial areas.
- Promoting Economic Growth: Improving commercial areas and providing public safety patrols to encourage more businesses to invest in Paramount.

Without taxing residents, this measure requires strict accountability and ensures every dollar raised will be spent locally in Paramount, investing in our community.

Vote YES on Measure P to ensure our industrial and commercial businesses keep Paramount safe, clean, and strong for future generations!
