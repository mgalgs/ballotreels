Laguna Beach Unified School District Measure A asks the district's voters whether to approve $83 million in school bonds.
The district would borrow the money and repay it from a tax on property in the district.
Measure A needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would upgrade aging classrooms, science labs and career technology, and fix roofs, plumbing and electrical systems.
The district's tax statement estimates an average tax of $8.68 per $100,000 of assessed value, and at most $8.85, through 2056-2057.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $157 million.
No argument against Measure A was submitted for the Orange County voter guide.
Supporters say Measure A would repair and upgrade Laguna schools without raising taxes.
A yes vote is in favor of letting the district sell the bonds.
A no vote is against letting the district sell the bonds.
