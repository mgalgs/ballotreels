Measure E asks the voters of this city whether the mayor should serve four-year terms instead of two-year terms.
Today, voters elect the mayor directly to a two-year term, and elect four council members to four-year terms.
Measure E would make the mayor's term four years, starting with the mayor elected in November 2028.
The mayor elected in 2026 would still serve two years, and council terms and elections would not change.
The City Council placed Measure E on the ballot, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
The county voter guide does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Opponents say voters chose two-year terms in 2004 by nearly 70 percent, and have not asked the council for a change.
Supporters say matching the four-year terms of council members frees the mayor from constant campaigning to focus on long-term goals.
A yes vote approves changing the mayor's term from two years to four, starting with the 2028 election.
A no vote keeps the mayor's two-year term.
