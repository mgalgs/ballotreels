## Quoted facts

- **Who votes:** Pismo Beach voters (general municipal election, November 3, 2026).
- **Ballot question** (R-2026-043): "CITY OF PISMO BEACH MEASURE _____ FOUR-YEAR MAYORAL TERMS MEASURE. Shall the measure changing the Mayor's term of office to four years, beginning with the term elected on November 7, 2028, be adopted?"
- **Impartial analysis** (VIG): "This measure is an ordinance that changes the term of office for the Mayor of Pismo Beach from two years to four years. Currently, the Pismo Beach City Council is composed of a Mayor, who is directly elected to a two-year term, and four Council Members, who are elected at-large to four-year terms." … "The Mayor elected at the November 3, 2026 election would serve a two year term as provided under existing law." … "This measure was placed on the ballot by the Pismo Beach City Council."
- **Arguments:** argument in favor (signed "s/ Ed Waage, Mayor" and "s/ Mary Ann Reiss, Mayor Pro Tem"), argument against, rebuttal to the argument in favor; the guide states "THERE WAS NO REBUTTAL TO THE ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE E-26 FILED".
