## Key facts, quoted

- **Ballot question:** "Shall the measure amending the policy establishing the City's Urban Limit Line to permit the extension of sewer, water, and recycled water facilities to serve existing commercial facilities located on parcels with a boundary that falls wholly or partially within one mile of the Urban Limit Line and in operation on or prior to November 3, 2026, be adopted?"
- **Threshold:** "To pass, a majority (50% + 1) of the votes cast by City of Woodland voters must approve the measure."
- **Placed by:** "On June 16, 2026, the City Council voted to place Measure "Z" on the November 3, 2026 general municipal election ballot". (The analysis says the election "will be held and consolidated with the Statewide Primary Election of the same date" — as printed; November 3 is the statewide general election.)
- **Effect:** "If approved by a majority of Woodland voters, Measure would amend Policy 2.A.1 of Goal 2.A of the City of Woodland's 2035 General Plan Land Use, Community Design and Historic Preservation Element to authorize the City to extend potable water, recycled water and sewer services from the City to existing commercial properties that are located within one mile of the City's Urban Limit Line."
- **History:** "In 2006, a majority of Woodland voters passed Measure "A", which established a permanent Urban Limit Line (ULL) to circumscribe urban development and protect agricultural lands surrounding the City. The City is prohibited from extending public services and facilities to properties located beyond the ULL."
- **Limits:** "The City would not be required to extend service to any qualifying commercial property, and any extension of service would be subject to City Council approval and all other applicable regulatory approvals. This exception would not apply to new commercial development, any residential development (new or existing), or any other non-commercial facilities (new or existing)."
- **Yes/No:** "A "yes" vote on Measure "Z" will allow the City to extend City sewer, potable water and services to existing commercial facilities within one mile of the ULL on a case by case basis, subject to City Council approval and all applicable regulatory approvals." / "A "no" vote on Measure "Z" will keep the language of Policy 2.A.1 in its current form as originally enacted by Measure "A", and will prohibit any extension of City services or facilities beyond the ULL."
- **Analysis signed:** "/s/ Ethan Walsh, City Attorney".
- **Argument in favor signers:** Blair Voelz, Farmer; Karen Rosenkilde Bayne, Educator; Kris Kristensen, Retired City Manager; Aniek Pflager, Woodland Business Leader; Erik Wilson, Agricultural Supplier. (Claims such as "$2 Million in Direct Community Benefits" are the authors'.)
- **Argument against:** "No arguments were filed AGAINST Measure Z by the 5:00 p.m. July 24, 2026 deadline." Rebuttals: "If only an Argument IN FAVOR OF is filed, there is no Rebuttal period."
