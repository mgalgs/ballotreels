Measure Z asks the voters of this city whether to extend sewer and water service to some businesses just outside the urban limit line.
In 2006, voters set a permanent urban limit line, to contain growth and protect the farmland around the city.
Today, the city may not extend public services to properties beyond that line.
Businesses on parcels within one mile of the line could be served, if they were operating by election day.
The city would not have to serve them, and each extension would need city council approval.
The voter guide for this county does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
No argument against Measure Z was filed by the July deadline, so only an argument in favor appears.
Supporters say Measure Z protects groundwater and water quality, and keeps the line's ban on new homes intact.
A yes vote lets the city extend service to those businesses, case by case.
A no vote keeps the ban on city services beyond the line.
