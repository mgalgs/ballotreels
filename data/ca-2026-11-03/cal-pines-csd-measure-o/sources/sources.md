## Facts with quotes

- **Ballot question (resolution p. 2):** "California Pines Community Services District Services Special Parcel Tax Measure — Shall the ordinance be adopted to provide additional annual revenue for community services district services, including power, water, and wastewater systems, collecting solid waste, and plowing and maintaining roads, by increasing the annual special parcel tax for the California Pines Community Services District from $66 to $116 on each nonexempt parcel in Lake Units 1B, 3, and 4, and the Mobilehome Park, generating approximately $98,000 a year, until ended by voters?"
- **Who pays (analysis):** "The services funded by the tax are provided to Lake Units 1B, 3, and 4, and the Mobile Home Park of the District (collectively, the "CSD Area")."
- **Services:** "maintaining power, water, and wastewater systems, solid waste collection, vector control, and road plowing and maintenance."
- **Start / revenue:** "If approved, the increased special parcel tax of One Hundred Sixteen dollars ($116.00) per fiscal year for each nonexempt parcel would take effect commencing fiscal year 2027-28. The tax would be collected by the Modoc County Tax Collector and generate approximately Ninety-Eight Thousand dollars ($98,000.00) per year."
- **Accountability:** "an annual report by the chief fiscal officer and an independent compliance audit commencing December 31, 2027."
- **Vote needed:** "Approval of Measure O requires at least two-thirds of the votes cast, as required by Article XIIIC, Section 2(d) of the California Constitution."
- **Duration:** "The authority to levy the special parcel tax shall remain in effect unless a later ordinance approved by two-thirds of District voters either repeals the tax or establishes a termination date."
- **Hearing:** resolution §3: "The Board held a duly-noticed public hearing on this proposed measure on June 24, 2026."
