Measure O asks the voters of this community services district whether to raise a yearly parcel tax for district services in part of the district.
Today, each parcel the tax applies to, in three of the district's lake units and its mobile home park, pays $66 a year.
Measure O needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
Measure O would raise the tax to $116 a year per parcel, starting in the 2027-2028 fiscal year.
The money would go to a special fund for services such as power, water and wastewater systems, trash collection, vector control and road plowing.
The tax would continue unless a later two-thirds vote of the district's voters ends it.
The impartial analysis estimates the tax would bring in about $98,000 a year.
No argument for Measure O appears among the official documents that the county has posted online for this ballot measure.
No argument against Measure O appears among the official documents that the county has posted online for this ballot measure.
A yes vote is in favor of raising the parcel tax from $66 to $116 a year.
A no vote is against the increase, keeping the existing tax of $66 a year.
