ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE CC

VOTE YES ON MEASURE CC.

Measure CC, The Charter Amendment Measure, would provide Burbank with GREATER LOCAL CONTROL OVER THE FUTURE OF CITY COUNCIL ELECTIONS.

Burbank's local election process is established in the City Charter. Measure CC would provide the City with greater flexibility to consider future election methods that best meet the community's needs.

State and federal election laws continue to evolve. Currently, Burbank's options for responding to new legal requirements are limited by the City Charter. Measure CC would allow the City to adapt to changes in the law and help REDUCE THE RISK OF FUTURE COSTLY LITIGATION.

This measure DOES NOT MANDATE A SPECIFIC ELECTION SYSTEM. It gives the City greater flexibility to consider future election methods based on Burbank's circumstances, community needs, and applicable laws.

As your City Council, we believe decisions about Burbank's election system should be made locally, with careful consideration of what works best for our residents and neighborhoods.

Measure CC is a practical way to strengthen local control, prepare for future legal changes and PROTECT TAXPAYER DOLLARS.

Join your City Council in supporting local decision-making and protecting Burbank's future. VOTE YES ON MEASURE CC.
