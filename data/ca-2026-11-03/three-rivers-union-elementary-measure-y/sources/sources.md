## Facts and quoted passages

- **Ballot question** (Exhibit B, p. 11; same words in Exhibit C, p. 12, and in Exhibit A's "Summary Statement", p. 8, which ends with a typo "be adopted??"):
  > To offset inflation and limited state funding, shall the Three Rivers Union Elementary School District's measure to levy a $120 parcel tax for eight years (raising approximately $276,000 annually) to fund school operations and maintenance costs including utilities; facility/grounds upkeep/safety improvements, and a community garden, with no money used for teacher or administrative salaries and setting aside at least 25% for improvements to and maintenance of Three Rivers School's upper athletic fields for community use during non-school hours, be adopted?
- **Who votes:** Exhibit C: "an election will be held on November 3, 2026, at which election the following measure shall be submitted to the qualified electors of the District". Section 3: "to call an election within the boundaries of the District".
- **Vote needed and rate:** Section 5: "Subject to approval by two-thirds of the voters, the parcel tax shall be in the amount of $120 per tax year, assessed against each parcel located wholly or partly in the District, beginning as of July 1, 2027, and continuing for a term of eight years."
- **Revenue:** recital: "a $120 parcel tax would generate approximately $276,000 annually for eight years".
- **Uses** (Exhibit A, "Purpose of the Measure", p. 8): "Costs related to the District's facilities and grounds maintenance, utilities and operational costs, costs of safety improvements and infrastructure repairs, the purchase of instructional materials and educational technology, the development of a community garden, and costs related to custodial, maintenance, and operational support expenses." Fields: "Field grading, irrigation, drainage, turf or surface improvements, fencing, accessibility improvements, lighting, and improvements to spectator areas."
- **Set-aside and salary bar** (Exhibit A, p. 7): "At least twenty-five percent (25%) of the proposed parcel tax collections from this measure will be set aside for improvements to and maintenance of Three Rivers School's upper athletic fields ... No proceeds from this parcel tax measure will be used for teacher or administrative salaries."
- **Exemptions** (Exhibit A, p. 9): "All property that is otherwise exempt from property taxes levied in any year will also be exempt from the parcel tax in such year." (No senior exemption is stated.)
- **Sunset** (Exhibit A, p. 7): "The proposed parcel tax is temporary, will automatically expire in eight years, and cannot be renewed without voter approval."
- **Accountability** (Exhibit A, p. 9): separate fund; "No later than January 1 of each year while the parcel tax is in effect, the chief fiscal officer of the District shall prepare and file with the Board of Trustees of the District a report detailing the amount of funds collected and expended".
- **District school:** recital: "It educates approximately 100 students from transitional kindergarten through eighth grade at its single elementary school campus in the heart of Three Rivers."
- **Argument by the Board authorized** (Section 11): officers may "prepare and submit for inclusion in the voter information pamphlet an argument in favor of passage of the Measure, and any rebuttal argument".
