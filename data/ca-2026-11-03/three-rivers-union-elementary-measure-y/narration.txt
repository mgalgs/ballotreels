Measure Y asks the voters of this school district whether to levy a parcel tax of $120 a parcel each year for eight years.
The tax would begin in July 2027, and would end after eight years unless voters approve it again.
Measure Y needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The money could pay for school operations and upkeep, including utilities, grounds, safety repairs and a community garden.
At least 25 percent would be set aside for the school's upper athletic fields.
None of the money could pay teacher or administrator salaries, and the district would report each year on what was collected and spent.
Property that is exempt from property taxes would also be exempt from this tax.
The ballot question says the tax would raise about $276,000 a year.

No argument against Measure Y appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
A yes vote levies the $120 parcel tax for eight years.
A no vote does not levy the tax.
