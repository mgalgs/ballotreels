Measure V asks the voters of this city whether the city treasurer should be appointed instead of elected.
Today, the voters of the city elect the city treasurer.
State law requires the city to have a treasurer, and lets its voters decide whether the office is elected or appointed.
The City Council would appoint a treasurer when the current term ends, or sooner if the office falls vacant.
An appointed treasurer would serve at the pleasure of the council, and would not have to live or vote in the city.
The duties, such as keeping and investing city funds, would not change.
The documents the county posts for Measure V do not include a cost estimate for the measure.

A no vote keeps the city treasurer an elected office.
