ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE CS

Please vote NO on the $77,000,000 Measure CS.

This measure is the second huge school bond in the past decade. It would cost Claremont property owners north of $150,000,000 in principal and interest over the next 30 years. It is the wrong answer for our school district that has been hemorrhaging local students for decades. Even now, the District serves only about 6,000 students and expects enrollment to decrease by 125 students annually for the foreseeable future. Only about 4,500 of the kids come from Claremont.

Why spend money at school facilities that may well have to be downsized or sold off? We are not told.

This Spring, Oakmont Elementary School combined fifth and sixth graders in a classroom taught by a single teacher because there were not enough students. A quarter of the district students are transfers from other districts, and those districts increasingly disapprove such transfers. Claremont is unaffordable for young families.

The project list contains NO specifics. It appears to have been prepared by a generic Artificial Intelligence engine. Voting for this bond would mean you are paying thousands of dollars over 30 years for a pig in a poke.

The District is UNABLE to manage your tax dollars prudently. Some earlier bond money still has not been reimbursed by the State. The District had to use money from the sale of the La Puerta Intermediate School site to finish the bond project list due to inadequate budgeting and management. Concerning La Puerta, the District lost $19,000,000 of potential revenue in a terrible deal, selling that property (against expert recommendation) to a developer for $12.5 million who turned around and sold it five months later for $31.2 million.

In these uncertain economic times, you can save your family hundreds of dollars annually by voting NO.
