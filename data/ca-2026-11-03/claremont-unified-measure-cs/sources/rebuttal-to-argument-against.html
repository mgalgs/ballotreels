REBUTTAL TO ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE CS

Measure CS will support high-quality public education that benefits all residents. Families benefit when they send their children to high-functioning schools with outstanding teachers and safe, well-cared-for facilities. Everyone benefits from the positive influence of our excellent schools on our community.

Most CUSD campuses are over 60 years old and in significant need of infrastructure repair and renewal. To minimize financial impact on property owners, the District selected projects that fit within the same cost structure as the past Measure Y bond, which will be paid in full before this bond is funded. Homeowners will not experience any increase in their annual property tax rate as a result of this bond, with the average Claremont homeowner paying $175/year.

Not passing this bond will make CUSD ineligible for anticipated matching State grants—missing an important opportunity to leverage local funding to access State funds, thus shifting more of the burden to our local community.

Measure G bond funds were effectively managed. The final project is nearing completion using proceeds from the La Puerta school site sale. A community oversight committee will also ensure proper management of these funds.

Let's continue to invest in the future viability of our schools and vote YES on Measure CS.

RICHARD FASS
Vice President, Retired, Pomona College

AMANDA HOLLIS-BRUSKY
President, League of Women Voters, Mt. Baldy Area
