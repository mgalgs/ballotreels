## Ballot question (verbatim, from the county measure list)

> CLAREMONT UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT CLASSROOM SAFETY, HEALTH AND REPAIR MEASURE. To repair/upgrade aging classrooms, heating/air conditioning/electrical/plumbing systems, improve safety/security, fix leaky/deteriorating roofs, repair/construct/equip classroom sites/facilities without increasing current taxes, shall the Claremont Unified School District's measure authorizing $77,000,000 of bonds at legal rates, levying approximately $32/$100,000 of assessed value ($5,000,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding, be adopted, requiring audits/independent oversight, no money for inappropriate administrator salaries, all funds staying local?

Supporters: John Faranda; Sue Keith; John Neiuber; Brett O'Connor; Phalana Tiller
Opponents: James M. Belna; Donna Lowe; Gary Lowe; Joyce H. Sauter; Deborah Barker-Benfield

Vote threshold: 55% of the votes cast (Prop 39 school bond).
