ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE CS

Throughout its history, Claremont has placed a high value on education, as it forms the foundation of our civic life. Our public schools are central to this: they prepare informed voters, educate a superior workforce, expand business opportunity, and promote strong property values.

This measure does not increase the current school bond tax rate. Homeowners would continue to pay the same tax currently paid under Measure Y, which will be fully paid off before the new bond takes effect. A YES vote strengthens our community by providing students with facilities that are safe, modern, and promote learning. This will provide $77 million for critical repairs and improvements at every school site, including:

- Installation of school safety and campus security systems, including emergency communications systems, remote door access, and creating single points of entry at elementary schools during the school day.
- Renovate and upgrade classrooms/learning spaces.
- Renovate failing restrooms.
- Improve or replace aging plumbing systems.
- Add drinking fountains/bottle filling stations.
- Modernize electrical systems to support technology needs.
- Replace/refurbish deteriorating and leaky roofs.
- Strengthen the commitment to sustainability by replacing outdated and inefficient heating, ventilation, and air-conditioning systems, lowering utility bills.
- Update athletic facilities, including the CHS turf field.

The District has involved the community in decision-making, conducting extensive outreach through 36 community meetings and an online survey with 834 respondents. The survey results can be reviewed at bit.ly/45qbPGY

State law specifies accountability measures, with a Citizen’s Oversight Committee and periodic independent financial audits.

Vote YES to join with homeowners, educators, employers and community leaders who have formed Claremont RISE (Renew Infrastructure, Sustain Excellence) to support our schools.
