REBUTTAL TO ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE CS

The proponents make only three identifiable (and erroneous) arguments for Measure CS. First, they imply that this measure is cost-free. Second, they claim extensive community involvement. And third, they tout the feckless "Citizens' Oversight Committee".

Measure CS was cynically reverse-engineered to match the tax rate on a thirty-year-old expiring bond. The fact is, when that bond expires your taxes will DECREASE, a welcome bit of good news in these troubled economic times. However this measure would effectively extend that year 2000 bond to the year 2060! Your cost is thousands and thousands of dollars more over the next three decades.

Proponents claim extensive outreach based on the number of meetings district administration attended. These were more one-way lectures than true back and forth. They claim 834 survey respondents to be "extensive". However, that number represents only a miniscule 3% of the 23,000 voters in the District. Further, the survey exercise was NON-SCIENTIFIC: respondents selected themselves and some 20% of those "surveyed" were employed by the District. The "survey" results cannot be taken seriously.

The Superintendent alone selects the "Citizens' Oversight Committee". For the previous Claremont Unified School District bond the committee met infrequently, asked no substantive questions, and was completely inconsequential. It lasted only about three years.

Unlike some other taxes, Measure CS has NO RELIEF or exemptions for seniors or those with fixed incomes. It will be too costly for all to pay for the District wish list with your tax money.

Visit AffordableClaremontSchools.com for more information. Please vote NO on Measure CS.
