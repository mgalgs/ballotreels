Measure CS asks the voters of this school district whether to approve $77 million in school bonds.
The district would sell the bonds and repay them from a yearly tax on property in the district.
Measure CS needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The bond money could pay only for the school projects the measure lists.
Those include repairing classrooms, heating, air conditioning, plumbing and electrical systems, improving safety and security, and replacing leaky roofs.
No tax rate statement was gathered; the ballot question itself estimates a tax of about $32 per $100,000 of assessed value.
The ballot question puts the yearly total at about $5 million while the bonds are outstanding, and says current taxes would not increase.
Supporters filed an argument for Measure CS; its text is not among the documents gathered here.
Opponents filed an argument against Measure CS; its text is not among the documents gathered here.
A yes vote approves the $77 million in school bonds.
A no vote rejects the $77 million in school bonds.
