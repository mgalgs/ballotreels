## Facts and the passages that establish them

### Ballot question

S1 PDF p. 74:

> MEASURE J
> To repair, modernize, and construct classrooms and career technology facilities for college/career readiness in agricultural science, chemistry, math, technology, and skilled trades; fix deteriorating facilities, roofs, plumbing/electrical systems; and improve school safety/security systems; shall Julian Union High School District's measure authorizing $9,000,000 in bonds at legal rates be adopted, levying approximately $30 per $100,000 assessed value (averaging $650,000) annually, while bonds are outstanding, with citizen oversight, independent audits, and all money locally-controlled?
> Bonds – Yes / Bonds – No

### Vote required

S1 PDF p. 74: "This measure requires 55% of those voting on the measure."

### Who votes on it

S3, Resolution 25-26-11, Section 2 (PDF p. 3): "The Board hereby orders an election and submits to the electors of the District the question of whether general obligation bonds of the District shall be issued and sold in a maximum principal amount of $9,000,000". Section 3: "such bond election shall be held solely within the boundaries of the District." Section 4: "The purpose of the election shall be for the voters in the District to vote on the Bond Measure".

S1 PDF p. 75 (full text): "By approval of this measure by at least 55% of the registered voters voting on the measure, the District will be authorized to issue and sell bonds of up to $9,000,000".

In short: the electors (registered voters) of the Julian Union High School District.

### Who placed it on the ballot

S1 PDF p. 74: "The Board of Education for the Julian Union High School District ("District") placed this measure on the ballot."

S3 PDF p. 7: "The foregoing Resolution was adopted by the Board of Education of the Julian Union High School District of San Diego County ... by the following vote, on June 18, 2026." AYES 5, NOES 0, ABSENT 0, ABSTAIN 0 (handwritten). Recorded as printed: the cover letter enclosing the resolution is dated June 15, 2026, three days before that adoption date.

### Impartial analysis

S1 PDF p. 74, "COUNTY COUNSEL IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS". Key statements:

> If approved by 55% of the votes cast, this measure will authorize the District to issue and sell general obligation bonds in an amount not to exceed $9,000,000.

> The District estimates the average tax rate would be $30 for every $100,000 of assessed valuation, and the highest tax rate would be approximately $30 for every $100,000 of assessed valuation. The tax is anticipated to be collected through fiscal year 2054-2055.

> The estimated total debt service, including principal and interest, required to be repaid if the bonds are issued and sold is approximately $18,215,253.

### Tax rate statement

S1 PDF p. 74, "TAX RATE STATEMENT", signed "Yvonne Fleet, Superintendent, Julian Union High School District" (no "/s/" printed):

> 1. ... is $30 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. It is currently expected that the tax will be collected until fiscal year 2054-2055.
> 2. ... is $30 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. This rate is projected to apply beginning in 2027-2028.
> 3. ... total debt service ... will be approximately $18,215,253.
