## Quoted facts

- **Who votes:** Supplemental Notice of Election: "the following measures will be voted upon by the qualified electors of the County of Santa Cruz ... County of Santa Cruz (Majority Voter Approval Required)".
- **Ballot label** (resolution, p. 2): "SANTA CRUZ COUNTY COASTAL PROTECTION MEASURE TO REGULATE OIL AND GAS DRILLING AND SEABED MINING FACILITIES: Measure A, passed in 1986, requires voters to approve land use changes for onshore facilities that support offshore oil and gas development. To protect our economy, environment, and quality of life, shall voters enact an ordinance to update Measure A to also require voter approval for land use changes related to deep-sea mining facilities and strengthen the law against legal challenges?" The Notice of Election prints the same question without the all-caps title.
- **Impartial analysis (County Counsel Jason M. Heath):**
  - "Santa Cruz County Code Chapter 16.55 ("Chapter 16.55") currently requires voter approval for any permit or similar authorization to develop, construct, or install any onshore facility supporting offshore oil and gas facilities ... The Chapter was adopted by voters as Measure "A" at the June 3, 1986, election".
  - "Measure "D" was placed on the ballot by the Santa Cruz County Board of Supervisors."
  - "First, it seeks to change the title and substance of the Chapter to include onshore facilities related to deep-sea mining activities. Second, it adds a new "Purpose" section ... Third, the Measure expands the "Findings" section".
  - "It also simplifies the Chapter's provision related to voter approval for onshore support facilities, creates an exemption for certain projects, and makes other miscellaneous changes to the Chapter."
  - "These changes to Chapter 16.55 would be permanent unless amended by voters in the future."
  - Yes: "a vote to amend Santa Cruz County Code Chapter 16.55 to modify its provisions as set forth above". No: "a vote to keep the language of Santa Cruz County Code Chapter 16.55 the same as it is now".
- **Fiscal impact (Laura Bowers, Auditor-Controller):** "The measure is not expected to have a significant fiscal impact on the County of Santa Cruz. The measure does not impose a tax or fee, authorize County expenditures, or require the County to establish a new program or add County staffing." "Any fiscal effects would depend on future proposals subject to the measure and cannot be reasonably estimated at this time."
- **Argument for** (transcribed from the scanned image): "Vote YES on Measure D to give Santa Cruz County voters the ultimate say on whether onshore industrial facilities for offshore oil, gas, and seabed mining will be allowed." Signers: Mariah Roberts (County Park Friends), Esabella Bonner (Black Surf Santa Cruz), Kristen Brown (Santa Cruz Area Chamber of Commerce), Yesenia Jimenez (Watsonville Wetlands Watch), Dan Haifley (former Executive Director, Save Our Shores).
