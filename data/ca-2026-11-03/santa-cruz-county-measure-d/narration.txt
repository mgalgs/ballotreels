County of Santa Cruz Measure D asks county voters whether to require a public vote on land use changes for deep-sea mining facilities.
Since 1986, a county law voters passed as Measure A has required voter approval for onshore facilities that support offshore oil and gas.
Measure D would extend that requirement to onshore facilities for deep-sea mining.
Measure D would also add a purpose, findings and definitions to the law, and exempt certain projects.
The county auditor-controller says Measure D is not expected to have a significant fiscal impact on the county.
Supporters say Measure D gives county voters the ultimate say on onshore facilities for offshore oil, gas and seabed mining.
No argument against Measure D appears among the measure documents that Santa Cruz County Elections has published for the November election.
A yes vote adds deep-sea mining facilities to the land use changes that need voter approval.
A no vote keeps the county law the same as it is now.
