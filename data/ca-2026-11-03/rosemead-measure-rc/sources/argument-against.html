ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE RC

NO ON MEASURE RC

The proposed term limits, 5-4 years terms, are not term limits. Nowhere in the United States are there 20-year term limits, 8 years or 12 years. Yes a 20 year term limit is more than a generation.

A city prosecutor can only prosecute misdemeanors. Most misdemeanors are punishable with a dollar fine and community service hours. The City Attorney is a partner in a large law firm with many prosecutors who could serve in this capacity if necessary.

Giving preference to veterans in contract bids for City projects is honorable. However, we should also consider other qualified businesses, such as companies owned by Rosemead residents, women, minorities, or people with disabilities who are not veterans. State law requires that contracts be awarded to the lowest responsible and most qualified bidder. Therefore, the City should ensure that its bidding process remains fair, competitive, and consistent with state law.

All of these items can be addressed through city ordinances. It is our taxpayer money.

We have been a General Law city since we became a city since 1959.

For these reasons, please vote No on Measure RC

BARBARA MURPHY
