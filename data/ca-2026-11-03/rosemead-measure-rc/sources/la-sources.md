## Ballot question and supporters/opponents (verbatim, from the county's final measures list)

> Shall the City of Rosemead Charter be adopted to establish Rosemead as a charter city to have more authority over municipal affairs, imposing term limits on City Councilmembers, establishing the position of City prosecutor, permitting use of City funds for economic development, continuing following California law for limiting compensation for Councilmembers, and giving the City Council authority to adopt an ordinance giving veterans and disabled veterans preference in awarding contracts?
>
> Supporters: Mayor Sandra Armenta; Councilman Steven Ly
>
> Opponents: Barbara Murphy; Nancy Eng; Frances Chavez

Vote threshold: Majority Vote 50%+1. Letter designation requested "RC", backup "R" (Resolution 2026-20, §3).

## What's present and what's missing

- **Full charter text**: present (`resolution.pdf`, Exhibit A, pp. 7-11 of 11).
- **Resolution placing the measure on the ballot**: present (Resolution No. 2026-20).
- **Impartial analysis**: missing — only the notice of its public-examination period was found (`notice-of-examination.pdf`), not the analysis itself.
- **Arguments for/against and rebuttal**: missing — same notice-only situation; this is the one measure in this batch with a confirmed opponent slate (Barbara Murphy, Nancy Eng, Frances Chavez) but no filed argument text was located.
- **Fiscal analysis/staff report** on the charter: not located.
- This would be the city's first charter if adopted; a 2008 charter attempt (then "Measure CC") reportedly failed, per press reporting — not independently confirmed from an official document, not staged.
