ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE RC

You deserve a safe, affordable, and quality Rosemead community. This Charter gives us the tools to deliver for you and your family. Vote YES on Measure RC!

Right now, Rosemead can only enforce its own laws with the County District Attorney's permission and because of this many crimes are not prosecuted. The Charter lets us create our own City Prosecutor, so Rosemead's own can prosecute criminals that violate our city codes and laws.

The Charter also gives our city new tools to grow our local economy. Right now, Sacramento limits how the City can support creating quality businesses. Under the Charter, the City can put more resources to create more quality markets, restaurants, cafes, and shops. We can add more tools that help create jobs, expand our dining and retail options, beautify our commercial corridor, and strengthen our tax base to pay for city services.

The Charter protects your hard-earned taxpayer money but making sure that we approve the best and most affordable services that are in the best interests of our community. It also allows the City to give veterans and disabled veterans a preference when awarding City contracts, honoring those who served.

This Charter includes real government reform. It imposes term limits on City Councilmembers, ensuring new and fresh ideas continue to serve Rosemead. It also limits compensation for your local elected officials. Most importantly, this Charter is our community's attempt to preserve Rosemead as a safe, affordable, quality place to raise our families.

Please join Mayor Sandra Armenta and your Rosemead City Council in voting YES on Measure RC. Because a YES vote means giving Rosemead the tools to build a safer, stronger, more prosperous Rosemead.
