Measure U asks the voters of this city whether to change the rules for a 2.4-acre site from office uses to senior housing and shops.
Measure U is a citizens' initiative, which qualified with 5,000 valid signatures from voters in the city.
Today the site's plans call for office uses, and it holds office buildings.
A new zoning district would limit the site to apartments mainly for people 55 or older, and businesses suited to senior housing.
Later city rules could not reduce what the initiative allows on the site, except by a vote of the people or by approving an application from the site's owner.
Measure U needs a majority of the vote to pass.
Neither the resolution placing Measure U on the ballot nor the initiative's text includes a cost estimate for the measure.
Supporters say seniors are pushed away from family; Measure U allows over 200 senior homes near transit without raising taxes.
The county elections office says on its page for this measure that no argument against Measure U was filed.
A yes vote adopts the initiative's changes to the plans and zoning for the site.
A no vote does not adopt the changes.
