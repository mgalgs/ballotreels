                        LINCOLN PUBLIC SAFETY/TAXPAYER PROTECTION MEASURE

Shall the measure to prevent cuts to public safety services (911 emergency/medical response, police/fire
protection, qualified police/firefighters); add life-saving equipment for medical emergencies; keep public
areas/streets/parks safe and clean; and other general government uses, by establishing a one-cent sales tax,
providing $6,000,000 annually with all funds spent locally, until ended by voters, with a citizen oversight
committee, and annual audits/public spending disclosure, be adopted?

                                        ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE F
Vote YES on F – Protect taxpayers, local control, and keep Lincoln safe!
YES on F keeps our money LOCAL, to keep Lincoln safe with money that cannot be taken by state bureaucrats.
Did you know? Lincoln usually has only as few as three police officers on patrol at any one time! Without Measure F, the
City risks reducing the number of officers even lower.
YES on F will:
   • Maintain 911 emergency response times
   • Add paramedics and advanced life-saving equipment
   • Prevent cuts to fire and police
   • Retain/attract qualified firefighters and police officers
   • Keep public areas and parks safe, and clean
YES on F will PREVENT CUTS to fire and police protection / response services.
YES on F will keep 911 response times short and maintain residential and wildfire protection.
YES on F will add paramedics and life-saving equipment to the Fire Department for medical emergencies.
YES on F ensures that OUR Lincoln dollars stay local to maintain our quality of life. Services funded with Measure F benefit
Lincoln. Not a penny can be taken by the State. We cannot depend on the County, State or Federal governments to address
Lincoln’s, unique local needs.
YES on F is transparent and accountable. Measure F requires strict accountability and disclosure of all spending, annual
audits, and independent citizen oversight to ensure funds are spent as promised and to hold city officials accountable.
We need YES on F. Lincoln is one of the safest cities in the state, let’s keep it that way!
Measure F is not a tax on homes/property, not applied to rent, utilities, food purchased as groceries, or prescription medicine.
Let’s keep Lincoln a safe, desirable place to live and raise a family — Join a unanimous City Council, community leaders,
Police, Firefighters and neighbors in voting YES on F!

Section 5. Codification. Subject to the voter approval, the
City Clerk, in consultation with the City Attorney, is hereby
authorized and directed to codify this ordinance in the

Section 6. Effective Date. Pursuant to California
Constitution Article XIII C, section (2)(b), and Elections
Code sections 9217 and 15400, this ordinance shall take
effect only if approved by a majority of the eligible voters
in the City, and shall be considered as adopted upon the
date that the vote is declared by the legislative body, and
shall go into effect 10 days after that date.
Section 7. Severability. If any section, subsection,
sentence, clause, or phrase of this ordinance is for any
reason held to be invalid or unconstitutional by the decision
of any court of competent jurisdiction, such decision shall
not affect the validity of the remaining portions of this
ordinance. City voters declare that they would have
adopted this ordinance, and each and every section,
subsection, sentence, clause, or phrase not declared
invalid or unconstitutional, without regard to whether any
portion of the ordinance would be subsequently declared
invalid or unconstitutional.
Section 8. Repeal or Amendment. This ordinance shall
not be repealed or amended, except by a measure
approved by a majority of electors voting on the issue at a
General Municipal Election, or at a special election called
for that purpose.
Section 9. Certification. The City Clerk shall certify the
final approval of this ordinance, publish the same as
required by law, and forward a certified copy of the
adopted ordinance to the California Department of Tax and
Fee Administration.
