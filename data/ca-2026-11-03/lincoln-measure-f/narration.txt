Measure F asks the voters of this city whether to add a 1 percent sales tax for public safety and other city services.
Today, the sales tax rate in the city is 7.25 percent.
The City Council placed Measure F on the ballot, and it needs a majority to pass.
Measure F would raise the rate to 8.25 percent.
Measure F would be a general tax: the money would go into the city general fund, for any lawful city purpose.
The City Council proposes using it to prevent cuts to police and fire, add medical emergency equipment, and keep streets and parks safe and clean.
The tax would last until voters repeal it.
The impartial analysis estimates the tax would raise about $6 million a year.
Supporters say the city usually has as few as three officers on patrol, and the state cannot take the money.
No argument against Measure F was submitted, and the Measure F voter guide pages include no argument against the measure.
A yes vote authorizes the 1 percent sales tax.
A no vote does not authorize the 1 percent sales tax.
