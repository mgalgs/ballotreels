City of Lemon Grove Measure E asks voters in the city whether pet stores may offer only birds from shelters and rescue groups.
Today, state law bars pet stores from selling dogs, cats and rabbits unless they come from a shelter, humane society or rescue group.
Measure E reached the ballot by a voter petition, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
Measure E would apply the same rule to birds in Lemon Grove pet stores.
Poultry for farms and backyard flocks would not be covered.
Each bird sold in violation would be a separate infraction, enforceable six months after the measure is adopted.
The San Diego County guide does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
No argument against Measure E appears in the San Diego County Voter Information Guide for this election.
Supporters say Measure E ends commercial bird sales in pet stores and helps rescue birds find homes.
A yes vote is a vote to adopt the ordinance.
A no vote is a vote against adopting the ordinance.
