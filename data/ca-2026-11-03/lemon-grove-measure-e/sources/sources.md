## Facts and the passages that establish them

### Ballot question

S1 PDF p. 80:

> MEASURE E
> Shall the Measure restricting the sale of birds in the City of Lemon Grove, requiring that birds be obtained exclusively from animal shelters, humane societies, or nonprofit rescue organizations and establishing related regulations be adopted

Printed **without** a closing question mark in S1, S2 and the resolution (S3). The City's argument-deadline notice (S7) prints it in quotation marks **with** a question mark.

### Vote required

S1 PDF p. 80: "This measure requires Simple Majority (50%+1) of those voting on the measure." S3 Section 3: "the Bird Initiative shall be deemed approved if it receives a majority of the votes cast on the measure."

### Who votes on it

S3, Section 1:

> That pursuant to Elections Code § 9215, there is called and ordered to be submitted to the qualified voters of the City of Lemon Grove, at the General Municipal Election to be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, the Bird Initiative

In short: the qualified voters of the City of Lemon Grove.

### Who placed it on the ballot

It is a citizen initiative. S1 PDF p. 80 (impartial analysis): "Measure E has been placed on the ballot because the City received a petition signed by the requisite number of voters seeking the adoption of rescue-only pet store standards for birds."

S4 (p. 1-2): "On December 22, 2025, petitioner/proponent Vicky Lynn Snow submitted to the City Clerk/Elections Official a Notice of Intent to Circulate Petition"; the City Clerk certified the petition's sufficiency "based on the signature verification results provided by the San Diego County Registrar of Voters". S3 recitals: the Council received the certificate "at its regular meeting of May 19, 2026" and "has elected to submit the Bird Initiative to the voters" rather than adopt it.

S3, page 3: "PASSED AND ADOPTED on July 21, 2026 ... AYES: Snow, Smith, Mendoza, Heredia, Altamirano; NOES: None; ABSENT: None; ABSTAIN: None". Signed Alysson Snow, Mayor; attested Joel G. Pablo, City Clerk.

### Impartial analysis

S1 PDF p. 80, "CITY ATTORNEY IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE E", signed "Kristen S. Steinke, City Attorney, City of Lemon Grove". Key statements:

> Under California Health and Safety Code section 122354.5, retail pet stores are barred from selling dogs, cats, and rabbits unless sourced from a qualified animal shelter, humane society, or non-profit rescue organization.

> If approved, Measure E would adopt Health and Safety Code section 122354.5 and extend those provisions to the retail sale of birds in the City of Lemon Grove. The Measure becomes effective immediately upon adoption and enforceable six months after its adoption.

> The Measure does not extend to poultry sold for agricultural or backyard flock purposes, including chickens, ducks, geese, or turkeys.

> Violations carry civil penalties (per animal) and violations may be punishable as an infraction.

> A "YES" vote is a vote to adopt the ordinance.
> A "NO" vote is a vote against adopting the ordinance.

The guide also prints, on the same page, an "OFFICIAL TITLE AND BALLOT SUMMARY" with ballot title "An Initiative Measure restricting the sale of birds in the City of Lemon Grove, requiring that birds be obtained exclusively from animal shelters, humane societies, or nonprofit rescue organizations and establishing related regulations."

Recorded as found: the analysis says violations "carry civil penalties (per animal)"; the full text (Sec. 6.10.040) says each bird is "a separate offense" and "Every violation of this chapter is punishable as an infraction", and names no civil penalty.

### Arguments and rebuttals

- **Argument in favor**, S1 PDF p. 81. First line: "Vote YES on Measure E to extend California's pet-store sourcing protections to birds in Lemon Grove." Signers: Kelly Flynn (President, Parrot Education & Adoption Center); Jenna Close (Founder, Moose's Flock Pigeon and Dove Rescue); Teri Crutchfield (Founder/Director, Saving Animals and Healing Hearts); Dr. Gary Weitzman (President/CEO, San Diego Humane Society); Vicky Lynn Snow (Lead Proponent).
- **Argument against**: **none printed.** S1 has no argument against Measure E and no printed "no argument was filed" notice (I searched the full text of S1).
- **Rebuttals**: none printed.

### Full text

S1 PDF pp. 81-82, "FULL TEXT OF MEASURE E / CHAPTER 6.10 — RESCUE-ONLY PET STORE STANDARDS; EXTENSION TO BIRDS", sections 6.10.001 through 6.10.060. It matches the text filed December 22, 2025 (S5) as far as I compared (the opening sections).

Key provision, Sec. 6.10.020(A): "no pet store shall display, sell, deliver, offer for sale, barter, auction, give away, or otherwise transfer any bird within the City of Lemon Grove unless the bird was obtained from an animal shelter, animal control agency, humane society, or nonprofit rescue organization, and the pet store receives no direct or indirect compensation or financial benefit for such adoption."

### Tax rate statement or fiscal analysis

None printed. Not a tax measure.
