Measure DD asks the voters of this fire district whether to continue a parcel tax for paramedic services for four more years.
The tax pays for paramedic care from the regional paramedic authority, which eight local governments formed in 1982.
This year the tax is $103.50 per housing unit, or per 1,500 square feet of other buildings, and it ends in June 2027.
Measure DD would allow up to $107.50 per housing unit or 1,000 square feet of other buildings, rising up to $4 a year instead of $3.
Each year the fire district board confirms the rate, and the yearly increase is not automatic.
The ballot question says all of the money would go to local paramedic services, with yearly audits.
The resolution placing Measure DD on the ballot records its adoption by the fire district board in June, and it needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The ballot question estimates the tax would raise about $105,000 a year.

No argument against Measure DD appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
A yes vote continues the paramedic tax for four more years.
A no vote does not continue the paramedic tax.
