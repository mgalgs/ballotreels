Measure O asks the voters of this city whether to approve a sales tax for public safety.
Today, the city has a half percent general sales tax, which ends in September 2030.
Measure O would start at a half percent, rise to 1 percent in October 2030, and end in 2047.
Half the money would go to police and crime prevention, and half to fire, paramedic and disaster services.
A citizens' committee would review the spending, and the tax would be audited every year.
Measure O needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The city attorney's impartial analysis estimates $4 million a year at a half percent, and $8 million at 1 percent.
No argument for Measure O is printed in its posted pages.
No argument against Measure O is printed in its posted pages.
A yes vote authorizes the special sales tax.
A no vote does not authorize the special sales tax.
