## Quoted passages

- Question (p.1): "To fund general city services such as fire, paramedic, police, 911 emergency response, disaster preparedness, keeping public areas safe/clean; addressing homelessness; maintaining parks, and addressing speeding/unsafe driving; shall the measure increasing Westminster’s tax paid by hotel/motel guests (transient occupancy tax), from 8% to 12%, providing approximately $452,000 annually in new funds until ended by voters, requiring audits, spending disclosures, funds controlled locally, be adopted?"
- Who votes / who placed it (p.3): "To be adopted, this measure requires the approval of a majority of the voters casting ballots on the measure. The measure was placed on the ballot by the City Council of the City of Westminster."
- Analysis (p.3): "The City of Westminster currently imposes an 8% transient occupancy tax on transients (hotel/motel guests) who occupy a hotel in Westminster for 30 consecutive days or less. This tax is commonly referred to as the City’s “hotel tax.” Measure O is an ordinance that increases the rate of the hotel tax to 12%."
- Analysis (p.3): "Proceeds of the hotel tax are deposited in the City’s general fund and available for any lawful municipal purpose."
