Westminster Measure O asks city voters whether to raise the tax on hotel and motel stays from 8 to 12 percent.
Today, guests who stay 30 days or less at a Westminster hotel or motel pay an 8 percent tax, collected by the hotel.
The City Council placed Measure O on the ballot, and it needs a majority to pass.
Measure O would raise the rate to 12 percent, starting January 1, 2027, until voters repeal it.
The money would go to the general fund, for any lawful city purpose, and be audited every year.
The ballot question says the higher rate would bring in about $452,000 a year in new funds.
No argument against Measure O was submitted for the Orange County Voter Information Guide.
No argument for Measure O was submitted for the Orange County Voter Information Guide.
A yes vote is a vote to increase the hotel tax rate to 12 percent.
A no vote is a vote to not increase the hotel tax rate.
