Vote YES on Measure S to Keep Monterey Moving Safely!

In 2014 Monterey's roads were at risk of failing. Thanks to voter-approved Measures P and S, we took local control of our street infrastructure. As of 2026, our community investment has successfully repaired 1,012 street segments, completed over $7.4 million in sidewalk repairs, and funded $5.8 million in critical upgrades to storm drain pipes and pump stations. Our pavement condition index climbed from a struggling 54 to an impressive 79.

Promises made, promises kept.

Now, we must protect that progress. Without action now, current funding will expire, and infrastructure will decline. It is five times cheaper to preserve our progress with ongoing investment, than costly reconstruction later.

No Tax Increase This measure is not a tax increase. It simply continues our existing, one-cent per dollar sales tax for eight additional years to 2035. Better yet, because Monterey attracts so many visitors, the majority of tax will be paid by non-residents but will be invested in our neighborhoods.

By law, every dollar generated must be spent exclusively on:

• Fixing local streets, filling potholes, and maintaining road safety.
• Repairing sidewalks and repairs due to trees to keep pedestrians safe.
• Upgrading traffic signals and curb ramps for senior and disabled accessibility.
• Upgrading and repairing deteriorating storm drains to protect our beaches and Monterey Bay from pollution.

Guaranteed Accountability

• Citizen Oversight: Funding is strictly monitored by an independent citizens' oversight committee.
• Independent Audits: Annual audits ensure funds are spent as promised.

The Citizen Oversight Committee composed of your neighbors has seen the progress made and there is more to be done. Vote YES on Measure S the Continuation of Street Infrastructure Rehabilitation Tax.

Keep our streets safe, our beaches clean, and our city vibrant. Monterey is worth the investment.

[signed] 8/13/26
