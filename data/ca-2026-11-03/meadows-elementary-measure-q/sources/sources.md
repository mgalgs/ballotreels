## Quoted facts

- **Ballot question:** "To construct a gymnasium and improve field lighting of athletic facilities; upgrade/repair parking lots; upgrade facilities for energy efficiencies; shall the Meadows Union Elementary School District's measure be adopted to issue $2,260,400 of bonds at legal rates, levy approximately $25/$100,000 of assessed value, generating approximately $164,694 annually while bonds are outstanding, with annual audits and public disclosure of all spending, independent citizens' oversight, NO money for salaries, all money staying local?"
- **Tax rate statement:** average annual rate "$25.00 per $100,000 (2.5¢ per $100) of assessed valuation. It is currently expected that the tax will be collected until fiscal year 2050-51." Total debt service "$3,952,662."
- **Impartial analysis (County Counsel):** "This measure, if approved by 55% of votes cast on the measure, will authorize the District to issue and sell up to $2,260,000 in general obligation bonds." "The final fiscal year in which the tax is anticipated to be collected is fiscal year 2050-2051." "The best estimate of the total debt service ... is $3,952,662."
