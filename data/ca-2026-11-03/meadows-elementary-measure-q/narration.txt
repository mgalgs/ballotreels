Measure Q asks the voters of this school district whether to approve about $2.26 million in school bonds.
The district would sell the bonds and repay them from a tax on property in the district.
Measure Q needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would build a gymnasium, improve field lighting, add energy upgrades such as solar panels, and repair parking lots.
Bond money could not pay salaries, and a citizens' oversight committee and yearly audits would check the spending.
The district's tax statement estimates $25 per $100,000 of assessed value each year, through 2050-2051.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $3.95 million.
No argument for Measure Q is printed in its posted pages.
No argument against Measure Q is printed in its posted pages.
A yes vote authorizes the district to issue the bonds.
A no vote does not authorize the district to issue the bonds.
