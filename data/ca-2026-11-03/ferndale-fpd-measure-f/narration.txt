Measure F asks the voters of this fire protection district whether to approve a new yearly parcel tax for fire and emergency services.
The new tax would replace the assessment the district now collects.
Each parcel would pay by its use, from $5 a year for a vacant lot to $600 for the largest lodgings.
A home would pay $60 a year, or $90 in a rural area without fire hydrants.
The rates could rise with inflation by up to 4 percent a year.
Owners with very low incomes could apply for a hardship waiver.
Measure F needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The ballot question estimates the tax would raise about $116,000 a year.
Supporters say the district's assessment has not changed in nearly 30 years, while fire and medical service costs rose dramatically.
No argument against Measure F was filed, so the county voter guide prints only the argument in favor of it.
A yes vote adopts the new special tax.
A no vote rejects the new special tax.
