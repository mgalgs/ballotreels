## Facts with quotes

- **Ballot question (Resolution 2026-01 §4):** "To ensure a stable, locally controlled source of funding to maintain and improve fire protection, emergency medical, and rescue services–including equipment, facility improvements, and firefighter safety–shall the Ferndale Fire Protection District Ordinance No. 2026-1, establishing a new special tax raising an estimated $116,000 annually until repealed by voters, be adopted?"
- **Vote needed (guide p. 17):** "As a "special tax," Measure F must be approved by two-thirds of the voters voting in the election, and the funds raised may only be used for the specified purposes in the ordinance".
- **Rates:** "Measure F would create a "parcel tax." This means that each taxable parcel is charged a flat annual fee depending on the type and intensity of use, which is broken down into "parcel use categories" and listed in the measure. These range from a vacant lot, which would be charged $5 per year, to a large commercial lodging of over 20 units, which would be charged $600 per year."
- **Relief:** "There is an appeal process by which a property owner can challenge the parcel use category assigned to their property. Measure F also includes a hardship waiver, which allows a tax exemption for property owners who fall within the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development's definitions of "very low" or "extremely low" income."
- **Replaces assessments:** "The Measure would repeal any current assessments imposed by the district and replace it with this parcel tax."
- **Accountability:** "Measure F requires the creation of a special fund into which all proceeds will be deposited, and requires an annual report".
- **Argument in favor signers (guide p. 18):** Rick Nicholson, Fire Chief, Ferndale Volunteer Fire Department; Jack Smith, Board Chair, Ferndale Fire Protection District; Dennis DelBiaggio, Local Business Owner, DCI Builders; Jay Russ, Local Rancher & Timberland Owner.
