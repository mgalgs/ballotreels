San Bernardino Community College District Measure Y asks the district's voters whether to approve $898 million in college bonds.
The district would borrow the money and repay it from a tax on property in the district.
Measure Y needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would repair and upgrade San Bernardino Valley College and Crafton Hills College, and prepare students for careers such as firefighting and health care.
The district's tax statement estimates a tax of $25 per $100,000 of assessed value each year, collected through 2058-2059.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $1.9 billion.
Supporters say Measure Y keeps college affordable and trains more firefighters and skilled workers.
No argument against Measure Y was filed for this year's County Voter Information Guide.
A yes vote is a vote to authorize the district to issue the bonds.
A no vote means the district would not be authorized to issue the bonds.
