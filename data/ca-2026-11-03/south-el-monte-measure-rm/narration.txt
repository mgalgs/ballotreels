Measure RM asks the voters of this city whether the City Council, instead of voters, should choose the mayor from among its members.
Today, voters elect a mayor to a four-year term, and four council members, all citywide.
Under state law, a council picks its own mayor unless voters choose an elected mayor, as voters here did in 1999.
Measure RM would return to five elected council members, who would pick one of themselves as mayor at least every two years.
The current mayor would finish the term, and the office would leave the ballot starting in November 2028.
Measure RM needs a simple majority to pass.
The county Registrar's list of measures does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Supporters say a rotating mayor prevents long-term concentration of power, and gives every council member an equal platform.
Opponents say voters should keep electing the mayor, who answers to every voter, not just three council colleagues.
A yes vote has the City Council select the mayor, and makes that seat a council seat.
A no vote keeps electing the mayor directly.
