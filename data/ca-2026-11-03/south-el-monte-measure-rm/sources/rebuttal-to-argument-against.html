REBUTTAL TO ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE RM

Choosing a Mayor shouldn't be about personality politics.

The overwhelming majority of cities in Los Angeles County have a rotating mayor system as a common model. A rotating mayor system strengthens TRANSPARENCY.

FACT #1, our current mayor is under investigation by the F.P.P.C. and has been CENSURED for her negligent actions. FACT #2, the Mayor is currently SUEING her own city because of her administrative misconduct. Taking legal action against the very municipality one is elected to serve creates an extraordinary conflict and undermines the trust of our residents in South El Monte. Our elected officials should be held accountable for their actions.

Why a Rotating Mayor Works Best for South El Monte:

- Promotes Transparency: City decisions require a majority vote. A rotating mayor fosters collaboration among council members rather than division, gridlock, or individual agendas.
- Public Oversight: Emphasizes the active monitoring and supervision of government actions by the public or external bodies.
- Institutional integrity – Highlights the adherence of government organizations to legal, moral, and ethical standards.
- Saves Taxpayer Dollars: Maintaining a separate, full-time mayor's office adds unnecessary costs. A rotating model uses existing resources efficiently.
- Focuses on Results, Not Campaigning: Instead of endless campaign cycles and political posturing, city leaders can focus 100% of their energy on tackling real issues—improving local parks, public safety and upgrading infrastructure.

Vote YES on the rotating mayor measure. Protect balanced leadership, encourage real teamwork, and keep our city council focused on serving all residents while putting the power of our voters FIRST!

HECTOR DELGADO
City Council Member
