## County measure list excerpt

See `county-measure-list-excerpt.txt`, cut from the LA County RR/CC's official "FINAL — GENERAL ELECTION November 3, 2026 — Measures Appearing on the Ballot" (Rev. 9/22/26, the `-v-2` printing):

> **City Council Reform Measure Shall the voters amend the South El Monte Municipal Code to return to five elected City Council members and a Mayor selected from among the five elected City Council members?**
>
> Supporters: Hector Delgado, Councilmember, City of South El Monte; Larry Rodriguez, Councilmember, City of South El Monte
> Opponents: Gloria Olmos, Mayor of South El Monte; Manny Acosta, Councilmember of South El Monte

Vote threshold: Majority Vote 50%+1.
