REBUTTAL TO ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE RM

Vote NO on Measure RM (NO ROTATING MAYOR). The Mayor of South El Monte should remain a leader chosen directly by the people — not selected through political maneuvering or a rotating system that weakens accountability.

South El Monte residents have already made their position clear. Over 70% of voters chose to elect our Mayor directly. That mandate should not be ignored or rewritten. If anyone wants to serve as Mayor, they should earn that responsibility the democratic way: by running for office and earning the trust of voters.

Measure RM does the opposite. It removes power from the people and hands it to a select majority on the City Council. Under RM, the title of Mayor becomes something passed around behind closed doors, with no accountability, no public vetting, and no guarantee of experience or readiness to lead. A rotating appointment is not leadership — it is convenience for politicians.

The Mayor's authority should come from the voters, not from internal council politics. Trust is not something councilmembers give each other; it is something the community gives through the ballot box.

South El Monte deserves a Mayor chosen by its residents — not a revolving title controlled by a majority that may or may not reflect the will of the people.

Do not let them take your voice away! Stand with your community!

Vote NO on Measure RM (NO ROTATING MAYOR)!

KIMBERLY VALENCIA
South El Monte Resident

RICHARD ANGEL
Former Councilmember
