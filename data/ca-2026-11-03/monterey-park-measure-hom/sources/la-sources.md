## Ballot question and supporters/opponents (verbatim, from the county's final measures list)

> Proposition to Create HOME Housing Overlays in Monterey Park Shall a measure amending the Monterey Park Land Use Element to keep residential neighborhoods intact through maintaining voter-approved land use restrictions and Monterey Park's local control over the state for housing decisions, continue prohibiting data centers, provide housing for residents, prevent crime/homeless encampments, attract businesses, and improve the local economy by allowing limited residential uses in underutilized industrial, commercial/office areas, requiring public input, ensuring environmental protections, protecting public safety, parks/open space, be adopted?
>
> Supporters: Henry Lo, Mayor; Jose Sanchez, Pro Tem; Thomas Wong, Council Member; Vinh Ngo, Council Member; Elizabeth Yang, Council Member
>
> Opponents: None submitted.

Vote threshold: Majority Vote 50%+1.
