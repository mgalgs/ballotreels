Measure N asks the voters of this city whether to raise the local sales tax and keep it until ended by voters.
Four of the five members of the City Council voted to place Measure N on the ballot.
The city has a general sales tax of 1.5 percent, set to expire in 2040.
Measure N would raise the rate from 1.5 percent to 1.875 percent.
The tax would no longer end in 2040, and would stay in effect until repealed by voters.
The tax would remain a general tax, its revenue audited each year, and Measure N needs a simple majority to pass.
The ballot question says the increase would raise about $1.8 million a year, and the total tax about $9.4 million.
Any argument against Measure N is filed with the city clerk, and is not online.
Any argument for Measure N is filed with the city clerk, and is not online.
A yes vote raises the sales tax to 1.875 percent until ended by voters.
A no vote keeps the 1.5 percent tax, which would expire in 2040.
