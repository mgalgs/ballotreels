## Quoted facts

- **Ballot question** (Resolution 2026-043, §3; OCR artefacts corrected): "To upgrade streets/sidewalks, address beach erosion, enhance wildfire preparedness, maintain City trees, and fund other general government purposes in City of Carmel-by-the-Sea, shall the measure increasing the transactions and use tax (also known as sales tax) by 0.375%, from 1.50% to 1.875%, providing approximately $1,800,000 annually in additional revenue and approximately $9,400,000 annually in total revenue, and extending the total 1.875% tax until ended by voters, with independent audits and all funds benefitting Carmel-by-the-Sea, be adopted?"
- **Current tax:** "the City currently has a general transactions and use tax at the rate of 1.5% which is set to expire in 2040".
- **Threshold** (§5): "a majority of those casting ballots on the Measure (50% plus 1)."
