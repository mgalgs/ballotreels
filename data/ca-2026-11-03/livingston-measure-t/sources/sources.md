## Facts and quotes

- Measure letter and label (notice p. 3): "MEASURE T (APPOINTIVE CITY CLERK)".
- Ballot question (notice p. 3): "Shall the office of the City Clerk be Appointive?" (YES / NO).
- Statement printed with the question (notice p. 3): "The City Clerk shall no longer be an elective official in the City of Livingston. From and after November 2028, or upon earlier vacancy in the office of City Clerk, the City Clerk shall be appointed by the City Council. The City Clerk so appointed shall hold office at the pleasure of the City Council and, notwithstanding California Government Code section 36502 to the contrary, is not required to be a resident or elector in the City. The City Council may by Ordinance vest in the City Manager its authority to appoint the City Clerk and City Treasurer."
- Spanish (notice p. 4): "MEDIDA T (SECRETARIO MUNICIPAL POR DESIGNACIÓN) … ¿Debería el cargo de Secretario Municipal ser un puesto de designación?"
- Placed on the ballot (agenda Jan 21, 2026, consent item 4): "Resolution Calling For A Municipal Election To Be Consolidated With The Statewide General Election On November 3, 2026, Seeking Voter Approval To Convert The Office Of City Clerk To An Appointed Office, Setting Dates For The Submission Of Arguments In Favor Of Or Against The Ballot Measure, And Requesting The Assistance Of The County Of Merced In Connection With That Election."
- The office is also on the ballot (notice p. 1): "For City Clerk … Short Term of Two Years … NO CANDIDATES".
