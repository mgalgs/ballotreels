Measure T asks the voters of this city whether the city clerk should be appointed instead of elected.
Today, the voters of the city elect the city clerk.
This same ballot lists a two-year term for city clerk, with no candidates.
The City Council would appoint the clerk from November 2028, or sooner if the office falls vacant.
An appointed clerk would serve at the pleasure of the council, and would not have to live or vote in the city.
The council could pass an ordinance giving the city manager the power to appoint the clerk and the treasurer.
The notice of the measures to be voted on does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
No argument for Measure T appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
No argument against Measure T appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
A yes vote makes the city clerk an appointed office.
A no vote keeps the city clerk an elected office.
