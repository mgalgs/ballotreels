City of Hayward Measure CC asks voters in the city whether to update the business license tax that businesses in Hayward pay.
Every business in Hayward pays the tax, which has not been updated since 1978.
Today the tax is set mainly by flat fees and the number of employees.
The City Council placed Measure CC on the ballot, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
Most businesses would pay by their gross receipts, from $0.30 to $3.75 per $1,000, with higher rates for higher-grossing businesses.
Every business would pay at least $60, and residents who do not run a business would not pay it.
The money could be used for any city purpose, starting in January 2027.
The City Attorney's impartial analysis estimates the update would raise an additional $12 million a year.
No argument against Measure CC was submitted, and none appears in the Alameda County Composite Voter Information Guide for this election.
Supporters say the tax, unchanged since 1978, no longer keeps pace with costs, and larger businesses would pay higher rates.
A yes vote would update and amend the fee schedule of the business license tax.
A no vote means the 1978 fee schedule would stay in place.
