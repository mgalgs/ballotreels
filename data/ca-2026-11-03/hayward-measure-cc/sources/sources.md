## Ballot question (guide p. 919)

> HAYWARD BUSINESS LICENSE TAX MODERNIZATION: Shall the measure updating the Hayward Business License Tax for the first time since 1978, to support general city services, including neighborhood police protection, firefighting, 911 response, libraries, and pothole repair, generating an additional $12 million annually until repealed, with a minimum tax of $60 and rates from $.30 to $3.75 per $1,000 of gross receipts, with higher rates for higher-grossing businesses, as stated in the ordinance, be adopted?

Transcribed from the guide; line-break hyphens rejoined and the YES/NO box labels omitted.

## Who votes on it

Voters in the City of Hayward. The guide section is headed "CITY OF HAYWARD MEASURE CC", and the Registrar's measure list (<https://alamedacountyca.gov/rov_app/measures/election/260>) files it under that jurisdiction. Vote threshold: see the analysis quotes below.

## Full text — not in the guide

The guide prints no full text for Measure CC and no tax rate statement. The impartial analysis (p. 919) says: "If you desire a copy of the measure, please call the office of the City Clerk at (510) 583-4400 and a copy will be mailed to you at no cost. You may also access more information about the measure on the City of Hayward website at https://www.hayward-ca.gov/your-government/elections/ballot-measures". That City page (retrieved 2026-10-28) links only argument forms, not the ordinance. The `rov-measure-packet.pdf` here (the Registrar's scanned packet for this measure) is the only archived copy of the City's filing; I did not OCR it, so whether it holds the full ordinance is unconfirmed.
