Measure U asks the voters of this town whether to continue a special tax for paramedic services for four more years.
A town staff report says this year's rate is $103.50, and the current tax ends in June 2027.
The resolution placing Measure U on the ballot says voters here last approved the tax in 2022.
The tax would be $107.50 a year for each home, and the same for each 1,000 square feet of other buildings.
The town council could raise the rate by up to $4 a year, to no more than $118 in the last year.
The tax would end on June 30, 2031, and the ballot question says all funds would go to local paramedic services.
Measure U needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The ballot question says the tax would raise about $111,000 a year.

A no vote does not continue the paramedic tax, which ends in June 2027.
