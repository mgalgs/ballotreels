November 3, 2026 Election Information

The Town of Ross will hold a Municipal Election on November 3, 2026 for two Council seats, a ballot measure to codify the reestablishment and operation of the Ross Fire Station, and a ballot measure to renew the Ross Valley Paramedic Authority four-year parcel tax to maintain rapid and local emergency medical service in the Town of Ross. 

Voting reminders
  • Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
  • Vote-by-mail ballots will be mailed beginning Oct. 5th.
  • Register or update your registration by Oct. 19th, or use same-day registration at a vote center.
    • https://registertovote.ca.gov/

For more information regarding the election process in Marin County, please visit the Marin County Elections Department webpage.

Paramedic Parcel Tax Renewal - Measure U

Ross voters will see Measure U on the November 3, 2026 ballot.

What is Measure U?

Measure U would continue an existing parcel tax that helps fund paramedic services provided by the Ross Valley Paramedic Authority (RVPA). Its paramedics, along with paramedics on a fire engine, respond to medical emergencies and provide advanced life support before ambulance transport to the emergency room. Measure U requires two-thirds voter approval to pass. 

Background Information:

For 44 years, local paramedic services in the Ross Valley have been supported by a parcel tax that provides funding to protect paramedic services. Paramedic funding helps support two ambulances as well as state-of-the-art fire department units with trained paramedics and equipment aboard fire engines. Both a fire engine and an ambulance are dispatched to 911 emergency calls.

What Measure U would do:

  • Continue the tax for four years, covering fiscal years 2027-28 through 2030-31.
  • Set the residential rate at $107.50 per dwelling unit for 2027-28.
  • Allow the Town Council to approve annual increases of up to $4.

Where does the money go?

The parcel tax funds roughly half of RVPA's operations. The rest comes primarily from ambulance transport revenue. RVPA tax funds may only be used for local paramedic services.

RVPA operations include:

  • Maintaining the number of on-duty paramedics
  • Training local paramedics and provide advanced lifesaving equipment
  • Preserving the existing level of paramedic response services

VOTER'S CHOICE ACT

The Voter's Choice Act is in effect for Marin County.

What is the Voter's Choice Act?
The Voter's Choice Act is a law that allows counties to offer voters more choices for how, when, and where they vote. Now, all active registered voters are automatically mailed a ballot, early voting is expanded for more days, and voters may vote in-person at a regional vote center. For further information on the Voter's Choice Act visit the Marin County Elections Department.

What is a Vote Center?
Voters may vote in-person at ANY vote center in Marin County. Assigned neighborhood polling places are replaced by regional vote centers that open at least 3 days before Election Day with more services. 

PLEASE NOTE ROSS NO LONGER HAS A POLLING PLACE. Residents can vote at ANY vote center in Marin County but there will not be one in Ross.

For more election information visit the Marin County Elections Department.
