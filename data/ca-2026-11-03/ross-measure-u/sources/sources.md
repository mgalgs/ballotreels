## Facts and quotes

- "The Town of Ross will hold a Municipal Election on November 3, 2026 for two Council seats, a ballot measure to codify the reestablishment and operation of the Ross Fire Station, and a ballot measure to renew the Ross Valley Paramedic Authority four-year parcel tax to maintain rapid and local emergency medical service in the Town of Ross."
- "Ross voters will see Measure U on the November 3, 2026 ballot."
- "Measure U would continue an existing parcel tax that helps fund paramedic services provided by the Ross Valley Paramedic Authority (RVPA). ... Measure U requires two-thirds voter approval to pass."
- "Continue the tax for four years, covering fiscal years 2027-28 through 2030-31." "Set the residential rate at $107.50 per dwelling unit for 2027-28." "Allow the Town Council to approve annual increases of up to $4."
- The Town points to the county for documents: "Full text of Measure U, the Town Attorney's impartial analysis, and the arguments for and against: Marin County Elections" → https://www.marincounty.gov/departments/elections/november-3-2026-general-election/information-about-measures-110326/list-measures-110326/measure-u-110326 (blocked).

Note: this page is the Town's informational summary, not the ballot pamphlet.
