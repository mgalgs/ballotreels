Pacific Grove Unified School District
Argument in Favor of Measure _

Vote YES on Measure _ to keep Pacific Grove schools strong and support student success.

Pacific Grove's public schools are where every child learns, grows, and gets the support to reach their full potential. Our schools help students build strong academic foundations, support student well-being and provide opportunities for every student to succeed.

A YES vote on Measure _ will provide Pacific Grove schools with locally controlled funding that cannot be taken by the State or federal government and spent elsewhere. Every dollar will directly support programs and services at Forest Grove Elementary School, Robert Down Elementary School, Pacific Grove Middle School, Pacific Grove Adult Education Center, Pacific Grove Community High School and Pacific Grove High School.

Measure _ will:
• Strengthen Reading and Math: Provide academic support services to improve students' reading comprehension, math skills and academic success.
• Support Student Mental Health and Wellness: Retain mental health counselors and maintain wellness programs that help our students feel safe, supported and ready to learn.
• Keep Before- and After-School Programs Affordable: Ensure local working families can count on safe, affordable programs offering childcare, homework assistance, music, art, sports, wellness activities, and enrichment classes.
• Support Students with Disabilities: Improve programs and services so students receive the support they need to learn and thrive.
• Retain Qualified Teachers and Staff: Retain strong educators, counselors and staff who know our students and are dedicated to helping them succeed.

Measure _ includes strict taxpayer protections:
• Full public disclosure of all spending, independent citizen oversight and annual audits are required.
• Measure _ Funds cannot be taken by the State or Federal government and spent elsewhere.
• Funds cannot be used for administrator salaries, pensions or benefits.

Keep Pacific Grove Unified School District schools strong!
