Impartial Analysis of Measure K

A Parcel Tax pursuant to Section 4 of Article XIIIA of the California Constitution and California Government Code sections 50079 et seq. and 50075 et seq., otherwise known as the Parcel Tax Law, authorizes a school district, upon approval by two-thirds of the electorate voting on the measure, to levy a qualified special tax for specified purposes.

The Board of Education for Pacific Grove Unified School District (hereafter "the District") has placed Measure K on the ballot, proposing a parcel tax be levied in an equal amount against each parcel of taxable real property in the District, in the amount of $85 per parcel for five years, commencing with fiscal year 2026-2027. A parcel is defined as any unit of land in the District that receives a separate tax bill from the County Tax Collector and shall be collected in the same manner and subject to the same penalty or penalties as other taxes fixed and collected by the County on behalf of the District. Exemptions exist for people over 65 years of age who meet certain qualifications or for people receiving Supplemental Security Income for a disability and who apply for an exemption.

Parcel tax funds will be used for such things as: Improving services for students with disabilities; keeping before and after school programs affordable and supporting student mental health counseling at various schools in the District; and enhancing reading and math programs.

Approval of the measure does not guarantee that all of the proposed projects of the District to be improved with the parcel tax will be funded beyond the local revenues generated by the measure.

As required by law, the District must follow certain accountability provisions to oversee the funds collected and expended, including the establishment of an independent Citizens' Oversight Committee, the annual completion of Performance and Financial Audits, and an annual report detailing proceeds received by the parcel tax and expenditures made. No administrator salaries, pensions or benefits will be allowed to be funded by the Parcel Tax. However, staff salaries are allowed under this measure.

The highest tax rate to be levied is $85 per parcel. The final fiscal year in which the tax is anticipated to be collected is 2031-2032.

A "Yes" vote on this measure is a vote in favor of the issuance of a parcel tax of $85 per parcel, annually for five years to be used for the purposes set forth in Measure K.

A "No" vote on this measure is a vote against the issuance of a parcel tax of $85 per parcel, annually for five years, to be used for the purposes set forth in Measure K.
