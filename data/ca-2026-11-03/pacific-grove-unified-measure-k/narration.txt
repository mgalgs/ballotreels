Measure K asks the voters of this school district whether to approve a parcel tax of $85 a year for five years.
A parcel tax charges the same amount on each parcel of taxable land in the district, whatever its value.
Measure K needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The tax would be collected each year from 2026-2027 through 2031-2032.
Some people over 65, and people receiving supplemental security income for a disability, could apply to be exempt.
The money would go to reading and math programs, services for students with disabilities, before- and after-school programs, and mental health counseling.
The ballot question says the tax would raise about $800,000 a year.
Supporters say the money stays local, unable to be taken by the state or federal government, and helps keep strong teachers, counselors and staff.
No argument against Measure K appears among the official documents gathered for this video; the county measure list links only the argument in favor.
A yes vote is in favor of a parcel tax of $85 per parcel each year for five years, for the purposes the measure sets out.
A no vote is against the parcel tax.
