Proposition 3 is about income tax rates for people earning over $371,000 a year.
In 2012, voters approved higher income tax rates on high incomes.
In 2016, voters extended those higher rates.
Today, the higher rates are set to end in 2031.
Proposition 3 would make the higher rates permanent.
The Legislative Analyst's Office says the higher rates would bring in $5 billion to $15 billion a year, depending on the stock market.
The Office says about 40 percent would go to schools and community colleges, and the rest to other state programs.
Supporters say Proposition 3 raises no one's taxes and prevents cuts to schools and health care.
Opponents say Proposition 3 makes temporary taxes permanent, and the state should fix its spending first.
A yes vote means the tax increase would become permanent.
A no vote means the tax increase would expire in 2031.
