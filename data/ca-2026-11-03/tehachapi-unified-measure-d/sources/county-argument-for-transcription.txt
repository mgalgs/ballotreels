Argument in Favor of Measure D

Tehachapi Unified School District

Our schools are our community's most important shared asset. Whether or not you have school-aged children, investing in our local schools safeguards property values and enhances overall quality of life.

TUSD has demonstrated responsible stewardship of taxpayer funds and district assets. The District refinanced past bonds saving taxpayers over $1.3 million and paid them off in 2022. Today, TUSD has zero outstanding bond debt and $0 in school bond taxes on local property bills.

Having exhausted all existing funding avenues, Measure D is a prudent, necessary reinvestment in our existing school facilities focused strictly on modernization of current facilities, not broad expansion.

Guided by two years of facility assessments and community input, Measure D addresses urgent repairs and long-awaited campus priorities:

• Essential Repairs & Safety: Replace aging campus roofs, replace failing heating and cooling (HVAC) systems, and upgrade district-wide security access controls.

• Classrooms & Arts: Modernize aging classrooms and gymnasium at Jacobsen Middle School, upgrade elementary kindergarten and special education classrooms with appropriate restrooms, and construct dedicated classrooms for performing arts.

• Athletics & Community Pride: Complete high school stadium and athletic field improvements so ALL Tehachapi High athletics can finally be hosted right on campus.

Strict Taxpayer Accountability & Protections:

• 100% Local: Every dollar stays in Tehachapi to improve local schools and cannot be taken by the State.

• Fiscal Oversight: Independent annual audits and a Citizens' Oversight Committee guarantee full transparency.

• No Overhead: Funds are legally restricted to facilities. No funds can go toward administrator or teacher salaries.

Measure D is a responsible investment in our students and our community's future. Please join us in voting YES ON MEASURE D!
