Measure D asks the voters of this school district whether to approve $61 million in school bonds.
The district would sell the bonds and repay them from a yearly tax on property in the district.
Measure D needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The bond money could pay only for the school facility projects the measure lists.
The ballot question names roofs, heating, cooling and plumbing, modernizing schools, transitional kindergarten and arts classrooms, a new high school stadium, and campus safety.
The district's tax rate statement estimates an average tax of $28.65 per $100,000 of assessed value, and at most $29.
The statement expects the tax to be collected until the 2057-2058 fiscal year.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $121.2 million.
Supporters say the district paid off its past bonds in 2022, and the measure addresses urgent repairs and long-awaited campus priorities.
No argument against Measure D appears among the official documents gathered for this video; only the argument in favor does.
A yes vote authorizes the district to issue $61 million in bonds.
A no vote does not authorize the bonds.
