Measure K asks voters in this city whether to add a one-half percent sales tax.
The City Council placed Measure K on the ballot, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
The tax would be added to existing sales taxes on purchases in the city, until voters repeal it.
As a general tax, the money would go into the general fund of the city.
The City estimates the tax would raise about $5.5 million a year, the City Attorney's impartial analysis says.
Opponents say the city should explore alternatives before asking residents to pay more, and sales taxes burden working families and seniors.
Supporters say Measure K helps prevent cuts to public safety and 911 response, and funds aging parks, streets and water services.
A yes vote is a vote to approve the tax.
A no vote is a vote against the tax.
