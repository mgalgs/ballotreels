## Facts and quotes

**Ballot question (p1):** "Rohnert Park Financial Viability, Community Health/Safety Measure. To maintain 911 response/fire protection/wildfire preparedness; protect local drinking water; keep parks clean/safe; prevent property crime/burglary/public safety officer reductions; repair streets/potholes; and other general city services; shall the measure, establishing a half-cent local sales tax, providing approximately $5,500,000 annually for locally controlled funding that cannot be taken by the state, until ended by voters, requiring audits/public spending disclosure, be adopted?"

**Vote needed:** "Simple Majority Needed to Pass" (p1); "In order for Measure K to pass, the City's voters must approve the Measure by a simple majority vote (50% + 1) vote." (p2)

**Who votes:** voters of the City of Rohnert Park ("if approved by a majority of the City's voters", p2).

**Impartial analysis (p2), key statements:**
- "...would enact Ordinance No. 998 authorizing a 0.5% (1/2¢) transactions and use tax in the City of Rohnert Park."
- "This tax would be a general tax, meaning that revenues raised from the tax can be used for any general municipal purpose and will be deposited in the City's general fund."
- "The City estimates that Measure K, if approved, would generate approximately $5,500,000 in annual tax revenue, but the actual tax revenue will vary based on the level of purchases occurring in Rohnert Park."
- "Certain necessities of life, such as groceries or prescription medicine, would be exempt from the tax."
- Signed: Michelle Marchetta Kenyon, City Attorney.

**Argument in favor (p3):** signed Gregory Nordin (Former Mayor), Pat Miller (Realtor), Frankie Lemus (Restaurant Owner), Andre Bailey (Trustee, SRJC), Jerrod Marshall (Public Safety Officers Association).

**Rebuttal to argument in favor (p3):** signed Gina Belforte, Joseph T. Callinan, Amy O. Ahanotu, Pam Stafford.

**Argument against (p4):** signed Gina Belforte, Joseph T. Callinan, Amy O. Ahanotu, Pam Stafford (each "Former Mayor Rohnert Park"). Closes: "We respectfully urge you to Vote No on Measure K."

**Rebuttal to argument against (p4):** signed Miriam Pengel, Andrew Smith, Lori Tapian Street, Martín Avila, Aaron Johnson.
