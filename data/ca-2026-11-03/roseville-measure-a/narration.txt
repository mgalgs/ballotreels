Measure A asks the voters of this city whether to raise city council pay from $600 a month to $2,550 a month.
Voters set council pay at $600 a month in 2000, and it has not changed since.
The City Council placed Measure A on the ballot, and it needs a majority to pass.
Measure A would set each council member's pay at up to $2,550 a month, with no extra for the mayor.
The pay would then rise each year with the state's consumer price index, by no more than 5 percent.
The Measure A voter guide pages do not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Opponents say automatic raises, which other city employees do not get, should be voted on apart from the pay increase.
Supporters say pay frozen for 26 years should reach the amount state law allows, so more people can afford to serve.
A yes vote is a vote for the amendment to the charter.
A no vote is a vote against the amendment to the charter.
