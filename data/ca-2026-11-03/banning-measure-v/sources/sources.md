## Quoted passages

- Question (p.23): "Shall Ordinance No. 1619, which amends the land uses for property south of West Westward Avenue and east of Sunset Avenue to allow Very High Density Residential use on 41 acres of the 49.2-acre site, be adopted?"
- What it is / who placed it (p.46): "Measure V is a referendum regarding City of Banning Ordinance No. 1619 which was adopted by the Banning City Council on April 14, 2026, (the “Ordinance”)." … "The Measure was placed on the ballot by a referendum petition signed by the requisite number of voters."
- Effect (p.46): "If Measure V is approved the Ordinance will become effective. If Measure V is not approved the Ordinance will not become effective."
- Analysis (p.46): "The Ordinance changes the zoning for 41 acres of a 49.2-acre site within the jurisdiction of the City from Public Facilities-School to Very High Density Residential."
