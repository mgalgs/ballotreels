## Facts and quotes

**Ballot question** (revised "Appendix B — Abbreviated Form of Bond Measure", transmitted to the Registrar July 28, 2026; identical to the Notice of Measures, 8/5/2026): "To fix deteriorating roofs, plumbing, gas, and electrical systems, remove asbestos/lead pipes, repair/upgrade aging classrooms, science labs, technology and school facilities to support college/ career readiness in math, science, technology, arts, engineering, and skilled trades; shall Dixon Unified School District's measure authorizing $49,000,000 in bonds at legal rates be adopted, levying approximately $30 per $100,000 assessed value ($3,000,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding, with citizen oversight, independent audits, and all money locally controlled?"

The transmittal letter (Superintendent Brett Barley) says the revision was made "in order to present to voters the projected tax rate for Measure U as dollars per $100,000 of assessed valuation, rather than cents per $100 of assessed valuation." The letter's subject line reads "General Obligation Bond Election (55%) – November 5, 2024" — as printed; the rest of the record is for November 3, 2026.

**Impartial analysis** (signed Carrie Blacklock, Solano County Counsel), key statements:
- "Measure U was placed on the ballot by the Governing Board of the Dixon Unified School District. If approved, Measure U would authorize the District to issue and sell general obligation bonds in an aggregate principal amount not to exceed $49,000,000".
- "Bond proceeds may be used only for the purposes specified in the measure and may not be used for teacher or administrator salaries or other school operating expenses."
- "Principal and interest on the bonds would be paid by property taxes levied on taxable property within the District. According to the District's Tax Rate Statement, the estimated average annual tax rate is $29 per $100,000 of assessed valuation, and the estimated highest tax rate is $30 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. The District estimates that taxes will be collected through fiscal year 2061-62 and that total debt service, including principal and interest, will be approximately $105,000,000."
- **Vote needed:** "Measure U requires approval by at least 55 percent of the votes cast on the measure to pass."

**Who votes:** voters of the Dixon Unified School District. Whether the district extends into another county (e.g. Yolo) is not stated in these documents.
