Measure LV asks the voters of this school district whether to renew a $98 parcel tax for twelve more years.
The current parcel tax of $98 a year, which voters renewed in 2015, is due to expire in June 2028.
The district's governing board placed Measure LV on the ballot, and it needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
Measure LV would levy $98 on each parcel every year for twelve years, starting in July 2028.
The money could pay for academic and arts programs, class sizes and teachers, but not for administrator salaries.
Owners 65 or older, and some with disabilities, could apply for an exemption on the home they live in.
The measure's full text estimates the tax would keep providing about $2 million a year.
The official list of measures from the county, which names who filed each argument, says no argument against Measure LV was submitted.
Supporters say the measure renews funding at the current rate without raising taxes, and that without it, teacher layoffs would follow.
A yes vote renews the $98 parcel tax for twelve years.
A no vote leaves the $98 parcel tax to expire in June 2028.
