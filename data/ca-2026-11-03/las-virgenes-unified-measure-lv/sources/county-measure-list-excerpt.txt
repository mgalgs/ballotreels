LAS VIRGENES UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT – (1)
VOTE PASSAGE – 2/3 Votes (66.6% of Votes)
LV     To protect the quality of education in local schools and continue funding for math,
       science, technology, engineering, arts, skilled trades, and advanced programs that
       prepare students for success in college/ careers; attracting and retaining highly qualified
       teachers; and maintaining manageable class sizes, shall Las Virgenes Unified School
       Districts measure renewing the existing school parcel tax at the current $98 rate (no
       increase) for twelve years be adopted, with senior exemptions and all funds ($2,000,000
       annually) benefiting local students? Supporters: Richard Sherman, CPHOA Pres.;
       Karen Zak Kent, DDS; Dr. Kirk Tamaddon, LVUSD Oversight Comm.; Joe Seetoo,
       CFA; Angie Falk Opponents: None submitted.
