## Ballot question and supporters/opponents (verbatim, from the county's final measures list)

> To protect the quality of education in local schools and continue funding for math, science, technology, engineering, arts, skilled trades, and advanced programs that prepare students for success in college/careers; attracting and retaining highly qualified teachers; and maintaining manageable class sizes, shall Las Virgenes Unified School Districts measure renewing the existing school parcel tax at the current $98 rate (no increase) for twelve years be adopted, with senior exemptions and all funds ($2,000,000 annually) benefiting local students?
>
> Supporters: Richard Sherman, CPHOA Pres.; Karen Zak Kent, DDS; Dr. Kirk Tamaddon, LVUSD Oversight Comm.; Joe Seetoo, CFA; Angie Falk
>
> Opponents: None submitted.

Vote threshold: 2/3 Votes (66.6% of Votes) (county list).

## What's present and what's missing

- **Impartial analysis**: present.
- **Full measure text**: present.
- **Tax rate statement**: not present and likely does not apply — this is a flat parcel-tax renewal (no increase, no new ad valorem levy), the category that doesn't trigger an Elections Code §9401 tax rate statement.
- **Argument for**: confirmed filed (named supporters match the county list), but the filed argument text was not staged — an earlier finder pass's `.txt` extract of the same ISB feed exists in the raw inputs but is a duplicate-format extract of ISB data, not a distinct document, so it was not copied in.
- **Argument against / rebuttals**: none — county list states "Opponents: None submitted," so no argument against and no rebuttals exist.
- **Resolution calling the election / staff report**: not found. (A district fact sheet, informational only, was in the raw inputs but not staged.)
