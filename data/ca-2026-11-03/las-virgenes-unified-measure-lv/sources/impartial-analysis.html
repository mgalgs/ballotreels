IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE LV

By Dawyn R. Harrison, County Counsel

This measure was placed on the ballot by the governing board (Board) of the Las Virgenes Unified School District (District). If approved by two-thirds (2/3) of the votes cast on the measure, this measure would authorize the District to raise additional revenue by levying a special parcel tax of $98 per parcel (Tax). If approved, the Tax will be levied annually for twelve years, beginning July 1, 2028. This Tax would extend an existing special parcel tax of $98 per parcel per year that is due to expire on June 30, 2028.

The measure defines "Parcel" (Parcel) as any parcel of land that lies wholly or partially within the District and that receives a separate tax bill for ad valorem property taxes from the Los Angeles County Treasurer and Tax Collector or the Ventura County Treasurer-Tax Collector (collectively, Tax Collector). Parcels that are otherwise exempt from ad valorem property taxes in any year would also be exempt from the Tax. Certain contiguous parcels under identical ownership and used solely for owner-occupied, single-family residential purposes may be treated as a single parcel under District procedures. The Tax would be collected by the applicable Tax Collector, with unpaid taxes to bear interest at the same rate applicable to unpaid ad valorem property taxes.

Proceeds from the Tax shall be used only for purposes specified in the Measure, including to:

- Continue offering core academic programs in math, science and technology;

- Continue funding for core programs in reading and the arts and funding for athletics;

- Maintain class sizes;

- Keep textbooks, technology, and instructional materials up-to-date; and

- Attract and retain highly qualified teachers, counselors, career advisors, and technology specialists.

Tax proceeds cannot be used for administrator salaries, benefits, or pensions. Tax proceeds must be deposited into a separate account, with a Citizens' Oversight Committee to provide oversight as to the expenditure of the proceeds. The chief fiscal officer of the District will also prepare an annual written report detailing the collection and expenditure of the proceeds.

An exemption may be applied for with the District on any Parcel owned by one or more persons who occupy such Parcel as their principal residence and is/are: (1) 65 years of age or older on or before to June 30 of the preceding fiscal year; (2) receives Supplemental Security Income for a disability regardless of age; or (3) receives Social Security Disability Insurance benefits regardless of age and whose yearly income does not exceed 250 percent of the 2012 federal poverty guidelines. Persons currently exempted from the District's expiring special parcel tax will remain exempt and not be required to reapply for exemption. The District's decisions regarding the Tax, including exemptions, would be final and binding.
