Measure Q asks the voters of this town whether to continue a special tax for paramedic services for four more years.
A town staff report says this year's tax is $87 for each home, and the current tax ends in June 2027.
The resolution placing Measure Q on the ballot says voters here last approved the tax in 2022.
The tax would start at $94.50 a year for each home, and the same for each 1,000 square feet of other buildings.
The rate could rise by up to $7.75 a year, to no more than $117.75.
The tax would end on June 30, 2031, and its money would be kept in a special fund for paramedic and emergency medical services only.
The town treasurer would report each year on the money collected and spent.
Measure Q needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The ballot question says the tax would raise about $786,000 a year.

A no vote does not continue the paramedic tax, which ends in June 2027.
