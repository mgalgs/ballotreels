Measure E asks the voters of this city whether to update parts of the city charter.
A city charter works like a local constitution, setting out the form of city government and some of its rules.
The City Council placed Measure E on the ballot unanimously, and it needs a majority to pass.
Measure E would replace gender-specific terms with gender-neutral ones, and fix typographical errors and outdated terms.
The City Council would have 60 days, instead of 30, to fill a vacancy on the council.
Ordinances and resolutions would no longer have to be read before adoption.
The Registrar of Voters' List of Local Measures does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
No argument against Measure E was filed with the county Registrar of Voters.
Supporters say Measure E modernizes the charter and makes council meetings more efficient.
A yes vote adopts the charter amendments.
A no vote rejects the charter amendments.
