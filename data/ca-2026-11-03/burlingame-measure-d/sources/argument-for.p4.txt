City of Burlingame

Argument in Favor of Measure __

Vote Yes on Measure __ to support a safe, strong Burlingame and protect local property values, all without raising taxes on residents.

We live in Burlingame because it offers an exceptional quality of life and great local services. We take pride in our Police Department, parks, local businesses, and community programs for youth and seniors. However, the growing number of visitors who stay in our community has added pressure onto our streets, sidewalks, and public safety services.

These community spaces, streets, and sidewalks now require ongoing maintenance that is beyond current available resources. If we don’t address these issues now, they will be more expensive to repair and maintain in the future.

Burlingame’s hotel tax rate is currently among the lowest in the County and has not been increased in more than 17 years. Voting YES on Measure __ ensures that hotel guests help fund the services they use, so local homeowners and residents don’t bear the full cost.

Well-maintained streets, safe sidewalks, and clean parks protect Burlingame’s quality of life and help preserve property values.

Measure __ Does Not Increase Taxes for Local Residents

• Measure __ ensures our hotel guests pay their fair share for city services
• The state and federal government cannot take one penny of Measure __
• Independent citizens’ oversight and annual financial audits are required

Vote YES on __: Keep Burlingame Safe and Desirable

• Repair and maintain streets, sidewalks, and potholes
• Maintain parks and healthy trees on city streets
• Strengthen emergency preparedness and public safety
• Support recreation programs and facilities for youth and seniors

Safe neighborhoods, beautiful parks and clean streets make Burlingame a desirable place to live, do business, and raise a family.

Join local leaders in voting Yes on Measure __ to protect our community and keep Burlingame Strong.
