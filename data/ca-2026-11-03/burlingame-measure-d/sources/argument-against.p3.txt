Argument against Measure _ – Burlingame Hotel Tax Increase

[Argument starts here]

The Burlingame City Council expects voters to approve a huge, 25 PERCENT tax hike by saying residents won’t have to pay.

But they want to dramatically increase the cost of visitors’ coming here for business or pleasure.

Councilmembers are basically saying, let’s discourage people from visiting and spending their business dollars here, and/or to seeing us, their family and friends. Even when they’re here to help while we’re in the hospital!

Does that make any sense?

Vote NO.

The ballot question says the tax would support “maintaining city streets/ sidewalks …repairing potholes,” and “enhancing emergency response, preparedness …wildfire prevention.…”

Which begs the question:

Why aren’t such core services as police, pothole repair, and emergency response services fully covered by the budget?

Don’t trust politicians!

Budgets demonstrate our representatives’ priorities. Why the heck aren’t those core services the highest priority, every day?

There’s no guarantee the money would reach those services, anyway — because Measure_ actually proposes a “general government use” tax. So it could be diverted to literally anything the councilmembers pick.

Councilmembers should safeguard, in their regular budget, our streets, sidewalks, and emergency response services.

Don’t we want more business from out-of-town visitors? more visits from loved ones?

It’s common sense: Don’t tax what you want more of!

Vote NO!

Send a message to councilmembers: Don’t treat vital core services as a mere afterthought, and penalize Burlingame’s visitors to cover expenses you’ve overlooked!

Vote NO!

Silicon Valley Taxpayers Association’s volunteers advocate for taxpayers throughout Santa Clara County. Visit SVtaxpayers.org.

[Argument ends here]
