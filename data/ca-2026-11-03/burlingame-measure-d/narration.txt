Burlingame Measure D asks city voters whether to raise the tax on hotel stays from 12 to 15 percent.
Today, guests who stay less than 30 days at a Burlingame hotel or other short-term lodging pay a 12 percent tax.
The City Council placed Measure D on the ballot, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
Measure D would raise the rate to 15 percent, paid by the hotel guest, until voters repeal it.
The money would go to the general fund, for any city purpose, and be audited every year.
The impartial analysis estimates about $5.7 million more a year, beside about $22 million the hotel tax brought in for 2025-2026.
Opponents say this is a 25 percent tax hike, and as a general tax it could fund anything the council picks.
Supporters say hotel guests would help fund the services they use, and the rate is among the lowest in the county.
A yes vote approves raising the hotel tax from 12 to 15 percent.
A no vote rejects Measure D and keeps the hotel tax at 12 percent.
