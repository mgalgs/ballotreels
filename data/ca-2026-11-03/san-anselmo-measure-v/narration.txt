Measure V asks the voters of this town whether to continue a parcel tax for paramedic services for four more years.
The tax pays for paramedic care from the regional paramedic authority, which eight local governments formed in 1982.
This year the tax is $103.50 per housing unit, or per 1,500 square feet of other buildings.
Measure V would charge $107.50 per unit, or per 1,000 square feet, then up to $4 more each year, to at most $118.
The money could go only to paramedic services, and the tax would end in June 2031.
Measure V needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The ballot question estimates the tax would raise about $688,000 a year.
No argument for Measure V appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
No argument against Measure V appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
A yes vote extends the tax for four years, with yearly increases of up to $4.
A no vote is a vote against extending the tax.
