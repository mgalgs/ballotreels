Measure D asks the voters of this water district whether to approve $350 million in bonds for water supply, storage and water quality facilities.
The board of directors ordered this election on June 10, 2026, and the county added it to the November 3 ballot.
Measure D needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The measure's text lists three projects: replacing a dam's outlet works and improving its spillway, a fish passage at a diversion facility, and wells to form a barrier against seawater intrusion.
The money could also pay for other district water conservation, storage and water quality facilities.
The bond money could not pay administrator salaries or other operating costs unless they relate to construction.
A district financial committee would oversee the spending, and the district would audit the bond funds every year.
The measure estimates a tax of $24 per $100,000 of assessed value, raising about $23 million a year on average, through 2057.

A yes vote authorizes the district to issue up to $350 million in bonds.
A no vote does not authorize the bonds.
