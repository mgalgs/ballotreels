Rebuttal to Argument Against Measure ☒

[Pursuant to §9285: Maximum 250 words]

Design-build construction has been a tried and proven bid process in California for more than thirty years. Unifying design and construction under a single contractor improves efficiency and communication, speeding project delivery and improving quality.

The opposition says “If it ain't broke, don't fix it”. But the current system is broken. That’s why City staff proposed allowing design-build contracting. They understand what the current system costs us all in money, time, and litigation. And that’s why Senate Bill 935 (R-Choi) passed with unanimous bipartisan support to permanently authorize local agencies to use design-build, “which has streamlined project delivery, reduced costs, and encouraged innovation in public works for decades.”

The opposition’s remaining arguments are also misleading. They claim that design-build will lead to “lowball” contract submissions, but that’s the problem with the current “low-bid” system, which primarily evaluates proposals on cost. They cite the Belmont Learning Center, but LAUSD chose to site BLC on an active earthquake fault and former oil field (www.presstelegram.com/2008/04/10/a-400-million-lesson/). They cite Jose Huizar's corruption conviction, but he was jailed for dealings with private developers, not city contractors (https://abc7.com/post/disgraced-former-la-city-councilman-jose-huizar-due-to-begin-prison-sentence/15401128/). These arguments aren't just wrong—they’re irrelevant.

Sunnyvale’s professional city staff recommended bringing the option of design-build construction to you, the voters. The independent, citizen-led Charter Review Committee and your elected City Council unanimously agreed. Sunnyvale residents deserve the best value and efficiency, not just the lowest bid.

Learn more at www.YES-on-GHI.com, and vote YES on Measure G.
