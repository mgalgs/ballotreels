Measure G replaces objective “low-bid-wins” with subjective "best-value" scoring, handing Council members the power to handpick contractors.  Campaign donors get a direct track to city contracts leading inevitably to inflated contracts.  Taxpayer dollars funneled to political donors turning $Thousand campaign contributions into $Million contracts. Donors expect a return on their investment.

- California electric utilities donated $200 Million to political campaigns leading to 2nd highest electricity rates in the US.
- Apartment owners and construction PACs consistently funnel campaign contributions to City Council candidates. Even candidates running unopposed collect $Thousands!
- Audits heavily criticized California High-Speed Rail Authority for using “design-build”. Costs for initial contract segments doubled!
- San Francisco Bay Bridge "Loma Prieta Earthquake" rebuild was to be $1.3Billion over 4-7 years, instead cost $6.5 Billion (500% increase!) taking 11 years!

Measure G removes vital checks on public spending.

Let's keep corruption illegal in Sunnyvale!
