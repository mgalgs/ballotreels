NO on G!  California makes it legal but it's a terrible idea!

Purposely vague Measure G would allow “design-build” and “best value” to be used instead of competitive bidding for the design and construction of City buildings.  Sunnyvale currently has one bid for design - another bid for the construction.  Low bid wins.

- “Design-build” makes designer and builder the same, incentivizing expensive design. The independent designer who normally protects the public by flagging problems, preventing corner-cutting is gone. When the builder oversees themselves, taxpayers bear the risk.
- Measure G allows contracts awarded on subjective “best value” scoring instead of “low-bid”. Los Angeles saw the consequences: Councilmember José Huizar pleaded guilty to accepting $1.5 million in bribes benefiting developers, involving $Billions in city projects because of “best value” procurement. The Los Angeles District Attorney warned subjective “best value” evaluations used in “design-build” can enable favoritism, undermining public trust.
- “Design-build” incentivizes contractors to “lowball” projects knowing “unforeseen” changes will increase costs - which taxpayers will pay.  City Council members inexperienced with building construction go along with whatever the contractor tells them is “unexpectedly” needed.
- Belmont Learning Center: a Los Angeles Unified School District “design-build” project doubled in cost, rising from $150 million to over $300 million, one of the most expensive high schools in U.S. history. Environmental hazards were missed, oversight broke down; investigations found “design-build” contributed to the disaster.

Sunnyvale staff in a 2016 report acknowledged that “design-build” raises conflict-of-interest concerns. The developer claimed it “minimizes change orders”,  but that came from the contractor(!), who benefits from lack of competition.

More examples and discussion at:

https://meetingthetwain.blogspot.com/2026/08/2026-against-sunnyvale-measure-g.html

Keep Sunnyvale’s competitive system to protect residents!

If it ain’t broke, don’t fix it!

NO on Measure G.
