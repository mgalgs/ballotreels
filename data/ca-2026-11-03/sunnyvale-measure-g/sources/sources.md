## Ballot question (draft resolution, p. 2)

> Shall Section 1309 of the City Charter be amended to allow the City Council to adopt an ordinance giving the City more options for delivery of public works construction projects by authorizing use of any purchasing or contracting method allowed by California law to select contractors for such projects, and to make clarifying changes to the wording of Section 1309 that do not alter the meaning of the section?

The resolution says the measure is to appear "in substantially the following form", so the printed ballot label could differ; not confirmed against the ROV ballot.

## Full text

Exhibit A (pp. 7–8), "Proposed Charter Amendments (Contracts on Public Works)". It adds an opening qualifier to §1309(a), "Unless an alternative project delivery method is authorized as provided in this section", and a new subsection (c):

> The use of best value alternative project delivery methods including, but not limited to, progressive design-build, construction manager at risk, fixed-price design-build, and design-build-operate contracts is authorized for all public works projects. The City Council shall establish, by ordinance, regulations for the award, use, evaluation, and approval of these contracts. (p. 8)

The existing lowest-bidder rule in §1309(a) stays: projects "shall be let to the lowest responsive and responsible bidder after notice of publication in the official newspaper" (p. 7). (pdftotext does not preserve the underline/strikeout markup, so check the PDF for exact wording changes.)

Recitals (p. 1): the Charter Review Committee "held sixteen public meetings between February and December 2025"; on May 19, 2026 the Council "directed staff to return to Council with all materials necessary to place three measures to amend the Charter".

Adoption: Legistar history, 2026-06-23, "approved with modifications": motion (Mehlinger/Klein) "to adopt three Resolutions calling a General Municipal Election ... with the following modification: - Authorize Mayor Klein and Vice Mayor Mehlinger to prepare and submit arguments in support of the ballot measures and rebuttals to an arguments against the measures." The roll-call vote text is cut off in the API record.
