Measure G asks the voters of this city whether to amend the city charter to allow more ways of hiring contractors for public works projects.
Today the charter requires design-bid-build: one contract to design a project, then a separate construction contract to the lowest responsive and responsible bidder.
The charter allows no other method, even where state law permits one.
Measure G would let the council use any contracting method state law allows, and set rules for those contracts by ordinance.
In design-build, one contractor both designs and builds a project, and is chosen by a process that can weigh more than the lowest bid.
The city attorney's impartial analysis does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Supporters say having one team design and build a project cuts errors, change orders and budget overruns, and finishes projects faster.
Opponents say design-build removes the independent designer who protects the public, and allows subjective best value scoring instead of low bids.
A yes vote lets the council authorize other ways of delivering public works projects.
A no vote adds no such options, and leaves the charter's wording as it is.
