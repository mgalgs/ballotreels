## Ballot question

City page and resolution Section 3 (p. 6) give the same text:

> To protect City of Concord services/ensure smaller businesses pay less than larger businesses, shall the measure be adopted to: repair potholes on neighborhood and major streets; maintain 9-1-1 emergency response; keep parks/playgrounds safe/clean; relieve traffic congestion along major corridors; maintain senior/youth programs; for general government use; by updating Concord’s 37-year-old business license rates to 0.1% of gross receipts until ended by voters, providing an additional $2,900,000 annually, requiring independent citizen oversight?

## Who votes on it

- City page: "Who can vote? Registered voters residing within the City of Concord."
- Resolution p. 6: "This question requires the approval of a majority of qualified electors casting votes."

## Key statements (resolution and staff report — the City's own documents, not an impartial analysis)

- Resolution p. 4: "the ordinance will change the existing tiered gross receipts business license tax rates to one-tenth of one percent (0.1%) of the annual gross receipts derived from certain business activity in the City of Concord and exempt from such gross receipts tax those businesses having annual gross receipts that do not exceed $5,000."
- Resolution p. 4: "the tax … will be in place until repealed by the voters of the City of Concord."
- Resolution p. 9: "PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of the City of Concord on July 28, 2026" (agenda copy; vote tallies blank).
- Staff report p. 7: "If approved by the voters, the proposed Business License Tax Simplification Measure would take effect on January 1, 2027, and is projected to generate approximately $3 million in additional annual, locally controlled revenue"
- Staff report p. 7: "Staff estimates the City's share of election costs specific to this Measure to be approximately $30,000."
