Measure O asks the voters of this city whether to replace the tiered business license tax on gross receipts with one rate, $1 for every $1,000.
Today, a business taxed on its gross receipts pays $1 per $1,000 on its first $25,000, and lower rates on each step above that.
Measure O would charge $1 per $1,000 on all gross receipts, with a $25 minimum, and exempt businesses taking in $5,000 a year or less.
City staff say it would take effect in January 2027, and it would last until voters repeal it.
Independent auditors would report each year on $2.9 million of the new money, for review by an existing citizens' oversight committee.
The City Council could amend these rules later, but not raise or extend the tax without voters.
It is a general tax, and it needs a majority of the vote to pass.
The ballot question says Measure O would provide an additional $2.9 million a year.
