CITY OF PALOS VERDES ESTATES – (1)
VOTE PASSAGE – 2/3 Votes (66.6% of Votes)
PVE    Palos Verdes Estates Law Enforcement Services and Pension Liability Payment
       Measure. Shall the measure to maintain Palos Verdes Estates Police Department
       services and to pay down the City's unfunded accrued pension liability, by extending the
       City's special parcel tax for 6 years at the new rate of $1,025 per taxable parcel plus 20¢
       per square foot of taxable building improvements, subject to 2% annual adjustment,
       generating approximately $8,700,000 annually, subject to annual audits, reporting and
       citizens' committee oversight, be adopted? Supporters: Michael Kemps Mayor; Ken
       Feldman, MD; John Nendick; Mark Saroyan,MD; Craig Quinn, Councilmember
       Opponents: None submitted.
