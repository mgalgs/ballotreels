## Ballot question and supporters/opponents (verbatim, from the county's final measures list)

> Palos Verdes Estates Law Enforcement Services and Pension Liability Payment Measure. Shall the measure to maintain Palos Verdes Estates Police Department services and to pay down the City's unfunded accrued pension liability, by extending the City's special parcel tax for 6 years at the new rate of $1,025 per taxable parcel plus 20¢ per square foot of taxable building improvements, subject to 2% annual adjustment, generating approximately $8,700,000 annually, subject to annual audits, reporting and citizens' committee oversight, be adopted?
>
> Supporters: Michael Kemps, Mayor; Ken Feldman, MD; John Nendick; Mark Saroyan, MD; Craig Quinn, Councilmember
> Opponents: None submitted.

Vote threshold: 2/3 Votes (66.6% of votes cast) — this is a special tax (county's final measures list).
