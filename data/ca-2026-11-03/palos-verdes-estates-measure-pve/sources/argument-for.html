ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE PVE

Vote YES on Measure PVE to protect the high-quality local police services that make Palos Verdes Estates one of the safest communities in California. Measure E expires June 30, 2027. This measure is supported by a broad, bipartisan group of residents, including original supporters of Measure E and neighbors on both sides of the Measure PF debate. The unified proponents support implementing reforms for cost efficiencies and diverse revenue to strengthen the City's long-term fiscal stability. This measure is a compromise between expiring Measure E and failed Measure PF, designed to address voter concerns raised:

AFFORDABILITY: A six-year measure at half the cost of Measure PF.
FISCAL STABILITY: The funds pay down the pension debt and avoids the $7 million deficit.
RETAINS OUR LOCAL PVE POLICE DEPARTMENT
DIRECTED FUNDING: All revenue is legally restricted to local police services, with $1 million every year dedicated to reducing pension debt.
NO NEW DEVELOPMENT: Funds cannot be used to construct new buildings.
ACCOUNTABILITY: Requires annual independent audits, public reporting, and oversight by the Finance Advisory Committee.
FAIR STRUCTURE: The existing 20-cent-per-square-foot rate remains unchanged. The flat base increase is the same for every parcel.
IMPACT IF THIS FAILS:
June 2027 is the last chance to avoid a $7 million annual deficit with the expiration of Measure E, which will begin depleting the City's Emergency Reserves.
If not passed, the cost will be $600,000 for a special election in March or June. The Council will be forced to consider cutting staffing, putting our safety at risk.
Once Emergency Reserves are depleted, the City would need to discuss selling assets.

A YES vote keeps our community safe, maintains current service level residents value, and helps to stabilize the City financially. Please join us in voting YES on Measure PVE.
