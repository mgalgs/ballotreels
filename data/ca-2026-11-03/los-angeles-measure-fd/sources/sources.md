# City of Los Angeles — Initiative Ordinance FD (Nov 3, 2026)

Citizen initiative: 0.5% sales tax for the Los Angeles Fire Department. Majority vote (per the RR/CC list and the Impartial Summary).

## Facts, with quotes

- **Question:** "Shall an ordinance providing funding for the Los Angeles Fire Department, including for hiring firefighters, paramedics, and other staff, purchasing and maintaining vehicles and equipment, building and upgrading fire stations, and brush clearance; by imposing a one-half percent (0.5%) sales tax in the City of Los Angeles; with annual audits and a citizen's oversight committee; generating approximately $345 million annually until ended by voters; be adopted?" (RR/CC list p. 12)
- **Who votes / threshold:** City of Los Angeles voters; RR/CC list p. 12 "Majority Vote 50%+1". Impartial Summary: "This measure would become effective if approved by a majority of voters." The ordinance text also contemplates a court challenge: "If approved by a simple majority of voters, and thereafter challenged in court of competent …" (see full text). The Argument Against and the Rebuttal to the Argument in Favor (Howard Jarvis Taxpayers Association) argue it is a special tax using a "loophole"; that is an argument, not an official finding.
- **Impartial Summary:** "This citizen-sponsored ballot initiative, if approved, would require the City to adopt an ordinance to impose a new one-half percent (0.5%) retail transactions and use tax (Sales Tax) in the City of Los Angeles." "The City's current Sales Tax rate is 10.25%. This new tax would remain in place unless repealed by the voters, or if City budget obligations are not met" "The COC would consist of five members, drawn from residents of the City." "The one-half percent sales tax proposed by this initiative would be imposed approximately 110 days after voter approval."
- **Financial Impact:** "This measure increases the City's sales tax by 0.5 percent (from 10.25 to 10.75 percent). It generates roughly $345 million annually for a special fund dedicated exclusively to support the operation and services of the Los Angeles Fire Department (LAFD)" "Should this funding level not be met, the tax cannot be levied during that fiscal year."
- **Signers:** Argument in favor — Doug Coates (President, United Firefighters of LA City), Jaime Moore (Fire Chief, LAFD), and LAFD firefighters/captains. Argument against and rebuttal to argument in favor — Howard Jarvis Taxpayers Association, Jon Coupal, President. Rebuttal to argument against — LAFD members incl. Brent Kneisler, Lucia Lima, Tanya Crabbe (Los Angeles Women in the Fire Service).
