Measure R asks the voters of this school district whether to approve a parcel tax for eight years, beginning July 1, 2027.
The tax would be charged each year by the square foot of building on a parcel.
A home would pay at most $89 a year, and other property at most $7,500.
Measure R needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The money could pay only for listed purposes, such as keeping teachers, counselors and health staff, and classes in coding and robotics.
Owners aged 65 or older who live in their home could apply for an exemption, and so could some residents with disabilities.
The ballot question says the tax would raise $1.4 million a year.
No argument against Measure R was filed, so the list of local measures from the county shows only the argument for it.
Supporters say Measure R protects strong core programs in math, science and reading, and that quality schools and classrooms protect property values.
A yes vote levies the parcel tax, with its yearly caps, for eight years beginning July 1, 2027.
A no vote does not levy the tax.
