List of Local Measures

November 3, 2026, General Election

The deadline for jurisdictions to file a resolution with our office, placing a measure question on the November 3, 2026 General Election ballot is August 7, 2026 at 5:00 p.m. Measure letters will be assigned to those resolutions received after the 5:00 p.m. deadline.

Refer to the Electronic Submittal of Primary Arguments, List of Supporters/Opponents, and Rebuttals webpage for further filing options.

Arguments For or Against a School District or Special District measure are filed with the Office of the Registrar of Voters/Candidate Services Division electronically at [email protected] or in-person at 1555 Berger Drive, Building 2, 3rd Floor, San Jose, CA 95112.

CITIES, SCHOOL DISTRICTS AND SPECIAL DISTRICTS BALLOT MEASURE FORMS
Ballot Measure Primary Argument Form [PDF]
Ballot Measure Rebuttal Argument Form (including Consent Form) [PDF]

Arguments For or Against a city measure are filed with the City Clerk of the respective City. Please reach out to the city to obtain ballot measure forms.

Arguments For or Against a Santa Clara Valley Open Space Authority measure are filed with the Santa Clara Valley Open Space Authority electronically at [email protected] or in-person at 33 Las Colinas Lane, San José, CA 95119.

Arguments For or Against a Midpeninsula Regional Open Space District measure are filed with the Midpeninsula Regional Open Space District electronically at [email protected] or in-person at 5050 El Camino Real, Los Altos, CA 94022.

MIDPENINSULA REGIONAL OPEN SPACE DISTRICT BALLOT MEASURE FORMS
Ballot Measure Primary Argument Form - MROSD [PDF]
Ballot Measure Rebuttal Argument Form (including Consent Form) - MROSD [PDF]

Download the "Measure Information Guide for County, School Districts and Special Districts" calling a measure election.​​​

Arguments and their submission process must comply with all rules. The deadlines for filing arguments for a School District, City or Special District measure on the November 3, 2026 General Election ballot are as follows:

Primary Arguments in Favor of or Against a Measure
August 11, 2026 at 5:00 p.m. (12:00 p.m. for City of San Jose)
Examination Period: August 12 - August 21 by 5:00 p.m.

Rebuttal Arguments to Arguments in Favor of and Against a Measure, and Impartial Analyses
August 18, 2026 at 5:00 p.m. (12:00 p.m. for City of San Jose)
Examination Period: August 19 - August 28 by 5:00 p.m.

FINAL PRINTING DEADLINE: August 28 by 5:00 p.m. is the deadline for any petitions for writ of mandate, including any appeals. Petitions and appeals should be resolved by this date so the Registrar of Voters can meet necessary ballot and county voter information guide printing deadlines.
