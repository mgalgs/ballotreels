ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE AT

VOTE NO ON MEASURE AT — DON'T PUT INGLEWOOD JOBS AND CITY SERVICES AT RISK

Inglewood has come a long way. Today, we're America's premier entertainment destination. SoFi Stadium, Intuit Dome and the Kia Forum bring millions of visitors to our city, supporting thousands of jobs and customers for local restaurants, hotels and small businesses.

But Inglewood's economic resurgence is still gaining momentum. Measure AT puts that progress — and City revenues — at risk.

Supporters claim raising ticket taxes will automatically mean more money for the city.

But higher taxes could DRAMATICALLY REDUCE City revenue.

It's true that the stadiums aren't going anywhere. But the events can.

Consider what just one blockbuster concert run means to our economy: six recent shows at SoFi Stadium generated an estimated $320 million in economic activity, supported 3,300 jobs and represented nearly $10 million in admissions tax revenue. Lose even one major concert like that to the LA Coliseum or Rose Bowl where neighboring cities are offering tax incentives—not tax increases—then Inglewood loses tax revenue and local restaurants, hotels and small businesses lose customers and workers lose their jobs.

That means less money for police, firefighters and essential services.

Inglewood already has one of the highest admissions taxes in the nation. The real problem is City Hall's wasteful spending. With all this new revenue, residents still see potholes, neglected streets, shuttered schools and basic neighborhood needs suffer.

The answer isn't a massive tax increase that drives business away. It's making City Hall more TRANSPARENT and ACCOUNTABLE spending money it already collects, building more affordable housing, improving the quality of city services and protecting the economic engine that generates those revenues.

Don't risk Inglewood jobs and small businesses because the mayor can't manage a budget.

Protect Inglewood's progress and our pocketbook.

VOTE NO ON MEASURE AT.
