Measure AT asks the voters of this city whether to change the tax on admission to large venues.
Measure AT is a citizens' initiative, on the ballot after its petition was certified.
Today, the admissions tax has an annual cap on the revenue it brings in.
Measure AT would set tiered tax rates of 10 percent and 2.5 percent, based on how many seats a venue has.
Measure AT would also remove the annual cap on admissions tax revenue.
Measure AT needs a majority yes vote to pass.
The ballot question says the measure would increase general fund revenues, but gives no amount.
Opponents say higher taxes could drive events and revenue away, risking jobs and services, and that the real problem is city spending.
The official county list of measures, which names who filed each argument, says no argument in favor of Measure AT was submitted.
A yes vote approves the ordinance changing the admissions tax on large venues.
A no vote rejects the ordinance changing the admissions tax on large venues.
