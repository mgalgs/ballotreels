Measure P asks the voters of this city whether to raise the local sales tax from 1 percent to 1.5 percent, and keep it until ended by voters.
The city has a general sales tax of 1 percent, which expires on April 1, 2029.
Measure P would raise the rate to 1.5 percent.
The tax would no longer expire in 2029, and would remain in effect until ended by voters.
As a general tax, the money would go to the general fund, and a community committee would recommend how to spend it.
Measure P needs a simple majority to pass.
The City Council resolution says the increase would provide about $1.2 million a year, and the total tax about $3.6 million.
Any argument against Measure P is filed with the city clerk, and is not online.
Any argument for Measure P is filed with the city clerk, and is not online.
A yes vote raises the sales tax to 1.5 percent until ended by voters.
A no vote keeps the 1 percent tax, which would expire in 2029.
