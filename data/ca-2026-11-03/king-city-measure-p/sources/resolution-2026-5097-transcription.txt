RESOLUTION NO. 2026-5097

A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF KING CITY, CALIFORNIA, SUBMITTING TO THE ELECTORS A BALLOT MEASURE REAUTHORIZING THE CITY'S EXISTING LOCALLY CONTROLLED TRANSACTIONS AND USE TAX AT A RATE OF 1.5% (1 ½ CENTS) AND EXTENDING THE DURATION THEREOF UNTIL IT IS ENDED BY VOTERS; APPROVING THE RELATED ORDINANCE; [...]

[Page 2]

[...]

WHEREAS, the City of King City ("City") currently has a 1% transactions and use (sales) tax pursuant to Chapter 3.22 of Title 3 of the King City Municipal Code, which is a general tax and expires on April 1, 2029; and

[...]

WHEREAS, pursuant to Elections Code Section 9222, the City Council desires to submit to the voters at the general municipal election to be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, a question relating to a transactions and use (sales) tax, which will reauthorize the City's transactions and use (sales) tax by increasing the City's transactions and use (sales) tax rate by 0.5% so that the total tax rate is 1.5% (1 ½ cents), where such increase will provide approximately $1,200,000 annually and where the total tax rate will provide approximately $3,600,000 annually, and extending the duration of such transactions and use tax until it is ended by voters.

[Page 3]

2. Submission of Measure. Pursuant to the laws of the State of California, including, but not limited to, Elections Code Sections 9222 and 10201, the City Council, hereby orders the submission to the qualified voters of the City of King City at the General Municipal Election to be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, a measure that, if approved, reauthorizes the City's existing transactions and use (sales) tax by increasing the tax rate thereof by 0.5% for a total of rate of 1.5% and extending the duration of such tax until it is ended by voters ("Measure"). The Measure shall be entitled the "King City Public Safety, Local Services Measure". The abbreviated form of the Measure to appear on the ballot is specified below in Section 3.

3. Ballot Language. The ballot language for the proposed Measure shall be as follows:

King City Public Safety, Local Services Measure. Shall the measure continuing King City's locally controlled funding for general city services, such as maintaining 911 fire, medical, police response, gang/violent crime prevention/investigation, and King City's financial stability; providing firefighter protective clothing/ breathing equipment; repairing streets/ potholes, by reauthorizing its sales tax at a rate of 1.5¢, providing approximately $3,600,000 annually until ended by voters; requiring audits, public disclosure, citizens' oversight, all funds benefit King City residents, be adopted?    YES    NO

ORDINANCE NO. 2026-844

AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF KING CITY, CALIFORNIA, AMENDING CHAPTER 3.22 OF TITLE 3 OF THE KING CITY MUNICIPAL CODE IN ITS ENTIRETY BY REAUTHORIZING THE EXISTING CITY'S TRANSACTIONS AND USE TAX BY INCREASING THE TOTAL TAX RATE TO 1.5% AND EXTENDING THE DURATION OF SUCH TAX UNTIL IT IS ENDED BY VOTERS

[Page 17]

Section 3.22.160. – Termination Date.

The taxes imposed by this chapter shall remain in effect until it is ended by voters.

Section 3.22.170. – General Tax.

The proceeds from the tax imposed by this chapter shall be for unrestricted general revenue purposes of the City and shall be placed into the General Fund of the City.

[Page 18]

Section 3.22.200. – Citizen Oversight.

Following the operative date, the City shall establish a community oversight advisory committee, which may be its budget advisory committee or a separate committee. The committee shall be charged with making recommendations with respect to expenditures of the transactions and use tax as part of the overall annual budget process. The recommendations of the committee shall be presented to the city council but shall not be binding on the city council.
