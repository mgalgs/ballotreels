## Quoted facts

- **Letter / placement** (city news): "On July 28, 2026, the City Council unanimously placed Measure P on the November 2026 Municipal Election ballot. Measure P is a proposed reauthorization of the city's existing voter-approved sales tax at a rate of one and a half cents (1.5¢ per dollar) that would generate $3.6 million annually".
- **Current tax** (resolution p. 2): "the City of King City ("City") currently has a 1% transactions and use (sales) tax pursuant to Chapter 3.22 of Title 3 of the King City Municipal Code, which is a general tax and expires on April 1, 2029".
- **What changes** (resolution p. 2): the question "will reauthorize the City's transactions and use (sales) tax by increasing the City's transactions and use (sales) tax by 0.5% so that the total tax rate is 1.5% (1 ½ cents), where such increase will provide approximately $1,200,000 annually and where the total tax rate will provide approximately $3,600,000 annually, and extending the duration of such transactions and use tax until it is ended by voters."
  - Note: the ballot question says "reauthorizing its sales tax at a rate of 1.5¢"; the resolution makes clear this is an increase from 1% plus an extension.
- **Threshold** (resolution p. 2): Revenue and Taxation Code 7285.9 "authorizes a city to adopt a transactions and use tax ordinance, which shall be operative if a majority of the electors voting on the measure vote to approve the imposition of the tax".
- **Accountability** (city news): "public disclosure of all spending, oversight by an independent commission of residents, and independent audits."
