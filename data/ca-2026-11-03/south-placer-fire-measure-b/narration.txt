South Placer Fire Protection District Measure B asks the district's voters whether to replace its fire taxes and assessment with one $375 yearly tax on each single-family parcel.
Today, the district charges an assessment and a special tax in its Loomis area, and a special tax in its Granite Bay area.
Measure B needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
From 2027-2028, the new tax would replace those charges until voters repeal it, and the board could raise it yearly with inflation.
The money could pay for firefighting, emergency medical service, equipment, staff and facilities.
The measure expects the tax to raise about $5.75 million a year.
Supporters say Measure B helps reopen two closed fire stations, and replaces an outdated patchwork of funding with one districtwide measure.
Opponents say Granite Bay would pay $305 more a parcel and Loomis would pay less, when the entire district should pay.
A yes vote is a vote to authorize the special tax.
A no vote is a vote against the proposed special tax.
