                                       ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE B
Measure B is an investment in our community’s safety.
South Placer Fire District keeps local families, businesses and neighborhoods safe every day. But the costs of providing
fire protection and emergency medical services have outpaced the District’s current funding system.
The warning signs are already visible. Two Granite Bay fire stations have been closed since 2022. Positions have remained
vacant for months, essential equipment maintenance has been deferred, and the cost of a new fire engine has more than
doubled, from $680,000 in 2021 to $1.5 million in 2025.
Measure B will support reopening two strategically located fire stations, bringing fire fighters, emergency medical responders
and critical response equipment closer to the people and businesses they serve.
Measure B will also help maintain essential equipment, recruit and retain experienced emergency responders, strengthen
firefighting capabilities, and support reliable fire and emergency medical response across the District.
Measure B replaces an outdated patchwork of funding mechanisms with one consistent districtwide funding measure
dedicated to fire and emergency medical services within South Placer Fire District. Funding from Measure B will stay local
and cannot be used by other local, county, state or federal agencies.
Without sustainable funding, SPFD faces deeper service reductions, delayed equipment maintenance and replacement,
and ongoing vacancies in critical emergency response positions.
Vote Yes on Measure B to protect local fire and emergency medical services and keep South Placer Fire District
“Ready to Respond.”
Signed,
Edward Horton, Retired Loomis Fire Chief
Diane Means, Director, Placer West Fire Safe Council
Randolph Elder, CFP, Chair, Loomis Fire Assessor District

                          REBUTTAL TO THE ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE B
We don’t dispute any of the reasons this ballot measure is needed; those are the same reasons this should be funded by
BOTH divisions of South Placer Fire District. If there is a Districtwide need for funding, the entire District should pay, not
just Granite Bay.
Measure B will reduce the current taxes paid by all Loomis residents, but increase the special tax of Granite Bay Division
residents by $305 per parcel resulting in a $3 million increase in funding that will all come from Granite Bay Division. That’s
on top of the $10 MILLION in property taxes Granite Bay already pays to South Placer Fire District. The Measure B special
tax will cost more every year with its automatic cost-of-living (COLA) increase that has no rate cap.
Measure B funds will be used to maintain equipment, attract and retain experienced emergency responders, and support
fire and emergency medical response across the District. If South Placer Fire District can do that in Loomis for no extra cost
to Loomis, why does Granite Bay have to pay an additional $3 million per year for the same services?
District revenues have already increased enough to open our closed fire stations. When those stations closed in 2022, the
revenue of the District was about $14 million. As of June 30, 2025, the revenue is more than $18 million. South Placer Fire
District’s website shows the cost to run one station is about $2 million; those stations should already be open.
The increasing costs of South Placer Fire District should be shared by both divisions. Please vote NO on Measure B until
there is a more equitable solution.
Signed,
Diane Will, B.S., M.S. Accounting Degrees
Sandra Harris, Granite Bay Community Advocate
Jeffrey Moss, General Contractor
Anita Wright, Retired Budget Financial Analyst

                                          ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE B
If there is a Districtwide need for funding, the entire District should pay, not just Granite Bay.
This Ballot Measure increases the special tax currently paid by Granite Bay Division residents by $305 per parcel, yet
reduces the amount paid by all Loomis residents. Since Loomis taxes will be going down, that means the entire amount of
this funding measure will be coming solely from Granite Bay; and that’s on top of the $10 MILLION in property taxes Granite
Bay Division already pays to South Placer Fire District.
Loomis residents pay approximately $413 per parcel in special taxes and fees compared to the $70 per parcel Granite Bay
residents are paying. But that’s not a complete picture. The major source of funding for South Placer Fire District is property
taxes.
In addition to the special taxes noted above, a portion of the base amount of your property taxes is paid to South Placer
Fire District (Placer County Tax Distribution Reports). The reports show, for Granite Bay Division, more than $10 MILLION
dollars in property taxes is paid to South Placer Fire District. For Loomis residents, only $500,000 in property taxes goes to
South Placer Fire District. That’s it; for the entire population of the Loomis Division. And that’s not even enough to run one
fire station. That’s why their assessments are so high.
With this ballot measure the District says “everyone will pay the same amount.” But that’s not true, because Granite Bay
will still pay $10 million more in property taxes to South Placer Fire District.
To recap, Loomis property taxes provide just $500,000 to SPFD and Granite Bay property taxes provide $10 MILLION. This
ballot measure asks Granite Bay Division alone to pay $3 million MORE; Loomis will pay less than they’re paying now.
We support our firefighters and South Placer Fire District but the increasing costs of the District should be shared by both
divisions, not just Granite Bay. Please vote NO until there is a more equitable solution.

                              REBUTTAL TO THE ARGUMENT AGAINST MEASURE B
The argument against Measure B is flat-out wrong!
As long-time homeowners, the signers opposing Measure B enjoy significant Prop 13 protections which means they
contribute very little to the Fire District’s revenues.
Measure B makes the special fire tax FAIR AND EQUITABLE ACROSS THE ENTIRE FIRE DISTRICT. Beyond base Prop
13 property taxes, EVERYONE WILL PAY THE EXACT SAME FIRE TAX for emergency services. Everyone deserves
dependable protection, regardless of where they live or how long they have lived here.
We are ONE Fire District serving ONE community. Emergency services are provided based on need; medical and
emergency responders don’t ask if an emergency is in Loomis or Granite Bay.
The opponents confuse two issues: how Prop 13 taxes are distributed, and the additional revenue needed to prevent fire
station closures.
Prop 13 tax distribution was established by the State Legislature in 1979—not the Fire District. Measure B addresses what
we CAN control: creating a stable, LOCALLY-CONTROLLED funding source with citizen oversight for South Placer Fire
District.
The issue is simple: revenues have not kept pace with rising operational costs.
Measure B provides dedicated funding to maintain reliable emergency services. It keeps solutions LOCAL, makes funding
FAIR AND EQUITABLE, and protects life-saving response times.
Help prevent future fire station closures and help avoid choices that further reduce service levels when seconds count.
Vote YES on Measure B!
