## Key facts, quoted

- **Ballot question** (MB1): "To reopen closed fire stations; improve response times/firefighting capabilities; and attract/retain experienced emergency medical responders, shall South Placer Fire Protection District repeal two parcel taxes, require its Board to repeal an assessment, and establish a single, uniform $375 annual parcel tax on single-family parcels, and other parcels as specified, to generate approximately $5,750,000 annually until ended by voters, with considerations for low-income residents, disclosures, audits and citizen oversight, be adopted?" (The county list PDF prints "To reopen closed fire stations:" with a colon; the measure PDF has a semicolon.)
- **Placed by / threshold:** "This measure was placed on the ballot by the governing board of the District through adoption of Resolution No. 14-2025/2026 on May 19, 2026. Approval by two-thirds of the voters voting on the measure is required for its adoption."
- **Tax:** "This measure would authorize the South Placer Fire Protection District ("District") to levy a special tax on taxable real property within the District beginning with the 2027-28 fiscal year. The special tax would continue to be levied annually unless subsequently repealed or amended by the voters."
- **Rates:** "maximum annual tax rates of $375 per parcel for single-family residential property; $187.50 per dwelling unit for multifamily residential property; $187.50 per mobile home pad in mobile home parks; $0.25 per square foot for most nonresidential structures; and $0.50 per square foot for nonresidential structures classified by the Fire Chief as high or severe hazard occupancies."
- **Replaces:** "The measure also repeals and replaces the District's existing special taxes and directs the District's Board of Directors to repeal any existing fire suppression assessments levied by the District."
- **Inflation:** "Beginning with the 2028-29 fiscal year, the Board of Directors may annually increase the tax by an amount not exceeding the annual percentage change in the California Consumer Price Index."
- **Uses:** "fire protection, fire prevention, and emergency medical services, acquisition, operation and maintenance of fire suppression equipment, personnel costs, and capital improvements."
- **Yes/No:** "A "YES" vote on this Measure is a vote to authorize the special tax described in the measure." / "A "NO" vote on this Measure is a vote against the proposed special tax."
- **Analysis signed:** "Clayton T. Cook, County Counsel".
