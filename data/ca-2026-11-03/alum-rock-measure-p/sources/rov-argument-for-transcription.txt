[Original page 2]

Alum Rock Union School District
Argument in Favor of Measure P
School Facilities Repair Measure

Vote Yes on Measure P!

Our schools are more than just buildings—they are the heart of our hardworking community. They educate our children, prepare them for future success, boost our property values, and support local businesses. By investing in our schools, we are investing in our community's future.

Many Alum Rock Union Elementary schools are more than 60 years old and urgently need repairs. Vote YES on Measure P for safe, functional classrooms with funds that are spent locally and cannot be taken by the State or Federal government.

With NO increase in your current tax rate, your YES vote on Measure P will fund:

• Repairing and replacing leaky roofs
• Replacing old, inefficient heating, ventilation, filtration and air-conditioning systems
• Updating fire alarms and emergency communication systems
• Upgrading kitchens and equipment for healthy lunches
• Modernizing aging restrooms
• Improving student safety and campus security
• Fixing leaking and clogged plumbing systems and deteriorating sewer systems

[Original page 3]

Measure P also offers significant taxpayer protections:

• Guaranteed Local Investment: By law, Measure P's funds cannot be taken by the State or Federal government and spent elsewhere.
• Transparency: Public disclosure of all spending is required, ensuring responsible use of funds.
• Accountability: An independent citizens' bond oversight committee and third-party auditor will review all spending.
• Funds for Projects Only: No funds for administrators' salaries, pensions, or benefits.
• State Matching Funds: Measure P may qualify Alum Rock Union School District for additional State matching funds.

Your YES vote ensures Alum Rock students have safe, modern schools while protecting the value of our homes and businesses. Join teachers, parents, guardians, grandparents, neighbors, business owners, and community leaders in voting YES on Measure P!
