# Measure P — Alum Rock Union Elementary School District — General obligation bond, $100,000,000

- **Letter:** P, confirmed by the district's news post "Alum Rock November 3, 2026 Ballot Measures" ("Measure P General Obligation Bond - Improve our Facilities") and its Measure P FAQ.
- **Jurisdiction:** Alum Rock Union Elementary School District.
- **Kind:** school facilities general obligation bond (Prop. 39), placed by the Board of Trustees.
- **Who votes:** voters of the District (resolution p. 2, §3: the election "shall be held solely within the boundaries of" the District).
- **Threshold:** Resolution p. 3, §5: "The vote passage requirement for the measure is least 55 percent of the voters voting on the measure." (sic)
- **Status: agenda version.** Resolution No. 2-26/27, "FINAL FOR AGENDA" file posted under the July 16, 2026 board meeting. The signature block (p. 6) says "adopted ... on July 16, 2026" but the vote lines are blank and it is unsigned. Signed resolution not found.

## Ballot question (resolution p. 13, Appendix B "ABBREVIATED FORM OF BOND MEASURE")

> With funds that cannot be taken by the State or Federal government, shall Alum Rock Union Elementary School District's measure to repair leaky roofs and plumbing; replace heating, ventilation, and air conditioning; and upgrade emergency communication and security systems be adopted, authorizing $100 million of bonds with legal rates, without increasing current tax rates, average levies below $26 per $100,000 of assessed valuation (raising $6.3 million annually while outstanding), audits, independent oversight, and disclosure of all spending?

Agenda version; not confirmed against the printed ballot.

## Full text (Appendix A, pp. 7–12)

> By approval of this Measure by at least 55 percent of the registered voters voting on the ... (p. 7) ... authorized to issue and sell bonds of up to $100,000,000 in aggregate principal amount

Resolution p. 5 (estimated tax effect): "it is currently expected that the bonds issued pursuant to this measure will not increase the current cumulative tax rate supporting District bonds above the 2025-26 tax rate of $97.80 per $100,000 assessed value. Note that such bonds will extend the existing tax rate for additional years." It adds that the bonds "are secured by an ad valorem tax unlimited as to rate or amount."

FAQ (informational): "If approved by at least 55% of votes cast, Measure P will extend but not increase the current tax rate, and will authorize $100 million in bonds".

## Tax rate statement (Appendix C, p. 14; "The Tax Rate Statement to be printed in the voter guide starts below the line.")

> 1. The best estimate of the average annual tax rate which would be required to be levied to fund this bond issue over the entire duration of the bond debt service, based on a projection of assessed valuations available at the time of filing of this statement, is $25.52 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. The final fiscal year in which the tax is anticipated to be collected is 2053-54.

> 2. The best estimate of the highest tax rate which would be required to be levied to fund this bond issue, based on a projection of assessed valuations available at the time of filing of this statement, is $30.00 per $100,000 of assessed value. It is estimated that such rate would be levied starting in fiscal year 2033-34 and following.

> 3. The best estimate of the total debt service, including the principal and interest, that would be required to be repaid if all the bonds are issued and sold is approximately $170 million.
