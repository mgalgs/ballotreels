Measure AB asks the voters of this community college district whether to approve $830 million in college bonds.
The district would sell the bonds over time and repay them from property taxes in the district.
Measure AB is on the ballot in three counties, and needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money could upgrade classrooms, labs and equipment, modernize training facilities and repair leaky roofs.
The district's tax rate statement estimates an average tax of $22 per $100,000 of assessed value, and at most $25, until 2058-2059.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $1.76 billion.
Supporters say Measure AB trains local nurses, firefighters and skilled workers, and replaces expiring bonds without raising tax rates.
Opponents say the district has not shown how it came up with $830 million, and interest greatly raises the payback.
A yes vote will authorize the district to issue the bonds and levy taxes to repay them.
A no vote will not authorize the bonds or the taxes to repay them.
