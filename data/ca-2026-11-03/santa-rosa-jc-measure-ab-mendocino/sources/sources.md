## Facts and quotes

**Ballot question** (quoted in the guide pages, from p1): "Without increasing tax rates, to improve facilities to train students for careers in nursing/firefighting/first responders, building trades, agriculture, auto mechanics; attract/retain high quality faculty, prepare students for universities; repair leaky roofs; repair, construct, and acquire classrooms/sites/facilities/equipment, shall the Sonoma County Junior College District measure be adopted authorizing $830,000,000 in bonds at legal rates, levying $25 per $100,000 of assessed valuation ($55,300,000 annually), while bonds are outstanding, and with oversight/audits/local control?"

**Who votes (analysis):** "The Sonoma County Junior College District ("District") Board of Trustees ("Board") has placed on the ballot in Sonoma, Marin, and Mendocino Counties the question of whether to issue bonds in the amount of $830,000,000".

**Impartial analysis:** same text as the Sonoma guide, signed by Joshua A. Myers (Sonoma County Counsel, by Adam Brand), Katharine Elliott (Interim County Counsel, County of Mendocino, by Brina A. Blanton) and Brandon Halter (Marin County Counsel). Key line: "If all bonds are sold, the total debt services, including principal and interest, is estimated at $1,760,000,000."
