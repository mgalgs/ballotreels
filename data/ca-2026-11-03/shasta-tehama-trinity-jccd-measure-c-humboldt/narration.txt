Measure C asks the voters of this community college district whether to approve $129,630,000 in college bonds.
The district would sell the bonds and repay them from property taxes.
Measure C needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money could go to projects such as upgrading classrooms and career training facilities, a regional training center, and better disabled access.
The district's tax rate statement estimates $7.76 a year per $100,000 of assessed value, on average, through 2060-2061.
The statement estimates the highest rate at $15.29 per $100,000, from 2031-2032 through 2042-2043.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $264.8 million.
Supporters say Measure C would upgrade aging buildings, including main campus classrooms almost 60 years old, without increasing tax rates.
No argument against Measure C was filed, so the county voter guide prints only the argument in favor of it.
A yes vote is to authorize the district to issue the bonds.
A no vote is against authorizing the bonds.
