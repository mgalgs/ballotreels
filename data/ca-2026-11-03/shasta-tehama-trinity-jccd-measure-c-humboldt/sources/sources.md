## Facts with quotes

- **Ballot question (packet p. 13, Appendix B; identical in Trinity, Lassen and Modoc materials):** "To improve college facilities serving students, veterans, and residents; renovate job training spaces including welding, public safety, agriculture and nursing; make accessibility improvements for the disabled; and acquire, construct, and renovate classrooms/ buildings; shall Shasta-Tehama-Trinity Joint Community College District's measure be adopted authorizing $129,630,000 of bonds at legal rates, estimated levies averaging $7.76 per $100,000 assessed value (raising $7,800,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding with annual audits, independent citizens' oversight committee, and all funds remaining local?"
- **Humboldt guide heading error:** the guide headers on pp. 11–12 read "SHASTA-TEHAMA-TRINITY JOINT COMMUNITY COLLEGE DISTRICT / SPECIAL UTILITIES IMPROVEMENT AND OPERATIONS PARCEL TAX MEASURE", while the analysis beneath describes "the general obligation bond measure". Quote the analysis, not the heading.
- **Analysis (guide p. 11):** "If fifty-five percent (55%) of the qualified electors voting on Measure C vote for approval, the District's Board of Trustees would be authorized to issue bonds in the principal amount not to exceed $129,630,000 and to levy ad valorem taxes on taxable property within the District to pay for the amount of bonded indebtedness plus interest."
