Measure C asks the voters of this city whether to raise the tax on hotel and motel stays from 10 percent to 12 percent.
The City Council placed Measure C on the ballot; as a general tax, it needs a simple majority to pass.
Today, hotel guests pay a 10 percent tax on the rent for a room, a rate that has not risen since 1983.
The rate would rise to 12 percent on January 1, 2027, paid only by hotel guests, who are mostly visitors.
The money would go into the general fund for local city services, with a yearly financial report, until voters end the tax.
The impartial analysis says the city estimates the increase would bring in $3 million a year in added revenue.
The official county list of measures for this election says that no argument against Measure C was submitted, by any group or person.
Supporters say visitors would help pay the cost of the city services they use, and the money stays in the city for local priorities.
A yes vote is a vote for the 2 percent increase in the hotel tax.
A no vote is a vote against an increase in the hotel tax.
