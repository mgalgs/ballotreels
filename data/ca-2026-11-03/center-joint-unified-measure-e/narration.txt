Center Joint Unified School District Measure E asks the district's voters whether to approve $197 million in school bonds.
The district would borrow the money and repay it from a tax on property in the district.
Measure E needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would replace leaky roofs, decaying pipes and failing heating and cooling systems, update science and job training classrooms, and make room for growing enrollment.
The district's tax statement estimates an average tax of $54.94 per $100,000 of assessed value, and at most $60, through 2068-2069.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $517 million.
No argument against Measure E was filed with Sacramento County Voter Registration and Elections.
Supporters say Measure E repairs aging schools and improves safety, without increasing tax rates.
A yes vote allows the district to issue up to $197 million in bonds.
A no vote does not allow the district to issue the proposed bonds.
