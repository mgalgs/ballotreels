Measure O asks the voters of this city whether to continue a three-quarters of 1 percent sales tax until March 31, 2033.
A city staff report says voters approved this tax in 2015 and extended it in 2020, until March 31, 2027.
Measure O would keep the same rate for six more years, then end on its own.
The council minutes record that it is a general tax, needing a simple majority to pass.
The ballot question calls for annual audits and review by an oversight committee the city sets up.
The ballot question says the tax raises $1.4 million a year.
No argument for Measure O appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
No argument against Measure O appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
A yes vote continues the sales tax until March 31, 2033.
A no vote lets the tax expire on March 31, 2027.
