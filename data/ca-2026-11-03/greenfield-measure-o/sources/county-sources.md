## City documents, gathered 2026-10-30

All published by the City of Greenfield. The packets are cut to the item's pages; the URL is
the whole packet's.

- council-2026-05-26-item.pdf: May 26, 2026 City Council packet, pages 101-114 of
  https://www.greenfieldca.gov/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Agenda/_05262026-870 : the City Attorney's
  staff report (history of the tax, fiscal impact), the draft ordinance extending the 0.75%
  transactions and use tax (Municipal Code chapter 3.26; it expires on the sixth anniversary of
  the operative date), and the DRAFT Resolution No. 2026-41 placing it on the ballot.
- council-minutes-2026-05-26.pdf: pages 28-34 of the June 9, 2026 packet,
  https://www.greenfieldca.gov/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Agenda/_06092026-874 : the minutes of May 26,
  2026, recording that the Council adopted Resolution #2026-41 "as amended with the addition of an
  oversight committee", and the City Attorney's statement that the measure is a general tax
  needing 50% plus 1.

The adopted resolution is not online: the draft's ballot question differs from the county's list
(the county's adds the oversight committee, the $1.4 million and the March 31, 2033 end), which
matches the amendment the minutes record. The ordinance's caption says "an Additional Three
Years" while its staff report and section 3.26.150 say six years, ending March 31, 2033, as the
ballot question says. No impartial analysis or argument was found: the county's list says only
that "All related documents are filed and on public review with the city clerk".
