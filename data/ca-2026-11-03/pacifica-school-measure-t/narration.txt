Pacifica School District Measure T asks the district's voters whether to renew its parcel tax at $118 per parcel for ten years.
Today, the district levies a parcel tax that voters renewed in 2016, which expires on June 30, 2027.
Measure T needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
The tax would continue through June 2037, rising each year with inflation.
The money could pay for academic programs, teachers, libraries and help for struggling students, but not administrator salaries.
The impartial analysis says the tax is estimated to raise $1.3 million each year.
Opponents say enrollment fell by 280 since 2021, and other district funds can pay for administration without limits.
Supporters say Measure T keeps qualified teachers, libraries and outdoor education, without raising taxes by a single penny.
A yes vote would impose the $118 parcel tax, adjusted for inflation, for ten years.
A no vote would not allow the tax to be levied, and the current one ends in June 2027.
