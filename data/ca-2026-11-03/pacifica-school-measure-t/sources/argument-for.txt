AUG 10 2026
MARK CHURCH, CHIEF ELECTIONS OFFICER

Argument in Favor of Measure _

Vote Yes on Measure _ to renew local education funding that protects excellent academic programs, including library services and outdoor education, and retains qualified teachers without raising taxes by a single penny.

Pacifica's elementary and middle schools are a source of community pride, yet they remain among the lowest-funded schools in San Mateo County.

Measure _ provides dedicated local funding for qualified teachers, librarians and programs that support student success in English, math, science and technology. It also preserves Outdoor Education for every fifth-grade student. Without Measure _, Pacifica School District would have to reduce teachers, librarians and critical educational programs.

Measure _ continues Pacifica's tradition of supporting local schools with funding that stays in our community and cannot be taken by the state or used for administrators.

Vote Yes on _: Strong Schools Build Strong Communities
- Attract and retain qualified teachers
- Enhance core academic programs
- Support struggling students
- Strengthen library services
- Maintain technology instruction
- Support outstanding art, music and electives critical to a well-rounded education

Measure _ Is Fiscally Accountable and Does Not Increase Taxes
- Every dollar stays in Pacifica schools and cannot be taken by the state
- No money can be used for administrators' salaries
- An independent Citizens' Oversight Committee has ensured responsible spending for more than two decades
- Homeowners age 65 and older remain eligible for an exemption

Our local elementary and middle schools are an integral part of our community. Please join parents, teachers and community leaders in protecting quality education, strengthening our community and investing in Pacifica's future. Vote Yes on Measure _.
