IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE [TBD]

The Board of Trustees of the Pacifica School District (District) proposes to renew and extend an expiring special parcel tax to support various educational purposes, as outlined below. The District currently has in place a parcel tax (Measure D) that will expire June 30, 2027. This Measure, if approved by voters, would extend the parcel tax for an additional ten years, starting July 1, 2027 and ending June 30, 2037, in the amount of $118 per parcel. The amount of the tax is subject to annual adjustment for inflation based on the "Consumer Price Index for all Urban Consumers San Francisco Area" published by the U.S. Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics, and is estimated to raise $1,300,000 each year. [97]

The proposed parcel tax applies to any unit of real property wholly or partially in the District that receives a separate property tax bill from San Mateo County tax collection officials. Any person 65 years of age or older who owns and occupies a parcel within the District may apply for an exemption from the tax. An exemption is also available to any person who receives Supplemental Security Income for a disability, regardless of age, and to any person who receives Social Security Disability Insurance benefits, regardless of age, whose annual income does not exceed 250% of the 2012 federal poverty guidelines. [95]

The stated purposes of the proposed parcel tax are to: [10]

- Enhance core academic programs, including math, science, history, reading, and writing;
- Support art, music, and other electives;
- Attract and retain qualified teachers and staff;
- Maintain classroom computer and technology instruction;
- Strengthen school library programs; and
- Maintain programs to support struggling students. [47]

The proceeds of the parcel tax will be placed in a special account and used only for the purposes described in the Measure, which do not include administrator salaries or benefits. A report accounting for the amount of parcel tax revenues collected and expended, and the status of any projects or programs funded by the tax, will be filed with the Board of Trustees no later than December 31 of each year, commencing December 31, 2027. The District's existing Citizens' Oversight Committee will continue to review the expenditures of the parcel tax. [88]

The District's appropriations limit shall be increased annually, if necessary, to ensure the revenue from the tax may be spent for the purposes authorized. [24]

A "yes" vote on this Measure would impose on property within the District an annual parcel tax of $118, subject to annual adjustment for inflation, for ten years beginning July 1, 2027, for the purposes listed above. [35]

A "no" vote on this Measure would not allow the parcel tax to be levied. [15]

This Measure passes if at least two-thirds of those voting on the Measure vote "yes". [15]
