# Measure T — Pacifica School District (parcel tax, $118/parcel, 10 years)

- **Jurisdiction:** Pacifica School District (elementary and middle schools).
- **Kind:** Qualified special tax (parcel tax), the "Pacifica Quality Education and Teacher Retention Measure"; renews the 2016 Measure D parcel tax.
- **Who votes:** the electors of the District. Resolution Section 2 (p. 2): the resolution orders the County Superintendent of Schools "to call an election within the boundaries of the District on November 3, 2026, for the purpose of proposing to the electors of the District the measure contained in Exhibit A hereto", and asks the Registrar of Voters "to submit to the voters of the District at said election the measure as it appears in Exhibit A."
- **Vote threshold:** two-thirds. Section 5(e) (p. 3): "The proposition shall become effective upon approval of two-thirds of those voting thereon." Impartial analysis: "This Measure passes if at least two-thirds of those voting on the Measure vote "yes"."
- **Placed by:** Board of Trustees, Resolution No. 2026-06-10-B, adopted June 10, 2026.
- **Election:** November 3, 2026.

## Ballot question

> To renew expiring local funding without increasing taxes, with local funding for elementary/middle schools the state cannot take away, to support strong math, science, history, reading/writing programs; attract/retain qualified teachers/counselors; maintain art/music/outdoor education programs, libraries and computer instruction, shall Pacifica School District's measure be adopted, levying $118 per parcel, raising $1,300,000 annually for 10 years, with senior exemptions, independent oversight and no money for administrator salaries?

(resolution-full-text.pdf p. 5, Exhibit A, "I. Abbreviation of the Measure"; read from the scan. Printed in quotation marks, removed here, followed by "[70 Words]". Exhibit B (p. 6) repeats the same wording; checked.)

## Full text (resolution)

- Resolution No. 2026-06-10-B, "RESOLUTION CALLING PARCEL TAX ELECTION", Board of Trustees of the Pacifica School District (p. 1).
- Adopted: "PASSED AND ADOPTED this 10th day of June, 2026, by the following vote: AYES: Elizabeth Bredall, Bridget Hardt, Laverne Villalobos NOES: Ø ABSTAIN: Ø ABSENT: Kai Doggett" (p. 4; names handwritten). Signatures redacted by ACRE.
- Recitals (p. 1): "on June 7, 2016, Pacifica voters renewed a school parcel tax measure ("Measure D")" and "the existing parcel tax authorized by Measure D will expire as of June 30, 2027".
- Section 5(b) (p. 3): the Registrar is asked to print "the exact wording of the Abbreviation of the Measure contained in Exhibit A-I" on the ballot and "the Full Text of the Measure contained in Exhibit A-II in the voter information pamphlet".
- Full text, key findings (p. 7): "Nationally, California places near the bottom of the 50 states in educational funding. The District is one of the lowest funded school districts in the county".
- Full text, purposes (pp. 7-8): to "Enhance core academic programs like math, science, history, reading and writing"; "Support outstanding art, music and electives"; "Attract and retain qualified teachers and staff"; "Maintain classroom computer and technology instruction"; "Strengthen school library programs"; "Maintain programs to support struggling students."
- Full text, accountability (p. 8): an annual report to the Board "no later than December 31 of each year, commencing December 31, 2027", and "The Board shall provide for the District's existing Citizens' Oversight Committee to continue to oversee the special taxes collected pursuant to the Act".

## Impartial analysis

Headed "IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE [TBD]". **No author's name or office is printed** on the page, so the author is unconfirmed from this file. Bracketed numbers are per-paragraph word counts in the original.

- "The District currently has in place a parcel tax (Measure D) that will expire June 30, 2027. This Measure, if approved by voters, would extend the parcel tax for an additional ten years, starting July 1, 2027 and ending June 30, 2037, in the amount of $118 per parcel."
- "The amount of the tax is subject to annual adjustment for inflation based on the "Consumer Price Index for all Urban Consumers San Francisco Area" published by the U.S. Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics, and is estimated to raise $1,300,000 each year."
- "Any person 65 years of age or older who owns and occupies a parcel within the District may apply for an exemption from the tax."
- "The proceeds of the parcel tax will be placed in a special account and used only for the purposes described in the Measure, which do not include administrator salaries or benefits."
- "A "yes" vote on this Measure would impose on property within the District an annual parcel tax of $118, subject to annual adjustment for inflation, for ten years beginning July 1, 2027, for the purposes listed above."
- "A "no" vote on this Measure would not allow the parcel tax to be levied."

## Tax rate, exemptions, duration (from the full text, resolution pp. 7-8)

- Terms (p. 8): "Upon approval of two-thirds of those voting on this measure, the District shall be authorized to extend the existing parcel tax (Pacifica School District Parcel Tax of 2016) for ten (10) years until 2037. The net effect of this measure would be to authorize the District to impose a total of $118 in taxes per parcel annually each year between July 1, 2027, and June 30, 2037, subject to an annual adjustment for inflation pursuant to the "Consumer Price Index for all Urban Consumers San Francisco Area" published by the U.S. Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics."
- Senior exemption (p. 8): "any parcel owned and occupied by a person 65 years of age or older shall be exempt from the Act upon proper application to the District."
- Other exemptions (p. 8): "An exemption from the Funding for the Act shall be made for persons receiving Supplemental Security Income for a disability, regardless of age upon proper application to the District. An exemption from the Act shall be made for persons receiving Social Security Disability Insurance benefits, regardless of age, whose yearly income does not exceed 250 percent of the 2012 federal poverty guidelines issued by the United States Department of Health and Human Services."
- Appropriations limit (Section 6, p. 3): the Board will provide each year "for any increase in the District's appropriations limit as shall be necessary to ensure that proceeds of the proposed tax may be spent for the authorized purposes."
- Revenue: "raising $1,300,000 annually" (ballot question). No separate fiscal statement was found among ACRE's files.

## Notes

- The ballot question says "levying $118 per parcel" and does not mention the inflation adjustment; the full text and the impartial analysis say the $118 is "subject to an annual adjustment for inflation".
- The argument in favor and argument against say "Measure _"; the two rebuttals use "Measure T". Whether the printed guide fills in the letter is unconfirmed.
- The rebuttal to the argument against has seven signers; only the first five are printed under ACRE's rules. Its signers differ from the argument-in-favor signers, but no authorization form from the primary authors is in ACRE's file.
- Handwritten names and titles are my reading of the scans; the printed pamphlet was not checked.
