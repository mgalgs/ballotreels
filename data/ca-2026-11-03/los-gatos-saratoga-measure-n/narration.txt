Los Gatos-Saratoga Union High School District Measure N asks the district's voters whether to approve $321 million in school bonds.
The district would borrow the money and repay it from a yearly tax on property in the district.
Measure N needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would replace roofs, restrooms, plumbing, electrical and heating systems, improve safety, and update classrooms and labs at the two high schools.
The district's tax rate statement estimates a tax of $30 per $100,000 of assessed value each year, through 2056-2057.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $690 million.
Supporters say Measure N repairs high school buildings up to 100 years old, where roofs leak and plumbing fails.
Opponents say Measure N is more bond debt, and the roofs a 2014 bond listed for replacing need replacing again.
A yes vote allows the district to sell up to $321 million in bonds, repaid by a yearly property tax.
A no vote is against selling the bonds.
