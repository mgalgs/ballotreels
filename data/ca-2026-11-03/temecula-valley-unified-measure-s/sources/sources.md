## Quoted passages

- Question (p.23): "To provide safe, modern schools by repairing and upgrading aging classrooms, school facilities, science and career technology labs supporting college/career readiness in math, science, engineering, technology, arts, and skilled trades; repairing deteriorating roofs, plumbing, gas, and electrical systems; shall Temecula Valley Unified School District's measure be adopted authorizing $215,000,000 in bonds at legal interest rates, levying approximately $20 per $100,000 assessed value (averaging $13,000,000) annually, while bonds are outstanding, with citizen oversight, audits, and all money locally-controlled?"
- Who votes (p.34, Tax Rate Statement): "An election will be held in the Temecula Valley Unified School District (the “District”) on November 3, 2026, to authorize the sale of up to $215,000,000"
- Tax rate (p.34): average rate "is $20 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. It is currently expected that the tax will be collected until fiscal year 2055-2056"; highest rate "is $20 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. This rate is projected to apply beginning in fiscal year 2027-2028."; total debt service "will be approximately $382,999,538."
- Who placed it (p.35): "The Board of Education of the District, by adopting Resolution No. 2025-26/42 (“Resolution”), elected to call an election to place this Measure on the ballot."
- Analysis (p.35): "Measure S would enable the District to issue and sell bonds of up to a maximum amount of two-hundred and fifteen million dollars ($215,000,000), at interest rates not to exceed legal limits."
