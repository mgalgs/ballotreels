San Lorenzo Unified School District Measure J asks the district's voters whether to renew its $99 parcel tax for 12 years.
Today, the district levies a tax of $99 per parcel, which expires unless voters renew it.
Measure J needs two-thirds of the vote to pass.
From July 1, 2027, the tax would stay at $99 per parcel a year for 12 more years.
The money could go only to the school purposes the measure lists.
Owners 65 or older, and some people receiving disability benefits, could apply to be exempt.
The impartial analysis says the district estimates the tax would provide about $1.8 million a year.
Supporters say Measure J renews existing funding without raising tax rates, preventing cuts and teacher layoffs.
No argument against Measure J was submitted for this election's Alameda County Composite Voter Information Guide.
A yes vote renews the expiring school funding at the current rate for 12 years.
A no vote lets the expiring $99 parcel tax end.
