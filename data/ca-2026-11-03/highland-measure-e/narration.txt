City of Highland Measure E asks Highland voters whether to add a one-cent sales tax.
Today the total sales tax rate in Highland is 7.75 percent.
The City Council voted unanimously to put Measure E on the ballot, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
Measure E would add 1 percent, one cent on every dollar of taxable sales.
The tax would last until voters repeal it.
The money would go to the general fund, for any lawful city program or service.
The impartial analysis says city staff estimate the tax would raise about $6.35 million a year.
No argument against Measure E was submitted, so the county voter guide prints only the supporters' argument.
Supporters say Measure E maintains police, fire and emergency services, which Highland would otherwise have to cut.
A yes vote adopts the one-cent sales tax.
A no vote does not adopt the one-cent sales tax.
