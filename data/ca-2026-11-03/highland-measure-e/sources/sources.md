## Quoted facts

- **Who votes:** City of Highland voters (RES: election "to be consolidated with the Statewide General Election" in the City of Highland).
- **Ballot question** (RES_CityofHighland.pdf): "City of Highland Public Safety and Local Control Measure To maintain local funding for police, fire, emergency services; and improve critical infrastructure, including maintaining roads, bridges, drainage, 5th Street Corridor Interchange, and other improvement projects; shall the City of Highland enact a 1¢ sales tax, providing approximately $6.35 million annually, remaining in effect until ended by voters, with funds staying in the City and generated from purchases paid by both visitors and City residents?"
- **Impartial analysis** (VIG p. 191, City Attorney): "On June 9, 2026, the Highland City Council unanimously voted to place Measure E on the November ballot. If approved by the voters, Measure E would authorize a one percent (1%) retail transactions and use tax ("Sales Tax Ordinance") within the City of Highland." … "This sales tax would be a "general tax," meaning that revenues raised from the tax would go into the City's general fund to pay for any lawful City program, improvement, or service. City staff have estimated that the Sales Tax Ordinance will raise approximately $6.35 million annually in local revenue."
