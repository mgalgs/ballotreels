San Mateo County Measure AA asks county voters whether to add to the charter a commitment to the dignity, civil rights and equal protection of all county residents.
San Mateo County is one of 15 California counties with a charter, which its voters first approved in 1932.
The preamble at the start of the charter does not address this subject today.
Measure AA would add a paragraph affirming that commitment in a manner that promotes equity, fairness, inclusion, belonging, accessibility and opportunity.
The measure would not change any other part of the charter.
The Board of Supervisors placed Measure AA on the ballot, and it needs a majority of the vote to pass.
The published measure documents of San Mateo County do not include a cost estimate for the measure.
No argument for Measure AA appears in the published measure documents of San Mateo County.
No argument against Measure AA appears in the published measure documents of San Mateo County.
A yes vote means the charter preamble would be amended to add this commitment.
A no vote means the charter preamble would not be amended to add it.
