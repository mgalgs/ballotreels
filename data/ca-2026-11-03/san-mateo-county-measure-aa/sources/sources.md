# Measure AA — County of San Mateo — Charter amendment (Preamble: affirmation of rights)

- **Jurisdiction:** County of San Mateo (countywide).
- **Kind:** County Charter amendment, placed by the Board of Supervisors (Resolution No. 081882 and Ordinance No. 4920). It is the "Second Measure" of four in that resolution (L, AA, J, U).
- **Who votes:** all county voters. Resolution Sec. 9(b), PDF p. 8: "All qualified voters in San Mateo County shall be permitted to vote in the special election on the Charter amendments set forth in the Measures."
- **Threshold:** simple majority. Resolution Sec. 6, PDF p. 8: each Measure "will become effective only if submitted to the voters at the Countywide special election on November 3, 2026, and only after approval by a majority (i.e., 50% + 1) of the qualified voters voting in the special election."

## Ballot question (verbatim)

Resolution Sec. 3(a), PDF p. 4 (repeated in Ordinance Sec. 7(a), PDF p. 16), under the heading "COUNTY CHARTER AMENDMENT":

> Shall the San Mateo County Charter be amended to add a provision to the Charter Preamble affirming the County's commitment to the dignity, civil rights, and equal protection of all County residents?

## Full text

- **Resolution No. 081882**, Board of Supervisors, County of San Mateo. PDF p. 11: "Regularly passed and adopted this 9th day of June, 2026". AYES: "JACKIE SPEIER", "NOELIA CORZO", "RAY MUELLER", "LISA GAUTHIER", "DAVID J. CANEPA". NOES: "NONE".
- **Ordinance No. 4920**, calling the election. PDF p. 21: "Regularly passed and adopted this 9th day of June, 2026"; same five AYES, NOES "NONE". Introduced "at its regular meeting on May 19, 2026" (resolution recital, PDF p. 2).
- **What it adds** (Resolution Sec. 3(b), PDF p. 4): the Measure "would amend the Charter Preamble to add a new paragraph to read as follows: Preamble. The County of San Mateo affirms its commitment to the dignity, civil rights, and equal protection of all County residents in a manner that promotes equity, fairness, inclusion, belonging, accessibility, and opportunity."
- Sec. 6, PDF p. 8: "Each Measure may be voted on separately without prejudice to the other Measures".
- Sec. 9(g), PDF p. 9: "The County Attorney's Office shall prepare the impartial analysis of the Measures."

## Impartial analysis

`impartial-analysis.pdf`, p. 1. Headed "IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE [TBD]". **No author or signature is printed** on it; the resolution assigns it to the County Attorney's Office. It ends "(500 word limit. 198 words)".

- "Approval of Measure [TBD] ("Measure") would amend the Charter to add a new provision to the Charter's Preamble affirming the County's commitment to the dignity, civil rights, and equal protection of all County residents."
- "This Measure was placed on the ballot by ordinance and resolution passed by a majority vote of the Board of Supervisors ("Board")."
- "This Measure would add a new paragraph to the Charter's Preamble stating that the County affirms its commitment to the dignity, civil rights, and equal protection of all County residents in a manner that promotes equity, fairness, inclusion, belonging, accessibility, and opportunity, a subject not currently addressed by the Preamble."
- "The Measure would not change or otherwise affect the other provisions of the Charter."
- "This Measure requires approval by a majority of the qualified voters voting in the election."
