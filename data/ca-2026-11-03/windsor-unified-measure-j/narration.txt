Measure J asks the voters of this school district whether to approve $96 million in school bonds.
The district would sell the bonds over time and repay them from property taxes in the district.
Measure J needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money could replace leaky roofs, make health and safety improvements, and modernize classrooms, restrooms and other school buildings.
The district's tax rate statement estimates an average tax of $46 per $100,000 of assessed value, and at most $60, until 2060-2061.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $216 million.
No argument against Measure J was submitted for the county Registrar of Voters' printed voter guide pages on this measure.
Supporters say Measure J fixes leaking roofs and failing pipes by extending existing bond tax rates without an increase.
A yes vote will authorize the district to issue the bonds and levy taxes to repay them.
A no vote will not authorize the bonds or the taxes to repay them.
