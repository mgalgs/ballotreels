## Facts and quotes

**Ballot question (p1):** "Windsor Unified School District Classroom Repair/No Tax Increase Measure. To upgrade aging classrooms, labs, career training and school facilities for college/career readiness in math, science, technology, engineering, skilled trades; and fix deteriorating roofs, plumbing, HVAC, electrical, and safety systems, shall Windsor Unified School District's measure be adopted authorizing $96,000,000 in bonds at legal rates, without increasing projected tax rates, levying approximately $46 per $100,000 while bonds are outstanding ($6,000,000 annually), requiring independent oversight/audits and local control?"

**Vote needed:** "55% Needed to Pass" (p1).

**Who votes:** voters of the Windsor Unified School District ("The Windsor Unified School District ("District") Board of Trustees ("Board") has placed on the ballot the question of whether to issue $96,000,000 in bonds", p2).

**Impartial analysis (p2), key statements:**
- "As required by state law, Measure J prohibits using bond proceeds for teacher and administrator salaries, or other operating expenses."
- "Bonds will be repaid with funds raised from an increase in property taxes based upon the assessed value of taxable property in the District through fiscal year 2060-61. The "Tax Rate Statement" ... estimates the highest tax rate necessary to fund the bonds is $60 per $100,000 of assessed value. Those tax levies are projections, and could go up or down".
- "If all bonds are sold, the total debt services, including principal and interest, is estimated at $216,342,122."
- Signed Joshua A. Myers, County Counsel, by Adam Brand, Assistant County Counsel.

**Tax rate statement (p2):** average "$46 per $100,000 of assessed valuation ... collected until fiscal year 2060-61"; highest "$60 per $100,000 ... projected to apply from fiscal years 2042-43 through 2058-59"; total debt service "approximately $216,342,122". "...the District does not currently expect the fiscal year 2025-26 tax rate levied in connection with outstanding bonds, as projected by the District, to be increased as a result of the issuance of bonds pursuant to this bond measure." Signed Jeremy Decker, Superintendent.

Note: the question says "approximately $46 per $100,000" (the average rate); the analysis and tax rate statement give the highest rate as $60 per $100,000. Both as printed.

**Argument in favor (p3):** signed Michael LeCompt (Owner, Oak Tree Winery); Rotary Club of Windsor — Pamela Moulton (Past President); Warin Parker (former Mayor Town of Windsor); David Hamelburg (Business Owner, Thurston Printing).

**Argument against:** "No Argument Was Submitted Against Measure J" (p3).
