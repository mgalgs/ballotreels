Rebuttal to Argument in favor of Measure M - Gilroy Unified School District $295M Bond

[argument starts here]

Stop this huge new tax hike!

The Gilroy Unified School District is asking you to incur bond debt of $295 million.

That's an IOU to Wall Street bankers that you'd have to repay — through your taxes—for 30 plus YEARS.

Have they earned it?

Academic statistics:

• Percentage of students behind grade level in English = 59.77%
• Percentage behind grade level in math = 67.58%

How about enrollment?
• Drop in ADA enrollment since 2020-21 school year = 387 students.
• Chronic absenteeism rate = 21%

And teacher statistics:
• Cohort graduates meeting the UC/CSU course requirements = 50.9%
• Teachers' salaries of $85,611 (2020-21) rose to $106,112 (2024-25) -- an increase of $20,501*

When buying a home, truth-in-lending laws require that you be told the real, total cost of purchasing that home. For example:

• Borrowing $295,000,000; paying 3% interest for 30 years would incur interest expenses of approximately $265,500,000, plus principal, $295,000,000, for a lifetime total cost of $560,500,000 ... of YOUR and YOUR CHILDREN'S EARNINGS!

Consumer protection laws should apply to bond issues. As taxpayers, we deserve to know the full truth about Measure M. We don't know when this debt will begin, the market rate, nor when it ends.

Say NO to bond-debt taxes.

Questions:

• Will this bond-debt tax hike reverse declining English proficiency?
• Will it reverse declining math proficiency?
• Will it fix declining enrollment?
• Will it fix chronic absenteeism?
