[Original page 15]

Argument against Measure M - Gilroy Unified School District $295M Bond

[argument starts here]

Stop this huge new tax hike!

The Gilroy Unified School District is asking you to approve Measure M to incur bond debt of $295 million.

That's an IOU to Wall Street bankers that you'd have to repay—through your taxes—for around 30 YEARS.

Have they earned it?

The stats:

• Drop in ADA enrollment since 2020-21 school year = 387 students.
• Percentage of students behind grade level in English = 59.77%
• Percentage behind grade level in math = 67.58%
• Chronic absenteeism data = 21%
• Cohort graduates meeting the UC/CSU course requirements = 50.9%
• Teachers' salaries of $85,611 (2020-21) rose to $106,112 (2024-25)
  o An increase of $20,501

Education Data Partnership* shows enrollment of only 10,777 students in the district, so the bond debt could exceed $27,373 per student — NOT COUNTING interest expense, which usually doubles the cost.

Would that do anything to help the children?

When you buy a home, truth-in-lending laws require that you be told the real, total cost of purchasing that home. For example:

• Borrowing $295,000,000; paying 3% interest for 30 years would incur interest expenses of approximately $153,000,000, plus principal, $295,000,000, for a lifetime total cost of $447,000,000 ... of YOUR money!

Consumer protection laws should apply to bond issues. As taxpayers, we deserve to know the full truth about Measure M. We don't even know when this debt will begin, let alone what the market interest rate will be, when it does.

Just say NO to bond debt.

Questions:

• Will this bond-debt tax hike reverse declining English proficiency?
