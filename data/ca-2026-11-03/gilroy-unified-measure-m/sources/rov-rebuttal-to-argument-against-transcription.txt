[Original page 1]

Rebuttal Argument to Measure M
Gilroy Unified School District

Quality schools are key to providing an excellent education, preserving property values, and maintaining our great community.

Don't let the single opponent who doesn't live in Gilroy mislead you.

Consider the facts:

FACT: School bond measures improve academic performance. A 2023 study "Effectiveness and Efficiency of School Capital Investments Across the U.S." covered 28 states, encompassing approximately 70% of the nation's public school students, and 17,000 bond issues reported "...on average, capital projects raise both test scores and property values."

FACT: No other source of funding exists to renovate/upgrade our schools. The State provides funding for teachers, administration, and books, not for maintenance and renovation projects. Don't punish students for how the State chooses to provide funding. Measure M is the only solution to improve Gilroy Unified School District classrooms and school facilities.

FACT: Measure M has strict accountability and makes financial sense. Funds must be spent locally and cannot be taken by the State, and an independent citizens' oversight committee of local residents must annually audit and review spending by law.

Measure M is a carefully considered program that will upgrade safety, improve the quality of education for local students, and benefit our Gilroy community.

Join us and Vote YES on Measure M!
