- **Letter:** M, confirmed by the district's Measure M page and the voter-guide page proof ("COMPLETE TEXT OF MEASURE M").

## Full text (proof pp. 1–6)

Title (p. 1): "GILROY UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT BUILDING FUTURE READY CAMPUSES, RENOVATION, MODERNIZATION MEASURE"; "This measure may be known and referred to as the "Gilroy Unified School District Building Future Ready Campuses, Renovation, Modernization Measure" or as "Measure M"."

Accountability (p. 1): "the Board shall establish an independent citizens' oversight committee"; "the Board shall conduct an annual, independent performance audit"; and an "annual, independent financial audit".

Interpretation (p. 6): "The school facilities projects on the Bond Project List only authorize capital expenditures."

Signed (p. 6): "/s/ Anisha Munshi, Superintendent, Gilroy Unified School District" (tax rate statement).

District page (not a legal text): "Measure M is a $295.0 million general obligation (G.O.) bond program. This measure is intended to address the needs of the student population through modernization projects at the District's fourteen schools."

## Tax rate statement (proof p. 6)

> 1. The best estimate of the average annual tax rate that would be required to be levied to fund this bond issue over the entire duration of the bond debt service, based on assessed valuations available at the time of filing this statement, is $59.14 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. The final fiscal year in which the tax to be levied to fund this bond issue is anticipated to be collected is fiscal year 2059-60.

> 2. The best estimate of the highest tax rate that would be required to be levied to fund this bond issue, based on estimated assessed valuations available at the time of filing of this statement, is $60.00 per $100,000 of assessed valuation in fiscal year 2027-28.

> 3. The best estimate of the total debt service, including the principal and interest, that would be required to be repaid if all of the bonds are issued and sold is approximately $623,300,000.

The district page states "The tax rate per property owner is estimated to be $60 per $100,000 of assessed valuation."
