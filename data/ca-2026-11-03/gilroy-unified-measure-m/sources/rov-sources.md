# Santa Clara County Registrar of Voters documents (retrieved 2026-10-29)

Publisher: County of Santa Clara Registrar of Voters, "List of Local Measures"
(https://vote.santaclaracounty.gov/list-local-measures-4), saved here as
`rov-measure-list-2026-10-29.html`. It is the official measure list: each measure's
letter, ballot question, vote threshold, and links to its impartial analysis,
arguments and rebuttals, with "(none filed)" where an argument was not filed.

Each PDF below is the ROV's own posted copy (a redacted scan, no text layer), cut to
the pages that carry the document and its first signer statement, and converted to
grayscale at 120 dpi to keep the repo small. Pages kept are page numbers in the
ROV's original file.
