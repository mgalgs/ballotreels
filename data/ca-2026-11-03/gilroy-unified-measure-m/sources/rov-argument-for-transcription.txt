Argument in Favor of Measure M
Gilroy Unified School District

Strong schools are the foundation of a strong community and should be one of our highest priorities. High-quality schools improve student achievement, help maintain and increase property values, prepare children for a productive future, and contribute to stronger, safer neighborhoods.
Measure M would provide the Gilroy Unified School District with the resources needed to improve our neighborhood schools and enhance the quality of education provided to our students.
An investment in our neighborhood schools is an investment to improve our community.
By investing in our schools, we can provide students with better learning environments while strengthening the neighborhoods and community we all share. It's an investment in our children, our schools, and our future—and a win for all of Gilroy.

If passed, Measure M will provide funding to make critical facility improvements at Gilroy schools including:

• Repairing or replacing leaky roofs
• Renovating and constructing career technical education classrooms to better prepare students for career and jobs
• Improving student safety at drop-off and pick-up areas
• Upgrading or replacing deteriorating plumbing and sewer systems
• Making safety improvements including fire alarms and security cameras

Measure M makes financial sense and protects taxpayers.

• By law, spending must be reviewed and annually audited by an independent citizens' oversight committee.
• All bond funds must be spent locally and cannot be taken by the State.
• Funds are required to be spent only on schools, not for administrator or teacher salaries.

Measure M upgrades and renovates old and inadequate classrooms, improves the education of local children, and maintains the quality of our community. That's something we can all support. Please join us and VOTE YES ON MEASURE M!
