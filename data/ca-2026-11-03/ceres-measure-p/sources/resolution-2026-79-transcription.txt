RESOLUTION NO. 2026-79

Page 2, Section 1:

SECTION 1: Pursuant to Revenue and Taxation Code section 7285.9 and Government Code section 53724, the City Council by this Resolution approves by a two-thirds (2/3) vote the Measure. The Measure is a general tax as defined in Article XIII C of the State of California Constitution and Government Code section 53721 and shall not take effect until approved by a vote of at least a majority of voters voting on the question at the November 3, 2026, election.

Page 2, Section 2, the question submitted to the voters (the box beside it prints "Yes" and "No"):

SECTION 2: Pursuant to Elections Code section 9222, the City Council hereby submits the following question to the voters at the election on November 3, 2026:

“CERES 911 EMERGENCY RESPONSE, COMMUNITY SAFETY, ESSENTIAL SERVICES MEASURE. Shall the measure to maintain 911 response/police patrols; hire/retain qualified police officers; prevent gang activity from nearby cities; address homelessness/clear encampments; maintain essential and general city services, by establishing a 1¢ local sales tax providing approximately $9,400,000 annually until ended by voters, requiring independent audits, public spending disclosure, all funds benefitting Ceres residents, no money for the State, with citizens’ oversight, be adopted?”
