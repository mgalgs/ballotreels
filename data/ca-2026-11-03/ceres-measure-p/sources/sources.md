## Quoted facts

- Ballot question (Resolution 2026-79 §2, transcribed from a scan whose OCR was rough; check against the image): "CERES 911 EMERGENCY RESPONSE, COMMUNITY SAFETY ESSENTIAL SERVICES MEASURE. Shall the measure to maintain 911 response/police patrols; hire/retain qualified police officers; prevent gang activity from nearby cities; address homelessness/clear encampments; maintain essential and general city services, by establishing a 1¢ local sales tax providing approximately $9,400,000 annually until ended by voters, requiring independent audits, public spending disclosure, all funds benefiting Ceres residents, no money for the State, with citizens' oversight, be adopted?"
- Who votes / vote needed: analysis, "On June 22, 2026, the City Council of the City of Ceres (“City”), acting on its own motion, not by voter petition, voted to place Measure P on the ballot for the November 3, 2026, general municipal election. If approved by a majority of City voters (50% + 1), this measure would authorize an additional 1% transactions and use tax within the City."
- Rate: "The City’s current sales tax rate is 8.375%. If the measure passes, the new sales tax rate would be 9.375%."
- Revenue: "It is estimated that the proposed sales tax will provide an additional Nine Million Four Hundred Thousand Dollars ($9,400,000) in annual local funding."
- General tax: "This sales tax is a “general tax”, meaning that revenues raised from the tax would go into the City’s general fund to pay for any lawful City program, service, or improvement."
- Resolution §1: "The Measure is a general tax ... and shall not take effect until approved by a vote of at least a majority of voters voting on the question at the November 3, 2026, election."
