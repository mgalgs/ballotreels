Argument in Favor of Measure _

VOTE YES on Measure _ to fix potholes, maintain roads, and support emergency services, parks, libraries, water and wastewater infrastructure, and other essential services in Monterey County — without raising taxes on local residents' homes, purchases, or income.

Measure _ is a "hotel tax," applied only on visitors staying overnight in hotels and short-term accommodations in Monterey County. Funds from Measure _ would support the essential services that local residents rely on and ensure there are resources to address top priorities in the coming years.

Every year, many visitors travel through and stay in Monterey County. While they benefit from county roads, parks, lakes, beaches, and public safety support, their presence increases the demand on these essential services. Measure _ ensures overnight visitors will contribute toward the services and infrastructure, without increasing taxes on local residents.

Measure _ would not disadvantage Monterey County businesses. It would simply raise the tax rate for overnight visitors to similar levels it already is in many other local communities.

Vote YES on Measure _ to:

- Fix potholes and maintain streets and roads
- Enhance and develop drinking water systems and sewer infrastructure.
- Strengthen sheriff, fire, and emergency response services
- Preserve and improve County parks, lakes, and access to recreational facilities
- Support and provide vital health care services
- Provide services that prevent homelessness
- Protect library services and programs
- Address housing needs for middle-income workers—such as teachers, nurses, and first responders—who are critical to local community stability

Measure _ includes fiscal safeguards — mandatory audits, public reporting, and yearly prioritization based on community needs — ensuring transparency. These safeguards guarantee that all funds are spent only within Monterey County, preventing diversion to Sacramento.

Visitors will help pay their fair share for the roads, services, and public facilities they enjoy. Join firefighters, officers, seniors, businesses, labor leaders, and neighbors throughout Monterey County —vote YES on Measure _.
