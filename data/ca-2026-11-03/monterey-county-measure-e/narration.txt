Measure E asks the voters of this county whether to raise the hotel tax in the unincorporated areas.
The county taxes hotel, motel and other lodging stays of 30 days or less, at 10.5 percent of the rent.
Every ballot in the county carries the question, but the tax would rise only in the unincorporated areas.
Measure E would raise the rate from 10.5 percent to 12.5 percent.
The impartial analysis says the money goes to the general fund, for services such as roads, libraries and parks.
The higher rate would last until ended by voters, and needs a simple majority to pass.
The impartial analysis says the tax would provide about $7.6 million a year, to be spent locally.
No argument against Measure E appears among the official documents gathered for this video; only the argument in favor does.
Supporters say Measure E is paid only by overnight visitors, not local residents, and would fix potholes and maintain roads.
A yes vote is in favor of raising the tax to 12.5 percent.
A no vote is against raising the tax to 12.5 percent.
