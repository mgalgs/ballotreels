For 40 years, the City of Fresno - along with every incorporated city in Fresno County - has relied upon Measure C to provide funding for road infrastructure maintenance and construction along with transit services. Measure C was previously focused on regional growth, such as freeways and Veterans Boulevard - and sunsets June 2027.

Without this measure, the City of Fresno faces a $56 million funding shortfall related to critical local improvements such as road repairs, enhanced public safety, and strengthening transit reliability. It could also lead to layoffs of hundreds of employees, the idling of road repairs, and the elimination of bus routes that connect Fresno families to school, work, and medical services.

Measure D ensures that there will be absolutely no increase in the city's sales tax rate from what it is today.

As written, Measure D would fund transportation infrastructure for road construction along with maintaining FAX bus and other transportation services across our city.

Because it is a general tax, Measure D would also provide funding for upgraded police, fire, and emergency medical response services.

Measure D is designed with clear safeguards for City of Fresno taxpayers to ensure we are not double taxed for transportation infrastructure.

Measure D is NOT a forever tax, it expires either in 10 years or earlier if a countywide transportation sales tax measure is approved by the voters.

Measure D includes strict accountability and oversight requirements, including a Citizen's Oversight committee and annual audit requirements.

Fresno families deserve the highest quality roads, public safety, and essential services. Measure D ensures that we control our destiny and maintain a strong city, built on good roads and safe neighborhoods, for the foreseeable future.

On November 3, please join us in voting YES on Measure D!
