## Facts

**Ballot question.** resolution.pdf, PDF page 5 (resolution page "4 of 7"), boxed:
> "CITY OF FRESNO ROADS/PUBLIC SAFETY/CITY SERVICES MEASURE (NO TAX INCREASE): Without increasing current tax rates, shall the measure maintaining local streets, roads, sidewalks; improving police/fire staffing, 9-1-1 emergency response; maintaining transit services; accelerating airport improvements; other general services by establishing a 0.5¢ sales tax providing approximately $65,000,000 annually for 10 years or when a County transportation tax is adopted, whichever is sooner; with citizen oversight, all funds spent locally, be adopted?"

**Who votes.** resolution.pdf, PDF page 4, para. 4:
> "the following question shall be submitted to registered voters of the City of Fresno"

**What it does.** Impartial Analysis (Andrew N. Janz, City Attorney; filed Aug 25 2026):
> "Measure D asks voters of the City of Fresno whether a one-half percent (0.5%) transactions and use (sales) tax should be approved and imposed on retail transactions currently subject to sales tax within the City of Fresno."
> "Measure D is a general tax. Any additional revenue generated by this tax would go to the City of Fresno general fund and be available to be used for any City purpose ..."
> "This proposed tax sunsets upon the earlier of 10 years or until the County of Fresno adopts a transportation special sales tax of equal or greater tax rate for which the City of Fresno is a beneficial recipient."
> "The current sales tax rate in the City of Fresno is 8.35% 2026 would remain level if Measure D is approved by the majority of votes cast at the November 3, 2026, election." [sic]
> "The City estimates that approval of Measure D would generate approximately $65,000,000 each year in additional revenue ..."
> "If Measure D is approved, the sales tax rate of 8.35% to include this tax will begin on July 1, 2027."

**Why "no tax increase".** resolution.pdf, PDF page 5, synopsis:
> "The proposed ordinance would impose a 0.5% [half-cent per dollar] sales and use tax within the City of Fresno for a period of 10 years or upon implementation of a County of Fresno transportation infrastructure tax, whichever occurs first. It is estimated to raise approximately $65 million annually while retaining City of Fresno's current tax rate."
The record I read does not explain how the rate stays at 8.35%. Presumably the existing county Measure C half-cent expires as this one starts, but that is my inference and not confirmed here. Measure S's impartial analysis says Measure C "expires" when Measure S would begin, July 1, 2027.

**Vote needed.** County page heading: "Majority (50%+1) Required to Pass".
