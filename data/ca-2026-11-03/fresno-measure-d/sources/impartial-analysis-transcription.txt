IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS
Fresno Public Safety, Infrastructure Restoration, and City General Services Tax Measure

The City Council of the City of Fresno has placed the Fresno Public Safety, Infrastructure Restoration, and City General Services Tax Measure (Measure D) on the ballot for the November 3, 2026, election.

Measure D asks voters of the City of Fresno whether a one-half percent (0.5%) transactions and use (sales) tax should be approved and imposed on retail transactions currently subject to sales tax within the City of Fresno.

Measure D would not result in a tax on real property, rent, groceries, or prescription medication. Measure D is a general tax. Any additional revenue generated by this tax would go to the City of Fresno general fund and be available to be used for any City purpose, including but not limited to funding police, fire, and 9-1-1 emergency services, maintaining and improving roads, streets, sidewalks, and other City facilities and programs, and paying for other City services and obligations.

This proposed tax sunsets upon the earlier of 10 years or until the County of Fresno adopts a transportation special sales tax of equal or greater tax rate for which the City of Fresno is a beneficial recipient.

If Measure D is approved, all spending of the additional revenue generated by this tax would be subject to public disclosure, independent audit requirements, and citizen oversight.

The current sales tax rate in the City of Fresno is 8.35% 2026 would remain level if Measure D is approved by the majority of votes cast at the November 3, 2026, election.

If Measure D is approved, the tax would be collected at the same time and in the same manner as the existing sales tax is collected in the City of Fresno. The City estimates that approval of Measure D would generate approximately $65,000,000 each year in additional revenue for use by the City, and by law the additional funds would belong only to the City of Fresno and could not be taken by the State, County, or any other agency.

If Measure D is approved, the sales tax rate of 8.35% to include this tax will begin on July 1, 2027.

The above statement is an impartial analysis of Measure D. If you desire a copy of the full ordinance or measure, please call the Fresno City Clerk's office at 559-621-7650, and a copy will be provided at no cost to you.
