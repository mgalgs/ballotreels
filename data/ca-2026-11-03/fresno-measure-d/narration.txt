Measure D asks the voters of this city whether to adopt a half-cent sales tax for general city services, for up to 10 years.
A county sales tax for transportation, Measure C, is set to expire on June 30, 2027.
Today, the sales tax rate in the city is 8.35 percent.
Measure D's tax would begin on July 1, 2027, and the rate would remain at 8.35 percent.
The money would go to the general fund of the city, for any city purpose, such as police, fire and roads.
The tax would end after 10 years, or sooner if a new county transportation sales tax takes effect, including a pending citizens' initiative for one.
A citizens' oversight committee and yearly audits would review the spending, and Measure D needs a simple majority to pass.
The city attorney's impartial analysis says the city estimates about $65 million a year in added revenue.
Opponents say even a half-percent sales tax is a heavy burden, and it charges every shopper the same, whatever their income.
Supporters say Measure D keeps the sales tax at today's rate, and without it the city faces a $56 million shortfall.
A yes vote adopts the half-cent sales tax, starting in July 2027.
A no vote does not adopt the half-cent sales tax.
