Proposition 40 is about a one-time tax on billionaires.
A few hundred Californians are worth over one billion dollars.
Wealth is the value of everything a person owns, minus their debts, which is different from yearly income.
Billionaires living here on January 1, 2026 would owe a one-time 5 percent tax on their net worth.
Ninety percent of the money would go to health care.
The Legislative Analyst's Office expects the tax to raise tens of billions of dollars over several years.
It also sees a possible yearly drop in income taxes of under $1 billion, less than 1 percent of this year's General Fund spending.
Opponents, including Rene Bravo, the first signer against, say the tax would drive the wealthy away, costing billions.
Supporters, including Suzanne Jimenez, the measure's proponent, say the tax would replace federal cuts to health care funding.
A yes vote means the state would collect the one-time tax.
A no vote means the state would not collect the one-time tax.
