East Palo Alto Measure O asks city voters whether to borrow $125 million through bonds for a library, civic center, water pipes and storm drains.
Bonds are borrowed money, repaid with interest from a tax on property in the city.
Measure O needs a two-thirds vote to pass.
The bonds would buy land for a civic center with a police building, a library and city hall.
They would also pay for water tanks and pipes, storm drains, and land for a park.
The money would be kept in a separate fund, with a yearly independent audit.
The city estimates the tax would average $105.26 per $100,000 of assessed value a year, through 2057-2058.
The city estimates repaying all the bonds would take about $268 million, principal and interest.
Supporters say Measure O upgrades deteriorating water pipes, and builds a modern library with safe places for kids after school.
Opponents say the bonds could cost twice as much or more with interest, and safety repairs should come from today's budget.
A yes vote lets the city issue up to $125 million in bonds for these projects.
A no vote means the city could not issue the bonds.
