REBUTTAL TO ARGUMENT AGAINST MILPITAS MEASURE K

Apparently, out-of-town Special Interest groups either haven’t done their homework, or worse – don’t want large corporations to pay their fair share! Reject their lies – vote YES on K.

Milpitas’s small, locally owned businesses shouldn’t be stuck with a higher tax rate than Big Corporations. While opponents play politics, YES on K provides a solution to an unfair tax system.

The TRUTH is:

FACT: Measure K simply reforms an outdated, 50-year-old business tax structure.

FACT: Currently, some Milpitas small businesses pay 70 times higher in business tax per employee than Big Corporations! Measure K fixes this inequity, so corporations pay their fair share for the services we ALL use.

FACT: Measure K lowers taxes for the smallest businesses.

FACT: Measure K does NOT raise taxes on residents and homeowners.

Measure K was developed with the input of small businesses to reform a structure currently unfair to them.

Large corporations use 911 and Milpitas streets just like us. Yet Milpitas residents and small businesses are shouldering an unfair share of costs for these services.

It’s time we ask Big Business to pay their fair share!

Measure K is fiscally accountable and transparent to our residents and business community. Every dollar must be reinvested locally and is subject to independent audits and public disclosure of all spending.

Reject political rhetoric, support Milpitas’s small businesses, and maintain the quality of life for Milpitas families during these tough times.

To read official Measure K information: www.Milpitas.gov.
