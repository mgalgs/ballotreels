Argument against Measure K - Milpitas business license tax

Stop this huge new tax hike!

City of Milpitas councilmembers are letting their greed show. Either that, or they don’t understand the reality:

Businesses don't pay taxes.

What?!

Businesses create jobs, and goods that we value, and services we need. But they have countless expenses and considerations, to accomplish all that.

You ask: How can it be that businesses “don’t pay taxes”?

Taxes are just another cost of doing business, which owners must pass along, to stay in business. They pass costs along to…

• Customers, through higher prices;
• Employees, through lower wages/salaries; or
• Shareholders, through lower returns on investments (e.g., our 401(k)s or IRAs!)

Remember the gigantic tariffs on imported electronics? how our favorite gadgets’ prices spiked?

Gadget businesses necessarily passed the tariff expense along to us, through higher prices.

That illustrates a fundamental law of economics — like gravity. But it’s not just economists who feel those effects in everyday activities, like shopping or filling up the tank.

The cost of living here is already too high. Nothing seems affordable!

Businesses create goods and services that people want.
Businesses create jobs that people want.

Their outputs are amazing — enhancing so many lives.

Don’t trust politicians who would penalize businesses — the engine of our extraordinary standard of living — in order to fund pet projects.

If Measure K passes, Milpitas business owners who fall behind because skyrocketing taxation pushes customers away, may hatch plans to relocate to cities that appreciate them more.

Don’t tax what you want more of.

Want more goods and services available at lower prices? Want more jobs to be available, at higher wages/salaries? Then please:

Vote NO.

Let’s not penalize job and product providers! They help us, immeasurably, every day.

Now let’s help them:

Vote NO!

Silicon Valley Taxpayer Association’s volunteers advocate for taxpayers throughout Santa Clara County. Visit SVTaxpayers.org.
