ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF CITY OF MILPITAS MEASURE K

Let's keep our neighborhoods safe and clean by making large businesses and corporations pay their fair share: Vote Yes on K!

Measure K does not raise taxes for residents or homeowners.

Right now, small and locally owned businesses have a higher tax rate than larger, wealthier corporations – in some cases, over 70 times more.

This is simply not fair! And we have the power to make it right.

Voting Yes on K fixes this inequity by ensuring large corporations finally pay their fair share for the essential services we all rely on.

Yes on K reforms our business tax structure that's over 50 years old to make it fair for small business, while providing our community with local funding to maintain our quality of life – without raising taxes for residents.

Yes on K maintains our vital city services and fiscal stability.

Yes on K maintains emergency medical and ambulance services.

Yes on K replaces aging water and sewer lines, and repairs storm drains.

Yes on K keeps neighborhood streets and sidewalks clean and well-maintained and removes blight and illegal dumping.

With Washington and Sacramento politicians cutting funding for our city, our critical services – including 911 emergency response and street repair – are at stake.

That's why it's more important than ever that we remain self-reliant. Every penny of Measure K is for our community and must be reinvested locally to benefit Milpitas residents. No money can be taken by out-of-town politicians.

Measure K continues Milpitas' high standards for fiscal accountability and transparency with independent audits and public disclosure of all spending.

Join Milpitas small business owners, firefighters, police officers, community leaders and a unanimous City Council in voting Yes on K!

For official information about Measure K: www.Milpitas.gov
