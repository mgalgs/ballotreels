Rebuttal to Argument in Favor of Measure K
(Milpitas business license tax)

Stop this huge new tax hike!

Beware: Proponents’ claims about “fair shares” and “inequity” completely ignore reality:

Businesses don’t pay taxes.

Businesses create jobs, goods, services that we need and value. To do so, they have countless expenses and considerations.

Taxes are just one cost of doing business, which owners must pass along. To stay in business, they pass costs along to…
• Customers (higher prices);
• Employees (lower wages); or
• Shareholders (lower returns on investments — our 401(k)s!)

Proponents’ claim that Measure K would benefit small businesses is absurd.

MAYBE a FEW sole proprietorships would forfeit slightly less in business license taxes, but when their larger suppliers are taxed more, the small businesses’ costs rise accordingly — thus, the prices they charge us! (Remember: Businesses don't pay taxes.)

Don’t trust politicians who neglect “vital city services” like “aging water and sewer lines,” then have the nerve to stoke envy and penalize businesses to cover for their lack of planning.

It’s incredible how well businesses’ goods, services, and jobs enhance our lives.

But the cost of living here is already too high. Gas, groceries, rent — nothing seems affordable!

And if Measure K passes, Milpitas business owners, suffering when burdensome taxation costs push customers away, may relocate to cities who won’t penalize them.

Don’t tax what you want more of.

Want more goods and services, priced lower, and jobs, at higher salaries?

Vote NO.

Let’s not penalize our job and product providers!

Vote NO!
