ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE L

VOTE YES ON MEASURE L-PROTECT CUPERTINO'S PARKS

Right now, Cupertino's parks and open space can be rezoned for housing, offices, or industrial use—without ever asking the voters who use them. One council vote could pave over the playground your kids grew up on, or the trail where you walk your dog every morning.

Measure L changes that. As Cupertino grows denser, our parks and open space become more valuable than ever—the only places left to gather, play, and breathe. Measure L simply asks: shouldn't a decision this big be made by the people, not just five council members?

Measure L requires two-thirds voter approval before:

• Any park, parkland, or open space is rezoned to residential, commercial, or industrial use
• Any residential, commercial, or industrial development is built on protected parkland

Measure L does NOT stop the City from:

• Building playgrounds, trails, sports fields, and park improvements
• Restoring habitat and creeks, reducing wildfire risk, and protecting the environment

This isn't about blocking progress—it's about making sure decisions on shared community resources are made together, by the people who actually use them. Our parks belong to every resident, every family, every kid learning to ride a bike, and the wildlife that calls them home. Once they're gone, they're gone.

Join our current and former Mayors and City Council, sports leagues, hospitality, environmental, business, health, education, and community leaders across Cupertino.

VOTE YES ON MEASURE L-KEEP CUPERTINO'S PARKS IN THE PEOPLE'S HANDS.
