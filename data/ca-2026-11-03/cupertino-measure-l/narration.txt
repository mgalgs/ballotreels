Measure L asks the voters of this city whether rezoning or developing parks and open space should first need two-thirds voter approval.
Measure L covers land marked as parks and open space on the land use map of the general plan.
The City Council placed Measure L on the ballot, and Measure L itself needs a simple majority to pass.
Rezoning that land, or approving homes, businesses or industry on it, would need two-thirds of voters at a general election.
Uses already allowed under the park designation would not need a vote.
The City Council could add land by a four-fifths vote, but removing land or changing the measure would take two-thirds of voters.
The Registrar of Voters' List of Local Measures does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Supporters say voters, not just five council members, should decide on rezoning parks.
No argument against Measure L was filed with the county Registrar of Voters.
A yes vote approves the ordinance requiring a two-thirds vote.
A no vote rejects the ordinance.
