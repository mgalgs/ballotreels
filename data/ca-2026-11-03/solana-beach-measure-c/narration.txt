City of Solana Beach Measure C asks voters in the city whether to raise the hotel tax from 13 percent to 14 percent.
Today, guests at hotels and short-term rentals pay the city a 13 percent tax on their rent.
The City Council placed Measure C on the ballot, and it needs a simple majority to pass.
The rate would rise to 14 percent, and the added money could pay for any city purpose.
Cleaning, resort, parking and other fees would count as rent, so the tax would apply to them too.
Online booking companies would collect the tax for the city, and the tax would last until voters repeal it.
The City Attorney's impartial analysis estimates the increase would raise about $250,000 a year.
Supporters say only visitors pay the tax, and it helps keep lifeguards on beaches and restrooms clean.
No argument against Measure C appears in the San Diego County Voter Information Guide for this election.
A yes vote supports Measure C and its 1 percent increase in the hotel tax.
A no vote opposes Measure C and its 1 percent increase in the hotel tax.
