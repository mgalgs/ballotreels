City of Banning Measure W asks voters in the city whether to approve a development agreement for a commercial and industrial project.
The City Council approved the agreement in April 2026, and a referendum petition signed by voters put it on the ballot.
The agreement would give the property owner set rights to develop, in exchange for public benefits.
The owner would give the city land for open space, a park and a fire station, and pay toward roads and a fire station.
The owner could count up to $2 million of sales tax from the property against those benefits.
The Riverside County guide does not include a cost estimate for the measure.
The ballot question says the agreement includes over $40 million in public benefits to the city.
Supporters say the agreement guarantees more than $40 million in public benefits, and nearly $3 million a year for the city.
Opponents say it gives the developer a 30-year deal for nearly 5 million square feet of warehouses, which Banning cannot change alone.
A yes vote means Ordinance 1620 and the development agreement will take effect.
A no vote means Ordinance 1620 and the development agreement will not take effect.
