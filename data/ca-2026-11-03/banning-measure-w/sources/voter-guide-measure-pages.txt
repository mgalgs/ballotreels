              IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE “W”
    Overview. Measure W is a referendum regarding City of Banning Ordinance
No. 1620 which was adopted by the City Council on April 14, 2026, approving
a Development Agreement between the City and NP Banning Industrial, LLC.
The Measure was placed on the ballot by referendum signed by the requisite
number of voters.
     Development Agreement. The Ordinance vests development rights subject
to the terms and conditions of the Agreement and allows the Owner to develop
a master planned project including industrial, commercial, and public facilities,
parkland and open space, with on-and off-site improvements (“Project”) on the
Owner’s real property located within the City and County in exchange for various
public benefits.
     Under the terms of the Agreement and in exchange for the vested
development rights related to the Project, the Agreement requires the Owner
to provide the following benefits subject to the specified timing and terms of the
Development Agreement:
     1.     Pay the City the lesser of $8,000,000 or $1.44 per square foot of
entitled warehouse/logistics/industrial building square footage.
    2.     Construct, install, or fund the construction of road improvements
to Sunset Avenue, Lincoln Street, Bobcat Road, Highland Home Road, utility
improvements for sewer, water, recycled water, storm drains as well as other
public improvements.
    3.     Grant property for the Sun Lakes Boulevard extension.
    4.     Pay the City $5,100,000 towards the Sun Lakes Boulevard extension.
    5.     Grant the City at least 2 ½ acres for a fire station.
    6.     Grant the City approximately 2 acres for a water tank.
    7.     Grant the City property for a utility substation.
    8.     Grant the City property for a reverse osmosis facility.
   9.      Pay the City $10,000,000 for design and construction of an electrical
substation.
    10. Maintain all private roads, sidewalks, and landscape areas within and
fronting the public right-of-way on the Property.
    11.    Grant the City approximately 52.8 acres as open space.
   12. Grant the City approximately 12 acres for a park and construct and
maintain the park facilities.
     13. Pay the City $16,000,000 for a fire station and another $1,000,000
for fire equipment.
    14. Pay the City $750,000 for downtown beautification or small business
assistance.
    15.    Notification to City residents about job opportunities in the Project.
    16.    Pay the City $500,000 for an animal shelter.
The Owner may claim sales tax revenues generated by the industrial property
as a credit against the Public Benefits of up to $2,000,000.
    Summary:
    A “Yes” vote by a majority of those voting on Measure W means Ordinance
No. 1620 and the Development Agreement will become effective.
    A “No” vote by a majority of those voting on Measure W means Ordinance
No. 1620 and the Development Agreement will not become effective.
    The above statement is the impartial analysis of Measure “W.” If you
desire a copy of the Ordinance or Measure, please call the City Election
Official’s Office at (951) 922-3102 and a copy will be mailed to you at no cost.
