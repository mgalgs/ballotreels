## Quoted passages

- Question (p.23): "Shall Ordinance No.1620, approving a development agreement with NP Banning Industrial to develop a master planned project including commercial and industrial uses, various public facilities with on and off-site related improvements subject to the terms of the agreement which includes dedicating over 68 acres of land to the City for open space, a fire station, parks, and public utilities and over $40,000,000 in public benefits to the City, be adopted?"
- What it is / who placed it (p.49): "Measure W is a referendum regarding City of Banning Ordinance No. 1620 which was adopted by the City Council on April 14, 2026, approving a Development Agreement between the City and NP Banning Industrial, LLC. The Measure was placed on the ballot by referendum signed by the requisite number of voters."
- Analysis (p.49): the Agreement requires the Owner to "Pay the City the lesser of $8,000,000 or $1.44 per square foot of entitled warehouse/logistics/industrial building square footage."
