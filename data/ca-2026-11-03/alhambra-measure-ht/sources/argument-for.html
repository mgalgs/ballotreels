ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE HT

Fair to Everyone, Free for Residents — Vote YES on HT!

Yes on HT is a simple update to Alhambra's existing hotel tax. It just requires visitors staying in short-term neighborhood rentals to pay the same tax paid by hotel guests.

When Alhambra voters adopted our local hotel tax, short-term rentals didn't exist. Today, they're common in our neighborhoods, and these guests use the same city services — 911, roads, and parks — as hotel guests do.

Yes on HT simply closes that loophole. It does NOT raise the hotel tax rate. It simply makes the existing, voter-approved tax fair/equal for all visitors by ensuring all guests pay fairly for the services they use while here.

Yes on HT doesn't raise taxes on Alhambra residents who aren't lodging guests. It only applies to visitors staying overnight.

Vote YES on HT to:

-Keep Alhambra's streets and neighborhoods safe.

-Provide locally-controlled funds to keep 150 miles of streets, 13 miles of storm drains, and 80 acres of parks and playfields clean, safe, and maintained.

-Fix potholes and improve roads, alleys, intersections, bike lanes, and traffic signals.

When you call 911, seconds matter! YES on HT:

-Ensures Alhambra has enough police officers, firefighters, and paramedics to respond quickly to emergencies in our neighborhoods and downtown.

-Is accountable to YOU, the Alhambra taxpayer. Independent audits and public disclosure of how every dollar is spent mean funds are watched closely. By law, the State and County cannot take this money away. Every dime is spent for the benefit of Alhambra.

Join a unanimous City Council, local firefighters, public safety advocates, small business owners, and community leaders in keeping Alhambra safe and strong — by fairly updating our outdated ordinance to reflect today's realities.

For Fairness and Local Funds, vote Yes on HT!
