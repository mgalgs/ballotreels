CITY OF ALHAMBRA – (2)
VOTE PASSAGE – Majority Vote 50%+1
HT     Modernization/No Tax Rate Increase Measure. To maintain city services, such as: Fire
       protection, paramedic, 911 emergency response, crime prevention and keeping streets,
       infrastructure, and parks safe and well-maintained shall City of Alhambra's measure
       modernizing the hotel/motel transient occupancy tax be adopted at the current 12% rate
       for stays up to 30 days to include short-term lodging, providing an additional $134,000
       annually for city services until ended by voters, with all money locally controlled?
       Supporters: Alhambra Firefighters Association; Alhambra Police Officers Association
       Opponents: None submitted.
