Measure H asks the voters of this county whether to raise the tax on short-term lodging outside the cities from 5 to 12 percent.
Today, guests at hotels, vacation rentals, campgrounds and other lodging in unincorporated areas pay 5 percent of the rent, for stays of 30 days or less.
Measure H would raise that tax to 12 percent of the rent, starting in January 2027.
The money would go into the county general fund, for any lawful purpose set through the yearly budget.
The tax would last until repealed or changed by the board of supervisors or by voters, and the measure needs a simple majority.
The impartial analysis says the ballot question estimates the increase would raise about $58,500 more each year.
Opponents say the tax would make rural lodging less competitive and hurt small businesses that rely on tourism, with no spending guarantees.
Supporters say the county rate is far below many nearby counties and cities, and visitors would help pay for services they use.
A yes vote approves raising the tax from 5 to 12 percent of the rent.
A no vote keeps the tax at 5 percent.
