Strengthening Our Local Economy and Protecting Essential County Services

Measure H would increase the Transient Occupancy Tax (TOT)—a tax paid by tourists and visitors staying overnight at hotels and other lodging establishments in the unincorporated areas of Solano County—from 5% to 12%.

Solano County's current TOT rate is significantly lower than those of many surrounding counties and cities. As tourism and visitation continue to grow, increasing the TOT to 12% provides a reasonable way to recover a portion of the costs associated with serving visitors while bringing Solano County more in line with our regional peers.

A 12% TOT rate is estimated to generate approximately $58,000 in additional annual revenue, which would help fund essential County services, including public safety and health and welfare programs. All revenue generated by this measure will stay local and cannot be taken away by the State or Federal governments. This allows visitors to help support the community and services they use while minimizing the impact on local residents.

This measure helps strengthen our local economy, protect essential County services, maintain critical infrastructure, and ensure that visitors and large corporations contribute their fair share toward the public services and resources they use.

Most importantly, these revenues will stay local, helping support our community rather than being sent away to the State and Federal governments.

Cassandra R. James
Solano County Supervisor, District 1

Mitch Mashburn
Solano County Supervisor, District 5
