## Facts and quotes

**Ballot question** (Notice of Measures, 8/5/2026, https://content.solanocounty.gov/sites/default/files/2026-08/Notice_of_Measures.pdf): "To fund local programs and essential public services, shall Solano County increase its existing Transient Occupancy Tax, paid by tourists and others staying overnight at short-term lodging facilities in unincorporated areas (excluding cities), from 5% to 12% of the total rent charged, providing approximately an additional $58,500 annually, until ended by the voters?"

**Impartial analysis** (`impartial-analysis.pdf`, signed "Carrie Blacklock, Solano County Counsel"), key statements:
- "Measure H would amend Article II of Chapter 11 of the Solano County Code to increase the County's Transient Occupancy Tax ("TOT") within the unincorporated areas of Solano County from 5% to 12% of the rent charged for short-term lodging."
- "The tax generally applies to stays of 30 days or less and is paid by the transient occupant".
- "According to the ballot question, the increase is estimated to generate approximately $58,500 in additional annual revenue. The ordinance provides that the tax is a general tax, meaning the revenue would be deposited into the County's General Fund".
- "If approved by a majority of voters, the ordinance would become effective on January 1, 2027. The increased tax would remain in effect unless repealed or amended by the Solano County Board of Supervisors or by the voters. If the measure is not approved, the County's Transient Occupancy Tax would remain at 5%."

Note: the analysis says the increased tax remains "unless repealed or amended by the Solano County Board of Supervisors or by the voters"; the ballot question says "until ended by the voters". Both as printed.

**Who votes:** not stated in the analysis; the Notice says "submitted to the voters of Solano County". Not confirmed whether city voters vote on it (resolution is a scan).
