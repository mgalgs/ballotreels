## Key facts, quoted

- **Ballot question** (MP1): "To protect quality education in local schools, continue supporting math, science, engineering, technology, arts, and skilled trades/job training programs preparing students for success in college/careers; attracting/retaining highly qualified teachers; and maintaining manageable class sizes, shall Tahoe Truckee Unified School District's measure be adopted, renewing the expiring $148 annual school parcel tax with a $40 increase, for nine years, with senior exemptions, annual inflation adjustments, independent oversight/ audits, and all funds (approximately $7,000,000 annually) benefiting local students?"
- **Existing tax:** "In November 2018, the voters of the Tahoe Truckee Unified School District ("District") approved Measure AA. Measure AA imposed a special tax in the amount of $148 on all parcels of real property within the District for a nine-year period. Measure AA will expire in July of 2028, and the District is proposing this measure in its place."
- **New tax:** "This measure would authorize the District to levy a special parcel tax of $188 on each taxable parcel within the District for a period of nine years, beginning July 1, 2027." "The measure estimates that the tax would generate approximately $7 million annually, although actual revenues would vary based upon the number of taxable parcels and the annual inflation adjustment."
- **Uses:** "funding academic programs; career technical education; classroom equipment and instructional supplies; technology equipment, services, and curriculum; physical education; visual and performing arts; library services; and student counseling, nursing, and social-emotional support services."
- **Exemptions:** "qualifying property owners who are at least 65 years of age, persons receiving Supplemental Security Income due to disability, and qualifying recipients of Social Security Disability Insurance benefits meeting the income and residency requirements specified in the measure."
- **Placed by / threshold:** "This measure was placed on the ballot by the governing board of the District through adoption of Resolution No. 33-2025-2026 on June 24, 2026. Approval by two-thirds of the voters voting on the measure is required for its adoption."
- **Yes/No:** "A "YES" vote on this Measure is a vote to authorize the special tax described in the measure." / "A "NO" vote on this Measure is a vote against the proposed special tax. If the measure is not approved, Measure AA will continue to be collected according to its existing terms."
- **Analysis signed:** "Clayton T. Cook, County Counsel" (County of Placer).
- **Argument in favor:** signed "Dr. David Steakley, NTHS Science Teacher" (as extracted). No rebuttal to it was submitted.
- **Argument against signers:** Mark Wright, Chairman, Placer County Republican Party; John "MAC" Young, Chairman, Nevada County Republican Party; Todd White, El Dorado County GOP; Herb W. Morgan, Candidate CA State Controller; Eric Eisenhammer, President, Gold Country Taxpayers Association.
- **Rebuttal to argument against signers:** Amanda Helm, Member, TTUSD Oversight Committee; Edward Hilton, Tahoe City Pastor; Annie Stefani, Tahoe City Business Owner; John Neuberger, Principal, Truckee High School; Alejandro Brambila, Owner, Las Panchitas Restaurant.
