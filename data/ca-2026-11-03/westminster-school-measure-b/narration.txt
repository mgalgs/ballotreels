Westminster School District Measure B asks the district's voters whether to approve $128 million in school bonds.
The district would borrow the money and repay it from a tax on property in the district.
Measure B needs 55 percent of the vote to pass.
The money would upgrade aging classrooms and labs, fix roofs, plumbing, heating and electrical systems, and improve school security.
The district's tax statement estimates a tax of $30 per $100,000 of assessed value, the same every year, through 2060-2061.
The statement estimates the total to repay, with interest, at about $276 million.
Supporters say Measure B repairs aging schools and keeps all the money local.
No argument against Measure B was submitted for the Orange County voter guide.
A yes vote is in favor of letting the district sell the bonds.
A no vote is against letting the district sell the bonds.
