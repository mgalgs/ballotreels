## Quoted passages

- Question (p.1): "To provide safe, modern schools by repairing, upgrading and equipping aging classrooms, labs, instructional technology, and school facilities to support student achievement in math, science, technology, arts and engineering, fixing deteriorated roofs, plumbing, HVAC, electrical systems and improving school safety/security systems, shall Westminster School District’s measure authorizing $128,000,000 in bonds at legal rates be adopted, levying $30 per $100,000 assessed value ($8,000,000 annually) while bonds are outstanding, with citizen oversight, independent audits, all money locally-controlled?"
- Who placed it / threshold (p.5): "This measure was placed on the ballot by the governing board (“Board”) of the Westminster School District (“District”). This measure, if approved by 55 percent of the votes cast on the measure, will authorize the District to issue and sell up to $128,000,000 in general obligation bonds."
- Analysis (p.5): "The District’s stated best estimate in its tax rate statement of the average annual tax rate required to fund the bonds is $30 per $100,000 of assessed value. The final fiscal year in which the tax is anticipated to be collected is fiscal year 2060-61. The best estimate of the total debt service, including principal and interest, required to be repaid if all bonds are issued and sold is $276,000,000."
- Analysis (p.5): "State law requires the establishment of an independent citizens’ oversight committee for ensuring that bond proceeds are expended as specified in the measure and as provided by law."
