Measure EL asks the voters of this city whether to limit the mayor and council members to 12 total years in city office.
The City Council placed Measure EL on the ballot, as an amendment to the city charter.
Today, the city has no limit on the number of terms a mayor or council member may serve.
No one could file to run for mayor or council if winning would bring their time in city elective office past 12 years.
The count would include the term being sought, any current term up to that election, and time served in past terms.
Terms won or appointed on or before November 3, 2026 would not count toward the 12 years.
The limit would apply to write-in candidates too.
The county Registrar's list of measures and the impartial analysis do not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Supporters say 12 years gives officials enough time to gain experience and finish long-term projects, while term limits bring accountability and new leadership.
The official county list of measures for this election says that no argument against Measure EL was submitted, by any group or person.
A yes vote imposes term limits on the mayor and council members.
A no vote does not establish such limits.
