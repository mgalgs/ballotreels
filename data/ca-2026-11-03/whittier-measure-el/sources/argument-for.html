ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE EL

Term Limits on City Council

A YES vote on Measure EL will establish reasonable term limits for members of the Whittier City Council and Mayor, balancing the value of experienced leadership with the need for new perspectives and opportunities for public service.

Measure EL limits elected service on the Whittier City Council to a total of 12 years, regardless of whether that service is as a Councilmember, Mayor, or any combination of the two. This allows elected officials sufficient time to gain experience, complete long-term projects, and effectively represent the community while ensuring regular opportunities for new leadership.

Term limits promote accountability by encouraging competitive elections and giving voters more frequent opportunities to choose among new candidates with fresh ideas and diverse experiences. They help broaden civic participation and foster a City Council and Mayor that reflect the evolving needs of the Whittier community.

Term limits are widely used throughout California. Many cities; including Los Angeles, West Hollywood, Culver City, Glendale, Redondo Beach, Torrance, Santa Barbara, and Anaheim; have adopted term limits for their local elected officials. California also limits the terms of its Governor, other statewide constitutional officers, and members of the State Legislature.

Measure EL provides a balanced approach by allowing experienced leaders sufficient time to serve while ensuring opportunities for new voices and ideas in City government. We respectfully ask for your YES vote.

For accountable government, expanded opportunities for community leadership, and a healthy local democracy,

Vote YES on Measure EL.
