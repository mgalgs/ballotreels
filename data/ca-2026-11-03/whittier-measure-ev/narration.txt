Measure EV asks the voters of this city whether to move city elections from April to the day of the November statewide election.
The City Council placed Measure EV on the ballot, as an amendment to the city charter.
Today, the city holds its general elections every two years, on the second Tuesday in April.
City elections would still come every two years, but on the same day as the statewide general election in November.
The first would be held on November 7, 2028, instead of April 11, 2028.
The terms of the mayor and council members would be extended until the people elected after them take office.
A council member elected in April 2026, for example, would serve until the winner of the November 2030 election takes office.
The county Registrar's list of measures and the impartial analysis do not include a cost estimate for the measure.
Supporters say turnout fell after the city returned to April elections, and that November elections would draw more voters and save money.
Opponents say moving to November protects incumbents, helps special interests, costs taxpayers more money, and means waiting three weeks for results.
A yes vote moves city elections to the day of the statewide general election.
A no vote keeps city elections in April of even-numbered years.
