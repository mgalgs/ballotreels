## Ballot question and supporters/opponents (verbatim, from the county's final measures list)

> Shall the measure amending the City's charter so that the City's general municipal elections (currently held in April of each even-numbered year) are held in each even-numbered year on the same day as the November statewide general elections, be adopted?
>
> Supporters: None submitted.
> Opponents: None submitted.

Vote threshold: Majority Vote 50%+1 (county's final measures list).
