IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE EV

By Keith Collins, City Attorney

This measure, which was placed on the ballot by the Whittier City Council, proposes to adopt an ordinance that amends Whittier’s City Charter to change the date on which general municipal elections are held.

Whittier holds its general municipal elections in each even-numbered year. Currently, these elections are held on the second Tuesday in April. By contrast, Statewide General Elections are held on the first Tuesday following the first Monday in November.

This measure would continue to require that a general municipal election be held in each even-numbered year; however each such election would be held on the same date as the Statewide General Election.

If this measure passes, the first municipal general election with a new date will be held on Tuesday, November 7, 2028 (instead of April 11, 2028). The current terms of office of the Mayor and of each Member of the City Council will be extended until their successor takes office. For example, a Councilmember who was elected to a four-year term at the election held in April of 2026 will continue to hold office until the person elected at the November general election in 2030 takes office.

A “yes” vote is a vote in favor of changing the date of the City’s general municipal election to coincide with the Statewide General Election. A “no” vote is a vote to keep the City’s general municipal election date in April of even-numbered years.

The above statement is an impartial analysis of Measure EV.
