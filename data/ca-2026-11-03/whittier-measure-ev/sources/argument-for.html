ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE EV

Vote YES on Measure EV

Whittier has an opportunity to strengthen our democracy by aligning City elections with the statewide November election instead of holding them separately in April.

When Whittier's municipal election was held alongside the statewide election in 2020, more than 22,000 residents cast ballots. After the City returned to stand-alone April elections, voter participation declined significantly. Aligning our elections with the November statewide ballot will give more residents the opportunity to have their voices heard in local decisions.

The decisions made by our City government affect our daily lives; from maintaining our streets and sidewalks to supporting our parks, public safety, and planning for Whittier's future. These important decisions should reflect the participation of as many residents as possible.

Consolidating elections will also reduce the cost of administering separate municipal elections, saving taxpayer dollars while increasing civic engagement.

A YES vote means:

- Higher voter participation.

- Greater representation of the community.

- More efficient use of taxpayer funds.

- A stronger, more representative local government.

Join us in supporting a more engaged and representative Whittier. We respectfully ask for your YES vote.

Vote YES on Measure EV.
