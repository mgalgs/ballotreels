## Facts and quotes

**Ballot question** (city "Measure P" page, heading "Language That Will Appear on the Ballot", https://www.americancanyon.gov/Work/Elections-Central/Measure-P): "Shall an ordinance funding American Canyon's general City services, such as maintaining 911 response/neighborhood police patrols; preventing crime; reducing wildfire risk by managing brush/other flammable vegetation; addressing speeding/unsafe driving; keeping public areas/parks safe/clean; maintaining and expanding safe places for children to play; maintaining parks, trails, youth/adult recreation, senior programming, amongst others; by establishing a 1% sales tax, providing approximately $3,700,000 annually until ended by voters, requiring audits, spending disclosures, funds locally controlled, be adopted?"

**Measure title** (city Ballot Measures page): "AMENDING THE AMERICAN CANYON MUNICIPAL CODE BY ADDING CHAPTER 3.22 TO TITLE 3, IMPOSING A TRANSACTIONS AND USE TAX TO BE ADMINISTERED BY THE CALIFORNIA DEPARTMENT OF TAX AND FEE ADMINISTRATION"

**Impartial analysis** ("City Attorney's Impartial Analysis of Measure P", dated August 4, 2026, Matthew T. Summers, American Canyon City Attorney), key statements:
- "If approved, Measure P would impose a 1% transactions and use tax, more commonly known as a local sales tax, within the City of American Canyon."
- "All revenues from Measure P would belong to the City, and would be placed into the City's general fund to be used for any lawful purpose ... The City estimates that Measure P will generate approximately $3.7 million in new local revenue per year."
- **Who votes / vote needed:** "Measure P must be approved by a simple majority (50% plus one) of the City's voters. If approved, the tax will remain in effect until repealed by the American Canyon City Council or the voters."

Note: the analysis says the tax remains "until repealed by the American Canyon City Council or the voters"; the ballot question says "until ended by voters". Both as printed.

**Argument in favor:** "Vote YES on Measure P to keep American Canyon safe, clean, and financially strong". Signers are not in the PDF's text layer; check the PDF.
