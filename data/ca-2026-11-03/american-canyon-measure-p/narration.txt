City of American Canyon Measure P asks American Canyon voters whether to add a 1 percent sales tax for city services.
The City Council placed Measure P on the ballot in June, with one member voting no.
The tax would add $1 for every $100 of taxable purchases in American Canyon, on top of existing state and county sales taxes.
The money would go into the general fund of the city, for any lawful purpose.
The tax would last until repealed by the City Council or by voters, and needs a simple majority to pass.
The city attorney's impartial analysis says the city estimates Measure P would raise about $3.7 million a year.
Supporters say Measure P protects police and wildfire prevention, and visitors are expected to pay over half.
No argument against Measure P appears among the measure documents that the City of American Canyon posted.
A yes vote permits the city to impose the 1 percent sales tax.
A no vote prohibits the city from imposing the 1 percent sales tax.
