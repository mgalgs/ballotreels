## Quoted facts

- Ballot question: "Patterson Public Safety/ City Services Measure. Shall the measure funding City of Patterson services such as maintaining 911 response, helping prevent crimes/ thefts/ burglaries /human trafficking; keeping public areas, children’s play areas safe/ clean; repairing streets/ potholes; protecting local drinking water sources; retaining/ attracting businesses; supporting youth programs, for general government use by establishing a 1¢ sales tax providing approximately $9,000,000 annually until ended by voters; requiring audits/ citizens’ oversight, spending disclosure, funds used locally, be adopted?"
- Vote needed: notice, "this measure shall be approved upon receiving a majority of the votes cast on the measure (50% + 1)."
- Origin: analysis, "On June 16, 2026, the City Council of the City of Patterson (“City”), acting on its own motion, not by voter petition, voted to place Measure L on the ballot for the November 3, 2026, general municipal election."
- Rate: "The City’s current sales tax rate is 7.875%. If the measure passes, the new sales tax rate would be 8.875%."
- Revenue: "It is estimated that the proposed sales tax will provide an additional Nine Million Dollars ($9,000,000) in annual local funding."
- General tax: "This sales tax is a “general tax”, meaning that revenues raised from the tax would go into the City’s general fund to pay for any lawful City program, service, or improvement."
- Oversight: "The measure would also require the City Council to establish a seven-member Citizen Oversight Committee to annually review expenditure of tax revenues and prepare an annual accountability report".
