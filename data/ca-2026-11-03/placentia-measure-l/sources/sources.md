## Quoted passages

- Question (p.1): "Shall the measure increasing the rate of the City of Placentia’s existing transient occupancy tax (hotel tax), paid only by hotel guests, to 14%, funding city services such as police patrols, fire protection, 911 response, and road repairs; requiring citizen oversight and annual audits, generating about $715,000 annually, until ended by voters, be adopted?"
- Who placed it / threshold (p.3): "The measure was placed on the ballot by the City Council of the City of Placentia. To be adopted, this measure requires the approval of a majority of the voters casting ballots."
- Analysis (p.3): "The City of Placentia currently imposes a 10% transient occupancy tax on transients (hotel guests) who occupy a hotel room for 30 consecutive days or less."
- Analysis (p.3): "Thus, short-term rentals of homes or of rooms in homes would be subject to the tax."
- Analysis (p.3): "The ordinance requires that, in addition to independent annual audits of tax proceeds and expenditures, the City’s existing Citizens’ Oversight Committee review expenditures of funds generated by the Hotel Tax and prepare an annual report of such expenditures."
- Analysis (p.3): "A NO vote rejects the ordinance, and would leave the Hotel Tax at 10%."
