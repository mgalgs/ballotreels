Placentia Measure L asks the voters of Placentia whether to raise the hotel tax from 10 percent to 14 percent.
Today, hotel guests in Placentia pay a 10 percent tax on their room rent, for stays of 30 days or less.
The money goes into the general fund, which pays for services such as police patrols and road repairs.
Measure L would raise the rate to 14 percent, starting January 1, 2027.
The ballot question says the higher rate would bring in about $715,000 a year.
Supporters say visitors pay the tax, not residents, and the rate trails other Orange County rates.
No argument against Measure L was submitted to appear in the Orange County Voter Information Guide.
A yes vote approves raising the hotel tax to 14 percent.
A no vote rejects the increase, leaving the hotel tax at 10 percent.
