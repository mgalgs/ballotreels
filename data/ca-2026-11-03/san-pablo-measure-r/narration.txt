Measure R asks the voters of this city whether to add a sales tax of one-half percent for 5 years, then one-quarter percent for 5 years, after which it ends.
The city council placed Measure R on the ballot on July 13, 2026.
Measure R needs a simple majority to pass.
The tax would start in 2027, on April 1, July 1 or October 1, depending on a state bill, and end 10 years later.
It is a general tax on retail sales in the city.
Each year, independent auditors for the city would review the tax money, and a committee of at least five residents or local business representatives would review and report on its use.
The ballot question says the tax would raise about $2 million a year at first, then about $1 million a year.

No argument for Measure R appears among the official documents gathered for this video.
A yes vote adds the temporary sales tax for 10 years.
A no vote does not add the tax.
