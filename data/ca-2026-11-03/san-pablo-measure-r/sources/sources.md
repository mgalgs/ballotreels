## Ballot question (draft resolution, Section 1)

> To provide funding to maintain City services, including crime prevention, police protection, reducing illegal dumping, the City’s daily senior meal program, youth scholarship programs, assisting the homeless, street maintenance, and other general services, shall San Pablo enact a temporary ½-cent sales tax for 5 years, generating approximately $2,000,000 annually, then reduced to ¼-cent for 5 years, generating approximately $1,000,000 annually, then terminate; with all funds staying in San Pablo, citizen oversight, and mandated annual audits?

This is the text in the agenda draft. **Not confirmed** that the adopted resolution or the printed ballot uses identical words.

## Who votes on it

- Draft resolution, Section 1: "the City Council hereby submits the Ordinance to the voters at the Election"; "The Ordinance requires the approval of a simple majority (50% plus one) of those voting on it."
- Legistar matter 26-379: status "Passed", MatterPassedDate 2026-07-13, MatterEnactmentNumber "RES 2026-121".

## Key statements (draft resolution recitals — the City's own document, not an impartial analysis)

- "the Ordinance would impose a new general tax of one-half of one percent (0.5%) on the retail sale, or the use and consumption, of tangible personal property within the City’s limits for the first 5 years and then decrease to one-quarter of one percent (0.25%) for the next 5 years"
- "the effective date for the proposed Ordinance depends on whether the State passes Senate Bill 762 (Arreguin), which concerns the maximum transactions and use tax rate the City may impose at one time"
- "if SB 762 is not passed in time to take effect by the end of June 2027, then the proposed Ordinance would go into effect on October 1, 2027"
- "in any event, the proposed Ordinance would expire ten years after it goes into effect"
