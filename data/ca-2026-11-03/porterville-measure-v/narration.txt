Measure V asks the voters of this city whether to change how empty seats on the City Council are filled.
The City Council placed Measure V on the ballot in July 2026.
Today the council appoints someone to an empty seat until voters choose a successor at the next regular city election.
If the council does not fill the seat within 30 days, the mayor fills it.
Under Measure V, the council would appoint someone only when less than a year remains in the term.
With a year or more left, a special election would fill the seat, and the mayor would no longer fill seats.
As today, if most council seats become empty, a special election would fill them.
Measure V needs a majority of the vote to pass.
Neither the notice of election nor the resolution includes a cost estimate for Measure V.

A yes vote on Measure V changes how empty council seats are filled.
A no vote on Measure V keeps the current way empty council seats are filled.
