## Facts and quoted passages

- **Ballot question** (July 21 replacement resolution, Section 3, packet p. 838; identical in the June 16 resolution, p. 532):
  > Shall the City Charter be amended to require that vacancies on the City Council be filled by appointment of the City Council when less than one year remains in the unexpired term, and by a special election when one year or more remains in the unexpired term?
- **Who votes:** voters of the City of Porterville (Section 2: "a General Municipal Election for the purpose of submitting a City Council charter amendment proposal to the voters for approval").
- **Vote needed:** Section 5: "Passage of this measure requires a regular majority of votes."
- **History:** July 21 staff report (p. 829): "On June 16, 2026, the City Council adopted ... Resolution No. 45-2026 submitting a proposed amendment to Section 16 of the City Charter relating to Vacancies in Office ... The replacement resolutions do not alter the substance of the proposed Charter amendments previously approved by the City Council." Adoption of the July 21 replacement: NOT confirmed (minutes not found).
- **What the new Section 16 says** (Exhibit A, p. 841):
  > A vacancy in any legislative office, from whatever cause arising excluding any vacancy created due to a successful recall election, shall be filled by appointment of the City Council if less than one year remains in the unexpired term. If one year or more remains in the unexpired term, the vacancy shall be filled by a special election. Notwithstanding the foregoing, if for any reason the seats of a majority of the City Council become vacant, the City Clerk shall call a special election to fill the vacancies for the unexpired terms ...
- **Current rule being replaced** (Section 4, struck text, p. 838): "shall be filled by appointment by the council, such appointee to hold office until the next general municipal election, when a successor shall be chosen by the electors for the unexpired term; provided, that if the council fails to agree or for any other reason does not fill such vacancy within thirty days after the same occurs, then such vacancy shall be filled by the mayor". Strike-out formatting is only visible in the PDF.
- **June 16 staff report** (p. 525-526): "Under the proposed amendment, a vacancy would be filled by appointment of the City Council if less than one year remains in the unexpired term. If one year or more remains in the unexpired term, the vacancy would be filled by a special election in accordance with applicable law."
- **Cost** (June 16 staff report, p. 526): "approximately $35,000" per amendment; "approximately $70,000" for both.
- **Impartial analysis assigned** (Section 7) and **Council argument authorized** (Section 9), as for Measure U.
