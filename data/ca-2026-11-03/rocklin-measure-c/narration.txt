Measure C asks the voters of this city whether to add a half-cent sales tax for general city services.
The City Council voted unanimously to place Measure C on the ballot, and it needs a majority to pass.
The tax would be added to today's sales taxes, and collected the same way.
Measure C would be a general tax: the money would go into the city general fund, for any lawful city service.
The impartial analysis says the money could keep up services such as 911 response, police and fire, parks, open space and wildfire prevention.
The tax would start on April 1, 2027, and last until voters repeal it.
The impartial analysis estimates the tax would raise about $8 million a year.
Supporters say forecasts show the city falling short of funds for its services, and visitors would share the cost.
No argument against Measure C was submitted, and the Measure C voter guide pages include no argument against the measure.
A yes vote authorizes the half-cent sales tax.
A no vote does not authorize the half-cent sales tax.
