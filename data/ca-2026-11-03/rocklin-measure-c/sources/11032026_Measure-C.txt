                                      ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE C
Invest in Rocklin - VOTE YES on Measure C to maintain our safe community, family-friendly, small-town character,
and outstanding quality of life.
Rocklin is recognized for its safe neighborhoods, beautiful parks, outstanding emergency services, rapid 911
response times and its strong fiscal management. However, due to inflation and other external impacts, long-term
financial forecasts show the City will not have necessary funds to maintain the quality of life we all enjoy.
Measure C provides a prudent, responsible solution that protects what matters to Rocklin residents: safe
neighborhoods, rapid emergency response, clean, inviting parks for all to enjoy, economic prosperity, strong property values,
and a vibrant future for our city.
Passage of Measure C will:
•   Keep public areas and parks healthy, safe and clean
•   Maintain rapid 911 emergency response times
•   Maintain fire protection and police response services
•   Retain/attract qualified firefighters and emergency first responders
•   Maintain the City’s financial stability
Taxpayer Protections are MANDATORY! ALL funds stay LOCALLY CONTROLLED, benefiting City of Rocklin
residents ONLY. Funds must stay local and the State cannot take funding away from Rocklin residents. Public spending
and independent audits ensure local control of funds is maintained.
By law, essential purchases like groceries and prescription medications WON’T be taxed. Visitors to Rocklin will pay
their fair share, so residents won’t shoulder the entire cost.
Local firefighters, police, seniors, parents, residents, community and business leaders, urge your support. VOTING
YES will maintain Rocklin’s quality of life for years to come.
Your VOTE is an investment in YOUR future.
Maintain what matters. VOTE YES on C.
