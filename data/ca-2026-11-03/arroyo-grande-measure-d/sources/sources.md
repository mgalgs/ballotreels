## Quoted facts

- **Ballot question** (City of Arroyo Grande Notice of Election): "CITY OF ARROYO GRANDE MEASURE D-26 Shall the measure to repeal and replace Measure A (1990) and Measure E-16 (2016) to allow the City of Arroyo Grande to participate in the California State Water Project without requiring prior voter approval, providing another potential water supply source, be adopted?"
- **Vote needed** (city's Measure D-26 page): "Measure D requires approval by a simple majority of the eligible voters in the City of Arroyo Grande who cast their ballots during the November 3, 2026 election. If passed, the ordinance would take effect ten days after the City Council certifies the election results."
- **Who votes:** Arroyo Grande voters. Impartial analysis (p. 31): "If approved by a majority of Arroyo Grande voters, this Measure would repeal and replace Measure A (1990) and Measure E-16 (2016), and permit the City of Arroyo Grande to participate in the California State Water Project and purchase State water without prior voter approval and without declaring a local water emergency."
- **Impartial analysis** (p. 31, City Attorney Isaac Rosen): "On June 9, 2026 the City Council unanimously voted to place Measure "D-26" on the November 3, 2026 general municipal election ballot. By placing Measure "D-26" on the ballot, the City complies with California Elections Code section 9217, which requires a majority of voters to approve repealing or amending an ordinance previously adopted by voters." … "Instead, if Measure D-26 is approved, participation in the California State Water Project could be authorized by a majority vote of a quorum of the City Council." … "A "yes" vote on Measure "D-26" will authorize the City to participate in the California State Water Project without majority voter approval or a declared local water emergency. A "no" vote on Measure "D-26" will mean that the City may only participate in the California State Water Project with majority voter approval or with a Council-declared local water emergency."
- **Argument in favor** signed: "s/ Caren Ray Russom Mayor, City of Arroyo Grande Principal Officer, on behalf of the Arroyo Grande City Council" (p. 32).
