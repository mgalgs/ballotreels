       CITY OF SOUTH EL MONTE – (2)
       VOTE PASSAGE – Majority Vote 50%+1
CL    Term Limits Reform Measure Shall the terms served by the City Council in the City of
      South El Monte be limited to no more than four (4) four-year terms in their lifetime and
      where service for the unexpired balance of a vacated City Council seat (whether by
      appointment or election) will not count as service for a full four-year term even if such
      service is for less than four years in duration? Supporters: Gloria Olmos, Mayor; Rudy
      Bojorquez, Councilmember; Hector Delgado, Councilmember; Larry Rodriguez,
      Councilmember; Manuel Acosta, Councilmember Opponents: None submitted.
