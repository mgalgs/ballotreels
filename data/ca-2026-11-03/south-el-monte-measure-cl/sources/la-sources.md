## County measure list excerpt

See `county-measure-list-excerpt.txt`, cut from the LA County RR/CC's official "FINAL — GENERAL ELECTION November 3, 2026 — Measures Appearing on the Ballot" (Rev. 9/22/26, the `-v-2` printing):

> **Term Limits Reform Measure Shall the terms served by the City Council in the City of South El Monte be limited to no more than four (4) four-year terms in their lifetime and where service for the unexpired balance of a vacated City Council seat (whether by appointment or election) will not count as service for a full four-year term even if such service is for less than four years in duration?**
>
> Supporters: Gloria Olmos, Mayor; Rudy Bojorquez, Councilmember; Hector Delgado, Councilmember; Larry Rodriguez, Councilmember; Manuel Acosta, Councilmember
> Opponents: None submitted.

Vote threshold: Majority Vote 50%+1.
