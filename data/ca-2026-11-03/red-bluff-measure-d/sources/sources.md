## Facts with quotes

- **Ballot question (Resolution 21-2026 §1):** "MEASURE #__: APPOINTIVE CITY CLERK-TREASURER Shall the Offices of City Clerk and City Treasurer be Appointive?" Yes / No.
- **Measure text (§2):** "The City Clerk and City Treasurer shall no longer be elective officials in the City of Red Bluff. From and after November 7, 2028, for both City Clerk and City Treasurer, or upon an earlier vacancy in the office of City Clerk or City Treasurer, the City Clerk and City Treasurer shall be appointed by the City Council. ... are not required to be a resident or elector in the City. The City Council may by Ordinance vest in the City Manager its authority to appoint the City Clerk and City Treasurer."
- **Vote needed:** §3: "In the event the proposition receives approval by a majority of the votes cast, the proposition is adopted". Staff report: "A majority vote is required for this ballot measure to pass."
- **Legal basis (staff report p. 2):** "Pursuant to Government Code section 36508, the City Council must submit a local measure to the voters to determine whether the City Clerk and City Treasurer positions should be appointive."
- **Analysis:** "places before the voters the question of whether the City of Red Bluff offices of City Clerk and City Treasurer should be converted from elected positions to Council appointed positions. State law requires all general law cities, like the City of Red Bluff, to have a City Clerk and a City Treasurer." / "If a majority "Yes" vote on the measure occurs, the Council would be permitted to establish minimum professional qualifications for each office and to hire, manage, and remove the City Clerk and City Treasurer." / "The current terms of office for the City Clerk and Treasurer elected in November 2024 will expire on November 7, 2028". Signed "/s/ Sophia Meyer, City Attorney". (The analysis leaves the letter blank in places: "A "Yes" vote on Measure __".)
- **Fiscal (staff report p. 3):** "The Tehama County Clerk's Office estimates that the cost of adding this ballot measure to the November 3, 2026, election would be $5,000-8,000." / "The City would not incur additional salary expenses as the City Clerk position would remain at $3,600 per year and the City Treasurer position would remain at $3,600 per year."
- **Council vote:** staff report stamped "Approved 5-0-0"; resolution dated July 21, 2026. Note: Resolution §1 misprints the date as "November 33, 2026".
