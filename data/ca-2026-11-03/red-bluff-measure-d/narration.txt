Measure D asks the voters of this city whether the city clerk and city treasurer should be appointed instead of elected.
Today, the voters elect the clerk and the treasurer to four-year terms.
From November 2028, or sooner if an office falls vacant, the City Council would appoint them instead.
The council could set minimum qualifications, and neither would have to live in the city.
The council could let the city manager make the appointments.
Measure D needs a majority vote to pass.
The city staff report says adding the measure to this ballot costs $5,000 to $8,000.
The report says the city would then save on future elections for the two offices, with no change in pay.
Supporters say both jobs are highly technical, and that appointed officers would be held accountable through performance reviews.
No argument against Measure D appears among the filings the county elections office has posted for this election.
A yes vote makes the city clerk and city treasurer appointed positions.
A no vote means the city clerk and city treasurer will continue to be elected.
