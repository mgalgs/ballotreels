    Section 1. Chapter 3-30, Section 3-30.015 (Operative Date) of the                                      City Attorney
Santa Rosa Municipal Code is hereby amended to read as follows:

     “Operative date” means the first day of the first calendar quarter
     commencing more than 110 days after the effective date of this
     Chapter as amended by the City’s voters in 2026.

      Section 2. Chapter 3-30, Section 3-30.040 (Transactions Tax Rate)
of the Santa Rosa Municipal Code is hereby amended to read as follows:

     For the privilege of selling tangible personal property at retail, a
     general tax is hereby imposed upon all retailers in the incorpo-
     rated territory of the City at the rate of one percent of the gross
     receipts of any retailer from the sale of all tangible personal prop-
     erty sold at retail in said territory on and after the operative date
     of this chapter.

    Section 3. Chapter 3-30, Section 3-30.050 (Use Tax Rate) of the
Santa Rosa Municipal Code is hereby amended to read as follows:

     A general excise tax is hereby imposed on the storage, use or
     other consumption in the City of tangible personal property pur-
     chased from any retailer on and after the operative date of this
     chapter for storage, use or other consumption in said territory at
     the rate of one percent of the sales price of the property. The
     sales price shall include delivery charges when such charges are
     subject to State sales or use tax regardless of the place to which
     delivery is made.

     Section 4. Chapter 3-30, Sections 3-30.120 (Effective Date) and 3-
30.130 (Termination Date) are hereby repealed.

      Section 5. The taxes imposed by this Ordinance shall remain in effect
until repealed by the City Council or by the voters of the City of Santa
Rosa.

      Section 6. This Ordinance is exempt from the California Environmen-
tal Quality Act. (Pub. Resources Code, § 21000 et seq.) It can be seen
with certainty that there is no possibility that this Ordinance may have a
significant effect on the environment. (14 CCR § 15061(b)(3).) Further,
this Ordinance simply creates a government funding mechanism, which
does not involve any commitment to any specific project which may result
in a potentially significant physical impact on the environment. (14 CCR §
15378(b)(4).)

     Section 7. This Ordinance shall go into effect 10 days after the City
Council declares its approval by a simple majority of the City’s voters.
(Elec. Code, § 9217.)
