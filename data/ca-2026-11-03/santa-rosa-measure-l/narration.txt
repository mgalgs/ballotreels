City of Santa Rosa Measure L asks voters in Santa Rosa whether to replace a one-half percent sales tax with a 1 percent tax.
The sales tax in Santa Rosa totals 10 percent, including a one-half percent city tax that expires on March 31, 2031.
Measure L would raise the total to 10.5 percent, with no end date unless voters repeal it.
As a general tax, it could pay for any lawful city purpose, and it needs a simple majority.
Without a state bill the Governor had not acted on when the analysis was written, the full increase may not take effect.
The City Attorney's impartial analysis estimates $46 million a year, about $23 million more than the current tax.
No argument against Measure L was submitted for the Sonoma County voter guide, so no opposing view appears beside it.
Supporters say Measure L prevents more cuts to fire, police and 911 services, and keeps every city fire station open.
A yes vote is a vote in favor of the measure.
A no vote is a vote against the measure.
