I-194 is about political spending by artificial persons.
Artificial persons include nonprofits, trusts, partnerships, corporations, trade associations and unincorporated associations.
Today, Montana law gives artificial persons privileges such as limited liability.
Artificial persons could not contribute to candidate elections, or support or oppose parties or ballot issues.
Violators would forfeit all Montana business privileges, restored only after disgorgement and certified compliance.
News and commentary, the state, political subdivisions, individuals, political committees and public corporations would be exempt.
Montana's Voter Information Pamphlet prints no cost estimate for the initiative.
Opponents say it restricts free speech of businesses, churches and community groups, sparing out-of-state political committees.
Supporters say Montana grants artificial persons all their powers, and can end dark money in elections.
A yes vote would approve adding language to Montana law.
A no vote would make no change to Montana law.
