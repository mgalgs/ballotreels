CI-132 is a proposed change to Montana's Constitution about judicial elections.
The amendment was proposed by initiative petition.
Today, the sentence the amendment proposes is not in Montana's Constitution.
The amendment would add one sentence: judicial elections shall remain nonpartisan.
The complete text of the amendment is that one sentence.
Montana's Voter Information Pamphlet does not include a cost estimate for the amendment.
Supporters say judges should be elected on their record and qualifications, not politics, and a recent law lets political parties give judicial candidates nearly $90,000.
Opponents say voters are entitled to know the party preferences of judicial candidates, and out-of-state special interests have spent more than $2 million on the amendment.
A yes vote would approve amending the Montana Constitution as proposed.
A no vote would make no change to the current Montana Constitution.
