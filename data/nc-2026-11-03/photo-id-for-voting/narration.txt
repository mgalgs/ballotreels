This amendment is about showing photo identification to vote in North Carolina.
Today the state constitution requires photo ID from voters who vote in person.
State law also has absentee voters send a copy of their photo ID, or a signed statement of an exception.
The amendment would put photo ID for all voters, however they vote, in the constitution.
As today, the General Assembly would set the ID rules, which may include exceptions.
North Carolina's official explanation does not include a cost estimate for the amendment.
North Carolina publishes no official argument for the amendment.
North Carolina publishes no official argument against the amendment.
A vote for puts photo ID for all voters in the constitution.
A vote against keeps the constitution's photo ID rule for in-person voters only.
