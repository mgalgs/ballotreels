This amendment is about the cap on North Carolina's income tax rate.
The constitution now caps the income tax rate at 7 percent.
State law lowers the personal rate by half a point when revenue passes set targets.
The amendment would lower the cap to 3.5 percent, for personal and corporate income, from 2027.
The Fiscal Research Division found no fiscal impact, because rates are expected to stay under the new cap.
If revenue missed its 2025-2026 target, the Division adds, the 3.99 percent rate would stay, and the cap would then require a lower rate.
North Carolina publishes no official argument against the amendment.
North Carolina publishes no official argument for the amendment.
A vote for the amendment sets the cap at 3.5 percent.
A vote against the amendment keeps the cap at 7 percent.
