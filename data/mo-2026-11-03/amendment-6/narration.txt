Amendment 6 is about the initiative and referendum petition process.
Today, the Missouri Constitution gives citizens the right to propose initiatives and referenda.
The amendment would make the petition process a fundamental right, and allow courts to revise ballot summaries.
Changing a law enacted by initiative would take at least 80 percent of each legislative chamber, and a vote of the people.
Signature and majority vote requirements would stay the same.
The State Auditor's fiscal note summary says state and local governmental entities estimate no costs or savings.
Missouri publishes no official argument against the amendment.
Missouri publishes no official argument for the amendment.
A yes vote will prohibit the legislature from directly changing laws enacted by initiative, and keep the current signature requirements.
A no vote will not change the current constitutional provisions on initiatives and referenda.
