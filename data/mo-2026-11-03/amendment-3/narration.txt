Amendment 3 is about abortion, and about gender transition procedures for minors.
Today, Section 36 of the Constitution, approved by voters in 2024, provides reproductive healthcare rights, including abortion through fetal viability.
The amendment would repeal that section, and allow legislation to regulate abortion providers and facilities.
The amendment would allow restriction of abortion to cases of medical emergency, rape and incest under twelve weeks gestation, and fetal anomalies.
The amendment would prohibit gender transition procedures for minors, with exceptions for specific medical conditions.
The State Auditor's fiscal note summary says state and other local governmental entities estimate no costs or savings; one county estimates it may experience an unknown increase in tax revenue.
Missouri publishes no official argument for the amendment.
Missouri publishes no official argument against the amendment.
A yes vote will repeal Section 36, and prohibit gender transition procedures for minors.
A no vote will leave Section 36 in place, and not prohibit gender transition procedures for minors.
