Amendment 7 is about the Show-Me Prosperity Fund, a permanent public endowment to support state government.
Today, the Missouri Constitution has no such fund.
The General Assembly could put money into the fund, which could also accept gifts.
No money could be spent from the fund until its earnings are enough to eliminate state-imposed taxes.
Once eliminated, those taxes could not be enacted again, unless the fund is unable to meet its obligations.
The State Auditor's fiscal note summary says state and local governmental entities estimate no costs or savings.
The ballot language says there is no immediate impact on taxes, but the fund could lead to the elimination of state-imposed taxes if its earnings become sufficient.
Missouri publishes no official argument for the amendment.
Missouri publishes no official argument against the amendment.
A yes vote will establish the Show-Me Prosperity Fund.
A no vote will not establish the fund.
