Amendment 8 is about county sheriffs.
Today, state law requires counties to elect a sheriff every four years, and governs the duties and removal of sheriffs.
The amendment would put the election and duties of sheriffs into the Missouri Constitution.
An elected sheriff could be removed only through a court action brought by the Attorney General.
The new section would not apply to a city not within a county, or to certain charter counties.
The State Auditor's fiscal note summary says state and local governmental entities estimate no costs or savings.
Missouri publishes no official argument for the amendment.
Missouri publishes no official argument against the amendment.
A yes vote will place these provisions on sheriffs, including their removal, in the Missouri Constitution.
A no vote will leave the current statutes on sheriffs in effect, unless changed by law.
