State Question 847 is about yearly limits on growth in property value.
Today, the limit is 5 percent for most property, and 3 percent for homesteads and farmland.
Homeowners 65 or older under an income cap get a senior freeze: no increase at all.
From 2027, the limits would drop to 4 percent and 1.75 percent.
The senior freeze would lose its income cap.
Seniors would instead get a limit tied to income compared with the local median, from no increase up to 1.75 percent.
The ballot title says the measure would have a fiscal impact on the state, but the exact impact is unknown.
Oklahoma publishes no official argument against the measure.
Oklahoma publishes no official argument for the measure.
A yes vote approves the new limits and senior rules.
A no vote keeps the current limits and senior freeze.
