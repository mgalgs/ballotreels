State Question 845 would change the structure of the Judicial Nominating Commission.
The commission has fifteen members, and decides whether nominees for judicial office qualify.
Today, appointments are based on congressional districts as they stood in 1967.
The Governor and the Oklahoma Bar Association would each appoint six members, including one from each current congressional district.
At least eight members would select three at-large members.
Any number of commissioners could be licensed attorneys.
Limits on party membership would end for members the Governor appoints and at-large members.
The ballot title says the measure will not have a fiscal impact.
Oklahoma publishes no official argument for the measure.
Oklahoma publishes no official argument against the measure.
A yes vote approves the new rules for choosing members.
A no vote keeps the current rules for choosing members.
