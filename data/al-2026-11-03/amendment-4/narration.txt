Amendment 4 is about The Star-Spangled Banner in public schools.
Today, the Alabama Constitution requires no school board policy on The Star-Spangled Banner.
Local boards would have to require each public school to broadcast the song's first stanza weekly.
A board could instead let a school-approved band, choir or singer perform the stanza, using the Library of Congress version.
Alabama's ballot statement says there are no costs or taxes.
A Legislative Services Agency fiscal note says local board administrative costs could rise, by an undetermined amount.
The note also estimates $100,000 in proclamation expenses for the 2027 state fiscal year.
Alabama publishes no official argument for the amendment.
Alabama publishes no official argument against the amendment.
A yes vote means the Alabama Constitution will be changed.
A no vote means the Alabama Constitution will not be changed.
