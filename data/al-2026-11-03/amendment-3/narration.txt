Amendment 3 is about the Pledge of Allegiance and student-led prayer in public schools.
Today, the Alabama Constitution requires no school policy on the pledge or student prayer.
Local boards would have to require each public school to conduct the pledge daily.
The boards would also have to allow student-initiated and student-led prayer.
Students could opt out of the pledge or the prayer based on their own beliefs.
The local superintendent would review reported violations.
Alabama's ballot statement says there are no costs or taxes.
A Legislative Services Agency fiscal note estimates $100,000 in proclamation expenses for the 2027 state fiscal year.
Alabama publishes no official argument for the amendment.
Alabama publishes no official argument against the amendment.
A yes vote means the Alabama Constitution will be changed.
A no vote means the Alabama Constitution will not be changed.
