Amendment 2 is about merging county school systems.
Today, the Alabama Constitution has no process for merging county school boards.
Neighboring county boards could merge into one school system.
The State Board of Education would first study and publish the effects.
If 25 percent of voters in any system protest, majorities in each county and overall must approve.
Alabama's ballot statement says there are no costs or taxes.
Legislative Services Agency notes on earlier versions say county board costs could rise or fall, adding State Board work.
The notes also estimate $100,000 in proclamation expenses for the 2027 state fiscal year.
Alabama publishes no official argument for the amendment.
Alabama publishes no official argument against the amendment.
A yes vote means the Alabama Constitution will be changed.
A no vote means the Alabama Constitution will not be changed.
