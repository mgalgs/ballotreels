Initiative 86 is about force-feeding birds to enlarge their livers, and foods like foie gras.
The District's Office of Revenue Analysis knows of no bird farms in the District.
The District's environment department already inspects shops and restaurants under bans on foam containers and straws.
Initiative 86 would ban force-feeding a bird to enlarge its liver, and selling the products, including foie gras.
From July 2027, violations would bring fines of 1,000 to 5,000 dollars a day.
The Chief Financial Officer estimates that carrying out the ban would cost 314,000 dollars over four years.
The Chief Financial Officer says the money is not yet budgeted, and carrying out the ban depends on it.
No official argument against Initiative 86 was published.
No official argument for Initiative 86 was published.
A yes vote approves the ban.
A no vote rejects the ban.
