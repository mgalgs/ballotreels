Initiative 86 is about force-feeding birds to enlarge their livers, and foods like foie gras.
California banned the force-feeding and sales in 2004; a court struck the sales ban down, then an appeals court upheld it.
Chicago banned restaurant sales in 2006; a court upheld the ban, then the city repealed it in 2008.
New York City passed a sales ban in 2019; an appeals court upheld it in 2026, but a court order kept it from being enforced.
Pittsburgh passed a sales ban in 2023; Portland, Oregon, passed one that starts December 31.
The Chief Financial Officer knows of no bird farms in the District.
Initiative 86 would ban the force-feeding, and selling the products or bringing them in for business.
From July 2027, restaurants, stores and others breaking the ban could be fined $1,000 to $5,000 a day.
The Chief Financial Officer estimates a cost of $314,000 over four years, less than 1 percent of any year's local spending.
Carrying out the ban waits until its cost is budgeted.
No official argument against Initiative 86 was published.
No official argument for Initiative 86 was published.
A yes vote approves the ban.
A no vote rejects the ban.
