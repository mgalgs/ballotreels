Measure 1 is about the vote it takes to change North Dakota's Constitution.
An amendment reaches the ballot by a vote of the Legislature or by a citizen petition.
Today, an amendment passes with more than 50 percent of the vote.
The measure would require at least 60 percent, for amendments from the Legislature and from petitions.
The measure itself passes with a majority vote.
North Dakota's guide says the estimated fiscal impact of the measure is none.
North Dakota's guide includes no official argument against the measure.
North Dakota's guide includes no official argument for the measure.
A yes vote means you support raising the threshold to at least 60 percent of the vote.
A no vote means you want the threshold to stay at more than 50 percent of the vote.
