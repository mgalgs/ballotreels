Question 1 is about funding State employee agreements, each a memorandum of understanding.
Today, if bargaining stalls, a fact finder recommends terms, which are not binding.
The amendment would require the Governor to budget for every agreement, whether bargained or set by a neutral decision-maker.
A related law would add binding arbitration, except in higher education, but new spending would still need legislative approval.
The Department of Legislative Services says State spending would likely rise at least $150,000 a year, if not significantly more.
Personnel costs would also be affected, potentially significantly, the Department says.
Maryland publishes no official argument for the amendment.
Maryland publishes no official argument against the amendment.
A vote for means the Governor must budget money to pay for these agreements.
A vote against means the law about funding these agreements stays the same.
