Question 2 is about filling seats on the Commission on Judicial Disabilities.
The Commission investigates complaints against judges and recommends discipline.
Today, the law has no provisions on membership after a recusal, a disqualification, or an expired term.
The amendment would let the chair recall a former member, or ask the Governor to appoint a temporary substitute or extend a current term.
Substitutes must meet the same qualifications, and cannot be someone the Senate rejected for the Commission.
The Department of Legislative Services says the Commission would gain efficiencies, with no other material effect on State finances.
Maryland publishes no official argument for the amendment.
Maryland publishes no official argument against the amendment.
A vote for means temporary appointments to the Commission are allowed.
A vote against means current membership rules stay the same.
