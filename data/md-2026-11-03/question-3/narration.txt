Question 3 is about rules for congressional districts in Maryland.
Today, the State Constitution requires each legislative district to be compact, with adjoining territory and substantially equal population.
A 2022 circuit court ruling held that those standards also apply to congressional districts.
The amendment would say those standards apply only to State Senate and House of Delegates districts.
The amendment would also say the State Constitution sets no criteria for congressional boundaries.
The General Assembly could also have congressional map challenges start in the Supreme Court of Maryland.
The Department of Legislative Services expects no material effect on State operations or finances.
Maryland publishes no official argument for the amendment.
Maryland publishes no official argument against the amendment.
A vote for means the State Constitution would change as described.
A vote against means the State Constitution stays as it is.
