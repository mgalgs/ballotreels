State Issue 3 is about photo ID to vote in Ohio.
Today, Ohio's Constitution has no voter ID section; state law sets voting rules.
The amendment would require an Ohio driver license or state ID, a United States passport, or a military, National Guard or Veterans Affairs ID.
State law could allow another check for voters with a religious objection to being photographed.
Voters who vote another way, such as by mail, would need photo ID, unless state law accepts a signature and another identifier.
Ohio's issue report prints no cost estimate for the amendment.
Opponents say the amendment leaves absentee voting unprotected, and blocks county veterans and digital military IDs.
Supporters say photo ID helps verify voters' identity, and a constitutional rule keeps election laws stable.
A yes vote means approval of the amendment.
A no vote means disapproval of the amendment.
