Public Measure 1 is about the votes needed to pass a bill raising income taxes.
Today, the Iowa Constitution requires a majority of the members elected to each house to pass a bill.
A bill raising an income tax rate would need votes from two-thirds of the members elected to each house.
So would a bill creating a new state tax on income.
Taxes a local government chooses to impose would not be covered.
A lawsuit challenging how such a bill was passed would have to be filed within one year.
Iowa's ballot summary does not include a cost estimate for the amendment.
Iowa publishes no official argument against the amendment.
Iowa publishes no official argument for the amendment.
A yes vote adds the two-thirds requirement to the Constitution.
A no vote does not add that requirement to the Constitution.
