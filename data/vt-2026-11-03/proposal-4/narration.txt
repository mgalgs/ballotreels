Proposal 4 is about equality of rights in Vermont's Constitution.
The Legislature adopted the amendment in 2024, and again in 2026.
The proposal adds a new article, changing no existing one.
The article would guarantee equal protection under the law.
The State could not deny equal treatment on account of race, ethnicity, sex, religion, disability, sexual orientation, gender identity, gender expression, or national origin.
The article would not prevent measures intended to provide equality of treatment and opportunity for groups historically subject to discrimination.
Vermont's ballot does not include a cost estimate for the amendment.
Vermont publishes no official argument for the amendment.
Vermont publishes no official argument against the amendment.
A yes vote adds the equality of rights article to Vermont's Constitution.
A no vote does not add the equality of rights article to Vermont's Constitution.
