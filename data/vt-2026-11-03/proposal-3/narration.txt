Proposal 3 is about a right to collectively bargain.
The Legislature adopted the amendment in 2024, and again in 2025.
The amendment adds a new article to the Constitution.
Employees would have a right to organize or join a labor organization.
Employees would bargain through an exclusive representative of their choosing, over wages, hours and working conditions.
No law could interfere with, negate, or diminish the right to bargain.
Nor could a law prohibit agreements requiring membership in the labor organization as a condition of employment.
Vermont's ballot does not include a cost estimate for the amendment.
Vermont publishes no official argument against the amendment.
Vermont publishes no official argument for the amendment.
A yes vote adds the right to collectively bargain to the Constitution.
A no vote does not add the right to collectively bargain to the Constitution.
