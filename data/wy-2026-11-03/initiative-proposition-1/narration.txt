Proposition One is a proposed law about property taxes on Wyoming homes.
Wyoming law today lists property exempt from tax.
The proposition would exempt fifty percent of the assessed value of a qualifying primary residence from property tax.
Owners would need a year living in Wyoming, and six months in the home.
The ballot's estimate says state revenue would fall about $92.6 million in fiscal year 2028, and $95.9 million in 2029.
That estimate covers the state only, not local governments.
It says the earliest tax year would be 2027.
Wyoming publishes no official argument against the proposition.
Wyoming publishes no official argument for the proposition.
Voting for it means the law would be enacted.
Voting against it means the law would not be enacted.
It passes if approved by a majority of those voting in the General Election.
