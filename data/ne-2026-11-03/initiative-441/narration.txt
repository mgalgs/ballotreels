Initiative Measure 441 is a proposed law about placing sports wagers online.
Today, sports wagers must be placed in person or at a kiosk at a licensed racetrack.
The initiative would add wagers through an online platform, by anyone located in Nebraska.
Each racetrack gaming operator could use up to two online platforms run by contracted providers.
The Secretary of State's informational pamphlet does not include a cost estimate for the initiative.
Opponents say Initiative 441 puts out-of-state gambling interests ahead of Nebraska taxpayers, and every teenager with a phone will face aggressive advertising.
Supporters say Initiative 441 creates real consumer protections, and directs 70 percent of wagering tax revenue to a property tax credit fund.
A vote for enacts statutes allowing and regulating online sports wagers.
A vote against means no such statutes will be enacted.
