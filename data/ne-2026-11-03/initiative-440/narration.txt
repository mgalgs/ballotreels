Initiative Measure 440 is a proposed constitutional amendment about placing sports wagers online.
Today, the constitution allows laws for games of chance at licensed racetracks.
The amendment would allow laws letting people in Nebraska place sports wagers through the Internet.
The online wagers would be offered by the gaming operators at licensed racetracks.
The Secretary of State's informational pamphlet does not include a cost estimate for the initiative.
Opponents say Initiative 440 sends hundreds of millions of dollars out of Nebraska to gambling conglomerates, and online betting fuels corruption in sports.
Supporters say Initiative 440 keeps the money in Nebraska, and directs more than $166 million to property tax relief over ten years.
A vote for amends the constitution to allow laws for online sports wagers.
A vote against means the constitution will not be amended in this manner.
