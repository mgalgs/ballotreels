Amendment 1 is about how many terms in a row a state legislator may serve.
The Legislature placed the amendment on the ballot in 2025.
Today, a legislator who serves two four-year terms in a row cannot serve again for four years.
Serving more than half of a term counts as serving a full term.
The amendment would raise the limit to three consecutive four-year terms, or 12 years.
After three terms in a row, a legislator could not serve again for four years.
The Legislature's resolution does not include a cost estimate for the amendment.
Nebraska publishes no official argument for the amendment.
Nebraska publishes no official argument against the amendment.
A vote for raises the limit to three consecutive four-year terms.
A vote against keeps the limit at two consecutive four-year terms.
