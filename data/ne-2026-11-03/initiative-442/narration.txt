Initiative Measure 442 is about school sports and biological sex.
Today, the constitution lacks the section this initiative would add.
Schools would designate each team as male, female, or coed or mixed, based on biological sex.
Teams for females would not be open to students of the male sex.
The rule would cover public schools and colleges, and private schools that compete against them.
The Secretary of State's informational pamphlet does not include a cost estimate for the initiative.
Supporters say Initiative 442 ensures female athletes are not forced to compete against biological males, which poses safety risks.
Opponents say Initiative 442 requires gender verification for girls but not boys, and subjects all girls to greater barriers.
A vote for adds these rules to the constitution.
A vote against means the constitution will not be amended in this manner.
