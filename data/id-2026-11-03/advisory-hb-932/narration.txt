House Bill 932, an advisory question, asks which of six guns should be designated the state gun of Idaho.
The question is non-binding.
To designate a state gun, the Legislature would have to act in a future session.
The ballot lists the Winchester Model 1894, the Winchester Model 1873 “Gun that Won the West”, and the 1873 Colt Single Action Army Revolver “Peacemaker”.
Then the M1 Garand rifle, the Colt M1911 .45 automatic Colt pistol, and the Remington Model 700 bolt-action rifle.
The Voter Pamphlet describes each one.
The bill's fiscal note, prepared by a supporter of the bill, says the bill is expected to have no direct impact on the General Fund or local government revenues.
Voters choose only one of the six.
