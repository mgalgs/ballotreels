Proposition 1 is about abortion and reproductive care.
Today, Idaho prohibits abortion except to prevent the pregnant woman's death, and in the first trimester for reported rape or incest.
The measure would create a right to abortion before fetal viability.
After viability, only in medical emergencies threatening the woman's life or health.
The Division of Financial Management estimates spending would rise $3,100 to $7,800 a year, less than 0.001 percent of the state's Medicaid share.
Supporters say the measure lets patients and their doctors, not the government, make urgent decisions in medical emergencies.
Opponents say the measure would allow abortion until seven or eight months of pregnancy, and override parental consent.
A yes vote creates a right to abortion before viability, and after it in medical emergencies.
A no vote keeps Idaho's current law.
