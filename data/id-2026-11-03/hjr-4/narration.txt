House Joint Resolution 4 is about who can legalize marijuana and other drugs in Idaho.
Today, Idaho's controlled substances laws can be changed by the Legislature, or by voters through a ballot initiative.
The amendment would give the Legislature exclusive authority to legalize marijuana, narcotics or other psychoactive substances.
The amendment's fiscal note says its only cost is putting it on the ballot.
Opponents say the amendment takes power from the people, who are as capable as legislators on drug policy.
Supporters say drug legalization is too important to decide lightly, and belongs in public hearings before the Legislature.
A yes vote means only the Legislature could legalize marijuana, narcotics or other psychoactive substances.
A no vote means no change: drug laws could still be changed by initiative.
