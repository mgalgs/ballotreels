Amendment 1 is about Florida's Budget Stabilization Fund, money the state sets aside.
The state can draw on the fund for a revenue shortfall or an emergency.
In 2025 the fund held $4.4 billion; its ceiling is 10 percent of yearly general revenue.
Amendment 1 would raise the ceiling to 25 percent, $12.2 billion.
The Legislature would have to add money every year, unless certain conditions are met.
The Legislature could also withdraw money for critical state needs.
The Senate's analysis says that, with revenue unchanged, the state would add $750 million a year for at least 10 years.
No official argument against Amendment 1 was published.
No official argument for Amendment 1 was published.
A yes vote raises the fund's ceiling to 25 percent.
A no vote keeps the fund's ceiling at 10 percent.
