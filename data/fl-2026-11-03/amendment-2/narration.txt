Amendment 2 is about property taxes on equipment and other movable property kept on Florida farmland.
Only local governments, like counties, cities and school districts, tax this kind of property in Florida.
Today, the first $25,000 of this property's assessed value is exempt.
Amendment 2 would exempt this property entirely if it is usually kept on farmland, used for farming or agritourism, and owned by the landowner or leaseholder.
The exemption would first apply to taxes for 2027.
Florida's amendment booklet prints no estimate of how Amendment 2 would change local tax revenue.
No official argument for Amendment 2 was published.
No official argument against Amendment 2 was published.
A yes vote exempts this farm property from property tax.
A no vote keeps this farm property subject to property tax.
