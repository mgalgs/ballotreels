Amendment 3 is about local property taxes in Florida.
Today, a homestead, a home its owner lives in, has up to about $50,000 exempt.
The amendment would raise that to $150,000 for non-school taxes in 2027, and $250,000 in 2028.
For other property, the cap on yearly assessment increases would fall from 10 to 5 percent.
Counties and cities could spend property taxes only on listed purposes, or others officials approve.
The Revenue Estimating Conference expects non-school property tax revenue to fall $11.86 billion a year once fully in effect; in 2025, counties levied $20.7 billion, cities $9.4 billion, other districts $6.2 billion.
No official argument against the amendment was published.
No official argument for the amendment was published.
A yes vote raises the exemption and lowers the cap.
A no vote keeps today's exemption and cap.
