Amendment 82 is about natural gas for cooking and heating.
Today, the state regulates investor-owned natural gas utilities, and some towns restrict gas appliances in new buildings.
The amendment would add a constitutional right to purchase and sell natural gas for homes and businesses.
Legislative Council Staff says this may limit natural gas rules by state and local governments.
Legislative Council Staff says state costs may increase minimally.
Local governments that restrict gas in building codes may face legal costs.
Supporters say natural gas reliably heats homes in the coldest weather, and access should face no government interference.
Opponents say natural gas adds to climate change and air pollution, and local communities should make energy decisions.
A yes vote establishes a constitutional right to purchase and sell natural gas.
A no vote maintains current law and regulations.
