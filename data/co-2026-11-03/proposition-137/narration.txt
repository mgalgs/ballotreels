Proposition 137 is about the state sales tax collected on sporting goods.
Today, that tax goes into the general state budget, and revenue above the state limit is refunded.
The proposition would direct that money to conservation, wildfire risk mitigation, and outdoor recreation programs.
The money would be exempt from the limit, reducing refunds in years when revenue exceeds it.
Legislative Council Staff estimates $175 million would move from the General Fund in the 2027-2028 budget year.
Opponents say the proposition is a tax increase in disguise, and constrains how legislators balance budget priorities.
Supporters say the proposition funds conservation and wildfire mitigation without raising tax rates, through a user-based model.
A yes vote directs this revenue to conservation, wildfire, and outdoor recreation programs.
A no vote keeps the revenue in the general budget.
