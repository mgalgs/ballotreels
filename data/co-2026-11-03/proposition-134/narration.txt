Proposition 134 is about who may play on school sports teams.
Today, schools, districts and athletic associations set their own policies on who may play, within state and federal law.
Students would play on teams matching their biological sex, or on coeducational teams.
Legislative Council Staff says it is unclear how this would affect Colorado's anti-discrimination law.
Legislative Council Staff expects minimal costs for schools, and state enforcement costs if a district intentionally does not comply.
Supporters say females should be able to compete against other females, and the proposition protects the safety of female athletes.
Opponents say the proposition could subject many athletes to physical inspections, and that targeting at-risk students leads to harassment.
A yes vote means every school team must be for biological males or biological females only, or mixed.
A no vote means schools may continue to set their own policies.
