Proposition 132 is about criminal penalties for fentanyl.
Today, penalties depend on the amount and intent, and possessing a small amount is a misdemeanor.
The proposition would make possessing any amount of fentanyl a felony.
Distributing any amount would carry a mandatory 8 to 32 years in prison.
Judges could no longer reduce penalties for certain mitigating circumstances.
Legislative Council Staff estimates General Fund costs of $0.9 million the first year, rising to $68.2 million by year five.
Opponents say the proposition is unlikely to prevent overdose deaths, and may discourage people from seeking treatment.
Supporters say fentanyl warrants stronger penalties, and mandated prison for dealers will likely make the public safer.
A yes vote makes possession of any amount of fentanyl a felony.
A no vote keeps minor possession charges a misdemeanor.
