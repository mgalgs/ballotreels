Proposition 136 is about Colorado's state income tax rate.
Today, the state income tax rate is 4.4 percent, for individuals and corporations.
The proposition would set that rate as the maximum in state law, starting in 2027.
The current rate itself would not change.
If Amendment 87 also passes, the exact outcome is unclear; the legislature or a court would determine how to resolve it.
Legislative Council Staff says the proposition has no fiscal impact.
Opponents say the proposition is designed to put Amendment 87 in legal jeopardy, with no effect after the election.
Supporters say the proposition reinforces voter support for the current rate, and that rate is among the lowest nationally.
A yes vote establishes the current 4.4 percent rate as the maximum rate.
A no vote keeps the current rate without a new limit in state law.
