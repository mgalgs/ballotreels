Proposition 133 is about penalties for child sex trafficking.
Today, the crime carries 8 to 48 years in prison, plus five years of parole.
The proposition would make the penalty life in prison without parole.
The crime would also include trading anything of value for sexual activity with a minor.
Legislative Council Staff says state prison costs would begin in budget year 2041-42.
Those costs start at about $180,000 a year, and grow by that amount each year for several years.
Opponents say harsher sentences are unlikely to deter trafficking, and may punish the victims forced to help traffickers.
Supporters say this crime causes serious, long-lasting harm, and a life sentence ensures offenders cannot hurt more children.
A yes vote raises the penalty to life in prison without parole.
A no vote keeps the existing range of 8 to 48 years in prison.
