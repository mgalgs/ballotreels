Proposition 135 is about surgery on minors to alter their biological sex characteristics.
Today, such surgery that is medically necessary may be covered by private insurance or a public health care program.
The proposition would ban such surgery on minors as a treatment in response to their perception of sex or gender.
Male circumcision, treatment of medically verifiable disorders of sex development, and non-surgical care like counseling would still be allowed.
Government funds and insurance could not pay for the banned surgeries.
Legislative Council Staff expects any change in state and local spending to be minimal.
Supporters say minors lack the maturity for permanent, life-altering decisions, and some may regret surgery they cannot undo.
Opponents say the overly broad ban interferes with needed care, and medical decisions belong to families and providers.
A yes vote prohibits these surgeries on minors.
A no vote maintains current Colorado law.
