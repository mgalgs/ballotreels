Amendment 85 is about how ballot titles are written.
The state Title Board writes the ballot title for each citizen initiative.
Today, the law requires plain language in ballot titles, but no grade-level standard.
Some laws require added wording, like how a tax change affects taxpayers.
The amendment would require ballot titles at no more than a grade 8 reading level.
Laws requiring wording in initiative titles that conflicts with that standard would be barred.
Legislative Council Staff says workload for the Title Board may increase minimally.
Opponents say current law provides ample guidance, and undefined terms could block laws giving voters critical information.
Supporters say a constitutional standard helps craft simpler titles, and protects initiative titles from interference by legislators.
A yes vote requires plain-language titles, defined as a grade 8 reading level.
A no vote maintains current law, with no grade-level standard.
