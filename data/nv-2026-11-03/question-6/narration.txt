Question 6 would add a fundamental right to abortion to Nevada's Constitution.
Proposed by petition, the amendment must pass at two successive general elections: 2024 and 2026.
Today, state law allows abortion within 24 weeks, or later for the pregnant individual's life or health.
The right would cover abortion by a qualified healthcare professional until fetal viability, or later when necessary to protect the pregnant individual's health or life.
The Legislature's 2024 fiscal note says the financial effect cannot be determined.
Opponents say the amendment changes Nevada's clear abortion law, and may force taxpayers to fund abortions.
Supporters say the amendment keeps politicians out of healthcare decisions, and protects abortion rights for good.
A yes vote would create a constitutional right to abortion.
A no vote would keep the Constitution in its current form.
