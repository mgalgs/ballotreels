Question 7 would put voter identification rules in Nevada's Constitution.
Approved by voters in 2024, the amendment needs a second approval to take effect.
Today, Nevada voters show identification only in rare situations.
In-person voters would show a photo ID, such as a driver's license or passport.
Voters who vote by mail would add the last four digits of a license or Social Security number.
The Legislature's 2024 fiscal note estimates about $6,750 for voter system changes.
The note also assumes free IDs for some voters; that cost cannot be determined.
Supporters say requiring photo ID helps make elections more secure, and has not lowered turnout elsewhere.
Opponents say ID requirements keep eligible voters from voting, and do nothing to prevent voter fraud.
A yes vote means identification in person, and certain information by mail.
A no vote keeps the Constitution in its current form.
