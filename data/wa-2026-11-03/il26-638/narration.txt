Initiative Measure IL26-638 is about who may compete in girls' school sports.
Today, schools may have separate teams by sex.
State rules bar sports discrimination based on sex or gender identity.
IL26-638 would bar students it defines as biologically male from girls-only sports.
The Attorney General says the measure would generally prevent transgender girls from playing on girls' teams.
Students in girls-only sports would need a health care provider to verify their sex.
The Office of Financial Management expects minor state costs to update guidance.
School districts may also have costs, which the Office calls indeterminate.
Supporters say IL26-638 protects fairness and opportunities in girls' sports, in line with world sports policies.
Opponents say IL26-638 would subject girls to invasive sex exams, and discourage girls from playing sports.
A yes vote means the prohibition and verification would become law.
A no vote means the prohibition and verification would not become law.
