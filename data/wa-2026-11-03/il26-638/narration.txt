Initiative Measure IL26-638 is about who may compete in girls' school sports.
Today, schools may have separate teams by sex.
The measure would bar students it defines as biologically male from girls-only sports.
The Attorney General says the measure would generally prevent transgender girls from playing on girls' teams.
Students in girls-only sports would need a health care provider to verify their sex.
The Office of Financial Management expects minor state costs, and uncertain costs for school districts.
Supporters say the measure protects fairness and opportunities in girls' sports, in line with world sports policies.
Opponents say the measure would subject girls to invasive sex exams, and discourage girls from playing sports.
A yes vote means the prohibition and verification would become law.
A no vote means the prohibition and verification would not become law.
