Initiative Measure IL26-001 is about parents' rights in public schools.
In 2024, the Legislature passed Initiative 2081, a list of parents' rights at school.
In 2025, the Legislature changed the list.
Today, schools have up to 45 days to provide a child's records.
IL26-001 would restore the 2024 list, with records due in ten business days.
The 2025 entries on language access and special education would come off the list.
The Office of Financial Management says there would be no fiscal impact on the state.
School districts may have minor costs, which the Office calls indeterminate.
Supporters say IL26-001 restores parents' rights to school information, and stops schools keeping secrets from families.
Opponents say IL26-001 endangers child abuse victims, and ends immediate parental notice of crimes against students.
A yes vote means the 2025 amendments would be repealed.
A no vote means the 2025 amendments would not be repealed.
