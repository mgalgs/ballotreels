Initiative Measure IP26-645 is about taxes on individual income.
In 2026, the Legislature passed a 9.9 percent tax on yearly income over a million dollars, starting in 2028.
The measure would repeal that tax, and bar state and local taxes on individual income.
The Office of Financial Management estimates the state would lose $11.4 billion in revenue over budget years 2027 to 2031.
The Office also expects state agencies to save about $150 million over the same five years.
Opponents say the measure gives the wealthiest a tax break, and cuts funding for schools, healthcare and childcare.
Supporters say the measure repeals the income tax before it starts, so lawmakers can't expand it to everyone.
A yes vote means the 9.9 percent tax would be repealed.
A no vote means the 9.9 percent tax would not be repealed.
