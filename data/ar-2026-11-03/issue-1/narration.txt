Issue 1 is about who may vote in Arkansas.
Today, the Arkansas Constitution says voters must be citizens, state residents, at least 18, and registered.
The amendment would say only citizens meeting those qualifications may vote.
Anyone who does not meet the qualifications could not vote in any state or local election.
Arkansas does not publish a cost estimate for the amendment.
Opponents say, according to the University of Arkansas guide, voting is already limited to citizens, and it could prompt proof-of-citizenship laws many qualified voters could not meet.
Supporters say, according to the University of Arkansas guide, only-citizen wording sets a clear ceiling on who votes, and acts before any Arkansas city allows noncitizen voting.
A vote for means only qualified citizens may vote.
A vote against means the current wording would stay.
