Issue 4 asks about bonds for water and waste projects.
The University of Arkansas guide says voters must approve most general obligation bonds.
A state commission could fund water, waste, drainage, irrigation, and flood control projects.
Borrowing could total $500 million, at most $165 million for irrigation.
Lawmakers must approve issuing more than $60 million in two years.
General revenue would repay each series within 35 years.
Supporters say, according to the University of Arkansas guide, the future needs more money, and every county has received at least one of these loans.
Opponents say, according to the University of Arkansas guide, borrowing means paying interest at higher rates, and broad wording leaves unclear who could benefit.
A vote for means the bonds could be issued.
A vote against means the bonds could not be issued.
