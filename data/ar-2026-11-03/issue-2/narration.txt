Issue 2 is about the right to keep and bear arms.
Today, the Constitution gives citizens that right for their common defense.
The amendment would add hunting, recreation, and any other lawful purpose.
The right would include ammunition, firearm accessories, and components, without limitation.
The right would be called natural, fundamental, and individual.
Arkansas does not publish a cost estimate for the amendment.
Opponents say, according to the University of Arkansas guide, the constitution already protects the right, and the amendment could make firearm-safety laws harder to defend and enforce.
Supporters say, according to the University of Arkansas guide, the amendment clearly covers ammunition, accessories and components, and guards against renewed calls to disarm law-abiding citizens.
A vote for means the new wording would be added.
A vote against means the current wording would stay.
