Question 3 is about discrimination and preferential treatment by government.
The 2023 Legislature approved the amendment; the 2025 Legislature approved it again.
The amendment would add a new section to the state constitution.
The amendment would bar governmental entities from discriminating against, or granting preferential treatment to, any individual or group on the basis of race, sex, color, ethnicity or national origin.
The rule would cover public employment, education, contracting and administration.
Covered entities include the state, local governments, public schools and colleges, the Legislature and the courts.
Wisconsin's Type A Notice of Referendum does not include a cost estimate for the amendment.
Wisconsin publishes no official argument against the amendment.
Wisconsin publishes no official argument for the amendment.
A yes vote adds this section to the state constitution.
A no vote leaves the state constitution without this section.
