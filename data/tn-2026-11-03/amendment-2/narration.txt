Amendment 2 is about a state tax on property.
Today, the Constitution allows property to be taxed by the state, counties and cities.
The resolution says the Legislature repealed the state property tax in 1949, and property tax is now imposed at county and city rates.
The amendment would bar the Legislature from imposing a state property tax on any kind of property.
All property would still be subject to taxation, but not to a state tax.
The Fiscal Review Committee estimates a one-time local cost of $9,400 to print a longer sample ballot.
Tennessee publishes no official argument for the amendment.
Tennessee publishes no official argument against the amendment.
