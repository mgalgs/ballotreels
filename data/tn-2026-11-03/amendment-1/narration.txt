Amendment 1 is about bail before trial.
Today, the Constitution allows bail except for capital offenses, when the proof is evident or the presumption great.
Courts could deny bail to anyone accused of a crime punishable by death, an act of terrorism, or second degree murder.
The same goes for aggravated rape of a child, aggravated rape, grave torture, and any crime that requires serving eighty-five percent of the sentence.
Courts must record why they grant or deny bail.
