Amendment 1 is about who may vote in West Virginia.
The Legislature placed the amendment on the ballot, with two-thirds of each house agreeing.
Today, the state Constitution says the citizens of the state are entitled to vote.
The amendment would instead say only citizens of the United States who are citizens of this state are entitled to vote.
The section's other rules, including the 30-day residence requirement, would stay the same.
West Virginia's ballot does not include a cost estimate for the amendment.
West Virginia publishes no official argument against the amendment.
West Virginia publishes no official argument for the amendment.
A vote for changes the wording to only citizens of the United States who are citizens of this state.
A vote against keeps the current wording, the citizens of the state.
