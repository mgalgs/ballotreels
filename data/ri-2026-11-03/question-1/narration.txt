Question 1 would let Rhode Island borrow $275 million for higher education facilities.
A bond is a loan, repaid with interest from the state's general revenues.
Each of five bond questions is decided separately, by majority vote.
$165 million would build the University of Rhode Island's Integrated Health Building.
$50 million would renovate Rhode Island College's library.
$60 million would build a Workforce Innovation Center at the Community College of Rhode Island.
The handbook estimates $166,333,250 in interest, a total cost of $441,333,250.
Those are only estimates, based on 20-year loans at 5 percent interest.
The handbook includes no official argument for the question.
The handbook includes no official argument against the question.
Approve means you support the State issuing these bonds.
Reject means you are against the State issuing these bonds.
