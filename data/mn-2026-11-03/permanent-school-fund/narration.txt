The ballot asks whether to amend the constitution to increase the funding going to all school districts from the permanent school fund.
Today the constitution keeps the fund's original money untouched forever, and sends its interest and dividends to districts.
The amendment would have the fund pay districts yearly, preserving its purchasing power.
The fund could also pay administrative costs.
If the amendment passes, a separate law would pay 4.5 percent of the fund's three-year average value yearly.
The Secretary of State's constitutional amendments page does not include a cost estimate for the amendment.
Minnesota publishes no official argument against the amendment.
Minnesota publishes no official argument for the amendment.
A yes vote adopts the amendment and the payout law.
A no vote keeps today's rules.
Skipping the question has the same effect as voting no.
