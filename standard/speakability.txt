# Words and spellings the narration's transcript check mishears (`say`
# re-transcribes every phrase it synthesises and fails a take whose words
# differ from the script's). The narration is the script verbatim, so these
# are caught when the script is written: `python -m ballotreels check`
# fails a narrated line that matches an entry, but only in a script whose
# narration is missing or stale (every committed narration already passed
# the real transcript check). Card quotes and on-screen list items are not
# narrated and are not checked.
#
# One entry per line: a Python regular expression (case-sensitive; start
# it with (?i) for any case), " | ", why it is misheard, " | ", what to
# write instead. A pattern may use "|" for alternation, but not " | ".
\b[A-Z]{3,}s\b | an acronym written as a plural is heard as a word ("PACs" as "packs" or "pacts"; two-letter ones like "IDs" are spelled out and pass) | write out what it stands for ("political committees")
\bPACs?\b | "PAC" is said as a word and heard as "pack" | write out what it stands for ("political committee")
(?<!\w)(?:[A-Za-z]\.){2,} | an abbreviation with internal periods splits the phrase or is read letter by letter ("U.S.", "e.g.") | write the words ("United States", "for example")
\bCounsel\b | "Counsel" is heard as "Council" | name the body another way ("the Legislature's fiscal note")
(?i)\bmail voters?\b | "mail voters" is heard as "male voters" | say "voters who vote by mail"
(?i)\bconsents?\b | "consents" was heard as "can sense" | say "agrees" or "permission"
(?i)\b\w*[b-df-hj-np-tv-z]y['’]s\b | a possessive of a word ending in a consonant and "y" sounds just like its plural ("property's" is heard as "properties") | rephrase with "of" ("the value of the property")
[À-ÖØ-öø-ÿ] | the transcript check hears an accented letter without its accent ("René" as "Rene") | write the name without the accent ("Rene"); the name check folds accents when it matches the source
\bLuz\b | the name "Luz" is heard as "Luce" or "Lou's" | use the title and surname ("Senator Escamilla")
(?i)\bK through 12\b | the transcript writes "K-12", and "through" is a word the script has but the transcript lacks | write "K-12"
(?i)\b(?:first|second|third|fourth|fifth|sixth|seventh|eighth|ninth|tenth|eleventh|twelfth|\d+(?:st|nd|rd|th))-grade\b | the transcript writes a grade either as a word or as a numeral ("eighth" or "8th"), at random, and the check folds neither | write the grade as a plain number, which the check folds ("a grade 8 reading level")
(?i)\b(?:one|two|three|four|five|six|seven|eight|nine|ten) and a half\b | the transcript writes a decimal ("3.5") and the check does not fold "and a half" | write the decimal ("3.5 percent")
\b(?:19|20)\d\d to (?:19|20)\d\d\b | the transcript writes a span of years with a hyphen ("2027-2028") and drops the "to" | write the hyphen ("the 2027-2028 budget year")
(?i)\bGeorgia['’]s\b | "Georgia's" is heard as "George's" | name it another way ("the state's", "of Georgia")
\bRhody\b | "Rhody" is heard as "Roadie" | describe the fund instead of naming it
(?i)\bA vote for\b(?! the\b) | after "vote", "for" is transcribed as the digit "4" | say "A vote for the amendment" or "Voting for it"
\$\d[\d.,]* (?:million|billion) to \$\d[\d.,]* (?:million|billion) | a range of dollar amounts is transcribed in words ("one million to seven million dollars") | drop the figures ("get smaller amounts") or give the total; "up to $7 million" was transcribed "up to seven million dollars" too
(?i)\b\w+s['’]s\b | a possessive written "s's" is transcribed "s'" ("Kansas's" as "Kansas'") | rephrase ("the state's", "of Kansas")
(?i)\bbailable\b | "bailable" is heard as "available" | say "allows bail for" or "can be released on bail"
(?i)\bbanned s | "banned" before a word starting with "s" runs together and is heard as "bans" or "band" ("banned sales" as "bans sales") | rephrase ("passed a sales ban", "banned the sale of")
\b(?:19|20)\d\d-\d\d\b | a fiscal year with a two-digit end ("2049-50") is transcribed at random as a decimal ("2049.50") | write both years in full ("through 2049-2050")
\bCuyamaca\b | the name "Cuyamaca" is transcribed as "Kuyamaka", "Kwiamaka" or "Q. Yamaka" | name the district another way ("this community college district"); the card and title show its name
(?i)\bend it\b | "end it" runs together and is heard as "ended" | say "repeal it"
(?i)\bfavors\b | the transcript may spell it the British way ("favours") | say "supports"
