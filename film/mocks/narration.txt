# Facts from the Legislative Analyst's analysis of Prop 2
# (data/ca-2026-11-03/prop-2/sources/analysis.html).
California's tax revenue rises and falls with the economy.
In a bad recession, it can drop by tens of billions of dollars.
To get through those years, the state saves in a Rainy Day Fund.
Today, it saves until the fund holds 10 percent of General Fund taxes.
Proposition 2 would raise that to 20 percent.
